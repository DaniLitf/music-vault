# Opera Music

**Recording studio. North Hollywood, Los Angeles, USA.**

Opera Music is a North Hollywood recording studio purchased in October 2005 by Mark Hoppus and Travis Barker from former Poison guitarist Richie Kotzen. The space features two recording rooms, a lounge, and a small outdoor courtyard, and became the home base for +44 — buying it shifted the band from electronic bedroom demos to a more organic full-band sound. The October 2008 meeting between Tom DeLonge and Travis Barker there laid the grounds for Blink-182's reunion.
