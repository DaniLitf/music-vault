# One On One Recording Studio

**Recording studio. Los Angeles, United States.**

One On One Recording Studio was a Los Angeles studio used by [[Artists/M/Metallica]] for tracking *...And Justice for All* (1988) and their self-titled *Metallica* (1991), the latter produced by Bob Rock and marking the band's shift toward a more polished commercial sound.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/M/Metallica]] – *...And Justice for All* (1988)
- [[Artists/M/Metallica]] – *Metallica* (1991)
