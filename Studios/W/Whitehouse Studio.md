# Whitehouse Studio

**Recording studio. Newark, Delaware, USA.**

Whitehouse Studio is a recording studio in Newark, Delaware. Vocal tracking for the Descendents' *Hypercaffium Spazzinate* (2016) was done here, near singer Milo Aukerman's Delaware home.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/D/Descendents]] – *Hypercaffium Spazzinate* (2016, partial)
