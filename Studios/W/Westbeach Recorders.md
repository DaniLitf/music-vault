# Westbeach Recorders

**Recording studio. Los Angeles, USA.**

Westbeach Recorders was a Los Angeles recording studio co-founded by Bad Religion guitarist and Epitaph Records boss Brett Gurewitz. It became a hub for Southern California punk rock in the late 1980s and 1990s, recording albums for many Epitaph-era punk acts; [[Artists/B/Blink-182]] recorded their debut *Cheshire Cat* there in just three days.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/B/Blink-182]] – *Cheshire Cat* (1995)
- [[Artists/A/All]] – *New Girl, Old Story* (1991)
- [[Artists/A/Avenged Sevenfold]] – *Sounding the Seventh Trumpet* (2001)
