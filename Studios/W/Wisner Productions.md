# Wisner Productions

**Recording studio. St. Cloud, USA.**

Wisner Productions is the recording studio of producer James Paul Wisner, originally located in Davie, Florida and later relocated to St. Cloud, Florida. It has been a fixture of the Florida punk and hardcore scene since the mid-1990s, recording [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]]'s earliest demos as well as their debut album *Won*.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] – *As Friends Rust* demo (1996)
- [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] – *Won* (2001)
- [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] – *A Young Trophy Band in the Parlance of Our Times* (2002)
- [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] – *Up from the Muck* (2020, mixing)
