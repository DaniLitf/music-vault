# Wire Recording

**Recording studio. AUSTIN, TEXAS, USA.**

Wire Recording is a recording studio based in Austin, Texas, run by engineer Stuart Sullivan, known for work with roots-rock, alternative, and indie acts, including the sole album by supergroup Eyes Adrift.
