# Whitney Studios

**Recording studio. Los Angeles, California, USA.**

Whitney Studios was a recording studio in Burbank, Los Angeles, active in the 1960s and 1970s. It was the studio where the Alice Cooper band recorded their first two albums, *Pretties for You* (1969) and *Easy Action* (1970), under the guidance of Frank Zappa.
