# First Avenue

**Recording studio and music venue. MINNEAPOLIS, MINNESOTA, USA.**

First Avenue is a historic nightclub and music venue in Minneapolis, originally opened in 1970 as The Depot. It gained worldwide fame as the primary setting for Prince's film *Purple Rain* and has been a cornerstone of the Minneapolis music scene for decades. The club features its iconic star-covered exterior wall and a main room with a capacity of 1,554.
