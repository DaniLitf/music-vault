# Flora Recording & Playback

**Recording studio. Portland, USA.**

Flora Recording & Playback is a recording studio in Northeast Portland, Oregon, operated by Grammy-nominated producer Tucker Martine since 2011. Housed in a former motorcycle club clubhouse, the 2,500-square-foot space features a spacious live room, multiple isolation rooms, and a reverb chamber, and has hosted sessions for the Decemberists, R.E.M., Modest Mouse, Sufjan Stevens, and First Aid Kit.
