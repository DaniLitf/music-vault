# Framework Studio

**Recording studio. Birmingham, England.**

Framework Studio was a recording facility in Birmingham, England, known as the studio where Napalm Death recorded their 1996 album *Diatribes* and where the Napalm Death side project Little Giant Drug recorded their album *Prismcast*.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/N/Napalm Death]] – *Diatribes* (1996)
- [[Artists/L/Little Giant Drug]] – *Prismcast* (2000)
- [[Artists/M/Meathook Seed]] – *Basic Instructions Before Leaving Earth (B.I.B.L.E.)* (1999)
