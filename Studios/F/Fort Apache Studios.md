# Fort Apache Studios

**Recording studio. Boston / Cambridge, Massachusetts, USA.**

Fort Apache Studios is a legendary New England recording studio internationally renowned for alternative rock sessions produced there since 1986. Founded by musician/producer Joe Harvard and engineers Paul Q. Kolderie, Sean Slade, and Jim Fitting, the studio began recording Boston-area indie rock groups in 1986. Originally located in Roxbury, Boston, the studio relocated to Camp Street in Cambridge in 1988 and expanded to Edmunds Street in 1995. Fort Apache relocated to Bellows Falls, Vermont in 2002 under the management of producer Gary Smith. The studio became a home for alternative, indie, and grunge musicians during the 1980s and 1990s.
