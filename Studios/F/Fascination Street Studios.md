# Fascination Street Studios

**Recording studio. Örebro, Sweden.**

Fascination Street Studios is a recording studio in Örebro, Sweden, widely used by Scandinavian metal acts for mixing and mastering. Both studio albums by [[Artists/T/The Halo Effect]] were mixed and mastered there, and it has also been used by [[Artists/E/Ensiferum]].

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/T/The Halo Effect]] – *Days of the Lost* (2022)
- [[Artists/T/The Halo Effect]] – *March of the Unheard* (2025)
