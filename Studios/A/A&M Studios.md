# A&M Studios

**Recording studio. Los Angeles, California.**

A&M Studios is one of the most prestigious and historic recording facilities in Los Angeles, founded in 1964. The studio has hosted countless Grammy-winning artists and remains one of the industry's premier destination studios, known for its multiple recording rooms and professional acoustics.
