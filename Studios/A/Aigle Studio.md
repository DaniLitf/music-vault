# Aigle Studio

**Recording studio. Killiney/Artane, Dublin, Ireland.**

Aigle Studio is a private recording studio built by producer Nicky Ryan for Enya, used to record every one of her studio albums from her 1987 debut through *Dark Sky Island* (2015). It is closely associated with the production partnership of Nicky and Roma Ryan, who have worked with Enya throughout her career.
