# Abyssinian Sons

**Recording studio. CHICAGO, ILLINOIS, USA.**

Abyssinian Sons was a recording studio in Chicago owned and operated by Filter frontman Richard Patrick, used for the band's own recordings during the late 1990s, including the *Title of Record* sessions.
