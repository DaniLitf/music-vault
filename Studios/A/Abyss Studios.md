# Abyss Studios

**Recording studio. Pärlby, Sweden.**

Abyss Studios is the Swedish metal recording studio associated with producer and musician [[Producers/P/Peter Tägtgren]]. It is closely linked to Tägtgren's production work for Scandinavian extreme metal and industrial metal projects, including Lindemann.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/L/Lindemann (band)]] – *Skills in Pills* (2015)
- [[Artists/L/Lindemann (band)]] – *F & M* (2019)
