# The Abyss

**Recording studio. Pärlby, Sweden.**

The Abyss is a premier metal recording studio located in Pärlby, Sweden, founded and operated by Peter Tägtgren. Since its establishment in the early 1990s, the studio has become one of Scandinavia's most respected facilities for recording heavy music, particularly metal. The studio is known for its technical precision and has hosted countless productions for internationally renowned metal bands, making it a cornerstone of the Scandinavian metal production scene.
