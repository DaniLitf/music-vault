# Axis Studios

**Recording studio. ATLANTA, GEORGIA, USA.**

Axis Studios was a recording studio based in Atlanta, Georgia, active during the 1980s. It is best known for hosting early recording sessions for Southern rock band Georgia Satellites, including work later released as the *Keep the Faith* EP and sessions contributing to their 1986 self-titled debut album.
