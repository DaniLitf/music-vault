# Amiga Studio

**Recording studio. BERLIN, GERMANY.**

Amiga Studio was the in-house recording studio of Amiga, the state-owned record label of East Germany, located on Brunnenstraße in Berlin. It recorded much of the GDR's officially sanctioned rock and punk output, including Feeling B's debut album.
