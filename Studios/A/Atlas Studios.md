# Atlas Studios

**Recording studio. Chicago, USA.**

Atlas Studios is a recording studio in Chicago, Illinois, founded in 1996 by producer and engineer [[Producers/M/Matt Allison|Matt Allison]] to serve the city's punk rock scene. It became the home studio for much of [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio|Alkaline Trio]]'s catalog as well as records by The Lawrence Arms, The Menzingers, and Rise Against.
