# Air Studios

**Recording studio. London, England.**

Air Studios is a world-renowned recording facility in London, established in 1965. It is known for its spacious live rooms, extensive vintage equipment collection, and has hosted countless platinum-selling artists and landmark albums in rock, pop, and progressive music.
