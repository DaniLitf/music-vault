# Ardent Studios

**Recording studio. Memphis, USA.**

Ardent Studios is a recording studio in Memphis, Tennessee, founded in 1966, renowned for its warm analog sound and long history recording rock, soul, and punk acts. [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]] recorded much of their second album, *As the Eternal Cowboy* (2003), there as an all-analog session with minimal overdubs.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/A/Against Me!]] – *As the Eternal Cowboy* (2003)
- [[Artists/A/All]] – *Breaking Things* (1993)
- [[Artists/A/All]] – *Pummel* (1995, mixing)
