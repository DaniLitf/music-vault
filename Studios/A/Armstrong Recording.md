# Armstrong Recording

**Recording studio. Tulsa, Oklahoma, USA.**

Armstrong Recording is a studio in Tulsa, Oklahoma, operated by Descendents guitarist and producer Stephen Egerton. Guitar tracking for the Descendents' *Hypercaffium Spazzinate* (2016) was done here.

## Albums Recorded Here
