# Abbey Road Studios

**Recording studio. London, England.**

Abbey Road Studios, opened by EMI in 1931 in St John's Wood, London, is arguably the world's most famous recording studio, known above all for the Beatles' recordings and its landmark Studio Two. Additional recording for *Coverdale–Page* (1993) was done there.
