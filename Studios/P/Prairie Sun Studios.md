# Prairie Sun Studios

**Recording studio. COTATI, CALIFORNIA, USA.**

Prairie Sun Studios is a recording studio located on a former chicken ranch in Cotati, California, founded in the mid-1970s. It has hosted a wide range of rock, metal, and alternative acts over the decades, known for its rustic, ranch-style outbuildings converted into recording rooms.
