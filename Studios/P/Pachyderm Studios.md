# Pachyderm Studios

**Recording studio. Cannon Falls, Minnesota.**

Pachyderm Studios is a recording studio located in Cannon Falls, Minnesota, known for recording intense, focused album sessions in a private setting. The studio became famous as the recording location for several landmark alternative rock albums.
