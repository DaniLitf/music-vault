# Planet of Sound

**Recording studio. Wilmington, Delaware, USA.**

Planet of Sound is a recording studio in Wilmington, Delaware. The Descendents used it in April 2002 for additional recording on *Cool to Be You* (2004), convenient to singer Milo Aukerman's Delaware home.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/D/Descendents]] – *Cool to Be You* (2004, additional recording)
