# Pachyderm Studio

**Recording studio. Cannon Falls, USA.**

Pachyderm Studio is a residential recording studio in Cannon Falls, Minnesota, founded in 1988 by Jim Nickel, Mark Walk, and Eric S. Anderson. Set on a private forested estate outside the Twin Cities, it became internationally known after Nirvana recorded *In Utero* there with Steve Albini in 1993, and has also hosted sessions for PJ Harvey and Soul Asylum.
