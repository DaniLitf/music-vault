# Pulse Recording

**Recording studio. Los Angeles, USA.**

Pulse Recording is a recording studio in the Silver Lake area of Los Angeles, founded in 2004 by producer [[Producers/J/Josh Abraham|Josh Abraham]] on the site of the former Soundcastle Recording Studios. It has been used for rock and alternative productions including work with 30 Seconds to Mars, Staind, and Velvet Revolver.
