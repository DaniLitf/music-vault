# Paisley Park

**Recording studio. CHANHASSEN, MINNESOTA, USA.**

Paisley Park is a recording studio and complex built by Prince in 1987 in Chanhassen, Minnesota. It served as his primary creative hub for the majority of his career, housing his recording facilities, rehearsal spaces, and a soundstage.
