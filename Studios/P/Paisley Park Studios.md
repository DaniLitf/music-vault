# Paisley Park Studios

**Recording studio. Chanhassen, Minnesota, USA.**

Paisley Park Studios is a recording complex built by [[Artists/P/Prince|Prince]] in Chanhassen, Minnesota, which served as his primary creative hub from the late 1980s onward. The facility includes multiple recording studios, rehearsal spaces, and a soundstage.

The studio housed Prince's Paisley Park Records label and hosted recordings by [[Artists/M/Mazarati|Mazarati]] among other Prince-associated acts.
