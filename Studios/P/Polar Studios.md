# Polar Studios

**Recording studio. Stockholm, Sweden.**

Polar Studios is a renowned recording studio in Stockholm, Sweden, historically significant for European music production. The studio has hosted numerous major recording projects and served as a key facility for producing influential records across multiple genres.
