# Paramount Studios

**Recording studio. Hollywood, USA.**

Paramount Studios is a recording studio in Hollywood, California, where [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]] recorded their fourth album *New Wave* (2007) with producer Butch Vig, before the record was mixed at Electric Lady Studios in New York.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/A/Against Me!]] – *New Wave* (2007)
