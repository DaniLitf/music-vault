# Perry Vale Studios

**Recording studio. London, England.**

Perry Vale Studios is cited in sources for [[Artists/T/The Featherz]]'s earliest album-attempt sessions in 2012 with producer Pat Collier. Some of this material was later compiled on *Five-Year-Itch*.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/T/The Featherz]] – *Five-Year-Itch* (2017)
