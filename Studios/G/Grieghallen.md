# Grieghallen

**Recording studio. Bergen, Norway.**

Grieghallen is a concert hall and recording studio in Bergen, Norway, home to a recording facility made famous by engineer Eirik "Pytten" Hundvin during the early 1990s Norwegian black metal scene. Many landmark black metal albums were tracked there, giving it a legendary status in the genre's history.
