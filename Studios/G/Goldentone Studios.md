# Goldentone Studios

**Recording studio. Gainesville, USA.**

Goldentone Studios is a recording studio in Gainesville, Florida, run by producer Rob McGregor. It became a hub for the city's punk scene in the late 1990s and early 2000s, most notably recording [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]]'s debut album *Against Me! Is Reinventing Axl Rose* (2002).

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/A/Against Me!]] – *Against Me! Is Reinventing Axl Rose* (2002)
- [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] – *The Fists of Time* (1998, 1998 session tracks)
- [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] – *As Friends Rust* EP (1999)
- [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] – *Morningleaver / This Is Me Hating You* (2001)
- [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]] – *Culture / Kindred* split (1997)
- [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]] – *Heteronome* (1997)
