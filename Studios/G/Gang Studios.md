# Gang Studios

**Recording studio. Paris, France.**

Gang Studios was a recording studio in Paris used by French electronic and pop artists during the 1990s and 2000s. [[Artists/A/Air]] recorded parts of their debut album *Moon Safari* (1998) there after initial sessions in a home studio and a studio near Saint-Nom-la-Bretèche.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/A/Air]] – *Moon Safari* (1998)
