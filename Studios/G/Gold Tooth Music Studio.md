# Gold Tooth Music Studio

**Recording studio. Beverly Hills, California.**

Gold Tooth Music Studio is a professional recording facility in Beverly Hills, California, established in the 2000s. The studio is known for its modern equipment and has served as a recording location for contemporary rock and metal artists.
