**Recording studio. Huntington Beach, California.**

The Green Room was the personal recording studio of producer Gene Eugene (Eugene Andrusco), located in Huntington Beach, California. It was the home base for much of the Tooth & Nail Records / Christian alternative rock scene in the 1990s, hosting sessions for Starflyer 59, The Prayer Chain, Plankeye, Daniel Amos, and many others. The studio closed following Eugene's death in 2000.
