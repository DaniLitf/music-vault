# Village Recorder

**Recording studio. Los Angeles, California, USA.**

The Village Recorder is a historic recording studio located in West Los Angeles, California. Originally built as a Masonic lodge in 1919, it was converted into a recording studio in 1968. The studio has hosted countless iconic recordings across rock, pop, and metal.
