# Studio 13

**Recording studio. Fort Lauderdale, Florida, USA.**

Studio 13 is a Fort Lauderdale recording studio run by producer-engineer [[Producers/J/Jeremy Staska]], closely associated with the 1990s and 2000s South Florida hardcore and metalcore scene. Bird of Ill Omen recorded their final EP there in November 1998, with Staska credited by the band for delivering "a true Florida-sounding record".

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]] – *Born of You* (1995)
- [[Artists/B/Bird of Ill Omen]] – *When Love Would've Shown Interest in Us Both* (1999)
