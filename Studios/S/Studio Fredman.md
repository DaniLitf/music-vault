# Studio Fredman

**Recording studio. Gothenburg, Sweden.**

Studio Fredman is a recording studio in Gothenburg, Sweden, known for engineering and producing extreme metal records. The studio was used by Driller Killer to record their debut album *Brutalize* in 1993.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/D/Driller Killer (band)]] – *Brutalize* (1994)
