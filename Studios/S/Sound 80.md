# Sound 80

**Recording studio. Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA.**

Sound 80 was a pioneering recording studio founded in 1969 by engineer Tom Jung and composer/musician Herb Pilhofer. It became the world's first digital recording studio through a collaboration with 3M starting in 1975. The studio hosted major artists including Bob Dylan (portions of *Blood on the Tracks*), Prince (demo tapes for *For You*), Cat Stevens (*Izitso*), and Lipps, Inc. ("Funkytown"). The building was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 2020.
