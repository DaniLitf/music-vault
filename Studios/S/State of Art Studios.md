# State of Art Studios

**Recording studio. Bristol, England.**

State of Art Studios is a recording facility in Bristol, England, closely associated with the Bristol trip-hop scene. It served as a primary recording and pre-production space for Portishead's early work.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/P/Portishead]] – *Dummy* (1994)
- [[Artists/P/Portishead]] – *Portishead* (1997; pre-production)
