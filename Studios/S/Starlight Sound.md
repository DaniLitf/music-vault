# Starlight Sound

**Recording studio. Richmond, California, USA.**

Starlight Sound is a recording studio in Richmond, in the San Francisco Bay Area, used by Digital Underground and their circle in the early 1990s. Tupac Shakur's debut album *2Pacalypse Now* was recorded there between March and August 1991.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/T/Tupac Shakur]] – *2Pacalypse Now* (1991)
