# Sunlight Studios

**Recording studio. Stockholm, Sweden.**

Sunlight Studios is a recording studio founded in Stockholm in 1982 by [[Producers/T/Tomas Skogsberg]], later relocated to Gräddö. It became the birthplace of the "buzzsaw" Swedish death metal guitar sound and has also hosted garage rock acts including [[Artists/T/The Hellacopters]].

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/E/Entombed]] – *Left Hand Path* (1990)
- [[Artists/T/The Hellacopters]] – *Supershitty to the Max!* (1996)
