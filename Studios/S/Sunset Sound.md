# Sunset Sound

**Recording studio. HOLLYWOOD, CALIFORNIA, USA.**

Sunset Sound is a historic recording studio in Hollywood, California, opened in 1962 by Walt Disney Studios engineer Walt Heider. It became one of the most iconic studios in rock and pop history.
