# Signature Sound

**Recording studio. San Diego, USA.**

Signature Sound is a recording studio in San Diego, California. It was the primary facility for the six-week sessions beginning December 2001 that produced Box Car Racer's only album, with producer Jerry Finn shipping in equipment ahead of his arrival.
