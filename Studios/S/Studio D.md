# Studio D

**Recording studio. Sausalito, California, USA.**

Studio D is cited in Melvins histories as the studio where [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] recorded *Gluey Porch Treatments* in October 1986. The album became the band's first full-length release.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] – *Gluey Porch Treatments* (1987)
