# Suomenlinna Studios

**Recording studio. Helsinki, Finland.**

Suomenlinna Studios is a recording studio located on the Suomenlinna sea fortress island near Helsinki, Finland, used by a range of Finnish rock and psychedelic acts released through labels such as Svart Records.
