# Studio Barbarosa

**Recording studio. Bavon, Virginia, USA.**

Studio Barbarosa is a recording studio located in Bavon, Virginia, owned by producer Michael "Elvis" Baskette. It was used for the recording of Ratt's seventh studio album *Infestation*.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/R/Ratt]] – *Infestation* (2010)
