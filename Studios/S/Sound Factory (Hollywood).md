# Sound Factory (Hollywood)

**Recording studio. HOLLYWOOD, CALIFORNIA, USA.**

Sound Factory was a recording studio in Hollywood, California, used for a range of alternative rock and experimental music sessions in the 1990s and 2000s, including work by Mike Patton's Fantômas.
