# Strongroom Studios

**Recording studio. London, England.**

Strongroom Studios is a recording studio complex in Shoreditch, London, founded in 1987, which has hosted sessions for a wide range of electronic and rock artists since the late 1980s. The Prodigy used the studio's facilities during the making of their debut album *Experience* (1992).
