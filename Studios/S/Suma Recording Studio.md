# Suma Recording Studio

**Recording studio. Painesville, Ohio, United States.**

Suma Recording Studio is a historic recording facility located on 14 acres in Painesville, Ohio, just 30 minutes east of Cleveland. Founded in 1977 by Ken Hamann after he left Cleveland Recording Company (which dated back to 1934), the studio is known for its extensive collection of vintage analog equipment, custom-built recording consoles, and Neumann cutting lathe. It has hosted countless acts over the decades and continues operating under new ownership since 2018 following a major renovation.
