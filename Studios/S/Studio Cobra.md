# Studio Cobra

**Recording studio. Stockholm, Sweden.**

Studio Cobra is a Stockholm recording studio run by Swedish musician, engineer, and producer [[Members/C/Christian Gabel]]. It is tied to Gabel's production work and the tape-textured solo releases issued under his [[Artists/0-9/1900]] alias.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/0-9/1900]] – *1900* (2009)
- [[Artists/0-9/1900]] – *Tekno* (2016)
- [[Artists/0-9/1900]] – *Fältinspelningar* (2019)
- [[Artists/0-9/1900]] – *KONTRAGARDE* (2024)
