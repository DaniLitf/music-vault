# Seacroft Studios

**Recording studio. Southend, England.**

Seacroft Studios is cited in sources for [[Artists/T/The Featherz]]'s January 2016 Live and Unsigned session. Tracks from this session later appeared on the band's 2017 album *Five-Year-Itch*.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/T/The Featherz]] – *Five-Year-Itch* (2017)
