# Studio Apollo

**Recording studio. Paris, France.**

Studio Apollo was a recording studio in the Saint-Germain-des-Prés area of Paris. [[Artists/A/Air]] spent several months recording their second album *10 000 Hz Legend* (2001) there before finishing the sessions in Los Angeles.
