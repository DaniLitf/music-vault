# Soundstage

**Recording studio. Toronto, Ontario, Canada.**

Soundstage was a recording studio in Toronto, Canada, active in the 1970s. It was used extensively by producer Bob Ezrin, who recorded several Alice Cooper solo albums there including *Welcome to My Nightmare* (1975), *Alice Cooper Goes to Hell* (1976), *Lace and Whiskey* (1977), and *From the Inside* (1978).
