**Recording studio. Kingston, Jamaica.**

Studio One, founded by [[Producers/C/Coxsone Dodd]], is one of the most important recording studios in Jamaican music history, often called "the Jamaican Motown." Located in the Brentford Road area of Kingston, it was the birthplace of ska and the recording home for countless Jamaican artists, including the Wailers' earliest recordings.
