# Silence Studio Koppom

**Recording studio. Koppom, Sweden.**

Silence Studio Koppom is a recording studio in rural Koppom, Sweden, dating to the 1970s. [[Artists/T/The Hellacopters]] recorded *Grande Rock* (1999) there, seeking an engineer who could recreate a vintage 1970s rock sound.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/T/The Hellacopters]] – *Grande Rock* (1999)
