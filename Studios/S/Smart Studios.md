# Smart Studios

**Recording studio. MADISON, WISCONSIN, USA.**

Smart Studios was a recording studio founded in 1983 by Butch Vig and Steve Marker in Madison, Wisconsin. It became a key hub of the alternative rock scene, recording early Smashing Pumpkins and Killdozer material as well as being the home base for Vig and Marker's own band Garbage, before closing in 2010.
