# Surplus Studio

**Recording studio. VAN NUYS, CALIFORNIA, USA.**

Surplus Studio was a recording studio in Van Nuys, California, associated with producer Rhys Fulber, used for Fear Factory's *Mechanize* and *The Industrialist* sessions.
