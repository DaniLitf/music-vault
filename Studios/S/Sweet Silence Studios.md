# Sweet Silence Studios

**Recording studio. Copenhagen, Denmark.**

Sweet Silence Studios was a Copenhagen recording facility founded in 1976 by Freddy Hansson, later run by engineer Flemming Rasmussen. It became internationally known as the studio where [[Artists/M/Metallica]] recorded *Ride the Lightning* (1984), *Master of Puppets* (1986), and *...And Justice for All* (1988), and was demolished in 2009.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/M/Metallica]] – *Ride the Lightning* (1984)
- [[Artists/M/Metallica]] – *Master of Puppets* (1986)
- [[Artists/M/Metallica]] – *...And Justice for All* (1988)
