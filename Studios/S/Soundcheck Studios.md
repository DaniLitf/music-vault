# Soundcheck Studios

**Recording studio. Birmingham, England.**

Soundcheck Studios was a recording facility in Birmingham, England, where Benediction recorded their debut album *Subconscious Terror* in September 1989 and January 1990.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/B/Benediction]] – *Subconscious Terror* (1990)
