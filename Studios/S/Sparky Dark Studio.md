**Recording studio. Calabasas, California, USA.**

Sparky Dark Studio is a professional recording facility located in Calabasas, California. The studio was notably active in the mid-to-late 2000s, hosting major label and independent album productions by established artists. The facility was frequently used by producer Howard Benson for mixing and mastering work with various rock and alternative artists.
