# Sigma Sound Studios

**Recording studio. New York City, USA.**

Sigma Sound Studios was a recording studio in New York City, best known as the home of Philadelphia soul recordings in the 1970s. It was founded in 1968 by Joe Tarsia. In the 1980s, it became a favorite studio for new wave and art rock artists.
