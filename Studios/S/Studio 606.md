# Studio 606

**Recording studio. Northridge, USA.**

Studio 606 is a recording studio in Northridge, in the Los Angeles area, built by [[Members/D/Dave Grohl|Dave Grohl]] as a permanent headquarters for [[Artists/F/Foo Fighters|Foo Fighters]] after relocating his home studio from Virginia. Equipped with the vintage Neve 8028 console from the original Sound City Studios, it favors all-analog tape recording and has hosted sessions for the Foo Fighters and outside artists alike.
