# Silverbirch Studios

**Recording studio. Birmingham, England.**

Silverbirch Studios was a recording facility in Birmingham, England, where Benediction recorded their second album *The Grand Leveller* in May 1991.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/B/Benediction]] – *The Grand Leveller* (1991)
