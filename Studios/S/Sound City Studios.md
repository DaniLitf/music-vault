# Sound City Studios

**Recording studio. Van Nuys, United States.**

Sound City Studios is a Van Nuys, California recording facility famed for Nirvana's *Nevada* sessions and a wide range of classic rock records. [[Artists/M/Metallica]] used it, alongside Shangri-La Studios and their own HQ, while recording *Death Magnetic* (2008) with producer Rick Rubin.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/M/Metallica]] – *Death Magnetic* (2008)
