# Jupiter Sound

**Recording studio. Encinitas, USA.**

Jupiter Sound is a recording studio in Encinitas, California associated with Tom DeLonge, used across multiple [[Artists/A/Angels & Airwaves|Angels & Airwaves]] sessions including the *I-Empire* recordings and, alongside several other San Diego and Los Angeles facilities, the *Lifeforms* album.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/A/Angels & Airwaves]] – *I-Empire* (2007)
- [[Artists/A/Angels & Airwaves]] – *Lifeforms* (2021)
