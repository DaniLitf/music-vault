# Nothing Studios

**Recording studio. New Orleans, USA.**

Nothing Studios was Trent Reznor's private recording facility in New Orleans, Louisiana, built after the recording of *The Downward Spiral* (1994) and named after his record label, Nothing Records. Housed in a converted funeral parlor in the city's Garden District, the studio became the creative home for NIN's most ambitious work — most notably the sprawling double album *The Fragile* (1999), recorded there over nearly two years with co-producer Alan Moulder. *With Teeth* (2005) was partially recorded at Nothing Studios before Reznor relocated to Los Angeles following Hurricane Katrina in 2005.
