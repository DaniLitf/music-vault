# Nightingale Studio

**Recording studio. Nashville, USA.**

Nightingale Studio was a recording studio in Nashville, Tennessee. Punk band [[Artists/A/All]] recorded part of their 1992 album *Percolater* there, alongside sessions at Chapman Recording Studios in Kansas City.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/A/All]] – *Percolater* (1992)
