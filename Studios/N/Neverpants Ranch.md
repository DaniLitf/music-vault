# Neverpants Ranch

**Recording studio. San Diego, USA.**

Neverpants Ranch is Tom DeLonge's home studio in San Diego, California, where he taught himself additional instruments and built the atmospheric, layered sound of [[Artists/A/Angels & Airwaves|Angels & Airwaves]]' debut album. It served as the birthplace of the band, with drum tracking split off to Studio 666 and final mixes handled elsewhere.
