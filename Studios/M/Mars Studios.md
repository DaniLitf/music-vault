# Mars Studios

**Recording studio. Shalersville, Ohio, United States.**

Mars Studios (also known as Mars Recording Compound) was a recording studio located in Shalersville near Cleveland, Ohio, owned and operated by engineer Bill Korecky. Active primarily in the late 1980s through the 1990s and 2000s, the studio was a hub for local Cleveland metal and hardcore bands, offering affordable recording in a rural setting. Korecky engineered and produced numerous albums for regional acts before his passing in 2024.
