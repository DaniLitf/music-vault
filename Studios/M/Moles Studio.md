# Moles Studio

**Recording studio. Bath, England.**

Moles Studio is a recording facility in Bath, England, known for its work with Bristol-area artists. It was used by Portishead for mixing and tracklaying on their second album.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/P/Portishead]] – *Portishead* (1997; mixing, tracklaying)
