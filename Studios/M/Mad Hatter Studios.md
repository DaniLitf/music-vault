# Mad Hatter Studios

**Recording studio. Florida, USA.**

Mad Hatter Studios was a Florida recording studio used by South Florida hardcore bands in the late 1990s. Bird of Ill Omen chose it over Studio 13 for being closer to home and recorded their entire debut there in a five-day, low-budget session in May 1997 with producers Marek Stycos and Moe.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/B/Bird of Ill Omen]] – *Self, Dare You Still Breathe?* (1998)
