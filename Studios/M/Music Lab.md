# Music Lab

**Recording studio. Hollywood, Los Angeles, USA.**

Music Lab was a recording studio in Hollywood, California, used by Los Angeles underground and punk acts in the 1980s. The Descendents recorded *I Don't Want to Grow Up* there in April 1985 with producer/engineer David Tarling.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/D/Descendents]] – *I Don't Want to Grow Up* (1985)
