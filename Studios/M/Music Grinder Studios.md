# Music Grinder Studios

**Recording studio. Hollywood, USA.**

Music Grinder Studios is a recording studio in Hollywood, California, used by a range of rock and alternative acts. Notable for hosting the recording of Eleven's self-titled 1993 album.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/E/Eleven]] – *Eleven* (1993)
- [[Artists/E/Exodus]] – *Impact Is Imminent* (1990)
