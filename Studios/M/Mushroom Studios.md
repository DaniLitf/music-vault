# Mushroom Studios

**Recording studio. VANCOUVER, BRITISH COLUMBIA, CANADA.**

Mushroom Studios is a recording studio in Vancouver, British Columbia, that has hosted numerous rock and metal acts, including Fear Factory's *Obsolete* sessions.
