# Media Art

**Recording studio. Hermosa Beach, USA.**

Media Art was a recording studio in the South Bay area of Los Angeles where [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] recorded their debut EP *Nervous Breakdown* in January 1978, with staff engineer Dave Tarling producing and a young Spot assisting on microphone setup and rough mixes.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] – *Nervous Breakdown* (1979)
