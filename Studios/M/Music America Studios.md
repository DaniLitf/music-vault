# Music America Studios

**Recording studio. Rochester, United States.**

Music America Studios was a Rochester, New York recording facility founded by producer Paul Curcio in the late 1970s. It is best known as the location where [[Artists/M/Metallica]] recorded their debut album *Kill 'Em All* in a rushed two-week session in May 1983, chosen chiefly for its low cost.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/M/Metallica]] – *Kill 'Em All* (1983)
