# Morgan Studios

**Recording studio. London, England, UK.**

Morgan Studios was a major recording facility in London, active from the 1960s through the 1980s. Many notable albums were recorded there, including parts of Alice Cooper's *Billion Dollar Babies* (1973) and work by artists such as the Who, Black Sabbath, and Cat Stevens.
