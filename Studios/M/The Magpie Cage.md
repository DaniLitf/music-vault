# The Magpie Cage

**Recording studio. Baltimore, USA.**

The Magpie Cage is a recording studio in Baltimore, Maryland, operated by producer J. Robbins, known for its work with punk and post-hardcore bands. [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]] recorded their third album, *Searching for a Former Clarity* (2005), there.
