# Million Yen Studios

**Recording studio. Chicago, Illinois, USA.**

Million Yen Studios is a recording studio in the Avondale neighborhood of Chicago, Illinois, owned and operated by engineer Andy Gerber. Housed in a century-old building with three isolation rooms and a large live room, it specializes in tracking guitar-based rock and punk bands using a combination of Pro Tools HD and a vintage British mixing console.
