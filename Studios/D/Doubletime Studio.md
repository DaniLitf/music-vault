# Doubletime Studio

**Recording studio. San Diego, California, USA.**

Doubletime Studio is a San Diego-area recording studio where the young [[Artists/B/Blink-182]] (then Blink) recorded their 1994 demo *Buddha*, financed by Mark Hoppus's record-store manager Patrick Secor. The band regarded *Buddha* as their first legitimate release.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/B/Blink-182]] – *Buddha* (1994)
