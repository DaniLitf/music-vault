# Downtown Studios

**Recording studio. Boston, Massachusetts, USA.**

Downtown Studios (also known as Downtown Recorders) is a recording studio located in Boston's Cyclorama building in the South End. Founded in 1979 by musicians Mitch Benoff and Benny Kay, the studio has been a focal point of the Boston music scene for decades. The studio's design is distinctive in that the control booth is installed at an angle to the main recording booth, and there is no separate drum booth—the drummer performs with the rest of the band. Bands including Pixies, Human Sexual Response, and The Real Kids have recorded seminal works at this venue.
