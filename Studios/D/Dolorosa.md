**Recording studio. Nashville, Tennessee.**

Studio Dolorosa was a recording studio built by Duraluxe co-founders Chris Colbert and Troy Daugherty in Nashville, Tennessee in 1997. The studio was used to record early Duraluxe demos and several tracks on their debut album *Dolorosa* (1999), with additional recording and mixing completed at The Playground in Chicago.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/D/Duraluxe]] – *Dolorosa* (1999, partial)
