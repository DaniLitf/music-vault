**Recording studio. London, England.**

Island Studios, located at 8 Basing Street in London (now Sarm West Studios), was the recording facility of Island Records. Bob Marley and the Wailers recorded *Exodus* (1977) and *Kaya* (1978) here after moving to London following the 1976 assassination attempt. The studio was also used by numerous other Island artists.
