# Xenomorph Studio

**Recording studio.**

Xenomorph Studio is the production facility operated by Sebastian Komor (Xenomorph Productions). Icon of Coil recorded their debut album *Serenity Is the Devil* here.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/I/Icon of Coil]] – *Serenity Is the Devil* (2000)
