# Ultrasuede Studios

**Recording studio. Cincinnati, Ohio, USA.**

Ultrasuede Studios is a recording studio in Cincinnati, Ohio, used by regional garage rock and rock acts. [[Artists/T/The Greenhornes]] recorded their early albums *Gun for You* (1999) and *Dual Mono* (2002) there.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/T/The Greenhornes]] – *Gun for You* (1999)
- [[Artists/T/The Greenhornes]] – *Dual Mono* (2002)
