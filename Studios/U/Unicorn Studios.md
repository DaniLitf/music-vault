# Unicorn Studios

**Recording studio. West Hollywood, USA.**

Unicorn Studios, on Santa Monica Boulevard in West Hollywood, California, is where [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] recorded their landmark debut album *Damaged* (1981). The studio's name was coincidentally shared with Unicorn Records, the band's ill-fated distributor, whose bankruptcy tied up the album's follow-up material in legal limbo for over a year.
