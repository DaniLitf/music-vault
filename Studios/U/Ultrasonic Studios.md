# Ultrasonic Studios

**Recording studio. New Orleans, Louisiana, USA.**

Ultrasonic Studios was founded in 1977 by Jay Gallager and George Hallowell in the Uptown neighborhood of New Orleans, and became one of the city's most prolific recording facilities, hosting sessions for artists including Dr. John, Fats Domino, and Aaron Neville. The studio was destroyed by flooding after the 2005 levee failures during Hurricane Katrina and never reopened.
