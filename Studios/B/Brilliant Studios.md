# Brilliant Studios

**Recording studio. SAN FRANCISCO, CALIFORNIA, USA.**

Brilliant Studios was a recording studio in San Francisco, California, used by several Bay Area rock and metal acts during the 1990s and 2000s, including sessions for Faith No More and Fantômas.
