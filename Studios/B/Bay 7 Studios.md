**Recording studio. Valley Village, Los Angeles, California, USA.**

Bay 7 Studios is a two-room recording and mixing facility located at 12438 Magnolia Blvd in Valley Village, Los Angeles County. The studio opened in Summer 1998 and operated for 14 years under ownership by Dave Rouze and Jeff Sheehan, hosting chart-topping artists and major album productions. After 2012, the studio transitioned to operation under the name Megawatt Recording. Bay 7 has been used for tracking and mixing sessions by major acts including The Beach Boys, Tom Petty and the Heartbreakers, Tool, Queens of the Stone Age, Daughtry, P.O.D., Santana, and others.
