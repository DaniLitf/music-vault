# Big Fish Studios

**Recording studio. Encinitas, California, USA.**

Big Fish Studios is a recording studio in Encinitas, north of San Diego, where [[Artists/B/Blink-182]] recorded their second album *Dude Ranch* with producer Mark Trombino in late 1996. The studio has served the Southern California rock and punk scene since the 1990s.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/B/Blink-182]] – *Dude Ranch* (1997)
