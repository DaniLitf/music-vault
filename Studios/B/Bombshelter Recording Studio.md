# Bombshelter Recording Studio

**Recording studio. Chicago, USA.**

Bombshelter Recording Studio is a Chicago recording and multimedia studio owned and operated by [[Producers/D/Dan Wleklinski]] (Dan Precision) of [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]]. It is a fixture of the Chicago punk scene, where Wleklinski tracked and mixed 88 Fingers Louie's live and studio material, including the band's 2017 comeback album.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]] – *Thank You for Being a Friend* (2017)
- [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]] – *88 Fingers Louie LIVES* (2009, mixed here)
