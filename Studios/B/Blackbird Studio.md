# Blackbird Studio

**Recording studio. Nashville, Tennessee, USA.**

Blackbird Studio is a state-of-the-art recording facility in Nashville, Tennessee, founded in 1997 by producer John McBride and his wife, Martina McBride. The studio has hosted artists across many genres including Neil Young, Kings of Leon, and Nick Jonas.
