# Bearsville Studios

**Recording studio. BEARSVILLE, NEW YORK, USA.**

Bearsville Studios was a recording studio complex in Bearsville, New York, founded by Albert Grossman, that hosted a wide range of rock, metal, and alternative acts from the 1970s through the mid-2000s, including sessions for Faith No More and Fear Factory.
