# Broadoak Studios

**Recording studio. Sussex, England.**

Broadoak Studios is cited in sources for [[Artists/T/The Featherz]]'s 2014 sessions with producer/guitarist Kevin Armstrong. Material from these sessions later appeared on *Five-Year-Itch* and the single "You."

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/T/The Featherz]] – *Five-Year-Itch* (2017)
