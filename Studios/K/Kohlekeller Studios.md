# Kohlekeller Studios

**Recording studio. Germany.**

Kohlekeller Studios is a recording studio in Germany used by German metalcore/electronicore acts. Notable for producing Electric Callboy's (then Eskimo Callboy) 2014 album *We Are the Mess*.
