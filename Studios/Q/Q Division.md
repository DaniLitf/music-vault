# Q Division

**Recording studio. Boston / Cambridge / Somerville, Massachusetts, USA.**

Q Division is a legendary Boston-area recording studio with over 35 years of experience producing high-quality alternative rock, indie, and pop albums. Founded in 1986, the studio has recorded albums by Pixies, James Taylor, Aimee Mann, Morphine, Fountains of Wayne, and many others. The studio originally opened at 443 Albany Street in Boston, relocated to Davis Square in Somerville in 2000, and moved to its current location in Cambridge in 2023. Q Division is most recognized for recording Pixies' debut album *Surfer Rosa* in 1987, a landmark production in alternative rock.
