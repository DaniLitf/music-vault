# Quadra Studios

**Recording studio. Walthamstow, London, England.**

Quadra Studios is a Walthamstow recording studio cited in sources for [[Artists/T/The Featherz]]'s 2016–2017 recording sessions. Material from these sessions was incorporated into *Five-Year-Itch* and associated singles such as "Forget All You Know" and "Ordinary Girl?."

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/T/The Featherz]] – *Five-Year-Itch* (2017)
