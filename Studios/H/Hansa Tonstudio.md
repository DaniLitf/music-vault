# Hansa Tonstudio

**Recording studio. Berlin, Germany.**

Hansa Tonstudio (also known as Hansa Studios or "Hansa by the Wall") is a recording studio in Berlin, Germany, housed in a former concert hall near the Berlin Wall. It became internationally famous when David Bowie recorded *"Heroes"* (1977) there with producer Tony Visconti, and has since hosted sessions by U2, Depeche Mode, and Nick Cave, among many others.
