**Recording studio. Kingston, Jamaica.**

Harry J's Recording Studio, located at 10 Roosevelt Avenue in Kingston, was one of Jamaica's premier recording facilities in the 1970s. Owned by Harry J (Harold Johnson), the studio was used by Bob Marley and the Wailers for their early Island albums *Catch a Fire*, *Burnin'*, and *Natty Dread*, as well as countless other reggae classics.
