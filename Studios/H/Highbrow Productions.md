# Highbrow Productions

**Recording studio. Hook City, Nevada, USA.**

Highbrow Productions is David Coverdale's private home studio in Nevada, near his Lake Tahoe residence. Additional recording for *Coverdale–Page* (1993) was done there, credited in the album's liner notes as "Highbrow Productions, Hook City".

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/C/Coverdale-Page]] – *Coverdale–Page* (1993)
