# HQ

**Recording studio. San Rafael, United States.**

HQ is [[Artists/M/Metallica]]'s own privately-owned rehearsal and recording facility in San Rafael, California, in use since the mid-1990s. The band self-recorded much of *St. Anger* (2003) there and used it for parts of *Hardwired... to Self-Destruct* (2016) and *72 Seasons* (2023).

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/M/Metallica]] – *St. Anger* (2003)
- [[Artists/M/Metallica]] – *Hardwired... to Self-Destruct* (2016)
- [[Artists/M/Metallica]] – *72 Seasons* (2023)
