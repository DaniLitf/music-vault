# Headcase Studio

**Recording studio. Joensuu, Finland.**

Headcase Studio is a recording studio located in Joensuu, Finland. Notable for hosting the recording of Eläkeläiset's 1998 album *Werbung, Baby!*.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/E/Eläkeläiset]] – *Werbung, Baby!* (1998)
