# Third Wave Recording

**Recording studio. Torrance, USA.**

Third Wave Recording was a recording studio in Torrance, California, where engineer Richard Andrews worked with the Descendents family of bands from the mid-1980s onward. It served as the home studio for the punk band [[Artists/A/All]] throughout its early Cruz Records years, hosting sessions for the band's first several releases before it relocated out of California in the early 1990s.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/A/All]] – *Allroy Sez* (1988)
- [[Artists/A/All]] – *Allroy for Prez* (1988)
- [[Artists/A/All]] – *Allroy's Revenge* (1989)
- [[Artists/A/All]] – *Allroy Saves* (1990)
- [[Artists/A/All]] – *New Girl, Old Story* (1991)
