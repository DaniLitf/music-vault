# The Plant Studios

**Recording studio. Sausalito, United States.**

The Plant Studios was a Sausalito, California recording facility used by [[Artists/M/Metallica]] to record *Load* (1996) and *Reload* (1997) with producer Bob Rock, sessions from which both albums were culled.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/M/Metallica]] – *Load* (1996)
- [[Artists/M/Metallica]] – *Reload* (1997)
