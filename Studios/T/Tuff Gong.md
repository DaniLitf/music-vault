**Recording studio. Kingston, Jamaica.**

Tuff Gong is the recording studio and record label founded by [[Members/B/Bob Marley]] in Kingston, Jamaica. Located at 220 Marcus Garvey Drive, it served as the recording home for the Wailers' later albums including *Rastaman Vibration*, *Survival*, *Uprising*, and the posthumous *Confrontation*. It remains a working studio and a museum to Marley's legacy.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/B/Bob Marley]] – *Rastaman Vibration* (1976)
- [[Artists/B/Bob Marley]] – *Survival* (1979)
- [[Artists/B/Bob Marley]] – *Uprising* (1980)
- [[Artists/B/Bob Marley]] – *Confrontation* (1983)
