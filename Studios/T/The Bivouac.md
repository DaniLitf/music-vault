# The Bivouac

**Recording studio. Manahawkin, New Jersey, USA.**

The Bivouac (also known as The Bivouac Audio) is a recording studio in Manahawkin, New Jersey, run by producer and Bigwig guitarist/vocalist [[Producers/T/Tom Petta]]. The studio has been used primarily for mixing and mastering punk and hardcore records.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/E/Explode and Make Up]] – *Explode and Make Up* (2009, mixed and mastered here)
