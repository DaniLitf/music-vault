# The Lair

**Recording studio. Los Angeles, USA.**

The Lair was a recording studio in West Los Angeles, California, that operated for roughly two decades before closing in 2021. It was used by producer [[Producers/C/Cameron Webb|Cameron Webb]] for rock and punk sessions during the 2010s.
