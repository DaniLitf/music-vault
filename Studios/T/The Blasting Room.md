# The Blasting Room

**Recording studio. Fort Collins, USA.**

The Blasting Room is a recording studio in Fort Collins, Colorado, opened in 1994 by the punk band ALL and co-founded by drummer/producer [[Producers/B/Bill Stevenson|Bill Stevenson]] and engineer [[Producers/J/Jason Livermore|Jason Livermore]]. Specializing in punk and melodic hardcore, it has recorded albums for Descendents, Rise Against, NOFX, and Rancid, and has won a Latin Grammy for its production work.
