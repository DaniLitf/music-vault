**Recording studio. New York City, USA.**

The Hit Factory is a renowned recording studio in New York City, established in 1969 by Jerry Ragovoy and later operated by Ed Germano. Known for its world-class facilities, it has hosted artists from Michael Jackson to Madonna to 50 Cent.
