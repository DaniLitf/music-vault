# Total Access Recording

**Recording studio. Redondo Beach, USA.**

Total Access Recording is a studio in Redondo Beach, California, closely associated with the South Bay punk scene of the 1980s. Working with engineer/producer Spot, it hosted sessions for many SST Records acts, including the Descendents' landmark debut *Milo Goes to College* (1982).

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/D/Descendents]] – *Milo Goes to College* (1982)
- [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] – *My War* (1984), *Family Man* (1984), *Slip It In* (1984), *Loose Nut* (1985), *In My Head* (1985), *The Process of Weeding Out* EP (1985)
