# Thin Ice Studios

**Recording studio. Surrey, England, UK.**

Thin Ice Studios is the personal recording facility of guitarist and producer Karl Groom, operating since 1990 (originally in Berkshire before relocating to Surrey in 1995). It is best known for hosting recording sessions for progressive metal band Threshold, of which Groom is a member, as well as several early DragonForce albums.
