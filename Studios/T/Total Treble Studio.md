# Total Treble Studio

**Recording studio. Elkton, USA.**

Total Treble Studio is a recording studio in Elkton, Florida, built by [[Members/L/Laura Jane Grace|Laura Jane Grace]] in 2011 for [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]] projects and named after the band's own record label, Total Treble. It was used to record the band's sixth album, *Transgender Dysphoria Blues* (2014), and has also hosted sessions for other independent punk acts such as Cheap Girls.
