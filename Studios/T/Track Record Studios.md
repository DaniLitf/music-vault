# Track Record Studios

**Recording studio. LOS ANGELES, CALIFORNIA, USA.**

Track Record Studios was a recording studio in Los Angeles used by a range of rock and metal acts in the 1990s and 2000s, including Fear Factory's *Transgression* sessions.
