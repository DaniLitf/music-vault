# Earthbound Studios

**Recording studio. Braintree, England.**

Earthbound Studios is the private home studio of Liam Howlett, built in his hometown of Braintree, Essex, where he has written and produced the entirety of The Prodigy's output since the group's formation in 1990. It is where the band's debut album *Experience* (1992) was recorded, and it has remained Howlett's primary production base throughout the band's career.
