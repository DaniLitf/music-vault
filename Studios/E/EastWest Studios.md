# EastWest Studios

**Recording studio. HOLLYWOOD, CALIFORNIA, USA.**

EastWest Studios (formerly United Western Recorders) is a historic recording studio complex in Hollywood, California, dating back to 1962. It has hosted sessions for a huge range of artists across pop, rock, and metal, including Garbage's *Not Your Kind of People* and Ghost's *Meliora*.
