# Electric Lady Studios

**Recording studio. New York, New York.**

Electric Lady Studios is one of the most iconic and prestigious recording facilities in New York City, established in 1970. Known for its exceptional acoustics, multiple recording rooms, and historic legacy, the studio has hosted legendary artists across all genres of music.
