# Eldorado Recording Studios

**Recording studio. Burbank, USA.**

Eldorado Recording Studios is a recording studio in Burbank, California, where [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]] recorded the bulk of their fifth album, *White Crosses* (2010), with producer Butch Vig.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/A/Against Me!]] – *White Crosses* (2010)
