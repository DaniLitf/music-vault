# Estudios Koolarrow

**Recording studio. OAKLAND, CALIFORNIA, USA.**

Estudios Koolarrow is a recording studio in Oakland, California, associated with the Koolarrow Records label run by Faith No More bassist Billy Gould. It was used for the recording of Faith No More's 2015 reunion album *Sol Invictus*.
