# Enterprise Studios

**Recording studio. Los Angeles, California.**

Enterprise Studios was a professional recording facility in Los Angeles, California, known for its work with major rock and metal acts during the 1980s and 1990s. The studio featured state-of-the-art equipment and recording spaces.
