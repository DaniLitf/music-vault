# Reciprocal Recording

**Recording studio. Seattle, Washington.**

Reciprocal Recording is a legendary recording studio in Seattle, Washington, known for its role in the development of the grunge sound. The studio recorded numerous foundational albums by Pacific Northwest bands in the late 1980s and early 1990s.
