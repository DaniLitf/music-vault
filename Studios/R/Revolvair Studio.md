# Revolvair Studio

**Recording studio. Paris, France.**

Revolvair is the private Paris studio built and used by [[Artists/A/Air]] members [[Members/N/Nicolas Godin]] and [[Members/J/Jean-Benoît Dunckel]] from the early 2000s onward. The duo recorded and produced *Talkie Walkie* (2004), *Pocket Symphony* (2007), *Love 2* (2009), and *Le voyage dans la lune* (2012) there, later replacing it with a larger facility, Atlas Studio.
