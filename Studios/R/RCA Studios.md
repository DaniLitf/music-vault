# RCA Studios

**Recording studio. Chicago, Illinois, USA.**

RCA Studios in Chicago (also known as RCA Victor Studios) was one of the most important recording facilities in the Midwest, operating from the 1950s through the 1970s. Numerous classic recordings were made there across multiple genres.
