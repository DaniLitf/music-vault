# Robert Lang Studios

**Recording studio. Shoreline, USA.**

Robert Lang Studios is a recording studio near Seattle founded in 1974 by Robert Lang, who built it into the excavated basement and garage of his beach house over more than a decade. It is best known as the site of Nirvana's final studio recording, "You Know You're Right" (1994), and as the birthplace of Foo Fighters, recording Dave Grohl's self-titled 1995 debut. Other artists to record there include Alice in Chains, Soundgarden, Heart, Mastodon, and Death Cab for Cutie.
