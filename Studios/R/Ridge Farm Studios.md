# Ridge Farm Studios

**Recording studio. Surrey, England.**

Ridge Farm Studios is a historic recording facility in Surrey, England, known for its work in the 1970s and 1980s on progressive rock and metal recordings. Max Norman worked extensively here as an engineer.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/O/Ozzy Osbourne]] – *Blizzard of Ozz* (1980)
- [[Artists/O/Ozzy Osbourne]] – *Diary of a Madman* (1981)
- [[Artists/O/Ozzy Osbourne]] – *Bark at the Moon* (1983)
- [[Artists/P/Portishead]] – *Portishead* (1997; tracklaying)
