# RCA Studio B

**Recording studio. Nashville, Tennessee, United States.**

RCA Studio B is a historic recording studio on Music Row in Nashville, opened in 1957 and central to the development of the "Nashville sound." It hosted sessions by Elvis Presley, the Everly Brothers, Roy Orbison, and countless other country and pop artists through the 1960s and 1970s, and is now preserved as a museum and functioning studio operated by the Country Music Hall of Fame.
