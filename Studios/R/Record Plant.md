# Record Plant

**Recording studio. SAUSALITO, CALIFORNIA, USA.**

The Record Plant is a renowned recording studio complex originally founded in New York City in 1968, with subsequent locations in Los Angeles and Sausalito. It has hosted countless landmark recordings across multiple genres.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/A/Alice Cooper]] – *School's Out* (1972), *Billion Dollar Babies* (1973), *Muscle of Love* (1973), *Welcome to My Nightmare* (1975)
- [[Artists/V/Vince Neil]] – *Exposed* (1993)
- [[Artists/P/Prince]] – *For You* (1978)
