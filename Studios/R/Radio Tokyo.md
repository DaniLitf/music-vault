# Radio Tokyo

**Recording studio. Venice, California, USA.**

Radio Tokyo was a recording studio in Venice, California, run by engineer Ethan James and prominent in the 1980s LA underground scene. The Descendents recorded both *Enjoy!* (1986) and *All* (1987) there with engineer Richard Andrews and producer Bill Stevenson.
