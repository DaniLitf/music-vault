# Rancho Recordo

**Recording studio. Fenton, Michigan, USA.**

Rancho Recordo is a recording studio and creative space in the woods outside Fenton, Michigan, operated by producer and engineer [[Producers/M/Marc Hudson|Marc Hudson]] in its current form since 2008. Built around a vintage AMEK 2500 console and a large collection of vintage instruments and outboard gear, it has hosted sessions for Against Me!, Saves the Day, Taking Back Sunday, and The Swellers, among others.
