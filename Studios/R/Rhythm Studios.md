# Rhythm Studios

**Recording studio. Bidford-on-Avon, Warwickshire, England.**

Rhythm Studios is a recording facility near Stratford Upon Avon, run by producer and engineer Paul Johnston. It has hosted numerous metal and rock recording sessions since the 1990s.

- [[Artists/B/Benediction]] – *The Grotesque / Ashen Epitaph* (1994)
