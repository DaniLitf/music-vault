# Rockfield Studios

**Recording studio. Rockfield, Wales.**

Rockfield Studios is a residential recording studio near Monmouth, Wales, widely credited as the world's first residential (live-in) recording studio. Founded in 1963 by brothers Charles and Kingsley Ward, it has hosted sessions for a huge range of rock artists over six decades, including Queen's recording of "Bohemian Rhapsody."
