# RAK Studios

**Recording studio. London, England.**

RAK Studios is a legendary recording studio complex with residential facilities located in St. John's Wood near Regent's Park in central London. Founded in 1976 by English record producer Mickie Most, the studio has hosted countless major artists over its decades of operation. The RAK complex resides within a Victorian building that was once a school and church hall before being owned by ATV for television rehearsals. The studio features four recording rooms equipped with state-of-the-art and vintage equipment, including API and Neve mixing consoles. RAK has in-house accommodation for up to six people, catering facilities, and off-street parking.
