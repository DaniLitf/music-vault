# La Fabrique Studios

**Recording studio. Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, France.**

La Fabrique Studios is a recording studio housed in a converted 19th-century mansion in the south of France. It has been used by many international rock acts, including The Dead Daisies for their *Holy Ground* album.
