# La Fabrique

**Recording studio. Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, France.**

La Fabrique is a professional recording studio located in Saint-Rémy-de-Provence, Provence, France. The studio is situated in a rural setting providing a serene environment for extended recording sessions. It has become known for hosting major international recording projects in the modern era.
