# Little Mountain Sound Studios

**Recording studio. Vancouver, Canada.**

Little Mountain Sound Studios was a recording studio in Vancouver, British Columbia, that became one of the most successful rock studios of the 1980s and early 1990s under producers Bruce Fairbairn and Bob Rock. Albums by Bon Jovi, Aerosmith, AC/DC and Mötley Crüe were recorded there. The rhythm tracks for *Coverdale–Page* (1993) were cut at the studio with engineer Mike Fraser.
