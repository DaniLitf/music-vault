# Le Pig

**Recording studio. Beverly Hills, USA.**

Le Pig was a temporary recording studio set up by Trent Reznor at 10050 Cielo Drive in Benedict Canyon, Beverly Hills — a house notorious as the site of the 1969 Tate murders. Reznor rented the property in 1992 and converted it into a private studio he named "Le Pig," where he recorded *The Downward Spiral* (1994) with producer Flood. The location's dark history was a deliberate creative choice; Sharon Tate's former residence provided a psychological backdrop that Reznor felt matched the album's themes of self-destruction. The property was demolished in 1994 shortly after recording concluded.
