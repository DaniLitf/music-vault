# Chapman Recording Studios

**Recording studio. Kansas City, USA.**

Chapman Recording Studios was a recording studio in Kansas City, Missouri. Punk band [[Artists/A/All]] recorded part of their 1992 album *Percolater* there, their first release made after relocating from Los Angeles to rural Brookfield, Missouri.

## Albums Recorded Here
- [[Artists/A/All]] – *Percolater* (1992)
