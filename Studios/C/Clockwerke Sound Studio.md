# Clockwerke Sound Studio

**Recording studio. Cleveland, Ohio.**

Clockwerke Sound Studio was a recording studio located in Cleveland, Ohio. The 1987 album *Cross and Black* by the heavy metal band Centurion was recorded and mixed there in October 1986.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/C/Centurion]] – *Cross and Black* (1987)
