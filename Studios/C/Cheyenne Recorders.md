# Cheyenne Recorders

**Recording studio. Los Angeles, California, USA.**

Cheyenne Recorders is a recording studio located in Los Angeles, California. It was used for the recording of *Lost and Spaced* (1998) by [[Artists/E/ESP]].

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/E/ESP]] – *Lost and Spaced* (1998)
