# Cheshire Sound Studios

**Recording studio. ATLANTA, GEORGIA, USA.**

Cheshire Sound Studios was a recording studio located in Atlanta, Georgia, active in the 1980s. It is known for hosting sessions for the Southern rock band Georgia Satellites, including work on their breakthrough 1986 self-titled debut album.
