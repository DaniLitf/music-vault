# Crehate Studios

**Recording studio. Mölndal, Sweden.**

Crehate Studios is a recording studio in Mölndal, Sweden, owned and operated by producer Oscar Nilsson. Both studio albums by [[Artists/T/The Halo Effect]] were recorded there.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/T/The Halo Effect]] – *Days of the Lost* (2022)
- [[Artists/T/The Halo Effect]] – *March of the Unheard* (2025)
