# Cello Studios

**Recording studio. Hollywood, USA.**

Cello Studios was a recording studio operating from 1999 to 2005 at the historic 6000 Sunset Boulevard complex in Hollywood, California, originally built in the 1960s as Western Recorders and later part of Ocean Way Recording. During its short run under that name it hosted sessions for Blink-182, Rage Against the Machine, Tool, System of a Down, and Weezer, before being renamed EastWest Studios in 2006.
