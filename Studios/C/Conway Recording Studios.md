# Conway Recording Studios

**Recording studio. Los Angeles, USA.**

Conway Recording Studios is a family-run recording complex off Melrose Avenue in Hollywood, California, established in its modern form in 1976 by Buddy and Susan Brundo after Buddy purchased the original 1960s mastering room. Spanning nearly an acre with three recording rooms, it is one of the few remaining independently owned large studios in Hollywood.
