# Congress House Studio

**Recording studio. Austin, Texas, United States.**

The Congress House Studio is a residential recording studio in south Austin run by producer/engineer Mark Hallman since the mid-1980s. It has hosted recordings by artists including Ani DiFranco, Carole King, and Eliza Gilkyson.
