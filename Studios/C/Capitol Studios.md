# Capitol Studios

**Recording studio. Hollywood, California, USA.**

Capitol Studios is a recording studio housed beneath the Capitol Records Building in Hollywood, opened in 1956 and renowned for its underground echo chambers designed by Les Paul. It has hosted sessions by artists ranging from Frank Sinatra and The Beach Boys to Avenged Sevenfold, who recorded their self-titled 2007 album there.
