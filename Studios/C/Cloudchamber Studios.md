# Cloudchamber Studios

**Recording studio. Gothenburg, Sweden.**

Cloudchamber Studios is a recording studio on Hisingen in Gothenburg, Sweden, run by musician and producer Charlie Storm. It has been used for loop, synth, and additional-production work, including on All Ends' second album *A Road to Depression* (2010).
