# Caribou Ranch

**Recording studio. Nederland, Colorado, United States.**

Caribou Ranch was a residential recording studio built by producer James William Guercio on a former ranch in the Rocky Mountains near Nederland, Colorado. Operating from 1972 to 1985, it hosted numerous major acts recording in its remote, high-altitude setting, including Elton John, Chicago, and Earth, Wind & Fire.
