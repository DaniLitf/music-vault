# Can-Am Studios

**Recording studio. Tarzana, California, USA.**

Can-Am Studios in Tarzana, Los Angeles, served as the de facto house studio of Death Row Records in the mid-1990s. Tupac Shakur recorded prolifically there after signing to Death Row in late 1995, including *All Eyez on Me* and the seven-day sessions for *The Don Killuminati: The 7 Day Theory*.

## Albums Recorded Here

- [[Artists/T/Tupac Shakur]] – *All Eyez on Me* (1996)
- [[Artists/T/Tupac Shakur]] – *The Don Killuminati: The 7 Day Theory* (1996)
- [[Artists/A/Avenged Sevenfold]] – *Hail to the King* (2013)
