# Criteria Studios

**Recording studio. Miami, USA.**

Criteria Studios (now The Hit Factory Criteria Miami) was founded by Mack Emerman in 1958 and became one of America's most storied studios, hosting landmark recordings by the Bee Gees, Eric Clapton, Derek and the Dominos and Fleetwood Mac. Vocals and overdubs for *Coverdale–Page* (1993) were recorded there.
