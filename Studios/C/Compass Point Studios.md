# Compass Point Studios

**Recording studio. Nassau, Bahamas.**

Compass Point Studios was a legendary recording studio in Nassau, Bahamas, founded by manager Chris Blackwell of Island Records in 1977. Known for its relaxed atmosphere and state-of-the-art equipment, it became the recording home for numerous iconic albums, especially those in the reggae, rock, and pop genres. The house band, the Compass Point All-Stars (including Sly Dunbar, Robbie Shakespeare, and others), gave many recordings their distinctive sound.
