# Bård Birken

**Vocalist. Norwegian.**

Bård Birken is a Norwegian vocalist best known as the primary and sole vocalist of the industrial metal band Clawfinger, which he co-founded in 1990. He has been the consistent frontman throughout the band's entire career, delivering the aggressive and politically charged vocals that define Clawfinger's sound across their studio albums and live performances. Birken remained active with the band through their initial run (1990–2013) and their subsequent reunion period (2017–present).
