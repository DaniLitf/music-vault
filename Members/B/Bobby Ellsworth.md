# Bobby Ellsworth

**Vocalist. Born 1959-08-08 in New Jersey, USA.**

Bobby "Blitz" Ellsworth is the vocalist and co-founder of Overkill, one of the most enduring figures in East Coast thrash metal. He has been the band's frontman since their formation in 1980 and, alongside bassist D.D. Verni, is one of only two musicians to have appeared on every Overkill album. His aggressive, high-pitched vocal style is instantly recognizable and has remained consistent across four decades of the band's output.
