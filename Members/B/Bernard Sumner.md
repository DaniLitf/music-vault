# Bernard Sumner

**Bernard Sumner (born Bernard Dicken, January 4, 1956, in Salford, England) is a British musician, a founding member of both Joy Division and New Order, where he serves as vocalist, guitarist, and keyboardist.**

Sumner played guitar in Joy Division from the band's formation in 1976. Following Ian Curtis's death in 1980, he became New Order's vocalist and co-songwriter, helping steer the band toward synthesizer-based pop and electronic dance music. He also co-founded the Electronic project with Johnny Marr of The Smiths.
