# Bob Klose

**Guitarist. Born 1945-02-10 in England.**

Bob Klose was the original lead guitarist of Pink Floyd, appearing briefly during the band's early Cambridge days before departing in 1965 to focus on his studies. He left before the band recorded any material, and his place was taken over by Syd Barrett. Klose subsequently became a professional photographer and largely stayed out of music.
