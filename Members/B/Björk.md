# Björk

**Björk Guðmundsdóttir is an Icelandic singer, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist, one of the most celebrated and artistically adventurous recording artists of the late 20th and early 21st centuries.**

She first recorded as a child in 1977 and rose to international attention as the vocalist of The Sugarcubes before launching a solo career in 1993. Her work spans art pop, electronic, orchestral, ambient, and avant-garde music, earning her multiple Grammy nominations and an Academy Award nomination for *Dancer in the Dark* (2000).
