# Brent Diamond

**Keyboards, touring (2022–present).**

Brent Diamond is an English keyboardist best known as the touring keyboardist for [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – keyboards, touring (2022–present)
- [[Members/S/Steve Harris]] – bass technician

## Biography

Diamond replaced Michael Kenney in 2022 as Steve Harris' bass technician and as the band's live keyboard player. He provides keyboard and synthesiser parts essential to Iron Maiden's live sound.
