# Brent Hinds

**American guitarist and vocalist. Born 16 January 1974, died 20 August 2025.**

William Brent Hinds was an American guitarist and co-founder of Mastodon, known for a distinctive lead style that translated banjo and bluegrass techniques onto electric guitar. He died in a motorcycle accident in Atlanta in August 2025.
