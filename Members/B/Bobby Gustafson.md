# Bobby Gustafson

**Guitarist. Born 1964-09-02 in New Jersey, USA.**

Bobby Gustafson was the original lead guitarist of Overkill, co-founding the band and appearing on their first five studio albums from *Feel the Fire* (1985) through *The Years of Decay* (1989). His riff work helped define Overkill's classic thrash sound. He departed in 1990 following creative tensions and was subsequently replaced by a series of guitarists.
