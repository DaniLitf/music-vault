# Brendan Murphy

**Drummer. American.**

Brendan Murphy is a Chicago-based drummer who played in [[Artists/T/The Jesus Lizard]] during the final phase of their original run. He was formerly associated with the Wesley Willis Fiasco before replacing Jim Kimball in 1998.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Jesus Lizard]] – drums (1998–1999)
