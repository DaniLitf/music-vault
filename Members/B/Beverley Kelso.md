**Backing vocalist. Jamaican.**

Beverley Kelso was a Jamaican singer and an early member of the Wailers from 1963, providing backing vocals alongside [[Members/C/Cherry Smith]] on the group's formative Studio One recordings. She left the group in 1965 before their international breakthough.

## Associated Artists
