# Black Francis

**Black Francis (also known as Frank Black) is an American vocalist and guitarist born April 6, 1965, frontman and songwriter of [[Artists/P/Pixies]].**

He contributed vocals to Pigface and has had a prolific solo career under the name Frank Black.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/P/Pigface]] – vocals (guest)
- [[Artists/P/Pixies]] – vocals, guitar (1986–1993, 2004–present)
