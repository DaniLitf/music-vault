# Brian Eno

**Brian Eno is a British musician, composer, and record producer who pioneered ambient music, was a founding member of glam rock group Roxy Music, and became one of the most influential record producers in rock history.**

Eno coined the term "ambient music" with his 1978 album *Ambient 1: Music for Airports* and produced landmark albums for David Bowie (*Heroes*, *Low*, *Lodger*), Talking Heads (*More Songs About Buildings and Food*, *Fear of Music*, *Remain in Light*), and U2 (*The Unforgettable Fire*, *The Joshua Tree*, *Achtung Baby*).
