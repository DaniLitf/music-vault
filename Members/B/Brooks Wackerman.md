# Brooks Wackerman

**Drums. Born 1977-02-15.**

Brooks Wackerman is an American drummer from Long Beach, California, known for his technical versatility across punk, hardcore, and metal. He joined [[Artists/I/Infectious Grooves]] in 1993, playing on *Sarsippius' Ark*, *Groove Family Cyco*, and *Mas Borracho*, and drummed for [[Artists/S/Suicidal Tendencies]] in the late 1990s. He was [[Artists/B/Bad Religion]]'s drummer from 2001 to 2015 and has been the drummer of [[Artists/A/Avenged Sevenfold]] since 2015. He twice filled in for Travis Barker in [[Artists/B/Blink-182]] — at the 1999 Warped Tour and on the band's 2013 Australian tour, when Barker's fear of flying after his 2008 plane crash kept him home.

## Biography

Wackerman joined Infectious Grooves in 1993 and played on *Sarsippius' Ark*, *Groove Family Cyco*, and *Mas Borracho*. He drummed for Bad Religion from 2001 to 2015, appearing on albums from *The Process of Belief* (2002) through *True North* (2013), before replacing Arin Ilejay in Avenged Sevenfold in 2015, debuting on *The Stage* (2016).
