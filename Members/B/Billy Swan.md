# Billy Swan

**American singer, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist. Born 29 May 1942.**

Billy Swan is an American singer, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist best known for his 1975 hit single "I Can Help." He has worked as a session musician and songwriter for various country and rock artists.
