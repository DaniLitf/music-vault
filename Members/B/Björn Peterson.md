# Björn Peterson

**Guitarist. Swedish.**

Björn Peterson is a Swedish guitarist and the founding member of the crust punk band [[Artists/D/Disfear]], which he started in 1989 under the name Anti-Bofors alongside bassist Henke Frykman. He has been the band's primary songwriter and only continuous member throughout its entire history.
