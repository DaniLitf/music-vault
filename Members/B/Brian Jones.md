# Brian Jones

**Brian Jones was a British musician and founding member of The Rolling Stones, who formed the band in 1962 and named it after a Muddy Waters song, though he was eventually ousted by Mick Jagger and Keith Richards in 1969.**

Jones was a multi-instrumentalist who played slide guitar, dulcimer, sitar, and various other instruments on the band's records. He died by drowning in his swimming pool on July 3, 1969, aged 27.
