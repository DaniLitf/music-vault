# Brian Izzi

**Guitarist. American.**

Brian Izzi is an American guitarist who co-founded Trap Them in 2001 with vocalist Ryan McKenney, having played briefly in Backstabbers Incorporated and as a consistent member of December Wolves. He remained Trap Them's guitarist for the band's entire run until their 2017 breakup.
