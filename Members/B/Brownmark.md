# Brownmark

**Bassist and vocalist. Born in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

Mark Brown, known professionally as Brownmark, is an American bassist best known as the bassist for Prince's backing band The Revolution. He replaced André Cymone in 1982 and performed on *Purple Rain*, *Around the World in a Day*, and *Parade*, co-writing the hit "Jungle Love" with Prince.
