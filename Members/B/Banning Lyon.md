# Banning Lyon

**Drummer. American.**

Banning Lyon is a drummer who was one of the founding members of Dallas punk band [[Artists/H/Hagfish|Hagfish]] in 1991, contributing drums to the early formation alongside guitarists Zach and Doni Blair and vocalist James Newhouse. Lyon performed with Hagfish from 1991 to 1992, then briefly rejoined in 1993 before departing permanently when the band's lineup stabilized. His early demo recordings with Hagfish reflected the influence of the Descendents.
