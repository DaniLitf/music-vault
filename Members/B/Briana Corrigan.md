# Briana Corrigan

**Vocals. Born 1965.**

Briana Corrigan is an English singer, best known as a vocalist for [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]] from 1988 to 1994.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]]

## Biography
Corrigan joined The Beautiful South at their formation in 1988, providing female lead vocals on their early hits including "Song for Whoever". She left in 1994 to pursue a solo career.
