# Ben Lovett

**British musician and producer. Born 30 September 1986.**

Ben Lovett is a British musician and producer who met Marcus Mumford at King's College London and co-founded Mumford & Sons in 2007. Beyond the band, he founded the label and promotion company Communion in 2006 (not Island Records, which predates him by decades) and has developed music venues including London's Omeara and the Orion Amphitheater in Huntsville, Alabama.
