# Billy Corgan

**Billy Corgan is an American musician and the founder, lead vocalist, guitarist, and primary songwriter of The Smashing Pumpkins, one of the defining voices of 1990s alternative rock.**

Corgan's ambitious studio perfectionism — playing most instruments himself on *Siamese Dream* — and his confrontational personality made the Pumpkins both celebrated and controversial. After the band's initial dissolution, he formed Zwan and later relaunched the Pumpkins under his control.
