# Busta Jones

**Busta "Cherry" Jones was an American bassist, 1951–1995, known for his work with [[Artists/T/Talking Heads]] and [[Artists/B/Brian Eno]].**

Jones played bass on Talking Heads' groundbreaking *Remain in Light* album and toured with the band in 1980–1981. He also worked extensively with Brian Eno.

## Associated Artists
