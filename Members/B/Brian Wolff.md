# Brian Wolff

**Brian Wolff is an American drummer and co-founder of the New Orleans experimental post-rock trio Drums & Tuba.**

Wolff co-founded Drums & Tuba in the late 1990s, bringing a jazz-influenced rhythmic approach to the band's unusual tuba-guitar-drums lineup. The band released several albums on Accurate Records.
