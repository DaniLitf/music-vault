# Blaze Bayley

**Blaze Bayley is a British heavy metal vocalist who fronted Wolfsbane from 1984, then served as the lead singer of Iron Maiden from 1994 to 1999, replacing Bruce Dickinson on two studio albums before Dickinson's return.**

After leaving Iron Maiden, Bayley has maintained a prolific solo career.
