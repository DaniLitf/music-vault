**Guitarist for Madball. Born 1971.**

Brian "Mitts" Daniels is a guitarist who has been involved with Madball and other New York hardcore projects. He replaced Chad Gilbert in Hazen Street in 2005, providing rhythm guitar and backing vocals on their recordings. Daniels has been part of the New York hardcore scene for multiple decades, contributing to the evolution of the genre through his work with prominent NYHC bands.
