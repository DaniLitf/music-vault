# Buzz Osborne

**Buzz Osborne (born Roger Osborne) is an American guitarist and vocalist, co-founder of Melvins, and a member of Crystal Fairy.**

He is one of the most influential figures in heavy underground rock and was an early supporter and friend of Kurt Cobain.
