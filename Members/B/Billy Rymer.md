# Billy Rymer

**Drums. American. Former drummer of [[Artists/T/The Dillinger Escape Plan]].**

Billy Rymer joined The Dillinger Escape Plan in January 2009, replacing Gil Sharone, and remained the band's drummer through its final lineup and 2017 dissolution.
