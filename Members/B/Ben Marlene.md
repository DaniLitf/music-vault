# Ben Marlene

**Vocalist. Finnish (Swedish-speaking).**

Ben Marlene is a Swedish-speaking Finnish singer who fronted Swedish pop band Trance Dance from their 1985 formation until 1990. He previously sang in the Finnish group French Kiss, which released two albums between 1982 and 1984.
