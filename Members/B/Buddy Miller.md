# Buddy Miller

**Singer, songwriter, guitarist, producer. Born September 6, 1952 in Dayton, Ohio, USA.**

Buddy Miller is an American singer, songwriter, guitarist and record producer who has been a central figure in Americana and country music. He has released several solo albums and produced recordings for Emmylou Harris, Robert Plant and Alison Krauss, and Solomon Burke. He is married to singer-songwriter Julie Miller, with whom he frequently collaborates, and served as a long-time bandleader for Emmylou Harris.
