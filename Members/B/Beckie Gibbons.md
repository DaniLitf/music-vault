# Beckie Gibbons

**Bassist. British.**

Beckie Gibbons, known by the stage name Beki, is a British punk musician who played bass for Chaos UK from 1991 to 1993. She performed on the album *Enough to Make You Sick* and the *Total Chaos* compilation.
