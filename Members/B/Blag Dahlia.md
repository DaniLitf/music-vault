# Blag Dahlia

**Vocalist, producer, author. Born 1966-05-08.**

Blag Dahlia is an American punk singer and author best known as the founding vocalist, songwriter, and producer for [[Artists/T/The Dwarves]]. He has also released solo work under several aliases, including Earl Lee Grace and Ralph Champagne, and has produced albums for punk and garage-rock artists outside the Dwarves.
