# Ben Grosse

**Record producer, mixer, and multi-instrumentalist. American.**

Ben Grosse is an American record producer and mixer known for his work in hard rock and metal with artists including Marilyn Manson, Disturbed, Filter, Sevendust, Breaking Benjamin, and Alter Bridge. He also played keyboards and contributed programming on multiple The Dead Daisies albums, including *Radiance* (2022) and *Holy Ground* (2021).
