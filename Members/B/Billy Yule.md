# Billy Yule

**Drums. Active 1970.**

Billy Yule is an American drummer, best known for filling in with [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]] in 1970.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]]

## Biography
Billy Yule (brother of Doug Yule) played drums with The Velvet Underground during 1970 when Moe Tucker was on maternity leave. He appears on some recordings from that period.
