# Björn Gelotte

**Björn Gelotte is a Swedish guitarist and former drummer, best known as the longest-serving member of [[Artists/I/In Flames]] alongside Anders Fridén.**

Gelotte joined In Flames in 1995 as the drummer for *The Jester Race* (1996), then switched to guitar following Jesper Strömblad's decision to move from drums to guitar. Together with Anders Fridén, he is the only member to appear on every In Flames release from *The Jester Race* onward.
