# Brad Delson

**Guitarist. Born December 1, 1977.**

Brad Delson is an American musician best known as the lead guitarist of Linkin Park. He co-founded the band in 1996 with Mike Shinoda and Rob Bourdon while they were classmates at Agoura High School in California. Delson's guitar work has been featured on all of Linkin Park's studio albums.
