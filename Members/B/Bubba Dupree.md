# Bubba Dupree

**Guitar. Born DATE.**

Jon "Bubba" Dupree is an American guitarist, best known for his work with the hardcore punk band [[Artists/V/Void]] and the supergroup [[Artists/0-9/3rd Secret]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/V/Void]] – guitar (1980–1984, 2007–present)
- [[Artists/H/Hater]] – guitar (1993–1996)
- [[Artists/0-9/3rd Secret]] – guitar (2021–present)

## Biography

Dupree first gained attention as the guitarist for Void, a pioneering hardcore punk band from Washington, D.C., known for their chaotic and experimental approach. He later played in Hater with Matt Cameron and Ben Shepherd from Soundgarden. In 2021 he became a member of the supergroup 3rd Secret alongside Cameron, Krist Novoselic, and Kim Thayil.
