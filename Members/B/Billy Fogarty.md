**Vocalist. Born DATE.**

Billy Fogarty is an American rock vocalist who joined The Scream in 1992 after John Corabi left to join Mötley Crüe. He recorded the unreleased second album *Takin' It to the Next Level* before the band disbanded. Fogarty later formed the short-lived project DC-10 with Bruce Bouillet and Juan Alderete.
