# Brandon Pertzborn

**Drums.**

Brandon Pertzborn is an American drummer who auditioned for [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] in early 2014 after being contacted by Greg Ginn, learning a full setlist before joining as the band's touring drummer. He is married to Emily Vallely, daughter of former Black Flag singer Mike Vallely.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] – drums (2014–2019)
