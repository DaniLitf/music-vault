# Bo Kaspers

**Bo Kaspers (real name Martin Kaspar Eriksson) is a Swedish singer, pianist, and songwriter, the founder and frontman of Bo Kaspers Orkester.**

He founded the ensemble in Gothenburg in 1992 and has led it ever since, establishing himself as one of Sweden's premier jazz-pop songwriters with a string of critically acclaimed Swedish-language albums.
