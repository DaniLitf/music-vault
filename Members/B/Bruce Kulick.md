# Bruce Kulick

**Guitarist. Born December 12, 1953.**

Bruce Kulick is an American hard rock guitarist best known as the lead guitarist for KISS from 1984 to 1996. He was featured on six KISS studio albums including *Asylum* (1985), *Crazy Nights* (1987), and *Carnival of Souls* (1997), and also co-wrote the hit single "Psycho Circus" for KISS's 1998 reunion album. Before KISS, Kulick played with the bands Blackjack and The Good Rats, and has since performed with Grand Funk Railroad, Union, and as a solo artist.

## Biography

Bruce Kulick was born in Brooklyn, New York and learned guitar from his older brother Bob Kulick. He began his professional career in the mid-1970s, playing with the band The Good Rats before joining the hard rock band Blackjack alongside Michael Bolton. In 1984, he was hired as KISS's lead guitarist, becoming the band's longest-serving lead guitarist. After KISS returned to its original lineup in 1996, Kulick co-founded Union with John Corabi. He has also been a member of Grand Funk Railroad's reunion lineup and has released several solo albums.
