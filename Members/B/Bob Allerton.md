# Bob Allerton

**Guitarist. American.**

Bob Allerton is an American guitarist who has been a consistent presence in the Cleveland metal scene since the 1980s. He was a founding member of Abduction before joining Firedance/Rotterdam, and continued through the band's evolution into Torment and Ritual. He has also played in Chronic Rage and Storms of Tomorrow.
