# Bobby Z.

**Drummer. Born 1957 in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

Bobby Z. is an American drummer best known as the founding drummer of Prince's backing band The Revolution. He played on landmark albums including *1999*, *Purple Rain*, *Around the World in a Day*, and *Parade*, and co-wrote several songs with Prince.
