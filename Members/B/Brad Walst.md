# Brad Walst

**Bass. Canadian. Founding member of [[Artists/T/Three Days Grace]].**

Brad Walst co-founded Three Days Grace (as Groundswell) in 1992 and has played bass for the band throughout its history. His younger brother Matt Walst later joined as co-vocalist in 2013.
