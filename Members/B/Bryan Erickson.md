# Bryan Erickson

**American electronic musician. Born 1970.**

Bryan Erickson (also known as BV) is an American electronic musician best known as the vocalist and producer of the EBM/industrial project Bruderschaft. He is also known for his work with the band Velvet Acid Christ.
