# Bill Berry

**Drummer. Born 1958-07-31 in Duluth, Minnesota, USA.**

Bill Berry was the drummer and co-founder of R.E.M., present from their formation in 1980 through 1997. He co-wrote many of the band's songs and his driving drumming was central to their early sound. In 1995 he suffered a near-fatal brain aneurysm on stage; he recovered but chose to retire from performing, leaving R.E.M. in 1997. He has occasionally performed with the band since but has lived a private life in Georgia.
