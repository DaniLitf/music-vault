# Brian Reitzell

**Drummer, composer, music supervisor.**

Brian Reitzell is an American musician and composer, formerly the drummer of Los Angeles punk band [[Artists/R/Redd Kross]], who played drums on [[Artists/A/Air]]'s *The Virgin Suicides* and *10 000 Hz Legend* and toured with the band. He later became a prominent film and TV composer and music supervisor, best known for his frequent collaborations with director Sofia Coppola (including *Lost in Translation*, for which he received a BAFTA nomination) and for scoring the NBC series *Hannibal*.
