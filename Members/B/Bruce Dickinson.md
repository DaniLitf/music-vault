# Bruce Dickinson

**Lead vocals (1981–1993, 1999–present). Born August 7, 1958.**

Bruce Dickinson is an English singer, songwriter, pilot, fencer, and broadcaster best known as the iconic lead vocalist of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – lead vocals (1981–1993, 1999–present)
- [[Artists/S/Samson]] – lead vocals (1979–1981)
- Solo career – 7 studio albums (1990–present)

## Biography

Dickinson joined Iron Maiden in 1981, replacing Paul Di'Anno, and made his debut on *The Number of the Beast* (1982). His powerful operatic vocal style and commanding stage presence helped define the band's classic sound. He left in 1993 for a solo career before rejoining in 1999, ushering in a new era for the band. Outside music, he works as an airline captain and commercial pilot.
