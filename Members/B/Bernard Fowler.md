# Bernard Fowler

**Vocalist, songwriter. American.**

Bernard Fowler is an American vocalist best known for his long-running backing vocal work with The Rolling Stones since the late 1980s. He has also been a member of the experimental funk group Tackhead and released several solo albums blending R&B, soul, and hip hop.
