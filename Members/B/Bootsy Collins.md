# Bootsy Collins

**Bass, vocals, drums. American. Key member of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Bootsy Collins, previously James Brown's bassist, joined Parliament-Funkadelic in the early 1970s and became one of its most influential songwriters, rhythm arrangers, and bassists throughout the decade, his percussive, effects-heavy bass style becoming hugely influential on funk. He later led his own spinoff group, Bootsy's Rubber Band, while continuing to contribute to P-Funk projects.
