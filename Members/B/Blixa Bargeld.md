# Blixa Bargeld

**Blixa Bargeld is a German vocalist, guitarist, and founding member of Einstürzende Neubauten, as well as a long-serving member of Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds from 1983 to 2003, making him one of the most distinctive figures in post-punk and industrial music.**
