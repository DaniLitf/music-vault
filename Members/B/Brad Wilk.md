# Brad Wilk

**Drummer. Born 1968-09-05 in Portland, Oregon, USA.**

Brad Wilk is the drummer of Rage Against the Machine, known for his hard-hitting, groove-based playing that anchors RATM's politically charged sound. After the band's initial split he co-founded Audioslave with Tom Morello, Tim Commerford, and Chris Cornell. He later played with Prophets of Rage.
