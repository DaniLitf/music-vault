# Bob Marley

**Robert Nesta Marley was a Jamaican singer, songwriter, and musician who brought reggae music to global audiences and became one of the most recognisable and celebrated musicians of the 20th century.**

Marley's music blended reggae rhythms with themes of Rastafarian spirituality, social justice, and Pan-African consciousness, producing international hits like "No Woman No Cry," "Redemption Song," and "One Love." He died of acral lentiginous melanoma in 1981 at age 36, but his compilation *Legend* remains the best-selling reggae album in history.
