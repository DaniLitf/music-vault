# Bon Harris

**Synthesizer, programming, drums. Born ?.**

Bon Harris is a British musician and producer best known as the synthesizer player, programmer, and co-founder of the EBM/industrial band Nitzer Ebb. He co-founded the group in 1982 with Douglas McCarthy and David Gooday, and his aggressive synth programming and drum machine work helped define the EBM sound. Harris has also produced and remixed for other artists.
