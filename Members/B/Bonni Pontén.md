# Bonni Pontén

**Guitar, vocals. Swedish.**

Bonni "Bonta" Pontén was a Swedish guitarist and vocalist who was an early member of Anti Cimex, playing guitar and providing vocals during the band's 1981 formation. He also played in the Swedish punk band Asta Kask.
