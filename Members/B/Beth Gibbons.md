# Beth Gibbons

**Vocalist, songwriter. Born 4 January 1965.**

Beth Gibbons is an English singer and lyricist from Exeter, Devon, best known as the voice of [[Artists/P/Portishead]], the pioneering trip-hop group she co-founded in Bristol in 1991. Beyond Portishead she has built a distinctive solo and collaborative body of work, including the 2002 album *Out of Season* with Paul Webb (Rustin Man), a 2019 recording of Górecki's *Symphony No. 3* with the Polish National Radio Symphony Orchestra, and her acclaimed 2024 solo debut *Lives Outgrown*, which was nominated for the Mercury Prize. She has also guested on releases by artists including Kendrick Lamar ("Mother I Sober," 2022) and Gonga.
