# Bill Rieflin

**Drummer, multi-instrumentalist. Born 1960-09-25 in Seattle, Washington, USA. Died 2020-03-24.**

Bill Rieflin was an American drummer and multi-instrumentalist of remarkable range, equally at home in industrial metal, alternative rock, and progressive rock. He co-founded Pigface with Martin Atkins in 1990 and was a longtime member of Ministry and Revolting Cocks. In the 2000s he pivoted dramatically, joining R.E.M. as their touring and studio drummer for their final decade, and later becoming a member of King Crimson and Swans.
