# Bon Scott

**Bon Scott was an Australian-Scottish rock singer who served as AC/DC's lead vocalist from 1974 until his death, whose raw, raucous voice and charismatic personality defined the band's classic early sound.**

Scott died on February 19, 1980, from acute alcohol poisoning, aged 33. He was replaced by Brian Johnson.
