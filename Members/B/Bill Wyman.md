# Bill Wyman

**Bill Wyman is a British bassist and founding member of The Rolling Stones, who played with the band from their formation in 1962 until his retirement from the group in 1992.**

Known for his immovable, motionless stage presence — a stark contrast to Jagger's flamboyance — Wyman was nonetheless a rock-solid musical anchor. He has since led his own band, Bill Wyman's Rhythm Kings.
