# Beate Bartel

**German musician and producer. Born 1960.**

Beate Bartel is a German musician and producer best known as the bassist and electronics player of Liaisons Dangereuses and a founding member of Malaria!. She has been a significant figure in the German electronic music scene since the early 1980s, contributing to projects including Mania D, CHBB, and Matador.
