# Bob Ferguson

**Singer, songwriter, record producer. Born 1927-12-30.**

Bob Ferguson was an American country musician and songwriter who recorded solo sides for Challenge Records and Columbia Records in the 1950s–60s, often backed by his band the Scalawagers. He developed the comedy character "Grandpappy Eli Possumtrot," under which he recorded "Eli's Blue." As a songwriter, he penned two enduring country standards: "Wings of a Dove" (a million-selling hit for Ferlin Husky) and "The Carroll County Accident" (CMA Song of the Year 1969 for Porter Wagoner). He is best known for his work as a senior producer at RCA Victor, where he helped define the Nashville Sound producing scores of records for Dolly Parton, Porter Wagoner, Connie Smith, and others from 1963 to 1978.
