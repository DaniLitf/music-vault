# Brett Tuggle

**Keyboards. Born 30 January 1952, died 19 June 2022.**

Brett Tuggle was an American keyboardist and session musician, a long-time member of the David Lee Roth band (co-writing "Just Like Paradise") and Fleetwood Mac's touring keyboardist from 1997 to 2017. He played keyboards on the December 1993 [[Artists/C/Coverdale-Page]] Japan tour.
