# Brad Fickeisen

**Drummer. American.**

Brad Fickeisen, also known for his work with The Red Chord and No Salvation, became Trap Them's drummer in 2013, recording the band's final two albums *Blissfucker* (2014) and *Crown Feral* (2016) before the band's 2017 breakup.
