# Bob Dylan

**Robert Allen Zimmerman, known as Bob Dylan, is an American singer-songwriter widely regarded as one of the most influential musicians and lyricists of the 20th century, whose work helped transform popular music into a medium for literary and political expression.**

Dylan emerged from the early 1960s folk revival in New York City and ignited controversy when he went electric at the 1965 Newport Folk Festival. He was awarded the Nobel Prize in Literature in 2016 for "having created new poetic expressions within the great American song tradition."
