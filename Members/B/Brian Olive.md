# Brian Olive

**Guitar. American. Former member of [[Artists/T/The Greenhornes]].**

Brian Olive played guitar in The Greenhornes' original five-piece lineup from 1996 to 2000, departing before the band recorded *Dual Mono*. He went on to join the Toledo band [[Artists/S/Soledad Brothers]] under the alias Oliver Henry, and later reunited with former Greenhornes bandmates Craig Fox and Jared McKinney in a new Cincinnati-area project.
