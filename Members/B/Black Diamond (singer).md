**Vocalist.**

Black Diamond is a vocalist who performed on the [[Artists/T/Technotronic|Technotronic]] single "I Want You by My Side" (1996). She was brought in as a guest vocalist during the *Recall* album era, providing the lead vocal performance on one of the album's standalone singles.
