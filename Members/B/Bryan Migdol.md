# Bryan Migdol

**Drums.**

Bryan Migdol was the first drummer of [[Artists/B/Black Flag]], recruited by Keith Morris in early 1977 to play in the band's precursor, Panic, and helping shape the arrangements of several songs later recorded by Black Flag. He played all drum tracks on the band's debut EP *Nervous Breakdown* (1979), recorded in January 1978, before leaving in the summer of 1978 disillusioned with the band's heavy rehearsal schedule and lack of live shows.
