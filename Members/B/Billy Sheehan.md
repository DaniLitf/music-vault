# Billy Sheehan

**Bassist. Born March 19, 1953.**

Billy Sheehan is an American bassist known for his technical proficiency and two-handed tapping technique. He gained fame with Talas (1973–1985) and David Lee Roth's solo band (1985–1988) before co-founding the hard rock supergroup Mr. Big (1988–2002, 2009–present). He has also released solo albums and played in Niacin and Sons of Apollo.
