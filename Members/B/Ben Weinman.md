# Ben Weinman

**Guitar. American. Founding member of [[Artists/T/The Dillinger Escape Plan]].**

Ben Weinman co-founded The Dillinger Escape Plan in 1997 out of the earlier hardcore band Arcane, and was the band's only member to remain through its entire history until its 2017 dissolution. He founded the independent label Party Smasher Inc. to release the band's later albums, and joined [[Artists/S/Suicidal Tendencies]] as rhythm guitarist in 2018.
