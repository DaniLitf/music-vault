# Brett Gurewitz

**Guitarist. Born 1962-04-10.**

Brett Gurewitz is a founding member of Bad Religion and primary guitarist (1980–1994, 2001–present). He left the band in 1994 to focus on Epitaph Records, which he founded and built into a major independent punk label. Gurewitz rejoined Bad Religion in 2001 and has remained a core member since. His guitar work is fundamental to the band's melodic hardcore sound.
