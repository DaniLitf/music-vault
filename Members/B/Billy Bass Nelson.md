# Billy Bass Nelson

**Bass, guitar. American. Founding member of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Billy Bass Nelson was a teenage employee at George Clinton's New Jersey barbershop and became the first musician hired to back the Parliaments, the touring band that evolved into Funkadelic — a name he coined himself. He brought his friend Eddie Hazel into the band, quit in 1971, but contributed sporadically for years afterward and toured with the P-Funk All Stars from 1994. He died in January 2026.
