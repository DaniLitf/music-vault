# Brian Benoit

**Guitar. American. Former member of [[Artists/T/The Dillinger Escape Plan]].**

Brian Benoit, formerly of Jesuit, joined The Dillinger Escape Plan in late 1998, playing on *Calculating Infinity* (1999) and *Miss Machine* (2004). He suffered nerve damage (brachial plexus neuritis) in his left hand while touring in late 2004 and, aside from a brief 2005 return, did not play with the band again, though he rejoined them onstage for parts of their final 2017 shows.
