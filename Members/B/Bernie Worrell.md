# Bernie Worrell

**Bernie Worrell was an American keyboardist and composer born April 19, 1944, died June 24, 2016, best known as a founding member of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]] and for his work with [[Artists/T/Talking Heads]].**

Worrell was a classically trained keyboardist who helped define the P-Funk sound with his innovative synthesizer work. He toured and recorded with Talking Heads from 1980, appearing on *Remain in Light* and subsequent albums. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame as a member of Parliament-Funkadelic in 1997.
