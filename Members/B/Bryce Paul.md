# Bryce Paul

**Bryce Paul is an American musician best known as the former bassist of [[Artists/I/In Flames]].**

Paul joined In Flames in 2017, appearing on *I, the Mask* (2019) and *Foregone* (2023). He left the band in June 2023 on good terms.
