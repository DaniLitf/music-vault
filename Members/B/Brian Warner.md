# Brian Warner

**Vocalist, songwriter. Born 5 January 1969.**

Brian Hugh Warner, known by the stage name Marilyn Manson (combining Marilyn Monroe and Charles Manson), is an American musician who co-founded the band Marilyn Manson in Fort Lauderdale, Florida in 1989 with guitarist Scott Putesky. As the band's vocalist and only constant member, he has fronted albums including *Antichrist Superstar* (1996) and *Mechanical Animals* (1998), building a career defined by provocative imagery and controversy.
