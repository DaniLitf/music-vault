# Brad Parris

**American bassist.**

Brad Parris took guitar lessons from Nile founder Karl Sanders before joining the band as live bassist in 2015, appearing on *Vile Nilotic Rites* (2019) and as guest vocalist on *What Should Not Be Unearthed*. He left Nile amicably in 2022 to focus on other projects and family, and hosts the "Low End Warriors" bass podcast.
