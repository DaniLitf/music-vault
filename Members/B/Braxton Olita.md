# Braxton Olita

**Keyboards (touring).**

Braxton Olita is an American touring keyboardist who joined [[Artists/0-9/30 Seconds to Mars|30 Seconds to Mars]]'s live lineup in 2009. He has performed on Axiom keyboard rigs during the *Into the Wild* tour and subsequent tours in support of *This Is War* and later albums, remaining the band's touring keyboardist into the band's most recent tours.
