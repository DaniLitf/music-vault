**Drummer and percussionist. Born 17 May 1949.**

Bill Bruford is a British drummer best known for his foundational work with the progressive rock bands Yes (1968–1972) and King Crimson (1972 onwards), as well as numerous other progressive rock projects. A pioneering figure in progressive rock drumming, Bruford co-founded Yes in 1968 and recorded five influential albums with them including The Yes Album (1971), Fragile (1971), and Close to the Edge (1972). In 1972, he left Yes to join King Crimson and has remained a key member of various Crimson lineups throughout his career. Bruford also briefly served as Genesis's touring drummer in 1976, filling in temporarily for the Trick of the Tail Tour before the band settled on Chester Thompson.
