# Ben Shepherd

**Bassist. Born Ojai, California.**

Ben Shepherd (stage name of Brent Michael Tuttle) is an American rock bassist who joined Soundgarden in 1990, replacing Jason Everman and Hiro Yamamoto. He became the band's primary bassist through their commercial peak in the mid-1990s and was a core member during both the original lineup's dissolution and the band's 2010 reunion until 2017. Shepherd's bass work contributed significantly to Soundgarden's heavy, psychedelic sound.
