# Bruce "Denial Fiend" Day

**Drummer. Swiss.**

Jörg Neubart, known as Bruce "Denial Fiend" Day, was a Swiss drummer who replaced original Hellhammer drummer Pete Stratton in 1982, previously having played in the band Moorhead. He remained with Hellhammer through the *Apocalyptic Raids* EP era until the band's split in 1984.
