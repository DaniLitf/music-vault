# Bruno Kramm

**German musician, producer, label founder, co-founder of Das Ich. Born in Munich.**

Bruno Kramm co-founded Das Ich in 1989 with Stefan Ackermann and also founded the Danse Macabre record label the same year. He handles all music composition, programming, and production for Das Ich, as well as being politically active with the Pirate Party Germany and Alliance 90/The Greens. He released a solo spin-off project titled *Coeur* in 2000. Kramm has also produced and mixed numerous albums on Danse Macabre.
