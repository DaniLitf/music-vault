# Boris Gardiner

**Bassist, singer, and songwriter. Born 1943-01-13.**

Boris Gardiner is a Jamaican bassist, singer, and songwriter who became a prolific session player in the late 1960s and 70s, recording with The Upsetters, The Aggrovators, and countless other Jamaican acts. He also had a notable solo career, scoring a UK Top 20 hit with the instrumental "Elizabethan Reggae" (1970) and a UK number-one single with "I Wanna Wake Up with You" (1986).
