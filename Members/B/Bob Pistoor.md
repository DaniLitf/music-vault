# Bob Pistoor

**Guitarist. Dutch.**

Bob Pistoor was a guitarist in [[Artists/T/The Legendary Pink Dots]], joining for the band's 1988 US tour period. He later died of cancer and was succeeded in the group by Martijn de Kleer.
