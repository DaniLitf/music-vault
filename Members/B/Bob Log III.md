# Bob Log III

**Bob Log III is an American one-man band musician from Phoenix, Arizona, who performs solo blues and garage rock while playing guitar and kick drum simultaneously, wearing a full flight suit and a helmet with a telephone mouthpiece sealed inside.**

His deliberately absurd stage persona and ferocious slide guitar playing make his live performances legendary on the international festival circuit. He formerly performed as half of the duo Doo Rag before going solo in 1996.
