# Barry Gray

**Guitarist. British.**

Barry Gray, also known as Stret Majest Alarme, was a guitarist in [[Artists/T/The Legendary Pink Dots]] during much of the 1980s. The band's official listening guide also points to his later solo work under the Stret Majest Alarme name.
