# Bill Stevenson

**Drummer, songwriter. American.**

Bill Stevenson is a drummer and songwriter, the founding and only constant member of California punk band [[Artists/D/Descendents]] since 1978. He also drummed for [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] from late 1981 until his dismissal in 1985, appearing on five of their studio albums including *My War*. He also produces records, co-owning The Blasting Room studio, where he produced [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio]]'s *My Shame Is True* (2013) — see [[Producers/B/Bill Stevenson]] for his production work.
