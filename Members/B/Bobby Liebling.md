# Bobby Liebling

**Vocalist. Born 1953-11-09 in Washington D.C., USA.**

Bobby Liebling is the co-founder and sole continuous member of Pentagram, the cult doom metal band he established in the early 1970s. His haunted, bluesy vocal delivery is central to Pentagram's sound, and his turbulent personal life — documented in the 2011 film *Last Days Here* — has become almost inseparable from the band's mythology. Despite decades of lineup instability and personal struggles, Liebling has kept Pentagram active across more than forty years.
