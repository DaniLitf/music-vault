# Bernard "Touter" Harvey

**Keyboardist, vocalist. Born 1955-10-25.**

Bernard "Touter" Harvey is a Jamaican keyboardist who joined reggae band Inner Circle in 1973, becoming one of its longest-serving members. He has remained with the band through its Jacob Miller era, its 1980s Miami relocation, and its 1990s international hits "Bad Boys" and "Sweat (A La La La La Long)".
