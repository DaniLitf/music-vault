# Budgie

**Drummer. British.**

Budgie is a British drummer who played with [[Artists/S/Siouxsie and the Banshees]], the influential post-punk and gothic rock band, and was the other half of the duo [[Artists/T/The Creatures]] alongside Siouxsie Sioux.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/S/Siouxsie and the Banshees]] – drums
- [[Artists/T/The Creatures]] – drums, percussion (1981–2004)
