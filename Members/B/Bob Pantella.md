# Bob Pantella

**American drummer.**

Robert "Bob" Pantella is an American drummer who played in Raging Slab and Love Among Freaks before joining Monster Magnet in December 2003, replacing Michael Wildwood. He is also the longtime drummer of the Atomic Bitchwax and has played with The Obsessed and Riotgod.
