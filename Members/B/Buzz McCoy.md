# Buzz McCoy

**Producer and co-founder of My Life With the Thrill Kill Kult. American.**

Marston Daley, known professionally as Buzz McCoy, is an American producer and the co-founder of the industrial band My Life With the Thrill Kill Kult. In 1987, he met Frankie Nardiello (Groovie Mann) while touring with Ministry, and together they formed TKK, which became one of the pioneering acts in industrial music throughout the late 1980s and 1990s. McCoy's production expertise and programming established the band's distinctive electronic and industrial sound.
