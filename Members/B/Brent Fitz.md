# Brent Fitz

**Drummer. Born March 27, 1970.**

Brent Fitz is a Canadian drummer and multi-instrumentalist best known as the drummer for Slash featuring Myles Kennedy and the Conspirators, and for his work with the hard rock band Union. Born in Winnipeg, Manitoba, Fitz has been a first-call session and touring drummer for over three decades, playing with artists including Alice Cooper, Vince Neil, Theory of a Deadman, and Slash. He moved to Los Angeles in the mid-1990s, where he was recruited to join Union alongside Bruce Kulick and John Corabi.
