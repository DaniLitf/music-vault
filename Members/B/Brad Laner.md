# Brad Laner

**Guitarist, vocalist, songwriter. Born 6 November 1966.**

Brad Laner is an American musician from Los Angeles, best known as the founder and creative force behind the noise-pop/shoegaze band [[Artists/M/Medicine]]. A prolific experimental musician, he has also recorded solo and as a session player, and contributed guitar to [[Artists/M/M83]]'s *Hurry Up, We're Dreaming* (2011).

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/M/Medicine]] – vocals, guitar (1990–1995, 2013–present)
- [[Artists/M/M83]] – guest guitar on *Hurry Up, We're Dreaming* (2011)
