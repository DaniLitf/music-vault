# Brian Tichy

**Drummer. American.**

Brian Tichy is an American drummer known for his work with Ozzy Osbourne, Whitesnake, and Foreigner. He played drums for The Dead Daisies across three stints — in 2013, from 2014 to 2017, and from 2022 to 2024 — making him one of the band's most frequent collaborators behind the kit.
