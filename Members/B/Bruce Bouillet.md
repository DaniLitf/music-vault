**Guitarist. Born DATE.**

Bruce Bouillet is an American guitarist best known as a member of the hard rock band Racer X, alongside Paul Gilbert. After Racer X disbanded, he co-founded The Scream with bassist Juan Alderete and former Racer X bandmate Scott Travis, contributing to the 1991 album *Let It Scream*. Following The Scream's breakup, Bouillet formed the short-lived project DC-10 with Billy Fogarty and Juan Alderete.
