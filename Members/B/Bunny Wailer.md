# Bunny Wailer

**Bunny Wailer (born Neville Livingston) was a Jamaican musician and original co-founder of The Wailers alongside Bob Marley and Peter Tosh, and the last surviving founding member of the group.**

He left the touring Wailers in 1973 but continued as a solo artist for decades, releasing roots reggae albums under both his own name and the alias Jah B. He died in 2021.
