# Bill Ward

**Drummer. British.**

Bill Ward was a British heavy metal drummer born in 1948 and died in 2022, best known as the original and classic drummer of [[Artists/B/Black Sabbath]], helping define the sound of heavy metal.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/B/Black Sabbath]] – drums (1968–1979, 1991–2022)
