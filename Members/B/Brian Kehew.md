# Brian Kehew

**Keyboardist, engineer, music historian.**

Brian Kehew is an American keyboardist, recording engineer, and music historian who co-founded the vintage-synthesizer duo Moog Cookbook with [[Members/R/Roger Manning Jr.]]. He toured with [[Artists/A/Air]] as a keyboardist during the *Moon Safari* era and is noted as an authority on classic studio equipment and Beatles recording history.
