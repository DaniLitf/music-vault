# Brian May

**Guitarist, vocalist. Born 1947-07-19 in Hampton, Middlesex, England.**

Brian May is the lead guitarist and co-founder of Queen, one of the most recognizable guitar voices in rock. He built his own guitar — the Red Special — as a teenager with his father, and its distinctive tone runs through Queen's entire catalog. He studied astrophysics and completed his PhD in 2007, decades after putting it on hold to pursue music. After Freddie Mercury's death, he continued Queen alongside Roger Taylor, eventually partnering with vocalist Adam Lambert for extensive touring. He has also released several solo albums.
