# Stella

**Singer. Dutch.**

Stella is a Dutch singer who was the lead vocalist of Twenty 4 Seven from 1996 to 1997 after Nancy Coolen left the group. She performed on the album *Twenty 4 Hours A Day, Seven Days A Week* and the single "Friday Night".
