# Sami Yaffa

**Bass. Finnish. Born 1963-09-04.**

Sami Yaffa is a Finnish bassist, an original member of [[Artists/H/Hanoi Rocks]] who went on to play with [[Artists/N/New York Dolls]] (2004–2010) and [[Artists/M/Michael Monroe]]'s band. He toured with [[Artists/T/The Hellacopters]] as bassist in 2017, replacing founding member Kenny Håkansson, before Dolf de Borst took over the role.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/H/Hanoi Rocks]] – bass, founding member
- [[Artists/N/New York Dolls]] – bass (2004–2010)
- [[Artists/M/Michael Monroe]] – bass
- [[Artists/T/The Hellacopters]] – touring bass (2017)
