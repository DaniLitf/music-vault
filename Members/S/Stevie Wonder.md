# Stevie Wonder

**Singer, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist. Born 13 May 1950 in Saginaw, Michigan, USA.**

Stevland Morris, known as Stevie Wonder, is an American musician who signed with Motown Records at age 11 and became one of the most acclaimed artists in popular music. Blind since shortly after birth, he has mastered multiple instruments and written and produced music across soul, R&B, funk, and pop, winning 25 Grammy Awards.
