# Steve Vai

**Guitarist and songwriter. Born 6 June 1960 in Carle Place, New York, USA.**

Steve Vai is an American guitarist widely regarded as one of the greatest rock guitarists of all time. He began his career as a transcriptionist and guitarist for Frank Zappa (1980–1983), then played with Alcatrazz (1984) and David Lee Roth (1985–1988) before joining Whitesnake (1989–1990) and launching a successful solo career.
