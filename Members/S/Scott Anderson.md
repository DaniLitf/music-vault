# Scott Anderson

**Lead vocals. Canadian. Founding member of [[Artists/F/Finger Eleven]].**

Scott Anderson has fronted Finger Eleven (originally Rainbow Butt Monkeys) since its 1990 formation in Burlington, Ontario, singing lead on hits including "One Thing" and "Paralyzer."
