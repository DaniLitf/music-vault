# Shannon Leto

**Drums, percussion. Born March 9, 1970.**

Shannon Leto is an American drummer from Bossier City, Louisiana who co-founded [[Artists/0-9/30 Seconds to Mars|30 Seconds to Mars]] with his younger brother [[Members/J/Jared Leto|Jared Leto]] in 1998. The only other constant member of the band alongside Jared, he has remained its drummer across all six studio albums and, on the band's sixth album *It's the End of the World but It's a Beautiful Day* (2023), stepped into a co-production role alongside his brother.
