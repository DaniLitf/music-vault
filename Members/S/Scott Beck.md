# Scott Beck

**Vocalist (harsh and rap vocals). American.**

Scott Beck, known professionally as Strike, is an American vocalist who joined Mushroomhead in 2022 as an additional vocalist alongside Steve Rauckhorst. He provides harsh and rap vocals and contributes to the band's dual-vocal arrangements on their recent albums *Call the Devil* (2024) and beyond, part of a restructured lineup that also brought back guitarist Dave Felton and changed the rhythm section.
