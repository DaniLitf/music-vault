# Simon Gallup

**Simon Gallup is a British bassist who has been the primary bass player in The Cure since 1979, with only a brief departure in 1982–1984.**

Gallup is the second longest-serving member of The Cure after Robert Smith and is widely considered a key part of the band's distinctive sound.
