# Sylvain Bouvier

**Drums, percussion (2016–2024).**

Sylvain Bouvier is a French drummer best known as the live and recording drummer for the experimental project [[Artists/I/Igorrr]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Igorrr]] – drums, percussion (2016–2024)

## Biography

Bouvier joined Igorrr in 2016 as the project expanded from a solo studio project into a full live band. He played on *Savage Sinusoid* (2017) and *Spirituality and Distortion* (2020), and toured extensively with the band, including a North American tour supporting Ministry in 2018. He parted ways with Igorrr in 2024.
