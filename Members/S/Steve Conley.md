# Steve Conley

**Guitar. American.**

Steve Conley is an American guitarist who has been a member of [[Artists/F/Flotsam and Jetsam]] since 2013, recording the band's recent albums including *The End of Chaos* (2019) and *I Am the Weapon* (2024).
