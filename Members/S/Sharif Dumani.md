# Sharif Dumani

**Singer, songwriter, multi-instrumentalist. American.**

Sharif Dumani is an American singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist from Los Angeles, best known as the frontman and primary songwriter of Exploding Flowers. He started in punk bands in the late 1990s and has also played guitar for Alice Bag, Sex Stains, and Classics of Love, and has worked with Cody ChesnuTT, Nikki Sudden, Silver Apples, and The Tyde. He released his debut solo album *In Search of Memory* in 2024.
