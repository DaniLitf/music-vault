# Simon Dawson

**Drums, touring (2024–present).**

Simon Dawson is an English drummer best known as the touring drummer for [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] and a member of [[Artists/B/British Lion]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – drums, touring (2024–present)
- [[Artists/B/British Lion]] – drums (2012–present)

## Biography

Dawson has been Steve Harris's rhythm section partner in British Lion since 2012. In December 2024, following Nicko McBrain's retirement from touring, Dawson was announced as Iron Maiden's new touring drummer for the Run For Your Lives Tour (2025–2026).
