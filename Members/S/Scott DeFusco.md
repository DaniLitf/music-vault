# Scott DeFusco

**Drummer. American.**

Scott DeFusco was an early drummer for grindcore band Trap Them, playing alongside bassist Nat Coghlan from 2006 to 2007 during the period surrounding the band's debut album *Sleepwell Deconstructor*.
