# Shane Post

**Vocalist, guitarist. Avon Lake, Ohio.**

William Shane Post is an American vocalist originally from Avon Lake, Ohio, where he played guitar in the hardcore band Choice before moving to Florida in late 1996. He became [[Artists/B/Bird of Ill Omen]]'s second vocalist in March 1997, singing on both of the band's releases and at its 2012 reunion show; his move back to Ohio in 1998 was a key factor in the band's dissolution.

## Associated Artists
