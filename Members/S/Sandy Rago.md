**Vocalist. American. R.I.P. 2022.**

Sandy Rago (née Kruger) was an American vocalist best known as the frontwoman of the Cleveland heavy metal band Sacred Few. She joined the band in 1983, replacing original vocalist Ray Garsteck, and sang on their lone album *Beyond the Iron Walls* (1985). Her distinctive mid-range vocal style set Sacred Few apart from other female-fronted metal acts of the era.
