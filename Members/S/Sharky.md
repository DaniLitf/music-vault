# Sharky

**Dancer, vocalist. British.**

Sharky (real name Sheila Burke) was a founding member of The Prodigy, recruited as a dancer and vocalist by Keith Flint when the group formed in October 1990. She left the band shortly after they signed to XL Recordings in 1991, reducing the group to a four-piece.
