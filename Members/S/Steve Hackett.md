**Guitarist. Born 12 February 1950.**

Steve Hackett is an English guitarist best known as the lead guitarist of Genesis from 1971 to 1977, where he contributed to six studio albums, three live albums, and seven singles. A virtuoso musician, Hackett's early explorations of two-handed tapping and sweep picking techniques were far ahead of their time and influenced future guitarists including Eddie Van Halen and Brian May. Following his departure from Genesis to pursue a solo career, he released his first solo album "Voyage of the Acolyte" in 1975 while still with the band. His subsequent solo work has explored diverse genres including pop, blues, world music, and classical music. Hackett was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame as a member of Genesis in 2010.
