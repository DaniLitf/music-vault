# Signe Toly Anderson

**Vocals. American. Born 1941-09-02. Died 2016-01-28.**

Signe Toly Anderson was the original female vocalist of [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]], appearing on their debut album *Jefferson Airplane Takes Off* (1966). She left the band after the birth of her daughter and was replaced by [[Members/G/Grace Slick]]. She died on the same day as fellow Airplane member [[Members/P/Paul Kantner]] on January 28, 2016.

## Associated Artists
