# Scott Raynor

**Drums. Born 1978-05-23.**

Scott Raynor is an American drummer and founding member of [[Artists/B/Blink-182]], playing on the band's early demos and their first two albums, *Cheshire Cat* (1995) and *Dude Ranch* (1997). His fast, double-time drumming defined the band's early skate-punk sound. He was dismissed from the band in mid-1998 amid personal issues and heavy drinking, and was replaced by Travis Barker; he later played in smaller bands and worked with the youth charity StandUp for Kids.
