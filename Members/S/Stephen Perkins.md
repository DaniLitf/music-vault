# Stephen Perkins

**Drums, percussion. American. Born 1967-09-13.**

Stephen Perkins is the drummer and percussionist of [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]], one of the band's founding members. His polyrhythmic, world-music-influenced drumming is central to Jane's Addiction's sound. He has also been a core member of [[Artists/P/Porno for Pyros]] and [[Artists/I/Infectious Grooves]] throughout their various active periods.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]] – drums, percussion (1986–1991, 1997, 2001–2004, 2008–2024)
- [[Artists/P/Porno for Pyros]] – drums (1992–1998, 2009, 2022–present)
- [[Artists/I/Infectious Grooves]] – drums (1989–present)
