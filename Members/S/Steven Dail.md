# Steven Dail

**Bassist. American.**

Steven Dail is an American bassist from California, active since 1996. He is best known as the bassist for Starflyer 59 (joining in 2006) and for his long tenure in Project 86 from 1997 to 2010, where he also played guitar. His other projects include Neon Horse, White Lighter, Crash Rickshaw, and Low & Behold.
