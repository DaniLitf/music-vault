# Satyr

**Norwegian black metal musician. Born 23 November 1975.**

Satyr (born Sigurd Wongraven) is a Norwegian black metal musician, best known as the vocalist, guitarist, and primary songwriter of Satyricon. He is also the founder of the label Moonfog Productions and has been a prominent figure in the Norwegian black metal scene since the early 1990s.
