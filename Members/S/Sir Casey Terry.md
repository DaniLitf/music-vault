# Sir Casey Terry

**Lead vocalist. American.**

Sir Casey Terry was the lead vocalist of Mazarati, an American R&B and funk band from Minneapolis formed in the mid-1980s. A high school student in Minneapolis, Terry met fellow student and bassist Brown Mark, who went on to join Prince's band The Revolution and later produced Mazarati's recordings. Terry provided the smooth, expressive vocal style that defined the band's sound on their 1986 self-titled debut album on Paisley Park Records, and contributed backing vocals to Prince's No. 1 hit "Kiss." He also co-wrote an early version of "Jerk Out," which The Time later turned into a hit in 1990.
