# Simon McBride

**Guitar. Born April 9, 1979.**

Simon McBride is the guitarist of [[Artists/D/Deep Purple]], joining in 2022 as the replacement for Steve Morse. He previously toured with Ian Gillan and Don Airey and was a member of Snakecharmer.
