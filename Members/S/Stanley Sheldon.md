# Stanley Sheldon

**Bassist. Born June 25, 1950.**

Stanley Sheldon is an American bassist best known for his work with Peter Frampton, most notably on the iconic live album *Frampton Comes Alive!* He also substituted for Mel Schacher during Grand Funk Railroad tours in 2018.
