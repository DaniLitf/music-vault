# Sean Welch

**Bassist. Born 1965.**

Sean Welch is an English bassist, best known as the bassist of [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]]

## Biography
Sean Welch was the bassist for The Beautiful South from their formation in 1988 until their dissolution in 2007, appearing on all ten studio albums. His melodic bass lines were a key component of the band's sound, providing warmth and depth beneath the dual-vocal arrangements. Welch was one of the core members who remained with the band throughout their entire 19-year run.
