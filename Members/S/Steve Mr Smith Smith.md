# Steve "Mr. Smith" Smith

**Bassist. British.**

Steve "Mr. Smith" Smith is an English musician who played bass for deathgrind band Gorerotted from 1998 to 2001. He appeared on the album *Mutilated in Minutes* (2000) before leaving to form Infected Disarray.
