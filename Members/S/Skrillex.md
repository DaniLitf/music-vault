# Skrillex

**DJ and record producer. Born 15 January 1988 in Los Angeles, California, USA.**

Sonny Moore, known professionally as Skrillex, is an American electronic music producer and DJ who became one of the defining figures in the mainstream popularization of dubstep and EDM in the early 2010s. He has won seven Grammy Awards.
