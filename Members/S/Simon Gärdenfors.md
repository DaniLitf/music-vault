# Simon Gärdenfors

**Rapper, comedian, cartoonist, TV personality. Born March 21, 1978 in Hjärup, Sweden.**

Simon Gärdenfors is a Swedish multi-talented entertainer known as one half of the rap duo Far & Son alongside Frej Larsson and as a member of Las Palmas. Beyond music, he is a stand-up comedian, cartoonist, podcast host, and television personality who has appeared on *Släng Dig i Brunnen* (SVT) and starred in the TV series *Samhällsjudo* on TV12. He also directed and starred in the autobiographical film *Serietecknaren* and released the stand-up special *En Slapp Timme*, the first Swedish stand-up special to air on Comedy Central.
