# Sid Falck

**Drummer.**

Sid Falck served as Overkill's drummer from 1987 to 1993, appearing on *Under the Influence* (1988), *The Years of Decay* (1989), *Horrorscope* (1991), and *I Hear Black* (1993). His powerful thrash drumming was central to the band's sound during their most creatively consistent period.
