# Selle

**Drummer. Swedish.**

Selle is a Swedish drummer who played for Driller Killer from 1996 to 2000, performing on the albums *Fuck the World* (1997) and *Reality Bites* (1998), and the EP *What Goes Around Comes Around* (1999).
