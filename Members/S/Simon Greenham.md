# Simon Greenham

**Vocalist. British.**

Simon Greenham is a British punk vocalist who was the original singer of Chaos UK. Previously of The Pubix, he performed on the band's early EPs *Burning Britain* (1982) and *Loud Political & Uncompromising* (1982) before leaving the band in 1983.
