# Steve Felton

**Drummer. Born December 6, 1969.**

Steve "Skinny" Felton is an American musician and the founding drummer and visual director of the industrial metal band Mushroomhead, serving as the band's only constant member throughout numerous lineup changes since their formation in 1993. He is known for pioneering Mushroomhead's signature avant-garde aesthetic characterized by elaborate masks, theatrical costumes, and horror-inspired imagery. In addition to drumming, Felton serves as keyboardist, percussionist, and producer for all Mushroomhead albums.
