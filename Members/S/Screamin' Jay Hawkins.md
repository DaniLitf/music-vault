# Screamin' Jay Hawkins

**Singer and actor. Born 18 July 1929 in Cleveland, Ohio, USA. Died 12 February 2000 in Neuilly-sur-Seine, France.**

Jalacy Hawkins, known as Screamin' Jay Hawkins, was an American blues and R&B singer who pioneered shock rock theatrics in the 1950s. His 1956 recording "I Put a Spell on You" became a horror-blues standard, and his coffin-entrance live shows inspired generations of theatrical rock performers.
