# Summer

**Singer. American.**

Summer (born Paula Brown) is an American singer from Washington, D.C., who fronted Snap! for their third album *Welcome to Tomorrow* (1994) as the group shifted towards a progressive house sound. She performed on the singles "Welcome to Tomorrow (Are You Ready?)" and "The First the Last Eternity (Till the End)".
