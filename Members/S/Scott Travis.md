**Drummer. Born September 6, 1961.**

Scott Travis is an American drummer best known as the longtime drummer for the heavy metal band Judas Priest, which he joined in 1989. Before Judas Priest, he played with Racer X and briefly with The Scream, co-writing the song "I Don't Care" on their debut album *Let It Scream*.
