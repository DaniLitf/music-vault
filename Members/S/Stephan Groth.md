# Stephan Groth

**Danish-Norwegian singer, frontman and founder of Apoptygma Berzerk. Born 10 August 1971.**

Stephan Groth was born in Odense, Denmark, and relocated with his family to Sarpsborg, Norway, in 1986. He founded Apoptygma Berzerk in 1989, initially as a duo with Jon Erik Martinsen. Groth is considered one of the pioneers of the futurepop genre alongside Ronan Harris of VNV Nation. He also fronts the pop-oriented side project Fairlight Children and has made guest appearances with Satyricon.
