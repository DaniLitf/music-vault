# Scott Leonard

**Drummer. American.**

Scott Leonard was the original drummer for the Las Vegas grindcore band Righteous Pigs from 1986 to 1988. He played on the band's debut album *Live and Learn* (1989). He was later a member of Voluntary Manslaughter and Monster Zero.
