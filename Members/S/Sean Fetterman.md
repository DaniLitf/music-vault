# Sean Fetterman

**Bassist.**

Sean Fetterman is an American bassist who played in [[Artists/B/Black Light Burns]]' initial touring lineup from 2007 to 2008. He previously played bass in [[Artists/T/Turn of the Screw]] alongside guitarist Nick Annis.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/B/Black Light Burns]] – bass (2007–2008)
- [[Artists/T/Turn of the Screw]] – bass (before 2007)
