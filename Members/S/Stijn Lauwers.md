# Stijn Lauwers

**Producer, programmer. Belgian.**

Stijn Lauwers, known by his stage name El Sati, was a founding member of the Belgian hip house project Hi Tek 3. He co-produced the group's output alongside Yosef Wolde-Mariam and Koenraad Van-Lishout.
