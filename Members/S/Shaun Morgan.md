# Shaun Morgan

**Lead vocals, guitar. South African. Founder of [[Artists/S/Seether]].**

Shaun Morgan founded Seether (originally Saron Gas) in Pretoria in 1999 and remains its only original member, writing and recording most of the band's guitar parts across its catalogue.
