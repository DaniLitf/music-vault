# Stuart Sutcliffe

**Stuart Sutcliffe was a British artist and musician who was an early bassist for The Beatles during their Hamburg years, often cited as the person who suggested the group adopt the name "Beatles."**

A talented visual artist, Sutcliffe left the band in 1961 to remain in Hamburg with his fiancée Astrid Kirchherr and study art. He died of a brain hemorrhage on April 10, 1962, aged 21.
