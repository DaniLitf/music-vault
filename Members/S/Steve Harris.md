# Steve Harris

**Bass, backing vocals, keyboards (1975–present). Born March 12, 1956.**

Steve Harris is an English musician, songwriter, and producer best known as the founder, bassist, and primary songwriter of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – bass, backing vocals, keyboards (1975–present)
- [[Artists/B/British Lion]] – bass, backing vocals, founder (2012–present)

## Biography

Harris formed Iron Maiden in 1975 and has written or co-written the majority of the band's material. His signature bass style — fast, melodic, and galloping — is central to the Maiden sound. He has overseen every aspect of the band's career and is widely regarded as one of the greatest bassists in heavy metal history.
