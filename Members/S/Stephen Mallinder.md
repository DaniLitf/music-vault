# Stephen Mallinder

**Stephen Mallinder is a British musician, vocalist, and bassist who co-founded Cabaret Voltaire in Sheffield in 1973 and later co-founded Wrangler in 2013.**

As one half of Cabaret Voltaire alongside Richard H. Kirk, Mallinder helped pioneer industrial music and electronic dance music in the late 1970s and 1980s. He has also had a solo career and academic career as a lecturer.
