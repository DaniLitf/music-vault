# Steve Hillage

**Guitarist, producer. Born 1951-08-02.**

Steve Hillage is a British guitarist and producer known for psychedelic/progressive rock and electronic projects including System 7. He contributed guitar to early [[Artists/T/The Orb]] recordings and performances.
