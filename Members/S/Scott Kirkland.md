# Scott Kirkland

**Electronic musician, producer. American.**

Scott Kirkland is an American electronic musician who co-founded [[Artists/T/The Crystal Method]] with Ken Jordan in 1992. Following Jordan's departure in 2017, he has continued the project as a solo act.
