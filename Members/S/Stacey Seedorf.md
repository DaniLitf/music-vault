# Stacey Seedorf

**Rapper and vocalist. Dutch.**

Stacey Seedorf, known as Stay-C, is a Dutch rapper and vocalist who joined Twenty 4 Seven in 1993 as the replacement for Captain Hollywood. She performed on the albums *Slave to the Music* and *I Wanna Show You* and sang on many of the group's most successful singles. After a hiatus she returned to the group from 2010 to 2023.
