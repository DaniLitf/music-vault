# Stan Cullimore

**Guitarist, author. Born 1962-05-06.**

Stan Cullimore is an English musician and children's author best known as guitarist and co-founder of [[Artists/T/The Housemartins]]. After the band split, he moved into writing and television-related work while occasionally reconnecting with former bandmate Paul Heaton.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Housemartins]] – guitar, vocals (1983–1988)
