# Stephen Egerton

**Guitar. American.**

Stephen Egerton is an American guitarist and record producer who joined [[Artists/D/Descendents]] in 1986 from the Salt Lake City band [[Artists/M/Massacre Guys]], alongside bassist [[Members/K/Karl Alvarez]]. He is also a founding member of [[Artists/A/All]], and as a producer/engineer he co-produced Descendents albums including *Everything Sucks* (1996) and *Hypercaffium Spazzinate* (2016) — see [[Producers/S/Stephen Egerton]]. He runs Armstrong Recording studio in Tulsa, Oklahoma, where parts of *Hypercaffium Spazzinate* were tracked.

## Associated Artists
