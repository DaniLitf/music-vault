# Suzie McNeil

**Singer, harmonica player. Canadian.**

Suzie McNeil is a Canadian singer and songwriter who rose to prominence as a finalist on the reality show *Rock Star: INXS*. She has released solo albums and contributed backing vocals and harmonica to various rock recordings.
