# Stephen Looker

**Guitarist.**

Stephen Looker is an American guitarist who joined the reformed Gainesville line-up of [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] in June 1997 and remained until March 1998, when he was replaced by Joseph Simmons. He left before the band's studio releases and does not appear on any of its records.
