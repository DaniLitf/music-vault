# Shelby J.

**Vocalist. Born in Oakland, California.**

Shelby J. is an American singer who joined Prince's New Power Generation in the 2000s. She contributed vocals to Prince's *3121*, *Planet Earth*, and *Art Official Age* albums and was a fixture in Prince's live shows. She also sang with Sheila E. and Larry Graham.
