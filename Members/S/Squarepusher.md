# Squarepusher

**Electronic musician and bassist. Born 17 January 1975 in Chelmsford, Essex, England.**

Tom Jenkinson records and performs as Squarepusher, releasing technically complex electronic music through Warp Records since 1996. He is known for combining drum and bass with IDM and live bass guitar performance.
