# Stef Kamil Carlens

**Belgian musician and producer. Born 1970.**

Stef Kamil Carlens is a Belgian musician and producer known for his work with the EBM/industrial band Diskonnekted and other electronic projects. He has also been involved in various other Belgian electronic music acts.
