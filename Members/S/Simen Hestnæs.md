# Simen Hestnæs

**Simen Hestnæs, known as ICS Vortex, is a Norwegian musician who was a vocalist and bassist in both Borknagar (1997–2001) and Dimmu Borgir (1999–2009), and is the creative link between those two bands.**

His operatic clean baritone voice provided a distinctive contrast to the black metal extremity of both bands. He has also released solo material as ICS Vortex.
