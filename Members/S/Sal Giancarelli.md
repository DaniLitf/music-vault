# Sal Giancarelli

**Drums. American. Member of [[Artists/S/Staind]].**

Sal Giancarelli, originally Jon Wysocki's drum tech, filled in on tour after Wysocki's 2011 departure and was made an official member of Staind that November. He also drummed for bandmate Mike Mushok's side project [[Artists/S/Saint Asonia]] from 2017 to 2020.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/S/Staind]] – drums (2011–present)
- [[Artists/S/Saint Asonia]] – drums (2017–2020)
