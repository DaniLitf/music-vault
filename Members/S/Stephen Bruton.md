# Stephen Bruton

**American guitarist and songwriter. Born 7 November 1948, died 9 May 2009.**

Stephen Bruton was an American guitarist and songwriter best known for his long collaboration with Kris Kristofferson and his work with the country-rock band KGC (Kris Kristofferson, Guy Clark, Billy Swan).
