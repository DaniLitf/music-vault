# Steve Guney

**Session musician. British.**

Steve Guney contributed sampling to the track "Sea of Tranquility" on Meathook Seed's debut album *Embedded* (1993), collaborating alongside Napalm Death bassist Shane Embury on the nearly 14-minute ambient-industrial piece.
