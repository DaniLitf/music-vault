# Scott Reynolds

**Vocals. American.**

Scott Reynolds is an American punk vocalist from Fredonia, New York, who became the second lead singer of [[Artists/A/All]] in 1989, fronting the band through *Allroy's Revenge* (1989), the live album *Trailblazer* (1990), *Allroy Saves* (1990), and *Percolater* (1992). After leaving All in 1993 he pursued a solo and collaborative career, including the project 40Engine with former All guitarist [[Members/S/Stephen Egerton]], and has rejoined All for periodic reunion performances, including in 2025.
