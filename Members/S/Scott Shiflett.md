# Scott Shiflett

**Bassist, vocalist. American.**

Scott Shiflett is the bassist of the Southern California punk rock band [[Artists/F/Face to Face]], joining in 1995 after the departure of founding bassist Matt Riddle. Shiflett became a core member of the band's classic lineup and has remained with the band through their 2004 disbandment and subsequent 2008 reunion to the present day.
