# Svein Berge

**Producer, multi-instrumentalist. Born 1973-09-01 in Tromsø, Norway.**

Svein Berge is one half of Röyksopp alongside Torbjørn Brundtland. The two grew up together in Tromsø before moving to Bergen and forming Röyksopp in the late 1990s. Berge and Brundtland share production and composition duties equally across Röyksopp's catalog.
