# Stefan Ackermann

**German vocalist, co-founder of Das Ich. Born in Berlin.**

Stefan Ackermann co-founded Das Ich in Bayreuth in 1989 with Bruno Kramm. He writes all of Das Ich's lyrics and delivers distinctive German-language vocals. In 2012, he suffered a brain hemorrhage but recovered and returned to performing. Ackermann's theatrical stage presence and baritone vocal delivery are central to the band's identity.
