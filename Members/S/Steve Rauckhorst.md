# Steve Rauckhorst

**Vocalist (clean and harsh vocals). American.**

Steve Rauckhorst is an American vocalist best known for joining Mushroomhead in March 2018 as the replacement for departing co-founder Jeffrey Hetrick. He has provided both clean and harsh vocals on the band's recent albums *A Wonderful Life* (2020) and *Call the Devil* (2024), and continues as a core member of the band's current lineup.
