# Steven Shane McDonald

**Bassist. Born 1967-05-24.**

Steven Shane McDonald is an American bassist best known as a member of [[Artists/R/Redd Kross]]. He joined [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] in 2015 and appears on later Melvins albums including *Basses Loaded* and *Pinkus Abortion Technician*.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] – bass (2015–present)
- [[Artists/R/Redd Kross]] – bass
