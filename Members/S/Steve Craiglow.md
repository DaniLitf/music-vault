**Bassist. American.**

Steve Craiglow is an American bassist who played in the Chicago heavy metal band White Heat in the early 1980s. He was a member of the band's only stable lineup alongside vocalist Jeff Hatrix, guitarist Carlos Cordon, and drummer John Perkins.
