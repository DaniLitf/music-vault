# Shirin von Gehlen

**Female vocalist, dancer. German.**

Shirin von Gehlen (also known as Shirin Amour) is a German singer and dancer who has been the female vocalist and frontwoman for the Eurodance project Captain Hollywood Project since its revival in 2008. She performs alongside Tony Dawson-Harrison as a live duo act, and also works as a choreographer for video, music, and stage productions worldwide.
