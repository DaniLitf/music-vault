# Stephen Chiatovich

**Bassist. American.**

Stephen Chiatovich was the bassist for the Las Vegas grindcore band Righteous Pigs from 1987 until the band's breakup in 1990. He played on both of the band's studio albums, *Live and Learn* (1989) and *Stress Related* (1990).
