# Sam Rivers

**Bassist. Born 2 September 1977 in Jacksonville, Florida, USA. Died 18 October 2025.**

Sam Rivers was an American bassist and co-founder of [[Artists/L/Limp Bizkit]], anchoring the band's rhythm section from 1994 until his death in 2025. He also guested on [[Artists/B/Black Light Burns]]' track "I Have a Need" from *Cruel Melody* (2007).

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/L/Limp Bizkit]] – bass, backing vocals (1994–2025)
- [[Artists/B/Black Light Burns]] – guest bass on "I Have a Need" (2007)
