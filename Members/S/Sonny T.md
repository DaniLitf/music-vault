# Sonny T.

**Bassist. Born in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

Sonny T. is an American bassist who joined Prince's New Power Generation for the 1990 Nude Tour. He played on *Diamonds and Pearls*, *Love Symbol Album*, and the NPG album *Exodus*, on which he performed lead vocals. After leaving Prince in 1996 he played with Nick Jonas and the Administration.
