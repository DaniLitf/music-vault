# Syd Barrett

**Guitarist, vocalist, songwriter. Born 1946-01-06 in Cambridge, England. Died 2006-07-07.**

Syd Barrett was a co-founding member of Pink Floyd and the creative force behind their earliest work, writing most of the songs on their debut *The Piper at the Gates of Dawn* (1967). His whimsical, psychedelic songwriting and charismatic stage presence were central to the band's initial identity. By 1968 his mental health had deteriorated — widely attributed to heavy LSD use — and he left the band, being replaced by his friend David Gilmour. He went on to release two solo albums before withdrawing entirely from public life. He died of pancreatic cancer in 2006.
