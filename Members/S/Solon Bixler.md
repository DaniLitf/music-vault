# Solon Bixler

**Guitar. Born January 4, 1977.**

Solon Bixler is an American indie rock guitarist who joined [[Artists/0-9/30 Seconds to Mars|30 Seconds to Mars]] in 2001 as the band expanded to a four-piece, but left in early 2003 over touring-related issues and was replaced by [[Members/T/Tomo Miličević|Tomo Miličević]]. He subsequently played in a revolving cast of bands including Earlimart, All Smiles, and Sea Wolf, and later fronted Great Northern on lead vocals and guitar.
