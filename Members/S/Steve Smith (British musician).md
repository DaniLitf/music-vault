# Steve Smith

**Bassist. British.**

Steve Smith, known as Mr. Smith, was the original bassist for the deathgrind band Gorerotted, playing from 1998 to 2001. He performed on the debut album *Mutilated in Minutes* and appeared on the *Split Your Guts Vol. 1* EP.
