# Steve Albini

**Guitarist, recording engineer, producer. Born 22 July 1961 in Pasadena, California, USA.**

Steve Albini is a musician and audio engineer known for his work with Big Black, Shellac, and as the founder of Electrical Audio studio. He recorded Pigface's debut album *Gub* and has engineered landmark albums including Nirvana's *In Utero* and Pixies' *Surfer Rosa*.
