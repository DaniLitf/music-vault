# Steven Severin

**Bassist. British. Born 1955-09-25.**

Steven Severin is an English bassist and co-founder of [[Artists/S/Siouxsie and the Banshees]], which he formed with Siouxsie Sioux in London in 1976 after meeting her at a Roxy Music concert. He was the band's sole other constant member alongside Siouxsie throughout its entire history, wrote many of its lyrics, and co-founded the side project the Glove in 1983 before beginning a solo/soundtrack career after the Banshees split in 1996.
