# Svend Ruelökke

**Bassist. Swedish.**

Svend Ruelökke is a Swedish bassist who played for Driller Killer across two stints: 1997 to 1999 and 2001 to 2004. He performed on the albums *Reality Bites* (1998), *And the Winner Is…* (2000), and *Cold, Cheap & Disconnected* (2002).
