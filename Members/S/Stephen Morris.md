# Stephen Morris

**Stephen Morris (born October 28, 1957, in Macclesfield, England) is a British drummer and musician, a founding member of Joy Division and New Order.**

Morris joined Joy Division in 1977 as their drummer and became integral to the band's sound. After Ian Curtis's death in 1980, he co-founded New Order with the remaining members, and also formed The Other Two side project with his wife Gillian Gilbert.
