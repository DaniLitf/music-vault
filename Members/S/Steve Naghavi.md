# Steve Naghavi

**Vocalist, programmer, keyboardist. Born December 23, 1967 in Germany.**

Steve Naghavi is a German musician best known as the founder, lead vocalist, and primary songwriter of the synthpop band And One, which he formed in Berlin in 1989 with Chris Ruiz. He has been the band's only constant member throughout all lineup changes.
