# Steevi Jaimz

**Vocalist. Welsh.**

Steevi Jaimz (born Steven Denham) is a Welsh singer who joined Tigertailz in the mid-1980s, replacing original vocalists Phil Harling and Jim Dovey, and fronted the band on their debut album *Young and Crazy* (1987). He later formed the project Jaimz-Vokins and continues to perform as the Steevi Jaimz Band, having recovered from a major stroke suffered in 2017.
