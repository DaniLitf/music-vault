# Sebastian Komor

**Polish-Norwegian electronic musician, best known for his work in Icon of Coil and Zombie Girl. Born 7 May 1976.**

Sebastian Komor was born in Zabrze, Poland, and moved to Norway in 1981. He became a central figure in the Fredrikstad EBM/industrial scene alongside Andy LaPlegua, with whom he co-founded Icon of Coil in 1997. Komor's production work spans multiple aliases including Komor Kommando, Squarehead, Moonitor, and Melt, and he has produced albums for Ayria, Alphaville, and others. He relocated to Edmonton, Alberta, Canada in 2004.
