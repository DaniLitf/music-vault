# Stephen LaCour

**Bassist. American.**

Stephen LaCour was the bassist for grindcore band Trap Them from 2007 to 2011, the longest-serving bassist of the band's early era, playing through the release of *Seizures in Barren Praise* (2008) and *Darker Handcraft* (2011).
