# Steve Edmondson

**Bassist. Born 1969-11-30 in Halifax, England.**

Steve Edmondson is the founding bassist of Paradise Lost, one of the band's longest-serving members alongside Nick Holmes, Gregor Mackintosh, and Aaron Aedy. His bass work has anchored the band's rhythm section across their entire discography from *Lost Paradise* (1990) onward.
