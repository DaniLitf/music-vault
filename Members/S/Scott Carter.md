# Scott Carter

**Drummer. American.**

Scott Carter is a drummer who performed with Dallas punk band [[Artists/H/Hagfish|Hagfish]] in 1993 during the recording of their debut studio album *Buick Men*. Carter was briefly with the band but was replaced by Tony Barsotti during the recording sessions, with Barsotti becoming the band's permanent drummer for their subsequent stable lineup from 1993 onward.
