# Scot Coogan

**Drummer and vocalist. Born April 23, 1971.**

Scot Coogan is an American musician known for his drumming and vocal work as both a session and touring musician. He has performed with Brides of Destruction, Ace Frehley, L.A. Guns, Lynch Mob, Lita Ford, and Blue Man Group, among many others.
