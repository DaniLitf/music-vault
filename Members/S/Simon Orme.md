# Simon Orme

**Vocalist and guitarist. From Walsall, England.**

Simon Orme was the vocalist and guitarist for Little Giant Drug, the alternative rock side project of Napalm Death members Mitch Harris and Shane Embury. He was recruited from neighbouring Walsall to provide vocals for the band's only album *Prismcast* (2000).
