# Susanne Sundfør

**Vocalist, songwriter, pianist. Born 19 March 1986.**

Susanne Sundfør is a Norwegian singer-songwriter from Haugesund whose albums, including *The Silicone Veil* (2012) and *Ten Love Songs* (2015), have topped the Norwegian charts. She sang the vocal theme on [[Artists/M/M83]]'s *Oblivion* soundtrack (2013) and has also collaborated with Röyksopp, including on the single "Running to the Sea".
