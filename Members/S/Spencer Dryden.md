# Spencer Dryden

**Drums. American. Born 1938-04-07. Died 2005-01-11.**

Spencer Dryden replaced [[Members/S/Skip Spence]] as drummer of [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]] in 1966, playing on the band's most acclaimed albums including *Surrealistic Pillow* (1967) and *Crown of Creation* (1968). He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame as a member of Jefferson Airplane in 1996. After leaving the band in 1970 he played with New Riders of the Purple Sage until 1976.
