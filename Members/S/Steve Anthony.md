# Steve Anthony

**Drummer. American.**

Steve Anthony is an American drummer who performed on the 1987 album *Cross and Black* by the Cleveland heavy metal band Centurion. He recorded drums and percussion on the album, which was tracked at Clockwerke Sound Studio in October 1986.
