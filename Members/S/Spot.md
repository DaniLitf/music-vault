# Spot

**Record producer; occasional bass. Born 1951.**

Spot (Glen Lockett) briefly played bass with [[Artists/B/Black Flag]]'s precursor band Panic in 1977 before turning to recording engineering full-time. As the de facto in-house producer for SST Records from 1979 to 1985, he produced or engineered the bulk of the band's catalog, from *Nervous Breakdown* through *In My Head*, as well as landmark records by the Descendents, Minutemen, Hüsker Dü, and Meat Puppets. See [[Producers/S/Spot]] for his full production career.
