# Scott Borland

**Keyboardist, multi-instrumentalist. Born in USA.**

Scott Borland is an American musician, the younger brother of [[Members/W/Wes Borland]]. He co-founded the comedic metal project [[Artists/B/Big Dumb Face]] with Wes in 1998, playing keyboards, guitar, bass and singing. He has also contributed session keyboards to several [[Artists/L/Limp Bizkit]] recordings.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/B/Big Dumb Face]] – keyboards, vocals, guitar, bass (1998–2004, 2017–present)
- [[Artists/L/Limp Bizkit]] – session keyboards
