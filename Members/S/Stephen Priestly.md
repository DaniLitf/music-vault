# Stephen Priestly

**Drummer. Swiss.**

Stephen Priestly was the session drummer on Celtic Frost's debut album *Morbid Tales* (1984), and later returned for a subsequent lineup of the band alongside guitarist Oliver Amberg and bassist Curt Victor Bryant.
