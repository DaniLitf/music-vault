# Stoli Michaels

**Singer.**

Stoli Michaels is an American singer who has performed as a vocalist for Snap! since the 2010s, appearing alongside Penny Ford in promotional photos and live performances. She has been part of the group's touring lineup during their continued live performances of their 1990s hits.
