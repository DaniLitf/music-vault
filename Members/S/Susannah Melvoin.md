# Susannah Melvoin

**Vocalist. Born 26 January 1964 in Los Angeles, California.**

Susannah Melvoin is an American singer and the twin sister of guitarist Wendy Melvoin. She joined The Revolution as a backing vocalist in 1985, appearing on *Parade* and singing lead on the album's track "Sometimes It Snows in April". She was also the lead singer of The Family, a Prince side project.
