# Sascha Konietzko

**German musician and producer. Born 21 June 1961.**

Sascha Konietzko is a German musician and producer best known as the founder, bassist, and keyboardist of KMFDM. He formed MDFMK during KMFDM's 1999 hiatus and is known for his "ultra-heavy beat" production style.
