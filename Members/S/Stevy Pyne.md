**Guitarist and backing vocalist.**

Stevy Pyne is an American guitarist best known as the guitarist of [[Artists/S/Semi Precious Weapons]], joining the band in November 2008 to replace Aaron Lee Tasjan. Pyne remained with the group until their dissolution in 2014, after which he became a touring member of Gwen Stefani's backing band.
