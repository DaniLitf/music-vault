# Stevo Bruno

**Bassist and record producer. American.**

Stevo Bruno is a multi-platinum record producer, engineer, and bassist based in Los Angeles. He owned Klown Records in Santa Monica and was the bassist in Revel 8 and the short-lived supergroup Zen Lunatic alongside John Corabi and Jimmy D'Anda. As a producer, his credits include Five Finger Death Punch, Mötley Crüe, Prong, and Brides of Destruction.
