# Scott Sorry

**Bassist. Born October 30, 1978 – died October 30, 2025.**

Scott Sorry was an American bassist and singer-songwriter. He played with Amen, Brides of Destruction, and The Wildhearts, and later formed Sorry and the Sinatras. He died on his 47th birthday after a long battle with glioblastoma.
