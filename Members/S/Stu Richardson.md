**Bassist. Born 15 August 1973.**

Stu Richardson was a founding bassist of Lostprophets, providing the rhythmic foundation for the Welsh rock band from 1997 through the band's breakup in 2013. He was part of all five studio albums and remained an essential member throughout the group's evolution from nu-metal to post-hardcore. After the band's dissolution, Richardson reflected publicly on his experiences during the turmoil surrounding the band's frontman.
