# Steve "St. Evil" Powell

**Drummer. British.**

Steve "St. Evil" Powell is an English drummer who played for extreme metal band Anaal Nathrakh from 2006 to 2016. He also joined death/grind band Detrimentum on drums for their 2012 album *Inhuman Disgrace*, produced by Russ Russell.
