# Stephen Pearcy

**Lead vocalist. Born July 10, 1956.**

Stephen Pearcy is an American singer best known as the founding frontman of the glam metal band Ratt. He formed the band that would become Ratt in the early 1970s and led them through their commercial peak in the 1980s. After leaving Ratt in 1992, he formed the band Arcade and later performed with Vicious Delite and Vertex, among other projects.
