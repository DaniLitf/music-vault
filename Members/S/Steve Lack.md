# Steve Lack

**Steve Lack is an American bassist who was a founding member of the Chicago alternative rock band Veruca Salt, playing with the group from 1992 until 1998.**

Lack was the bassist on Veruca Salt's first two albums, *American Thighs* (1994) and *Eight Arms to Hold You* (1997), before departing with the rest of the original lineup in 1998.
