# Sharon Doorson

**Singer. Born 1987-09-16.**

Sharon Doorson, also known as Miss Cherry, is a Dutch singer who briefly performed as a vocalist of Twenty 4 Seven in 2010. She was the lead vocalist on the group's 2010 live performances before being replaced by Li-Ann later that same year. Prior to her involvement with Twenty 4 Seven she was a contestant on the television show Popstars: The Rivals.
