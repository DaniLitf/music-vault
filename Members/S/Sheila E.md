# Sheila E.

**Drummer, percussionist, singer, and songwriter. Born 12 December 1957 in Oakland, California.**

Sheila E. is an American percussionist and singer widely regarded as one of the greatest drummers of her generation. She rose to fame in the 1980s through her collaboration with Prince, serving as a drummer and percussionist in his touring band and releasing her own hit solo albums *The Glamorous Life* and *Romance 1600*. She also performed on Prince's side project Madhouse and has worked with artists including George Duke, Herbie Hancock, and Marvin Gaye.
