# Synyster Gates

**Lead guitarist. Born 1981-07-07.**

Synyster Gates (Brian Elwin Haner Jr.) is an American guitarist and founding member of Avenged Sevenfold, known for blending shred-style lead guitar with jazz and classical influences absorbed from his father, guitarist Brian Haner Sr. Before Avenged Sevenfold's rise, he briefly fronted the side project Pinkly Smooth with bandmate Jimmy "The Rev" Sullivan.
