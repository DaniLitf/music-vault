# Sterling Morrison

**Guitar, bass, backing vocals. August 29, 1942 – August 30, 1995.**

Sterling Morrison was an American guitarist, best known as a founding member of [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]]

## Biography
Morrison co-founded The Velvet Underground and served as their guitarist throughout their classic period. His clean, straightforward guitar style provided the rhythmic foundation for the band's sound. After leaving the music industry in the 1970s, he became a tugboat captain before returning for the band's 1990 and 1993 reunions. He died of non-Hodgkin lymphoma in 1995.
