# Salla

**DJ, producer. Swedish.**

Salla, born Cristian Salazar Campos, is a Swedish-Chilean DJ and producer best known as a founding member of [[Artists/T/The Latin Kings]]. Together with his brother Chepe, he was central to the group's production work and later helped build Redline Records and The Salazar Brothers' role in Swedish hip hop.
