# Shaun Ryder

**Vocals. British. Born 1962-08-23.**

Shaun Ryder is an English singer, best known as the frontman of [[Artists/H/Happy Mondays]] and later [[Artists/B/Black Grape]]. He wrote lyrics for and sang on "Don't Take My Kindness for Weakness" on [[Artists/T/The Heads]]' *No Talking, Just Head* (1996).

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/H/Happy Mondays]] – lead vocals
- [[Artists/B/Black Grape]] – lead vocals, co-founder
- [[Artists/T/The Heads]] – guest vocals (1996)
