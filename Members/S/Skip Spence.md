# Skip Spence

**Drums, guitar, vocals. Canadian-American. Born 1946-04-18. Died 1999-04-16.**

Skip Spence was an original member of [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]], playing drums on their debut album *Jefferson Airplane Takes Off* (1965). He left to co-found Moby Grape, playing guitar and contributing to their acclaimed 1967 debut. He is also known for the cult solo album *Oar* (1969), recorded shortly after a mental breakdown. He died of lung cancer in 1999.
