# Steve Howe

**British guitarist and producer. Born 8 April 1947.**

Stephen James Howe is a British guitarist and producer best known as the guitarist of progressive rock band Yes (1970–1981, 1990–present) and as a founding member of Asia. He is known for his virtuoso acoustic and electric guitar work across rock, jazz, and classical styles.
