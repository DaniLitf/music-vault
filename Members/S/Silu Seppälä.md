# Silu Seppälä

**Finnish musician. Born 1960.**

Silu Seppälä is a Finnish bassist who played with Leningrad Cowboys from 1989 to 2000. He appeared in director Aki Kaurismäki's short film *Rocky VI* (1986) and the band's breakthrough feature *Leningrad Cowboys Go America* (1989).
