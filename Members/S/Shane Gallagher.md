# Shane Gallagher

**Lead guitar.**

Shane Gallagher is an American guitarist best known as the lead guitarist of [[Artists/0-9/+44]], the supergroup Mark Hoppus and Travis Barker formed after Blink-182's 2005 breakup. Previously a member of Los Angeles rock band the Nervous Return, he was invited by Hoppus to play on a few early +44 tracks and was soon drafted as a full member, staying until the band went inactive in 2009.
