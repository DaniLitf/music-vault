# Sakke Järvenpää

**Finnish musician. Born 1960.**

Sakke Järvenpää is a Finnish musician who, alongside Mato Valtonen, was a member of comedy rock band Sleepy Sleepers before the pair co-conceived Leningrad Cowboys with director Aki Kaurismäki in a Helsinki bar in 1986, as a joke on the waning power of the Soviet Union. He has remained a core member of the band since, appearing in Kaurismäki's *Leningrad Cowboys Go America* (1989).
