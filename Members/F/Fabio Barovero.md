# Fabio Barovero

**Italian musician. Born 14 August 1966.**

Fabio Barovero is an Italian musician and composer from Turin who played accordion in the folk band Loschi Dezi before co-founding the world music band Mau Mau in 1991. He is also a film composer, winning the Silver Ribbon (Nastro d'Argento) award for Best Film Score for *La Febbre*.
