# Frank Navetta

**Guitar, vocals. Died 2008-10-31.**

Frank Navetta was an American guitarist and songwriter who co-founded [[Artists/D/Descendents]] in 1977, naming the band and writing much of its early material. He left during the band's first hiatus in 1983, burning his equipment and moving to Oregon, but returned as a guest on *Everything Sucks* (1996, writing "Doghouse") and for the 2002 sessions that became *9th & Walnut* (2021). He died on October 31, 2008 after a short illness.
