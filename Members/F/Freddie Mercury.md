# Freddie Mercury

**Vocalist, pianist. Born 1946-09-05 in Stone Town, Zanzibar. Died 1991-11-24.**

Freddie Mercury was the lead vocalist, pianist, and principal songwriter of Queen, widely regarded as one of the greatest singers in rock history. Born Farrokh Bulsara in Zanzibar, he moved to England as a teenager and co-founded Queen in 1970. His extraordinary four-octave vocal range, flamboyant stage presence, and songwriting versatility — from hard rock to opera to disco — made him a singular figure. He released two solo albums, *Mr. Bad Guy* (1985) and *Barcelona* (1988, with opera singer Montserrat Caballé). He died of bronchopneumonia resulting from AIDS in November 1991, days after publicly acknowledging his diagnosis.
