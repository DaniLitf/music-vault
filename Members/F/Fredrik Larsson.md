**Bassist. Swedish.**

Fredrik Larsson is a Swedish bassist best known for his work with the heavy metal band Hammerfall. He was the original bassist on their landmark debut album Glory to the Brave in 1997 and returned as the band's bassist in 2007 after being replaced. His bass work has been essential to Hammerfall's heavy metal sound during both his tenures with the band.
