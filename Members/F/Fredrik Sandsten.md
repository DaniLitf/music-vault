# Fredrik Sandsten

**Drummer. Born 1969-12-31.**

Fredrik Sandsten is a Swedish drummer from Gothenburg whose career took off in the early 1990s. He was a founding member of [[Artists/T/The Soundtrack of Our Lives]], playing drums and singing backing vocals across all of the band's albums from 1995 until their 2012 split, and again since the 2023 reunion. During and after his years with the band he became an in-demand session drummer, working with several of Sweden's biggest artists (including live work with Håkan Hellström) as well as internationally, and has also worked as a musician at Göteborgs Stadsteater.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Soundtrack of Our Lives]] – drums, backing vocals (1995–2012, 2023–present)
