# Fred Wesley

**Trombone, arranger. American. Horn arranger for [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Fred Wesley was a trombonist and bandleader in James Brown's band before leaving with saxophonist Maceo Parker and other Brown alumni in 1975 to join George Clinton's Parliament-Funkadelic, where he arranged the collective's horn parts (often with Bernie Worrell) as part of the Horny Horns.
