# Frank Healy

**Bassist and guitarist. Born 30 August 1962.**

Frank Healy is an English musician who played bass with Benediction from 1991 to 2017, appearing on the majority of the band's discography. He was briefly a guitarist for Napalm Death in 1987 and has also been a member of Cerebral Fix, Sacrilege, and Memoriam.
