# FM Einheit

**FM Einheit (born Frank-Martin Strauss) is a German percussionist born December 18, 1958, best known as a long-time member of [[Artists/E/Einstürzende Neubauten]].**

He contributed experimental percussion to Pigface.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/P/Pigface]] – percussion (guest)
- [[Artists/E/Einstürzende Neubauten]] – percussion (1980–1996)
