**Lead guitarist and vocalist. British.**

Francis Rossi is the founder and only continuous member of the legendary British rock band Status Quo, maintaining his role for over five decades since 1967. A prolific guitarist and songwriter, he was appointed OBE in 2010 for his contributions to music. Before Status Quo, he played in The Paladins, The Spectres, and Traffic Jam, and has released multiple solo albums including King of the Doghouse and One Step at a Time.
