# Frank Lenz

**Drummer, multi-instrumentalist, and producer. Born June 18, 1967.**

Frank Lenz is an American drummer and multi-instrumentalist from Southern California who has performed and recorded with numerous indie artists including Starflyer 59, The Lassie Foundation, Pedro the Lion, The Weepies, and Richard Swift. He has released several solo albums blending pop, jazz, and R&B influences, and works as a session musician and producer from his studio in Redlands, California.
