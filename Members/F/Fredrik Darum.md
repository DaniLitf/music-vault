# Fredrik Darum

**Norwegian guitarist, former touring member of Apoptygma Berzerk.**

Fredrik Darum served as a live guitarist for Apoptygma Berzerk from 1999 to 2001, supporting the band's live performances during the *Welcome to Earth* era.
