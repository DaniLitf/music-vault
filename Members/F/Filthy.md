**Music producer, turntablist, and instrumentalist. Swedish.**

Filthy is a Swedish music producer and turntablist known for his work in the hip-hop duo Afasi & Filthy and as a solo producer. He produced major works for international artists including Madonna, Katy Perry, and Britney Spears, establishing himself as a significant figure in contemporary pop and electronic music production.
