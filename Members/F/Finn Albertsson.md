# Finn Albertsson

**Musician.**

Finn Albertsson, known by the persona "Cybernoid," co-founded the Swedish synthpop band S.P.O.C.K in 1988, after Eddie Bengtsson wrote songs to be performed at Albertsson's birthday party with vocalist Alexander Hofman.
