# Fredrik Brarud

**Norwegian drummer, former member of Apoptygma Berzerk.**

Fredrik Brarud was the drummer for Apoptygma Berzerk from 2002 to 2009, performing on the albums *Harmonizer*, *You and Me Against the World*, and *Rocket Science*.
