**Keyboardist. Born September 29, 1975.**

Falk Maria Schlegel (real name Christian Jost) is a German keyboardist best known as a core member of power metal band Powerwolf since 2003, where he handles keyboards and contributes to the band's theatrical orchestral arrangements. Before Powerwolf, he was keyboardist for progressive thrash metal band The Experience (1993 onward) and performed under the pseudonyms Ray Kitzler and Ray Volver in the project Red Aim (2002-2003).
