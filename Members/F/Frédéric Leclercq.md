**Bassist and guitarist. Born 23 June 1978.**

Frédéric Leclercq is a French musician and producer known for his 13-year tenure as bassist for British power metal band DragonForce (2006–2019). He contributed to five studio albums, including the platinum-certified album Inhuman Rampage. Since leaving DragonForce, he has played with multiple metal projects including Kreator, Sinsaenum, Maladaptive, and Amahiru.
