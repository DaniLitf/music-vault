# Fjalle Wisemark

**Vocals, bass. Swedish.**

Fjalle Wisemark (also known as Anders Nelander) is a Swedish musician who was the vocalist and bassist for the Malmö hardcore punk band Black Uniforms across their entire active period from 1981 to 1990. He performed on all of the band's recordings and was a core member alongside Cliff Lundberg.
