# Fredrik Åkesson

**Guitarist. Born 1975-09-15 in Sweden.**

Fredrik Åkesson is a Swedish guitarist who joined Opeth in 2007, replacing founding guitarist Peter Lindgren. Before Opeth, he performed with the melodic death metal band Arch Enemy from 2005 to 2007. His technically proficient playing style, combining heavy riffing with intricate melodic work, has contributed significantly to Opeth's sound through the progressive direction of their later albums.
