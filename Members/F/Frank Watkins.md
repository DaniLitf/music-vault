# Frank Watkins

**Bassist. Born February 2, 1968 — died October 18, 2015.**

Frank Watkins was an American death metal bassist best known for his long tenure with Obituary from 1993 to 2010, during which he appeared on *World Demise* (1994), *Back from the Dead* (1997), *Frozen in Time* (2005), and *Xecutioner's Return* (2007). He was also the bassist for the Norwegian black metal band Gorgoroth from 2007 to 2010.
