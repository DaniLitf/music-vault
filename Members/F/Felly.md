**Fashion model. Congolese.**

Felly Kilingi, known mononymously as Felly, is a Congolese-born fashion model who served as the public face of [[Artists/T/Technotronic|Technotronic]] during their breakthrough in 1989–1990. She appeared on the cover of the "Pump Up the Jam" single and album and lip-synced in the music video, though she did not actually perform on the recordings — the real vocals were provided by [[Members/Y/Ya Kid K|Ya Kid K]].

The deception was later admitted by producer [[Members/J/Jo Bogaert|Jo Bogaert]], who stated that Felly was hired purely to create an image for the group. She did not speak English at the time and had no involvement in the music production.
