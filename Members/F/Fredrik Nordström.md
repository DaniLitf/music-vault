# Fredrik Nordström

**Keyboards, record producer. Swedish.**

Fredrik Nordström played keyboards for [[Artists/C/Ceremonial Oath]] in 1992–1993 and went on to become one of the Gothenburg metal scene's most prolific record producers and studio owners (Studio Fredman).
