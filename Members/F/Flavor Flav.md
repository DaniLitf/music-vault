**Rapper and hype man. American.**

Flavor Flav is the stage name of William Jonathan Drayton Jr., the legendary hype man and founding member of the influential hip-hop group Public Enemy. Known for his energetic performance style, oversized clock necklace, and iconic "Yeah Yeah!" ad-libs, he has been a cornerstone of Public Enemy's sound since 1985 and was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame with the group in 2013. He has received six Grammy nominations and pursued solo projects alongside his work with Public Enemy.
