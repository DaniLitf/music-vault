**Keyboardist and turntablist. American.**

Frank Delgado is the keyboardist and turntablist for the American nu metal band Deftones. He joined the band as a full official member in 1999 and has been a crucial part of their sound since, credited on all major studio albums from White Pony onward. His work blending keyboards, programming, and turntable effects helped define Deftones' signature atmospheric and electronic-influenced metal sound.
