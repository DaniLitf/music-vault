# Fast Eddie Clarke

**Guitarist. Born 5 October 1950. Died 10 January 2018.**

Edward "Fast Eddie" Clarke was a British guitarist who played on Motörhead's first six albums from 1976 to 1982, forming part of the classic lineup alongside Lemmy Kilmister and Phil "Philthy Animal" Taylor, and co-writing the band's signature song "Ace of Spades." After leaving Motörhead he formed Fastway with UFO's Pete Way. He died of pneumonia in 2018, the last surviving member of the classic lineup.
