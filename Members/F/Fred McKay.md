# Fred McKay

**Drummer. American.**

Fred McKay was the original drummer for Duraluxe, playing on their debut album *Dolorosa* (1999). He performed on all tracks, providing the percussive backbone for the band's swirling psychedelic rock sound. McKay was part of Duraluxe's first line-up alongside Chris Colbert, Troy Daugherty, and Tommy Wedge, and left around 2000.
