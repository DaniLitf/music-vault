# Frederic Yonnet

**Harmonica player. Born in France.**

Frederic Yonnet is a French harmonica player who performed with Prince's New Power Generation during the 2000s. He appeared on Prince's *Planet Earth* album and performed live with the band. He also plays with Stevie Wonder and has been called the "Jimi Hendrix of the harmonica."
