# Frank Romano

**Guitarist. American.**

Frank Romano is an American guitarist who played in the Cleveland thrash/power metal band Purgatory from 1986 through 1990 and during the 1991 reunion, replacing Dave Felton. He performed on the band's second album *Dark Alley Surgery* as well as post-Hatrix material with vocalist Andy Boyer.
