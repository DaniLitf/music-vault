# Fenriz

**Drummer. Norwegian.**

Gylve "Fenriz" Nagell is a Norwegian musician best known as the drummer of Darkthrone, one of the most influential bands in the second wave of black metal. He was also an early member of Dødheimsgard, playing bass and additional vocals from 1994 to 1995 before departing prior to their debut album.
