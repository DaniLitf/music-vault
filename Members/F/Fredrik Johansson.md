# Fredrik Johansson

**Guitarist. Swedish.**

Fredrik Johansson is a Swedish guitarist, formerly of [[Artists/D/Dimension Zero]] and a guest lead guitarist on In Flames' *The Jester Race*, who took over guitar duties in [[Artists/A/All Ends]] in spring 2005. He is frequently confused with the similarly-named former Dark Tranquillity rhythm guitarist of the same name, who is a different person.
