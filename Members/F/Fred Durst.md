# Fred Durst

**Vocals. American. Frontman of [[Artists/L/Limp Bizkit]].**

Fred Durst founded Limp Bizkit in 1994 and has fronted the band throughout its history. In 1997, after initially trying to have the then-unknown band [[Artists/S/Staind]] pulled from a Limp Bizkit support slot over their album cover art, he was won over by their performance, signed them to Flip Records, and co-produced their major-label debut *Dysfunction* (1999) alongside [[Producers/T/Terry Date]].
