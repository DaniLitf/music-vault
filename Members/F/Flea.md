# Flea

**Flea (born Michael Balzary) is an Australian-American bassist born October 16, 1962, co-founder of [[Artists/R/Red Hot Chili Peppers]].**

He contributed bass to Pigface's early tours and recordings. One of the most recognisable bassists in rock, he was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2012.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/P/Pigface]] – bass (1990–1994)
- [[Artists/R/Red Hot Chili Peppers]] – bass (1983–present)
- [[Artists/S/Satellite Party]] – guest bass, *Ultra Payloaded* (2007)
