**Vocalist, producer, songwriter. Born November 4, 1975.**

Freddy Cricien is the founding member and lead vocalist of Madball, a cornerstone band in the New York hardcore scene since 1988. He co-founded the supergroup Hazen Street in 2004 with musicians from Madball and H2O, blending hardcore punk with rap-rock elements. Cricien has been involved in the hardcore and hip-hop scenes for over three decades and is the younger brother of Roger Miret, founder of Agnostic Front.
