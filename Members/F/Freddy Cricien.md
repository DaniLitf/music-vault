**Vocalist and New York hardcore legend. Born November 4, 1975.**

Freddy Cricien is the founding member and lead vocalist of Madball, one of the most influential bands in the New York hardcore scene since 1986. Known for his aggressive vocal style and uncompromising approach to hardcore, Cricien has been instrumental in shaping NYHC's sound and ethos across multiple decades. The band's name, Hazen Street, was inspired by the address of Rikers Island Prison where Cricien served time, reflecting the band's connection to New York's street culture and correctional system history.
