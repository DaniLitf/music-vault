# Fredrik Thordendal

**Swedish guitarist. Born 11 February 1970.**

Fredrik Thordendal is a Swedish guitarist best known as the lead guitarist and founding member of Meshuggah. He is known for his pioneering use of extended-range guitars and polyrhythmic playing.
