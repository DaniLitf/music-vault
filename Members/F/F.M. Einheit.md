**Percussionist. Born December 18, 1958.**

F.M. Einheit (Frank Martin Strauß) is a German percussionist and composer best known for his innovative custom metal-instrument designs and uncompromising sound with industrial pioneers Einstürzende Neubauten (1982-1995). Before joining Neubauten, he performed in Hamburg punk bands Abwärts and Palais Schaumburg in the early 1980s; after his tenure with Neubauten, he continued experimental collaborations with numerous projects including Stein, Gry, and Vladimir Estragon.
