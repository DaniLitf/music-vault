**Keyboardist. Swedish.**

Finn Eriksson, known by his stage name Cybernoid, is a Swedish keyboardist and synthesizer player. He was a founding member of the science fiction-themed Swedish rock band S.P.O.C.K in 1988, contributing to their early sound before departing after 1989, with subsequent reappearances as the band evolved through multiple era changes.
