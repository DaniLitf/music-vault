**Drummer. Swedish.**

Fredrik Andersson is a Swedish metal drummer who was a member of the melodic death metal band Amon Amarth from 1998 to 2015, a 17-year tenure during which he provided the thunderous percussion backbone for some of the band's most celebrated albums. He is also known for his work with the death metal band A Canorous Quintet prior to joining Amon Amarth.
