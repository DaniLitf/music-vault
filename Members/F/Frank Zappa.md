# Frank Zappa

**Frank Zappa was an American musician, composer, and bandleader from Baltimore, Maryland, one of the most prolific figures in rock history, whose over 60-album catalogue spans rock, jazz, classical, and avant-garde music, and who died of prostate cancer in December 1993.**
