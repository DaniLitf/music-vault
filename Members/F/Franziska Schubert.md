# Franziska Schubert

**Bassist. German.**

Franziska Schubert performed as Franzi Underdrive in the early lineup of [[Artists/T/The Inchtabokatables]]. She played bass from the band's founding through the period around their debut album *Inchtomanie*.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Inchtabokatables]] – bass as Franzi Underdrive (1991–1992)
