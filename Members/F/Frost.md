# Frost

**Norwegian black metal drummer. Born 28 May 1968.**

Frost (born Kjetter-Vidar Haraldstad) is a Norwegian black metal drummer, best known as the drummer of Satyricon. He is also known for his work with 1349, Gorgoroth, and other extreme metal bands, and is considered one of the most technically proficient drummers in black metal.
