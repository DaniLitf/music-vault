**Lead guitarist and vocalist. American.**

Franz Kenneth Stahl is an American guitarist best known as a founding member and continuous core member of the Washington D.C. punk and alternative rock band Scream, which he co-leads with his brother Pete. Since 1981, the Stahl brothers have been the only constant members of Scream through multiple lineup changes. Franz briefly played with Foo Fighters from 1997 to 1999 during The Colour and the Shape era, in addition to his work with Wool and other projects.
