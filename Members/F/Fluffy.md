# Fluffy

**Guitarist. British.**

Fluffy is an English musician, best known as the guitarist and founding member of deathgrind band Gorerotted / The Rotted. He has been the band's primary guitarist since its formation in 1997, appearing on all three Gorerotted albums and both The Rotted albums, and released the band's debut album *Mutilated in Minutes* (2000) through his own label, Dead Again Records. He remains in the reunited Gorerotted lineup as of 2026, one of only three members to have appeared on all three original Gorerotted albums.
