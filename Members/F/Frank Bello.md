**Bassist. American.**

Frank Bello is the bassist for the legendary American thrash metal band Anthrax, a position he has held since 1984 with only a brief departure from 2004 to 2005. He is the nephew of Anthrax drummer Charlie Benante and has been instrumental in the band's sound through their most iconic era. In recent years, he has also appeared as a touring bassist for Satyricon.
