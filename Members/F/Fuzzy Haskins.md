# Fuzzy Haskins

**Vocals, guitar, drums. American. Original member of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Fuzzy Haskins was first tenor of the Parliaments, also contributing guitar and drums, known for his gospel-style singing. He left P-Funk in 1977 and later co-founded the group Original P with fellow original Parliaments members Ray Davis, Calvin Simon, and Grady Thomas, retiring in 2011. He died in 2023.
