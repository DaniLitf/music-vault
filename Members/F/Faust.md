**Drummer. Born 1971.**

Faust (Bård Eithun) is a Norwegian drummer best known as the original drummer of Norwegian black metal pioneers Emperor (1992-1994), appearing on the band's classic first two albums, In the Nightside Eclipse and Anthems to the Welkin at Dusk. He returned for limited reunion performances in 2013-2014, though visa issues prevented his full participation in some dates.
