**Lead guitarist. German.**

Frank Gosdzik, known professionally as Frank Blackfire, is a German metal guitarist who has been a key figure in the thrash metal scene since the 1980s. He was a founding member of Sodom in 1987 and has continued as their guitarist on multiple tenures, with a break in the 1990s when he joined Kreator. His distinctive playing style and contributions have been central to both bands' sound and influence in European thrash metal.
