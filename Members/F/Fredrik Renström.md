# Fredrik Renström

**Swedish musician.**

Fredrik Renström was a founding member of [[Artists/C/Cult of Luna]] in 1998, part of its original lineup alongside Klas Rydberg, Johannes Persson, Magnus Lindberg, and Erik Olofsson.
