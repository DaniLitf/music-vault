# Frej Larsson

**Rapper, musician, producer. Born September 9, 1983 in Ronneby, Sweden.**

Frej Larsson is a Swedish rapper and musician known for his work with Slagsmålsklubben, Maskinen, and the duo Far & Son (alongside Simon Gärdenfors). He joined Slagsmålsklubben in 2002 after meeting them at the Emmabodafestivalen and co-founded the electro-hip hop group Maskinen in 2007 with Herbert Munkhammar. He also runs the record label Goldenbest Records and has released solo material including the album *Livin La Vida Larsson* (2016) and the autobiography *Fifty Shades of Frej* (2018).
