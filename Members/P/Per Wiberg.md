# Per Wiberg

**Keyboardist. Born 1974-01-23 in Sweden.**

Per Wiberg is a Swedish keyboardist who joined Opeth in 2005 and contributed to *Ghost Reveries* (2005) and *Watershed* (2008) before departing in 2011. He is also known for his work with Spiritual Beggars, the Swedish heavy psych/hard rock band, where he has been a central member since the mid-1990s.
