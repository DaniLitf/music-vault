# Pete Jones

**Drummer. British.**

Pete Jones is an English drummer who joined progressive metal band Haken in 2011, replacing founding drummer Ray Hearne, and recorded several albums with the band before later departing. He also joined the progressive jazz-fusion supergroup Nova Collective as keyboardist in 2014.
