# Phil Wilson

**Bassist, vocalist. British.**

Phil Wilson was a Scottish-born musician, best known as the bassist and vocalist of deathgrind band Gorerotted. Born in Glasgow and raised in Hamilton, he left a year into a Glasgow University degree to pursue music, first forming the band Regorge before relocating to London to join Gorerotted in 2001. He appeared on *A New Dawn for the Dead* (2005), contributing both bass guitar and vocals, and continued into the band's early The Rotted incarnation before departing in 2008; he later worked for London Underground before moving to the US, where he worked stage crew for Rugged Entertainment. Wilson died before the band's 2026 reunion, which was announced in his memory.
