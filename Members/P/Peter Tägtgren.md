# Peter Tägtgren

**Vocalist, multi-instrumentalist, and producer. Born 14 October 1971 in Skara, Sweden.**

Peter Tägtgren is a Swedish musician and record producer who founded the death metal band Hypocrisy in 1991 and later created the solo industrial metal project Pain in 1996. A prolific producer, he has worked with numerous Scandinavian metal bands at his Abyss Studio. He collaborated closely with Till Lindemann to co-write and co-produce *F&M* (2019) as the Lindemann project.
