# Pete Hurley

**Guitarist. British. Died 2014.**

Pete Hurley was a British guitarist and founding member of Extreme Noise Terror, playing on the band's early releases including the debut album *A Holocaust in Your Head*. Prior to ENT, he played in Discharge-influenced acts Freestate and Victims of War alongside Phil Vane.
