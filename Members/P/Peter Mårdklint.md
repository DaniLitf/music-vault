# Peter Mårdklint

**Guitarist. Swedish.**

Peter Mårdklint is a Swedish guitarist who joined [[Artists/A/All Ends]] as second guitarist in August 2005, remaining with the band through its EP, both studio albums, and eventual hiatus.
