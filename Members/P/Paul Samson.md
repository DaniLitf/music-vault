# Paul Samson

**Guitarist and vocalist. Born 4 June 1953. Died 9 August 2002.**

Paul Samson was an English guitarist closely associated with the New Wave of British Heavy Metal, who formed his eponymous band Samson in 1977 with bassist Chris Aylmer and drummer Clive Burr. He continued the band through numerous lineup changes over the following decades and also worked as a record producer and blues musician. He died of cancer in 2002 while recording a new Samson album.
