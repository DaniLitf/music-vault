# Pig Killer

**Drummer. British.**

Pig Killer (later known as Dazzle Monroe) was the original drummer and a founding member of Extreme Noise Terror during their formative years in 1985–1987. He returned briefly in 1993–1995 and later joined The Featherz as Dazzle Monroe.
