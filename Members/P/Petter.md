# Petter

**Rapper. Born 11 April 1974 in Stockholm, Sweden.**

Petter Askergren is a Swedish rapper who became one of the most prominent Swedish-language hip-hop artists of his generation. He broke through with his 1999 debut *Ni måste va' tokiga* and maintained a long career through the 2000s and 2010s.
