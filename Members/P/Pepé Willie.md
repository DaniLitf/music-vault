# Pepé Willie

**Musician, producer, and mentor. Born 22 July 1948 in Brooklyn, New York, USA. Died 1 June 2025 in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

Pepé Willie was an American musician and producer known as the "Godfather of the Minneapolis Sound." He formed the funk band 94 East in 1975 and was the first person to record a teenage Prince in a professional studio. Raised in a musical family in Brooklyn, Willie worked with his uncle Clarence Collins of Little Anthony & the Imperials and later moved to Minneapolis in 1974, where he mentored Prince, Morris Day, and André Cymone. He was one of the earliest architects of the Minneapolis Sound.
