# Peter Tosh

**Peter Tosh was a Jamaican musician and original member of The Wailers alongside Bob Marley and Bunny Wailer, who went on to a significant solo career after leaving the group in 1974.**

Known for his militant stance against oppression and his advocacy for the legalisation of marijuana, Tosh was one of reggae's most uncompromising voices. He was murdered in Kingston, Jamaica in 1987.
