# Peter Tosh

**Jamaican reggae musician. Born 19 October 1944, died 11 September 1987.**

Peter Tosh was a Jamaican reggae musician and a founding member of The Wailers alongside Bob Marley and Bunny Wailer. He pursued a successful solo career after leaving The Wailers in 1974, producing politically charged roots reggae albums like *Legalize It* (1976) and *Equal Rights* (1977). He was murdered during a home invasion in Kingston in 1987.
