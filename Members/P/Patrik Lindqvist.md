# Patrik Lindqvist

**Drums. Active 1993–1996.**

Patrik Lindqvist is a Swedish drummer, formerly of [[Artists/T/Teddybears]] during their early hardcore punk period.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/Teddybears]] – drums (1993–1996)

## Biography
Patrik Lindqvist was a drummer in the grindcore band Skull, which later evolved into Teddybears STHLM. He played in the early incarnation of Teddybears during the transition from hardcore punk. He was one of several drummers who rotated through the band in the 1990s before the lineup stabilized.
