**Drummer, percussionist, and vocalist. Born 30 January 1951.**

Phil Collins is a multi-talented British musician best known as the drummer, percussionist, and eventual lead vocalist of Genesis. He joined Genesis in August 1970 as the drummer and became the band's lead vocalist in 1975 following Peter Gabriel's departure, while continuing to perform drums and percussion on most studio recordings. Collins achieved massive success both with Genesis and as a solo artist, becoming one of the most commercially successful musicians of the 1980s. Between 1982 and 1990, he scored three UK and seven US number-one singles as a solo performer, accumulating more US top-40 singles during that decade than any other artist. A trained drummer since age five and former child actor, Collins balanced his drumming career with Genesis while maintaining his highly successful solo trajectory, making him one of the most prolific and commercially successful recording artists of his era.
