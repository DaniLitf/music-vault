# Paul McCartney

**Paul McCartney is a British musician, singer, and songwriter who co-founded The Beatles and is widely considered one of the most successful songwriters in music history.**

After The Beatles dissolved in 1970, McCartney immediately formed Wings with his wife Linda McCartney and Denny Laine, achieving major commercial success throughout the 1970s. He has maintained an extensive solo career spanning decades.
