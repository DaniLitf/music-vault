# Peter Stjärnvind

**Drummer. Swedish.**

Peter Stjärnvind is a Swedish drummer who was a member of Entombed from 1997 to 2009, performing on *DCLXVI: To Ride, Shoot Straight and Speak the Truth*, *Same Difference*, *Uprising*, *Morning Star*, *Inferno*, and *Serpent Saints: The Ten Amendments*. He was replaced by Olle Dahlstedt in 2009.
