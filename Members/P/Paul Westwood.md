# Paul Westwood

**Drummer. British.**

Paul Westwood is a London-based drummer who joined progressive metal band To-Mera in July 2006, replacing founding drummer Akos Pirisi, and remained with the band through their final album *Exile* (2012). He has also drummed for the black metal band Fen and was an early member of De Profundis before leaving to focus on To-Mera and Skaldic Curse.
