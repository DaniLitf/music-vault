# Penny Ford

**Singer-songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, record producer. Born June 11, 1964.**

Penny Ford is an American singer-songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, and record producer from Cincinnati, Ohio, who rose to international fame as the lead singer of the Eurodance group Snap! on their debut album *World Power* (1990), which featured the hits "The Power" and "Ooops Up". She has also been a member of Soul II Soul and Klymaxx, and worked as a backing vocalist for The Gap Band early in her career.
