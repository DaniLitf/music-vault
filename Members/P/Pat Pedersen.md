# Pat Pedersen

**Bassist. American.**

Pat Pedersen is an American bassist who replaced [[Members/D/Daniel House]] in [[Artists/S/Skin Yard]] in March 1991, joining during the tour supporting *1000 Smiling Knuckles*. He remained with the band for the recording of their final album, *Inside the Eye* (1993).

## Associated Artists
