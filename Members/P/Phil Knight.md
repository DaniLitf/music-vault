# Phil Knight

**Keyboardist, electronic musician. British.**

Phil Knight, also known as The Silverman, is a core member of [[Artists/T/The Legendary Pink Dots]] and one of the band's long-running keyboard/electronics architects. He retired from touring in 2022, though his exact post-2022 studio status has not been formally clarified in all sources.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Legendary Pink Dots]] – keyboards, electronics as The Silverman (1980–2022)
- [[Artists/M/Mimir]] – keyboards/electronics
