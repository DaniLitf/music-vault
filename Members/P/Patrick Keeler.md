# Patrick Keeler

**Drums. American. Founding member of [[Artists/T/The Greenhornes]].**

Patrick Keeler co-founded The Greenhornes in Cincinnati in 1996. In the mid-2000s he and bandmate Jack Lawrence backed Loretta Lynn on *Van Lear Rose* (2004) and co-founded [[Artists/T/The Raconteurs]] with Jack White and Brendan Benson, which curtailed Greenhornes activity for several years.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Greenhornes]] – drums, percussion (1996–2012, founder)
- [[Artists/T/The Raconteurs]] – drums, percussion, backing vocals (2005–present)
- [[Artists/J/Jack White]] – drums (2024 No Name band)
