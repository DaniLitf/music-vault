# Puma Larsson

**Bass. Swedish.**

Puma Larsson is a Swedish bassist who was an early member of the Malmö hardcore punk band Black Uniforms from 1981 to 1983, playing on their formative releases before the band went on hiatus. He later played in The Bristles.
