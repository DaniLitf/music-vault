# Per Hägglund

**Saxophone, keyboards. Swedish. Founding member of [[Artists/I/Imperiet]].**

Per Hägglund joined Imperiet as saxophonist and keyboardist at its 1983 formation, remaining until his departure in 1987, shortly before the band disbanded.
