# Phoenix

**Bassist. Born 1977-02-08.**

Phoenix (born David Michael Farrell) is an American musician best known as the bassist and backing vocalist for the multi-platinum rock band [[Artists/L/Linkin Park|Linkin Park]]. He has been a member since the band's earliest incarnation as Xero, though a brief touring commitment with [[Artists/T/Tasty Snax|Tasty Snax]] caused him to miss the recording of *Hybrid Theory*. He rejoined in time for the album's release and has appeared on every subsequent Linkin Park album.

Farrell grew up in Mission Viejo, California, and attended UCLA, where he roomed with future bandmate Brad Delson. His stage name "Phoenix" reportedly comes from a tattoo on his back. He maintains a lower public profile than his bandmates but has contributed to songwriting and backing vocals across all Linkin Park albums.
