# Phil Vane

**Vocalist. British. Died 17 February 2011.**

Phil Vane was a British vocalist and founding member of Extreme Noise Terror, where he shared vocal duties with Dean Jones from 1985 until his death in 2011. He briefly joined Napalm Death in 1996, leading to a temporary trade of vocalists with Mark Greenway, and later returned to ENT until his passing from a cerebrovascular accident at age 46.
