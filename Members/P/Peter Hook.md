# Peter Hook

**Peter Hook (born February 13, 1956, in Salford, England) is a British bassist and vocalist, best known as a founding member of Joy Division and New Order, and for his distinctive high-register bass-playing style.**

Hook co-founded Joy Division in 1976 and played bass on their two landmark studio albums. After Ian Curtis's death, he continued as bassist in New Order, which became one of the most influential post-punk and dance bands of the 1980s. He departed New Order acrimoniously in 2007 and has since toured Joy Division and New Order material with his band The Light.
