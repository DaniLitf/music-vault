# Paul Mario Day

**Lead vocals (1975–1976; died 2025).**

Paul Mario Day was an English singer best known as the original lead vocalist of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – lead vocals (1975–1976)

## Biography

Day was Iron Maiden's first vocalist, fronting the band in its earliest days. He sang on the band's first demo recordings before leaving in 1976. He later fronted the NWOBHM band More and passed away in 2025.
