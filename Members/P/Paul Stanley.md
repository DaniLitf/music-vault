# Paul Stanley

**Rhythm guitarist, co-lead vocalist. Born January 20, 1952.**

Paul Stanley is an American musician, singer, and songwriter, best known as the rhythm guitarist, co-lead vocalist, and co-founder of KISS. As "The Starchild", he provided many of the band's biggest hits. He also leads Paul Stanley's Soul Station and has a solo career.
