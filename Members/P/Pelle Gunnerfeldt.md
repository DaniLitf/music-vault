# Pelle Gunnerfeldt

**Guitarist, producer. Swedish.**

Pelle Gunnerfeldt is a Swedish guitarist and record producer, best known as the guitarist of the post-hardcore/alternative rock band Fireside, which formed in Luleå in the early 1990s. With Fireside, he has released six studio albums spanning post-hardcore, emo, and garage rock styles. As a producer, he has worked with Entombed on *Inferno* (2003) and other acts, bringing a dynamic approach shaped by his background in alternative and punk rock.
