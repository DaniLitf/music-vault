# Pete Mittler

**Bass guitarist.**

Pete Mittler, also known as Sensitive Pete, is an American bass guitarist from Chicago, Illinois. He has spent decades in the city's punk scene, starting on guitar in [[Artists/T/The Methadones]] before switching to bass, and going on to play bass in [[Artists/T/The Bomb]], [[Artists/N/Naked Raygun]] (joining in 2010 after founding bassist Pierre Kezdy's stroke), [[Artists/T/The Bollweevils]], and [[Artists/E/Explode and Make Up]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/E/Explode and Make Up]] – bass (c. 2006–2010)
- [[Artists/T/The Methadones]] – guitar, later bass (1993–2010)
- [[Artists/T/The Bomb]] – bass (2003/2004–present)
- [[Artists/N/Naked Raygun]] – bass (2010–2014)
- [[Artists/T/The Bollweevils]] – bass
