# Patrik Arve

**Vocals, guitar. Active 1991–present.**

Patrik Arve is a Swedish musician, best known as the vocalist and guitarist of [[Artists/T/Teddybears]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/Teddybears]]

## Biography
Patrik Arve co-founded Teddybears (originally named Skull, then Teddybears STHLM) in 1991, initially as a hardcore punk vocalist before the band evolved into their eclectic rock/electronica sound. Alongside Joakim Åhlund, he is one of only two members to appear on every Teddybears release, from *You Are Teddybears* (1993) through *Rock On!* (2016). Arve is also responsible for the band's distinctive visual identity, including the signature bear heads worn in performances and promotional material.
