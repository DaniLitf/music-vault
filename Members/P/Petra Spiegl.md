# Petra Spiegl

**Female vocalist. German.**

Petra Spiegl is a German singer who was the female vocalist for the Eurodance project Captain Hollywood Project from 1995 to 1996. She replaced Nina Gerhard and sang on the singles "Flying High," "Find Another Way," and "The Way Love Is," as well as the album *The Afterparty* (1996), before the project went on hiatus.
