# Paul Geary

**Drums. American. Founding member of [[Artists/E/Extreme]].**

Paul Geary co-founded Extreme with Gary Cherone in 1985, having played together in earlier Boston bands Adrenalin and The Dream. He left the band in 1994 to pursue artist management (later managing Extreme itself), returning briefly for reunion dates and as a special guest.

## Associated Artists
