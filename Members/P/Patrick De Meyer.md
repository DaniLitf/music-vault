**Keyboardist, programmer, and record producer. Belgian.**

Patrick De Meyer is a Belgian musician and producer who contributed keyboards, synthesizer work, and programming to [[Artists/T/Technotronic|Technotronic]]'s debut album *Pump Up the Jam* (1989) and later co-produced and programmed on the *Recall* album (1995). He was also a member of Belgian electronic act T99.

His synthesizer work helped define Technotronic's signature house sound, though he received limited credits on early singles due to the studio-based nature of the project.
