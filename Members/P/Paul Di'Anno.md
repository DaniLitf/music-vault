# Paul Di'Anno

**Lead vocals (1978–1981; died 2024). Born May 17, 1958 — died October 21, 2024.**

Paul Di'Anno was an English singer best known as the original lead vocalist of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – lead vocals (1978–1981)

## Biography

Di'Anno sang on Iron Maiden's first two albums — *Iron Maiden* (1980) and *Killers* (1981) — helping establish the band's early sound with his raw, punk-influenced vocal style. He was replaced by Bruce Dickinson in 1981. He continued performing and recording with various projects until his death in 2024 at age 66.
