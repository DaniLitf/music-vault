# Promoe

**Rapper. Born 1976-04-28.**

Nils Mårten Ed, known as Promoe, is a Swedish rapper who co-founded hip-hop group Looptroop with Embee in 1991 while attending school together in Västerås. He has also pursued a prolific solo career, including *Government Music* (2001), *White Man's Burden* (2006), and the Swedish-language *Kråksången* (2009), which topped the charts on the strength of single "Svennebanan." He follows a vegan, straight edge lifestyle.
