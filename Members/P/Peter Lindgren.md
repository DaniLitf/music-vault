# Peter Lindgren

**Guitarist. Born 1974-05-04 in Sweden.**

Peter Lindgren was a founding guitarist of Opeth, playing on all of the band's albums from *Orchid* (1995) through *Ghost Reveries* (2005) before departing in 2007. His dual-guitar work alongside Mikael Åkerfeldt was central to Opeth's early and mid-period sound. He left the band for personal reasons and did not pursue a prominent career in music afterward.
