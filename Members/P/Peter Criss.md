# Peter Criss

**Drummer, co-vocalist. Born December 20, 1945.**

Peter Criss is an American musician, best known as the original drummer and co-vocalist of KISS. As "The Catman", he sang lead on the band's first Top 10 hit "Beth". He has also led his own solo projects and bands.
