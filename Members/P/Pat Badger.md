# Pat Badger

**Bass, backing vocals. American. Member of [[Artists/E/Extreme]].**

Pat Badger, previously of the Berklee-based act In the Pink, joined Extreme in 1986 and has remained the band's bassist through all of its active periods.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/E/Extreme]] – bass, backing vocals (1986–1996, 2004, 2006–present)
