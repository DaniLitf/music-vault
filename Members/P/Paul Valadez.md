# Paul Valadez

**Bassist. Born October 30, 1959.**

Paul Valadez was an American bassist and a founding member of the alternative rock band Adam Again. He previously played with Gene Eugene and Greg Lawless in the band Martus, which eventually evolved into Adam Again. His grooving, melodic basslines anchored the band's funk-infused alternative rock sound from their inception in 1982 until their disbandment in 2000. Valadez died on September 20, 2013, at age 53, from complications of diabetes.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/A/Adam Again]] – bass (1982–2000)

## Biography

Paul Valadez was born on October 30, 1959. He met Gene Eugene and Greg Lawless in the early 1980s, and together they formed the band Martus, which performed in the Huntington Beach church scene. This group eventually became Adam Again. Valadez played bass on every Adam Again album, providing the rhythmic foundation that defined the band's groove-heavy sound. After Adam Again ended, Valadez served as a worship leader in churches across California and Idaho. He died in Post Falls, Idaho on September 20, 2013.
