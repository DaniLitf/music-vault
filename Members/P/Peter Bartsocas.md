# Peter Bartsocas

**Guitarist, bassist, engineer.**

Peter Bartsocas is an American guitarist and recording engineer from South Florida who played bass in [[Artists/M/Morning Again]] before quitting in October 1996 to join [[Artists/B/Bird of Ill Omen]] on guitar, staying through the band's dissolution and returning for its 2012 reunion; he also formed the one-show side-project Wishes for World War III in 1998. He served several stints in [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]]: touring guitarist in mid-1998, fill-in bassist during the band's turbulent European tour of 2000, and guitarist again in 2015, and later engineered James Glayat's guitar tracks for the *Up from the Muck* EP (2020) at Tall Pine in Brooklyn, New York.
