# Pete Nash

**Bassist, vocalist. British.**

Pete Nash is a British bassist and vocalist who played for both Doom and Extreme Noise Terror. He was the bassist for Doom from 1987 to 1990 and again in 1992, appearing on the classic debut album *War Crimes (Inhuman Beings)*. He also played bass for Extreme Noise Terror in 1988–1990.
