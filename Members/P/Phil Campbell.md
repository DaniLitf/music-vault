# Phil Campbell

**Welsh guitarist. Born 7 May 1961. Died 13 March 2026.**

Phil Campbell was a Welsh guitarist from Pontypridd who joined Motörhead in February 1984 alongside fellow guitarist Michael "Würzel" Burston, debuting on *Orgasmatron* (1986), and became the band's sole guitarist from 1995 until Lemmy Kilmister's death in 2015. He then formed Phil Campbell and the Bastard Sons with his sons. He died in March 2026 after a long illness following a major operation.
