# Paul Pellegrin

**Bassist. American.**

Paul Pellegrin is an American musician who played bass in the band Fluffy (2) alongside Chris Colbert. Through the reissue of Fluffy's final album as Duraluxe's first release, Pellegrin's performances appeared on the Duraluxe release *Rock Music W/Singing*.
