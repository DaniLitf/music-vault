# Paul Woodfield

**Guitarist. British.**

Paul "Woody" Woodfield was the rhythm guitarist for Extreme Noise Terror, serving from 2001 to 2010 and again from 2011 to 2014. He also took over lead guitar duties for studio and live work in early 2001.
