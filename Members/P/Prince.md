# Prince

**Singer, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist. Born 7 June 1958 in Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA. Died 21 April 2016 in Chanhassen, Minnesota.**

Prince Rogers Nelson was an American musician who was one of the most influential figures in popular music. He taught himself to play over two dozen instruments, wrote and produced the majority of his output, and released dozens of albums over a career spanning four decades. His 1984 film and soundtrack *Purple Rain* made him a global superstar. He died of an accidental fentanyl overdose in 2016.
