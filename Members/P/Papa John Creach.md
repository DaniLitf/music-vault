# Papa John Creach

**John "Papa John" Creach** (May 28, 1917 – February 22, 1994) was an American violinist best known for his work with [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]] and [[Artists/J/Jefferson Starship]].

- [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]] – violin, vocals (1970–1972)
- [[Artists/J/Jefferson Starship]] – violin (1974–1975)
- [[Artists/H/Hot Tuna]] – violin (1971–1977)

Creach joined Jefferson Airplane in 1970 at age 53, adding a unique violin element to the band's sound. He was a longtime collaborator with [[Members/J/Jorma Kaukonen]] and [[Members/J/Jack Casady]] in Hot Tuna.
