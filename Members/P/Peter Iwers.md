# Peter Iwers

**Bass (1998–2016).**

Peter Iwers is a Swedish bassist best known for his long tenure as the bassist of [[Artists/I/In Flames]] and his work in [[Artists/T/The Halo Effect]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/In Flames]] – bass (1998–2016)
- [[Artists/T/The Halo Effect]] – bass, founding member

## Biography

Iwers replaced his brother Anders as In Flames' bassist in 1998, playing on every album from *Colony* (1999) through *Battles* (2016). He left the band in 2016 and later co-founded The Halo Effect, a melodic death metal supergroup alongside other former In Flames members.
