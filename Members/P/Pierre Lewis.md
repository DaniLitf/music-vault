# Pierre Lewis

**Keyboardist. Born in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

Pierre Lewis is an American keyboardist who played in the original lineup of the Minneapolis funk band 94 East, performing on the band's early recordings alongside Pepé Willie, Wendell Thomas, and Dale Alexander.
