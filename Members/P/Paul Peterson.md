# Paul Peterson

**Keyboardist and vocalist.**

Paul Peterson, also known as St. Paul Peterson, is an American keyboardist who joined The Time in 1983 as a replacement for Monte Moir, appearing in the film *Purple Rain* and on the *Ice Cream Castle* album. After the Time's initial breakup, he was a member of the short-lived project The Family alongside Jerome Benton and Jellybean Johnson.
