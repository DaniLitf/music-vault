# Perry Farrell

**Vocals, guitar, keyboards. American. Frontman of [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]].**

Perry Farrell is best known as the lead vocalist of Jane's Addiction, and founded the annual Lollapalooza music festival in 1991, originally conceived as a farewell tour for the band. After Jane's Addiction's 2004 breakup he formed the alternative rock/electronica project [[Artists/S/Satellite Party]] with guitarist Nuno Bettencourt, and has also fronted [[Artists/P/Porno for Pyros]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]] – lead vocals, guitar, keyboards, programming (1985–2024)
- [[Artists/P/Porno for Pyros]] – lead vocals (1992–1998, 2009, 2022–present)
- [[Artists/S/Satellite Party]] – lead vocals (2004–2008, founder)
