# Peter Straus

**Bassist, songwriter. American.**

Peter Straus, credited in [[Artists/T/The Dwarves]] as Salt Peter, is a former Dwarves bassist. He is also credited in Dwarves-related accounts as a writer of "Doing the Sponge," later sung by Blag Dahlia for *SpongeBob SquarePants*.
