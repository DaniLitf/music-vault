# Pete DiStefano

**Guitarist. American.**

Pete DiStefano is an American guitarist who co-founded [[Artists/P/Porno for Pyros]] with Perry Farrell and Stephen Perkins in 1992 after the breakup of Jane's Addiction, becoming a key songwriter for the band across both of its 1990s albums. After the band's split he battled heroin addiction and testicular cancer before returning to music with the project Venice Underground and session work on film scores.
