# Phil Anselmo

**Vocalist. Born 1968-06-30 in New Orleans, Louisiana, USA.**

Phil Anselmo is the vocalist of Pantera, one of the most influential and controversial figures in heavy metal. He joined Pantera in 1987 and fronted the band through their commercial breakthrough with *Cowboys from Hell* (1990) and their most acclaimed records through *Reinventing the Steel* (2000). He is also the founder and vocalist of Down, the New Orleans-based sludge/doom supergroup formed in 1991, as well as the frontman of Superjoint Ritual and numerous other extreme metal projects. His powerful, versatile vocal delivery defined the groove metal era.
