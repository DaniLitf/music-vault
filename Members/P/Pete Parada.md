# Pete Parada

**Drummer. American.**

Pete Parada is a drummer from Arkport, New York, who briefly played drums for [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio]] as a touring replacement in 2001. He is best known for his long tenure in [[Artists/T/The Offspring]] (2007–2021), the band's second-longest-serving drummer, and for his earlier work in [[Artists/F/Face to Face]] and Saves the Day. He was dismissed from The Offspring in 2021 after declining a COVID-19 vaccine on his doctor's advice.
