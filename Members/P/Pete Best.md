# Pete Best

**Pete Best is a British musician who was the original drummer of The Beatles from 1960 to 1962, famously replaced by Ringo Starr shortly before the band achieved worldwide fame.**

Best played on the band's early Hamburg recordings and Decca audition tapes. After his dismissal he pursued a solo career and other projects, and has spoken publicly about his experience in the band.
