# Peter Christopherson

**Peter "Sleazy" Christopherson was a British musician, visual artist, and music video director, co-founder of Coil alongside John Balance, and formerly a member of industrial pioneers Throbbing Gristle.**

He directed music videos for many major artists and was a founding figure of the industrial music movement through his work with Throbbing Gristle (1975–1981) and Coil (1982–2004). He died in his sleep in 2010.
