# Pierre-Marie Maulini

**Guitarist.**

Pierre-Marie Maulini is a French guitarist who played in [[Artists/M/M83]]'s live band during the 2000s, in the era following Nicolas Fromageau's departure. He is listed among the project's former live members on Wikipedia.
