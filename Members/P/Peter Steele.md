# Peter Steele

**Peter Steele was an American musician and the founder, lead vocalist, and bassist of Type O Negative, whose imposing physical presence (6'8") matched his band's massive, glacially slow gothic metal sound.**

Steele had previously fronted the hardcore band Carnivore before forming Type O Negative in 1989. He died of heart failure on April 14, 2010, effectively ending the band.
