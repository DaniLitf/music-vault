# Pete Townshend

**Pete Townshend is a British musician and co-founder of The Who, whose songwriting — encompassing rock operas (*Tommy*, *Quadrophenia*), anthems, and introspective solo work — established him as one of rock's most important composers.**

Townshend's windmilling guitar style and tendency to destroy instruments became iconic rock imagery. He has also written books and maintains an active career outside of The Who.
