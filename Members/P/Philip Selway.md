# Philip Selway

**Drummer. Born 1967-05-23 in Hemingford Grey, England.**

Philip Selway is the drummer and a founding member of Radiohead. His precise, intricate drumming has been central to the band's rhythm across all their stylistic phases, from straightforward rock to the polyrhythmic complexity of *Kid A* and *In Rainbows*. He has released two solo albums of folk-influenced music.
