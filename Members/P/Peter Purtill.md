# Peter Purtill

**Guitarist. British.**

Peter "Pooch" Purtill (also spelled Pyrtle) was the guitarist for Discharge from 1982 to 1984, replacing Anthony "Bones" Roberts. He introduced heavier metal elements to the band's sound and played on the *Warning* EP and singles "The Price of Silence" and "The More I See".
