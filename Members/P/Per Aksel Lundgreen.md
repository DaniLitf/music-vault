# Per Aksel Lundgreen

**Norwegian keyboardist, former member of Apoptygma Berzerk.**

Per Aksel Lundgreen played keyboards for Apoptygma Berzerk from 1990 to 1994, contributing to the early development of the band's sound before the release of their debut album *Soli Deo Gloria*.
