# Paul Lemos

**American musician and producer. Born 1960.**

Paul Lemos is an American musician and producer best known as the founder, vocalist, and multi-instrumentalist of the experimental/industrial band Controlled Bleeding. His work spans industrial, noise, dark ambient, and avant-garde music.
