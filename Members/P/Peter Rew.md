# Peter Rew

**Guitarist. Born 28 September 1967.**

Peter "Rewy" Rew (born Rewinski) is a founding member and lead guitarist of Benediction, contributing to the band's signature death metal sound since 1989. He is also the primary songwriter alongside Darren Brookes and has been the band's longest-serving member.
