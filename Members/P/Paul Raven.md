# Paul Raven

**Bassist. Born 16 January 1961 in Wolverhampton, England. Died 20 October 2007.**

Paul Raven was the bassist for Killing Joke and also played with Ministry, Godflesh, and Prong. He performed on Pigface recordings and was a key bridge between the industrial and post-punk scenes.
