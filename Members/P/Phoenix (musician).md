# Phoenix (musician)

**Bassist. Born February 8, 1977.**

Dave Farrell, known by his stage name Phoenix, is an American bassist best known as the bassist of Linkin Park. He joined the band in 1999, replacing original bassist Dave "Joe" Farrell (no relation), and has appeared on every Linkin Park album since *Meteora* (2003). Before joining Linkin Park, he played in the band Tasty Snax.
