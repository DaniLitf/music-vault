# Paul Barker

**Bassist, producer. Born 1960-02-08 in San Francisco, California, USA.**

Paul Barker is an American musician and producer who was the longtime bassist of Ministry from 1986 to 2003, co-producing many of their most acclaimed albums alongside Al Jourgensen. He was also a founding member of Revolting Cocks and a frequent contributor to Pigface. His heavy, distorted bass sound helped define the industrial metal genre.
