# Poison Ivy

**Guitarist. American. Born 1953.**

Poison Ivy is the guitarist and co-founder of the psychobilly band [[Artists/T/The Cramps]], whose surf rock-influenced guitar style was a defining element of the band's sound. She formed the band with Lux Interior in 1976 and has remained the sole continuous musical force behind the band.
