# Dead

**Swedish vocalist. Born 16 January 1969, died 8 April 1991.**

Per Yngve Ohlin, known as Dead, was a Swedish vocalist best known as the lead vocalist of Mayhem (1988–1991). He was known for his nihilistic stage persona and his self-inflicted stage injuries. He committed suicide in 1991, an event that became notorious in black metal history.
