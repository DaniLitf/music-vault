**Lead vocalist and founding member. Born 13 February 1950.**

Peter Gabriel is an English musician best known for his eight-year tenure as the lead vocalist and principal lyricist of Genesis (1969–1975) and his highly successful solo career. During his time with Genesis, he performed on critically acclaimed progressive rock albums including Nursery Cryme (1971), Foxtrot (1972), and Selling England by the Pound (1973). Gabriel left Genesis in 1975 to pursue his own artistic vision, launching a solo career that achieved international success, particularly with his self-titled albums and his fifth studio album "So" (1986), which became certified triple platinum in the UK and five times platinum in the US. A multiple award winner, Gabriel has been inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame both as a member of Genesis and as a solo artist.
