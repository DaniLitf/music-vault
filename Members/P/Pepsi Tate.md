# Pepsi Tate

**Bassist. Born 1965-03-10, died 2007-09-18.**

Pepsi Tate (born Huw Justin Smith), nicknamed "The Boy," was a Welsh musician and founding bassist of glam metal band Tigertailz, playing on all of the band's classic-era albums. He died of pancreatic cancer in September 2007, weeks after marrying Welsh opera singer Shân Cothi, having pursued a second career as a TV producer/director for BBC Wales after leaving the band.
