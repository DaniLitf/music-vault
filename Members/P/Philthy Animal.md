# Philthy Animal

**British drummer. Born 21 September 1954, died 12 November 2015.**

Phil Taylor, known as Philthy Animal, was an English drummer best known as the drummer of Motörhead (1975–1984, 1987–1992). He died in 2015, the same year as Lemmy.
