# Peter Buck

**Guitarist. Born 1956-12-06 in Oakland, California, USA.**

Peter Buck is the guitarist and co-founder of R.E.M., whose arpeggiated, jangly playing style became one of the defining sounds of American indie rock in the 1980s. He moved to Athens, Georgia, where he worked at a record store and co-founded R.E.M. with Michael Stipe. After R.E.M. dissolved in 2011, he has remained active in various collaborations and projects.
