# Peter Baumann

**Keyboardist, composer. German. Born 1953.**

Peter Baumann is a German electronic musician best known as a member of [[Artists/T/Tangerine Dream]] from 1971 to 1977, contributing to seminal albums such as *Zeit*, *Phaedra*, and *Rubycon*. After leaving the group, he founded his own record label, Private Music, and released several solo electronic albums.
