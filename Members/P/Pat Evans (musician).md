# Pat Evans (musician)

**Drummer. British.**

Pat Evans, known by the stage names Devilman and Fastman, is a British punk drummer who played for Chaos UK in two stints: 1992 to 1995 and again briefly in 1997. He performed on *100% Two Fingers in the Air Punk Rock* and *Floggin' the Corpse*.
