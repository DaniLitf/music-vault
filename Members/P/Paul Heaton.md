# Paul Heaton

**Lead vocalist, songwriter. Born 1962.**

Paul Heaton is an English singer-songwriter, best known as the frontman of [[Artists/T/The Housemartins]] (1983–1988) and [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]] (1988–2007). Known for his witty, socially observant lyrics and distinctive vocal style.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Housemartins]] – vocals, harmonica, guitar, trombone (1983–1988)
- [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]] – vocals, co-founder (1988–2007)

## Biography
Heaton formed The Housemartins while at university in Hull. After the band's breakup, he co-founded The Beautiful South with fellow Housemartins member Dave Hemingway. Following The Beautiful South's dissolution in 2007, Heaton has continued as a solo artist and collaborator.
