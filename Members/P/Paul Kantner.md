# Paul Kantner

**Paul Kantner (March 12, 1941 – January 28, 2016) was an American guitarist and vocalist, a co-founding member of Jefferson Airplane and the primary architect of its evolution into Jefferson Starship.**

Kantner was one of the main songwriters of Jefferson Airplane, shaping the band's politically charged and psychedelic direction on albums like *Volunteers* (1969). He was virtually the only continuous member from Jefferson Airplane through Jefferson Starship and into Starship, keeping the band's identity alive for nearly five decades. He died on January 28, 2016, the same day as original Airplane vocalist Signe Toly Anderson.
