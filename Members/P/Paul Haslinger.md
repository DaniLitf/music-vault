# Paul Haslinger

**Keyboardist, composer. Austrian. Born 1962.**

Paul Haslinger is an Austrian electronic musician and composer who was a member of [[Artists/T/Tangerine Dream]] from 1986 to 1990. After leaving the band, he moved to Los Angeles and established a successful career as a film and television composer, scoring works such as *Underworld* and *Fear the Walking Dead*.
