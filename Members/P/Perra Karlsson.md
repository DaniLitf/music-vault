# Perra Karlsson

**Drummer. Swedish.**

Perra Karlsson was the drummer of Benediction from 2011 to 2013, joining as a replacement for Nick Barker. He was one of several drummers who filled the position between Neil Hutton's departure and the arrival of Giovanni Durst.
