# Phil Caivano

**American guitarist and producer.**

Phil Caivano is an American guitarist who co-founded the thrash band Blitzspeer in the late 1980s and played bass for hardcore band Murphy's Law before joining Monster Magnet in 1998, the same year as their breakout album *Powertrip*. He has also produced albums for Agnostic Front, Hatebreed, and L7, and released a solo album in the 2020s.
