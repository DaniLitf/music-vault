# Peter Cetera

**Peter Cetera is an American bassist and vocalist who was a founding member of Chicago from 1967 to 1985, and subsequently had a successful solo career with hits including "Glory of Love."**

His tenor vocals became the sound of Chicago's soft rock era in the late 1970s and early 1980s.
