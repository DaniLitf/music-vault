# Pat Mastelotto

**American drummer. Born 14 September 1955.**

Pat Mastelotto is an American drummer best known for his work with progressive rock band King Crimson (1994–present) and as a session drummer for various rock and pop artists. He is known for his powerful, technically proficient drumming style.
