# PJ Harvey

**Singer, songwriter, and musician. Born 9 October 1969 in Bridport, Dorset, England.**

Polly Jean Harvey is a British musician who records and performs as PJ Harvey. She debuted in 1992 with *Dry* and has become one of the most critically acclaimed British artists of her generation, winning the Mercury Prize twice — for *Stories from the City, Stories from the Sea* (2001) and *Let England Shake* (2011).
