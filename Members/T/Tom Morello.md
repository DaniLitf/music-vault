# Tom Morello

**Guitarist. Born 1964-05-30 in Harlem, New York City, USA.**

Tom Morello is the guitarist of Rage Against the Machine, one of the most innovative and politically committed guitarists in rock. His technique — combining traditional riffing with DJ-style scratching and unusual effects — helped define RATM's sound. After the band's first split he co-founded Audioslave with the remaining RATM members and Chris Cornell. He has also recorded as The Nightwatchman (political folk) and co-founded Prophets of Rage in 2016.
