# Terry Wapram

**Guitars (1977–1978).**

Terry Wapram is an English guitarist best known as a former guitarist of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – guitars (1977–1978)

## Biography

Wapram played guitar in Iron Maiden during the Dennis Wilcock era, contributing to the band's developing sound in the late 1970s. He left in 1978.
