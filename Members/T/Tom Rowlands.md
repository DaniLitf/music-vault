# Tom Rowlands

**Electronic musician, producer. Born 1971-01-11.**

Tom Rowlands is a British electronic musician and producer best known as one half of [[Artists/T/The Chemical Brothers]]. In 2025 he formed [[Artists/T/Tomora]] with [[Members/A/Aurora Aksnes]] after prior collaborations on Chemical Brothers and AURORA material.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Chemical Brothers]] – production, programming (1989–present)
- [[Artists/T/Tomora]] – production, member (2025–present)
- [[Artists/A/AURORA]] – production collaboration (2024)
