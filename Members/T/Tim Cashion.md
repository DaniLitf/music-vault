# Tim Cashion

**Keyboardist. American.**

Tim Cashion is an American keyboardist best known as the longtime keyboardist for Grand Funk Railroad, joining the band in 2000 and remaining through the present. He has also performed with Bob Seger, Robert Palmer, and the Silver Bullet Band.
