# Tinna Karlsdotter

**Vocalist. Swedish.**

Tinna Karlsdotter is a Swedish singer, one of the two founding vocalists of the Gothenburg alternative metal duet act [[Artists/A/All Ends]], alongside Emma Gelotte. She remained with the band throughout its active years from 2003 until its hiatus.
