# Tyler Bates

**American guitarist and composer. Born 5 June 1965.**

Tyler Bates is an American guitarist, composer, and record producer best known as the guitarist of Marilyn Manson (2014–present) and for his prolific work as a film and television composer, including scores for *300*, *Guardians of the Galaxy*, and *John Wick*.
