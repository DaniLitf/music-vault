# Taneli Jarva

**Bassist. Finnish.**

Taneli Jarva played bass for Impaled Nazarene from 1992 to 1996, contributing to the band's development during their early to mid-1990s period. He is also known as the vocalist and bassist for Sentenced and The Black League, making him a notable figure in Finnish metal.
