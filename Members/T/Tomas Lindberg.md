# Tomas Lindberg

**Vocals. Born September 16, 1973. Died September 16, 2025.**

Tomas Lindberg was a Swedish vocalist best known as the frontman of [[Artists/A/At the Gates]], whose aggressive, precise delivery became a template for melodic death metal vocals. He also sang for [[Artists/C/Ceremonial Oath]] and fronted crust punk band [[Artists/D/Disfear]] from 1998 until his death. Lindberg died of cancer on his 52nd birthday.
