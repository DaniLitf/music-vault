# Tomas Lindberg

**Vocals. Born September 16, 1973.**

Tomas Lindberg is a Swedish vocalist best known as the frontman of [[Artists/A/At the Gates]], whose aggressive, precise delivery became a template for melodic death metal vocals. He also sang for [[Artists/C/Ceremonial Oath]] in the mid-1990s alongside his main band.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/A/At the Gates]] – lead vocals (1990–1996, 2007–present)
- [[Artists/C/Ceremonial Oath]] – vocals (1993–1995)
