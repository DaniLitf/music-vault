# Tim Smith

**Drummer. Born ?.**

Tim Smith is an American drummer who was a member of the power pop band Jellyfish. He played drums on the band's debut album *Bellybutton* (1990) and their follow-up *Spilt Milk* (1993), contributing to the band's lush, Beatle-influenced arrangements.
