# Thomas Josefsson

**Bass. Swedish.**

Thomas Josefsson played bass for [[Artists/C/Cemetary]] from 1993 to 1997, appearing on the band's gothic-metal-era albums *Godless Beauty*, *Black Vanity*, *Sundown*, and *Last Confessions*.
