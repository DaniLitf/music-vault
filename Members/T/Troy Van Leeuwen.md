# Troy Van Leeuwen

**Guitarist, multi-instrumentalist. Born 1968-09-10 in California, USA.**

Troy Van Leeuwen is an American guitarist who has been a member of Queens of the Stone Age since 2002, contributing guitar, lap steel, and keyboards. Before QOTSA he was a member of A Perfect Circle (touring) and has filled in for other bands. His versatile, textured playing has been part of every QOTSA album since *Songs for the Deaf*.
