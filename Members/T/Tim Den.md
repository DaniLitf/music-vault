# Tim Den

**Session drummer.**

Tim Den is an American drummer from the Boston, Massachusetts area. He was brought in as a session musician for Chicago's [[Artists/E/Explode and Make Up]], recording the drum tracks on the band's self-titled 2009 EP even though bandmate [[Members/M/Mike Pinaud]] was the group's officially credited drummer at the time.
