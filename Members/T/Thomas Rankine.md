# Thomas Rankine

**Bassist.**

Thomas Rankine is an American bass guitarist from Miami, Florida, who founded [[Artists/B/Bird of Ill Omen]] in 1996 after stints in Burning Strong and Stonecast, leading the band through its 1996–1998 run and 2012 reunion and fronting its successor project Dead Mens' Theory. He later replaced Guillermo Amador in [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] in April 2001, playing on *Won* (2001) and *A Young Trophy Band in the Parlance of Our Times* (2002), and stayed with the band through its final 2002 tours and the transition to the name Salem.
