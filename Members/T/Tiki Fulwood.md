# Tiki Fulwood

**Drums, vocals. American. Original drummer of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Tiki Fulwood was Funkadelic's original drummer, quitting in 1971 but reappearing on several Parliament-Funkadelic releases through the rest of the decade. He also worked briefly with Miles Davis before dying of cancer in 1979.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]] – drums, vocals (1968–1971, then sporadic)
