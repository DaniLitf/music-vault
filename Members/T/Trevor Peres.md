# Trevor Peres

**Guitarist / vocalist. Born 1966 in Tampa, Florida.**

Trevor Peres is a founding member and rhythm guitarist of the death metal band Obituary, with whom he has performed since 1984. In the Meathook Seed side project, Peres took on vocal duties (his only recorded vocal performance), bringing a raw, aggressive delivery to the debut album *Embedded* (1993). He is also known for his work with the bands Catastrophic and Death In June.
