# Trey Many

**Drummer and talent agent. American.**

Trey Many is an American drummer who played for Starflyer 59 from 2006 to 2019, appearing on albums including *My Island*, *Dial M*, and *Slow*. He also performed with Velour 100, His Name Is Alive, Liquorice, and Lo Tom. Outside of music, he founded Aero Booking and later worked as a talent agent at Paradigm.
