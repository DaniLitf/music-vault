# Tony Pines

**Drums. American.**

Tony Pines is an American musician who played drums for the metalcore band Integrity in two stints, from 1988 to 1991 and again from 2002 to 2003. He is also known by the stage name "Chubby Fresh" and was the band's original drummer.
