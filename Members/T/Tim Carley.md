# Tim Carley

**Guitarist. British.**

Tim Carley, known by his stage name Fluffy, is a British guitarist and founding member of the deathgrind band Gorerotted. He played on all three Gorerotted studio albums and continued with the band through its name change to The Rotted. He is the only member to have appeared on every Gorerotted and The Rotted release.
