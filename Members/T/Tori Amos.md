# Tori Amos

**Myra Ellen Amos, known as Tori Amos, is an American singer-songwriter and pianist born on August 22, 1963 in Newton, North Carolina.**

Amos trained as a classical pianist from childhood and moved to Los Angeles in the 1980s before breaking through with her debut album *Little Earthquakes* in 1992. Her confessional style, drawing on personal trauma including sexual assault, made her a significant figure in alternative rock and art pop. She has released over a dozen studio albums and remains one of the most critically acclaimed artists of her generation.
