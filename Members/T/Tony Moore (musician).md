# Tony Moore

**Keyboards (1977).**

Tony Moore is an English keyboardist best known as the former keyboardist of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – keyboards (1977)

## Biography

Moore was Iron Maiden's keyboardist for a brief period in 1977, making him the only keyboardist to have been a full member of the band. The experiment was short-lived and the band returned to a guitar-driven sound.
