# Troy Daugherty

**Guitarist and vocalist. American.**

Troy Daugherty is an American musician best known as the co-founder, guitarist, and vocalist of the alternative rock band Duraluxe. He previously played guitar in the New Zealand Christian rock band Hoi Polloi, appearing on their 1994 recordings. After forming Duraluxe in 1997 with Chris Colbert, Daugherty co-wrote and co-produced the band's albums *Dolorosa* (1999) and *The Suitcase* (2002), establishing a warm, psychedelic-tinged sound.
