# Tom Waits

**Thomas Alan Waits, known as Tom Waits, is an American singer-songwriter, musician, composer, and actor born on December 7, 1949 in Pomona, California.**

Waits began his career as a troubadour in the early 1970s before radically transforming his sound in the 1980s toward experimental, percussive music drawing on blues, jazz, cabaret, and avant-garde influences. His 1985 album *Rain Dogs* is often cited as one of the greatest albums of all time. He has also appeared in numerous films as an actor, directed by Jim Jarmusch, Francis Ford Coppola, and others.
