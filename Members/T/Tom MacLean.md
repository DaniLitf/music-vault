# Tom MacLean

**Guitarist and multi-instrumentalist. British.**

Tom MacLean is an English progressive metal multi-instrumentalist, taught classical guitar at age 10 before switching to electric at 14. He co-founded and served as guitarist and main songwriter for To-Mera from 2004 to 2012, and separately joined Haken as bassist in 2007, recording three albums with them before leaving in 2013 to focus on session and production work.
