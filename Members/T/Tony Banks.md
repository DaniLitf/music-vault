**Keyboardist and founding member. Born 27 March 1950.**

Tony Banks is the keyboardist and one of the principal songwriters and lyricists of Genesis, co-founding the band in 1967 while studying at Charterhouse. He remained a constant member throughout the band's entire history until their final reunion tour in 2022, serving as a keyboardist and primary composer alongside Mike Rutherford. Banks is renowned for his mastery of the Hammond T-102 organ, Mellotron, and Yamaha CP-70 piano, instruments that helped define the Genesis sound. Beyond Genesis, he pursued a prolific solo career spanning progressive rock, pop, and classical music.
