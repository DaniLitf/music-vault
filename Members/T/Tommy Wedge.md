# Tommy Wedge

**Bassist. American.**

Tommy Wedge was the original bassist for Duraluxe, playing on their debut album *Dolorosa* (1999) and contributing vocals to several tracks. He was a core member of the band's early line-up alongside Chris Colbert, Troy Daugherty, and Fred McKay. After Duraluxe relocated to Athens, Georgia, Wedge left the band and was eventually replaced by Jason 71.
