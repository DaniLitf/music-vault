# Tony Barsotti

**Drums. American.**

Tony Barsotti is an American drummer best known as the longtime drummer of the Texas punk band [[Artists/H/Hagfish|Hagfish]], joining in 1993 for the recording of their debut album *Buick Men* and remaining with the band through their original run until 2001 and their subsequent sporadic reunions. He contributed studio drums to [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio|Alkaline Trio]] in 2020. After Hagfish's official breakup in 2001, Barsotti transitioned to furniture design and custom artwork for high-end clients, while occasionally reuniting with Hagfish for live performances.
