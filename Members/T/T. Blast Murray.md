# T. Blast Murray

**Guitar. American.**

T. "Blast" Murray is an American session and touring guitarist who played guitar on every track of [[Artists/T/The Heads]]' *No Talking, Just Head* (1996) and co-wrote the album's songs with the band. He has also worked as a session and touring musician with Lee "Scratch" Perry, Terence Trent D'Arby, Tom Tom Club, and Eurythmics, and later released solo material as an "ambient noise funk" producer.
