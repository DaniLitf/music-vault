# Thunderstick

**Drummer.**

Barry Graham Purkis, known as Thunderstick, is an English drummer known for wearing a leather mask and performing inside a metal cage on stage. He replaced Clive Burr in Samson at the end of 1978, drumming on the album *Survivors* (1979), and remained with the band until 1981, later reuniting with Paul Samson and Chris Aylmer for a Samson revival project in the early 1990s.
