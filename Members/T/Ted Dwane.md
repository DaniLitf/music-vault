# Ted Dwane

**British musician. Born 15 August 1984.**

Ted Dwane played bass in the experimental folk band Moulettes before co-founding Mumford & Sons in December 2007. He plays double bass, bass guitar, drums, and guitar, and in 2013 underwent emergency treatment for an acute subdural hematoma suffered onstage.
