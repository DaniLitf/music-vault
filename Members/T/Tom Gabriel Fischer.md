# Tom Gabriel Fischer

**Guitarist and vocalist. Swiss.**

Tom Gabriel Fischer, widely known as Tom G. Warrior, co-founded the extreme metal band Hellhammer in 1982, which he and Martin Eric Ain rebranded as Celtic Frost in 1984. Regarded as one of the most influential figures in extreme metal, he later founded Triptykon following Celtic Frost's final breakup in 2008.
