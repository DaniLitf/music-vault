# Ted Key

**Bassist, vocalist. British.**

Ted Key is a British musician best known as an early bassist in [[Artists/T/The Housemartins]]. He joined from the Hull band The Gargoyles and left after the band's first John Peel session and debut single period.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Housemartins]] – bass, vocals (1984–1985)
