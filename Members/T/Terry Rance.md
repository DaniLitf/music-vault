# Terry Rance

**Guitars (1975–1976).**

Terry Rance is an English guitarist best known as one of the original guitarists of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – guitars (1975–1976)

## Biography

Rance was one of Iron Maiden's first two guitarists alongside Dave Sullivan, playing in the band's earliest incarnation. He left in 1976 and was replaced by Dave Murray.
