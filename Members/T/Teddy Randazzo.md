# Teddy Randazzo

**Songwriter, singer, arranger, and record producer. Born 13 May 1935 in Brooklyn, New York, USA. Died 21 November 2003 in Orlando, Florida.**

Teddy Randazzo was an American songwriter and producer who composed classic hits such as "Goin' Out of My Head," "It's Gonna Take a Miracle," "Pretty Blue Eyes," and "Hurt So Bad." He began his career in the Three Chuckles, appeared in Alan Freed movies, and later mentored a young Pepé Willie. He contributed mellotron to recordings by the Minneapolis funk band 94 East. Randazzo was inducted into the Songwriters Hall of Fame in 2007.
