# Till Lindemann

**Vocalist, lyricist. Born 1963-01-04.**

Till Lindemann is a German vocalist, lyricist, and performer best known as the frontman of [[Artists/R/Rammstein]]. He co-founded [[Artists/L/Lindemann (band)]] with Peter Tägtgren and continued releasing music under his own name after the duo ended.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/R/Rammstein]] – lead vocals (1994–present)
- [[Artists/L/Lindemann (band)]] – vocals (2014–2020)

## Biography

Lindemann's theatrical vocal delivery and lyrics helped define Rammstein's Neue Deutsche Härte style. His side project with Peter Tägtgren explored industrial metal in English and German across *Skills in Pills* and *F & M*.
