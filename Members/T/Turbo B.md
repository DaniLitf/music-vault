# Turbo B

**Rapper. Born April 30, 1967.**

Turbo B (born Durron Maurice Butler) is an American rapper and beatboxer from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, best known as the frontman of the German Eurodance group Snap!. He performed on the international hits "The Power" (1990) and "Rhythm Is a Dancer" (1992) before leaving the group due to creative differences. After Snap!, he formed the group Centory and pursued solo projects.
