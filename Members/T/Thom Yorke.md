# Thom Yorke

**Vocalist, multi-instrumentalist. Born 1968-10-07 in Wellingborough, England.**

Thom Yorke is the lead vocalist, guitarist, and primary lyricist of Radiohead, one of the most acclaimed and influential musicians of the past three decades. His falsetto-to-baritone vocal range and emotionally intense delivery are central to Radiohead's sound across all their stylistic phases. He has also pursued a prolific solo career (including *The Eraser*, *Tomorrow's Modern Boxes*, and *ANIMA*) and co-founded the electronic-rock group Atoms for Peace with Flea of Red Hot Chili Peppers.
