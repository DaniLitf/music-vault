# Travis Barker

**Drums, producer. Born 1975-11-14.**

Travis Barker is an American drummer and record producer, best known as the drummer of [[Artists/B/Blink-182]] since 1998, when he left [[Artists/T/The Aquabats]] to replace Scott Raynor — famously learning the 20-song setlist in 45 minutes before his first show. His marching-band-schooled, genre-crossing style (hip hop, reggae, Afro-Cuban) diversified Blink-182's sound, and he became a prolific collaborator across punk and hip hop, playing in [[Artists/B/Box Car Racer]], [[Artists/T/Transplants]], and [[Artists/0-9/+44]]. He survived a 2008 plane crash that killed four people, and has since built a major career as a producer, including producing Blink-182's *One More Time...* (2023) — see [[Producers/T/Travis Barker]].
