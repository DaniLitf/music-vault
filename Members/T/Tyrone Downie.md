# Tyrone Downie

**Tyrone Downie was a Jamaican keyboard player and backing vocalist who was a member of Bob Marley and the Wailers from 1974 until Marley's death in 1981.**

He played on landmark albums including *Natty Dread*, *Rastaman Vibration*, *Exodus*, and *Uprising*, and continued performing with Wailers lineups after Marley's passing. He died in 2017.
