# Titus Jany

**Drummer. German.**

Titus Jany drummed for [[Artists/T/The Inchtabokatables]] under stage names including Kokolorus Mitnichten and Dr. Tinitus Banani. After the band went on hiatus, he played with Potentia Animi and later with Robert Beckmann in Grüßaugust.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Inchtabokatables]] – drums as Kokolorus Mitnichten / Dr. Tinitus Banani (1991–2002)
