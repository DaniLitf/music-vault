# Twiggy Ramirez

**American bassist. Born 20 June 1971.**

Jeordie White, known by his stage name Twiggy Ramirez, is an American bassist best known for his work with Marilyn Manson (1993–2002, 2008–2017). He was a key contributor to the band's most successful albums including *Antichrist Superstar* and *Mechanical Animals*.
