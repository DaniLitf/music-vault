# Teemu Raimoranta

**Guitarist. Finnish.**

Teemu Raimoranta, also known as "Somnium," played guitar for Impaled Nazarene from 2000 to 2003 and also played guitar for Finntroll. He tragically died on March 16, 2003, after falling from a bridge, cutting short his contributions to the extreme metal scene.
