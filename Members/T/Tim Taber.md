# Tim Taber

**Vocalist, drummer. American.**

Tim Taber is an American musician who was the lead vocalist and drummer of The Prayer Chain, originally playing drums alongside using drum machines before Wayne Everett joined on drums. After The Prayer Chain disbanded, he founded Floodgate Records and Transparent Productions, working with bands including Cool Hand Luke and The Myriad.
