# Tim Skold

**Guitar, vocals, production. Swedish Born 1966-12-14.**

Tim Skold is a Swedish musician best known for his work with KMFDM and as a member of Marilyn Manson's band. He also played bass in the Scandinavian heavy metal supergroup Doctor Midnight & The Mercy Cult from 2009 to 2011.
