# Trent Reznor

**Singer, songwriter, producer, composer. Born 17 May 1964 in Mercer, Pennsylvania, USA.**

Trent Reznor is an American musician, singer, songwriter, and composer best known as the founder and frontman of Nine Inch Nails. He contributed early vocals and guitar to Pigface's debut album *Gub* (1990) and has gone on to win multiple Grammy and Academy Awards for his film scores with Atticus Ross. His work has defined the sound of industrial rock for multiple generations.
