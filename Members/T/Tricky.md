# Tricky

**Adrian Thaws, known as Tricky, is a British musician, producer, and actor born on January 27, 1968 in Knowle West, Bristol, England.**

Tricky first came to attention as an associate of Massive Attack, contributing to their debut album *Blue Lines*. His 1995 solo debut *Maxinquaye* is widely regarded as a defining trip-hop album, featuring vocalist Martina. He has since released over a dozen albums, continuing to work across electronic, hip-hop, and rock genres.
