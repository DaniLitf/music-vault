# Tony M.

**Rapper, dancer. Born in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

Tony M. was a rapper and dancer in Prince's New Power Generation from its inception. He served as lead vocalist on the NPG's debut album *Gold Nigga* and co-wrote several songs including "Sexy MF." He left Prince's band alongside the other dancers in 1993.
