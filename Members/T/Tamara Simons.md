**Bassist for Goon.**

Tamara Simons is an American bassist currently performing with the Los Angeles-based indie rock band Goon. She provides the bass foundation for the band's introspective alternative rock sound, contributing to their acclaimed album Dream 3 (2025). Simons is part of Goon's core ensemble lineup since the project's expansion from Kenny Becker's solo work into a full band.
