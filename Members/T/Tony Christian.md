# Tony Christian

**Rhythm guitarist, keyboardist, backing vocalist. American.**

Tony Christian, born Bruce DeShazer in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, was the rhythm guitarist, keyboardist, and backing vocalist of Mazarati. He began his musical journey at age 10, singing and playing drums and organ in his father's church, before moving to Minneapolis at 19 to join Mazarati under the alias Tony Christian. After Mazarati's disbandment in 1989, he formed the musical duo The Wild Pair with fellow member Marr Starr (Marvin Gunn), and together they provided the voice of MC Skat Kat on Paula Abdul's No. 1 hit "Opposites Attract." He continued to participate in Mazarati reunion performances, including the 2009 Prince Family Reunion concert in Minneapolis.
