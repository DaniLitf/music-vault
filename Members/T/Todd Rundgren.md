# Todd Rundgren

**Musician, singer-songwriter, record producer. Born June 22, 1948.**

Todd Rundgren is an American musician, singer-songwriter, and record producer known for his eclectic style spanning pop, rock, progressive rock, and electronic music. He rose to fame as a member of The Nazz before launching a prolific solo career with hits including "Hello It's Me" and "Bang the Drum All Day." As a producer, he helmed landmark albums including Grand Funk Railroad's *We're an American Band* and *Shinin' On*, as well as works for Meat Loaf, XTC, and Patti Smith.
