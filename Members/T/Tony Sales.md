# Tony Sales

**Bassist. American.**

Tony Fox Sales is an American bassist, son of entertainer Soupy Sales, who backed David Bowie and Iggy Pop on the 1977 tour for *The Idiot* alongside his brother Hunt Sales before the pair co-founded [[Artists/T/Tin Machine]] with Bowie and Reeves Gabrels in 1988.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/Tin Machine]] – bass, vocals (1988–1992)
- [[Artists/I/Iggy Pop]] – touring bass (1977)
