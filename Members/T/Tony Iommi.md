# Tony Iommi

**Tony Iommi is a British guitarist and co-founder of Black Sabbath, widely regarded as the inventor of heavy metal guitar, whose downtuned, blues-based riffs — played despite losing two fingertips on his right hand in a factory accident — defined the genre's sonic template.**
