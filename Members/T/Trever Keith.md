# Trever Keith

**Vocalist, guitarist. American.**

Trever Keith is the founding member and primary vocalist of the Southern California punk rock band [[Artists/F/Face to Face]], formed in 1991. He has been the band's main songwriter and frontman throughout their career, including during their 2004 breakup and subsequent 2008 reunion. Beyond Face to Face, Keith founded Antagonist Records, which released the band's albums *Laugh Now, Laugh Later* (2011) and *Three Chords and a Half Truth* (2013).
