# Thea Austin

**Singer-songwriter. Born June 10, 1960.**

Thea Tereese Austin is an American singer-songwriter and composer from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, best known as the lead vocalist and co-writer of Snap!'s international hit "Rhythm Is a Dancer" (1992). She wrote or co-wrote all songs on Snap!'s second album *The Madman's Return* (1992). She later formed the duo Soulsearcher, which had a UK Top 10 hit with "Can't Get Enough" (1999).
