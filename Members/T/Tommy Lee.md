# Tommy Lee

**Drummer. Born October 3, 1962.**

Tommy Lee is an American drummer and founding member of Mötley Crüe, one of the most influential glam metal bands of the 1980s. Known for his energetic performances and later ventures into electronic music and film acting, he has remained a prominent figure in rock music for over four decades.
