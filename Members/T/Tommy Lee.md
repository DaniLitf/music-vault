# Tommy Lee

**Drummer. Born October 3, 1962.**

Tommy Lee is an American drummer and co-founder of Mötley Crüe, one of the most successful and influential glam metal bands of the 1980s. Known for his energetic performances, innovative drum solos with spinning and suspended kits, and versatile artistic output spanning heavy metal, rap metal, electronic dance music, and solo work, he has remained a prominent figure in rock and electronic music for over four decades.

## Biography

Tommy Lee began playing drums at age five and joined the Sunset Strip band Suite 19 in the late 1970s, where he met bassist Nikki Sixx. In 1981, he co-founded Mötley Crüe at age 17 (after dropping out of high school) alongside Sixx, Mick Mars, and Vince Neil. During his tenure with Mötley Crüe, Lee became known for his theatrical live performances featuring innovative drum kit designs that spun and suspended him above the crowd. The band released a string of platinum albums throughout the 1980s, including *Shout at the Devil* (1983), *Theatre of Pain* (1985), *Girls, Girls, Girls* (1987), and the commercially dominant *Dr. Feelgood* (1989).

In 1999, Lee left Mötley Crüe to pursue solo and side projects. He formed Methods of Mayhem with producer DJ Eazy E (later DJ Aero), exploring rap metal and electronic music. He released multiple solo albums, including *Tommyland: The Ride* (2005) and *Andro* (2020), experimenting across genres from country-pop to EDM. Guest appearances include drums on The Smashing Pumpkins' *Monuments to an Elegy* (2014) and drums on Post Malone's "Over Now" (2018). Lee rejoined Mötley Crüe in 2004 and participated in their 2014 farewell tour, with a legal agreement preventing any member from touring under the Mötley Crüe name without all members' participation.
