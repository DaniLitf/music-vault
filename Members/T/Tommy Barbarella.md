# Tommy Barbarella

**Keyboardist. Born in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

Tommy Barbarella is an American keyboardist who joined Prince's New Power Generation for the 1990 Nude Tour. He played on *Diamonds and Pearls* and *Love Symbol Album* and co-wrote several songs with Prince. After leaving Prince's band in 1996 he played with Nick Jonas and the Administration.
