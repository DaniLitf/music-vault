# Tracii Guns

**Guitarist. Born January 20, 1966.**

Tracii Guns is an American guitarist best known as the co-founder of L.A. Guns and a founding member of Guns N' Roses. He was also a member of the supergroups Brides of Destruction and Contraband, and has played with numerous other acts throughout his career.
