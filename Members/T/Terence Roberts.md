# Terence Roberts

**Vocalist, drummer, guitarist. British.**

Terence "Tezz" Roberts is a British punk musician and a founding member of Discharge. He initially served as vocalist in 1977 before switching to drums (1977–1980), then later played guitar from 2014 onward. After leaving Discharge in the early 1980s, he formed Broken Bones with his brother Anthony. He rejoined Discharge in 2001.
