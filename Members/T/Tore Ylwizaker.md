# Tore Ylwizaker

**Composer, keyboards, electronics. Norwegian. Core member of [[Artists/U/Ulver]].**

Tore Ylwizaker was invited into Ulver in 1997 by Kristoffer Rygg, and together the pair steered the band away from black metal into experimental electronic, ambient, and art rock territory, beginning with *Themes from William Blake's The Marriage of Heaven and Hell* (1998). He has remained one of the band's two constant members ever since.
