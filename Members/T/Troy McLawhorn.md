# Troy McLawhorn

**Guitar. American. Former member of [[Artists/S/Seether]]; member of Evanescence.**

Troy McLawhorn (previously of Dark New Day and doubleDrive) joined Seether as a touring guitarist in February 2008 and was soon made an official member, before departing in 2011 to rejoin Evanescence.
