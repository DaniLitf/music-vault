# Tina Weymouth

**Bass, keyboards, backing vocals. Born November 22, 1950.**

Tina Weymouth is an American musician, best known as the bassist for [[Artists/T/Talking Heads]] and co-founder of [[Artists/T/Tom Tom Club]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/Talking Heads]]
- [[Artists/T/Tom Tom Club]]

## Biography
Weymouth co-founded Talking Heads with her husband Chris Frantz and David Byrne. Her melodic, rhythmic bass playing was a signature element of the band's sound. With Frantz she formed Tom Tom Club in 1981.
