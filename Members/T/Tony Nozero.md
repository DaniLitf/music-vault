# Tony Nozero

**Tony Nozero is an American guitarist and co-founder of the New Orleans experimental trio Drums & Tuba.**

Nozero provides guitar for Drums & Tuba, complementing the tuba and drums lineup with textured, effects-heavy playing drawn from post-rock and experimental traditions.
