# Tony Lombardo

**Bass, vocals. American.**

Tony Lombardo is an American bassist who joined the earliest lineup of [[Artists/D/Descendents]] in 1979 and whose hard-driving melodic bass lines defined the band's classic sound on *Milo Goes to College* (1982). He sang "It's a Hectic World" on the band's 1980 debut single and, during the mid-80s hiatus, performed with Ray Cooper as the Ascendents. He returned as a guest on *Everything Sucks* (1996) and played on the 2002 sessions released as *9th & Walnut* (2021).

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/D/Descendents]] – bass (1979–1983, 1985, 1996, 2002), vocals (1979–1980, 2002)
- [[Artists/A/All]] – bass, co-writer on *New Girl, Old Story* (1991, credited as TonyAll)
