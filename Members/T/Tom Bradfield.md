# Tom Bradfield

**Musician. British.**

Tom Bradfield is an English musician who has been part of brutal death metal band Infected Disarray, formed in 2000 as a side project of members from the UK death metal underground, appearing on the band's 2009 album *Redisseminating Obscenity*.
