# Thurston Moore

**Guitarist, vocalist. Born 1958-07-25 in Coral Gables, Florida, USA.**

Thurston Moore is the co-founding guitarist and vocalist of Sonic Youth, known for pioneering the use of alternate guitar tunings and extended techniques in rock music. Along with Lee Ranaldo and Kim Gordon, he developed the noise rock aesthetic that defined Sonic Youth's sound. His marriage to Kim Gordon and subsequent separation in 2011 led to the band's indefinite hiatus. He has remained prolific as a solo artist and collaborator.
