# Tuomo Louhio

**Guitarist. Finnish.**

Tuomo Louhio, also known as "Tuomio," played guitar for Impaled Nazarene from 2003 to 2007, following the death of Teemu Raimoranta. His guitar work continued the band's extreme metal legacy during this period.
