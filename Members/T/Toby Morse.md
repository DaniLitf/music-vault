**Vocalist and straight-edge hardcore punk icon. Born April 8, 1970.**

Toby Morse is the founding member and lead vocalist of the New York hardcore band H2O, a cornerstone of the straight-edge movement since 1994. Beginning his involvement in the punk scene as a roadie for Gorilla Biscuits, Killing Time, and Sick of It All in the late 1980s, Morse has been a central figure in NYHC for over three decades. In 2009, he founded the nonprofit organization "One Life, One Chance" to inspire young people to make healthy life choices and maintain a positive mental attitude (PMA), extending his straight-edge philosophy beyond music into activism.
