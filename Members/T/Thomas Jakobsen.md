# Thomas Jakobsen

**Norwegian drummer, former member of Apoptygma Berzerk.**

Thomas Jakobsen played drums for Apoptygma Berzerk from 2009 to 2011, serving as the band's drummer during the transition between the *Rocket Science* and *Exit Popularity Contest* eras.
