# Terry Lewis

**Bassist, songwriter, and record producer. Born 1956-11-21.**

Terry Lewis is an American musician and record producer best known as one half of the production duo Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis. He was a founding member of The Time, playing bass from 1981 to 1983 before being fired alongside Jimmy Jam. Together with Jam, he produced numerous landmark albums for Janet Jackson, including *Control* and *Rhythm Nation 1814*, and earned multiple Grammy Awards. He has periodically reunited with The Time for anniversary shows.
