# Tupac Shakur

**Rapper, actor. Born June 16, 1971. Died September 13, 1996.**

Tupac Shakur was an American rapper and actor born in East Harlem, New York City, to Black Panther Party members Afeni Shakur and Billy Garland. After starting out as a dancer and roadie for Digital Underground, he became one of the best-selling and most influential artists in hip-hop history, with more than 75 million records sold and a Diamond-certified double album, *All Eyez on Me* (1996). He was shot four times in a drive-by shooting in Paradise, Nevada, on September 7, 1996, and died six days later; he was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2017.

## Biography

Raised by his mother Afeni Shakur, Tupac relocated to the San Francisco Bay Area in 1988, where he joined Digital Underground before launching his solo career with *2Pacalypse Now* (1991). His music combined political and social commentary with gangsta rap, and albums such as *Me Against the World* (1995) and *All Eyez on Me* (1996) topped the Billboard 200. After serving prison time in 1995 he signed to Suge Knight's Death Row Records, becoming a central figure in the East Coast–West Coast rivalry and a high-profile feud with the Notorious B.I.G. He was also a successful actor, starring in *Juice* (1992), *Poetic Justice* (1993), *Above the Rim* (1994), *Gridlock'd* (1997), and *Gang Related* (1997).
