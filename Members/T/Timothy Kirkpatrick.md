# Timothy Kirkpatrick

**Drummer.**

Timothy Kirkpatrick is an American drummer who first joined [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] in September 1997 and played on the band's classic 1998–2000 releases before returning for its 2008 reunion. He was also a member of the Florida hardcore band [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]], with whom he toured Europe in 1998.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] – drums (1997–2000, 2008–present)
- [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]] – drums (1997–1998)
