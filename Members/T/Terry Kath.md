# Terry Kath

**Terry Kath was an American guitarist and co-founder of Chicago, widely regarded as one of the most talented guitarists of his era — Jimi Hendrix reportedly told him "you're better than me" — who died in a tragic accidental self-inflicted gunshot in 1978.**

His bluesy, versatile playing defined Chicago's early hard rock and jazz-rock sound before his untimely death at age 31.
