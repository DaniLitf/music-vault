# Thomas Öberg

**Thomas Öberg is a Swedish vocalist and co-founder of bob hund, one of Sweden's most beloved cult indie rock bands.**

He has been the face and voice of bob hund since their formation in Malmö in 1989, known for his distinctive delivery and cryptic Swedish-language lyrics that have resonated deeply with the band's devoted following.
