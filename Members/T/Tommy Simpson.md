# Tommy "Bonehead" Simpson

**Bassist. American.**

Tommy "Bonehead" Simpson is an American bassist, formerly of Crisis Party, who was a founding member of Love Battery in Seattle in 1989. He left the band by mid-1990, replaced by ex-U-Men bassist Jim Tillman, before later rejoining the band.
