# Tom Araya

**Bassist, vocalist. Born 1961-06-06 in Viña del Mar, Chile.**

Tom Araya is the bassist and vocalist of Slayer, one of thrash metal's most iconic frontmen. His screaming vocal style and precision bass playing were central to Slayer's sound across their entire career from *Show No Mercy* (1983) through *Repentless* (2015). He is also notable for being a practicing Catholic who has spoken publicly about his faith despite Slayer's dark subject matter.
