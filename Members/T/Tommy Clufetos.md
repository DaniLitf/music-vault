# Tommy Clufetos

**Drummer. American.**

Tommy Clufetos is an American drummer who has played with Rob Zombie, Ozzy Osbourne, and Black Sabbath. He has performed with The Dead Daisies across multiple stints including 2015 touring, 2021–2022, and 2024 to present.
