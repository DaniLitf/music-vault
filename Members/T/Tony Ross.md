# Tony Ross

**Guitarist. American.**

Tony Ross is an American guitarist who played in the Cleveland thrash/power metal band Purgatory from 1986 to 1990, replacing Greg Perry shortly before the band's 1990 split. He rejoined for the 2024 reunion run.
