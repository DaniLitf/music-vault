# Tommy Church

**Guitarist. American.**

Tommy Church is an American guitarist who played in the Cleveland thrash/power metal band Purgatory during their 2018–2019 reunion. He is best known as a former guitarist for Mushroomhead, having joined the band in 2012 and appearing on *The Righteous & The Butterfly* (2014) before exiting in 2018.
