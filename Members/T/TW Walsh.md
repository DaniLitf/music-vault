# TW Walsh

**Mastering engineer and musician. American.**

TW Walsh is an American mastering engineer and musician who has worked extensively in the indie rock world. He provided mastering and synthesizer contributions on Starflyer 59 albums *IAMACEO* (2013) and *Young in My Head* (2019). He also played guitar with Pedro the Lion and is a member of Lo Tom and The Soft Drugs.
