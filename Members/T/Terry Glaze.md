# Terry Glaze

**Vocalist, guitarist. Born 1966-02-26 in Texas, USA.**

Terry Glaze was the original vocalist of Pantera, fronting the band during their early glam metal years from 1981 to 1988. He appeared on the band's first three independent albums — *Metal Magic* (1983), *Projects in the Jungle* (1984), and *I Am the Night* (1985) — before departing and being replaced by Phil Anselmo, who arrived just as the band was transitioning to a heavier groove metal sound.
