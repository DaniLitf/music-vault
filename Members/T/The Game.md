# The Game

**Rapper. Born 1979-11-29.**

The Game is an American rapper from Compton, California, placed into G-Unit in late 2003 by Dr. Dre and Jimmy Iovine to promote him ahead of his Aftermath debut. His album *The Documentary* (2005), executive produced by Dr. Dre and 50 Cent, was a massive success, but a bitter feud with 50 Cent led to his ousting from the group in February 2005. He went on to a long solo career and years of diss-track exchanges with his former groupmates.
