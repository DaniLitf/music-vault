# Tim Kelleher

**Bass, keyboards (touring). Born October 4, 1980.**

Tim Kelleher is an American musician from Phoenix, Arizona and a founding member of the metal band My Darling Murder. He joined [[Artists/0-9/30 Seconds to Mars|30 Seconds to Mars]] as touring bassist in 2007, following Matt Wachter's departure, also contributing keyboards and occasional rhythm guitar on stage while studio bass duties were handled by Jared Leto and Tomo Miličević. Kelleher left at the end of 2010 to refocus on My Darling Murder, and later played in Filter from 2013 to 2015.
