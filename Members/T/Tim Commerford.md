# Tim Commerford

**Bassist. Born 1968-02-26 in Irvine, California, USA.**

Tim Commerford is the bassist of Rage Against the Machine, childhood friends with Zack de la Rocha and a founding member of the band. His heavy, aggressive bass style has been a cornerstone of RATM's sound. After the band's initial breakup he co-founded Audioslave with Tom Morello, Brad Wilk, and Chris Cornell, and later joined Prophets of Rage.
