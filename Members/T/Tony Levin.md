# Tony Levin

**American bassist and Chapman Stick player. Born 6 June 1946.**

Tony Levin is an American bassist and Chapman Stick player best known for his work with progressive rock band King Crimson (1981–present) and Peter Gabriel. He is known for his distinctive bass style and pioneering use of the Chapman Stick.
