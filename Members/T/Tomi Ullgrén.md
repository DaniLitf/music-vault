# Tomi Ullgrén

**Guitarist. Finnish.**

Tomi Ullgrén, also known as "UG," played guitar for Impaled Nazarene from 2007 to 2022, making him one of the band's longer-serving guitarists. His 15-year tenure saw him contribute to multiple albums and live performances with the extreme metal group.
