# Tom Schmitz

**Keyboardist, samples. American.**

Tom Schmitz, known professionally as Shmotz, is an American musician best known as a founding keyboardist and sampler of the industrial metal band Mushroomhead, where he provided keyboards and sampled sounds from the band's formation in 1993 until his departure in 2015. His work on samples and keyboards shaped Mushroomhead's signature industrial and experimental sound across all studio albums from their 1995 self-titled debut through *The Righteous & the Butterfly* (2014).
