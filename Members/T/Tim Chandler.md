# Tim Chandler

**Bassist. Born July 26, 1958.**

Tim Chandler was an American bassist best known for his long-standing work with Daniel Amos and The Swirling Eddies. A key figure in the Christian alternative rock scene since the late 1970s, his melodic bass lines helped define the sound of both bands over several decades.
