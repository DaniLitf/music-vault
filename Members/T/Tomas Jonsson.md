# Tomas Jonsson

**Vocals, bass. Swedish.**

Tomas "Freke" Jonsson is a Swedish musician best known as the vocalist and founding member of the pioneering D-beat band Anti Cimex. He originally played bass in the band's 1981 formation and switched to vocals in 1982, remaining as the band's frontman until their breakup in 1993. He later fronted the Swedish crust punk band Wolfpack and was previously a member of Shitlickers and Moment Maniacs.
