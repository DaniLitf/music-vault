# Thorsten Quaeschning

**Keyboardist, composer. German.**

Thorsten Quaeschning is the longest-serving member of [[Artists/T/Tangerine Dream]] after founder Edgar Froese. He joined the band in 2005 as an electronic musician and composer, and following Froese's death in 2015, has continued to lead the ensemble, releasing multiple albums under the Tangerine Dream name.
