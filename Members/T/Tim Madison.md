# Tim Madison

**Drummer, vocalist, author. Born 1967-02-26.**

Tim Madison, better known as Vadge Moore, is an American musician and author best known as a 1990s drummer for [[Artists/T/The Dwarves]]. After leaving the band he moved into noise and industrial music with Chthonic Force and later wrote memoirs and occult/esoteric prose.
