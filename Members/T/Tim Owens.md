# Tim "Ripper" Owens

**Vocalist. Born September 13, 1967.**

Tim "Ripper" Owens is an American vocalist who served as the lead singer of Judas Priest from 1996 to 2003, replacing Rob Halford. Before joining Judas Priest, he sang for Winter's Bane and later went on to perform with Iced Earth, Yngwie Malmsteen, and Charred Walls of the Damned.
