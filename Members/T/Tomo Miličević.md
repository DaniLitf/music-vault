# Tomo Miličević

**Lead guitar, violin, keyboards. Born September 3, 1979.**

Tomo Miličević is a Bosnian-born American multi-instrumentalist who grew up in Troy, Michigan after his family emigrated from Sarajevo to escape the Bosnian War. Classically trained on violin before taking up guitar, he joined [[Artists/0-9/30 Seconds to Mars|30 Seconds to Mars]] as lead guitarist in 2003, replacing Solon Bixler, and remained with the band for fifteen years — recording bass, violin, and keyboard parts alongside guitar across *A Beautiful Lie*, *This Is War*, *Love, Lust, Faith and Dreams*, and *America* — before amicably departing in 2018.
