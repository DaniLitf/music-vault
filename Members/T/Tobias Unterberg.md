# Tobias Unterberg

**Cellist. German.**

Tobias Unterberg performed as B. Deutung in [[Artists/T/The Inchtabokatables]], providing cello in the band's classical-instrument rock lineup. He later toured with Deine Lakaien and [[Artists/S/Subway to Sally]], and played cello on [[Artists/N/New Model Army]]'s album *Carnival*.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Inchtabokatables]] – cello as B. Deutung (1991–2002)
- [[Artists/S/Subway to Sally]] – touring cello
- [[Artists/N/New Model Army]] – cello on *Carnival*
