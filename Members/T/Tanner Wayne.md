# Tanner Wayne

**Drums (2018–2025).**

Tanner Wayne is an American drummer best known as the former drummer of [[Artists/I/In Flames]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/In Flames]] – drums (2018–2025)

## Biography

Wayne joined In Flames in 2018, replacing Joe Rickard, and appeared on *I, the Mask* (2019) and *Foregone* (2023). He left the band in May 2025 and was replaced by Jon Rice.
