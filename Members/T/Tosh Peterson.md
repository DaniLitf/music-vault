# Tosh Peterson

**Drummer. American.**

Tosh Peterson is a drummer from Los Angeles who built a career as a touring and session musician before joining [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio]] in March 2026, replacing Atom Willard. A frequent hired-gun drummer since his teens, he has toured and recorded with Lady Gaga, Troye Sivan, [[Artists/F/Filter]], Fall Out Boy, and Machine Gun Kelly, and was Lady Gaga's touring drummer immediately prior to joining Alkaline Trio.
