# Tommy Funderburk

**Backing vocals. American.**

Tommy Funderburk is an American session vocalist who has sung backing vocals for a long list of rock and pop acts, and fronted the AOR projects Airplay and King of Hearts. He contributed backing vocals to five tracks on the [[Artists/C/Coverdale-Page]] album (1993).
