# Ted Skogmann

**Norwegian drummer, longtime member of Apoptygma Berzerk.**

Ted Skogmann has been the core drummer for Apoptygma Berzerk across two periods (1999–2002 and 2011–present), also contributing additional guitar. He has performed on several of the band's studio albums and live releases.
