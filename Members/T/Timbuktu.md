# Timbuktu

**Jason Diakité, known as Timbuktu, is a Swedish hip-hop artist and reggae singer born on January 11, 1975 in Lund, Sweden to an American father and Swedish mother.**

Timbuktu became one of the most respected voices in Swedish hip-hop, blending reggae influences with socially conscious, poetic lyricism. His 2009 album *Rosenraseri* is widely regarded as a milestone of Swedish hip-hop. He also wrote the memoir *En halvt dold liv* (2015) about searching for his family history in the American South.
