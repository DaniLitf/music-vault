# Tony Yayo

**Rapper. Born 1978-03-31.**

Tony Yayo is an American rapper from South Jamaica, Queens, and a founding member of G-Unit with 50 Cent and Lloyd Banks. His incarceration during the recording of *Beg for Mercy* (2003) led to Young Buck joining the group; his face appeared on the album cover's brick wall because he could not be photographed. His solo debut *Thoughts of a Predicate Felon* was released in 2005, and he remained loyal to 50 Cent through the group's final 2022 breakup.
