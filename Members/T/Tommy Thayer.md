# Tommy Thayer

**Lead guitarist, vocalist. Born November 7, 1960.**

Tommy Thayer is an American musician, best known as the lead guitarist of KISS since 2002. He previously played in Black 'n' Blue and has worked as a producer. He adopted the "Spaceman" persona originally created by Ace Frehley.
