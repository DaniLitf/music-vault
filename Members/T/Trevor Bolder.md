# Trevor Bolder

**Bassist. British.**

Trevor Bolder was a British bassist who played in David Bowie's backing band the Spiders from Mars during the Ziggy Stardust era, later joining Uriah Heep as a longtime member. He died of pancreatic cancer in 2013.
