# Tom DeLonge

**Vocals, guitar, producer. Born 1975-12-13.**

Tom DeLonge is an American musician best known as co-founder, guitarist, and co-lead vocalist of [[Artists/B/Blink-182|Blink-182]], the pop-punk band he helped make into one of the genre's biggest acts through albums like *Enema of the State* and *Take Off Your Pants and Jacket*. After Blink-182 went on hiatus in 2005 he founded [[Artists/A/Angels & Airwaves|Angels & Airwaves]] as a more atmospheric, cinematic outlet, and also produced or co-produced most of that band's catalog himself. He briefly reunited with Blink-182 (2009–2015, 2022–present) while continuing Angels & Airwaves, and has since become known for founding To The Stars, a media company focused on his other passion, UFO/UAP research.
