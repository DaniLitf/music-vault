# Tyler Smith

**Bass.**

Tyler Smith originally joined [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] in early 2013 but had to withdraw before touring due to personal obligations. When the band's subsequent bassist departed, he re-auditioned and rejoined as touring bassist in 2014.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] – bass (2014)
