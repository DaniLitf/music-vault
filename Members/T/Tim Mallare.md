# Tim Mallare

**Drummer.**

Tim Mallare served as Overkill's drummer from 1993 to 2005, spanning a long stretch of the band's career including *W.F.O.* (1994), *The Killing Kind* (1996), *From the Underground and Below* (1997), *Necroshine* (1999), *Bloodletting* (2000), and *Killbox 13* (2003).
