# Thomas "Tom Warrior" Gabriel Fischer

**Guitarist, vocalist. Born 1963-07-19.**

Thomas Gabriel Fischer is a Swiss musician who formed the extreme metal band Hammerhead in May 1982, which became Hellhammer later that year, and went on to co-found the highly influential Celtic Frost with Martin Eric Ain in 1984. He later led Celtic Frost's various incarnations until 2008, then founded Triptykon, and formed Hellhammer tribute act Triumph of Death in 2019.
