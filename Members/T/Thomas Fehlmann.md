# Thomas Fehlmann

**Electronic musician, producer. Born 1957-11-09.**

Thomas Fehlmann is a Swiss-born electronic musician and producer who became a major creative member of [[Artists/T/The Orb]] from the 1990s onward. His production style was especially prominent on the group's Kompakt-era releases.
