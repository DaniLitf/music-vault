# Torbjørn Brundtland

**Producer, multi-instrumentalist. Born 1976-02-04 in Tromsø, Norway.**

Torbjørn Brundtland is one half of Röyksopp alongside Svein Berge. The two have worked as an inseparable creative duo since forming Röyksopp, with Brundtland providing production and composition alongside Berge across all their albums.
