# Tatè Nsongan

**Cameroonian percussionist. Born 1958 in Douala, Cameroon.**

Bienvenu Tatè Nsongan is a Cameroonian percussionist who won Cameroon's National Music Festival in the late 1970s and toured with Miriam Makeba before settling in Italy, where he co-founded the world music band Mau Mau in 1991 with Luca Morino and Fabio Barovero. In 2000 he also founded the group Kin Koba.
