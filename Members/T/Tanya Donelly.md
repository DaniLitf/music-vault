# Tanya Donelly

**Vocalist, guitarist. American. Born 1966.**

Tanya Donelly is an American musician best known as a co-founder of [[Artists/T/The Breeders]] alongside Kim Deal, and as a member of Throwing Muses. She later formed and fronted the band [[Artists/B/Belly]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Breeders]] – guitar, vocals (1989–1992)
- [[Artists/B/Belly]] – vocals, guitar (1991–1996, 2016–present)
