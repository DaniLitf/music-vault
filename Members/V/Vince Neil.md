# Vince Neil

**Vocalist. Born February 8, 1961.**

Vince Neil is an American rock vocalist best known as the lead singer of Mötley Crüe, whose high-pitched vocals and charismatic stage presence helped define the glam metal sound of the 1980s. He has pursued a solo career and appeared in various entertainment projects throughout his decades in music.
