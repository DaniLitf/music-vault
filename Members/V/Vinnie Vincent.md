# Vinnie Vincent

**Lead guitarist, songwriter. Born August 6, 1952.**

Vinnie Vincent is an American guitarist and songwriter, best known for his tenure as lead guitarist for KISS from 1982 to 1984. He co-wrote songs on the *Creatures of the Night* and *Lick It Up* albums and later formed the Vinnie Vincent Invasion.
