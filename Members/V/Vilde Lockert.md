# Vilde Lockert

**Vocalist. Norwegian.**

Vilde Lockert co-founded the industrial/alternative rock band Magenta with her husband Anders Odden in the summer of 1995, serving as the band's vocalist across all of their releases from the 1997 EP *Magenta (The Secret Sky)* through *Songs for the Dead* (2015).
