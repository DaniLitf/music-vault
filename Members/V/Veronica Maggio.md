# Veronica Maggio

**Veronica Maggio is a Swedish singer-songwriter born on September 14, 1985 in Stockholm, one of the most commercially and critically successful Swedish-language pop artists of her generation.**

Maggio debuted in 2006 and steadily built a devoted following through melodic, warmly produced pop records with sharply observed lyrics in Swedish. Her 2010 album *Satan i Gatan* and its follow-ups made her one of Sweden's biggest pop stars. She has won multiple Grammisgalan awards (Swedish Grammys) and consistently topped Swedish charts.
