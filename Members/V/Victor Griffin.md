# Victor Griffin

**Guitarist. Born 1962-02-28 in Virginia, USA.**

Victor Griffin is the guitarist most associated with Pentagram's classic sound, joining the band in 1978 and playing a central role in their first major recordings. He departed and rejoined the band multiple times over the decades, and his heavy, riff-driven playing on albums like *Relentless* (1985) and *Day of Reckoning* (1987) is considered foundational to doom metal. He has also led his own projects, including Place of Skulls.
