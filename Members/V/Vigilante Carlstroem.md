# Vigilante Carlstroem

**Rhythm guitar, backing vocals. Swedish. Born 1978-10-30.**

Mikael Karlsson Åström, known onstage as Vigilante Carlstroem, is a Swedish guitarist and founding member of garage rock band [[Artists/T/The Hives]], a childhood friend of the Almqvist brothers who joined the band at its 1993 formation.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Hives]] – rhythm guitar, backing vocals, founder (1993–present)
