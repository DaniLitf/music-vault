# Vince Clarke

**Vince Clarke is a British musician and songwriter who co-founded Depeche Mode in 1980 before leaving to form Yazoo and later Erasure, making him one of the most prolific synth-pop composers in British music history.**

He wrote many of Depeche Mode's early hits including "Just Can't Get Enough" before departing after their debut album.
