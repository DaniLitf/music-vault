# Vitaliy Vyhovskyi

**Guitarist. Born in Ukraine.**

Vitaliy Vyhovskyi, known on stage as Vitalis Winkelhock, is the guitarist for 1914, joining the band in 2016. He performs on their albums *The Blind Leading the Blind* (2018), *Where Fear and Weapons Meet* (2021), and *Viribus Unitis* (2025).
