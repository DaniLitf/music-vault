# Vinnie Paul

**Drummer. Born 1964-03-11 in Ennis, Texas, USA. Died 2018-06-22.**

Vinnie Paul Abbott was the drummer of Pantera and the brother of guitarist Dimebag Darrell. His powerful, groove-oriented drumming was central to Pantera's sound across all their major albums. After Pantera's breakup he co-founded Damageplan with Dimebag, and after Dimebag's murder in 2004, went on to form Hellyeah. He passed away from heart failure in June 2018.
