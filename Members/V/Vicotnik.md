# Vicotnik

**Multi-instrumentalist. Norwegian.**

Yusaf "Vicotnik" Parvez co-founded avant-garde/black metal band Dødheimsgard in 1994, originally on drums and backing vocals, and has remained the band's sole constant member, later moving to guitar, keyboards, and lead vocals across the band's many lineup changes and stylistic shifts.
