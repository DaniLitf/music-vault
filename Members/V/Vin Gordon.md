# Vin Gordon

**Trombonist. Born 1949-08-04.**

Vin Gordon is a Jamaican trombonist trained at Kingston's Alpha Boys School who began his career with The Skatalites in 1964 and became the main trombonist at Studio One, recording for nearly every major Jamaican producer across ska, rocksteady, and reggae. In 1977 Bob Marley brought him to London to record the *Exodus* and *Kaya* albums, and his horn work went on to influence British reggae acts such as Aswad.
