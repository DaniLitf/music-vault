# Vanessa Carlton

**Singer, songwriter, pianist. American. Born 1980.**

Vanessa Carlton is a pop and rock singer-songwriter signed to Murder Inc. Records / The Inc. from 2006 to 2007. She released *Heroes and Thieves* (2007) under the label, marking a significant departure from the label's typical hip-hop and R&B focus. Carlton is best known for her 2002 hit "A Thousand Miles."
