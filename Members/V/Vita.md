# Vita

**Female rapper. American.**

Vita was a female rapper and one of Murder Inc.'s original artists, signed from 1999 to 2002. She appeared on early Murder Inc. compilations and released tracks under the label before her departure in April 2002, which resulted in her debut album *La Dolce Vita* being indefinitely shelved.
