# Viktor Lager

**Guitarist, vocalist. Swedish.**

Viktor Lager is a Swedish guitarist and vocalist for Alarma Man, an instrumental post-punk/math-rock band from Gothenburg, appearing on the band's 2005 self-titled debut album and 2010's *Love Forever*. He also joined Swedish post-punk/garage rock band Division of Laura Lee for their 2013 album *Tree*, remaining a current member.
