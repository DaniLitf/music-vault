# Victor Dominicis

**Guitarist. British.**

Victor Dominicis (known as Vic) is a British punk guitarist who played for Chaos UK from 1992 to 1995. He performed on the albums *100% Two Fingers in the Air Punk Rock* and *Floggin' the Corpse*.
