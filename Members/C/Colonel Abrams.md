# Colonel Abrams

**Singer and songwriter. Born 25 May 1949 in Detroit, Michigan, USA. Died 24 November 2016 in New York City, New York.**

Colonel Abrams was an American singer and songwriter who became one of house music's early pioneers. He began his career as frontman for the Minneapolis funk band 94 East (which featured Prince on guitar) before launching a successful solo career in the 1980s. His 1985 single "Trapped" was a top-five hit in the UK and topped the US Dance chart, establishing him as a leading voice in electronic dance music.
