# Charli Baltimore

**Rapper, songwriter. American.**

Charli Baltimore is a rapper and songwriter signed to Murder Inc. Records from 2001 to 2003. She appeared on multiple label compilations and collaborated with Ja Rule on tracks including "Down Ass Bitch" from his album *Pain Is Love* (2001). She left the label in 2003 to pursue independent opportunities.
