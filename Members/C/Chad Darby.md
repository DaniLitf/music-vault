# Chad Darby

**Bassist.**

Chad Darby is an American bass guitarist who was called upon by [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] after Kaleb Stewart's 2019 dismissal, recording bass for the comeback EP *Up from the Muck* (2020). He served in the role until around 2022, when the band began using other fill-in bassists.
