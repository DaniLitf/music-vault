# Chris Colbert

**Audio engineer. American.**

Chris Colbert is an American musician, audio engineer, and mixer. He was the co-founder and guitarist/vocalist of Duraluxe, and previously fronted the band Fluffy. He mixed Cush's self-titled album (2000) and has collaborated extensively with artists from the Tooth & Nail / Northern Records scene.
