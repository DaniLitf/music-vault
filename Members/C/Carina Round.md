# Carina Round

**Vocalist. Born April 20, 1979.**

Carina Round is a British vocalist and musician best known for her work with the experimental rock band Puscifer, led by Tool's Maynard James Keenan. She joined Puscifer in 2009 and has remained a core member and vocalist for the project, contributing to multiple studio albums and live performances. Prior to Puscifer, she was a member of the short-lived experimental band Early Winters (2011–2017), and has appeared as a guest vocalist on various other projects.
