# Cora Coleman Dunham

**Drummer. Born in the United States.**

Cora Coleman Dunham is an American drummer who played with Prince's New Power Generation from 2010. She replaced John Blackwell as Prince's drummer and performed on *20Ten* and the Welcome 2 tour. She is married to bassist Josh Dunham, who also played in the NPG.
