# Clive Burr

**Drums (1979–1982; died 2013). Born March 8, 1956 — died March 12, 2013.**

Clive Burr was an English drummer best known as the drummer of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] from 1979 to 1982.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – drums (1979–1982)
- [[Artists/S/Samson]] – drums (1977–1978, pre-Maiden)
- Trust – drums (post-Maiden)
- Praying Mantis – session drums
- Elixir – drums

## Biography

Burr drummed on Iron Maiden's first three albums — *Iron Maiden* (1980), *Killers* (1981), and *The Number of the Beast* (1982) — the latter of which became a worldwide hit. He was replaced by Nicko McBrain in 1982. He died in 2013 from multiple sclerosis.
