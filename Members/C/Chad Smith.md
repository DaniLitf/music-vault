# Chad Smith

**Drummer. Born October 25, 1961.**

Chad Smith is an American drummer best known for his work with the Red Hot Chili Peppers, joining the band in 1988 and becoming one of the most recognizable drummers in rock music. His powerful and distinctive drumming style has been integral to the band's sound across multiple decades and albums. Beyond the Chilis, Smith has also performed with Chickenfoot and led his own project Chad Smith's Bombastic Meatbats, establishing himself as a versatile percussionist across multiple genres.
