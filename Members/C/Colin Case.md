**Vocalist.**

Colin Case, also known as Einstein, was a vocalist who briefly performed with [[Artists/T/Technotronic|Technotronic]] in 1990, appearing on the single "Turn It Up" alongside Melissa Beckford. He was brought in as part of a short-lived new lineup following Ya Kid K's initial departure.

After "Turn It Up" failed to chart as strongly as Technotronic's earlier singles, Case and Melissa Beckford left the group, and the project shifted direction with new vocalist Reggie for the *Body to Body* album.
