# Chris Fehn

**Percussionist. American. Born 1973-02-24.**

Chris Fehn is an American percussionist known for his long tenure with the industrial metal band Slipknot from 1998 to 2019, contributing custom percussion, samples, and keys to the band's intense and layered sound across multiple albums and world tours. He was a key member of Slipknot's distinctive nine-piece lineup, recognized for his energetic stage presence and technical contributions to the band's evolving musical style.
