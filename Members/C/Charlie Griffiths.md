# Charlie Griffiths

**Guitarist. British.**

Charlie Griffiths is an English guitarist who joined progressive metal band Haken in 2011, replacing founding member Matthew Marshall, and has performed and co-written on the band's albums since. He also released a solo instrumental album, *Tiktaalika*, in 2020.
