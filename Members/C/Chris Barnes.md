**Death metal vocalist. Born December 29, 1967.**

Chris Barnes is an American death metal vocalist best known as the frontman of [[Artists/S/Six Feet Under]], which he founded in 1993. Barnes was the co-founder and original lead vocalist of [[Artists/C/Cannibal Corpse]] (1988–1995), recording four studio albums including the landmark *Tomb of the Mutilated* (1992). He designed the original Cannibal Corpse logo and is considered among the most influential extreme metal vocalists of all time.
