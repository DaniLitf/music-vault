# Christoph Schneider

**Drummer. German. Born 1966-05-11.**

Christoph Schneider, known by the stage name Doom, is the drummer for the German industrial metal band Rammstein and has been with the group since its formation in 1994. Before joining Rammstein, he performed as the drummer for Feeling B (1990–1994), an East German new wave band. Schneider's precise and powerful drumming style is a cornerstone of Rammstein's sound, complementing the band's aggressive industrial aesthetic.
