# Chris Dangerous

**Guitarist/Drummer. Swedish. Born 1978-06-12.**

Chris Dangerous (Arne Christian Jääskeläinen) is a Swedish guitarist and drummer best known as a founding member and key performer in the garage rock band The Hives, which emerged from Fagersta, Sweden in 1993 and became internationally recognized for their high-energy, stripped-down rock sound and theatrical live performances.
