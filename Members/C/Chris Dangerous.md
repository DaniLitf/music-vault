# Chris Dangerous

**Drums, percussion. Swedish. Born 1978-06-12.**

Christian Grahn, known onstage as Chris Dangerous, is a Swedish drummer and founding member of garage rock band [[Artists/T/The Hives]], who reportedly coined the band's name after reading about the skin condition "hives" in a dictionary. He has drummed for the band since its 1993 formation, aside from a 2019 hiatus for surgery recovery during which he was replaced on tour by Joey Castillo.
