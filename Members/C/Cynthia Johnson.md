# Cynthia Johnson

**Singer.**

Cynthia Johnson is an American singer who was the original lead vocalist of Flyte Tyme, the Minneapolis funk band that evolved into The Time. She performed with the band from 1972 to 1979 before being replaced by Alexander O'Neal.
