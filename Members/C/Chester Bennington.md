# Chester Bennington

**Vocalist. American. Born 1976-03-20; died 2017-07-20.**

Chester Bennington was an American vocalist best known as the lead singer of Linkin Park from 1996 until his death in 2017, defining the nu-metal and alternative rock sound of multiple generations through his powerful and emotionally charged vocal performances. He briefly served as lead vocalist for Stone Temple Pilots from 2013 to 2015, bringing his distinctive voice to the grunge-influenced rock band during a period of transition.
