**Drummer and founding member. Active 1967–1968.**

Chris Stewart was one of the founding members of Genesis, serving as the band's original drummer and percussionist from the formation of the group in January 1967 until mid-1968. He performed on the band's first single "The Silent Sun" (1968) and second single "A Winter's Tale" (1968), as well as the debut album From Genesis to Revelation (1969). However, Stewart was forced to leave the band early in their development when his parents refused to allow him to leave school to focus on the band, marking the first of several drummer changes in Genesis's early years.
