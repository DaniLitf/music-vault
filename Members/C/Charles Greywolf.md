# Charles Greywolf

**Bassist/Keyboardist. German. Born 1975-09-05.**

Charles Greywolf (stage name of David Vogt) is a German musician best known as the bassist and keyboardist for the symphonic metal band Powerwolf, which he joined in 2004. Beyond Powerwolf, he has participated in other musical projects including Flowing Tears, Heavatar, and Red Aim, contributing to Germany's progressive and metal music scenes.
