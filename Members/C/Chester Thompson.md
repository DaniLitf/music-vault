**Drummer and percussionist. Born 11 December 1948.**

Chester Thompson is an American drummer and percussionist born in Baltimore, Maryland, who served as Genesis's touring and session drummer from 1977 through the early 1990s and for later reunion tours. Thompson replaced Bill Bruford as the band's touring drummer in December 1976 and became a crucial part of Genesis's live sound during the Banks/Collins/Rutherford era. While initially presented by the band as an "employee" rather than a full member, Thompson's steady, professional drumming work contributed significantly to Genesis's touring and live recordings throughout his tenure with the band.
