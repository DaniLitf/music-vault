# Cecilia Loera

**Vocals. American.**

Cecilia Loera is an American singer who fronted [[Artists/D/Descendents]] for roughly six months on a trial basis in 1979, after guitarist Frank Navetta and bassist Tony Lombardo tired of singing. She was ultimately not made a permanent member; the band instead recruited Milo Aukerman, whose arrival pushed the Descendents toward their faster hardcore style.
