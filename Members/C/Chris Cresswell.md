**Guitarist, vocalist. Canadian.**

Chris Cresswell is a guitarist and vocalist who joined Hot Water Music in 2017 as a full-time member. He is the lead vocalist of Canadian punk rock band The Flatliners, which he has fronted since 2002. Cresswell initially filled in for Chris Wollard during live performances and has since become a permanent contributing member on studio recordings, including providing lead vocals on the 2022 track "Turn the Dial" and the 2024 album Vows.
