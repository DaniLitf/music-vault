# Chance Howard

**Vocalist. Born in the United States.**

Chance Howard is an American singer who performed with Prince's New Power Generation during the 2000s. He contributed backing vocals on Prince's *Musicology*, *3121*, and *Planet Earth* albums and toured as part of Prince's vocal section. He is also a session vocalist for various R&B artists.
