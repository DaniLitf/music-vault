# César Vidal

**Vocals, guitar. Born DATE.**

César Vidal is a Spanish-born Swedish musician, best known as the lead vocalist and guitarist for the Swedish rock band [[Artists/C/Caesars]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/C/Caesars]] – vocals, guitar (1995–present)

## Biography

Vidal was born in Spain and moved to Sweden where he formed Twelve Caesars (later Caesars) in 1995 with Joakim Åhlund and David Lindqvist. He is the band's primary vocalist and guitarist, and has been a constant member throughout the band's name changes from Twelve Caesars to Caesars Palace to Caesars.
