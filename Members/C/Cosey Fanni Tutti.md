# Cosey Fanni Tutti

**Vocalist, guitarist, and electronic musician. British. Born 1951-11-04.**

Christine Carol Newby, professionally known as Cosey Fanni Tutti, is a British artist, vocalist, and electronic musician best known as a founding member of the industrial music pioneers Throbbing Gristle (1976–1981, 2004–2010). Her provocative performances and innovative use of electronic sound were instrumental in establishing the industrial music genre. Before Throbbing Gristle, she was part of the performance art collective COUM Transmissions. After Throbbing Gristle's initial breakup, she continued making music through the duo Chris & Cosey and later the project Carter Tutti, maintaining an influential presence in experimental and electronic music.
