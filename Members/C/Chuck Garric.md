# Chuck Garric

**Bassist. American.**

Chuck Garric is an American rock bassist best known as the longtime bassist for Alice Cooper since 2003, appearing on albums such as *The Eyes of Alice Cooper* (2003), *Dirty Diamonds* (2005) and *Along Came a Spider* (2008). He has also been a member of ESP (Eric Singer Project), Dio, L.A. Guns, and Beasto Blanco.
