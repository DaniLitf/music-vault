# Christer Lindbladh

**Drummer and recording engineer. Swedish.**

Christer Lindbladh is a Swedish drummer and recording engineer who was the original drummer for Driller Killer from 1993 to 1996, playing on the debut album *Brutalize* (1994) and *Total Fucking Hate* (1995). He also played in Atomvinter, Gbg Punx, Libresse, Perukers, and Punkrock Allstars, and works as a recording engineer at Evil Grill.
