# Corey Lowery

**Bass, guitar, vocals. American. Founding bassist of [[Artists/S/Saint Asonia]]; member of [[Artists/S/Seether]].**

Corey Lowery, formerly of Dark New Day, was a founding member of Saint Asonia in 2014, playing bass and backing vocals. He departed in the summer of 2018 to join Seether as rhythm guitarist and backing vocalist after filling in on Seether's Nickelback support tour.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/S/Saint Asonia]] – bass, backing vocals (2014–2018, founder)
- [[Artists/S/Seether]] – rhythm guitar, backing vocals (2018–present)
