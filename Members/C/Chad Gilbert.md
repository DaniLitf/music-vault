**Guitarist and vocalist for New Found Glory and Shai Hulud. Born March 9, 1981.**

Chad Gilbert is the founding member and lead guitarist of the pop-punk band New Found Glory, for which he also provides backing vocals. In addition to his work with New Found Glory, Gilbert has been deeply involved with the hardcore band Shai Hulud, serving as vocalist across two separate periods (1995–1998 and 2012–2013). He was briefly involved with Hazen Street in 2004, co-writing and recording the band's self-titled debut, but was dismissed due to contractual obligations to his primary label.
