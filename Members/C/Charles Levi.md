# Charles Levi

**Charles Levi is an American bassist, best known as a member of [[Artists/M/My Life With the Thrill Kill Kult]] and a long-time touring and session member of [[Artists/P/Pigface]].**

He contributed bass across multiple Pigface tours and albums.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/P/Pigface]] – bass (tour 1990–1996)
- [[Artists/M/My Life With the Thrill Kill Kult]] – bass (1990–present)
