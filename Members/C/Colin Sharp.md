# Colin Sharp

**Colin Sharp is a British musician who was the vocalist and bassist of Biting Tongues throughout the band's existence from 1979 to 1993.**

He was a key creative force in the Manchester post-punk and industrial scene, steering the band's angular sound across multiple albums on their own and independent labels.
