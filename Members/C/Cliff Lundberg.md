# Cliff Lundberg

**Vocalist, guitarist, bassist and drummer. Born 15 March 1967 in Malmö, Sweden.**

Cliff Lundberg is a Swedish multi-instrumentalist best known as the frontman and sole constant member of the crust punk band Driller Killer, which he formed in 1993 after his previous band Anti Cimex disbanded. He previously played guitar in Anti Cimex (1990–1993), drums in Black Uniforms (1981–1990), and bass in Moderat Likvidation. He also briefly played bass for At the Gates in 1992 and auditioned for Bathory before the *Under the Sign of the Black Mark* album. Outside of extreme music, he has played in Perukers, Machinegun Kelly, and Virgin Sin.
