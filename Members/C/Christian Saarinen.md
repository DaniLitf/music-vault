# Christian Saarinen

**Guitar. Swedish.**

Christian Saarinen was a brief early guitarist for [[Artists/C/Cemetary]] in 1991–1992, ahead of the recording of the band's debut album *An Evil Shade of Grey* (1992), before being replaced by Anton Hedberg.
