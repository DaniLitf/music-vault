# Charlie Claeson

**Drummer. Swedish.**

Charlie Claeson is a Swedish drummer who was the final drummer for Driller Killer from 2006 until the band's breakup in 2009. He was also the longtime drummer for the pioneering Swedish D-beat band Anti Cimex from 1981 to 1993, appearing on all of their classic recordings. He has been involved in numerous other punk and rock projects including Troublemakers, Not Enough Hate, Psychotic Youth, The Great German Re-Research, Death Dealers, The Partisans, Wolfhour, Vengeance by Proxy, and Bring the Drones.
