# Christian Andreu

**Guitarist. French. Born 1976-11-15.**

Christian Andreu is a French guitarist and a founding member of the progressive metal band Gojira (initially called Godzilla until 2001), which he joined in 1996. He is a key figure in the band's evolution from extreme metal to progressive and avant-garde sounds, contributing complex guitar arrangements and serving as a principal songwriter alongside frontman Joe Duplantier.
