**Vocalist, guitarist. American.**

Chuck Ragan is the co-founder and primary songwriter of Hot Water Music. Born in 1979, he has been central to the band's direction since their formation in 1994, co-writing much of their catalog and delivering emotionally raw vocals. During Hot Water Music's 2005 hiatus, Ragan developed a primarily acoustic solo career, releasing albums including Feast or Famine (2007), and became known for The Revival Tour, a grassroots touring project featuring punk, bluegrass, and alt-country performers.
