# Cordell Mosson

**Bass, guitar, drums. American. Longtime member of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Cordell Mosson joined Funkadelic in 1972 alongside his friend and bandmate Garry Shider, and became the primary bassist for Funkadelic and later Parliament once Bootsy Collins shifted focus to his solo career. From the late 1970s he mostly played rhythm guitar, touring with the P-Funk All Stars until his death in 2013.
