# Cozy Powell

**Drummer. British. Born 1947-12-29. Died 1998-04-05.**

Colin Trevor Flooks, professionally known as Cozy Powell, was a legendary British rock drummer renowned for his powerful, progressive drumming style and extensive work across multiple high-profile bands. He began his career with The Jeff Beck Group (1970–1972) and subsequently played for Rainbow (1975–1980), serving as the drummer for some of their most celebrated albums. Powell's career also included drumming for the Michael Schenker Group, Whitesnake, Emerson Lake & Powell, Black Sabbath, and Brian May's band, establishing him as one of rock's most versatile and respected percussionists until his death in a traffic accident in 1998.
