# Corey Taylor

**Vocalist, songwriter. Born 1973-12-08 in Des Moines, Iowa, USA.**

Corey Taylor is the lead vocalist of Slipknot and co-founder of Stone Sour, one of heavy metal's most versatile and commercially successful frontmen. He joined Slipknot in 1997 and has fronted them across all their studio albums. Stone Sour, the hard rock band he co-founded with guitarist James Root, has operated simultaneously and released six studio albums. He has also released solo work and written books.
