# Cliff Burton

**Bass. Born February 10, 1962. Died September 27, 1986.**

Cliff Burton joined [[Artists/M/Metallica]] in 1982, on the condition that the band relocate from Los Angeles to the San Francisco Bay Area, and redefined the role of bass in metal by bringing melodic lead lines, wah-pedal effects, and classical/blues influence to songs like "(Anesthesia) Pulling Teeth" and "Orion." He died at age 24 in a tour bus accident near Ljungby, Sweden, while touring behind *Master of Puppets*, and was posthumously inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame with Metallica in 2009.
