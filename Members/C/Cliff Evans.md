# Cliff Evans

**Bassist.**

Cliff Evans is an American bassist known for his work with The Melvins as a touring and session bassist, contributing to the band's live performances and recordings. He also appeared as a bassist on the collaborative psychedelic rock project Crystal Fairy, which brought together members of The Melvins with vocalist Teri Gender Bender of Le Butcherettes.
