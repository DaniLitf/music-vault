# Chris Aylmer

**Bassist. British. Born 1948-02-07; died 2007-01-09.**

Chris Aylmer was a British bassist known for his work with the heavy metal band Samson, joining in 1977 and remaining associated with the band through multiple reunions and lineup changes until 2000. He was a key figure in the New Wave of British Heavy Metal (NWOBHM) movement during the late 1970s and 1980s.
