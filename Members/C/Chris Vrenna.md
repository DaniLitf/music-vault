# Chris Vrenna

**Chris Vrenna is an American drummer and producer born February 23, 1967, best known as a former member of [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] and frontman of [[Artists/T/Tweaker]].**

Vrenna was a core member of Nine Inch Nails from 1989 to 1997, appearing on *Pretty Hate Machine*, *Broken*, *The Downward Spiral*, and the *Further Down the Spiral* remix album. He also contributed to Pigface as a guest drummer and programmer.
