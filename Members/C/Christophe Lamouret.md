# Christophe Lamouret

**Vocalist. French.**

Christophe Lamouret was the vocalist for the French industrial metal band Out and performed lead vocals on Meathook Seed's second album *Basic Instructions Before Leaving Earth (B.I.B.L.E.)* (1999). His vocal style on the album marked a shift from the death metal approach of the debut toward a more industrial rock sound.
