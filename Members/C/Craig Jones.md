# Craig Jones

**Sampler, keyboardist, and synthesizer player. American. Born 1972-02-11.**

Craig Jones is an American musician best known as the sampler, keyboardist, and synthesizer player for the heavy metal band Slipknot, which he joined in 1996. His contribution of samples, electronic sounds, and synthesizers adds texture and atmospheric depth to Slipknot's aggressive industrial metal sound. Jones is recognizable by his unique mask and performance style during live shows, and his role in the band has been crucial in shaping their complex sonic identity across multiple albums and world tours. He parted ways with Slipknot in June 2023.
