# Chuck Schuldiner

**Chuck Schuldiner was an American guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter, the founder of Death and widely acknowledged as the "Father of Death Metal," whose relentless artistic evolution across seven albums remains the gold standard of the genre.**

He died of pontine glioma (brain cancer) at age 34 in December 2001, leaving behind a legacy that continues to influence metal musicians worldwide.
