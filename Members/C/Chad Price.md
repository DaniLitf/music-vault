# Chad Price

**Vocals. American.**

Chad Price is an American singer who joined [[Artists/A/All]] as its third and longest-serving lead vocalist in 1993, fronting the band through *Breaking Things* (1993), *Pummel* (1995), *Mass Nerder* (1998), and *Problematic* (2000). In 1996, while still active in All, he co-founded the alt-country band Drag the River with Jon Snodgrass, and has also played bass in touring bands for singer-songwriter Rocky Votolato.
