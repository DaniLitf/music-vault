# Chris Brinkman

**Chris Brinkman** (died 1997) was an American guitarist and original guitarist of [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]].

- [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]] – guitar (1985–1986)

Brinkman was a childhood friend of [[Members/E/Eric Avery]] and was the band's original guitarist. He was fired due to drug problems. He died of a drug overdose in 1997 at age 31.
