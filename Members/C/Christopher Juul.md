# Christopher Juul

**Keyboardist and music producer. Danish. Born 1989.**

Christopher Juul is a Danish keyboardist and music producer best known as a co-founder and creative force behind the neofolk and experimental music ensemble Heilung, which he established in 2014. In addition to his work with Heilung, Juul operates Lava Studio in Copenhagen, where he has produced and engineered music for numerous artists. His work combines electronic and organic instrumentation with a focus on atmospheric and traditional Nordic influences.
