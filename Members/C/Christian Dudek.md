# Christian Dudek

**Drummer. Born 1965.**

Christian "Witchhunter" Dudek was the legendary drummer for German thrash metal pioneers Sodom, joining at age 16 in 1982. He was instrumental in shaping the band's aggressive early sound and remained their primary member through the 1980s and early 1990s. A defining force in extreme metal drumming, Dudek passed away in 2008 at age 42.
