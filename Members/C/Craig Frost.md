# Craig Frost

**Keyboardist. Born April 27, 1948.**

Craig Frost is an American keyboardist best known as the keyboardist for Grand Funk Railroad, which he joined in 1972 as the band's first expanded member, contributing to their most commercially successful albums. He also played with Bob Seger & the Silver Bullet Band and was a member of Flint, the band formed by former Grand Funk members after the group's 1976 breakup.
