# Cameron Shockley-Mallet

**Vocalist. American.**

Cameron Shockley-Mallet is an American vocalist and performer best known as the lead vocalist of the industrial and EBM band Combichrist, which he co-founded in 2003. He has been the primary frontman and driving creative force behind Combichrist since the band's inception, directing their aggressive electronic and industrial sound through multiple studio albums and live performances. Shockley-Mallet's vocal delivery and theatrical presence have become synonymous with the band's identity in the industrial music scene.
