# Chris Casket

**Multi-instrumentalist. British.**

Chris "Chino" Casket was a multi-instrumentalist who played rhythm guitar and bass for Extreme Noise Terror both in the studio (2009–2014) and live (2010–2012). He contributed to the albums *Law of Retaliation* and the self-titled album.
