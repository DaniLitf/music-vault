# Chuck D

**Lead vocalist and rapper. American. Born 1960-08-01.**

Chuck D is the lead vocalist, primary lyricist, and founding member of the influential hip hop group Public Enemy, which he formed in 1985. He is widely recognized for his deep, powerful voice and socially conscious lyrics that address systemic racism, police brutality, and inequality. Beyond Public Enemy, Chuck D has been active in numerous other projects, including Prophets of Rage and various collaborations, maintaining his role as one of hip hop's most politically engaged and influential figures.
