# Carl Restivo

**Bass, guitar. American. Member of [[Artists/S/Satellite Party]].**

Carl Restivo played bass for Satellite Party throughout its run, moving to guitar and bass duties after Nuno Bettencourt's 2007 departure. He also filled in on bass for [[Artists/E/Extreme]] during their brief 2004 reunion tour.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/S/Satellite Party]] – bass, guitar (2004–2008)
- [[Artists/E/Extreme]] – bass, backing vocals (2004)
