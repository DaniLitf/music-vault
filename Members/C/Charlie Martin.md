# Charlie Martin

**Drummer. American.**

Charlie Martin is an American drummer and the son of Starflyer 59 founder Jason Martin. He joined Starflyer 59 as their drummer beginning with the album *Young in My Head* (2019) and has continued recording and performing with the band on subsequent releases.
