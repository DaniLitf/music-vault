# Christopher Guest

**Guitarist and vocalist. American. Born 1948-02-05.**

Christopher Haden-Guest is best known as the guitarist and singer for the comedy rock band Spinal Tap, portraying the character Nigel Tufnel since the band's formation in 1978. Beyond his work with Spinal Tap, Guest is an accomplished filmmaker and actor who has directed and starred in numerous mockumentary films. His comedic approach to music and film has made him a prominent figure in entertainment, blending musicianship with sharp satirical humor across multiple decades.
