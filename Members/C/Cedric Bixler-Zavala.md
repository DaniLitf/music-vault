# Cedric Bixler-Zavala

**Cedric Bixler-Zavala is an American vocalist and percussionist who co-founded At the Drive-In in 1993 and The Mars Volta in 2001, and in 2008 formed One Day as a Lion with Zack de la Rocha.**

After At the Drive-In's initial breakup, Bixler-Zavala and guitarist Omar Rodríguez-López formed The Mars Volta, which became one of the most ambitious progressive rock acts of the 2000s. He has been a vocal advocate against music industry abuses and has spoken about personal experiences with Scientology.
