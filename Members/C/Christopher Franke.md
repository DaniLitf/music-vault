# Christopher Franke

**Keyboardist and synthesizer programmer. German. Born 1953-04-06.**

Christopher Franke is a pioneering synthesizer programmer and keyboardist best known for his work with the electronic music group Tangerine Dream from 1970 to 1987. During his time with the band, Franke helped establish their signature sequencer-driven sound and contributed to numerous landmark albums. Since departing Tangerine Dream, he has pursued a successful solo career and composed music for numerous film and television productions, establishing himself as a respected figure in electronic and film composition.
