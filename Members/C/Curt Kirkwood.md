# Curt Kirkwood

**Curt Kirkwood is an American guitarist and co-founder of the Meat Puppets, the influential Arizona punk/country/psychedelic band, and also co-founded the supergroup Eyes Adrift with Krist Novoselic and Bud Gaugh.**
