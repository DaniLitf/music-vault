# Cale Gontier

**Bass, backing vocals. Canadian. Member of [[Artists/S/Saint Asonia]].**

Cale Gontier, cousin of frontman Adam Gontier, joined Saint Asonia in 2018 as bassist and backing vocalist, replacing Corey Lowery. He previously played bass for Art of Dying.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/S/Saint Asonia]] – bass, backing vocals (2018–present)
