# Chris Carter

**Synthesizer/Keyboardist. British. Born 1953-01-28.**

Chris Carter is a British synthesizer and keyboard player best known as a founding member and primary composer of the pioneering industrial band Throbbing Gristle (1975–1981, 2004–2010), which fundamentally shaped the industrial and post-industrial music genres. He has continued his musical work through parallel projects including Chris & Cosey (1982–2003) and Carter Tutti (2003–present), maintaining an innovative approach to electronic music production.
