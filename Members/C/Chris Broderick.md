# Chris Broderick

**Chris Broderick is an American guitarist, best known for his work with [[Artists/M/Megadeth]] and [[Artists/I/In Flames]].**

Broderick joined In Flames in 2019 as a touring guitarist, becoming an official member in 2022. He made his studio debut with the band on *Foregone* (2023). Prior to In Flames, he was the lead guitarist for Megadeth from 2008 to 2014, appearing on *Endgame* (2009), *Thirteen* (2011), and *Super Collider* (2013). He previously played in Nevermore and Jag Panzer.
