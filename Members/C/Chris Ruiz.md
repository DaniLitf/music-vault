# Chris Ruiz

**Vocalist, drummer, keyboardist. Born 1968 in Germany.**

Chris Ruiz is a German musician who co-founded the synthpop band And One with Steve Naghavi in Berlin in 1989. He performed on the band's early albums before departing in 1991, later returning from 2002 to 2011 before leaving permanently to form the band PAKT.
