# Chris Chaney

**Chris Chaney** is an American bassist best known as the longtime bassist of [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]].

- [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]] – bass (2002–2004, 2011–2022)
- [[Artists/A/Alanis Morissette]] – bass (touring and recording)

Chaney replaced [[Members/M/Martyn LeNoble]] during the recording of *Strays* (2003) and became the band's longest-tenured non-core bassist, appearing on *Strays* and *The Great Escape Artist*.
