# Carol Heller

**Vocals, rhythm guitar.**

Carol Heller is an American singer and guitarist, formerly of the all-girl punk quartet Get the Girl. In 2005 Travis Barker invited her to sing on early [[Artists/0-9/+44]] demos, where she traded and shared vocals with Mark Hoppus — notably on early versions of "Make You Smile". She grew uneasy with the band's heavier direction and left at the end of 2005, wanting to start a family.
