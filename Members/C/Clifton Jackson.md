# Clifton "Jackie" Jackson

**Bassist. Born March 1947.**

Clifton "Jackie" Jackson is a Jamaican bass player and one of the most prolific session musicians in ska, rocksteady, reggae, and dub, having been the principal bassist at Duke Reid's Treasure Isle studio from 1966 into the early 1970s. He was part of the core session group that became The Upsetters under Lee "Scratch" Perry, and later became a long-serving member of Toots and the Maytals.
