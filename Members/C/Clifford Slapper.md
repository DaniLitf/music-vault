# Clifford Slapper

**Pianist, writer. British.**

Clifford Slapper is a pianist and Mike Garson biographer who collaborated with [[Artists/T/The Featherz]] and Danie Cox on David Bowie-related material. He is credited in the Featherz history around performances and recordings of "Moonage Daydream" and "We Are the Dead."
