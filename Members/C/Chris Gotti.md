# Chris Gotti

**Music executive, label co-founder. American.**

Christopher "Chris" Lorenzo is a music executive and co-founder of Murder Inc. Records with his brother Irv Gotti. Serving as vice president of the label throughout its peak years (1999–2009), Chris was instrumental in building the Murder Inc. roster and overseeing operations during the label's rise to prominence in early 2000s hip-hop and R&B. He and Irv were acquitted of money-laundering charges in 2005 after a federal investigation.
