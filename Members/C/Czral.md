# Czral

**Drummer, guitarist and vocalist. Norwegian.**

Czral (Carl-Michael Eide) is a Norwegian musician best known for Ved Buens Ende, Dødheimsgard, and as co-founder (under the name Aggressor) of black/thrash band Aura Noir, in which he has played vocals, guitar, and bass since 1993 (also drums until 2005, when a serious accident left him paralyzed from the ankles down). He also drummed for Cadaver (billed as Cadaver Inc.) from 2002 to 2004, appearing on the album *Discipline* (2001).
