# Czral

**Drummer. Norwegian.**

Czral (Carl-Michael Eide) is a Norwegian musician best known for Ved Buens Ende, Dødheimsgard, and Aura Noir, who also drummed for Cadaver (billed as Cadaver Inc.) from 2002 to 2004, appearing on the album *Discipline* (2001).
