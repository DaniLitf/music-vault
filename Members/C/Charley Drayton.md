# Charley Drayton

**Drummer. American.**

Charley Drayton is an American drummer who has performed with The Rolling Stones and Johnny Cash. He played drums for The Dead Daisies in 2013 and again from 2013 to 2014, bringing a versatile session background to the band's early rhythm section.
