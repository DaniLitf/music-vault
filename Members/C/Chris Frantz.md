# Chris Frantz

**Chris Frantz is an American drummer and producer born May 8, 1951, co-founder of [[Artists/T/Talking Heads]] and [[Artists/T/Tom Tom Club]].**

Frantz co-founded Talking Heads in 1975 with David Byrne and Tina Weymouth (his wife). In 1981, he and Weymouth formed the side project Tom Tom Club, which had a major hit with "Genius of Love".
