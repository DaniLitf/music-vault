# Chris Connelly

**Chris Connelly is a Scottish vocalist and songwriter, known for his work with [[Artists/M/Ministry]], [[Artists/P/Pigface]], and [[Artists/R/Revolting Cocks]].**

Connelly moved to Chicago in the late 1980s and became a central figure in the Wax Trax! industrial scene. His baritone vocals and theatrical style have made him a distinctive presence across dozens of industrial and alternative releases.
