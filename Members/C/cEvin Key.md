# cEvin Key

**Multi-instrumentalist, programmer, producer. Born 1961-02-13 in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada.**

cEvin Key (born Kevin Crompton) is the co-founder and primary programmer/composer of Skinny Puppy, responsible for the band's foundational electronic architecture. Alongside Nivek Ogre he built Skinny Puppy from a bedroom project into one of the defining industrial bands of the 1980s. He has also released extensive solo material under the name cEvin Key and led the project Download.
