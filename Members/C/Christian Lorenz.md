# Christian Lorenz

**Keyboardist. German. Born 1966-11-16.**

Christian Lorenz, commonly known as Flake, is a founding member and keyboardist of the German industrial metal band Rammstein. Before that, he performed with the East German new wave band Feeling B from 1983 to 1993, where he developed his signature electronic sound. Since joining Rammstein in 1994, Lorenz has been essential to the band's distinctive aesthetic, combining keyboard work with visual performance elements.
