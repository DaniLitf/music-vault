# Chuck Biscuits

**Drums. Born 1965-04-17, died 2009-10-24.**

Chuck Biscuits (born Charles Montgomery) was a Canadian drummer who played several months with [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] in 1982, appearing on the *Live at the On Broadway 1982* recording, after earlier stints in D.O.A. He went on to drum for Circle Jerks and briefly Red Hot Chili Peppers, before joining Samhain and then Danzig, playing on that band's first four studio albums, and later Social Distortion.
