# Chris Manning

**Bassist. Born ?.**

Chris Manning is an American bassist best known as a member of the power pop band Jellyfish. He played bass on the band's debut album *Bellybutton* (1990) and contributed to their distinctive baroque pop sound alongside Andy Sturmer and Roger Manning Jr.
