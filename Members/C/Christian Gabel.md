# Christian Gabel

**Christian Gabel is a Swedish multi-instrumentalist, drummer, and producer born 1975 in Karlstad, best known as the drummer for [[Artists/B/bob hund]] and creator of the solo project [[Artists/0-9/1900]].**

Gabel grew up on Hammarö outside Karlstad. He founded Gabel Recordings and Studio Cobra in Stockholm. His solo project 1900 features low-key, melancholic tape-experiment recordings. He has also played with [[Artists/T/Thåström]] and was a member of Mindjive.
