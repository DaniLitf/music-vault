# Charlie Dominici

**Vocalist. American. Born 1951-06-16; died 2023-11-17.**

Charlie Dominici was an American vocalist best known for his work as the original lead singer of progressive metal band Dream Theater from 1987 to 1989, contributing vocals on their critically acclaimed debut album "When Dream and Day Unite." Before joining Dream Theater, he was a member of the band Franke and the Knockouts (1981), and his powerful vocal range helped define the early sound of Dream Theater during their formative years.
