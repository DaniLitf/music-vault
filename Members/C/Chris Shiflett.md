# Chris Shiflett

**Guitarist. American. Born 1971-05-06.**

Chris Shiflett is an American guitarist best known as a lead guitarist for the rock band Foo Fighters since 1999, contributing to multiple platinum albums and worldwide tours. Before joining Foo Fighters, he was a member of the punk rock band No Use for a Name (1995–1999) and has also performed with the punk cover band Me First and the Gimme Gimmes (1995–2019).
