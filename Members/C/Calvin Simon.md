# Calvin Simon

**Vocals, percussion. American. Original member of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Calvin Simon was an original member of the Parliaments, leaving the collective in 1977. He later co-founded Original P with fellow original members Ray Davis, Fuzzy Haskins, and Grady Thomas, retiring in 2005. He died in 2022.
