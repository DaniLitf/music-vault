# Cliff Williams

**Bassist and backing vocalist. British. Born 1949-12-14.**

Clifford Williams is a British bassist best known as the longtime bassist of AC/DC, joining the legendary Australian rock band in 1977 and remaining a core member for nearly four decades. His steady, powerful bass lines anchored the band's rhythm section alongside Phil Rudd's drums, providing the foundation for AC/DC's most iconic recordings including the album *Back in Black* (1980). Before joining AC/DC, Williams performed with the bands Home and Bandit. He retired from full-time touring in 2016 but returned in 2020 for the band's 50th anniversary tour.
