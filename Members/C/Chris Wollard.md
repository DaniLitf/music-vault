**Vocalist, guitarist. American.**

Chris Wollard is the co-founder of Hot Water Music and has been a core member since 1994. Originally based in Bradenton, Florida, he met the other band members through the active Sarasota/Bradenton music scene and relocated to Gainesville to form Hot Water Music. Wollard shares lead vocal and guitar duties with Chuck Ragan and is known for his energetic stage presence. In 2017, he took a break from performing live due to health concerns, though he continues to participate in studio recordings.
