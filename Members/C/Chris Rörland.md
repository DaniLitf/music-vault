# Chris Rörland

**Guitarist. Swedish. Born 1986-12-27.**

Chris Rörland is a Swedish guitarist known for his work with the symphonic power metal band Sabaton, contributing to the band's distinctive sound and live performances. He has also been associated with the metal band Nocturnal Rites as a guitarist, maintaining an active presence in the Swedish metal music scene.
