# Christoffer Larsson

**Bassist and guitarist. Swedish.**

Christoffer Larsson is a Swedish musician who played bass for Driller Killer from 2005 to 2009 and switched to guitar for the band's final year. He joined during the Driller Killer's final lineup configuration and performed on their later releases and live shows.
