# Charlie Watts

**Charlie Watts was a British drummer and founding member of The Rolling Stones, whose jazz-influenced, understated style underpinned the band's sound for nearly six decades.**

Widely considered one of rock's greatest drummers, Watts was also a passionate jazz collector and performer. He died on August 24, 2021, aged 80.
