**Backing vocalist. Jamaican.**

Cherry Smith was a Jamaican singer and an early member of the Wailers, providing backing vocals alongside [[Members/B/Beverley Kelso]] on the group's early recordings at Studio One. She left the group in 1966 and later moved to the United States, where she died in 2008.

## Associated Artists
