# Corpo-Mente

**Electronic/avant-garde band collective.**

Corpo-Mente is a Finnish electronic and avant-garde music collective founded circa 2015, centered around composer and electronic musician Gautier Serre (French-born, 1984). The project brings together multiple musicians and electronic producers to create experimental electronic and ambient soundscapes. The core creative force is Serre, with various collaborators contributing to the project's evolving sound across releases and live performances. The band represents a collaborative approach to electronic music composition and production within the experimental music scene.
