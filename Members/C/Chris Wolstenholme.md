# Chris Wolstenholme

**Bassist and backing vocalist. Born 1978.**

Chris Wolstenholme is the co-founder and bassist of Muse, a founding member since the band's formation in 1994 alongside Matt Bellamy and Dominic Howard. In addition to his foundational bass work, Wolstenholme contributes backing vocals and has occasionally taken lead vocal duties on select tracks throughout Muse's career. His bass lines have evolved alongside the band's sound, ranging from straightforward alternative rock foundations to intricate, synth-driven passages in their later progressive and electronic-influenced work.
