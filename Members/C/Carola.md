# Carola

**Singer. Born 1966-09-12.**

Carola Häggkvist, known simply as Carola, is a Swedish pop and schlager singer who won Eurovision in 1991 with "Fångad av en stormvind" and is one of the best-selling artists in Swedish history — her 1983 debut *Främling* remains the best-selling album ever in Sweden. She has balanced mainstream commercial pop with a prominent Christian gospel music career throughout her multi-decade recording history, representing Sweden at Eurovision three times (1983, 1991, 2006).
