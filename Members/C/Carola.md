# Carola

**Carola Häggkvist, known simply as Carola, is a Swedish pop and schlager singer who won Eurovision in 1991 with "Fångad av en stormvind" and is one of the best-selling artists in Swedish history.**

She has balanced mainstream commercial pop with a prominent Christian gospel music career throughout her multi-decade recording history.
