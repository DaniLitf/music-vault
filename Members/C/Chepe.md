# Chepe

**Rapper/Producer. Swedish.**

Chepe (Christian Salazar Campos) is a Swedish rapper and producer known for his work with the hip-hop group The Latin Kings, where he was a key member during their active years. He has also collaborated with The Salazar Brothers, contributing to Swedish hip-hop and urban music scenes.
