# Clive Deamer

**Drummer, percussionist. Born February 1961.**

Clive Deamer is an English session drummer and percussionist from Bath, Somerset, best known as the touring and session drummer for [[Artists/P/Portishead]] and as the second drummer for [[Artists/R/Radiohead]] alongside Philip Selway since 2011, appearing on their Grammy-nominated album *A Moon Shaped Pool*. His work with Portishead and Roni Size resulted in two Mercury Prize-winning albums: *Dummy* (1994) and *New Forms* (1997). A versatile musician spanning jazz, alternative rock, and trip-hop, Deamer has also played with Robert Plant and the Strange Sensation, Hawkwind (1983–1985), Big Town Playboys, Jeff Beck, Siouxsie Sioux, Goldfrapp, and Tom Jones. He is a founding member of the Bristol-based jazz quartet Get the Blessing, formed in 2000.
