# Clive Deamer

**Drummer. British.**

Clive Deamer is an English drummer best known as the drummer for trip-hop pioneers Portishead, having performed on all three of their studio albums. He is also a highly sought-after session drummer who has worked with Massive Attack, Roni Size/Reprazent, and Get the Blessing, among others.
