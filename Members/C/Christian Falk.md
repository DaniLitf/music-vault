# Christian Falk

**Bass. Swedish. Founding member of [[Artists/I/Imperiet]].**

Christian Falk joined Imperiet as bassist when it formed from the ashes of Ebba Grön in 1983, remaining through the band's run until its 1988 breakup. He later became a successful pop producer and songwriter.
