# Craig Powell

**Lead guitarist. American.**

Craig "Screamer" Powell was the lead guitarist of Mazarati, an American R&B and funk band from Minneapolis active from 1986 to 1989. Known by his nickname "Screamer," he brought a rock edge to the band's funk and R&B sound on their self-titled debut album on Paisley Park Records and the follow-up *Mazarati 2* on Motown. After Mazarati's disbandment, Powell later reformed the band under the name Mazarati Revisited alongside keyboardist Marr Starr, performing the group's back catalogue and planning new material.
