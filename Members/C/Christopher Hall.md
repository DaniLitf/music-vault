# Christopher Hall

**Lead vocalist, guitarist, and keyboardist. American. Born 1965-05-18.**

Robert Christopher Hall is the founding member and primary creative force of Stabbing Westward, serving as the band's lead vocalist, guitarist, and keyboard programmer since its formation in 1985. Known for his raw emotional delivery and innovative use of drum machines and electronic elements, Hall has been instrumental in defining the industrial rock sound that Stabbing Westward pioneered. He has also pursued solo and side projects, including the band The Dreaming, which he founded in 2001.
