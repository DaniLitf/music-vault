**Backing vocalist. Jamaican.**

Constantine "Vision" Walker was a Jamaican backing vocalist who performed with the Wailers from 1966 to 1967, contributing to the group's transitional period following the departure of [[Members/J/Junior Braithwaite]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/B/Bob Marley]] – backing vocals (1966–1967)
