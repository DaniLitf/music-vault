# Chuck (Chaos UK drummer)

**Drummer. British.**

Chuck is a British punk drummer best known as the long-time drummer of Chaos UK. He joined in 1983 as part of the classic Mower/Gabba/Chaos/Chuck lineup and played on *Short Sharp Shock*, *Earslaughter*, and *The Chipping Sodbury Bonfire Tapes*. He left briefly in 1992 but returned and continues to perform with the band.
