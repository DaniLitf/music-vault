# Colin Greenwood

**Bassist. Born 1969-06-26 in Oxford, England.**

Colin Greenwood is the bassist and a founding member of Radiohead. He is the elder brother of Jonny Greenwood. His bass playing provides the melodic and rhythmic foundation across Radiohead's catalog, adapting from conventional bass work to synthesizer and programming as the band moved toward electronic territory.
