# Christopher Beckham

**Guitarist, bassist, vocalist.**

Christopher "Floyd" Beckham is an American multi-instrumentalist from the Florida hardcore scene. A former [[Artists/M/Morning Again]] bass guitarist, he joined [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]] on bass in 1997 and stayed until its 1998 breakup. He later joined [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] in late 2000, playing bass and then guitar with backing vocals on *Morningleaver / This Is Me Hating You* (2001), *Won* (2001) and *A Young Trophy Band in the Parlance of Our Times* (2002); after Damien Moyal quit in February 2002, Beckham briefly switched to lead vocals before leaving the band in July 2002.
