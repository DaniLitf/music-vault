# Carl Palmer

**British drummer and percussionist. Born 20 March 1950.**

Carl Frederick Palmer is a British drummer and percussionist best known as the drummer of progressive rock bands Emerson, Lake & Palmer and Asia. He is considered one of the most technically proficient rock drummers, known for his powerful style and extensive use of gongs and percussion.
