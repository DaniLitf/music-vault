# Calle Fredriksson

**Guitarist. Swedish.**

Calle Fredriksson is a Swedish guitarist for Alarma Man, an instrumental post-punk/math-rock band from Gothenburg, appearing on the band's 2005 self-titled debut album and 2010's *Love Forever*. Per Discogs, Alarma Man is his only documented recording credit.
