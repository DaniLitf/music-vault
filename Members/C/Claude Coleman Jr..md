# Claude Coleman Jr.

**Drummer and vocalist. American. Born 1968-08-29.**

Claude Coleman Jr. is the drummer and multi-instrumentalist for the experimental rock duo Ween, which he co-founded in 1989 with Dean Ween. Known for his versatile approach to percussion and his contributions to Ween's eccentric, genre-defying sound, Coleman Jr. has remained with the band through its various iterations and hiatuses. Beyond Ween, he has pursued solo work and appeared with other projects including Eagles of Death Metal and Amandla, showcasing his diverse musical interests.
