# Canan Rosén

**Guitarist and vocalist. Swedish.**

Canan Rosén is the guitarist and co-vocalist of Stockholm punk band Twin Pigs, formed in 2015, and serves as the band's primary riff-writer, bringing ideas to rehearsal for the group to develop together. She cites riot grrrl and emo as key influences on her songwriting.
