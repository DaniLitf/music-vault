# Chris Watson

**Chris Watson is a British musician and sound artist who was an original member of Cabaret Voltaire from 1973 to 1981, and subsequently became one of the world's leading field recording artists and sound designers.**

After leaving Cabaret Voltaire, he joined the avant-garde group Hafler Trio and has built an acclaimed career recording natural and industrial soundscapes, with work for wildlife documentaries and art installations.
