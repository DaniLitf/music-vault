# Cari Lekebusch

**Cari Lekebusch is a Swedish techno producer and DJ from Stockholm, one of the most prolific artists in the Swedish and European underground techno scene since the early 1990s.**

Operating through his own H-Productions label and releasing under numerous aliases, Lekebusch has built an enormous catalogue across multiple decades and helped define a Nordic approach to functional, hard-edged techno.
