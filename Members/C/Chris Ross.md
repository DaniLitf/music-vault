# Chris Ross

**Bassist/Keyboardist. Australian.**

Chris Ross is an Australian bassist and keyboardist known for his work with the rock band Wolfmother from 2004 to 2008, during a key period of the band's development. Beyond Wolfmother, he has participated in other projects including Good Heavens (2), The Army Of Shadows, The Slew, and Ambulance Men.
