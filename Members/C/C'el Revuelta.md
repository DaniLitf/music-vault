# C'el Revuelta

**Bass. Died 2017-05-03.**

C'el Revuelta replaced Kira Roessler on bass for [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] in 1986, playing on the band's final tour that year without appearing on any official studio release, and later returned for the band's 2003 reunion shows. He died on May 3, 2017, after a long battle with stage 4 brain cancer.
