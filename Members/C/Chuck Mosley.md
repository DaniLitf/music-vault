# Chuck Mosley

**Lead vocalist. American. Born 1959-12-26. Died 2017-11-09.**

Chuck Mosley was an American vocalist best known as the original lead singer of Faith No More from 1984 to 1988, during which time the band developed their signature mathcore and funk metal sound. His raw, energetic vocal style was integral to the band's early identity and their landmark debut album *Introduce Yourself* (1987). In addition to Faith No More, Mosley performed with numerous other bands including Bad Brains, Cement, VUA, and Primitive Race, maintaining an active presence in underground music until his death in 2017.
