# Cosmic

**Rapper. Swedish.**

Tommy Isacsson, known as Cosmic (or Cos.M.I.C), is a Swedish rapper recruited by founding members Promoe and Embee into Looptroop in 1993 for their debut EP *Superstars*. He left the group indefinitely in 2007 to spend more time with family, but rejoined by 2010 and appeared on the album *Professional Dreamers* (2011).
