**Guitarist. American.**

Carlos Cordon is an American guitarist who played in the Chicago heavy metal band White Heat in the early 1980s. He was also a member of the band Inner Silence. He played alongside vocalist Jeff Hatrix, bassist Steve Craiglow, and drummer John Perkins in White Heat's only lineup.
