# Chuck Dukowski

**Bassist and vocalist. American. Born 1954-02-01.**

Chuck Dukowski (born Gary Arthur McDaniel) is an American bassist and vocalist known for his aggressive playing style and significant contributions to Black Flag from 1977 to 1983, where he played on every release from *Nervous Breakdown* (1979) to *Everything Went Black* (1982). An outspoken anarchist, he shaped much of the band's political ethos. Before and after Black Flag, he has been active in numerous other projects including Würm, SWA, October Faction, FLAG, and The Chuck Dukowski Sextet, demonstrating his versatility across punk, hardcore, and experimental music.
