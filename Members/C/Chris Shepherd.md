# Chris Shepherd

**Guitarist. British.**

Chris Shepherd is a guitarist who played with [[Artists/T/The Featherz]] during the 2016–2017 lineup. He appears in the documented period around the "Forget All You Know" video and *Five-Year-Itch* material.
