# Charlie Quintana

**Drums, percussion, backing vocals.**

Charlie "Chalo" Quintana is an American drummer best known for his work with [[Artists/I/Izzy Stradlin and the Ju Ju Hounds]], [[The Plugz]], and [[Artists/B/Bob Dylan]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Izzy Stradlin and the Ju Ju Hounds]] – drums (1992–1994)
- [[The Plugz]] – drummer (punk band)
- [[Artists/B/Bob Dylan]] – touring drummer
- [[Social Distortion]] – session/touring drummer
- [[Cruzados]] – drummer

## Biography

Quintana was a founding member of the pioneering LA punk band The Plugz. He later drummed for Bob Dylan's touring band before joining the Ju Ju Hounds. His versatile style spans punk, rock, and roots music.
