# Claude Schnell

**Keyboardist. American. Born 1955-05-17.**

Claude Schnell is an American keyboardist best known for his work with the heavy metal band Dio, with whom he performed from 1983 to 1990. His keyboard contributions added orchestral and symphonic elements to Dio's classic period, enhancing the band's dramatic sound. Beyond Dio, Schnell has worked with other bands including Last in Line and Magic, maintaining an active presence in the heavy metal scene and contributing to numerous studio and live recordings.
