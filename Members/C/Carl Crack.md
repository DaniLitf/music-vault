# Carl Crack

**Vocalist. Born May 5, 1971. Died September 6, 2001.**

Carl Crack was a German vocalist and performer best known as a member of the influential industrial and digital hardcore band Atari Teenage Riot, joining in 1992. He was the primary vocalist on the group's most significant releases during the 1990s, contributing to their aggressive, politically charged sound that blended hardcore punk aesthetics with electronic and industrial elements. Crack died in 2001 at the age of 30, ending his active period with Atari Teenage Riot.
