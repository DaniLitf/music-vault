# Charlie Benante

**Drummer. American. Born 1962-11-27.**

Charlie Benante is the drummer for the thrash metal band Anthrax, having joined in 1983 and remaining a core member throughout their career. He is also known for his work with Stormtroopers of Death (SOD), a crossover metal side project, and has participated in touring with Pantera starting in 2022.
