# Charlie Clouser

**Keyboardist, programmer, producer, composer. Born 19 June 1963 in Hanover, New Hampshire, USA.**

Charlie Clouser is an American keyboardist, programmer, and composer best known as a former member of [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] (1994–2000) and for composing the theme music for the *Saw* film franchise. During his time with NIN, he contributed to *The Downward Spiral* (1994) tour and performed on *The Fragile* (1999), contributing keyboards and programming.

## Biography

Clouser began his career as an engineer and producer before joining Nine Inch Nails in 1994. He replaced James Woolley as the band's keyboardist and contributed to the *Further Down the Spiral* remix album and *The Fragile*. After leaving NIN in 2000, he established himself as a film and television composer, writing the iconic theme for the *Saw* franchise. He has also produced remixes for artists including Marilyn Manson and Rob Zombie.
