# Chris Gibbs

**Bassist. Born in USA.**

Chris Gibbs is an American bassist who toured with the comedic metal project [[Artists/B/Big Dumb Face]] in 2001 in support of *Duke Lion Fights the Terror!!*. Within the band he performed under the stage name "Sinister T-Bone Bass Slapper".
