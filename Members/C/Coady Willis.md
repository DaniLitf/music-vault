# Coady Willis

**Drummer. American.**

Coady Willis is an American drummer known for [[Artists/B/Big Business]] and his tenure as a second drummer in [[Artists/T/The Melvins]]. He joined Melvins with Jared Warren in 2006 and returned for later touring, including dates where he filled in while Dale Crover recovered from surgery.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] – drums (2006–2016, 2023–present touring/second drummer)
- [[Artists/B/Big Business]] – drums
