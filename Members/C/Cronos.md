**Vocalist and bassist. British. Born January 15, 1963.**

Conrad Thomas Lant, known professionally as Cronos, is the founding member, vocalist, and bassist of the pioneering thrash and black metal band Venom. He formed Venom in 1979 and has been instrumental in shaping the band's sonic identity and visual aesthetic, designing their iconic logo and album artwork. Beyond Venom, Cronos has collaborated with numerous metal acts including Probot, Cradle of Filth, and Massacre, establishing himself as a prolific and influential figure in extreme metal.
