# Craig Fox

**Lead vocals, guitar. American. Founding member of [[Artists/T/The Greenhornes]].**

Craig Fox co-founded The Greenhornes in 1996 and remained the band's vocalist and guitarist throughout its run, even as bandmates Jack Lawrence and Patrick Keeler spent much of the mid-2000s working with The Raconteurs.
