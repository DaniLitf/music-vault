# Cole Whittle

**Bassist, keyboardist, singer. American.**

Cole Whittle is an American musician best known as the bassist and keyboardist for DNCE from its formation in 2015 until his departure in 2019. He was previously a member of the glam rock band Semi Precious Weapons. He co-wrote several DNCE songs including "Body Moves" and "Unsweet."
