# Carlton Barrett

**Carlton Barrett was a Jamaican drummer and co-leader of the Wailers, whose reggae drumming — particularly his one-drop style — defined the sound of Bob Marley's classic recordings.**

Along with his brother Aston, he formed the rhythmic core of the Wailers from 1969. He was murdered in Kingston, Jamaica in 1987.
