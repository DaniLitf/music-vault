# Chris Corner

**Vocalist/Guitarist. British. Born 1974-01-23.**

Chris Corner is a British vocalist and guitarist best known as the lead singer and primary songwriter of the electronic rock band Sneaker Pimps (1994–2005, 2015–present), which achieved significant commercial and critical success during the 1990s electronic music boom. He is also the founder and lead artist of IAMX (2004–present), an electronic solo project that has established a devoted international following.
