# Claudia Sarne

**Singer. Born in the United Kingdom.**

Claudia Sarne is a British singer who co-founded the band 12 Rounds with her husband Atticus Ross. She provided lead vocals on both of the band's albums, *Jitter Juice* (1996) and *My Big Hero* (1998), and performed on their tours with Sneaker Pimps and Marilyn Manson.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/0-9/12 Rounds]] – lead vocals (1995–2000)

## Biography

Sarne met Atticus Ross in London, and together they formed 12 Rounds in the mid-1990s. Her vocals defined the band's trip-hop sound. After the band went on hiatus, she has maintained a low public profile while Ross's career in film composition and Nine Inch Nails continued.
