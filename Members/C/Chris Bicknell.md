# Chris Bicknell

**American musician and producer. Born 1960.**

Chris Bicknell is an American musician and producer best known as a multi-instrumentalist and programmer of the experimental/industrial band Controlled Bleeding. He has contributed extensively to the band's output since the early 1980s.
