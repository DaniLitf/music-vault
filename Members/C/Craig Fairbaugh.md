# Craig Fairbaugh

**Rhythm guitar, keyboards, backing vocals.**

Craig Fairbaugh is an American guitarist who served as rhythm guitarist of [[Artists/0-9/+44]] from early 2006 until the band went inactive in 2009, joining after Carol Heller's departure. Before +44 he played in the Los Angeles punk band Mercy Killers, and he has also performed with Lars Frederiksen and the Bastards.
