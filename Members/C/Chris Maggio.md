# Chris Maggio

**Drummer. American.**

Chris Maggio drummed for grindcore band Trap Them from 2009 to 2013, the band's longest-serving drummer of that era, playing through their Maryland Deathfest appearance and touring cycles before Brad Fickeisen took over in 2013.
