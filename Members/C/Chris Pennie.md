# Chris Pennie

**Drums. American. Founding member of [[Artists/T/The Dillinger Escape Plan]].**

Chris Pennie co-founded The Dillinger Escape Plan in 1997, drumming on the band's early EPs and first three studio albums. He left in 2007 amid writing *Ire Works*, partly due to a strained relationship with Ben Weinman, and went on to join Coheed and Cambria.
