# Chad Yaro

**Guitarist, vocalist. American.**

Chad Yaro is a guitarist and vocalist for the Southern California punk rock band [[Artists/F/Face to Face]]. Yaro joined the band in 1994 as an additional guitarist and remained a core member through their classic era, appearing on albums including *Face to Face* (1996), *Standards and Practices* (1999), and *Reactionary* (2000). He departed in 2000 to focus on family, rejoined for the 2008 reunion, and left again in 2014.
