# Chris Kosnik

**American bassist and vocalist.**

Chris Kosnik is an American bassist who played in the band Godspeed before co-founding the Atomic Bitchwax in the late 1990s, of which he remains a member. In 2013 he joined Monster Magnet as bassist, replacing Jim Baglino.
