# Cassandra O'Neal

**Keyboardist, vocalist. Born in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

Cassandra O'Neal is an American keyboardist and vocalist who joined Prince's New Power Generation in 2010. She performed on *20Ten* and toured with Prince during his Welcome 2 and Hit and Run tours. She has also played with Sheila E. and various Minneapolis-area artists.
