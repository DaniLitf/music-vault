# Chad Blinman

**American record producer, engineer, and former live keyboardist of Das Ich.**

Chad Blinman performed keyboards as a live member of Das Ich from 1994 to 1996. He is better known as a record producer and engineer, producing Face to Face's *Standards and Practices*, *Ignorance Is Bliss*, and *Reactionary*, as well as work with Saves the Day, Alkaline Trio, and Viva Death. He later served as an associate professor at Berklee College of Music.
