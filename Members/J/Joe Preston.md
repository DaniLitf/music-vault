# Joe Preston

**Bassist. American.**

Joe Preston is an American bassist who played in [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] during the early 1990s and recorded the *Joe Preston* solo EP in the band's KISS-inspired EP series. He has also been associated with heavy and experimental underground projects beyond Melvins.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] – bass (1991–1992)
