# Jean-Yves Thériault

**Bass. Born December 15, 1962.**

Jean-Yves "Blacky" Thériault is a Canadian bassist, composer, and producer who co-founded [[Artists/V/Voivod]] in 1982, leaving in 1991 to co-found the contemporary dance company Holy Body Tattoo. He rejoined Voivod in 2008, recording *Target Earth* (2013), before departing again in 2014.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/V/Voivod]] – bass (1982–1991, 2008–2014, founding member)
