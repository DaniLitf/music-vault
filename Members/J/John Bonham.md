# John Bonham

**British drummer. Born 31 May 1948, died 25 September 1980.**

John Henry Bonham was an English drummer best known as the drummer of Led Zeppelin (1968–1980). He is widely regarded as one of the greatest and most influential drummers in the history of rock music, known for his powerful, thunderous style and technical virtuosity. His death in 1980 led to the dissolution of Led Zeppelin.
