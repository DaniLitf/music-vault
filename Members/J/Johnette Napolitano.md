# Johnette Napolitano

**Vocals, bass. American. Born 1957-03-22.**

Johnette Napolitano is an American singer and bassist, best known as the frontwoman of [[Artists/C/Concrete Blonde]]. She was the primary lead vocalist for [[Artists/T/The Heads]] on their album *No Talking, Just Head* (1996), including the single "Damage I've Done," and toured with the band as their main frontwoman in 1996.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/C/Concrete Blonde]] – lead vocals, bass, co-founder
- [[Artists/T/The Heads]] – guest/touring vocals (1996)
