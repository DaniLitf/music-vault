# Jason "Mr Gore" Merle

**Vocalist. British.**

Jason "Mr Gore" Merle was a founding vocalist of deathgrind band Gorerotted, performing with the band from 1997 to 2003. Before joining, he had already performed as a DJ under the name "Mr Gore and his Mobile Metal Roadshow" at venues around Walthamstow; in the band he contributed high-pitched punk-style yells and screams that contrasted with Goreskin's deep death growls, defining the early dual-vocal sound on *Mutilated in Minutes* and *Only Tools and Corpses*. After leaving Gorerotted, he formed the project Gorebound alongside fellow original Gorerotted vocalist Graham "Nutty Strangeways" Hodis.
