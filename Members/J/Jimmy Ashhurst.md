# Jimmy Ashhurst

**Bass, backing vocals.**

Jimmy Ashhurst is an American bassist best known as a member of [[Artists/I/Izzy Stradlin and the Ju Ju Hounds]] and [[Broken Homes]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Izzy Stradlin and the Ju Ju Hounds]] – bass, backing vocals (1992–1994)
- [[Broken Homes]] – bassist
- [[Members/I/Izzy Stradlin]] – session bassist (solo albums)

## Biography

Ashhurst co-wrote several songs on the Ju Ju Hounds' debut album and continued to collaborate with Izzy Stradlin on his subsequent solo releases.
