# Jonny Greenwood

**Guitarist, multi-instrumentalist. Born 1971-11-05 in Oxford, England.**

Jonny Greenwood is the lead guitarist and primary instrumentalist of Radiohead, responsible for much of the band's sonic experimentation. He plays guitar, keyboards, ondes Martenot, and orchestral instruments. Alongside his work with Radiohead, he has become one of the most respected film score composers of his generation, with work for Paul Thomas Anderson (*There Will Be Blood*, *Phantom Thread*, *The Power of the Dog*), winning numerous awards. He is the brother of Colin Greenwood.
