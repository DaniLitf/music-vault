# Josh Homme

**Guitarist, vocalist, producer. Born 1973-05-17 in Joshua Tree, California, USA.**

Josh Homme is the founder and driving force of Queens of the Stone Age, one of the defining rock acts of the 2000s. He co-founded Kyuss in the late 1980s, developing the palm desert sound before dissolving the band in 1995 and forming QOTSA. Known for his baritone vocals, melodic guitar riffs, and meticulous production sensibility, he has also produced albums for other artists, co-founded the Desert Sessions project, and been a key collaborator across heavy rock and alternative music.
