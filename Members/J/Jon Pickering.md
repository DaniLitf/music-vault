# Jon Pickering

**Vocalist, bassist, guitarist. British.**

Jon Pickering is a British punk musician and founding member of Doom, originally playing bass and vocals in the precursor band The Subverters. He was the band's primary vocalist during their classic early period (1987–1990) and also played guitar and bass at various times.
