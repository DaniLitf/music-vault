# John Contreras

**Bassist. American.**

John Contreras is a punk rock bassist from Chicago who played bass for [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]] during the band's 2009–2010 reunion, stepping in when founding bassist Joe Principe was unavailable due to [[Artists/R/Rise Against]] commitments. He appears on the live album *88 Fingers Louie LIVES* (2009), recorded at Chicago's Bottom Lounge.
