# Jeremy "Milky" Gray

**Guitarist. British.**

Jeremy "Milky" Gray was an English guitarist and founding member of deathgrind band Gorerotted, playing guitar from 1997 to 1999. He left after recording the *Her Gash I Did Slash* demo to join the band Infestation full-time.
