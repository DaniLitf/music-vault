# John Blackwell

**Drummer. Born 9 September 1973 in New Orleans, Louisiana. Died 4 July 2017.**

John Blackwell was an American drummer who played with Prince's New Power Generation from 2002 to 2013. He appeared on *Musicology*, *3121*, *Planet Earth*, and numerous Prince tours. He also played with Cameo and D'Angelo before his death from a brain tumor in 2017.
