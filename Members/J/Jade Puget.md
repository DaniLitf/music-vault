# Jade Puget

**American musician. Born 11 November 1973.**

Jade Puget is an American musician, best known as the guitarist and keyboardist of the rock band AFI. He joined AFI in 1998 and has been a key contributor to the band's sound since *The Art of Drowning* (2000). He is also the guitarist of the electronic band Blaqk Audio.
