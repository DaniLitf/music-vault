# Jerry Harrison

**Keyboards, guitar, backing vocals. Born February 21, 1949.**

Jerry Harrison is an American musician, best known as the guitarist and keyboardist for [[Artists/T/Talking Heads]] and previously of [[Artists/T/The Modern Lovers]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/Talking Heads]]
- [[Artists/T/The Modern Lovers]]
- [[Artists/T/The Heads]] – keyboards, guitar, production, co-founder (1994–1996)

## Biography
Harrison first gained attention as a member of the pioneering proto-punk band The Modern Lovers. He joined Talking Heads in 1977, replacing an early member, and remained through the band's entire career. He also worked as a producer for bands like Live and Crash Test Dummies.
