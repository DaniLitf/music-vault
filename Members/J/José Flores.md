# José Flores

**Drummer.**

José Flores, nicknamed "Little Jorge", is a drummer who joined [[Artists/B/Bird of Ill Omen]] in August 1997 after George Rios's departure, playing the band's fall shows and December 1997 tour. When the band returned from Canada that December, his expired student visa saw him denied re-entry into the United States — an event that put the band on permanent hiatus — and he eventually relocated to Spain.
