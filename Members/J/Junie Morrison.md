# Junie Morrison

**Keyboards, multi-instrumentalist, vocals, songwriter. American. Musical director of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Junie Morrison, previously of the Ohio Players and a solo artist, joined P-Funk in 1978 as musical director, composing or co-writing several of the collective's biggest hits and serving as lead vocalist, producer, and arranger. He stopped touring with the band after 1981 but continued contributing to later albums, sometimes credited as J.S. Theracon. He died in 2017.
