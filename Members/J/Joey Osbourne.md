# Joey Osbourne

**Drummer/Vocalist. American.**

Joey Osbourne is an American drummer and vocalist known for his work with the stoner rock band Altamont (1994–present), where he performs dynamic percussion and vocal contributions. He is also associated with the heavy rock band Acid King, contributing to the underground metal and stoner rock scenes.
