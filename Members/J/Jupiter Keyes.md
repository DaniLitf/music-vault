# Jupiter Keyes

**Guitar, synthesizer. American. Former member of [[Artists/H/HEALTH]].**

Jupiter Keyes, a university acquaintance of Jake Duzsik, was a founding member of HEALTH in 2005, playing guitar and synthesizer. He left the band in 2015 during the European tour for *Death Magic*.
