**Singer, songwriter, and pop songwriter. Born 1980.**

Justin tranter is an American singer, songwriter, and pop songwriter best known as the frontman of [[Artists/S/Semi Precious Weapons]] (2004–2014). After the band disbanded, tranter became one of pop's most successful songwriters, co-penning hits including Justin Bieber's "Sorry" (Billboard No. 1), Selena Gomez's "Good for You," and Gwen Stefani's "Used to Love You." tranter continues to write major pop songs as of 2025.
