# James Black

**Lead guitar, backing vocals. Canadian. Founding member of [[Artists/F/Finger Eleven]].**

James Black has played lead guitar in Finger Eleven since its 1990 formation, and introduced drummer Rich Beddoe to the band after meeting him at an Alice in Chains concert in Toronto.
