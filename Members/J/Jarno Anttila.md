# Jarno Anttila

**Guitarist. Finnish.**

Jarno Anttila was the longest-serving guitarist in Impaled Nazarene, playing with the band from 1992 to 2010. His 18-year tenure spanned many of the band's most influential albums and helped define their extreme metal guitar sound.
