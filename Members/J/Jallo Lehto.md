# Jallo Lehto

**Drums. Finnish.**

Jallo Lehto is a Finnish drummer who played for [[Artists/D/Disfear]] from 1989 to 1995, appearing on their early EPs and debut album *Soul Scars*. He replaced Jan Axelsson in the band's first year and was a part of the lineup that recorded *A Brutal Sight of War* and *Soul Scars*.
