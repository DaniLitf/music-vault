# John Bates

**Vocalist.**

John Bates is an American vocalist who contributed vocals to the early solo-record sessions that became [[Artists/B/Black Light Burns]] (circa 2003–2005). His contributions were removed as Wes Borland found his own voice as a singer, and Bates did not appear on the released albums.

## Associated Artists
