# Jennie Asplund

**Guitarist. Born 24 November 1979 in Robertsfors.**

Jennie Asplund is a Swedish guitarist who co-founded Sahara Hotnights in 1991 with her sister Johanna and childhood friends from Robertsfors. The band's 2001 album *Jennie Bomb* is titled after her nickname.
