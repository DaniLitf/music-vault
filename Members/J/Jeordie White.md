# Jeordie White

**Bassist, guitarist. Born 20 June 1971 in Starke, Florida, USA.**

Jeordie White, formerly known by the stage name Twiggy Ramirez, is an American bassist and guitarist best known as a founding member of [[Artists/M/Marilyn Manson]] (1993–2014, 2016–2017) and a touring musician for [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] (2005–2008). He also played with [[Artists/A/A Perfect Circle]] (2013–2014) and contributed to projects by [[Artists/F/Foo Fighters]] and [[Artists/G/Guns N' Roses]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/F/Foo Fighters]] – touring bassist (2014)
- [[Artists/G/Guns N' Roses]] – bassist during *Chinese Democracy* sessions

## Biography

White co-founded Marilyn Manson with frontman Brian Hugh Warner and played on all major Marilyn Manson releases from *Portrait of an American Family* (1994) through *The Pale Emperor* (2014) before parting ways. In 2005, he joined Nine Inch Nails for the *With Teeth* tour, playing bass. In 2013, he replaced long-time bassist Jeordie (no relation) in A Perfect Circle. He has battled legal issues and changed his stage name back to his birth name Jeordie White in 2018.
