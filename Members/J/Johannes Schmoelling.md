# Johannes Schmoelling

**Keyboardist, composer. German. Born 1950.**

Johannes Schmoelling is a German electronic musician who was a member of [[Artists/T/Tangerine Dream]] from 1980 to 1987, replacing Peter Baumann. He played on albums including *Tangram* and *Logos* and also released several solo albums.
