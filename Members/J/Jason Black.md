**Bassist. American.**

Jason Black is the bassist of Hot Water Music and a co-founder of the band. He initially met George Rebelo while playing together in jazz bands in school and was studying at the University of Florida when he encouraged the other members to move to Gainesville. Black has been the band's anchor in the rhythm section since their 1994 formation. Beyond Hot Water Music, he was a member of Senses Fail from 2008 to 2012, playing on their albums Life Is Not a Waiting Room and The Fire.
