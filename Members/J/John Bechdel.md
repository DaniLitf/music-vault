# John Bechdel

**Keyboardist. Born 23 August 1964 in Beech Creek, Pennsylvania, USA.**

John Bechdel is an American keyboardist who became a fixture of the industrial rock scene, serving as Killing Joke's live keyboardist around the *Extremities* era before joining the supergroup Murder, Inc. He went on to long tenures with Prong, Fear Factory, and Ministry, where he remains the touring keyboardist.
