# Josh Eustis

**Electronic musician, producer, engineer.**

Josh Eustis is an American electronic musician best known as the founder of [[Artists/T/Telefon Tel Aviv]]. He contributed synthesizers, sound design and engineering to [[Artists/B/Black Light Burns]]' debut *Cruel Melody* (2007) and has been a touring and studio member of [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/Telefon Tel Aviv]] – founder, production, vocals (1999–present)
- [[Artists/B/Black Light Burns]] – synthesizers, sound design, engineering (2005–2007)
- [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] – touring/studio member (2013–2014)
