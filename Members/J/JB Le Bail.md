# JB Le Bail

**Harsh vocals (2021–present).**

JB Le Bail (Jean-Baptiste Le Bail) is a French vocalist best known for his growled extreme vocals in [[Artists/I/Igorrr]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Igorrr]] – harsh vocals (2021–present)
- [[Svart Crown]] – vocalist (black/death metal)
- [[Dirty Black Summer]] – vocalist

## Biography

Le Bail joined Igorrr in 2021 alongside guitarist Martyn Clément and vocalist Aphrodite Patoulidou, replacing departing vocalists Laurent Lunoir and Laure Le Prunenec. He provides harsh/growled vocals for the band's live shows and recordings.
