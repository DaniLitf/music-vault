# Jack White

**Guitarist, vocalist, producer. American.**

Jack White is an American musician best known as the co-founder, guitarist, and vocalist of [[Artists/T/The White Stripes]], with whom he built a stripped-down blues-punk sound into one of the defining rock acts of the 2000s. He has since co-founded [[Artists/T/The Raconteurs]] and [[Artists/T/The Dead Weather]], and pursued a solo career, while also producing albums for other artists including Loretta Lynn.
