# John Carroll

**Drummer, backing vocalist. American.**

John Carroll is a punk rock drummer from Chicago who joined [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]] in 1998 for the *Back on the Streets* era and has drummed in every lineup since, including the 2009 reunion and the 2013–present run. Between 88 Fingers Louie stints he drummed for the Chicago noise-punk band [[Artists/P/Paper Mice]]. Carroll has been an integral part of the band's reunion-era albums and tours.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]] – drums, backing vocals (1998–1999, 2009–2010, 2013–present)
- [[Artists/P/Paper Mice]] – drums
