# Jack Casady

**Jack Casady** (born April 13, 1944) is an American bassist best known as a member of [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]] and co-founder of [[Artists/H/Hot Tuna]].

- [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]] – bass, rhythm guitar (1965–1972, 1989, 1996)
- [[Artists/H/Hot Tuna]] – bass, guitar, vocals (1969–present)
- [[Artists/J/Jefferson Starship]] – bass (touring)
- [[KBC Band]] – bass (1985–1987)

Casady is known for his melodic, jazz-influenced bass playing. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame as a member of Jefferson Airplane in 1996.
