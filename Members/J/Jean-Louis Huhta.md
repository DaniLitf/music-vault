# Jean-Louis Huhta

**Drums, percussion. Swedish.**

Jean-Louis Huhta was a Swedish drummer and percussionist who played for Anti Cimex from 1984 to 1987. He joined the band during their mid-1980s period and performed on the *Criminal Trap* mini-LP and related releases.
