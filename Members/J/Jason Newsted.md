# Jason Newsted

**Bass. Born March 4, 1963.**

Jason Newsted joined [[Artists/M/Metallica]] in late 1986 as Cliff Burton's replacement, having previously played in the thrash band [[Artists/F/Flotsam and Jetsam]], and remained the band's bassist through *Load*, *Reload*, and *Garage Inc.* before departing in 2001 over creative-control tensions. He went on to join [[Artists/V/Voivod]] and later led his own band [[Artists/N/Newsted]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/F/Flotsam and Jetsam]] – bass (1985–1986)
- [[Artists/V/Voivod]] – bass (2002–2008)
- [[Artists/N/Newsted]] – vocals, bass (2012–2014, founder)
