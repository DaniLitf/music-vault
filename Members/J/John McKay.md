# John McKay

**Guitarist, songwriter. British.**

John McKay is an English guitarist and songwriter who played a pivotal role in the sound of [[Artists/S/Siouxsie and the Banshees]], serving as the band's guitarist from July 1977 until September 1979. He is credited with composing the melody of the band's first hit single "Hong Kong Garden" (1978) and much of the material found on their first two albums, *The Scream* (1978) and *Join Hands* (1979). McKay's innovative, jagged, and unorthodox guitar style—characterized by a "metal-shard roar"—has proven highly influential on subsequent generations of musicians, with artists like Steve Albini, Johnny Marr, Thurston Moore, and The Edge acknowledging his influence. More recently, McKay released his debut solo album *Sixes and Sevens* in 2025.
