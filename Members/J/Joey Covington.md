# Joey Covington

**Joey Covington** (June 27, 1945 – June 4, 2013) was an American drummer best known as a member of [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]] and [[Artists/H/Hot Tuna]].

- [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]] – drums, percussion (1970–1972)
- [[Artists/H/Hot Tuna]] – drums (1971–1973)

Covington replaced [[Members/S/Spencer Dryden]] in 1970 and appeared on the albums *Bark* (1971) and *Long John Silver* (1972).
