**Backing vocalist. Born 23 October 1952.**

Judy Mowatt is a Jamaican singer who was a member of the I Threes, the female backing vocal trio for Bob Marley and the Wailers from 1974 to 1981. She had previously been part of the Gaylettes and went on to a solo career after Marley's death.
