# John Wetton

**British singer, bassist, and keyboardist. Born 12 June 1949, died 31 January 2017.**

John Kenneth Wetton was a British musician best known as the bassist and vocalist of progressive rock band King Crimson (1972–1974) and as the lead vocalist, bassist, and keyboardist of Asia. He also worked with UK, Roxy Music, and Uriah Heep. Wetton was known for his powerful vocal range and melodic bass playing.
