# Jack Lawless

**Drummer. Born September 20, 1987 in New Jersey.**

Jack Lawless is an American musician best known as the drummer for the Jonas Brothers' touring band since 2006 and a former member of the funk-pop band DNCE alongside Joe Jonas. He grew up in Middletown Township, New Jersey and began drumming for the Jonas Brothers in early 2007, replacing Alexander Noyes. He also played drums on Demi Lovato's debut album *Don't Forget* and is a member of the band Ocean Grove.
