# Jim Shapiro

**Jim Shapiro is an American drummer who was a founding member of the Chicago alternative rock band Veruca Salt, playing with the group from 1992 until 1998.**

Shapiro was part of Veruca Salt's classic lineup that recorded *American Thighs* (1994) and *Eight Arms to Hold You* (1997), departing along with Nina Gordon during the band's first split.
