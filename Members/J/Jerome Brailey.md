# Jerome Brailey

**Drums, percussion. American. Former member of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Jerome Brailey was the most prominent drummer in Parliament-Funkadelic during its mid-to-late-1970s commercial peak. He left the collective acrimoniously in 1978 alongside Glenn Goins, going on to form his own band Mutiny, in which he publicly criticized George Clinton's management style.
