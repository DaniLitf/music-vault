# Joe Comeau

**Guitarist.**

Joe Comeau served as Overkill's lead guitarist from 1995 to 1999, playing on *From the Underground and Below* (1997) and *Necroshine* (1999). He later became the vocalist of Liege Lord and has been active in several New Jersey metal acts.
