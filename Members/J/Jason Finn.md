# Jason Finn

**Drummer. American.**

Jason Finn is an American drummer who joined [[Artists/S/Skin Yard]] after Matt Cameron departed for Soundgarden, playing on the band's final album *Inside the Eye* (1993). He later achieved wider success as the drummer of The Presidents of the United States of America.
