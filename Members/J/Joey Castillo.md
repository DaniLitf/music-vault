# Joey Castillo

**Drums. American. Born 1966-03-31.**

Joey Castillo is an American drummer, best known for his tenure with [[Artists/Q/Queens of the Stone Age]] (2004–2012). He filled in on drums for [[Artists/T/The Hives]] during their 2019 US tour with Refused while founding drummer Chris Dangerous recovered from surgery.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/Q/Queens of the Stone Age]] – drums (2004–2012)
- [[Artists/T/The Hives]] – touring drums (2019)
