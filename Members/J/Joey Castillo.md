# Joey Castillo

**Drummer. American.**

Joey Castillo is a drummer best known for his work with [[Artists/Q/Queens of the Stone Age]] from 2002 to 2012. He joined the band after a memorable last-minute audition where he played through half a song at rehearsal before being offered the position for a tour starting the next day. With Queens of the Stone Age, he recorded the studio albums *Lullabies to Paralyze* (2005), *Era Vulgaris* (2007), and contributed to *...Like Clockwork* (2013) before departing. Before joining Queens of the Stone Age, Castillo was the drummer of [[Artists/D/Danzig]] from 1994 to 2002, recording three studio albums with the band. He has also worked with numerous other acts including Circle Jerks, The Bronx, DOOM Regulator, and Zakk Sabbath.
