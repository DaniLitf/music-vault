# James Woolley

**Keyboardist, programmer. Born 11 August 1966.**

James Woolley is an American keyboardist and programmer best known as a former touring member of [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] (1991–1994). He replaced David Hayles during the *Pretty Hate Machine* tour and performed on the *Downward Spiral* tour, contributing keyboard programming to the *Further Down the Spiral* remix album.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] – keyboards, programming (1991–1994)

## Biography

Woolley joined Nine Inch Nails in 1991 as the keyboardist for the *Pretty Hate Machine* tour cycle and remained through the groundbreaking *Self-Destruct* tour (1994–1995) supporting *The Downward Spiral*. He was replaced by Charlie Clouser after the tour ended. He also contributed to the *Further Down the Spiral* remix album (1995).
