# Justin Chancellor

**Justin Chancellor is a British bassist who has been the bassist of Tool since 1995, having replaced Paul D'Amour and appeared on every Tool album from *Ænima* onward.**

Chancellor's melodic, effects-heavy bass style is a key component of Tool's layered sound.
