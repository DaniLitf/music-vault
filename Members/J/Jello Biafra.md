# Jello Biafra

**Jello Biafra (born Eric Boucher) is an American vocalist, lyricist, and political activist, the frontman of Dead Kennedys, and one of the most outspoken political voices in American punk music.**

After Dead Kennedys dissolved, he founded Alternative Tentacles Records, performed spoken word, collaborated with Pigface, and formed Lard with Ministry's Al Jourgensen.
