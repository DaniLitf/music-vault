# Josh Klinghoffer

**Josh Klinghoffer** (born October 3, 1979) is an American multi-instrumentalist and singer best known as a former guitarist of [[Artists/R/Red Hot Chili Peppers]] and for filling in with [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]].

- [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]] – guitar (2022–2023; fill-in)
- [[Artists/R/Red Hot Chili Peppers]] – guitar, keyboards, backing vocals (2009–2019)
- [[Artists/P/Pearl Jam]] – touring guitarist
