# Jon Ekström

**Drums. Swedish.**

Jon Nils Emanuel Ekström is a Swedish musician who co-founded [[Artists/D/Detektivbyrån]] in Gothenburg in 2005, playing drums, sound box, and small bells, before leaving in February 2009 and being replaced by Marcus Sjöberg.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/D/Detektivbyrån]] – drums, sound box, small bells (2005–2009, founding member)
