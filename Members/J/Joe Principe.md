# Joe Principe

**Bassist, vocalist. American.**

Joseph Daniel Principe is the bassist, backing vocalist, and co-founder of the punk rock band [[Artists/R/Rise Against]]. Before Rise Against, he co-founded the Chicago punk band [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]] in 1993 with Dom Vallone, Mr. Precision (Dan Wleklinski), and Denis Buckley, where he remained for six years of touring and recording. After 88 Fingers Louie disbanded in 1999, Principe met Tim McIlrath at a Sick of It All concert and formed Rise Against, which would become one of the most successful punk rock bands of the 2000s. Beyond music, Principe is straight edge, vegan, and an active animal rights advocate.
