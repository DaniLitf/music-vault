# Jesus Mendez Jr.

**Drums. American.**

Jesus Mendez Jr. is an American drummer whom Jason Newsted met during his post-Metallica EchoBrain period; Newsted recruited him as the founding drummer of [[Artists/N/Newsted]] in 2012. He later co-founded the band Underlord with Newsted guitarist Jessie Farnsworth.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/N/Newsted]] – drums (2012–2014, founding member)
