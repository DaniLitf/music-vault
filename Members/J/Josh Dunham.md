# Josh Dunham

**Bassist. Born in the United States.**

Josh Dunham is an American bassist who joined Prince's New Power Generation in 2010 alongside his wife Cora Coleman Dunham. He performed on *20Ten* and toured with Prince. He also recorded with Sheila E. and other Prince-associated artists.
