# Jari Mäenpää

**Jari Mäenpää is a Finnish multi-instrumentalist, vocalist, and the sole creative force behind Wintersun, as well as a former member of Ensiferum.**

His perfectionist approach to production — Wintersun's *Time I* took eight years to release — has become legendary in metal circles.
