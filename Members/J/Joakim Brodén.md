# Joakim Brodén

**Vocalist, keyboardist. Born 1980-01-29 in Falun, Sweden.**

Joakim Brodén is the vocalist and keyboards player of Sabaton, the band he co-founded in 1999. His powerful, clear tenor voice is central to Sabaton's sound, and his keyboard work adds the symphonic/orchestral layer beneath the band's power metal riffs. He has been with the band since their formation and is one of its two founders alongside Pär Sundström.
