**Keyboardist, musical director, and songwriter. Born 3 March 1948.**

Jackie Mittoo was a Jamaican keyboardist, composer, and musical director who was a founding member of [[Artists/T/The Skatalites]] and served as the musical director and keyboardist for the Wailers' early recordings at Studio One from 1963 to 1967. He was one of the most influential instrumentalists in Jamaican music, shaping the sound of ska, rocksteady, and early reggae as the in-house arranger at Studio One.
