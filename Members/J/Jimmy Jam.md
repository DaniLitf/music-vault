# Jimmy Jam

**Keyboardist, songwriter, and record producer. Born 1959-06-06.**

Jimmy Jam is an American musician and record producer best known as one half of the production duo Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis. He was a founding member of The Time, playing keyboards from 1981 to 1983 before being fired alongside Terry Lewis after missing a show due to being stranded in a blizzard. Together with Lewis, he went on to produce dozens of multiplatinum albums for artists including Janet Jackson, Human League, and Boyz II Men, becoming one of the most successful production teams in music history. He has reunited with The Time for anniversary performances.
