# Joe Letz

**Drummer. Born 1980-08-26.**

Joe Letz is an American drummer associated with industrial and electronic rock acts including [[Artists/C/Combichrist]]. He played drums in [[Artists/L/Lindemann (band)]]'s live lineup.
