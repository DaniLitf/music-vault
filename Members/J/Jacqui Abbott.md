# Jacqui Abbott

**Vocals. Born November 10, 1973.**

Jacqui Abbott is an English singer, best known as a vocalist for [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]] from 1994 to 2000.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]]

## Biography
Abbott replaced Briana Corrigan as female vocalist in 1994, featuring on the albums *Miaow*, *Blue Is the Colour*, and *Quench*. She duetted on the hit single "Perfect 10". She left in 2000 and was replaced by Alison Wheeler.
