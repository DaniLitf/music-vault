# James Iha

**James Iha is an American guitarist and co-founder of The Smashing Pumpkins, who played on the band's classic 1990s albums before departing in 2000 and returning for the 2018 reunion.**

During his years away from the Pumpkins, Iha worked as a solo artist, record producer, and as a member of A Perfect Circle and Tinted Windows.
