# Jesper Strömblad

**Jesper Strömblad is a Swedish musician and songwriter, the founder of In Flames, who co-founded Dimension Zero as a side project and wrote much of In Flames' influential early melodic death metal catalogue.**

He founded In Flames in 1990 and wrote much of their material, initially on drums then switching to guitar. He left In Flames in 2010 due to personal issues.
