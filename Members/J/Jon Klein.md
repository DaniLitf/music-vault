# Jon Klein

**Guitarist, producer. British.**

Jonathan David Klein, professionally known as Jon Klein, is an English guitarist and producer born in Bristol on April 9, 1960. He is best known for his seven-year tenure with [[Artists/S/Siouxsie and the Banshees]] from 1987 to 1994, during which he recorded three albums: *Peepshow* (1988), *Superstition* (1991), and *The Rapture* (1995). Known for his playful yet dramatic performance style, Klein influenced other guitarists including Robin Finck of Nine Inch Nails. Beyond the Banshees, Klein has worked as a guitarist and producer with numerous artists including Talvin Singh, Sinéad O'Connor, Shriekback, Fangoria, and more recently Jah Wobble, with whom he co-produced multiple albums. He is also an accomplished fine artist, having studied at Sir John Cass College of Art and exhibiting internationally.
