# Jamie Hince

**Guitar. British. Co-founder of [[Artists/T/The Kills]].**

Jamie Hince, previously of the bands Scarfo and Blyth Power, formed The Kills in London in 2001 with American singer Alison Mosshart after she moved to London following the breakup of her band Discount. The pair originally performed as "Hotel" and "VV" before settling on The Kills as a name.
