# Jason Popson

**Vocalist (rap/harsh vocals). American.**

Jason Popson, known professionally as J Mann or J-Mann, is an American musician best known as a co-founding member and dual vocalist of the industrial metal band Mushroomhead, where he provided harsh and rap vocals alongside Jeffrey Hetrick's clean vocals from 1993–2004 and again from 2013–2022. He contributed vocals and songwriting to numerous Mushroomhead albums across both periods and is integral to the band's signature dual-vocal sound.
