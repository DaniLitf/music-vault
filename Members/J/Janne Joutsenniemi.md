# Janne Joutsenniemi

**Bassist, producer. Finnish.**

Janne Joutsenniemi is a Finnish bassist, songwriter, and record producer best known as the bassist of the alternative metal band Suburban Tribe and the groove metal band Stone. He has also served as bassist and backing vocalist for Ensiferum from 2006 onward, contributing to multiple studio albums. As a producer, he has worked with Stam1na, achieving gold certifications in Finland, and has produced and engineered albums across the Finnish metal scene.
