# Jeff Scott Soto

**American singer. Born 4 October 1965.**

Jeff Scott Soto is an American singer best known as the lead vocalist of progressive metal supergroup Sons of Apollo. He has also worked with Yngwie Malmsteen, Talisman, Journey, and W.E.T., and has a successful solo career.
