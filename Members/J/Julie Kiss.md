# Julie Kiss

**Vocalist. Hungarian.**

Julie Kiss is a Hungarian vocalist known for an ethereal, goth-influenced singing style, formerly the lead vocalist of Hungarian goth metal band Without Face. She founded progressive metal band To-Mera in South Oxfordshire, England in 2004 with bassist Lee Barrett, choosing the band's name for its connection to her interest in Egyptology.
