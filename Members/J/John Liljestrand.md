# John Liljestrand

**Drums. Swedish.**

John Liljestrand (sometimes credited as simply John) is a Swedish drummer who played for the Malmö hardcore punk band Black Uniforms from 1986 to 1990 after the band reformed. He performed on the *Faces of Death* album and the *Splatter Punx on Acid* compilation. He also played in Page and Perukers.
