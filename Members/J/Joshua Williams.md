# Joshua Williams

**Drummer.**

Joshua Williams is an American drummer from the South Florida hardcore scene and a founding member of vegan straight edge metalcore band [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]], which he formed in December 1993 with guitarist Richard Thurston and vocalist Mark Mitchell. He later filled in for Timothy Kirkpatrick during [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]]'s June 2014 Japanese tour, which supported the compilation album *Greatest Hits?*.

## Associated Artists
