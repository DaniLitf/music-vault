# Jellybean Johnson

**Drummer, guitarist, and vocalist. Died 2025-11-21.**

Jellybean Johnson was an American drummer, guitarist, and vocalist who was a founding member of The Time, playing drums from the band's formation in 1981 through its various reunions. He was a member of the precursor band Flyte Tyme and, after the Time's initial 1985 breakup, joined the short-lived project The Family. He remained an active member of The Time through nearly all of its iterations until his death in 2025 at age 69.
