# Jimmy Cauty

**Electronic musician, artist, producer. Born 1956-12-19.**

Jimmy Cauty is a British musician and artist best known as one half of [[Artists/T/The KLF]] with Bill Drummond. He co-founded [[Artists/T/The Orb]] with Alex Paterson and was central to the early ambient house sessions before leaving in 1990.
