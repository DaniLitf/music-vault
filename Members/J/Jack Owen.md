**Guitarist.**

Jack Owen is an American guitarist best known as a former member of [[Artists/C/Cannibal Corpse]] (1988–2004) and currently a member of [[Artists/S/Six Feet Under]] (2017–present). He also produced Six Feet Under's 2024 album *Killing for Revenge*.
