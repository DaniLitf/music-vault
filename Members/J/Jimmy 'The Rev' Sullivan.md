# Jimmy "The Rev" Sullivan

**Drummer, pianist, and occasional co-lead vocalist. Born 1981-02-09, died 2009-12-28.**

Jimmy "The Rev" Sullivan was an American drummer who co-founded Avenged Sevenfold in Huntington Beach, California in 1999 and played on the band's first five studio albums, also contributing piano and occasional lead vocals. He died at age 28 in December 2009 from a drug and alcohol overdose; parts he had already written were used posthumously on 2010's *Nightmare*. Outside Avenged Sevenfold, he fronted the avant-garde metal side project Pinkly Smooth (as "Rathead") alongside bandmate Synyster Gates.
