# John Cooke

**Guitarist. British.**

John Cooke is a guitarist associated with [[Artists/N/Napalm Death]] and participated in the Melvins/Napalm Death collaboration *Savage Imperial Death March*. The project was recorded as a true 50/50 collaboration between members of both bands.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/N/Napalm Death]] – guitar
- [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] – collaborator on *Savage Imperial Death March* (2026)
