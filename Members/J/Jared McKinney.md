# Jared McKinney

**Keyboards. American. Former member of [[Artists/T/The Greenhornes]].**

Jared McKinney played keyboards in The Greenhornes' original lineup from 1996 to 2001, departing before the band's transition to a trio for *Dual Mono*. He later reunited with former bandmates Craig Fox and Brian Olive in a new, still-untitled Cincinnati-area project.
