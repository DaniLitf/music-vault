# John Petrucci

**American guitarist and songwriter. Born 12 July 1967.**

John Petrucci is an American guitarist who co-founded Dream Theater while attending Berklee College of Music with bassist John Myung and drummer Mike Portnoy, and has produced the band's albums since 1999's *Metropolis Pt. 2: Scenes from a Memory*. He is considered one of the most technically proficient guitarists in modern rock and metal, and has released solo albums including *Suspended Animation* (2005) and *Terminal Velocity* (2020).
