# Jan Klemm

**Violinist. German.**

Jan Klemm performed as Herr Jeh in [[Artists/T/The Inchtabokatables]], playing violin in the group's unusual string-led rock format. Before The Inchtabokatables, he and Tobias Unterberg played with Eric Fish in the band Catriona.

## Associated Artists
