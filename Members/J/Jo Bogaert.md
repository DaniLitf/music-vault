**Record producer and musician. Belgian.**

Jo Bogaert is a Belgian musician, songwriter, and record producer best known as the creator and driving force behind the Eurodance project [[Artists/T/Technotronic|Technotronic]]. Working under the alias Thomas De Quincey, he produced all of Technotronic's albums and singles, crafting the signature hip house sound that produced worldwide hits like "Pump Up the Jam" and "Get Up! (Before the Night Is Over)".

Born in the United States but relocated to Belgium, Bogaert was a philosophy teacher before pursuing music production. He had previously worked on various new beat projects including Acts of Madmen and Nux Nemo. His production approach fused house music with hip-hop elements, helping define the Eurodance sound of the early 1990s.
