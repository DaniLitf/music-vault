# Jack Irons

**Jack Irons is an American drummer born July 18, 1962, best known as a founding member of [[Artists/R/Red Hot Chili Peppers]] and later drummer for [[Artists/P/Pearl Jam]].**

Irons co-founded the Red Hot Chili Peppers in 1983 but left in 1988 due to mental health issues following the death of his friend Hillel Slovak. He later drummed for Pearl Jam from 1994 to 1998. He was also the person who passed a demo tape from Eddie Vedder to Pearl Jam, helping launch Vedder's career.
