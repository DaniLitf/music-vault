# Jon Nödtveidt

**Jon Nödtveidt was a Swedish guitarist and vocalist, the founder of Dissection, whose melodic black metal albums *The Somberlain* and *Storm of the Light's Bane* are considered among the greatest extreme metal recordings ever made.**

He served a prison sentence (1997–2004) for accessory to murder before reforming Dissection and releasing one final album. He died by suicide in August 2006.
