# Johnny Christ

**Bassist. Born 1984-11-18.**

Johnny Christ (Jonathan Lewis Seward) is an American bassist who joined Avenged Sevenfold in 2003, replacing original bassist Justin Sane in time to record *Waking the Fallen*, and has remained the band's bassist ever since.
