# Jared Leto

**Vocals, guitar, bass, keyboards. Born December 26, 1971.**

Jared Leto is an American musician and Academy Award-winning actor from Bossier City, Louisiana who co-founded [[Artists/0-9/30 Seconds to Mars|30 Seconds to Mars]] with his older brother [[Members/S/Shannon Leto|Shannon Leto]] in 1998. As the band's frontman, principal songwriter, and — from *Love, Lust, Faith and Dreams* (2013) onward — its primary producer, he has steered the group's evolution from progressive-metal-tinged space rock to platinum-selling alternative rock and electronic-leaning art pop. Leto has pursued acting in parallel throughout, resisting early on any attempts to promote the band using his Hollywood profile.
