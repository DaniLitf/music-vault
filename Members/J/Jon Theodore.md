# Jon Theodore

**Drummer. Born 1973-09-20.**

Jon Theodore is an American drummer known for his technically complex and polyrhythmic playing style. He was the drummer for The Mars Volta from their formation until 2008, appearing on the band's first three albums. He formed One Day as a Lion with Zack de la Rocha in 2008, and later rejoined The Mars Volta when they reunited in 2012.
