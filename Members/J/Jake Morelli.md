# Jake Morelli

**Guitarist. American.**

Jake Morelli is an American guitarist who was a member of the Jonas Brothers' early touring band from 2005 to 2007, playing guitar during the band's formative years while they opened for acts such as Kelly Clarkson, Jesse McCartney, and the Backstreet Boys. He has also performed with The Roots.
