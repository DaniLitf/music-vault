**Vocalist. Born May 5, 1960.**

Jeff Hatrix (also known as Jeffrey Nothing) is an American vocalist best known as the clean vocalist for the industrial metal band Mushroomhead and the frontman of the thrash metal band Hatrix. He began his career in Chicago with White Heat (1982–1984) before moving back to Cleveland to co-found Purgatory, and later Hatrix and Mushroomhead. His distinctive vocal style and theatrical mask-based persona made him a defining figure in Cleveland's metal scene.
