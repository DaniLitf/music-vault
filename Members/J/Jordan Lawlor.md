# Jordan Lawlor

**Multi-instrumentalist, vocalist.**

Jordan Lawlor is an American multi-instrumentalist who joined [[Artists/M/M83]]'s touring band in 2011, recruited by Anthony Gonzalez for the *Hurry Up, We're Dreaming* tour, and contributed guitar, bass, and vocals through the *Junk* era. He later launched his own project under the name J.Laser.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/M/M83]] – guitar, bass, vocals (2011–2019)
