# John Paul Jones

**John Paul Jones (born John Baldwin, January 3, 1946, in Sidcup, England) is a British multi-instrumentalist, composer, and record producer, best known as the bassist and keyboardist of Led Zeppelin.**

Jones was a highly in-demand session musician and arranger in London before joining Led Zeppelin in 1968. He played bass, keyboards, mandolin, and other instruments throughout Zeppelin's catalog and served as the band's musical anchor. After Zeppelin dissolved following John Bonham's death in 1980, he maintained a career as a composer, producer, and occasional performer, joining Them Crooked Vultures in 2009.
