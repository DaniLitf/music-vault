# Jose Medeles

**Drummer. American.**

Jose Medeles is a drummer who played with the Southern California punk rock band [[Artists/F/Face to Face]] in 1998 as a touring replacement during their self-titled album tour. He served as a temporary fill-in drummer before Pete Parada was hired as the permanent replacement for Rob Kurth.

## Associated Artists
