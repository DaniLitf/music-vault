# Jarrod Alexander

**Drummer. American.**

Jarrod Alexander is a drummer from Jackson, Mississippi, and a Berklee College of Music graduate who has toured with numerous punk and rock acts. He filled in as [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio]]'s touring drummer in 2018–2019, and is best known as the touring and reunion-era drummer of My Chemical Romance since 2011. He was also the original drummer of Death By Stereo and toured Europe with [[Artists/T/The Vandals]] in 2001.
