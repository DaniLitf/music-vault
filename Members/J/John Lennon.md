# John Lennon

**John Lennon was a British musician, singer, and songwriter who co-founded The Beatles with Paul McCartney, and whose partnership with McCartney produced some of the most celebrated songs in popular music history.**

He was the primary creative driver of The Beatles' more experimental and political work, and pursued a significant solo career after the band's dissolution in 1970, producing landmark records such as *Imagine* (1971). He was shot and killed outside his New York apartment on December 8, 1980.
