# Jon Wysocki

**Drums. American. Founding member of [[Artists/S/Staind]].**

Jon Wysocki was Staind's original drummer, brought into the band by Mike Mushok in 1995. He left in 2011, frustrated with the rushed production of the band's self-titled seventh album, and joined Soil. He died in 2024 at age 53.
