**Drummer and percussionist. Active 1969–1970; died 2009.**

John Mayhew was the drummer for Genesis during the recording and release of their second album, Trespass (1970). He briefly served as the band's third drummer, replacing John Silver in August 1969. However, Mayhew was dismissed from the band before the release of Trespass, making way for Phil Collins to join as Genesis's drummer in August 1970. Though his time with Genesis was brief, Mayhew's drumming on Trespass represents an important transitional moment in the band's early progressive rock era.
