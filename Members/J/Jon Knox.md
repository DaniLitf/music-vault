# Jon Knox

**Drummer, producer, and beatmaker. American.**

Jon Knox is an American session drummer best known as the drummer for Adam Again (1990–2000) and Christian rock band White Heart (1992–1997). He has also played with Future User, the electronic rock side project of Rage Against the Machine bassist Tim Commerford, and Full on the Mouth. In addition to performing, Knox is a producer, composer, and beatmaker who endorses Pearl drums and SoulTone cymbals.

## Biography

Jon Knox began his career as a session drummer in the early 1980s. He joined Adam Again around 1990, appearing on the albums *Homeboys*, *Dig*, and *Perfecta*. Concurrently, he was the drummer for White Heart from 1992 to 1997, appearing on *Tales of Wonder*, *Highlands*, and *Inside*. Knox also helped form Rage Against the Machine through his early connections with Tim Commerford, and later played in Commerford's side project Future User.
