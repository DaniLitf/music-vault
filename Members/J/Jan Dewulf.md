# Jan Dewulf

**Belgian electronic musician, member of Diskonnekted and Your Life On Hold.**

Jan Dewulf is a Belgian electronic musician who collaborated with Dirk Ivens on the Dive album *Where Do We Go From Here?* (2020). He is also a member of Diskonnekted (who collaborated with Dive on *Frozen*, 2006) and Your Life On Hold.
