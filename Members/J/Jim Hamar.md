# Jim Hamar

**Vocalist. American.**

Jim Hamar is an American vocalist known for his work in the Cleveland metal scene. He was the founding vocalist of the progressive metal band Rotterdam (which later evolved into Torment and Ritual), after leaving the band Breaker where he had previously sung. He has also performed in Nightcrawler and Havoc.
