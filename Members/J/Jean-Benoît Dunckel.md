# Jean-Benoît Dunckel

**Musician, producer. Born 7 September 1969.**

Jean-Benoît Dunckel is a French musician best known as one half of the electronic duo [[Artists/A/Air]]. A classically trained pianist who studied mathematics and physics, he formed the band Orange with [[Members/N/Nicolas Godin]] and others in the 1980s before the pair founded Air in 1995 and co-produced all of the duo's albums. Recording as Darkel, he released a solo album in 2006, and has since released further solo records (*H+*, *Carbon*) and film scores, as well as the side project Tomorrow's World with Lou Hayter.
