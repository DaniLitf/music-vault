# Joey Jordison

**Drummer. Born 1975-04-26 in Des Moines, Iowa, USA. Died 2021-07-26.**

Joey Jordison was a co-founding drummer of Slipknot, considered one of the most technically precise and creative drummers in heavy metal. His drumming on *Iowa* (2001) and *Vol. 3: (The Subliminal Verses)* (2004) is widely celebrated. He was unexpectedly dismissed from Slipknot in 2013 and later revealed the dismissal was related to a diagnosis of transverse myelitis, a neurological condition affecting his ability to play. He died in July 2021.
