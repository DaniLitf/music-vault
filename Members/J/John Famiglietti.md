# John Famiglietti

**Bass, pedals, electronics. American. Founding member of [[Artists/H/HEALTH]].**

John Famiglietti co-founded HEALTH in 2005 with Jake Duzsik in Los Angeles, and has served as the band's bassist and a key producer across all of its albums.
