# Joakim Åhlund

**Guitar, vocals. Born 1971.**

Joakim Åhlund is a Swedish musician and producer, best known as a guitarist and vocalist for [[Artists/T/Teddybears]] and guitarist for [[Artists/C/Caesars]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/Teddybears]]
- [[Artists/C/Caesars]]

## Biography
Åhlund co-founded Teddybears in 1991 and has been a core member throughout their evolution. He also played guitar in the Swedish rock band The Caesars. His brother is Klas Åhlund.
