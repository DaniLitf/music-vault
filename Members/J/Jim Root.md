# Jim Root

**Guitarist. Born 1971-10-02 in Las Vegas, Nevada, USA.**

Jim Root is the lead guitarist of Slipknot and a co-founding member of Stone Sour. He joined Slipknot in 1999 and has contributed the band's riff-based guitar work across their studio albums. He is identified in Slipknot as #4 and wears a mask as with all band members. Stone Sour, which he co-founded with Corey Taylor, has released six studio albums as a more straightforward hard rock project.
