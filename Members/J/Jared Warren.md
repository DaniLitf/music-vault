# Jared Warren

**Bassist, vocalist. American.**

Jared Warren is an American bassist and vocalist best known for [[Artists/B/Big Business]]. He joined [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] with drummer Coady Willis in 2006, creating the band's double-drummer era.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] – bass, vocals (2006–2015)
- [[Artists/B/Big Business]] – bass, vocals
