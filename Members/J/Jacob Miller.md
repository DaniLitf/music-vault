# Jacob Miller

**Vocalist. 1952–1980.**

Jacob Miller was a Jamaican vocalist who became reggae band Inner Circle's permanent frontman in 1974 while continuing to record as a solo artist, fronting the band through its most popular era of the mid-to-late 1970s and appearing in the 1978 film *Rockers*. At the peak of his career only Bob Marley was more popular in Jamaica; Miller died in a car crash in March 1980, ending the band's first era.
