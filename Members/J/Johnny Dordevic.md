# Johnny Dordevic

**Vocalist. Serbian-American.**

Johnny Dordevic is a Serbian-American vocalist who was the lead singer of Entombed briefly from 1991 to 1992, following the departure of original vocalist L.G. Petrov. He performed on the *Clandestine* sessions before being replaced when Petrov returned to the band.
