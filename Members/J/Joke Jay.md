# Joke Jay

**Vocalist, drummer. German.**

Joke Jay is a German musician who has been a member of the synthpop band And One across two tenures, providing vocals and drums. He first joined in 1992 and left in 2001, returning in 2011 when original members Chris Ruiz and Gio van Oli departed.
