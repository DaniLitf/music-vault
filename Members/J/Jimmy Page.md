# Jimmy Page

**Guitar, production. British. Founding member of [[Artists/L/Led Zeppelin]].**

Jimmy Page founded Led Zeppelin in 1968 after stints with the Yardbirds, and became one of rock's most influential guitarists and producers. Outside Led Zeppelin, he was a frequent collaborator with folk singer-songwriter [[Artists/R/Roy Harper]], contributing guitar (sometimes under the pseudonym "S. Flavius Mercurius") to several of Harper's albums including *Stormcock* (1971), *Lifemask* (1973), and *Valentine* (1974), touring with him in the 1980s, and inspiring Led Zeppelin's tribute song "Hats Off to (Roy) Harper" (1970).
