# Jonathan Olsson

**Bassist. Swedish.**

Jonathan Olsson is a Swedish bassist associated with [[Artists/P/Pain]] and documented as a live bassist for [[Artists/L/Lindemann (band)]]. His work links Peter Tägtgren's Pain and Lindemann projects.
