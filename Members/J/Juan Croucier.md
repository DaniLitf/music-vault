# Juan Croucier

**Bassist. Born August 22, 1959.**

Juan Croucier is an American bassist and vocalist best known as the bassist for the glam metal band Ratt and for his earlier work with Dokken. He joined Ratt in 1982 and played on the band's classic albums from the 1980s and their reunion-era recordings. He is the only bassist to appear on all of Ratt's platinum-selling albums.
