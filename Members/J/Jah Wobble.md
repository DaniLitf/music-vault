# Jah Wobble

**Bassist. Born 1958-08-11.**

Jah Wobble is a British bassist best known as a founding member of Public Image Ltd. He contributed bass to [[Artists/T/The Orb]] recordings including "Blue Room" and later appeared on "Doughnuts Forever."

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/P/Public Image Ltd]] – bass (1978–1980)
- [[Artists/T/The Orb]] – bass/guest (1992, 2018)
