# Jan Axelsson

**Drums. Swedish.**

Jan Axelsson (also known as Janne Axelsson) is a Swedish drummer who was the original drummer of [[Artists/D/Disfear]] at the band's formation in 1989 during their Anti-Bofors period. He appeared on the band's earliest recordings before being replaced by Jallo Lehto later that same year.
