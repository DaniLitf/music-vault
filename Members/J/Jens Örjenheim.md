# Jens Örjenheim

**Drums. Born DATE.**

Jens Örjenheim is a Swedish musician, best known as the original drummer for the Swedish rock band [[Artists/C/Caesars]] from 1995 to 1999.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/C/Caesars]] – drums (1995–1999)

## Biography

Örjenheim was the original drummer for Twelve Caesars (later Caesars) from the band's formation in 1995 until 1999, when he was replaced by Nino Keller. He appeared on the band's early recordings as Twelve Caesars.
