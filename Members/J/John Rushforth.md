# John Rushforth

**Drummer. British.**

John Rushforth, known as Junky Jon, is a British drummer best known for playing on all three studio albums by deathgrind band Gorerotted (1999–2008), one of only three members to appear on every Gorerotted record. He returned for the band's 2026 reunion under its original name, and has also drummed for Detrimentum, Infected Disarray, and Hades Lab, and runs the death metal label Wasted Existence Records.
