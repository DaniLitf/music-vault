# Jake Duzsik

**Vocals, guitar. American. Founding member of [[Artists/H/HEALTH]].**

Jake Duzsik co-founded HEALTH in 2005 in Los Angeles after meeting bassist John Famiglietti while working at a Guitar Center; he brought in guitarist Jupiter Keyes, a university acquaintance, and drummer B.J. Miller to round out the lineup. He remains the band's vocalist and primary lyricist.
