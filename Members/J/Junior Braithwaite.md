**Vocalist. Born 26 April 1949.**

Junior Braithwaite was a Jamaican singer and an original member of the Wailers, joining the group shortly after its formation in 1963. He contributed lead vocals to early singles including "It Hurts to Be Alone" before leaving the group in 1964. Braithwaite later lived in the United States and Chicago, and was shot dead in Kingston, Jamaica in 1999.
