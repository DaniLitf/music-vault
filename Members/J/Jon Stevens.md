# Jon Stevens

**Vocalist. New Zealander.**

Jon Stevens is a New Zealand-born singer best known as the frontman of Noiseworks and as a member of INXS. He co-founded The Dead Daisies in 2012 and served as lead vocalist from 2012 to 2015.
