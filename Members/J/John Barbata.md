# John Barbata

**John Barbata** (April 1, 1945 – May 8, 2024) was an American drummer best known for his work with [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]], [[Artists/J/Jefferson Starship]], [[The Turtles]], and [[Crosby, Stills, Nash & Young]].

- [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]] – drums, percussion (1972)
- [[Artists/J/Jefferson Starship]] – drums (1974–1979)
- [[The Turtles]] – drums (1966–1969)
- [[Crosby, Stills, Nash & Young]] – drums (touring)
