# Jani Lehtosaari

**Bassist. Finnish.**

Jani Lehtosaari played bass for Impaled Nazarene from 1996 to 2000, providing the rhythmic foundation during this period of the band's career. His bass work contributed to the band's extreme metal sound across several albums and live performances.
