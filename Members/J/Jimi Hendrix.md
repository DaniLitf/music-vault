# Jimi Hendrix

**Jimi Hendrix (born Johnny Allen Hendrix, November 27, 1942 – September 18, 1970) was an American guitarist, singer, and songwriter from Seattle, Washington, universally regarded as one of the greatest electric guitarists in history.**

Hendrix relocated to London in 1966 and formed the Jimi Hendrix Experience, releasing three landmark albums in rapid succession. His innovative use of feedback, distortion, and the whammy bar, combined with his showmanship — including playing guitar with his teeth and setting his instrument on fire at Monterey — made him a defining figure of late-1960s rock. He died in London in 1970 from asphyxia after ingesting barbiturates.
