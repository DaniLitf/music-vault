# James Plotkin

**Guitarist, bassist, programmer, producer. Active 1986–present.**

James Plotkin is an American multi-instrumentalist and producer from New York City known for his work across industrial metal, noise rock, and experimental music. He co-founded OLD with Alan Dubin and is widely regarded as a versatile figure in the avant-garde underground, having collaborated with artists across drone, ambient, and extreme metal. He later reunited with Dubin in Khanate, and has produced and mixed records for numerous heavy artists.
