# John Frusciante

**Guitar. American. Guitarist for [[Artists/R/Red Hot Chili Peppers]].**

John Frusciante joined Red Hot Chili Peppers in 1988, departing and rejoining twice more (1998–2009, 2019–present) around solo work and personal struggles; his melodic, textural guitar style shaped the band's most successful albums. He also guested on Perry Farrell's [[Artists/S/Satellite Party]] album *Ultra Payloaded* (2007), alongside bandmate Flea.
