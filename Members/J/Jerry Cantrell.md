# Jerry Cantrell

**Jerry Cantrell is an American guitarist, vocalist, and principal songwriter of Alice in Chains, whose heavily downtuned, dissonant guitar style and high harmonies with Layne Staley defined the band's unique heavy sound.**

He has also released two solo albums and briefly collaborated with various artists after Staley's death.
