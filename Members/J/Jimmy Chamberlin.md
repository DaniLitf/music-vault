# Jimmy Chamberlin

**Jimmy Chamberlin is an American drummer and co-founder of The Smashing Pumpkins, whose jazz-influenced, technically adventurous style distinguished the band's sound on their classic albums.**

Chamberlin was fired from the Pumpkins in 1996 following the drug-related death of touring keyboardist Jonathan Meltzer, rejoined in 1999, and returned again for the band's 2018 reunion.
