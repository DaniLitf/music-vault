**Bassist. Born September 5, 1963.**

Juan Alderete is an American bassist known for his work in Racer X, The Scream, and as a longtime touring musician with Marilyn Manson. He was also a member of the experimental band Big Sir and has played with artists ranging from the Mars Volta to Deltron 3030.
