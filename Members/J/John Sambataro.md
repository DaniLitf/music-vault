# John Sambataro

**Backing vocals, guitar. American.**

John "Johnne" Sambataro is an American session singer and guitarist based in Miami, whose credits include Firefall, McGuinn Clark & Hillman and extensive Bee Gees-related session work. He sang backing vocals on several tracks of the [[Artists/C/Coverdale-Page]] album (1993).
