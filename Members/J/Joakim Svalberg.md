# Joakim Svalberg

**Keyboardist, vocalist. Born 1984-02-14 in Sweden.**

Joakim Svalberg is a Swedish musician who joined Opeth as their keyboardist in 2011, coinciding with the band's shift toward a fully progressive rock sound on *Heritage*. His classical and jazz training has contributed to the more orchestral and sophisticated keyboard arrangements on Opeth's later albums.
