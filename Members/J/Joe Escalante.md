# Joe Escalante

**Bassist. American.**

Joe Escalante is the bassist and co-founder of the California punk band The Vandals, forming the group in 1980 and remaining a core member through their continuous 45+ year history. He has been instrumental in the band's evolution from hardcore punk roots to their later comedic and satirical punk direction, appearing on all major studio albums and maintaining the group through numerous lineup changes.
