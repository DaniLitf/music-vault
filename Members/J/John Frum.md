# John Frum

**Drummer. American.**

John Frum is a drummer who performed with Dallas punk band [[Artists/H/Hagfish|Hagfish]] in 1992, joining the Blair brothers (Zach and Doni) during a transitional period in the band's early years. Frum brought guitarist Mike Brown into the band to fill out the sound, but internal conflict led to both Frum and Brown departing after an attempted recording project. The Blair brothers subsequently continued as the core of Hagfish with a new vocalist and drummer.
