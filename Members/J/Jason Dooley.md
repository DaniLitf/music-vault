# Jason Dooley

**Drummer.**

Jason Dooley is an American drummer who played in Florida vegan straight edge metalcore band [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]] until his departure following the band's summer 1997 European tour. He was also recruited for the June 1997 Gainesville reformation of [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]], but other commitments kept him from taking an active role, and he was replaced by Timothy Kirkpatrick in September 1997 without appearing on any releases.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]] – drums (mid-1990s–1997)
- [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] – drums (1997)
