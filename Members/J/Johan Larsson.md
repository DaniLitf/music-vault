# Johan Larsson

**Bass (1990–1997).**

Johan Larsson is a Swedish musician best known as the original bassist and a founding member of [[Artists/I/In Flames]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/In Flames]] – bass (1990–1997)
- [[Artists/H/Hammerfall]] – former bassist (session/touring)

## Biography

Larsson was a founding member of In Flames in 1990 alongside Jesper Strömblad and Glenn Ljungström. He played on the band's first three albums — *Lunar Strain* (1994), *The Jester Race* (1996), and *Whoracle* (1997) — before leaving the band later in 1997.
