# Ja Rule

**Rapper, songwriter, actor. American. Born 1976.**

Jeffrey "Ja Rule" Atkins is a rapper and musician who became the flagship artist of Murder Inc. Records, achieving multi-platinum success with albums including *Venni Vetti Vecci* (1999), *Pain Is Love* (2001), and *Rule 3:36* (2000). Known for collaborations with Ashanti and chart-topping features, Ja Rule was central to Murder Inc.'s commercial dominance in the early 2000s before the label faced federal investigations and his career decline following the 50 Cent feud.
