# Joe Jonas

**Singer, songwriter, musician, actor. Born August 15, 1989 in Casa Grande, Arizona.**

Joe Jonas is an American singer, songwriter, musician and actor who rose to fame as the lead vocalist of the Jonas Brothers alongside his brothers Kevin and Nick. After the band's initial breakup in 2013, he formed the funk-pop band DNCE, whose debut single "Cake by the Ocean" became a worldwide hit. He has also released two solo albums, *Fastlife* (2011) and *Music for People Who Believe in Love* (2025), and served as a judge on *The Voice Australia*.

## Biography

Joe Jonas became a teen idol as the frontman of the Jonas Brothers, starring in the Disney Channel films *Camp Rock* and *Camp Rock 2: The Final Jam* and the television series *Jonas* / *Jonas L.A.*. After the band's hiatus, he formed DNCE with guitarist JinJoo Lee, drummer Jack Lawless, and bassist Cole Whittle, achieving success with their debut album. He has also pursued acting, appearing in films such as *Devotion* (2022), and has served as a coach on *The Voice Australia*.
