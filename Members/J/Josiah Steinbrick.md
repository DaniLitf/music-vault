# Josiah Steinbrick

**Multi-instrumentalist, bassist, composer, record producer. American.**

Josiah Clark Steinbrick is a multi-instrumentalist and record producer from Providence, Rhode Island, currently based in Los Angeles. He played bass and keyboards for the hardcore punk band [[Artists/F/F-Minus]], and collaborated with [[Members/M/Matt Skiba]] on the indie rock project [[Artists/H/Heavens]]. Beyond his work with established bands, Steinbrick has produced and recorded with numerous artists including Cate Le Bon, Devendra Banhart, Charlotte Gainsbourg, and others. He provided the score for the 2020 film *Horse Girl*.
