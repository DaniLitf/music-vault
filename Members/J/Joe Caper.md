# Joe Caper

**Vocalist. American.**

Joe Caper was the vocalist for the Las Vegas grindcore band Righteous Pigs. He performed on both of the band's studio albums, *Live and Learn* (1989) and *Stress Related* (1990). Caper also performed in the bands The Henchmen and Mule Skinner.
