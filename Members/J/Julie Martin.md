# Julie Martin

**Vocals. American.**

Julie Martin is an American vocalist best known as one half of the duo Bon Voyage alongside her husband Jason Martin (of Starflyer 59). She performs lead vocals on all Bon Voyage recordings, and was previously a vocalist for the band Havalina.
