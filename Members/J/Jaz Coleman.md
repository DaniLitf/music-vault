# Jaz Coleman

**British singer, keyboardist, and producer. Born 17 January 1960.**

Jeremy Ivor James "Jaz" Coleman is a British singer, keyboardist, and producer best known as the lead vocalist and keyboardist of post-punk band Killing Joke. He has also worked as a classical composer and conductor, and has collaborated with artists such as Björk and Geordie Walker.
