# James Hetfield

**Vocals, rhythm guitar. Born August 3, 1963.**

James Hetfield co-founded [[Artists/M/Metallica]] with Lars Ulrich in Los Angeles in 1981 and has served as the band's lead vocalist, rhythm guitarist, and primary lyricist ever since. Known for his aggressive down-picked riffing and baritone vocal delivery, he is widely regarded as one of the definitive guitarists and songwriters of thrash metal.
