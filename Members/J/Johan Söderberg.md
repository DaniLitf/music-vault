# Johan Söderberg

**Director, editor, musician. Born 1962-11-14.**

Johan Söderberg is a Swedish film director and editor who was a member of Swedish multimedia/ambient music collective Lucky People Center, and co-directed with Erik Pauser the documentary *Lucky People Center International* (1998). He has since worked extensively in film and music video, editing Madonna's "Hung Up" (2005) and "Sorry" (2006) videos, and is a professor at Dalarna University.
