# Jason Bittner

**Drums. Born January 11, 1970.**

Jason Bittner is an American drummer from Schenectady, New York, best known as the longtime drummer of Shadows Fall, who also drummed for [[Artists/F/Flotsam and Jetsam]] (2014–2017) and [[Artists/O/Overkill]] (2017–2024) before leaving the latter to refocus on Shadows Fall and his band Category 7.
