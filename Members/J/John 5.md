# John 5

**Guitarist. Born 1971-07-31 in Grosse Pointe, Michigan, USA.**

John 5 is an American guitarist who has served as Rob Zombie's lead guitarist since 2005. Before joining Zombie he was the lead guitarist of Marilyn Manson from 1998 to 2004, appearing on *Mechanical Animals* (1998), *Holy Wood* (2000), and *The Golden Age of Grotesque* (2003). He has also released several solo albums showcasing his versatile playing across country, blues, and metal.
