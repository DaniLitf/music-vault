# John 5

**American guitarist. Born 31 July 1971.**

John William Lowery, known by his stage name John 5, is an American guitarist noted for his virtuoso technique across rock, metal, country, and classical styles. He played for David Lee Roth before joining Marilyn Manson (1998–2004), then became Rob Zombie's longtime touring and recording guitarist (2005–present), and joined Mötley Crüe in 2022, becoming a full member in 2023.
