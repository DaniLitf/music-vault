# Janick Gers

**Guitars (1990–present). Born January 27, 1957.**

Janick Gers is an English guitarist best known as a member of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] and former member of [[White Spirit]] and [[Gillan]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – guitars (1990–present)
- [[Gillan]] – guitarist (1981–1982)
- [[White Spirit]] – guitarist (NWOBHM band)
- [[Members/B/Bruce Dickinson]] – guitarist on solo album *Tattooed Millionaire* (1990)
- [[Fish]] – session guitarist (Marillion solo work)

## Biography

Gers entered Iron Maiden in 1990 after Bruce Dickinson recommended him, replacing Adrian Smith. He had previously played on Dickinson's solo debut *Tattooed Millionaire*. Although initially seen as Smith's replacement, Gers proved his worth as a songwriter and has co-written several Maiden classics. Since Smith's return in 1999, Maiden has performed with three guitarists.
