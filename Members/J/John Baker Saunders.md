# John Baker Saunders

**Bassist. 1954–1999.**

John Baker Saunders was an American blues bassist, born in Montgomery, Alabama, who worked with Chicago blues artists including Hubert Sumlin before touring Europe with Seattle band the Walkabouts. In 1994 he met Pearl Jam's Mike McCready in drug rehabilitation, and after treatment the two formed Mad Season with vocalist Layne Staley and drummer Barrett Martin, releasing the gold-certified album *Above* (1995). Saunders died of a heroin overdose in January 1999, permanently ending the band.
