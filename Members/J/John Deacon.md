# John Deacon

**Bassist. Born 1951-08-19 in Oadby, Leicestershire, England.**

John Deacon was the bassist of Queen from 1971 until his retirement from music in 1997. Despite being the quietest and most reserved member, he wrote some of the band's biggest hits, including "Another One Bites the Dust," "I Want to Break Free," and "You're My Best Friend." He has not performed publicly since the Freddie Mercury Tribute Concert in 1992 (aside from one subsequent performance) and has remained entirely retired from public life.
