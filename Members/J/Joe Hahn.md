# Joe Hahn

**Turntables, samples, keyboardist. Born March 15, 1977.**

Joe Hahn is an American musician and director best known as the DJ and turntablist of Linkin Park. He co-founded the band in 1996 and has been responsible for the group's electronic elements, sampling, and turntable work. Hahn has also directed many of Linkin Park's music videos and short films.
