# Juan Atkins

**Juan Atkins (born September 12, 1962, in Detroit, Michigan) is an American electronic musician and DJ widely regarded as the originator of techno music.**

Growing up in Belleville, Michigan, Atkins began making electronic music influenced by Kraftwerk and Parliament-Funkadelic. He formed Cybotron with Rick Davis in the early 1980s, released the first Detroit techno records, and co-founded the Belleville Three with Derrick May and Kevin Saunderson. He was also a founding member of Underground Resistance before pursuing solo work as Model 500. He is often credited as the father of Detroit techno.
