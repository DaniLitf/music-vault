# James Bowman

**Guitarist. American.**

James Bowman is a guitarist and Laura Jane Grace's longtime friend from Naples, Florida, who joined [[Artists/A/Against Me!]] in 2001 and has played lead guitar on every one of the band's studio albums since. Aside from Against Me!, Bowman has worked as a guitar technician for touring acts including My Chemical Romance, and guested on guitar for Grace's side project [[Artists/D/Devouring Mothers]] on their 2018 album *Bought to Rot*.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/A/Against Me!]] – lead guitar (2001–present, brief absence 2001)
- [[Artists/D/Devouring Mothers]] – guitar, guest (2018)
