# Jim Barr

**Bassist. British.**

Jim Barr is an English bassist from Bristol, best known as the touring bassist for [[Artists/P/Portishead]] and as a founding member of the jazz quartet Get the Blessing alongside drummer [[Members/C/Clive Deamer]]. He has also played as a session musician with [[Artists/P/Peter Gabriel]] and [[Artists/M/Massive Attack]], and has been a regular fixture of the Bristol music scene since the 1990s.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/P/Portishead]] – bass (touring, 1994–present)
- Get the Blessing – bass (2000–present)
- [[Artists/P/Peter Gabriel]] – session bass
- [[Artists/M/Massive Attack]] – session bass
