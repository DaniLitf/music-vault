# Johnny Cash

**Johnny Cash (born John R. Cash, February 26, 1932 – September 12, 2003) was an American singer-songwriter from Kingsland, Arkansas, one of the best-selling music artists of all time and a towering figure in country, rockabilly, and Americana.**

Cash signed to Sun Records in 1955 and rose to fame alongside Elvis Presley, Jerry Lee Lewis, and Carl Perkins as part of the Million Dollar Quartet. He became a crossover icon known for his deep bass-baritone voice and his Man in Black persona. His late-career collaboration with producer Rick Rubin on the American Recordings series (1994–2010) brought him renewed critical acclaim, including a Grammy-winning cover of Nine Inch Nails' "Hurt." He died four months after his wife June Carter Cash.
