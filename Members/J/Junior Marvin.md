# Junior Marvin

**Junior Marvin is a British-Jamaican guitarist who joined Bob Marley and the Wailers in 1976 and played on several of the band's most celebrated albums, including *Exodus* and *Kaya*.**

He brought a rock and soul guitar approach to the Wailers' sound and continued performing with various Wailers lineups after Marley's death in 1981.
