# Jyro Xhan

**Electronic/industrial musician. American.**

Jyro Xhan is an American electronic and industrial musician best known as the frontman of the pioneering Christian electronic band Mortal (credited as Jyro). He also fronted Fold Zandura and played in Cush, and is regarded as a pioneer of Christian industrial music.
