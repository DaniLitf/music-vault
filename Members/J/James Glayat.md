# James Glayat

**Guitarist.**

James Glayat is an American guitarist who joined [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] in October 1998, shortly before the band's first European tour, and played through August 2000 before rejoining for the 2008 reunion. Since 2019 he has also contributed backing vocals, and he appears on releases from *As Friends Rust / Discount* (1998) through *Lightless* (2024).

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] – guitar (1998–2000, 2008–present), backing vocals (2019–present)
