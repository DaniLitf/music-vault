# Jerome Benton

**Dancer and backing vocalist.**

Jerome Benton is an American dancer and vocalist who performed with Prince's backing band The Revolution as a dancer and backing vocalist from 1980 to 1986. He appeared in the films *Purple Rain* and *Under the Cherry Moon* and was a regular presence in Prince's live shows.
