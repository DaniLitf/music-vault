# Johan Längquist

**Johan Längquist is a Swedish vocalist who sang on Candlemass's debut album *Epicus Doomicus Metallicus* (1986), one of the most influential doom metal recordings ever made, and returned to the band as permanent vocalist in 2018.**

His powerful, operatic delivery on that debut album made him one of the most celebrated vocalists in doom metal history despite his original tenure being brief.
