# Jay Bentley

**Bassist. Born 1962-10-12.**

Jay Bentley is Bad Religion's bassist and a founding member, participating in the band since 1980 with brief absences (1994–1995). His bass lines are central to the band's melodic hardcore sound. Bentley has been a continuous presence in Bad Religion's post-1995 reunification and remains active with the band.
