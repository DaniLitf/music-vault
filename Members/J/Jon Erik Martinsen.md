# Jon Erik Martinsen

**Norwegian musician, co-founder of Apoptygma Berzerk.**

Jon Erik Martinsen co-founded Apoptygma Berzerk with Stephan Groth in 1989, playing keyboards on early demos including *Victims of Mutilation*. He left the band shortly after due to creative differences.
