# Jeronimo Gomez

**Bassist.**

Jeronimo Gomez is an American bass guitarist and a founding member of [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]], playing in the original Davie, Florida line-up from September 1996 to February 1997. His playing on the band's 1996 demo later surfaced on *The Fists of Time* (1998) and archival compilations.
