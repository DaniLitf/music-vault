# Johan Gummesson

**Bassist. Swedish.**

Johan Gummesson is a Swedish bassist who joined Driller Killer in 2009 as part of the band's final lineup, playing bass during their last year of activity. He was announced as part of the new lineup in early 2008 alongside Christoffer Larsson and Charlie Claeson.
