# John Lloyd Taylor

**Lead guitarist, musical director. Born March 23, 1982.**

John Lloyd Taylor is an American musician best known as the lead guitarist and musical director for the Jonas Brothers from 2005 to 2013. A graduate of Berklee College of Music, he joined the band during their early touring days and was name-checked in the song "Lovebug." He later directed the documentary *Jonas Brothers: Chasing Happiness* (2019) and works as a talent agent at United Talent Agency.
