# Jeff Walker

**Jeff Walker is a British bassist, vocalist, and lyricist, a co-founder of Carcass, and the author of the band's notorious gore-themed lyrics as well as their later melodic death metal evolution.**

He has been Carcass's constant member throughout all phases of their career, from goregrind origins through to the melodic death metal of *Heartwork* and the band's successful 21st-century reunion.
