# Jimmy Bain

**Jimmy Bain was a Scottish bassist who played with Rainbow and was a founding member of Dio, appearing on every Dio studio album from 1983 to 1990 and again on *Killing the Dragon* (2002).**

He died in January 2016 while on tour with Last in Line.
