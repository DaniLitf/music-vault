# John Cale

**Viola, bass, keyboards, backing vocals. Born March 9, 1942.**

John Cale is a Welsh musician, composer, and record producer, best known as a founding member of [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]] and for his influential solo work.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]]
- [[Artists/N/Nico]] (producer/collaborator)

## Biography
Cale co-founded The Velvet Underground with Lou Reed, bringing his classical and avant-garde training to the band. His contributions — droning viola, experimental arrangements — were essential to their early sound. After leaving the band in 1968, he became a highly regarded solo artist and producer, working with artists like Nico, Patti Smith, The Stooges, and Happy Mondays.
