# John Paul Jones (musician)

**John Paul Jones (born John Baldwin) is a British bassist, keyboardist, and arranger born January 3, 1946, best known as the bassist and keyboardist of [[Artists/L/Led Zeppelin]].**

Jones was a prolific session musician and arranger before co-founding Led Zeppelin in 1968. After the band disbanded following John Bonham's death in 1980, he resumed session work and production, collaborating with artists such as R.E.M., Heart, and Diamanda Galás. He later joined [[Artists/T/Them Crooked Vultures]] in 2009 with Dave Grohl and Josh Homme.
