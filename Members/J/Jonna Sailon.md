# Jonna Sailon

**Vocalist. Swedish.**

Jonna Sailon is a Swedish singer who joined [[Artists/A/All Ends]] in March 2009 as replacement vocalist for Emma Gelotte, performing on the band's second and final studio album, *A Road to Depression* (2010).

## Associated Artists
