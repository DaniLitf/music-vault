# John Balance

**John Balance (born Geoffrey Burton) was a British vocalist and artist, co-founder of Coil alongside Peter Christopherson, whose experimental, occult-influenced music and visual art made him one of the most singular figures in post-industrial culture.**

He died in an accidental fall from a balcony at his home in 2004. His work with Coil continues to be hugely influential in experimental and underground music.
