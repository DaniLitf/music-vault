# Jim Kimball

**Drummer. Died 2025.**

Jim Kimball was an American drummer who replaced Mac McNeilly in [[Artists/T/The Jesus Lizard]] in 1996. He was also associated with Mule and The Denison/Kimball Trio, connecting him to guitarist Duane Denison's post-Jesus Lizard work.
