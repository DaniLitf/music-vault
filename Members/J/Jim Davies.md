# Jim Davies

**Guitarist. Born 1973-10-18 in Maldon, Essex, England.**

Jim Davies is an English guitarist best known as a touring and recording guitarist for The Prodigy, contributing to *The Fat of the Land* (1997) and *Always Outnumbered, Never Outgunned* (2004), and for Pitchshifter, whom he joined in 1998. He later became a prolific production-music composer for TV and film, and has recorded solo under his own name and as part of Victory Pill and Shadow Addict.
