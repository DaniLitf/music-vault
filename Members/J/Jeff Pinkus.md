# Jeff Pinkus

**Bassist. American.**

Jeff Pinkus is an American bassist best known for his work with the Butthole Surfers. He played with [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] in the 2010s and collaborated on albums including *Hold It In* and *Pinkus Abortion Technician*.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] – bass (2013–2018)
- [[Artists/B/Butthole Surfers]] – bass
