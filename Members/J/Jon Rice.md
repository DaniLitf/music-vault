# Jon Rice

**Drums (2026–present).**

Jon Rice is an American drummer best known as the current drummer of [[Artists/I/In Flames]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/In Flames]] – drums (2026–present)
- [[Job for a Cowboy]] – former drummer
- [[Uncle Acid & the Deadbeats]] – drummer
- [[Umbra Vitae]] – drummer

## Biography

Rice joined In Flames in 2025 as a touring drummer following the departure of Tanner Wayne, and became a full member in 2026. He made his recording debut with the band on their fifteenth studio album. Prior to In Flames, he played with Job for a Cowboy, Uncle Acid & the Deadbeats, and has toured with Behemoth, Skeletonwitch, and The Red Chord.
