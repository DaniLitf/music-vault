**Keyboardist and turntablist. Born 16 July 1975.**

Jamie Oliver (born Richard J. Oliver) was the keyboardist and turntablist for Lostprophets, joining as the band's photographer and evolving into a full member after being told that non-band members could not join them on tour. He quickly learned to play turntables and keyboards and became an integral part of the band's sound from 1998 onwards, contributing to the distinctive electronic and orchestral elements across the band's later albums.
