# Joe Berry

**Saxophonist, keyboardist, EWI player.**

Joe Berry is an American multi-instrumentalist who joined [[Artists/M/M83]]'s touring band in 2016 for the *Junk* tour, playing saxophone, keyboards, and EWI (electronic wind instrument). He has remained part of the live line-up on subsequent tours, including in support of *Fantasy* (2023), and appears in the band's live credits on AllMusic.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/M/M83]] – saxophone, keyboards, EWI (2016–present)
