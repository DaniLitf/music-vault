# Jerome Fontamillas

**Keyboardist, guitarist, and backing vocalist. Born June 20, 1967.**

Jerome Fontamillas is a Filipino-American musician best known as the keyboardist and guitarist for the rock band Switchfoot. He previously co-founded the Christian industrial band Mortal and the alternative rock band Fold Zandura alongside Jyro Xhan. Fontamillas began touring with Switchfoot in 2000 and became a full-time member in 2001, contributing to their major-label breakthrough *The Beautiful Letdown* (2003) and every subsequent album.
