# Jay Pepper

**Guitarist. Welsh.**

Jay Pepper (born John Pepper) is a Welsh guitarist, best known as the founding guitarist and longest-serving member of glam metal band Tigertailz. He has been with the band since its formation in 1984 through all eras and lineups.
