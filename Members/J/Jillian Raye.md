# Jillian Raye

**Vocals. Born DATE.**

Jillian Raye is an American singer, best known as a vocalist for the supergroup [[Artists/0-9/3rd Secret]] and for her work in the band [[Artists/G/Giants in the Trees]] alongside Krist Novoselic and Jennifer Johnson.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/G/Giants in the Trees]] – vocals (2016–2019)
- [[Artists/0-9/3rd Secret]] – vocals (2021–present)

## Biography

Raye met Krist Novoselic through a mutual acquaintance and began collaborating with him on what would become Giants in the Trees. She co-wrote and sang on the band's releases before joining Novoselic's next project, 3rd Secret, as a co-lead vocalist alongside Jennifer Johnson. She also appears on the album cover of 3rd Secret's self-titled debut.
