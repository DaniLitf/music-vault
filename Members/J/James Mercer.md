# James Mercer

**Singer-songwriter and guitarist. Born May 1, 1976.**

James Mercer is an American singer-songwriter and guitarist best known as the founder, lead vocalist, and primary songwriter of The Shins. Born in Hawaii and raised in Albuquerque, New Mexico, Mercer formed the band in 1996 and led them to mainstream success with albums like *Oh, Inverted World* and *Chutes Too Narrow*. He also formed the duo Broken Bells with producer Danger Mouse.
