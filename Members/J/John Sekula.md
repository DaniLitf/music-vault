# John Sekula

**Guitarist. Born ?.**

John Sekula was an American guitarist who was an early member of the industrial metal band Mushroomhead. He played on the band's self-titled debut album (1995) and early releases, contributing to the band's heavy, layered sound during their formative period in Cleveland.
