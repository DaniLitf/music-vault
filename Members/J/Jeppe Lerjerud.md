# Jeppe Lerjerud

**Vocals. Swedish.**

Jeppe Lerjerud is a Swedish vocalist who was the original frontman of [[Artists/D/Disfear]] from 1989 to 1998, appearing on the band's early releases including *Soul Scars* and *Everyday Slaughter*. He departed before the band's reformation with Tomas Lindberg.
