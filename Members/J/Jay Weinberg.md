# Jay Weinberg

**Drums (2024–present).**

Jay Weinberg is an American drummer best known for his work with [[Artists/I/Infectious Grooves]], [[Artists/S/Slipknot]], and [[Against Me!]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Infectious Grooves]] – drums (2024–present)
- [[Artists/S/Slipknot]] – drums (2014–2023)
- [[Artists/A/Against Me!]] – drums (2011–2012)
- The Bouncing Souls – touring drummer

## Biography

Weinberg, son of noted drummer Max Weinberg, first gained prominence as the drummer for punk band Against Me! before joining Slipknot in 2014. He performed on Slipknot's *.5: The Gray Chapter* (2014), *We Are Not Your Kind* (2019), and *The End, So Far* (2022). After leaving Slipknot in 2023, he joined Infectious Grooves in 2024.
