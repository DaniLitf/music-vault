# Justin Meldal-Johnsen

**Bassist, multi-instrumentalist. Born 8 May 1970.**

Justin Meldal-Johnsen is an American bassist and multi-instrumentalist best known as the touring bassist for [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] (2005–2012) and a long-time collaborator with [[Artists/B/Beck]] and [[Artists/A/Air]]. He has also performed and recorded with [[Artists/M/M83]] and [[Artists/T/Tori Amos]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] – bass (2005–2012)
- [[Artists/B/Beck]] – bass, guitar (touring and studio, 1998–present)
- [[Artists/A/Air]] – bass, guitar (touring, 2000–2010)
- [[Artists/M/M83]] – bass (touring, 2011–2012)
- [[Artists/T/Tori Amos]] – bass (touring)

## Biography

Meldal-Johnsen established himself as a session and touring musician in the late 1990s, working extensively with Beck and Air. He joined Nine Inch Nails in 2005 as the bassist for the *With Teeth* tour and remained through the *Year Zero*, *Ghosts*, and *The Slip* tours, appearing on the live DVD *Beside You in Time*. He left the band in 2012 and was replaced by Pino Palladino.
