# James Kent

**Multi-instrumentalist, producer. Born c. 1990 in Paris, France.**

James Kent is the sole member of Perturbator, the French synthwave and dark electronic project he founded around 2010. Working entirely alone, he writes, records, and produces all material, drawing on influences from classic synth scores, industrial music, and metal to create a cinematic, dystopian sound. His albums — including *Dangerous Days* (2014) and *The Uncanny Valley* (2016) — are considered landmark works in the synthwave genre.
