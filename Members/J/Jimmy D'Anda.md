# Jimmy D'Anda

**Drummer. American.**

Jimmy D'Anda is an American rock drummer best known as the founding drummer of the Los Angeles glam metal band Bulletboys, with whom he recorded the self-titled debut album (1988) and toured with Ozzy Osbourne, Cheap Trick, and Bon Jovi. He also played with George Lynch's Lynch Mob and was part of the short-lived supergroup Zen Lunatic alongside John Corabi and Stevo Bruno.
