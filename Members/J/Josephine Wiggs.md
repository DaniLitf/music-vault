# Josephine Wiggs

**Bassist. American.**

Josephine Wiggs is an American bassist best known as a member of [[Artists/T/The Breeders]] from 1990 to 1997, playing on their breakthrough album *Last Splash*. She also played in the band [[Artists/B/Belly]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Breeders]] – bass (1990–1997)
- [[Artists/B/Belly]] – bass (1991–1996)
