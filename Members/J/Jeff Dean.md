# Jeff Dean

**Guitarist and recording engineer.**

Jeff Dean is an American punk rock guitarist and recording engineer based in Chicago, Illinois, originally from Las Vegas, Nevada. He got his start in the Las Vegas hardcore band [[Artists/T/Tomorrows Gone]] in 1994, and has since played guitar in [[Artists/T/The Bomb]] alongside Jeff Pezzati of Naked Raygun, [[Artists/N/Noise By Numbers]] with Dan Vapid of [[Artists/T/The Methadones]], the Chicago punk supergroup [[Artists/D/Dead Ending]], [[Artists/A/All Eyes West]], and [[Artists/E/Explode and Make Up]]. He also works as a recording engineer at Million Yen Studio in Chicago.
