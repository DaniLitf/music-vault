# Johan Edlund

**Vocalist, guitarist. Swedish.**

Johan Edlund is a Swedish musician and the sole constant member of [[Artists/T/Tiamat]] since founding it (as Treblinka) in 1987, steering the band from its death/doom metal roots into pioneering gothic metal and later atmospheric rock territory.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/Tiamat]] – vocals, guitar, keyboards, founder (1987–present)
