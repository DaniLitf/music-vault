# Jerome Grable

**Bassist. American.**

Jerome Grable was the original bassist of Obituary (then known as Executioner) from 1984 to 1987, appearing on the band's earliest demo recordings. He was replaced by Daniel Tucker before the band signed with Roadrunner Records.
