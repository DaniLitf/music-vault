# Jeramie Kling

**Drummer, multi-instrumentalist, and audio engineer. Born 1982-09-30 in Flint, Michigan, USA.**

Jeramie Kling is an American extreme metal drummer, recording engineer, and producer known for his prolific work across dozens of death, thrash, and speed metal projects. He was a founding member of melodic death metal band The Absence, drumming on four of its albums, and used the alias "War Machine" during his 2018–2023 tenure behind the kit for Venom Inc. Beyond drumming, Kling also sings, plays guitar and bass, and works as a front-of-house sound engineer for touring metal acts such as Sepultura, Exodus, and Deicide. In August 2024 he joined thrash metal veterans Overkill as touring drummer, becoming a permanent member by mid-2025.
