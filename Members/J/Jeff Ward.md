# Jeff Ward

**Jeff Ward was an American drummer who performed with [[Artists/M/Ministry]], [[Artists/R/Revolting Cocks]], [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]], and [[Artists/L/Lard]].**

He contributed drums to Pigface as a guest, bridging several major industrial acts.
