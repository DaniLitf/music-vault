# John Tardy

**Vocalist. Born March 24, 1968 in Tampa, Florida.**

John Tardy is an American death metal vocalist best known as the lead vocalist of Obituary, which he co-founded with his brother Donald Tardy and Trevor Peres in 1984. His guttural, low-pitched growl became one of the most recognizable voices in death metal. He has appeared on every Obituary release and has also performed in the side project Tardy Brothers with his brother Donald.
