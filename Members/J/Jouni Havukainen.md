# Jouni Havukainen

**All instruments, programming (1985–present).**

Jouni Havukainen is a Swedish musician and sound artist best known as the creator and sole member of the martial industrial project [[Artists/I/In Slaughter Natives]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/In Slaughter Natives]] – all instruments, programming (1985–present)

## Biography

Havukainen founded In Slaughter Natives in 1985 in Motala, Sweden, and has been its only constant member. The project became a cornerstone of the Cold Meat Industry label and is regarded as a pioneer of martial industrial and death industrial music.
