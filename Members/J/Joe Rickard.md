# Joe Rickard

**Drums (2016–2018).**

Joe Rickard is an American drummer and producer best known as a former drummer for [[Artists/I/In Flames]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/In Flames]] – drums (2016–2018)
- [[Red]] – former drummer (Christian rock band)
- [[Foreknown]] – drummer

## Biography

Rickard joined In Flames initially as a session drummer for the album *Battles* (2016) and subsequently became a full member. He also mixed and produced several In Flames recordings. He left the band in 2018 and was replaced by Tanner Wayne.
