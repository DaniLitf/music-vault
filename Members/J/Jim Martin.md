# Jim Martin

**Rhythm guitar (2013; live).**

Jim Martin is an American guitarist best known as the former guitarist of [[Artists/F/Faith No More]] and a former touring member of [[Artists/I/Infectious Grooves]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Infectious Grooves]] – rhythm guitar (2013; live)
- [[Artists/F/Faith No More]] – lead guitar (1983–1993)
- [[Eureka]] – guitarist
- [[Voodoo Cult]] – guitarist

## Biography

Martin was the original guitarist of Faith No More, appearing on *We Care a Lot* (1985), *Introduce Yourself* (1987), and *The Real Thing* (1989). He performed live with Infectious Grooves during their 2013 reunion shows.
