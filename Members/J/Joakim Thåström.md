# Joakim Thåström

**Vocals, guitar, keyboards, songwriting. Swedish. Founder of [[Artists/E/Ebba Grön]] and [[Artists/I/Imperiet]]; solo artist as [[Artists/T/Thåström]].**

Joakim Thåström (nicknamed Pimme) formed the pioneering Swedish punk band Ebba Grön in Stockholm in 1977, fronting it until its 1983 breakup. He continued with the surviving members as Rymdimperiet, soon renamed Imperiet, one of the biggest Swedish rock acts of the 1980s. Since 1989 he has released solo albums under his own name, moving from synth-driven industrial rock to guitar-driven rock across a decades-long solo career.
