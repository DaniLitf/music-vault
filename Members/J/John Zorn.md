# John Zorn

**Saxophonist, composer, producer. Born 1953-09-02.**

John Zorn is an American avant-garde composer, saxophonist, and producer based in New York City, and one of the most prolific and wide-ranging figures in experimental music. He is the founder of Tzadik Records and has led dozens of projects spanning free jazz, hardcore, klezmer, and noise, most notably Naked City and Painkiller (with Bill Laswell and Mick Harris). He appeared as a guest saxophonist on recordings by OLD.
