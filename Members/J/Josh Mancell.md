# Josh Mancell

**Drummer and composer. American.**

Josh Mancell is an American composer and multi-instrumentalist from Ann Arbor, Michigan, best known for composing music for the *Crash Bandicoot* and *Jak and Daxter* video game series. He has received two Daytime Emmy Award nominations for his work on *Clifford the Big Red Dog*. Outside of his composing career, he has played drums in Exploding Flowers since 2010 and was a member of The Moon Upstairs. He previously worked at Mutato Muzika, the music production company co-founded by Mark Mothersbaugh of Devo.
