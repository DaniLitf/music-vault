# Jason Martin

**Guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter. Born December 17, 1973.**

Jason Martin is an American musician best known as the founder, guitarist, and vocalist of Starflyer 59, the band he formed in 1993 and has led ever since. Beyond Starflyer 59, he has been involved in numerous side projects including Bon Voyage, Neon Horse, Lo Tom, Low & Behold, The Brothers Martin, Dance House Children, Enemy Ships, and Pony Express, and also works as a truck driver.
