# Joey Santiago

**Joey Santiago is an American guitarist born June 10, 1965, best known as the lead guitarist of [[Artists/P/Pixies]].**

Known for his distinctive surf-rock influenced guitar style, he performed as a guest with Pigface.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/P/Pigface]] – guitar (guest)
- [[Artists/P/Pixies]] – lead guitar (1986–1993, 2004–present)
