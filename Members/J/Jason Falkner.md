# Jason Falkner

**Multi-instrumentalist, songwriter.**

Jason Falkner is an American multi-instrumentalist and songwriter, a former member of the power pop band Jellyfish and of The Grays, who has released several acclaimed solo albums. He toured as a guitarist with [[Artists/A/Air]] during the *10 000 Hz Legend* era.
