# Josh Wink

**Josh Wink (born Joshua Winkelman, April 20, 1970, in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania) is an American electronic music DJ, producer, and co-founder of the Ovum Recordings label.**

Wink emerged from the Philadelphia house and techno scene in the early 1990s and broke internationally with the acid house track "Higher State of Consciousness" (1995), which became a dancefloor anthem across Europe. He has released on labels including Strictly Rhythm and Nervous Records, and continues to DJ and produce.
