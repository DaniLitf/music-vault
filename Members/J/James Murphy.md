# James Murphy

**Guitarist. Born July 30, 1967.**

James Murphy is an American heavy metal guitarist known for his work with Obituary, Death, Cancer, and Testament. He has been the lead guitarist of Obituary twice — first in 1991–1992 recording touring for *Cause of Death*, and again later. He also played on Death's *Spiritual Healing* (1990) and released several solo albums.
