# Juha Sievers

**Drums. Swedish.**

Juha Sievers was a founding drummer of [[Artists/C/Cemetary]], playing on the band's earliest demos and their 1992 debut *An Evil Shade of Grey* before leaving in 1993.

- [[Artists/C/Cemetary]] – drums (1989–1993, founding member)
