# John Engelbert

**John Engelbert is a Swedish guitarist and vocalist, best known as one half of the Stockholm rock duo Johnossi.**

Engelbert co-founded Johnossi with drummer Ossi Bonde and contributes guitar and lead vocals to the band's stripped-down, high-energy rock sound.
