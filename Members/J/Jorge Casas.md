# Jorge Casas

**Bass. Cuban-American.**

Jorge Casas was a Miami-based session bassist, producer and long-time musical director for Gloria Estefan and the Miami Sound Machine. He was hired during the Miami sessions for the [[Artists/C/Coverdale-Page]] album (1993), playing bass on all eleven tracks.
