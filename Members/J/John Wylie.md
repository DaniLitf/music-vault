# John Wylie

**Guitarist and record label founder.**

John Wylie is an American guitarist central to the 1990s South Florida metalcore scene. After playing in Tension and Organized Pain, he joined [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]] in 1994 and remained through the band's 1998 breakup; in December 1995 he co-founded [[Artists/M/Morning Again]] with fellow ex-Culture drummer Louie Long. Wylie is also the founder of Eulogy Recordings, the Florida hardcore label whose first signing was [[Artists/B/Bird of Ill Omen]].
