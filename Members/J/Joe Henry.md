# Joe Henry

**Singer-songwriter, guitarist, record producer. Born December 2, 1960 in Charlotte, North Carolina, USA.**

Joe Henry is an American singer-songwriter, guitarist, and record producer known for his literate, genre-blending songwriting and production work across folk, country, jazz, and rock. He has released numerous solo albums including *Short Man's Room* (1992), *Trampoline* (1996), and *Scar* (2001). As a producer, he has crafted acclaimed albums for Emmylow Harris, Solomon Burke, Allen Toussaint, and Bettye LaVette. He is the brother of Madonna's former husband, actor Sean Penn.
