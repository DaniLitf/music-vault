# Jim Dearmen

**Bass.**

Jim Dearmen, known to his bandmates only as "Kansas," was a friend of Keith Morris recruited as bassist for [[Artists/B/Black Flag]]'s precursor band Panic in early 1977. Described by Morris as "just this huge stoner," he frequently skipped rehearsals alongside drummer Bryan Migdol and left the band before the summer of 1977, never recording with the group.
