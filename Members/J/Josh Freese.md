# Josh Freese

**Drums (1992–1993; session). Born December 25, 1972.**

Josh Freese is an American drummer and session musician best known for his work with [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]], [[Artists/A/A Perfect Circle]], [[Artists/D/Devo]], and [[Artists/T/The Vandals]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Infectious Grooves]] – drums (1992–1993; session)
- [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] – touring/session drummer (2005–2008)
- [[Artists/A/A Perfect Circle]] – drums (1999–present)
- [[Artists/T/The Vandals]] – drums (1989–present)
- [[Artists/D/Devo]] – drums (2011–2016)
- [[Artists/G/Guns N' Roses]] – session drummer (2008)
- [[Sting]] – touring drummer
- [[Artists/T/The Damning Well]] – drums (2002–2003)
- [[Artists/B/Black Light Burns]] – drums, percussion (2005–2007)

## Biography

Freese is one of the most prolific session drummers in rock music, with over 300 albums to his credit. He played on Infectious Grooves' *Sarsippius' Ark* (1993) and continues to perform with A Perfect Circle and The Vandals.
