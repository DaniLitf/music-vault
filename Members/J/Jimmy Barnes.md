# Jimmy Barnes

**Vocalist. Australian.**

Jimmy Barnes is an Australian rock icon, best known as the frontman of the legendary pub rock band Cold Chisel. He has also had a highly successful solo career spanning decades and is regarded as one of Australia's greatest vocalists.
