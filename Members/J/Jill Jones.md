# Jill Jones

**Singer and songwriter. Born 11 July 1962 in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

Jill Jones is an American singer who provided backing vocals for Prince's band from 1982 to 1983 during the formative period of what would become The Revolution. She later released a self-titled debut album in 1987 on Paisley Park Records, produced by Prince.
