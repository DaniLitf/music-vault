**Bassist.**

Jenni Tarma is a Finnish bassist who studied electric bass at Berklee College of Music in Boston. She played bass for the indie rock band Jubilee and has had extensive touring experience with various international artists including Kylie Minogue, Melanie C, Har Mar Superstar, and Junior Senior.
