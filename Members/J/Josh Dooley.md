# Josh Dooley

**Keyboardist and guitarist. American.**

Josh Dooley is an American musician who played keyboards on Starflyer 59's *Leave Here a Stranger* (2001) and later contributed guitar on *My Island* (2006). He is also a member of the band MAP.
