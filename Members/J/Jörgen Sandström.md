# Jörgen Sandström

**Bassist, vocalist. Swedish.**

Jörgen Sandström is a Swedish bassist and vocalist who was a member of Entombed across two stints: from 1999 to 2005 and again from 2006 to 2014. He performed on *Uprising*, *Morning Star*, *Inferno*, and *Serpent Saints: The Ten Amendments*, and also provided backing vocals for the band.
