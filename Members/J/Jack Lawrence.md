# Jack Lawrence

**Bass. American. Founding member of [[Artists/T/The Greenhornes]].**

Jack Lawrence co-founded The Greenhornes in Cincinnati in 1996. In the mid-2000s he and bandmate Patrick Keeler backed Loretta Lynn on *Van Lear Rose* (2004) and co-founded [[Artists/T/The Raconteurs]] with Jack White and Brendan Benson, which curtailed Greenhornes activity for several years. He also plays bass in [[Artists/T/The Dead Weather]] and banjo/autoharp in the Detroit band Blanche.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Greenhornes]] – bass (1996–2012, founder)
- [[Artists/T/The Raconteurs]] – bass, banjo, backing vocals (2005–present)
- [[Artists/T/The Dead Weather]] – bass, backing vocals, occasional drums (2009–present)
- [[Artists/J/Jack White]] – bass (solo touring)
