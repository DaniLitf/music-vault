# Jerome Dillon

**Drummer. Born 14 March 1970.**

Jerome Dillon is an American drummer best known as the drummer for [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] from 1999 to 2005. He replaced Chris Vrenna and performed on *The Fragile* (1999) tour, the *And All That Could Have Been* live album, and the *With Teeth* (2005) album and tour before leaving due to health issues.

## Associated Artists

## Biography

Dillon joined Nine Inch Nails in 1999 as the band's drummer for the *Fragility* tour, replacing Chris Vrenna. He became the first drummer to record on a Nine Inch Nails studio album since Vrenna, contributing to *With Teeth* (2005). He left the band during the *With Teeth* tour due to a heart condition and was replaced by Josh Freese.
