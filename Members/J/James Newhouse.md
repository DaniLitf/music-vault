# James Newhouse

**Vocalist. American.**

James Newhouse was the original lead vocalist of Dallas punk band [[Artists/H/Hagfish|Hagfish]], joining founders Zach and Doni Blair alongside drummer Banning Lyon in 1991, having previously sung for the band Terminal Rot. Newhouse performed with Hagfish from 1991 to 1992, helping establish the band's early sound with a demo tape reflecting Descendents influences. He was fired from the group in 1993 and died in a car accident in October 1994 at age 26.
