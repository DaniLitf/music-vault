# Jesse Johnson

**Guitarist and vocalist.**

Jesse Johnson is an American guitarist and vocalist who was a founding member of The Time, playing guitar and contributing backing vocals from 1981 to 1985. After leaving the band, he launched a solo career with the album *Shockadelica* (1986) and its hit single of the same name. He has reunited with The Time for various performances in 1990, 2008, 2011, and 2017.
