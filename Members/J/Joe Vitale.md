# Joe Vitale

**American musician and producer. Born 1956, died 2023.**

Joe Vitale (born Joseph Edward Vitale) was an American drummer, multi-instrumentalist, and songwriter. He is best known for his work as a touring and session drummer for Crosby, Stills & Nash, Joe Walsh, and the Eagles, as well as his contributions to Controlled Bleeding.
