# J.G. Thirlwell

**J.G. Thirlwell is an Australian composer, producer, and multi-instrumentalist, the creator of the Foetus project, a foundational figure in the industrial music underground since 1981, a guest contributor to Pigface, and composer of the cartoon *The Venture Bros.*.**
