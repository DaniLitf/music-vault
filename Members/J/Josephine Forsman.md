# Josephine Forsman

**Drummer. Born 20 May 1981 in Robertsfors.**

Josephine Forsman is a Swedish drummer who co-founded Sahara Hotnights in 1991 with childhood friends from Robertsfors, Västerbotten. The band began playing together in their early teens before winning a regional battle-of-the-bands contest in 1995.
