# Jens Kidman

**Swedish vocalist and guitarist. Born 8 June 1966.**

Jens Kidman is a Swedish vocalist and guitarist best known as the lead vocalist and founding member of Meshuggah. He provides the band's distinctive rhythmic vocal delivery.
