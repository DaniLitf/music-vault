# Jennifer Johnson

**Vocals. Born DATE.**

Jennifer Johnson is an American singer, best known as a vocalist for the supergroup [[Artists/0-9/3rd Secret]] and for her work in the band [[Artists/G/Giants in the Trees]] alongside Krist Novoselic and Jillian Raye.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/G/Giants in the Trees]] – vocals (2016–2019)
- [[Artists/0-9/3rd Secret]] – vocals (2021–present)

## Biography

Johnson was a member of Giants in the Trees, a folk/rock band formed by Krist Novoselic in 2016, alongside Jillian Raye. When Novoselic proposed combining material from various collaborators in 2021, Johnson and Raye became co-vocalists in the resulting supergroup 3rd Secret, which also features members of Soundgarden, Pearl Jam, and Void.
