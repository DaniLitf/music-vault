# Jimmy Cliff

**Jimmy Cliff (born James Chambers, July 30, 1948, in Somerton, Saint James, Jamaica) is a Jamaican reggae singer, songwriter, musician, and actor, one of the first Jamaican artists to achieve international fame.**

Cliff began recording in Kingston as a teenager in the early 1960s and was a major force in bringing ska and reggae to international audiences. His role in and soundtrack to the 1972 film *The Harder They Come* is a reggae landmark. In 2010, he was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame.
