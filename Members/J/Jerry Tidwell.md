# Jerry Tidwell

**Lead guitarist. American.**

Jerry Tidwell was the original lead guitarist of Obituary (then Executioner) from 1984 to 1988, appearing on the band's early demos. He was replaced by Allen West shortly before the recording of *Slowly We Rot* (1989).
