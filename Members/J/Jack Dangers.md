# Jack Dangers

**Electronic musician, producer, DJ. Born 1965-01-11.**

Jack Dangers (born John Stephen Corrigan) is an English electronic musician, DJ, and producer, best known as the founder and sole permanent member of [[Artists/M/Meat Beat Manifesto]]. He has also been part of Perennial Divide and the Tino collective, and has remixed and produced for artists including David Bowie, Nine Inch Nails, DJ Spooky, and Depeche Mode. He has lived in San Francisco since the 1990s.
