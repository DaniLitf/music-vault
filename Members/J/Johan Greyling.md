# Johan Greyling

**Guitar. South African. Founding member of [[Artists/S/Seether]].**

Johan Greyling was Seether's original lead guitarist when the band formed as Saron Gas in Pretoria in 1999, departing in 2002 around the time of the band's relocation to the United States and renaming to Seether.

## Associated Artists
