# James Love

**Rhythm guitar. American. Former member of [[Artists/T/The Dillinger Escape Plan]].**

James Love, formerly of Fenix*TX, filled in as The Dillinger Escape Plan's touring guitarist from late 2004 through 2006 after Brian Benoit's injury, and formally joined as rhythm guitarist from 2012 until his 2015 replacement by Kevin Antreassian.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Dillinger Escape Plan]] – rhythm guitar (2004–2006 touring; 2012–2015)
