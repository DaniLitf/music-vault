# JinJoo Lee

**Guitarist. Born November 15, 1987 in Seoul, South Korea.**

JinJoo Lee is a South Korean guitarist best known as the lead guitarist of DNCE alongside Joe Jonas. She grew up in South Korea and moved to the United States at age 17 to study at Berklee College of Music before joining CeeLo Green's backing band Scarlet Fever. She has also performed with Charli XCX, Jordin Sparks, and Miley Cyrus.
