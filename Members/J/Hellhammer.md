# Hellhammer

**Norwegian drummer. Born 2 February 1969.**

Jan Axel Blomberg, known as Hellhammer, is a Norwegian drummer best known as the drummer of Mayhem and as a session and live drummer for Dimmu Borgir. He is considered one of the most technically proficient drummers in extreme metal.
