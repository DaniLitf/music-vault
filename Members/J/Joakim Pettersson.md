# Joakim Pettersson

**Guitar. Swedish.**

Joakim "Jocke" Pettersson was a Swedish guitarist and a founding member of Anti Cimex, playing guitar from 1981 to 1986. He played on the band's early EPs *Anarkist Attack*, *Raped Ass*, *Victims of a Bomb Raid*, and the *Criminal Trap* mini-LP before leaving the band.
