# José Martinez

**Guitarist. Miami, Florida.**

José Martinez is an American guitarist from Miami, Florida, and a founding guitarist of [[Artists/B/Bird of Ill Omen]], having played in its 1996 precursors Crestfallen and Fork in the Road. He left the band in October 1996 when he moved to another city, and went on to play in To the Bitter End and [[Artists/P/Poison the Well]].
