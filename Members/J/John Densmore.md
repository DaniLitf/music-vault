# John Densmore

**John Densmore is an American drummer and co-founder of The Doors, known for his jazz-influenced playing style that contributed to the band's unique sound.**

Densmore has been protective of The Doors' legacy and refused to allow the band's music to be used in commercial advertising, a position that led to legal disputes with other surviving members.
