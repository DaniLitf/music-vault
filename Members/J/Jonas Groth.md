# Jonas Groth

**Norwegian vocalist, keyboardist, and producer, brother of Stephan Groth.**

Jonas Groth is the younger brother of Stephan Groth and joined Apoptygma Berzerk as a live keyboardist in 2009, later becoming a full-time member and co-producer. He has contributed to the albums *Exit Popularity Contest* and recent EPs. Outside of APB, he runs the side project Piston Damp with Truls Sønsterud.
