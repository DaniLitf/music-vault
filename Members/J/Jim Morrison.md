# Jim Morrison

**Jim Morrison was an American singer, poet, and lyricist best known as the lead vocalist and primary lyricist of The Doors, whose charismatic and often provocative stage presence made him one of the most iconic rock frontmen of the 1960s.**

Morrison's poetic, literary approach to songwriting and his unpredictable live performances defined The Doors' identity. He died in Paris on July 3, 1971, aged 27, in circumstances that remain officially undetermined.
