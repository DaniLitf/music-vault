# John Entwistle

**John Entwistle was a British bassist and co-founder of The Who, known as "The Ox," whose melodic, aggressive bass playing — audible and distinctive even over Pete Townshend's roaring guitar — redefined the role of bass in rock music.**

Entwistle died of a cocaine-induced heart attack on June 27, 2002, the night before The Who were to begin their US tour.
