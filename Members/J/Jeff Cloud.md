# Jeff Cloud

**Bassist and label founder. American.**

Jeff Cloud is an American musician best known as the bassist for Starflyer 59 from 1997 to 2004, appearing on several of the band's early albums. He also played synthesizers for Joy Electric from 1996 to 2002 and founded the Velvet Blue Music label, which released albums by Starflyer 59, Joy Electric, and other indie acts. He also fronts the project Pony Express.
