# Jorma Kaukonen

**Jorma Kaukonen** (born December 23, 1940) is an American guitarist and vocalist. He was the lead guitarist of [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]] and co-founder of [[Artists/H/Hot Tuna]].

- [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]] – lead guitar, vocals (1965–1972, 1989, 1996)
- [[Artists/H/Hot Tuna]] – guitar, vocals (1969–present)

Kaukonen is widely regarded as one of the most influential guitarists of the psychedelic era, blending blues, folk, and rock. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame as a member of Jefferson Airplane in 1996.
