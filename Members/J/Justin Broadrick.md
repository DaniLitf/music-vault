# Justin Broadrick

**Justin Broadrick (born September 15, 1969, in Birmingham, England) is a British musician, the co-founder and driving force behind industrial metal band Godflesh and the sole creator of post-metal/shoegaze project Jesu.**

Broadrick began making music as a teenager, joining the pre-Napalm Death lineup Head of David before co-founding Godflesh with G.C. Green in 1988. Godflesh became one of the most influential industrial metal bands of the 1990s. After Godflesh dissolved in 2002, Broadrick launched Jesu and a variety of ambient/experimental side projects under aliases including JK Flesh. Godflesh reunited in 2010.
