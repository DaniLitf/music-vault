# Johan Gustafsson

**Bass. Swedish.**

Johan Gustafsson, known in [[Artists/T/The Hives]] as "The Johan and Only," is a Swedish bassist previously of Swedish punk band Randy. He joined The Hives in late 2013, replacing founding bassist Dr. Matt Destruction, who retired for health reasons.
