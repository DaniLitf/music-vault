# James Michael

**Vocalist, rhythm guitar, producer. American. Born 1968-09-26.**

James Michael is an American musician, songwriter, and record producer, born in Holland, Michigan. He is best known as lead vocalist and rhythm guitarist of [[Artists/S/Sixx A.M.]], the band he co-founded with Nikki Sixx and DJ Ashba in 2006, and has separately worked as a producer/songwriter for artists including Kelly Clarkson, Mötley Crüe, and Scorpions.
