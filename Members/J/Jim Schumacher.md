# Jim Schumacher

**Keyboardist, vocalist. American.**

Jim Schumacher was a keyboardist and vocalist who was a founding member of [[Artists/T/Ten Inch Men]], contributing to their early sound and the 1986 EP *Hours in Pain* before departing the band.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/Ten Inch Men]] – keyboards, vocals (1983–1986)
