# Jesse Sandoval

**Drummer. American.**

Jesse Sandoval is an American drummer best known as the drummer for The Shins from the band's formation in 1996 until 2009. He played on the first three albums, including *Oh, Inverted World*, *Chutes Too Narrow*, and *Wincing the Night Away*.
