# Joseph Simmons (guitarist)

**Guitarist.**

Joseph Simmons is an American guitarist who played in Dinner Time and Lock Down before becoming a founding guitarist of [[Artists/B/Bird of Ill Omen]] in 1996, leaving in January 1997 to join [[Artists/M/Morning Again]]. He joined [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] in March 1998 and has appeared on every release since *The Fists of Time* (1998); alongside Damien Moyal, he was one of the two members who held the band together through its 2000–2002 line-up turmoil, and he returned with the 2008 reunion. He was also a member of the Florida hardcore band [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]].
