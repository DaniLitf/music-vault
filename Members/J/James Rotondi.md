# James Rotondi

**Guitarist, music journalist.**

James Rotondi is an American guitarist and longtime music journalist for *Guitar Player* magazine, who toured as a guitarist with [[Artists/A/Air]] during the *10 000 Hz Legend* era. He has also worked as a session and touring musician for a range of rock and pop acts.
