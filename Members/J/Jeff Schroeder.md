# Jeff Schroeder

**Guitarist and songwriter. American. Born February 4, 1974.**

Jeffrey Kim Schroeder is an American guitarist and musician best known for his work with the shoegaze band The Lassie Foundation (1996–2006, 2007–2008) and his extensive tenure with the alternative rock band The Smashing Pumpkins (2007–2023). Joining the Smashing Pumpkins as a touring guitarist in 2007, he became a core member contributing to five studio albums and developing a distinctive guitar style that complemented founder Billy Corgan's work. Beyond his music career, Schroeder pursued graduate studies in comparative literature at UCLA, specializing in Asian American literature and critical theory.
