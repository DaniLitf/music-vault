# Jim Tillman

**Bassist. American.**

Jim Tillman is an American bassist, the main-lineup bass player for Seattle band the U-Men, appearing on their self-titled 1984 EP, *Stop Spinning* (1985), and the *Deep Six* compilation (1986). He replaced Tommy Simpson as Love Battery's bassist by mid-1990, playing on the expanded *Between the Eyes* release (1991) and *Dayglo* (1992) before leaving the band.
