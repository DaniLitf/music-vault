# John Fields

**Multi-instrumentalist, producer. American.**

John Fields is an American record producer and multi-instrumentalist who has worked with a wide range of pop and rock artists. He contributed bass, guitar, keyboards, and percussion to The Dead Daisies' debut album in addition to producing it.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Dead Daisies]] – bass, guitar, keyboards, percussion (2013)
- [[Artists/N/Nick Jonas and the Administration]] – bass, producer (2009–2011, 2016)
