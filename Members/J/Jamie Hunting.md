# Jamie Hunting

**Bassist. American.**

Jamie Hunting is an American bassist best known as a member of the hard rock supergroup Union and for his work with David Lee Roth and Eddie Money. In the 1980s he toured and recorded with Eddie Money and Fiona, and in the early 1990s he joined David Lee Roth's solo band, playing on the *Your Filthy Little Mouth* tour. He was later spotted by Bruce Kulick while playing in Hollywood and recruited for Union, with whom he released two studio albums and toured internationally.
