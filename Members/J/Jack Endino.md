# Jack Endino

**Guitarist, record producer. American. Born 1964.**

Jack Endino is an American musician and producer based in Seattle, known as guitarist and co-founder (with Daniel House) of [[Artists/S/Skin Yard]] in 1985. He is even better known as a producer and engineer, having founded Reciprocal Recording in 1986 and recorded seminal albums for Nirvana (including their debut *Bleach*), Soundgarden, Mudhoney, and countless other Sub Pop and Seattle-scene bands, earning him the nickname "Godfather of Grunge."
