# Jessie Farnsworth

**Guitar, vocals. American.**

Jessie Farnsworth is an American guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter best known for touring and recording with Jason Newsted's band [[Artists/N/Newsted]] as a founding member, sharing bills with acts including Iron Maiden, Megadeth, and Slayer. He later co-founded the band Underlord with Newsted drummer Jesus Mendez Jr.
