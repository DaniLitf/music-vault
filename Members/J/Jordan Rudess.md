# Jordan Rudess

**American keyboardist and songwriter. Born 4 November 1956.**

Jordan Rudess is an American keyboardist, classically trained at Juilliard's Pre-College Division from age nine, who joined Dream Theater in 1999, replacing Derek Sherinian and appearing on every album since *Metropolis Pt. 2: Scenes from a Memory*. He also formed the trio Levin Minnemann Rudess with Tony Levin in 2013 and founded the music software company Wizdom Music in 2010.
