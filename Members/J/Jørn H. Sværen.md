# Jørn H. Sværen

**Multi-instrumentalist, vocals, lyrics. Norwegian. Core member of [[Artists/U/Ulver]].**

Jørn H. Sværen, an author, joined Ulver in 2000 following the release of *Perdition City*, becoming the third constant member alongside Kristoffer Rygg and Tore Ylwizaker.
