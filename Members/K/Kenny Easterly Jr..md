# Kenny Easterly Jr.

**Drummer. American.**

Kenny Easterly Jr. is an American drummer who played in the Cleveland thrash/power metal band Purgatory during their 2018–2019 reunion, alongside his father, founding drummer Kenny Easterly. He has also played in Maggotbreath, Fistula, and Cryptkicker.
