# Kurt Cobain

**Kurt Cobain was an American vocalist and guitarist born February 20, 1967, died April 5, 1994, best known as the co-founder and frontman of [[Artists/N/Nirvana]].**

Cobain co-founded Nirvana in Aberdeen, Washington in 1987 with Krist Novoselic, preceding the band with the early project [[Artists/F/Fecal Matter]]. Nirvana's breakthrough album *Nevermind* (1991) helped bring alternative rock into the mainstream. Cobain's distinctive songwriting combined pop melodies with heavy distortion and introspective, often dark lyrics. He died by suicide in 1994.
