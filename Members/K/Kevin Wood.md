# Kevin Wood

**Guitarist. Born 1961-09-16.**

Kevin Wood is an American guitarist who co-founded Malfunkshun in 1980 with his younger brother, vocalist Andrew Wood, and drummer Regan Hagar. After Andrew's death in 1990, Kevin continued playing in Seattle bands including the Fire Ants and Devilhead, and has since kept Malfunkshun's legacy alive by performing its music with a revamped lineup.
