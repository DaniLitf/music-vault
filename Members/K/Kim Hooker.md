# Kim Hooker

**Vocalist. Welsh.**

Kim Hooker is a Welsh singer, formerly of the band Rankelson, who fronted glam metal band Tigertailz on their breakthrough album *Bezerk* (1990) and again during their reunion from 2005 to 2012. He departed in 2012 amid reported conflict with the rest of the band, and went on to launch the band Bare Knuckle Messiahs.
