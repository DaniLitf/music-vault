# Kevin Drake

**Guitar (touring). Born March 26, 1979.**

Kevin Drake is an American multi-instrumentalist from Los Angeles who originally auditioned as a bassist for [[Artists/0-9/30 Seconds to Mars|30 Seconds to Mars]] before being brought on as an additional touring guitarist in 2001. He remained with the band for a little over a year before departing over touring-related issues, and later formed the band Dillusion with members of Hyper Crush and After Midnight Project.
