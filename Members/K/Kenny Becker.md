**Singer, guitarist, and founder of Goon.**

Kenny Becker is an American musician and founder of the Los Angeles-based indie rock band Goon. Originally creating Goon as a solo project, Becker has expanded the project into a full band featuring guitarist Dillon Peralta, drummer Andy Polito, and bassist Tamara Simons. Goon's music is characterized by introspective, guitar-driven indie and alternative rock. The band released their recent album Dream 3 in July 2025, which has been featured in major music publications and received critical acclaim. Becker continues to lead the band while maintaining the project's creative direction.
