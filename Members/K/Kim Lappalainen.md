# Kim Lappalainen

**Guitarist. Finnish.**

Kim Lappalainen played guitar for Impaled Nazarene from 2022 to 2024, serving as a guitarist during this period of the band's career. His contributions helped maintain the band's extreme metal sound during his tenure.
