# Kelly David-Smith

**Drums. American.**

Kelly David-Smith co-founded [[Artists/F/Flotsam and Jetsam]] in Phoenix, Arizona in 1981 and remained its drummer for over three decades before leaving in December 2014; he personally selected Jason Bittner as his replacement.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/F/Flotsam and Jetsam]] – drums (1981–2014, founding member)
