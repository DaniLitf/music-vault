# Khn de Poitrine

**Microtonal guitars, bass, loop station, vocals. Born Unknown.**

Khn de Poitrine is one half of the anonymous Canadian math rock duo [[Artists/A/Angine de Poitrine]]. He performs with oversized papier-mâché masks and polka-dotted costumes, playing a custom-built double-neck hybrid microtonal guitar/bass.
