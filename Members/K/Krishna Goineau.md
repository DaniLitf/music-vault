# Krishna Goineau

**French-German vocalist. Born 1960.**

Krishna Goineau is a French-German vocalist best known as the lead singer of Liaisons Dangereuses. His distinctive multilingual delivery in French and Spanish added an "exotic" edge to the band's confrontational electronic sound. He was previously involved in the Spanish punk band Xeerox.
