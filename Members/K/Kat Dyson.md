# Kat Dyson

**Guitarist. Born in New Orleans, Louisiana.**

Kat Dyson is an American guitarist who joined Prince's New Power Generation in 1996 alongside Mike Scott. She contributed to the *Newpower Soul* album and toured extensively with Prince. She also played with the band Tchukon and has worked with Mavis Staples.
