# Kaela Sinclair

**Vocalist, keyboardist.**

Kaela Sinclair is an American singer-songwriter from Denton, Texas, who joined [[Artists/M/M83]] as vocalist and keyboardist in 2016 after being chosen from the band's open search for a new member, first touring in support of *Junk*. She also records as a solo artist and has released her own albums, including *Sun & Mirror* (2013).
