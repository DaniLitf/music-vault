# Kevin Jonas

**Lead guitarist, vocalist. Born November 5, 1987 in Teaneck, New Jersey.**

Kevin Jonas is an American musician, singer, songwriter and actor who rose to fame as the lead guitarist of the Jonas Brothers alongside his younger brothers Joe and Nick. After the band's breakup in 2013, he ventured into reality television with *Married to Jonas* and *Celebrity Apprentice*, and co-founded the influencer marketing company The Blu Market. He released his debut solo single "Changing" in November 2025.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/J/Jonas Brothers]] – lead guitar, vocals (2005–2013, 2019–present)

## Biography

Kevin Jonas was raised in Wyckoff, New Jersey and attended Eastern Christian High School. Unlike his younger brothers, he originally did not intend to become a musician but learned guitar from a book he found. He became a core member of the Jonas Brothers, contributing rhythm and lead guitar. After the band's breakup, he focused on business ventures including JonasWerner construction and The Blu Market. He co-hosts the ABC reality competition series *Claim to Fame* with his brother Frankie. In June 2024 he was diagnosed with and treated for skin cancer.
