# Kim Deal

**Bass, vocals. American. Born June 10, 1961.**

Kim Deal is an American bassist and vocalist best known as the co-founding member and bassist of [[Artists/P/Pixies]] and co-founder of [[Artists/T/The Breeders]]. She played with Pixies from their formation in 1986 until 2013, providing bass and vocals on the band's classic 1980s and early 1990s albums. While still a member of Pixies, she co-founded The Breeders in 1989, which became successful in their own right. After leaving Pixies, she focused on The Breeders and various solo projects.
