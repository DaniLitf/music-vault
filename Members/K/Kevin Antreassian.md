# Kevin Antreassian

**Rhythm guitar. American. Former member of [[Artists/T/The Dillinger Escape Plan]].**

Kevin Antreassian, a former guitar student of Ben Weinman, joined The Dillinger Escape Plan as rhythm guitarist in May 2015, replacing James Love, and remained through the band's final album *Dissociation* (2016) and 2017 breakup, surviving a tour bus crash in Poland in February 2017 with fractured vertebrae.
