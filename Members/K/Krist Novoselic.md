# Krist Novoselic

**Bass, accordion. Born May 16, 1965.**

Krist Novoselic is an American musician best known as the bassist and co-founder of the grunge band [[Artists/N/Nirvana]]. He has also played in several post-Nirvana projects and served as a political activist.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/N/Nirvana]] – bass (1987–1994)
- [[Artists/0-9/3rd Secret]] – bass, accordion (2021–present)
- [[Artists/G/Giants in the Trees]] – bass, accordion (2016–2019)
- [[Artists/S/Sweet 75]] – bass (1995–2000)
- [[Artists/E/Eyes Adrift]] – bass (2002–2003)

## Biography

Novoselic co-founded Nirvana with Kurt Cobain in 1987, and the band went on to become one of the most influential rock bands of all time. After Nirvana's end following Cobain's death in 1994, Novoselic formed Sweet 75 and later Eyes Adrift. He has been active in politics and served as a board member of the advocacy group FairVote. In 2021 he co-founded 3rd Secret with Jillian Raye and Jennifer Johnson.
