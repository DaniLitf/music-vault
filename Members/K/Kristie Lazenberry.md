# Kristie Lazenberry

**Singer. Born in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

Kristie Lazenberry is an American vocalist who was a core member of the Minneapolis funk band 94 East. She sang background vocals and wrote songs alongside Pepé Willie and Marcy Ingvoldstad, and remained one of the band's three core members through its history.
