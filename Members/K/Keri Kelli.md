# Keri Kelli

**Guitarist. American.**

Keri Kelli is an American guitarist known for his extensive work as a touring and session musician with numerous rock and metal acts. He has played guitar for Ratt, L.A. Guns, Alice Cooper, Slash's Snakepit, and Skid Row, among others, establishing himself as one of the most prolific guitarists in the hard rock circuit.
