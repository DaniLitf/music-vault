**Guitarist.**

Keri Kelli is an American hard rock guitarist who has played with numerous rock acts including RATT, Alice Cooper, Vince Neil, and L.A. Guns. He was part of Bobby Blotzer's Angel City Outlaws project alongside John Corabi and Robbie Crane, and was also a member of the supergroup Twenty 4 Seven.
