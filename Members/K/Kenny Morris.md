# Kenny Morris

**Drummer. British.**

Kenny Morris was an English musician, painter, and founding drummer of [[Artists/S/Siouxsie and the Banshees]], joining the band in February 1977. Noted for his innovative approach to drumming—playing mostly tom-toms and rarely using hi-hat—he is regarded as an influential figure in post-punk avant-garde music. Morris played on the band's debut album *The Scream* (1978) and *Join Hands* (1979) before departing at the beginning of the *Join Hands* tour in September 1979. His distinctive drumming style has been cited as an influence by notable musicians including Stephen Morris of Joy Division and Kevin Haskins of Bauhaus. After leaving the Banshees, Morris eventually moved to Ireland in 1993 to pursue art, running an art gallery in Kildare and later performing with the post-punk goth band Shrine of the Vampyre. He passed away on January 15, 2026.
