# Karin Oliver

**Vocalist. American.**

Karin Oliver is an American vocalist best known as the primary vocalist for the experimental rock project His Name Is Alive during their 4AD years, singing on the albums *Livonia*, *Home Is in Your Head*, *Mouth by Mouth*, and *Stars on E.S.P.*
