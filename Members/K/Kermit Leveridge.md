# Kermit Leveridge

**Vocals. British.**

Kermit Leveridge, known as "Kermit," is an English rapper and singer from Manchester, best known as a member of [[Artists/R/Ruthless Rap Assassins]] and later co-founder of [[Artists/B/Black Grape]] alongside Shaun Ryder. He provided additional vocals on "Don't Take My Kindness for Weakness" on [[Artists/T/The Heads]]' *No Talking, Just Head* (1996), credited as Paul "Kermit" Leverage.
