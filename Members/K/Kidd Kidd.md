# Kidd Kidd

**Rapper. Born in New Orleans, Louisiana.**

Kidd Kidd is an American rapper from New Orleans who first gained attention as a Young Money artist featured on Lil Wayne's *Tha Carter III*. He signed to G-Unit Records and was added to G-Unit as its newest member during the group's 2014 reunion, appearing on the EPs *The Beauty of Independence* (2014) and *The Beast Is G Unit* (2015). He left the group and label in April 2018 to run his own label, RLLNR Entertainment.
