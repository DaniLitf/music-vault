# Koenraad Van-Lishout

**Producer, programmer. Belgian.**

Koenraad Van-Lishout, known by his stage name Kovali, was a founding member of the Belgian hip house project Hi Tek 3. He co-produced the group's music alongside fellow members Yosef Wolde-Mariam and Stijn Lauwers.
