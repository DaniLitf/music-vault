# Kelvin Morris

**Vocalist. British.**

Kelvin "Cal" Morris is a British vocalist best known as the iconic frontman of Discharge from 1977 to 1987. His harsh, shouted vocal style defined the D-beat genre and was hugely influential on hardcore punk, crust punk, and extreme metal. Originally a roadie, he took over vocals when Tezz switched to drums. He sang on all of Discharge's classic early material including *Hear Nothing See Nothing Say Nothing*.
