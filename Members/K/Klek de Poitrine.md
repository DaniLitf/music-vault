# Klek de Poitrine

**Drums, vocals. Born Unknown.**

Klek de Poitrine is one half of the anonymous Canadian math rock duo [[Artists/A/Angine de Poitrine]]. He and Khn de Poitrine have been musical collaborators since age 13. Klek's drums are covered with black cloth to darken their tone and maintain the visual aesthetic.
