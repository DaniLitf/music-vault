# Kerry King

**Guitarist. Born 1964-06-03 in Los Angeles, California, USA.**

Kerry King is a co-founding guitarist of Slayer, known for his aggressive riffing style and feedback-heavy solos. He was the primary continued public face of Slayer throughout their career and has remained active post-disbandment, releasing his first solo album *From Hell I Rise* in 2024.
