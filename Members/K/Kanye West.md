# Kanye West

**Kanye West (legally changed name to Ye in 2021) is an American rapper, record producer, and fashion designer from Chicago who became one of the most commercially successful and critically acclaimed hip hop artists of all time, known equally for his musical ambition and his controversial public behavior.**

West's albums from *The College Dropout* (2004) through *My Beautiful Dark Twisted Fantasy* (2010) are widely considered among the greatest in hip hop history. His later career has been marked by public controversies including antisemitic statements.
