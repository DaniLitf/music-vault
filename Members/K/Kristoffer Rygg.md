# Kristoffer Rygg

**Vocals, composer. Norwegian. Founder of [[Artists/U/Ulver]].**

Kristoffer Rygg, known as Garm, founded Ulver in Oslo in 1993 at age 16, initially in the Norwegian black metal mould. He briefly sang for [[Artists/B/Borknagar]] (1995–1997) as the band's original vocalist while also fronting Ulver, before departing both black metal and Borknagar as Ulver evolved into an experimental electronic and art rock project. He remains Ulver's sole constant original member and primary creative driver.
