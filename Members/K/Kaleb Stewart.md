# Kaleb Stewart

**Bassist, vocalist. Died March 2021.**

Kaleb Stewart was an American bass guitarist and backing vocalist who anchored [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]]'s classic 1998–2000 line-up, even filling in on lead vocals in 2000, and returned for the band's 2008 reunion before being dismissed in June 2019. A well-loved figure in the Florida punk scene, he died in March 2021, and Tampa musicians held benefit shows in his memory.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] – bass guitar, backing vocals (1998–2000, 2008–2019); lead vocals (2000)
