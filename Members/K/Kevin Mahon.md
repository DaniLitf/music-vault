# Kevin Mahon

**Drummer. American.**

Kevin Mahon is a drummer who joined [[Artists/A/Against Me!]] shortly after its formation, playing on the band's earliest demo recordings including *Vivida Vis!* (1998). He left the band in 2001 following a van accident that ended the group's first tour as a four-piece.
