# Kenny Aronoff

**Kenny Aronoff** (born March 7, 1953) is an American drummer and percussionist who has performed with numerous major artists, including [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]].

- [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]] – drums (1989; session and touring)
- [[John Mellencamp]] – drums (1980–1996)
- [[Artists/T/The Smashing Pumpkins]] – drums (touring)
