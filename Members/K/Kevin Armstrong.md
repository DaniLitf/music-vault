# Kevin Armstrong

**Guitarist, keyboardist. British.**

Kevin Armstrong is a British session musician who played additional guitar and keyboards on [[Artists/T/Tin Machine]]'s first two studio albums and its first tour, having previously worked with David Bowie on the Live Aid performance in 1985.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/Tin Machine]] – additional guitar, keyboards (1988–1989)
