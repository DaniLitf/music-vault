# Kevin Rutmanis

**Bassist. American.**

Kevin Rutmanis is an American bassist who played with [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] from the late 1990s through 2005. Before joining Melvins he was associated with the Cows, and he later appeared again on Melvins-related releases.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] – bass (1998–2005)
