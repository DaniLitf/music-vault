# Karl Cochran

**Guitarist, bassist and vocalist. Died 2025.**

Karl Cochran was an American rock guitarist and vocalist best known as a founding member of ESP (Eric Singer Project), contributing guitar, bass and vocals to their debut album *Lost and Spaced* (1998). He also performed with Ace Frehley, Joe Lynn Turner, Voodooland, and co-wrote the theme song for the Disney XD series *I'm in the Band*.
