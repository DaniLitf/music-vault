# King ov Hell

**Bassist. Norwegian.**

King ov Hell (Tom Cato Visnes) is a Norwegian bassist best known for his work with black metal band Gorgoroth from 1999 to 2007. Following a legal dispute over the Gorgoroth name, he and bandmate Gaahl formed God Seed in 2009; when Gaahl departed later that year, King reworked the unfinished material into the band Ov Hell with vocalist Shagrath, before Gaahl rejoined God Seed in 2012 to release *I Begin*.
