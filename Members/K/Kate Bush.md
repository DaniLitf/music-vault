# Kate Bush

**Vocals, piano, songwriting, production. British. Solo artist, active 1978–present.**

Kate Bush began writing songs at age 11 and was signed to EMI as a teenager after David Gilmour of Pink Floyd financed her demo recordings. Her 1978 debut single "Wuthering Heights" made her the first female artist to top the UK Singles Chart with a self-written song. She has produced all her own albums since 1982 and is known for her theatrical performances, eclectic art-pop style, and long creative hiatuses between albums, including a twelve-year gap between *The Red Shoes* (1993) and *Aerial* (2005).
