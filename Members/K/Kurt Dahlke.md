# Kurt Dahlke

**Electronic musician, producer. Born 1958 in Germany.**

Kurt Dahlke, also known as Pyrolator, is a German electronic musician who was an original member of DAF, playing electronic instruments on their debut album. He left the band in 1979 and co-founded the influential German electronic act Der Plan. He also played with Fehlfarben and A Certain Frank.
