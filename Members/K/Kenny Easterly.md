# Kenny Easterly

**Drummer. American.**

Kenny Easterly is an American drummer and a founding member of the Cleveland thrash/power metal band Purgatory, formed in 1984 alongside Jeff Hatrix and Mark Alexander. He played on all of the band's early releases including *Purgatory* (1985) and *Tied to the Trax* (1986), and has participated in every reunion since. Outside of Purgatory, he has played in Mystik, Nightcrawler, Zyklóne, and Victor Fist.
