# Kevin Figueiredo

**Drums. American. Former member of [[Artists/S/Satellite Party]].**

Kevin Figueiredo drummed for Satellite Party from 2005 until 2007, when he departed alongside guitarist Nuno Bettencourt to rejoin the reunited hard rock band [[Artists/E/Extreme]], where he has drummed ever since.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/S/Satellite Party]] – drums (2005–2007)
- [[Artists/E/Extreme]] – drums, percussion (2007–present)
