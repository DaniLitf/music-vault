# Keith Flint

**Keith Flint was a British dancer, vocalist, and visual icon of The Prodigy, who began as a dancer before becoming a vocalist, most famously on the 1997 hit "Firestarter."**

Flint became one of the most recognizable faces of the 1990s electronic music scene through his extreme hair and makeup style. He died on March 4, 2019, aged 49.
