# Ken Jordan

**Electronic musician, producer. American.**

Ken Jordan is an American electronic musician who co-founded [[Artists/T/The Crystal Method]] with Scott Kirkland in 1992. He was the duo's half until his departure in 2017, contributing to their first four albums.
