# Klas Åhlund

**Klas Åhlund is a Swedish guitarist, songwriter, and producer, a co-founder of Teddybears, and an early member of Caesars, also widely known for co-writing and producing many of Robyn's biggest hits.**

He co-wrote "With Every Heartbeat" and "Dancing on My Own" for Robyn, and has worked as a producer for numerous Swedish artists. He is the brother of fellow Teddybears member Joakim Åhlund.
