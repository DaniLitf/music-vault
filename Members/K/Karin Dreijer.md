# Karin Dreijer

**Karin Dreijer is a Swedish musician and producer, one half of the electronic duo The Knife with her brother Olof Dreijer, and the sole creator of the Fever Ray solo project, known for her unconventional electronic productions and gender-fluid artistic persona.**
