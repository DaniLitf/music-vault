# KK Downing

**Guitarist. Born October 27, 1951.**

KK Downing is a British guitarist and co-founder of Judas Priest, having played with the band from its formation in 1969 until his departure in 2011. After leaving Judas Priest, he formed KK's Priest and has continued to perform and record music in a similar heavy metal style.
