# Kimmo Luttinen

**Drummer, guitarist. Finnish.**

Kimmo Luttinen, also known as "Sir," was a founding member of Impaled Nazarene, playing drums from 1990 to 1995 and guitar from 1992 to 1995. He is the brother of vocalist Mika Luttinen and has also played in The 69 Eyes and numerous other Finnish metal bands throughout his career.
