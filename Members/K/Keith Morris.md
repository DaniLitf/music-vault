# Keith Morris

**Vocals. Born 1955-09-18.**

Keith Morris is an American singer who co-founded [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] with childhood friend Greg Ginn in 1976, fronting the band's earliest lineup and debut EP before leaving in 1979, burned out from constant rehearsing and struggling with addiction. He went on to found Circle Jerks the same year and later fronted Off!, remaining one of hardcore punk's most influential vocalists across five decades.
