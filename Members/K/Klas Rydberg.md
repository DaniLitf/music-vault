# Klas Rydberg

**Vocals. Swedish.**

Klas Rydberg was the founding vocalist of [[Artists/C/Cult of Luna]], singing on the band's early releases before departing; he has cited Radiohead as a key influence on the band's sound.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/C/Cult of Luna]] – vocals (1998–2003 approx., founding member)
