# Kevin Patricks

**Drummer. American.**

Kevin "Blondie" Patricks was the drummer of Mazarati, an American R&B and funk band from Minneapolis active from 1986 to 1989. He played drums and percussion on the band's self-titled debut album on Paisley Park Records and contributed to the rhythm section that drove the group's funk-rock sound. Mazarati's association with producer Brown Mark and Prince led to touring alongside artists including Sheila E., New Edition, and LL Cool J, with Patricks anchoring the band's live performances.
