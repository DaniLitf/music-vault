# Keith Moon

**Keith Moon was a British drummer and co-founder of The Who, widely regarded as one of the greatest and most original rock drummers in history, known for his explosive, unpredictable style and equally chaotic personal life.**

Moon's approach to drums — treating them as a lead instrument rather than keeping time — was unlike anything in rock music at the time. He died of an accidental drug overdose on September 7, 1978, aged 32.
