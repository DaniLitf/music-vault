# Kyle Weeks

**Vocalist, sampler. Born in USA.**

Kyle Weeks is an American musician and a childhood friend of the Borland brothers. He was a member of the comedic metal project [[Artists/B/Big Dumb Face]] during its original run, contributing vocals and sampling.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/B/Big Dumb Face]] – vocals, sampling (1998–2004)
