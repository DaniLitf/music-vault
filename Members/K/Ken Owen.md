# Ken Owen

**Ken Owen is a British drummer and co-founder of Carcass who played on all their classic albums from 1985 to 1995. He suffered a near-fatal brain haemorrhage in 1999 and has been unable to perform since.**

He was the rhythmic backbone of Carcass through their entire original run, from the goregrind debut through to *Heartwork*.
