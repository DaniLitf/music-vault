# Kirk Johnson

**Percussionist, drummer, dancer. Born in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

Kirk Johnson was a percussionist and dancer in Prince's New Power Generation from its formation. He remained with Prince longer than most members, contributing to albums from *Diamonds and Pearls* through *Newpower Soul* and beyond. He also served as a programmer and producer on later NPG recordings.
