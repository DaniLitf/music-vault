# Kevin Whitworth

**Guitarist. American.**

Kevin Whitworth is an American guitarist, formerly of Crisis Party, who co-founded psychedelic grunge band Love Battery in Seattle in 1989 with vocalist Ron Nine. Alongside Nine, he has been one of the band's only two consistent members throughout its history, praised by AllMusic for "inspired flights of fancy, including some of the best slide guitar work in alternative rock."
