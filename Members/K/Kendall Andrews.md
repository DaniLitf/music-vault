# Kendall Andrews

**Lead guitarist. American.**

Kendall "Ken" Andrews is an American guitarist known as the lead guitarist of Obituary since 2012. He replaced Allen West and has performed on the albums *Inked in Blood* (2014), *Obituary* (2017), and *Dying of Everything* (2023), as well as on worldwide tours.
