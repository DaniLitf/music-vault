# Keith Sterling

**Keyboardist. Born January 1952.**

Keith Sterling is a Jamaican session keyboardist from a musical family who has played in numerous backing bands including The Upsetters, The Aggrovators, Soul Syndicate, and the Boris Gardiner Happening. He later became a member of Sly and Robbie's Taxi Gang and Word, Sound and Power, playing on every Peter Tosh album from *Bush Doctor* onward, and currently performs with the Wailers Band.
