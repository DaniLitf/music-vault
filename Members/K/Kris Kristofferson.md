# Kris Kristofferson

**Kris Kristofferson (born Kristoffer Kristofferson, June 22, 1936 – September 28, 2024) was an American singer-songwriter and actor, one of the most celebrated songwriters in country music history, known for writing "Me and Bobby McGee," "Help Me Make It Through the Night," and "Sunday Mornin' Comin' Down."**

Kristofferson served in the U.S. Army before becoming a janitor at Columbia Recording Studios in Nashville where he got his songs to Johnny Cash and other artists. He became a major songwriter before a parallel acting career in films like *Pat Garrett and Billy the Kid* and the *Blade* trilogy. He was a member of the country supergroup the Highwaymen alongside Willie Nelson, Waylon Jennings, and Johnny Cash.
