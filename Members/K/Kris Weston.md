# Kris Weston

**Producer, engineer. Born 1971-01-25.**

Kris Weston, also known as Thrash, is a British producer and engineer who was a key early member of [[Artists/T/The Orb]]. He helped shape *The Orb's Adventures Beyond the Ultraworld* and *U.F.Orb* before leaving the group in 1994.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Orb]] – production, engineering as Thrash (1991–1994)
