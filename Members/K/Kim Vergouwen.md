**Vocalist. Dutch.**

Kim Vergouwen is a Dutch singer who performed as the female vocalist for [[Artists/0-9/2 Unlimited]] during their 1998 revival line-up, replacing original vocalist [[Members/A/Anita Doth]]. Alongside fellow new vocalist [[Members/R/Romy van Ooijen]], she appeared on the album *II* and the singles "Wanna Get Up" and "Edge of Heaven". Little else is publicly documented about her career outside this period.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/0-9/2 Unlimited]] – vocals (1998)
