# Kimmo Korhonen

**Guitarist. Finnish.**

Kimmo Korhonen is the most recent addition to Impaled Nazarene, joining as guitarist in 2025. His addition continues the band's tradition of featuring skilled Finnish guitarists in their extreme metal lineup.
