# Kip Hanrahan

**Percussionist, composer, impresario. Born 1954-12-09.**

Kip Hanrahan is an American percussionist, composer, and record producer, active since the late 1970s. He founded the American Clavé label in 1979 and has led numerous ensemble projects blending Latin jazz, avant-garde jazz, rock, and spoken word. His work as bandleader includes albums such as *Desire Develops an Edge* (1983), *Days and Nights of Blue Luck Inverted* (1987), and *All Roads Are Made of the Flesh* (1995), while his Conjure series sets the poetry of Ishmael Reed to music. He produced three landmark albums for [[Artists/A/Astor Piazzolla]] in the 1980s.
