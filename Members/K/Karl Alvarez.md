# Karl Alvarez

**Bass. Born Salt Lake City, Utah.**

Karl Alvarez is an American bassist and songwriter who joined [[Artists/D/Descendents]] in 1986 from the Salt Lake City band [[Artists/M/Massacre Guys]], together with guitarist [[Members/S/Stephen Egerton]]. He has been the band's bassist ever since, and is also a founding member of [[Artists/A/All]], the parallel band formed with the same rhythm section in 1987. Alvarez created the "Allroy" mascot character for All and wrote Descendents songs including "Feel This" (2016), penned after the death of his mother.

## Associated Artists
