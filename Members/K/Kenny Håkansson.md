# Kenny Håkansson

**Bass. Swedish. Born 1972-11-25.**

Kenny Håkansson is a Swedish bassist, a founding member of [[Artists/T/The Hellacopters]] and a childhood friend of Nicke Andersson. He was the band's bassist from its 1994 formation until his 2016–2017 departure, including the band's original run and 20th-anniversary reunion shows, and was replaced by Sami Yaffa.
