# Kim Gordon

**Bassist, vocalist, guitarist. Born 1953-04-28 in Rochester, New York, USA.**

Kim Gordon is a co-founder and bassist/vocalist of Sonic Youth, one of the most influential women in rock history. Her bass playing and vocals gave Sonic Youth a distinct feminine presence within the male-dominated alternative rock scene. She has also pursued a visual art career and released solo albums, including *No Home Record* (2019). She and Thurston Moore separated in 2011, which led to Sonic Youth's disbandment.
