# Kristofer Steen

**Guitarist. Born 1974-01-24 in Umeå, Sweden.**

Kristofer Steen is a co-founding guitarist of Refused, contributing to the band from their formation through their dissolution and subsequent reunion. His guitar work contributed to the aggressive, experimental sound of *The Shape of Punk to Come*.
