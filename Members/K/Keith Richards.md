# Keith Richards

**Keith Richards is a British guitarist, songwriter, and founding member of The Rolling Stones, widely regarded as one of the most influential rhythm guitarists in rock history.**

Richards' open-G tuning approach and riff-based guitar style defined the Stones' sound, and his songwriting partnership with Mick Jagger ("the Glimmer Twins") produced hits spanning six decades. He has also released solo albums.
