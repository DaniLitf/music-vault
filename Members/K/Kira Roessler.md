# Kira Roessler

**Bass. Born 1961-06-12.**

Kira Roessler is an American bassist, sister of 45 Grave keyboardist Paul Roessler, who joined [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] in 1983 and played on the band's most prolific stretch of releases — *Family Man*, *Slip It In*, *Loose Nut*, and *In My Head* — before being fired in September 1985. She has since performed in the duo Dos with Minutemen bassist Mike Watt, and built an award-winning career as a dialogue editor, working on projects including *Mad Max: Fury Road* and *Game of Thrones*.
