# 50 Cent

**Rapper, songwriter, actor, record executive. Born 1975-07-06.**

Curtis James Jackson III, known professionally as 50 Cent, is an American rapper and entrepreneur from Queens, New York City. He rose to fame with his debut album *Get Rich or Die Tryin'* (2003), which became one of the best-selling hip-hop albums of all time. Jackson survived a shooting in 2000 where he was struck by nine bullets, an event that shaped his public persona. Beyond music, he built a business empire including G-Unit Records, television production (the *Power* franchise), books, and various investments.
