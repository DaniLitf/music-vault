# Nico

**Vocals. October 16, 1938 – July 18, 1988.**

Nico was a German singer, model, and actress, best known for her haunting vocals on [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]]'s debut album and for her distinctive solo work.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]]

## Biography
Born Christa Päffgen, Nico was introduced to The Velvet Underground by Andy Warhol, who insisted she be featured on their debut album *The Velvet Underground & Nico*. Her deep, accented vocals became iconic on songs like "All Tomorrow's Parties" and "Femme Fatale". After leaving the band, she pursued a solo career with albums produced by John Cale, including *Chelsea Girl*, *The Marble Index*, and *Desertshore*.
