# Nikki Sixx

**Bass. Born December 11, 1958.**

Nikki Sixx is an American bassist and songwriter, best known as the co-founder and primary lyricist of [[Artists/M/Mötley Crüe]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/M/Mötley Crüe]] – bass, co-founder (1981–2015, 2018–present)

## Biography

Sixx co-founded Mötley Crüe in Los Angeles in 1981 and served as the band's primary lyricist and conceptual force throughout their career. He nearly died from a heroin overdose in 1987, an experience that inspired the song "Kickstart My Heart." He has also led the side project Sixx:A.M. and authored the memoir *The Heroin Diaries* (2007). The band reunited in 2018 after a self-imposed retirement.
