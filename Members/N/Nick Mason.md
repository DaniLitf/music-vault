# Nick Mason

**Drummer. Born 1944-01-27 in Birmingham, England.**

Nick Mason is the only member to appear on every Pink Floyd album, serving as the band's drummer from their formation in 1965 through their final release *The Endless River* (2014). While not the most technically flashy drummer, his creative use of space, texture, and unconventional percussion was central to Pink Floyd's experimental sound. He has periodically collaborated with other artists, most notably in Nick Mason's Saucerful of Secrets, which performs early Floyd material live.
