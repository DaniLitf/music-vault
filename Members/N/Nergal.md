# Nergal

**Nergal (born Adam Darski) is a Polish musician and the founder, guitarist, and vocalist of Behemoth, who transformed the band from raw black metal to blackened death metal and became one of the most outspoken and controversial figures in extreme metal.**

He survived leukemia in 2010–2011 and has since been a vocal defender of artistic freedom against religious censorship in Poland.
