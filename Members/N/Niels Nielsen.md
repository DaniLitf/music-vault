# Niels Nielsen

**Keyboards, live (2017–2023, 2024–present).**

Niels Nielsen is a Swedish keyboardist and live musician best known as the touring keyboardist for [[Artists/I/In Flames]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/In Flames]] – keyboards, live (2017–2023, 2024–present)

## Biography

Nielsen has served as In Flames' live keyboardist since 2017, providing atmospheric and electronic elements to the band's live performances. He took a brief hiatus in 2023 before returning in 2024.
