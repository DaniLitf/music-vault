# Niklas Fors

**Bassist, vocalist. Swedish.**

Niklas Fors is a Swedish bassist and vocalist for Alarma Man, an instrumental post-punk/math-rock band from Gothenburg, appearing on the band's 2005 self-titled debut album and 2010's *Love Forever*, on which he also contributed choir vocals. Per Discogs, Alarma Man is his only documented recording credit.
