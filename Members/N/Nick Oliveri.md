# Nick Oliveri

**Bassist, vocalist. Born 1971-10-21 in Palm Desert, California, USA.**

Nick Oliveri was a bassist and co-founding member of Queens of the Stone Age, having played with Josh Homme since their days in Kyuss. His frenetic, aggressive stage presence and raw vocals appeared throughout QOTSA's early albums, most prominently on *Rated R* (2000) and *Songs for the Deaf* (2002). He was fired in 2004 following personal issues. He subsequently played with numerous acts including Mondo Generator and has remained active in the desert rock scene.
