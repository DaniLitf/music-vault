# Nina Gerhard

**Female vocalist. German.**

Nina Gerhard is a German singer best known as the female vocalist for the Eurodance project Captain Hollywood Project from 1992 to 1995. Her vocals appeared on the project's biggest hits including "More and More," "Only with You," and "All I Want," and on the albums *Love Is Not Sex* (1993) and *Animals or Humans* (1994). She was the most prominent female voice of the project during its peak commercial success.
