# Nat Wright

**Bassist. American.**

Nat Wright is a punk rock bassist from Chicago who is known from the Chicago punk band [[Artists/S/Shot Baker]]. He joined [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]] as its bass player in 2013 and has remained in the lineup since, playing on *Thank You for Being a Friend* (2017) and subsequent recordings and tours.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]] – bass guitar (2013–present)
- [[Artists/S/Shot Baker]] – bass guitar
