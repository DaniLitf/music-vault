# Niclas Engelin

**Guitars (1997–1998, 2011–2023).**

Niclas Engelin is a Swedish guitarist best known as a former member of [[Artists/I/In Flames]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/In Flames]] – guitars (1997–1998, 2011–2023)
- [[Artists/T/The Halo Effect]] – guitarist, founding member

## Biography

Engelin first played guitars on In Flames' *Colony* (1999) as a session musician before briefly joining full-time. He officially returned to In Flames in 2011, replacing Jesper Strömblad, and appeared on *Sounds of a Playground Fading* (2011) through *Foregone* (2023). He left in 2023 and co-founded The Halo Effect, a melodic death metal supergroup featuring three other former In Flames members.
