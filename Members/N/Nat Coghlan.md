# Nat Coghlan

**Bassist. American.**

Nat Coghlan was an early bassist for grindcore band Trap Them, playing on the band's live lineup from 2006 to 2007 alongside drummer Scott DeFusco during the period surrounding their debut album *Sleepwell Deconstructor*.
