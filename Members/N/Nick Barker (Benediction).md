# Nick Barker (Benediction)

**Drummer. Born ?.**

Nick Barker is a British extreme metal drummer best known for his work with Cradle of Filth, whom he played with from 1992 to 1996. He also served as a drummer for Benediction, filling the position between Ian Treacy and Perra Karlsson. Barker has been a prolific session and touring drummer across the death metal and black metal scenes.
