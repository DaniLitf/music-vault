# Nick Jonas

**Singer, songwriter, musician, actor. Born September 16, 1992 in Dallas, Texas.**

Nick Jonas is an American singer, songwriter, musician and actor who rose to fame as a member of the Jonas Brothers alongside his brothers Joe and Kevin. In 2009 he formed the side project Nick Jonas and the Administration, recruiting musicians from Prince's New Power Generation to explore a more soulful, blues-rock sound. He has also maintained a successful solo career with albums including *Nick Jonas* (2014), *Last Year Was Complicated* (2016), and *Spaceman* (2021).
