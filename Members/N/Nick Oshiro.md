# Nick Oshiro

**Drums. American. Former member of [[Artists/S/Seether]].**

Nick Oshiro, formerly of Static-X, briefly served as Seether's full-time drummer in 2002 after the band used session drummers on *Disclaimer*, but left before recording any major releases with them.
