# Nahuel Gauna

**Drummer. Florida.**

Nahuel Gauna is a drummer who played in the Florida bands Milkshed and To the Bitter End before joining [[Artists/B/Bird of Ill Omen]] in August 1998. He drummed on the band's final EP *When Love Would've Shown Interest in Us Both*, recorded at Studio 13 in November 1998, and returned for the band's 2012 reunion show in Miami.
