# Nina Gordon

**Nina Gordon is an American guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter, born on November 14, 1967 in Chicago, Illinois, co-founder of alternative rock band Veruca Salt and a solo artist.**

Gordon co-founded Veruca Salt in 1992 with Louise Post and co-wrote many of the band's best-known songs, including "Seether." She departed in 1998 amid well-publicised tensions with Post and released two solo albums before reuniting with Veruca Salt in 2013. Her debut solo album *Tonight and the Rest of My Life* (2000) received strong reviews.
