# Neil Taylor

**British keyboardist and programmer, former member of Mesh.**

Neil Taylor was a founding member of Mesh, joining shortly after the band's formation in 1991 when his former bandmate Richard Silverthorn recruited him. He contributed keyboards, programming, and occasional songwriting until leaving the band in September 2006 to pursue other interests. He introduced influences from the KLF and dance music into Mesh's sound.
