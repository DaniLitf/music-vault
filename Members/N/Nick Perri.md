# Nick Perri

**Guitar. American. Touring member of [[Artists/S/Satellite Party]].**

Nick Perri, guitarist of the rock band Silvertide, joined Satellite Party in 2007 to fill in for the departed Nuno Bettencourt for the remainder of that year's tour.
