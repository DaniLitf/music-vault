# Neil Hutton

**Drummer. British.**

Neil "Steel Neil" Hutton was the long-time drummer of Benediction from 1994 to 2007, playing on albums from *The Dreams You Dread* through *Killing Music*. He was previously a member of Warlord and later joined the hardcore band Stampin' Ground. His tenure with Benediction is the second-longest of any drummer in the band's history.
