**Drummer and percussionist. Son of Phil Collins; active 2021–2022.**

Nic Collins is the son of Phil Collins and served as Genesis's drummer during their final reunion tour, The Last Domino? Tour (2021–2022). Due to his father's health issues, Nic stepped in to handle drumming duties while Phil Collins remained as vocalist during the band's farewell tour, allowing the legendary Genesis frontman to continue performing despite physical limitations. This final reunion marked the last performances of Genesis before the band officially ended.
