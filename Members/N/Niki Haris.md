# Niki Haris

**Singer, dancer. Born April 17, 1962.**

Niki Haris (born Gina Nichole Haire) is an American singer and dancer, best known as one of Madonna's backing vocalists from 1987 to 2001. She also served as guest lead vocalist on several Snap! singles in the early 1990s, including "Do You See the Light". She is the daughter of jazz musician Gene Harris.
