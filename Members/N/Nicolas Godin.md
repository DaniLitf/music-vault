# Nicolas Godin

**Musician, producer. Born 25 December 1969.**

Nicolas Godin is a French musician best known as one half of the electronic duo [[Artists/A/Air]]. He studied architecture in Versailles, where he met [[Members/J/Jean-Benoît Dunckel]]; the pair played together in the band Orange before founding Air in 1995 and going on to co-produce all of the duo's albums. Godin has also released solo albums, including *Contrepoint* (2015), inspired by J.S. Bach, and *Concrete and Glass* (2020).
