# Nagash

**Norwegian black metal and gothic metal musician. Born 28 January 1976.**

Nagash (born Stian Arnesen) is a Norwegian black metal and gothic metal musician, best known as the bassist and vocalist of The Kovenant (formerly Covenant), and for his work with Dimmu Borgir and Lacuna Coil. He has been a prominent figure in the Norwegian black metal scene since the mid-1990s.
