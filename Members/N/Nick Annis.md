# Nick Annis

**Guitarist, backing vocalist.**

Nick Annis is an American guitarist who played guitar and sang backing vocals in [[Artists/B/Black Light Burns]] from 2007 until the band's dissolution in 2016. He previously played in [[Artists/O/Open Hand]] and [[Artists/T/Turn of the Screw]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/B/Black Light Burns]] – guitars, backing vocals (2007–2016)
- [[Artists/O/Open Hand]] – guitar (before 2007)
- [[Artists/T/Turn of the Screw]] – guitar (before 2007)
