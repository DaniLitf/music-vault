# Nils Andersson

**Vocals. Swedish.**

Nils "Nillen" Andersson was a Swedish vocalist who was the original singer for Anti Cimex from 1981 to 1982. He performed on the band's debut EP *Anarkist Attack* before being replaced by Tomas Jonsson. He also played in the band DNA.
