# Nancy Coolen

**Singer and television presenter. Born 1973-09-10.**

Nancy Anna Francina Coolen, known as Nance, is a Dutch singer and television presenter. She was discovered at age 15 by producer Ruud van Rijen and became the lead vocalist of Twenty 4 Seven, performing on hits such as "I Can't Stand It" and "Slave to the Music". After leaving the group in 1996 she pursued a solo career and worked as a television presenter for Dutch networks including TMF and TROS, most notably hosting the game show Lingo.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/Twenty 4 Seven]] – vocals (1989–1996, 2021–present)

## Biography

Nancy Coolen was born on 10 September 1973 in Asten, North Brabant, Netherlands. At age 15 she was discovered in a club by producer Ruud van Rijen, who formed Twenty 4 Seven around her and rapper Captain Hollywood. She performed on all of the group's early hits. After leaving the group in 1996, she launched a solo career with singles including "Love Is..." and "Big Brother is Watching You", and became a television presenter.
