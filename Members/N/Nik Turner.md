# Nik Turner

**Saxophonist, vocalist. British.**

Nik Turner was a founding member of [[Artists/H/Hawkwind]], playing saxophone and flute and contributing vocals through the band's classic early-1970s space rock era, departing in 1976. He continued to perform Hawkwind's early material under various names for decades after. He died in 2022.
