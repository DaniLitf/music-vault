# Neal Smith

**Drummer and founding member of the Alice Cooper band. Born 1947-09-23.**

Neal Smith is an American musician best known as the drummer for the original Alice Cooper band. He joined the band in 1967 while they were still called Nazz, replacing original drummer John Speer. Smith's powerful, inventive drumming drove the band's hard rock sound on all of their classic albums.

After the Alice Cooper band dissolved in 1975, Smith formed the short-lived spin-off Billion Dollar Babies with Michael Bruce and Dennis Dunaway, releasing *Battle Axe* (1977). He has since reunited with Alice Cooper for numerous shows and contributed to later albums including *Welcome 2 My Nightmare* (2011), *Detroit Stories* (2021), and the 2025 reunion album *The Revenge of Alice Cooper*. He also co-wrote songs with Cooper and Bob Ezrin for the 2011 album.
