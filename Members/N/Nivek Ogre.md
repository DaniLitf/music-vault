# Nivek Ogre

**Vocalist, performance artist. Born 1962-12-05 in Calgary, Alberta, Canada.**

Nivek Ogre (born Kevin Ogilvie) is the co-founding vocalist and theatrical frontman of Skinny Puppy, one of the most distinctive voices in industrial music. His extreme stage performances, incorporating body horror imagery and confrontational theatrics, helped define the industrial genre's live aesthetic. He has also been a core member of Pigface and fronts the solo project ohGr.
