# Nino Keller

**Nino Keller is a Swedish drummer and member of Caesars, who joined the band in 2000 and has played on the majority of their recordings.**

He appeared on the breakthrough album *39 Minutes of Bliss (In an Otherwise Meaningless World)* and has been the band's drummer through their most commercially successful period.
