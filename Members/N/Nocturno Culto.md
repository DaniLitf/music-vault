# Nocturno Culto

**Vocalist, guitarist. Norwegian.**

Ted Skjellum, known as Nocturno Culto, is a Norwegian musician who joined Darkthrone in 1988, becoming one half of the band alongside Fenriz since guitarist Zephyrous departed in 1993. He contributed vocals, guitars, bass, and keyboards across the band's black metal era and beyond, and directed the 2007 documentary film *The Misanthrope* about black metal and Norwegian life.
