# Nicolas Fromageau

**Guitarist, keyboardist, programmer. Co-founder of M83.**

Nicolas Fromageau is a French musician from Antibes who co-founded [[Artists/M/M83]] with Anthony Gonzalez in 1999, having previously played with him in the post-rock group My Violent Wish. He co-wrote and co-produced M83's first two albums, *M83* (2001) and *Dead Cities, Red Seas & Lost Ghosts* (2003), and left the project after the touring for the latter in 2004. In 2009 he formed the band [[Artists/T/Team Ghost]].
