# Nick Menza

**American drummer. Born 23 July 1964, died 21 May 2016.**

Nick Menza was an American drummer best known as the drummer of Megadeth (1989–1998). He performed on several of Megadeth's most celebrated albums including *Rust in Peace* and *Countdown to Extinction*. He died of heart failure on stage in 2016.
