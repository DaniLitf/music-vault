# Nicholaus Arson

**Lead guitar, backing vocals. Swedish. Born 1977-01-26.**

Niklas Almqvist, known onstage as Nicholaus Arson, is a Swedish guitarist and co-founder of garage rock band [[Artists/T/The Hives]] with his brother Per. He has played lead guitar for the band since its 1993 formation and is credited as the band's primary songwriter under the pseudonym "Randy Fitzsimmons," a fictional sixth member the band claims manages and writes for them.
