# Nico Elgstrand

**Bassist, guitarist. Swedish.**

Nico Elgstrand is a Swedish musician best known as a former member of Entombed, where he served as bassist from 2004 to 2010 before switching to guitar from 2010 to 2014. Following the band's trademark dispute, he joined Entombed A.D. alongside L.G. Petrov, Olle Dahlstedt, and Victor Brandt, performing as guitarist until the group's later activity. He has also worked as a producer and engineer on multiple Entombed and Entombed A.D. releases, as well as albums for Grand Magus.
