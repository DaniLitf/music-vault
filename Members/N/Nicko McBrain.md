# Nicko McBrain

**Drums (1982–present; not touring as of 2024). Born June 5, 1952.**

Nicko McBrain is an English drummer best known as the longtime drummer of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – drums (1982–present)

## Biography

McBrain joined Iron Maiden in 1982, replacing Clive Burr, and debuted on *Piece of Mind* (1983). His distinctive drumming style and powerful stage presence made him a fan favourite for over four decades. In December 2024, he announced his retirement from touring due to health issues but remains a studio member of the band.
