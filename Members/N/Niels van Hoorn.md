# Niels van Hoorn

**Saxophonist, horn player. Dutch.**

Niels van Hoorn, also credited as Niels van Hoornblower, is a Dutch horn player associated with [[Artists/T/The Legendary Pink Dots]]. He joined the band's expanded late-1980s lineup and contributed saxophone/horns to their psychedelic experimental sound.
