# Nick Barker

**Drummer. Born 1973.**

Nicholas Howard Barker is a highly prolific English extreme metal drummer known for his work with Cradle of Filth, Dimmu Borgir, Brujeria, and numerous other bands. He played drums for Benediction as a live and session member from 2005 to 2011. Barker is widely regarded as one of the most accomplished drummers in extreme metal, having performed on seminal albums such as *Dusk... and Her Embrace* and *Puritanical Euphoric Misanthropia*.
