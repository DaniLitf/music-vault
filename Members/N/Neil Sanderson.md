# Neil Sanderson

**Drums, backing vocals. Canadian. Founding member of [[Artists/T/Three Days Grace]].**

Neil Sanderson co-founded Three Days Grace (as Groundswell) in Norwood, Ontario in 1992 and has drummed for the band throughout its history, including through its 2013 lineup change and Adam Gontier's 2024 return.
