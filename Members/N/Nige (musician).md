# Nige (musician)

**Bassist. British.**

Nige is a British punk musician who played bass for Chaos UK during the 1983 lineup transition. He replaced Adrian Rice on bass when Chaos moved to vocals for the self-titled debut LP, and was part of the band briefly that year.
