**Vocalist, bassist, and electronic musician. Born 1969.**

Nicholas James Bullen is an English musician best known as a co-founder of [[Artists/N/Napalm Death]], where he performed on the band's earliest recordings before departing in 1986. In 1991 he reunited with former Napalm Death drummer Mick Harris to form [[Artists/S/Scorn]], initially an industrial metal project that evolved into a pioneering dub-influenced electronic act. Bullen remained with Scorn until 1995, contributing vocals, bass, electronics, and guitar across their first four albums.

## Associated Artists
