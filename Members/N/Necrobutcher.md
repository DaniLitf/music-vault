# Necrobutcher

**Norwegian bassist. Born 13 April 1968.**

Jørn Stubberud, known as Necrobutcher, is a Norwegian bassist best known as a founding member and bassist of Mayhem. He left the band in 1991 following Dead's suicide but returned in 1995.
