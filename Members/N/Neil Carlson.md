# Neil Carlson

**Neil Carlson is an American tuba player and electronics musician, co-founder of the experimental trio Drums & Tuba.**

Carlson is the defining instrumental voice of Drums & Tuba, playing tuba as the band's bass instrument while also incorporating electronics and processing into the sound.
