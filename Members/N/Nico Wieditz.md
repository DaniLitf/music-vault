# Nico Wieditz

**Keyboardist. German.**

Nico Wieditz is a German keyboardist who joined And One in 2012, replacing Gio van Oli. He has performed on the band's albums *S.T.O.P.* (2012) and *Magnet* (2014) and continues as a touring member.
