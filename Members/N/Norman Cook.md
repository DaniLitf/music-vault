# Norman Cook

**Norman Cook (born Quentin Leo Cook) is a British musician, DJ, and producer from Brighton, best known as Fatboy Slim, one of the most successful and influential figures in big beat and electronic dance music.**

He first gained recognition as bassist of The Housemartins in the mid-1980s before pivoting to dance music and founding Beats International. His 1998 Fatboy Slim album *You've Come a Long Way, Baby* was a defining moment in big beat culture, and his DJ set at Brighton Beach in 2002 drew an estimated 250,000 people.
