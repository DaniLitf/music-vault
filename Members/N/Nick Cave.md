# Nick Cave

**Nick Cave is an Australian vocalist, songwriter, poet, and screenwriter, best known as the frontman of Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds since 1984 and previously of The Birthday Party, one of the most critically acclaimed songwriters in alternative and post-punk music.**
