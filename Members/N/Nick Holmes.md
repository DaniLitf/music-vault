# Nick Holmes

**Vocalist. Born 1971-01-07 in Halifax, England.**

Nick Holmes is the founding vocalist of Paradise Lost and one of the most distinctive voices in gothic and doom metal. He has fronted the band since their formation in 1988, guiding them through stylistic shifts from doom/death metal on *Lost Paradise* (1990) to gothic rock and electronic phases in the late 1990s and back to their darker roots in the 2010s. Holmes has also collaborated with Bloodbath and maintained a solo career.
