# Nuno Bettencourt

**Guitar. American. Guitarist for Extreme and co-founder of [[Artists/S/Satellite Party]].**

Nuno Bettencourt, best known as the guitarist of the hard rock band Extreme, co-founded Satellite Party with Perry Farrell in 2004 as a collaborative project. He left in July 2007, citing discomfort with the band's direction, and rejoined a reunited Extreme.
