# Daniel Hill

**Bassist. Norwegian.**

Daniel Hill joined Magenta as bassist in 2006, giving the band a stable live rhythm section for the first time in their history and contributing to one of the band's most creative periods, spanning *Art and Accidents* (2009) onward.
