# Dean Fertita

**Guitarist, keyboardist. Born 1973-09-14 in Michigan, USA.**

Dean Fertita is an American multi-instrumentalist best known for his work with Queens of the Stone Age, which he joined in 2007. He has also been a member of The Dead Weather alongside Jack White, and a touring contributor to The Raconteurs. His keyboard and guitar work add layers of texture to QOTSA's later material.
