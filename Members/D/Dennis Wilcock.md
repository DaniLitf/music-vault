# Dennis Wilcock

**Lead vocals (1976–1978).**

Dennis Wilcock is an English singer best known as an early vocalist of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – lead vocals (1976–1978)

## Biography

Wilcock sang in Iron Maiden during their formative club years. He was known for his theatrical stage shows involving makeup, blood, and fire — elements that influenced the band's later visual style. He left in 1978 and was replaced by Paul Di'Anno.
