# Dale Crover

**Guitarist/Vocalist/Drummer. American. Born 1965-10-23.**

Dale Crover is an American musician best known as the longtime drummer of the sludge/stoner metal band Melvins since 1986, establishing himself as one of the most influential drummers in heavy music. He is also known as a guitarist and vocalist for the stoner rock band Altamont (1994–present), where he performs alongside other notable musicians in the alternative metal underground.
