# Dan Knight

**Guitarist. British.**

Dan Knight is a guitarist who filled in for Gorerotted on their Cryptopsy UK tour (2005–2007) while regular guitarist Matt Hoban recovered from a broken ankle. He is otherwise known as the guitarist of Ted Maul.
