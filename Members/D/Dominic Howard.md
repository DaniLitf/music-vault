# Dominic Howard

**Drummer. Born 1977.**

Dominic Howard is the co-founder and drummer of the English rock band Muse, a founding member alongside Matt Bellamy and Chris Wolstenholme since 1994. Beyond his powerful, precise drumming style, Howard also contributed significantly to the band's visual aesthetics and performance staging, helping shape Muse's theatrical live presence. His technical drumming has been integral to the band's evolution across multiple musical styles, from alternative rock through progressive and electronic-influenced phases across their extensive discography.
