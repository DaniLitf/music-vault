# Derri Daugherty

**Guitarist and vocalist. American.**

Derri Daugherty is an American musician best known as the guitarist and vocalist for the Lost Dogs, a Christian alternative rock supergroup formed in 1992. He was previously a member of the band The Choir, contributing to their distinctive ambient rock sound throughout the 1980s and 1990s.
