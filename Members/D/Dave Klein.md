# Dave Klein

**Bass.**

Dave Klein is an American bassist known for playing in Screeching Weasel from 2011 to 2013. He joined [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] as touring bassist in 2013 and left the band in 2014.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] – bass (2013–2014)
