# Denny Carmassi

**Drums. Born 25 December 1947.**

Denny Carmassi is an American hard rock drummer from San Francisco, best known for his work with Montrose, Gamma and Heart. He played drums on the [[Artists/C/Coverdale-Page]] album (1993) and its Japan tour, and subsequently joined [[Artists/W/Whitesnake]] for the mid-1990s.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/C/Coverdale-Page]] – drums (1991–1993)
- [[Artists/W/Whitesnake]] – drums (1994–1997)
