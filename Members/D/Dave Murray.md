# Dave Murray

**Guitars (1976–1977, 1978–present). Born December 23, 1956.**

Dave Murray is an English guitarist best known as the longest-serving guitarist of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – guitars (1976–1977, 1978–present)
- [[Urchin]] – former guitarist (pre-Maiden)
- [[The Electric Chairs]] – former guitarist

## Biography

Murray joined Iron Maiden briefly in 1976 before rejoining permanently in 1978. Alongside Steve Harris, he is one of only two members to appear on every Iron Maiden album. Known for his melodic, neoclassical soloing style, he is considered one of heavy metal's most influential guitarists.
