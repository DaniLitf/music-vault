**Rapper, songwriter. Swedish. Born July 24, 1975.**

Douglas "Dogge" Doggelito is a Swedish rapper and founding member of The Latin Kings, one of Sweden's most successful hip-hop groups. Alongside his DJ brothers Chepe and Salla, he co-founded the group in 1991 and has been a central figure in Swedish rap culture. Beyond his work with The Latin Kings, Dogge has pursued a solo career, authored books on slang and hip-hop culture, and worked in acting and media.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Latin Kings]] – Rapper, vocalist (1991–2005, 2019–present)

## Biography

Dogge started rapping in 1983 and co-founded The Latin Kings in 1991 with his cousins/brothers Chepe and Salla. The group's debut album "Välkommen till förorten" (1994) won Grammy Awards and established The Latin Kings as pioneers in Swedish hip-hop. Following the group's initial breakup in 2005, Dogge pursued solo projects and expanded his work into education, authoring "Förortsslang" with linguist Ulla-Britt Kotsinas. The Latin Kings reunited in 2019 and continue performing. Beyond music, Dogge has worked in television, film, and commercial production.
