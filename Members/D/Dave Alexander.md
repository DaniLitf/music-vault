**Bassist and composer. American. June 3, 1947 – February 10, 1975.**

David Michael Alexander was the original bassist for The Stooges, a proto-punk and garage rock pioneer. Although a novice when he joined in 1967, Alexander quickly became instrumental in arranging and composing the band's early material, contributing to the arrangements and compositions of legendary tracks like "We Will Fall," "Little Doll," "Dirt," and "1970." He was fired from The Stooges in 1970 after appearing intoxicated at the Goose Lake International Music Festival, but his early contributions remain foundational to proto-punk history. He was posthumously inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2010 as a member of The Stooges.
