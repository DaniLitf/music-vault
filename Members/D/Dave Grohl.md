# Dave Grohl

**Drummer, guitarist, vocalist. Born 1969-01-14 in Warren, Ohio, USA.**

Dave Grohl is one of the most versatile and celebrated musicians in rock history. He rose to fame as the drummer of Nirvana, appearing on *Nevermind* (1991) and *In Utero* (1993). After Kurt Cobain's death he founded and fronted Foo Fighters, playing nearly all instruments on their debut. He joined Queens of the Stone Age as drummer for *Songs for the Deaf* (2002) and returned for *...Like Clockwork* (2013). He also co-founded Them Crooked Vultures with Josh Homme and Led Zeppelin's John Paul Jones in 2009.
