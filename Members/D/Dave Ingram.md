# Dave Ingram

**Vocalist. Born 25 January 1969.**

Dave Ingram is a British death metal vocalist best known for his work with Benediction and Bolt Thrower. He first joined Benediction in 1990 after original vocalist Mark Greenway departed, appearing on classic albums such as *Transcend the Rubicon* and *Grind Bastard*. He left in 1998 to join Bolt Thrower, performing on *Honour Valour Pride*, before returning to Benediction in 2019. Ingram has also been involved in numerous other projects including Down Among the Dead Men, Echelon, and Hail of Bullets.
