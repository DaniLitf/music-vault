# Daniel O'Sullivan

**Composer, multi-instrumentalist. British. Member of [[Artists/U/Ulver]].**

Daniel O'Sullivan, also known for his work with Æthenor, Guapo, and Mothlite, was enlisted into Ulver in 2009 as the band's fourth core member, expanding it from a trio to a quartet.
