# Dan Ford

**Drummer. British.**

Dan Ford is an English drummer who served as a touring musician for deathgrind band Gorerotted in 1997, leaving to focus on forming SikTh. He remained SikTh's drummer until 2008, later playing in Perceval Gage and Haarp.
