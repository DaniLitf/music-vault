# David Isberg

**Vocalist.**

David Isberg was the original vocalist of Opeth, co-founding the band in 1989 before departing in 1992. He is credited with recruiting Mikael Åkerfeldt into Opeth and then handing over the frontman role to him. Isberg did not continue in music after leaving the band.
