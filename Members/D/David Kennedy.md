**Guitarist for Angels & Airwaves. Born July 8, 1976.**

David Kennedy is an American guitarist and songwriter best known for his work with Angels & Airwaves, the art-rock project co-founded with Tom DeLonge in 2005. He was an early member of Hazen Street, a supergroup mixing hardcore punk and rap rock that formed in 2004, contributing lead guitar to their self-titled debut album. His guitar work spans punk, rock, and experimental genres across multiple influential bands.
