# Dean Jones

**Vocalist. British.**

Dean Jones is a British vocalist and founding member of Extreme Noise Terror, where he has been the band's primary vocalist since its formation in 1985. He is known for his dual-vocalist partnership with the late Phil Vane and for co-writing most of the band's politically charged lyrics.
