**Keyboardist, mixer, and DJ. British. Born April 30, 1971.**

Darren Emerson is a keyboardist and DJ best known as a core member of Underworld from 1990 to 2000. His addition to the duo of Karl Hyde and Rick Smith completed the band's transformation into a techno and electronic force, providing the rhythmic and textural foundation for albums like Dubnobasswithmyheadman, Second Toughest in the Infants, and Beaucoup Fish. Emerson's production and mixing work was instrumental in Underworld's rise to prominence during the 1990s dance music boom, particularly the success of tracks like "Born Slippy .NUXX" and their work on the Trainspotting soundtrack.
