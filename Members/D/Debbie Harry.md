# Debbie Harry

**Vocals. American. Born 1945-07-01.**

Debbie Harry is an American singer, best known as the frontwoman of new wave band [[Artists/B/Blondie]]. She provided guest vocals on two tracks of [[Artists/T/The Heads]]' only album, *No Talking, Just Head* (1996).

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/B/Blondie]] – lead vocals
- [[Artists/T/The Heads]] – guest vocals (1996)
