# Denny Giardina

**Vocalist. American.**

Denny Giardina was the vocalist for the Cleveland heavy metal band Centurion from 1984 to 1989. He sang on the band's only album, *Cross and Black* (1987), recorded at Clockwerke Sound Studio in Cleveland.
