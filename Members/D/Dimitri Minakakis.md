# Dimitri Minakakis

**Vocals. American. Founding member of [[Artists/T/The Dillinger Escape Plan]].**

Dimitri Minakakis co-founded The Dillinger Escape Plan in 1997 (having previously fronted the earlier band Arcane with Ben Weinman) and sang on the band's early EPs and debut album *Calculating Infinity* (1999), leaving in 2001 citing the rigors of touring. He rejoined the band for its 2023–2024 reunion shows marking the 25th anniversary of *Calculating Infinity*.
