# Del Dettmar

**Synthesizer player. British.**

Del Dettmar was the synthesizer and electronics player for [[Artists/H/Hawkwind]] during its classic early-1970s space rock lineup, contributing to the sound of albums including *Doremi Fasol Latido* (1972) and *Space Ritual* (1973).
