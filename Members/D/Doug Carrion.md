# Doug Carrion

**Bass. American.**

Doug Carrion is an American bassist who replaced Tony Lombardo in [[Artists/D/Descendents]] in 1985, playing on *Enjoy!* (1986) before leaving the following year. He came to the band from the Los Angeles punk band [[Artists/A/Anti]] and later played bass in Washington D.C. melodic hardcore band [[Artists/D/Dag Nasty]], appearing on *Field Day* (1988). He has since worked widely in the punk scene as a musician and producer.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/D/Descendents]] – bass, backing vocals (1985–1986)
- [[Artists/A/Anti]] – bass (early 1980s)
- [[Artists/D/Dag Nasty]] – bass (late 1980s)
