# Dirk Da Davo

**Belgian electronic musician. Born 1965.**

Dirk Da Davo (born Dirk Daenen) is a Belgian electronic musician best known as the vocalist, bassist, and founder of the EBM/industrial band Absolute Body Control. He later formed the electro-industrial project The Neon Judgement with Frank Weyckers.
