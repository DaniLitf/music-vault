# Danny Heifetz

**American drummer. Born 17 April 1964.**

Danny Heifetz, grandson of violinist Jascha Heifetz, joined Mr. Bungle in 1988 on drums after an injury sidelined previous drummer Hans Wagner, and played on all three of the band's studio albums until its 2000 breakup. He later relocated to Melbourne, Australia, where he teaches drums and percussion professionally.
