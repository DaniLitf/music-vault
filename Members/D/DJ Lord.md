**DJ, turntablist. American. Born March 11, 1975.**

Lord Aswod is an American DJ and turntablist best known for replacing Terminator X as the DJ and turntablist for hip-hop legends Public Enemy in 1999. A specialist in hip-hop and drum and bass, he has maintained this role for over 25 years and has also worked with Cypress Hill and other prominent hip-hop acts. He is a multiple DJ battle champion and has released solo material under the name 2MP (2 Much Posse).
