# David Bowie

**David Bowie (born David Jones) was a British rock musician, singer-songwriter, and actor, one of the most critically acclaimed and commercially successful artists of the 20th century, whose constant reinvention across multiple artistic personas made him the archetype of artistic evolution in rock.**

From Ziggy Stardust's glam excess to the Berlin Trilogy's krautrock/ambient experimentation to *Let's Dance*'s mainstream pop, Bowie never remained in one mode. He died of liver cancer two days after releasing *Blackstar*, his 25th studio album, in 2016.
