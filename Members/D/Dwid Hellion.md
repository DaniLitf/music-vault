# Dwid Hellion

**Vocals, keyboards. American.**

Dwid Hellion is an American musician who founded the metalcore band Integrity in 1988 and has remained its only constant member, handling vocals and keyboards. He is the band's primary songwriter and creative force, defining their sound across numerous releases and lineup changes.
