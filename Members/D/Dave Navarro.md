# Dave Navarro

**Dave Navarro** (born June 7, 1967) is an American guitarist best known as a member of [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]] and [[Artists/R/Red Hot Chili Peppers]].

- [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]] – guitar, keyboards, piano (1986–1991, 1997, 2001–2004, 2008–2024)
- [[Artists/R/Red Hot Chili Peppers]] – guitar (1993–1998)
- [[Deconstruction (band)|Deconstruction]] – guitar (1993–1994)
- [[The Panic Channel]] – guitar (2004–2008)

Navarro joined Jane's Addiction in 1986, replacing original guitarist [[Members/C/Chris Brinkman]]. He is known for his melodic, psychedelic-tinged guitar style and was ranked as one of the greatest guitarists of all time by numerous publications.
