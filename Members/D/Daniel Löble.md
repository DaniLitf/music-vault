**Guitarist. German.**

Daniel Löble is a guitarist for the German power metal legends Helloween. He has been a key member of the band, contributing to their continued output and helping to maintain their influential presence in the power metal scene. His work on Helloween's recent albums has helped keep the band's legacy alive for newer generations of metal fans.
