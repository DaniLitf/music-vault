# Dan Briggs

**Bassist. American.**

Dan Briggs is an American bassist best known for progressive metal band Between the Buried and Me and the instrumental jazz-fusion trio Trioscapes. In 2014 he began collaborating with Haken's Richard Henshall, which grew into the progressive jazz-fusion supergroup Nova Collective.
