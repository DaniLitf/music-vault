# Dolly Parton

**Dolly Parton is an American country singer-songwriter, actress, and philanthropist from Locust Ridge, Tennessee, one of the most celebrated country artists of all time, known for "Jolene," "I Will Always Love You," and her decades-long philanthropic work including the Imagination Library.**
