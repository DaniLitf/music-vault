# Dez Dickerson

**Lead guitarist. Born 7 August 1955 in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

Dez Dickerson is an American guitarist best known as the original lead guitarist for Prince's band from 1979 to 1983. He performed on the albums *Dirty Mind*, *Controversy*, and *1999*, contributing to Prince's early breakthrough sound before leaving the band in 1983 to pursue Christian music.
