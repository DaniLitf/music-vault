# Dexter Holland

**Vocalist, guitarist, songwriter. Born 1965-12-29.**

Dexter Holland is an American singer, guitarist, and songwriter best known as the co-founder and frontman of [[Artists/T/The Offspring]]. He appeared as a guest on [[Artists/T/The Dwarves]]' 2004 album *The Dwarves Must Die*, linking the band to the 1990s California punk mainstream.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Offspring]] – vocals, guitar (1984–present)
- [[Artists/T/The Dwarves]] – guest appearance (2004)
