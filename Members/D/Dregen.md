**Guitarist. Swedish. Born June 12, 1973.**

Andreas "Dregen" Svensson is a Swedish guitarist best known for his founding role in the garage rock band Backyard Babies and his work with The Hellacopters. A prolific musician across multiple genres including garage rock, punk rock, glam punk, and hard rock, Dregen has maintained a career spanning four decades with numerous side projects and collaborations. He continues to be an active force in Scandinavian rock music.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/B/Backyard Babies]] – Guitarist, founding member (1987–present; hiatus 2010–2015)
- [[Artists/T/The Hellacopters]] – Guitarist (1994–1999, 2016–present)

## Biography

Dregen was born in Sweden and co-founded Backyard Babies in Nässjö in 1987, establishing himself as a core member of the band's guitar-driven, hard rock sound. In 1994, he met Nicke Andersson and co-founded The Hellacopters, a side project that became hugely influential in Swedish rock and garage rock circles. During the late 1990s, Dregen balanced both bands before The Hellacopters initially disbanded at the end of the decade, allowing him to focus exclusively on Backyard Babies. The Hellacopters reunited in 2016 for their 20th anniversary, and Dregen rejoined both bands' touring and recording activities. Throughout his career, he has pursued numerous side projects and collaborations, releasing a solo debut album "Dregen" in 2013. He remains one of Sweden's most respected rock guitarists and continues to tour and record with both Backyard Babies and The Hellacopters.
