# Dave Mustaine

**Vocals, guitar. Born September 13, 1961.**

Dave Mustaine was [[Artists/M/Metallica]]'s original lead guitarist from 1981 until his acrimonious firing in April 1983 over substance abuse and personal clashes with Hetfield and Ulrich, despite receiving songwriting credits on *Kill 'Em All* and *Ride the Lightning*. He responded by forming [[Artists/M/Megadeth]] later that same year, going on to become one of thrash metal's "Big Four" alongside Metallica, Slayer, and Anthrax.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/M/Metallica]] – lead guitar (1981–1983)
- [[Artists/M/Megadeth]] – vocals, guitar (1983–present, founding member)
