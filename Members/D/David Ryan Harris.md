# David Ryan Harris

**Guitarist, singer, songwriter. American.**

David Ryan Harris is an American guitarist, singer and songwriter who has worked as a session and touring musician across multiple genres. He played lead guitar and provided backing vocals on Nick Jonas and the Administration's debut album *Who I Am* (2010) as a session contributor before being replaced by Sonny Thompson for the band's tour. He has also contributed to recordings by artists including Dido and John Mayer.
