# David Yow

**David Yow is an American vocalist, best known as the singer of [[Artists/T/The Jesus Lizard]].**

Known for his wild stage antics and distinctive vocal style, he contributed vocals to Pigface's early albums, appearing on *Gub* (1991) and subsequent recordings.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/P/Pigface]] – vocals (1990–1994)
- [[Artists/T/The Jesus Lizard]] – vocals (1987–1999)
