# Dennis Sanders

**Bassist, backing vocalist.**

Dennis Sanders is an American bassist who joined [[Artists/B/Black Light Burns]] in 2008 and remained until the band dissolved in 2016. He played bass and sang backing vocals across the band's later touring years and second-album era.
