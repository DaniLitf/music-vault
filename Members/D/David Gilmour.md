# David Gilmour

**Guitarist, vocalist. Born 1946-03-06 in Cambridge, England.**

David Gilmour joined Pink Floyd in 1967 to supplement the increasingly unreliable Syd Barrett, and took over as the band's lead guitarist and co-vocalist after Barrett's departure in 1968. His expressive, blues-influenced guitar playing — renowned for its lyrical tone and economy of notes — is among the most recognized in rock music history. He sang lead vocals on many of the band's most beloved songs and became the de facto band leader following Roger Waters' departure in 1985, steering Pink Floyd through *A Momentary Lapse of Reason* (1987) and *The Division Bell* (1994). He has also maintained a prolific solo career.
