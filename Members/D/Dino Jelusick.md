# Dino Jelusick

**Vocalist, keyboardist. Croatian. Born 1992-06-04.**

Dino Jelusick is a Croatian musician and vocalist known for his work as keyboardist and backing vocalist for Whitesnake since 2021. He is the founder of the band Animal Drive, which was the first Croatian rock band to sign with a major international label (Frontiers Records). Beyond his role in Whitesnake, Jelusick is an accomplished touring vocalist for Trans-Siberian Orchestra and has collaborated with numerous rock projects including Dirty Shirley (with George Lynch) and Whom Gods Destroy (with Derek Sherinian). He is also the 2003 winner of the inaugural Junior Eurovision Song Contest.
