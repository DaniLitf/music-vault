# Dave Hewitt

**Bass guitarist. British. Born 1950-05-04.**

Dave Hewitt is an English musician best known for his long tenure as bassist with the progressive rock band Babe Ruth, which he joined in 1970. He appeared on the band's classic albums and survived a near-fatal car crash on the M1 motorway in 1973. He also played bass with The Georgia Satellites in the early 1980s, before reuniting with Babe Ruth for a reunion in 2002.
