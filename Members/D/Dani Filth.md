# Dani Filth

**Dani Filth (born Daniel Davey) is a British vocalist and the founder of Cradle of Filth, known for his extreme vocal range spanning guttural lows to ear-piercing shrieks, and his theatrical black metal aesthetic drawn from horror literature and cinema.**
