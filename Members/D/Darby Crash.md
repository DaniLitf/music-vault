**Vocalist and punk pioneer. American. September 26, 1958 – December 7, 1980.**

Jan Paul Beahm, known as Darby Crash, was the charismatic and controversial lead vocalist of The Germs, a proto-punk and hardcore band that defined the Los Angeles punk scene in the late 1970s. Despite his brief career—The Germs disbanded in 1980—Crash became an iconic figure in punk history, known for his wild stage presence, provocative lyrics, and embodiment of punk's rebellious ethos. His influence extends far beyond his lifetime; he was immortalized in Penelope Spheeris' documentary The Decline of Western Civilization and later in the 2007 biopic What We Do Is Secret.
