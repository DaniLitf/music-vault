# Diego Tejeida

**Keyboardist. Peruvian.**

Diego Tejeida is a Peruvian keyboardist who joined progressive metal band Haken in 2011, replacing founding member Richard Henshall on keyboards (Henshall continuing on guitar), and has performed on the band's albums since.
