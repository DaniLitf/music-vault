# Dave Krusen

**Drummer. American. Born 1966-03-10.**

Dave Krusen is the original drummer for Pearl Jam, recruited in 1990 and featured on the band's debut album *Ten* (1991). He left the band in May 1991 after completing the album to enter rehabilitation for alcoholism. Despite his brief tenure, he was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame alongside Pearl Jam in 2017 and has since reunited with the band for occasional performances. He has also worked with numerous other bands including Candlebox, Unified Theory, and Hovercraft.
