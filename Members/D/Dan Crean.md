**Drummer.**

Dan Crean is an American drummer best known as a founding member of [[Artists/S/Semi Precious Weapons]], the glam rock band he co-founded in New York City in 2004. After the band's dissolution in 2014, Crean became a touring member of Gwen Stefani's backing band.
