# Dennis Stratton

**Guitars, backing vocals (1979–1980).**

Dennis Stratton is an English guitarist best known as the former guitarist of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – guitars, backing vocals (1979–1980)
- [[Praying Mantis]] – guitarist (NWOBHM)
- [[Lionheart]] – guitarist
- [[Remus Down Boulevard]] – guitarist

## Biography

Stratton played on Iron Maiden's self-titled debut album *Iron Maiden* (1980) and its singles. He was dismissed in 1980 due to musical differences and replaced by Adrian Smith. He continued his career with Praying Mantis and other NWOBHM acts.
