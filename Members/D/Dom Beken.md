# Dom Beken

**Electronic musician, producer. British.**

Dom Beken is a British musician and producer who worked with [[Artists/T/The Orb]] and with Alex Paterson in related projects including Transit Kings and High Frequency Bandwidth. He joined The Orb's orbit in the mid-2000s.
