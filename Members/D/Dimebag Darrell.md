# Dimebag Darrell

**Guitarist. Born 1966-08-20 in Ennis, Texas, USA. Died 2004-12-08.**

Dimebag Darrell Abbott is widely regarded as one of the greatest and most influential heavy metal guitarists of all time. As the lead guitarist of Pantera he helped pioneer the groove metal style with his explosive riffing, innovative use of squealing harmonics, and fluid soloing, evident across *Cowboys from Hell* (1990) through *Reinventing the Steel* (2000). Following Pantera's breakup he co-founded Damageplan with his brother Vinnie Paul. He was tragically shot and killed on stage in Columbus, Ohio, on December 8, 2004.
