# David Sandström

**Drummer. Born 1974-11-13 in Umeå, Sweden.**

David Sandström is the drummer and co-founder of Refused, whose powerhouse playing on *The Shape of Punk to Come* (1998) is considered one of the landmark hardcore drum performances. He has remained with the band through their reunion and subsequent albums.
