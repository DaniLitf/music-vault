# Denny Laine

**Denny Laine was a British guitarist and singer who co-founded Wings with Paul McCartney in 1971 and remained a member throughout the band's entire existence, and was previously the original vocalist of the Moody Blues.**

Laine sang lead on the Moody Blues' debut hit "Go Now" (1964) before departing. He died on December 5, 2023.
