# Darryl Jones

**Bassist. American.**

Darryl Jones is an American bassist celebrated for his work with Miles Davis and as a long-time touring musician for The Rolling Stones. He played bass for The Dead Daisies from 2013 to 2014 and joined them as a touring bassist in 2015.
