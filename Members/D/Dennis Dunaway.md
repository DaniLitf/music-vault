# Dennis Dunaway

**Bassist and founding member of the Alice Cooper band. Born 1946-12-09.**

Dennis Dunaway is an American musician best known as the bassist and co-founder of the original Alice Cooper band. He was a key songwriter, co-writing classics such as "I'm Eighteen," "School's Out," and "Billion Dollar Babies." His melodic, inventive bass lines were crucial to the band's sound throughout their original run from 1968 to 1975.

After the band's breakup, Dunaway formed the short-lived spin-off Billion Dollar Babies with Michael Bruce and Neal Smith, releasing *Battle Axe* (1977). He has since reunited with Alice Cooper for numerous shows and contributed to later albums including *Welcome 2 My Nightmare* (2011), *Detroit Stories* (2021), and the 2025 reunion album *The Revenge of Alice Cooper*. He also authored the autobiography *Snakes! Guillotines! Electric Chairs!*.
