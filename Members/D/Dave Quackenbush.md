# Dave Quackenbush

**Vocalist, guitarist. American.**

Dave Quackenbush is the lead vocalist and guitarist of The Vandals, joining the Orange County punk band in 1985 to replace original vocalist Stevo Jensen. He has been a key creative force in The Vandals' sound, contributing to their transition from hardcore punk to satirical comedy punk and remaining as a core member of the band's stable lineup since 1990 alongside guitarist Warren Fitzgerald, bassist Joe Escalante, and drummer Josh Freese.
