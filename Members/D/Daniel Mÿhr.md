**Guitarist. Swedish.**

Daniel Mÿhr is a guitarist for Sabaton, the Swedish heavy metal band renowned for their historical and military-themed compositions. His guitar work has been integral to Sabaton's signature sound and their live performances, contributing to the band's growing international fanbase and their status as one of modern metal's most distinctive acts.
