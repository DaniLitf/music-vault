# Dan Wleklinski

**Guitarist, vocalist, producer and engineer. American.**

Dan "Mr. Precision" Wleklinski is an American punk rock guitarist from Chicago, co-founder of [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]] in 1993 and the band's constant guitarist across all its eras. After the 1999 breakup he formed [[Artists/R/Rise Against]] with Joe Principe, playing guitar on its debut *The Unraveling* before departing in 2001. Beyond performing, he runs Bombshelter Recording Studio in Chicago, where he has recorded, mixed, and produced numerous projects including 88 Fingers Louie's comeback album *Thank You for Being a Friend* (2017) — see [[Producers/D/Dan Wleklinski]] for his production work.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]] – guitar, vocals (1993–1996, 1998–1999, 2009–2010, 2013–present)
- [[Artists/R/Rise Against]] – guitar (1999–2001)
