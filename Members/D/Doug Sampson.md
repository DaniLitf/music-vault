# Doug Sampson

**Drums (1978–1979).**

Doug Sampson is an English drummer best known as the early drummer of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – drums (1978–1979)
- [[Airforce]] – drummer

## Biography

Sampson played on Iron Maiden's early demos including *The Soundhouse Tapes* (1979) and performed live with the band. He left in 1979 due to the demands of touring and was replaced by Clive Burr.
