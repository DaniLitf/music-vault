# Dave Palmer

**Keyboardist.**

Dave Palmer is an American keyboardist known for his long-running collaboration with Fiona Apple, who also joined [[Artists/A/Air]]'s touring band during the *Talkie Walkie* era in 2004. He has worked as a session and touring keyboardist for a range of pop and rock artists.
