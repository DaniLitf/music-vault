# Dmytro Ternushchak

**Vocalist. Born in Ukraine.**

Dmytro Ternushchak, known on stage as Dietmar Kumarberg, is the founder and vocalist of the Ukrainian blackened death metal band 1914. He previously played in the Ukrainian metalcore band Skinhate as well as Ambivalence, ForceOut, and Johny B Gut. Themed around World War I, his project 1914 has gained international recognition for its critically acclaimed albums on Napalm Records.
