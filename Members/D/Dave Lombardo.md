# Dave Lombardo

**Drummer. Born 1965-02-16 in Havana, Cuba.**

Dave Lombardo is one of the most influential drummers in metal history, co-founding Slayer and appearing on their most acclaimed albums including *Reign in Blood* (1986) and *Seasons in the Abyss* (1990). His double-bass technique and speed set the template for thrash and death metal drumming. He is also the drummer of Fantômas, Mike Patton's avant-garde metal project, and has played with Misfits and Suicidal Tendencies.
