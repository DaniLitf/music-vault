# Danny Carey

**Danny Carey is an American drummer born May 10, 1961, best known as the drummer for [[Artists/T/Tool]].**

Known for his polyrhythmic technique and extensive kit, he has been a member of Tool since 1990 and contributed drums to Pigface's recordings and tours.
