# Dave Brock

**Guitarist, vocalist. British.**

Dave Brock is a British musician and the founder of [[Artists/H/Hawkwind]], and its only continuous member since its 1969 formation, serving as the band's guitarist, vocalist, and de facto leader across its entire space rock career.
