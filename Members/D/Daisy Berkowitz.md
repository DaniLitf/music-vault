# Daisy Berkowitz

**American guitarist. Born 28 April 1968, died 22 October 2017.**

Scott Putesky, known by his stage name Daisy Berkowitz, was an American musician best known as the guitarist and co-founder of Marilyn Manson. He played on the band's first three albums before departing in 1996.
