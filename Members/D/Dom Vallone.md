# Dom Vallone

**Drummer. American.**

Dominic "Dom" Vallone is a punk rock drummer from Chicago and founding member of [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]] who drummed on the band's early 7-inches and the *Totin' 40's & Fuckin' Shit Up* EP before leaving in 1995. The band's 1997 Fat Wreck Chords compilation *The Dom Years* is named after his tenure with the band, reflecting his early contributions to their sound.
