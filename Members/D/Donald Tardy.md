# Donald Tardy

**Drummer. Born 1970 in Tampa, Florida.**

Donald Tardy is best known as the longtime drummer for the death metal band Obituary, which he co-founded with his brother John Tardy and Trevor Peres in 1984. He has played on every Obituary release and is recognized for his powerful, groove-oriented drumming style that helped define the Florida death metal sound. Tardy also participated in the Meathook Seed side project, playing drums on its debut album *Embedded* (1993).
