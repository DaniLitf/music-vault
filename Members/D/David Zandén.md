# David Zandén

**Bass. Born March 28, 1988.**

David Zandén is a Swedish bassist, born in Ödeborg, who has been a member of [[Artists/W/Wintergatan]] since its formation, playing bass, guitar, and typewriter and contributing as a songwriter.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/W/Wintergatan]] – bass, guitar, typewriter (2012–present, founding member)
