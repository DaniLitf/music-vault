# Dave Sullivan

**Guitars (1975–1976).**

Dave Sullivan is an English guitarist best known as one of the original guitarists of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – guitars (1975–1976)

## Biography

Sullivan was one of Iron Maiden's founding guitarists alongside Terry Rance, playing during the band's earliest rehearsals and performances. He left the band in 1976.
