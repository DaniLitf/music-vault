# Denis Boardman

**Vocalist, guitarist, bassist. British.**

Denis Boardman is a British vocalist and multi-instrumentalist who joined Doom in 1995, initially playing guitar and bass before becoming their frontman from 2010 onward. He has been part of the band's current stable lineup.
