# David Lowy

**Businessman and guitarist. Australian.**

David Lowy is an Australian businessman and the founder of The Dead Daisies, having formed the band in 2012 with Jon Stevens. He also founded the band MARS and has served as a rhythm guitarist for The Dead Daisies since its inception.
