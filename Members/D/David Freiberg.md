# David Freiberg

**David Freiberg** (born August 24, 1938) is an American singer and multi-instrumentalist best known for his work with [[Quicksilver Messenger Service]], [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]], and [[Artists/J/Jefferson Starship]].

- [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]] – vocals, rhythm guitar (1972)
- [[Artists/J/Jefferson Starship]] – vocals, bass, guitar, keyboards (1974–1984)
- [[Quicksilver Messenger Service]] – vocals, bass, guitar (1965–1971, 1973–1975, 1986)
