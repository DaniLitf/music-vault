# Dale Griffin

**Drummer. British.**

Dale Griffin, known as "Buffin," was the founding drummer of [[Artists/M/Mott the Hoople]] from 1969 until the band's 1974 split, and later worked as a BBC radio producer, notably on John Peel's sessions. He died in 2016.
