# Darren Partington

**Keyboards, congas, electronic drums. Born November 1, 1969.**

Darren Partington is an English musician, best known as a former member of the electronic group [[Artists/0-9/808 State]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/0-9/808 State]] – keyboards, congas, electronic drums (1989–2015)
- [[Jeep]] – solo recording alias

## Biography

Partington joined 808 State in 1989 alongside Andrew Barker as the Spinmasters. He remained with the band until 2015, when he was sentenced to 18 months in prison for drug dealing, effectively ending his tenure. He has also recorded under the name Jeep.
