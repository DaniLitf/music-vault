# David Bazan

**Singer-songwriter and bassist. Born 1976.**

David Bazan is an American singer-songwriter best known as the founder and frontman of Pedro the Lion, one of the most influential bands in the Christian indie rock scene. After Pedro the Lion disbanded in 2006, he continued as a solo artist and formed Lo Tom with members of Starflyer 59 and Velour 100. His work often explores themes of faith, doubt, and personal transformation.
