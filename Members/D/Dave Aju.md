**Musician. British.**

Dave Aju is a member of Red Snapper, the British instrumental band known for their innovative blend of acid jazz, trip hop, and nu jazz. Founded in 1993, Red Snapper pioneered a distinctive sound that combined acoustic and electronic textures, with Aju contributing to their evolution as one of the most important electronic and acoustic fusion bands of the 1990s.
