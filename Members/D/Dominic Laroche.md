# Dominic Laroche

**Bass. Canadian.**

Dominic "Rocky" Laroche joined [[Artists/V/Voivod]] as bassist in 2014, replacing Jean-Yves "Blacky" Thériault, having previously performed with blues musician Steve Hill; he has recorded on the band's albums since *The Wake* (2018).
