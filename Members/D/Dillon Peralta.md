**Guitarist for Goon.**

Dillon Peralta is an American guitarist currently performing with the Los Angeles-based indie rock band Goon. He contributes to the band's guitar-driven alternative rock sound, complementing Kenny Becker's vocals and primary guitar work. Peralta has been part of Goon's full band lineup since its expansion from Kenny Becker's solo project, and performs on their recent album Dream 3 (2025).
