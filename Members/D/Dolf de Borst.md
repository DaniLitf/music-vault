# Dolf de Borst

**Bass, vocals. New Zealander.**

Dolf de Borst is the founder, bassist, and singer of New Zealand rock band [[Artists/T/The Datsuns]], which he formed in 1998. A longtime friend of [[Artists/T/The Hellacopters]] since touring with them as part of The Datsuns, he became the band's bassist upon its 2018 reformation, and also plays bass in Nicke Andersson's [[Artists/I/Imperial State Electric]].
