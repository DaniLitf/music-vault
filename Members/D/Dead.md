# Dead

**Vocalist. Born 16 January 1969. Died 8 April 1991.**

Per Yngve Ohlin, known as Dead, was a Swedish black metal vocalist who joined Mayhem in 1988 after earlier forming the band Morbid. Known for extreme stage performances and for pioneering the use of corpse paint, he took his own life on 8 April 1991 at the band's shared house in Kråkstad, Norway.
