# Dave Evans

**Singer. Australian. Born 1953-07-20.**

Dave Evans was the original lead vocalist of AC/DC, fronting the band from its formation in November 1973 until October 1974, when he was replaced by Bon Scott. He sang on AC/DC's debut single "Can I Sit Next to You, Girl" in 1974. After leaving AC/DC, Evans continued his music career with various bands and as a solo artist, recording multiple albums and EPs since 2000.
