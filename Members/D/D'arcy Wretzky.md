**Bassist and backing vocalist. American. Born May 1, 1968.**

D'arcy Wretzky was the original bassist of The Smashing Pumpkins and is credited on the band's first six studio albums, including the landmark Mellon Collie and the Infinite Sadness. She left the band in 1999 after contributing vocals and bass to iconic tracks like "Daughter" (which she co-wrote), "1979," and many others. Before and after her tenure with Pumpkins, she has performed with Catherine and Filter, establishing herself as a self-taught musician with a distinctive bass style that grounded the band's alternative rock sound.
