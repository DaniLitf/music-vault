# David Lee Roth

**David Lee Roth is an American singer and the original frontman of Van Halen, known for his flamboyant showmanship, acrobatic stage presence, and the anthemic party rock of the band's classic era.**

After leaving Van Halen in 1985, Roth pursued a solo career with initial success before rejoining the band for reunion periods in the 2000s and 2010s.
