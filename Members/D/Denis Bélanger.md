# Denis Bélanger

**Vocals. Born August 9, 1964.**

Denis "Snake" Bélanger is a Canadian singer and lyricist from Jonquière, Quebec, who co-founded [[Artists/V/Voivod]] in 1982 and has remained its vocalist ever since, known for lyrics exploring science fiction, Cold War politics, and post-apocalyptic themes.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/V/Voivod]] – vocals (1982–present, founding member)
