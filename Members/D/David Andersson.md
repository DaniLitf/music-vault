# David Andersson

**Guitarist. Swedish. Born 1975. Died 2022-09-14.**

David Andersson was a Swedish guitarist and songwriter known for his work with the melodic death metal band Soilwork. He joined Soilwork in June 2012 and remained a core member for ten years, contributing to four studio albums including *The Living Infinite* (2013) and *Verkligheten* (2019). He was praised for his guitar mastery and compositional contributions to the band's sound before his death in September 2022 at age 47.
