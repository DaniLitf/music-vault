# Danny Herrera

**Drummer. American.**

Danny Herrera is an American drummer best known for his work with grindcore pioneers Napalm Death, having joined the band in 2011. He has also been the drummer for extreme metal duo Anaal Nathrakh since 2009, contributing to their aggressive and relentless sound across multiple albums.
