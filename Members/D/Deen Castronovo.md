# Deen Castronovo

**Drummer and vocalist. American.**

Deen Castronovo is an American drummer and vocalist best known for his work with Journey and Bad English. He played drums for The Dead Daisies from 2017 to 2021, appearing on the albums *Burn It Down* and *The Lockdown Sessions*.
