# Don Airey

**Don Airey is an English keyboardist born June 21, 1948, best known as the keyboardist of [[Artists/D/Deep Purple]] and for his work with [[Artists/R/Rainbow]] and [[Artists/O/Ozzy Osbourne]].**

Airey replaced Jon Lord in Deep Purple in 2002. He has also played with Rainbow, Ozzy Osbourne, Gary Moore, and many others, making him one of rock's most prolific session keyboardists.
