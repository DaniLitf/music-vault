**Rapper, hype man, art director, graphic artist. American.**

Danny Boy is a rapper and creative force best known as the second emcee and hype man of House of Pain, where he contributed vocals, art direction, and graphic design that helped define the group's visual identity. His work designing the demo cover for House of Pain's early material caught the attention of Tommy Boy Records. After House of Pain disbanded, he continued involvement in music and other creative projects, and later co-founded La Coka Nostra with DJ Lethal and Everlast in 2004, reopening his focus on hip-hop.

## Biography

Danny Boy (Daniel O'Connor) is of Irish-American descent and attended William Howard Taft High School in Woodland Hills, California, where he met Everlast. He was instrumental in House of Pain's formation, coming up with the band name and designing much of their early promotional artwork. His creative contributions extended beyond music into fashion design during the band's later years. In 2019, O'Connor opened The Outsiders House Museum, dedicated to S.E. Hinton's novel and film The Outsiders.
