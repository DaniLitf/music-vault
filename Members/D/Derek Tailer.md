# Derek Tailer

**Guitarist.**

Derek Tailer has been the rhythm guitarist of Overkill since 1999, working alongside lead guitarist Dave Linsk. He joined at the same time as Linsk and has appeared on all albums from *Bloodletting* (2000) through the present. Before Overkill he played in various New Jersey-area metal bands.
