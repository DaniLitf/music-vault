**Bassist. American.**

Dave Dreiwitz is the bassist for Ween, the innovative and prolific duo known for their genre-defying approach to rock, funk, soul, and experimental music. As the band's bassist since their formation in 1984, Dreiwitz has been instrumental in creating Ween's distinctive and unpredictable sound, which has made them cult favorites and influential figures in alternative music.
