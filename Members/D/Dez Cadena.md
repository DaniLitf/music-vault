# Dez Cadena

**Vocalist, guitarist. American. Born 1961-06-02.**

Dez Cadena is an American punk musician best known as the third vocalist and later rhythm guitarist of Black Flag, joining the influential hardcore punk band in 1980 and appearing on the landmark album *Damaged* (1981). He transitioned from vocals to rhythm guitar during his tenure, helping define Black Flag's sound during a pivotal era. Beyond Black Flag, Cadena has performed backing vocals on the Descendents' album *All* (1987), fronted the band DC3, and served as guitarist for the reunited Misfits from 2001 to 2015.
