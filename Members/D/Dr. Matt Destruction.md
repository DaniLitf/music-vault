**Bassist. Swedish. Born June 13, 1973.**

Mattias Bernvall, known professionally as Dr. Matt Destruction or Matt Destruction, is a Swedish bassist and founding member of the garage rock band The Hives. A core member from the band's formation in 1993 through 2013, he was instrumental in shaping The Hives' sound during their rise to international prominence. Though he retired from active touring due to health reasons in 2013, his foundational contributions to the band remain significant.

## Biography

Mattias Bernvall was a founding member of The Hives in 1993 and served as the band's bassist throughout their most productive and commercially successful period. He played on six studio albums: "Barely Legal" (1997), "Veni Vidi Vicious" (1998), "Howler Moon" (2000), "The Black and White Album" (2007), "Lex Hives" (2012), and material leading up to his departure. Known for his steady, groove-oriented bass work that helped anchor The Hives' high-energy garage rock sound, Bernvall's 20-year tenure was crucial to the band's development and success. He retired from The Hives in late 2013 due to health complications. He has also contributed to other Swedish bands and projects throughout his career and made a brief appearance in The Hives' 2023 music video "Countdown to Shutdown."
