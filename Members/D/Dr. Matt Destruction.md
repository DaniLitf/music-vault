# Dr. Matt Destruction

**Bass. Swedish. Born 1978-03-18.**

Mattias Bernwall, known onstage as Dr. Matt Destruction, is a Swedish bassist and founding member of garage rock band [[Artists/T/The Hives]], a childhood friend of the Almqvist brothers who joined the band at its 1993 formation in Fagersta. He played bass on the band's first five studio albums before retiring in late 2013 for undisclosed health reasons, and was replaced by Johan Gustafsson ("The Johan and Only"). He made a brief cameo in the music video for "Countdown to Shutdown" (2023).
