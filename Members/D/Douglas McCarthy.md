# Douglas McCarthy

**Vocalist. Born ?.**

Douglas McCarthy is a British vocalist best known as the frontman of the EBM/industrial band Nitzer Ebb. He co-founded the group in 1982 with Bon Harris and David Gooday in Chelmsford, Essex, and his commanding vocal style became a defining element of the EBM genre. McCarthy has also collaborated with other industrial acts and released solo material.
