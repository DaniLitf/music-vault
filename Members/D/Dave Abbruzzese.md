**Drummer. American. Born May 17, 1968.**

Dave Abbruzzese is a drummer best known for his work with Pearl Jam during their rise to prominence in the early 1990s. He drummed on some of Pearl Jam's most iconic albums, including Vitalogy and No Code, contributing significantly to the band's sound during a pivotal period in grunge and alternative rock history. His energetic and versatile drumming style helped define Pearl Jam's dynamic live performances.
