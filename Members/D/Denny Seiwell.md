# Denny Seiwell

**Drummer. American. Born 1943-07-10.**

Denny Seiwell is a accomplished session and touring drummer who served as a founding member and drummer of Wings, Paul McCartney's band that followed the Beatles, from 1971 to 1973. He appeared on Wings' first two studio albums *Wild Life* (1971) and *Red Rose Speedway* (1973), as well as the iconic *Live and Let Die* (1973) soundtrack. Before and after his tenure with Wings, Seiwell has worked as a session and touring drummer with numerous artists including Billy Joel, Liza Minnelli, John Denver, and various television and film projects.
