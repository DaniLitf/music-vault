# Derek Sherinian

**Keyboardist. American. Born 1966-08-25.**

Derek Sherinian is a highly accomplished keyboardist known for his virtuosic playing and aggressive, "guitaristic" approach to the keyboard. He was the keyboardist for Dream Theater from 1994 to 1999, appearing on the critically acclaimed album *Falling into Infinity* (1997). Since leaving Dream Theater, Sherinian has maintained an exceptionally prolific career, founding and leading the progressive rock supergroup Planet X, the blues rock group Black Country Communion, and the metal orchestra Sons of Apollo. He has also recorded nine solo albums and toured extensively with artists such as Alice Cooper and Yngwie Malmsteen.
