# Dave Ogilvie

**Record producer, engineer, musician. Canadian. Born 1960.**

Dave Ogilvie is a renowned record producer and mixing engineer best known for his work with industrial and electronic music acts. He was the primary producer and engineer for Skinny Puppy throughout their classic period (1984–1996) and co-created some of the band's most iconic albums. He has also worked extensively with Marilyn Manson, Nine Inch Nails, and numerous other artists, earning a Western Canadian Music Awards nomination for Producer of the Year in 2011.
