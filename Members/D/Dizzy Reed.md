# Dizzy Reed

**Keyboardist. American.**

Dizzy Reed is an American keyboardist who has been a member of Guns N' Roses since 1990. He played keyboards for The Dead Daisies from 2013 to 2016, contributing to their live performances and studio recordings during that period.
