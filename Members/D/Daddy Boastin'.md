# Daddy Boastin'

**Toaster, reggae musician. Born 1959-07-11; died 2020-08-07.**

Daddy Boastin' was a Swedish-Jamaican reggae and dancehall artist who became a recurring guest on recordings by [[Artists/T/The Latin Kings]]. His toasting helped connect Swedish hip hop with reggae and dancehall influences in the 1990s.
