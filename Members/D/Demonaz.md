# Demonaz

**Guitarist, vocalist, lyricist. Norwegian.**

Harald Nævdal, known as Demonaz Doom Occulta, is a Norwegian musician who co-founded black metal band Immortal in 1991 with Abbath, writing the band's lyrics throughout its history. Tendinitis forced him to stop playing guitar in 1997, after which he continued as the band's manager and lyricist until returning to guitar and taking over vocals in 2015 following Abbath's departure.
