# Danny Lilker

**Bassist, guitarist. American.**

Danny Lilker is an American multi-instrumentalist and a foundational figure in the New York hardcore and extreme metal scenes. He was a founding member of Anthrax, playing bass on their debut album Fistful of Metal, and went on to found Nuclear Assault and co-found S.O.D. (Stormtroopers of Death), Brutal Truth, and Defecation.
