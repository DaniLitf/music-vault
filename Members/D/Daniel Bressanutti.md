**Musician. Belgian.**

Daniel Bressanutti is a member of Front 242, the influential Belgian industrial and electronic music band. Emerging as a key figure in the European industrial scene, Front 242 has been known for their driving electronic sound and provocative live performances, with Bressanutti contributing to the band's evolution and continued relevance in electronic and industrial music.
