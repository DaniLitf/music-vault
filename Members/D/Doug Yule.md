# Doug Yule

**Bass, vocals, keyboards. Born February 25, 1947.**

Doug Yule is an American multi-instrumentalist, best known as a member of [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]] from 1968 onward, replacing John Cale.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]]

## Biography
Yule joined The Velvet Underground in 1968, replacing John Cale on bass. He sang lead on several tracks on later albums including *The Velvet Underground* and *Loaded*. After Lou Reed's departure, Yule led the band through its final phase and recorded the controversial album *Squeeze* (1973) under the Velvet Underground name.
