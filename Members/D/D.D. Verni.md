# D.D. Verni

**Bassist, producer. Born 1961-06-19 in New Jersey, USA.**

D.D. Verni is the bassist and co-founder of Overkill, and alongside vocalist Bobby Ellsworth the only member to have been present for every album in the band's career since 1980. Beyond his role in Overkill, he has produced albums for other metal bands and has been central to the band's songwriting throughout their extensive discography.
