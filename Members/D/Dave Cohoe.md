# Dave Cohoe

**Drums, backing vocals. South African. Founding member of [[Artists/S/Seether]].**

Dave Cohoe was Seether's original drummer when the band formed as Saron Gas in 1999, appearing on the debut album *Fragile* before departing in 2002 due to family commitments as the band relocated to the United States.
