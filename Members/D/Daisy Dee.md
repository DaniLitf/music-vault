**Vocalist.**

Daisy Dee (born Daisy Rollocks) is a vocalist who contributed to [[Artists/T/Technotronic|Technotronic]]'s third album *Recall* (1995), appearing on two tracks. She provided additional vocal performances alongside frontwoman [[Members/Y/Ya Kid K|Ya Kid K]] during the album's recording.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/Technotronic]] – vocals (1995)
