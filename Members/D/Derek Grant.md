# Derek Grant

**Drummer, percussionist, backing vocalist. American.**

Derek Grant is a drummer, multi-instrumentalist, and producer who spent 22 years as drummer and backing vocalist of [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio]] from 2001 to 2023, appearing on every studio album from *Good Mourning* (2003) through *Blood, Hair, and Eyeballs* (2024), the latter completed before his departure. Based in Detroit as a teenager, he began his career at 15 as drummer for the ska-punk band [[Artists/T/The Suicide Machines]], and has also served as a touring fill-in drummer for [[Artists/T/The Vandals]]. He left Alkaline Trio in 2023 to focus on his mental health, production, and graphic design.
