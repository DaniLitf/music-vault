# Dylan Taylor

**Drummer.**

Dylan Taylor is an American drummer who toured with [[Artists/B/Black Light Burns]] from 2012 to 2013 in support of *The Moment You Realize You're Going to Fall*. He took over the drum position after Joe Letz's scheduled stint fell through.

## Associated Artists
