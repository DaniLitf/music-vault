# DJ Ashba

**Guitarist, producer. American. Born 1972-11-10.**

DJ Ashba is an accomplished guitarist and music producer known for his work as lead guitarist and co-writer with Sixx A.M., the supergroup founded by Mötley Crüe bassist Nikki Sixx, since 2007. He is also recognized for his tenure as lead guitarist of Guns N' Roses from 2009 to 2015, where he appeared on major international tours and the album *Chinese Democracy* era. Beyond performing, Ashba has worked extensively in music production and design, co-founding Funny Farms Studios with Nikki Sixx in 2006. He is a multi-instrumentalist who began studying music at age five and has collaborated with numerous artists across rock, metal, and other genres.
