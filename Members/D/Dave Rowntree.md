# Dave Rowntree

**Drummer. British. Born 1964-05-08.**

Dave Rowntree is the longtime drummer of Blur, having joined the British rock band in 1989 and remaining a core member through the band's classic 1990s albums and subsequent reunions. He brings technical precision to Blur's complex arrangements and has been integral to the band's sound across multiple eras. Beyond drumming, Rowntree is a composer for film and television, political activist, trained solicitor, and former computer programmer.
