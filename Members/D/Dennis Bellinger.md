# Dennis Bellinger

**Bassist, vocalist. American.**

Dennis Bellinger is an American bassist best known as the bassist for Grand Funk Railroad during their 1981–1983 reunion era, replacing Mel Schacher. He appears on the albums *Grand Funk Lives* and *What's Funk?*
