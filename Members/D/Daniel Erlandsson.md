# Daniel Erlandsson

**Daniel Erlandsson is a Swedish drummer best known as the long-time drummer of Arch Enemy, who has also served as Carcass's drummer during their reunion era.**

He briefly played on In Flames' *The Jester Race* (1996) before joining Arch Enemy in 1998, where he has been a core member across their most successful albums. He also drums for Carcass since their 2007 reunion.
