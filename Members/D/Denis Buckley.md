# Denis Buckley

**Lead vocalist.**

Denis Buckley is an American punk rock singer from Chicago, co-founder and frontman of [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]] from 1993 onward. After the band's 1999 breakup he sang in the melodic hardcore band [[Artists/E/Explode and Make Up]] with fellow Chicago scene veterans Jeff Dean and Pete Mittler, returning to 88 Fingers Louie for its 2009 and 2013 reunions.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]] – lead vocals (1993–1996, 1998–1999, 2009–2010, 2013–present)
- [[Artists/E/Explode and Make Up]] – vocals (c. 2006–2010)
