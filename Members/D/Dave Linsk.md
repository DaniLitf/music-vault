# Dave Linsk

**Guitarist. Born 1966-06-09 in New Jersey, USA.**

Dave Linsk has been the lead guitarist of Overkill since 1999, contributing to the band's albums from *Bloodletting* (2000) onward. Before joining Overkill he played in several New Jersey metal acts. His technically proficient style has helped define Overkill's sound through their most prolific latter-day period.
