# Dave Rotheray

**Guitarist. Born 1963.**

Dave Rotheray is an English guitarist and songwriter, best known as the guitarist of [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]]

## Biography
Rotheray was the primary guitarist for The Beautiful South throughout their entire run from 1988 to 2007, co-writing many of the band's songs with Paul Heaton.
