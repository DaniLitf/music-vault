**Keyboardist, synth programmer. Canadian. Born February 1, 1964. Died August 23, 1995.**

Dwayne Rudolph Goettel was a Canadian keyboardist and synthesizer programmer best known for his innovative work with the industrial pioneer band Skinny Puppy (1986–1995). A classically trained pianist, Goettel was celebrated as "the master of sampling" for his experimental approach to synthesis and sampling, which helped define Skinny Puppy's evolved industrial sound. His death in 1995 marked a significant loss to the industrial music community.

## Biography

Dwayne Goettel was born in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada, and received classical piano training from an early age. He began his professional music career in the early 1980s with the Edmonton electronic act Psyche and the band Voice before co-founding Water with Mandy Cousins in 1985. His most significant work came with his involvement in Skinny Puppy, which he joined in 1986 as keyboardist and synth programmer. During his nine-year tenure with Skinny Puppy, Goettel's sampling and synthesis work was instrumental in the band's evolution, contributing to albums including "Echoes Silence Patience & Grace" (1987), "Rabies" (1989), "The Process" (1992), and "Last Rights" (1992). Beyond Skinny Puppy, Goettel collaborated with producer cEvin Key on multiple projects including The Tear Garden and the industrial collective Cyberaktif. In 1995, he co-founded Download with Key, releasing the album "Furnace" posthumously. Goettel also released electronic music under the pseudonym aDuck. He died of a heroin overdose on August 23, 1995, at age 31 in Edmonton. Both "The Process" (Skinny Puppy) and "Furnace" (Download) were dedicated to his memory.
