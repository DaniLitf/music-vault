# Derek Moore

**Derek "Mo" Moore is a British bassist and vocalist, co-founder of the progressive rock band Nektar, with the band since their formation in Hamburg in 1969.**

Moore provides bass and backing vocals for Nektar, and has been the band's most consistently present member across all lineup changes. He was instrumental in Nektar's reunion in the 2000s.
