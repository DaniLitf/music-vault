# David Duet

**Vocalist and guitarist. American.**

David Duet is an American singer and guitarist, best known as the frontman of the Houston alternative rock band Dashboard Mary under the stage name Dave Nobody. He released one album with the band, *Somebody's in Here* (2007). After the band broke up, he moved back to Seattle.
