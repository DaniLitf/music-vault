# Dave Gahan

**Dave Gahan is a British vocalist and the frontman of Depeche Mode, whose baritone voice and charismatic, increasingly physical stage presence became integral to the band's identity.**

After surviving a near-fatal heroin overdose in 1996, he transformed himself and has become one of rock's most celebrated live performers.
