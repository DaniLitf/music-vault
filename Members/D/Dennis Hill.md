# Dennis Hill

**Guitarist, vocalist. American.**

Dennis Hill is a guitarist and vocalist for the Southern California punk rock band [[Artists/F/Face to Face]], joining in 2015 as a replacement for Chad Yaro. Hill has been the band's guitarist for recent studio albums and touring performances since his joining.
