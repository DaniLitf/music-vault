# Danne Andersson

**Guitar. Swedish.**

Danne Andersson is a Swedish guitarist who joined [[Artists/T/The Hellacopters]] as a touring guitarist in 1998 alongside Mattias Hellberg, helping complete the band's touring commitments after Dregen's departure. Little further biographical detail is reliably documented.
