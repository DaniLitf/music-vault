# Dan Spitz

**Guitarist. Born 1963-01-28 in New Jersey, USA.**

Dan Spitz served as Overkill's lead guitarist from 1990 to 1993, briefly filling the slot vacated by Bobby Gustafson. He appeared on *Horrorscope* (1991) before departing.
