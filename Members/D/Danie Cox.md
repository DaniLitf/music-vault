# Danie Cox

**Vocalist, guitarist, songwriter. Welsh.**

Danie Cox, later also known as Centric, is a Welsh vocalist and guitarist who leads [[Artists/T/The Featherz]]. She previously played in Poussez Posse, later sang with The Lurkers, and founded Slady, an all-female tribute to Slade in which she performs as Gobby Holder.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Featherz]] – vocals, guitar, songwriter (2011–present)
- [[Artists/P/Poussez Posse]] – guitar, backing vocals (2011)
- [[Artists/T/The Lurkers]] – guest vocals (2017–2020)
- [[Artists/S/Slady]] – vocals, guitar as Gobby Holder (2018–present)
