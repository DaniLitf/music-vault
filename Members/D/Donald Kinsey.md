**Guitarist. Born 12 October 1953.**

Donald Kinsey was an American guitarist who played with Bob Marley and the Wailers in 1975–1976, contributing to *Rastaman Vibration*. He was also a member of the Albert King band and later formed the reggae band the Kinsey Report.
