# Danny Lohner

**Bassist, guitarist, producer. Born 1970-12-13.**

Danny Lohner is an American musician and producer best known for his work with [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] and remixes under the name Renholdër. He played bass, guitar, and backing vocals in [[Artists/L/Lindemann (band)]]'s live lineup.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] – bass, guitar, keyboards (1994–2001)
- [[Artists/R/Renholdër]] – solo/remix alias
- [[Artists/L/Lindemann (band)]] – bass, guitar, backing vocals (live)
