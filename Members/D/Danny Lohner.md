# Danny Lohner

**Bassist, guitarist, multi-instrumentalist. Born 13 December 1970 in Corpus Christi, Texas, USA.**

Danny Lohner is an American multi-instrumentalist best known as a longtime contributing member of [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] (1994–2003), playing bass, guitar, and programming on tours and studio work. He was also a member of [[Artists/P/Pigface]] and [[Artists/T/Tapeworm]], the collaborative project involving members of NIN, Tool, and others. He has worked extensively as a producer and composer for film and television.

## Biography

Lohner joined Nine Inch Nails during the *Downward Spiral* tour and became a key part of the band's 1990s live sound. He played on *The Fragile* in a supporting capacity and was involved in the unreleased Tapeworm project with Maynard James Keenan and others. Post-NIN, he has worked as a film and television composer, contributing to the *Twilight* series score and producing tracks for various artists. He also appeared as a member of the supergroup Renholdër.
