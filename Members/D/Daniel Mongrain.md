# Daniel Mongrain

**Guitar. Born July 4, 1976.**

Daniel "Chewy" Mongrain is a Canadian guitarist, also known for co-founding the technical death metal band Martyr, who joined [[Artists/V/Voivod]] in 2008 as the replacement for the late Denis "Piggy" D'Amour and has recorded every studio album since *Infini* (2009).
