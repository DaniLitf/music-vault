# Daniel Lanois

**Record producer, musician, songwriter. Born September 19, 1951 in Hull, Quebec, Canada.**

Daniel Lanois is a Canadian record producer and musician whose atmospheric production style has shaped landmark albums across multiple decades. He rose to prominence collaborating with Brian Eno on U2's *The Unforgettable Fire* (1984) and *The Joshua Tree* (1987), and has produced seminal albums for Bob Dylan (*Time Out of Mind*), Emmylou Harris (*Wrecking Ball*), Neil Young (*Le Noise*), and Peter Gabriel (*So*). As a solo artist he has released albums including *Acadie* (1989), *For the Beauty of Wynona* (1993), and *Belladonna* (2005), and has won multiple Grammy and Juno Awards.
