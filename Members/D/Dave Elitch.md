# Dave Elitch

**Drummer. American. Born 1984-04-07.**

Dave Elitch is a respected progressive rock and metal drummer known for his work with The Mars Volta and Killer Be Killed. He is also recognized as a skilled drum instructor and percussion teacher in Los Angeles, teaching both aspiring and professional musicians and conducting masterclasses on drum technique and performance psychology.
