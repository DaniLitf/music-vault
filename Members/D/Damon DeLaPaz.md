# Damon DeLaPaz

**Drums, guitar.**

Damon DeLaPaz is an American musician best known as a member of the Texas pop-punk band [[Artists/F/Fenix TX]], where he moved from drums to guitar. In 1999 he filled in on drums for Travis Barker in [[Artists/B/Blink-182]] at the Warped Tour, when Barker was unable to perform. He is also listed in the current lineup of [[Artists/E/Excel]] per its most recent announced roster.
