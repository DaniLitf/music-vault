# Dirk Verbeuren

**Drummer. Born 1975-12-07 in Brussels, Belgium.**

Dirk Verbeuren is a Belgian drummer who was the drummer of Soilwork from 2005 to 2016 before being recruited by Megadeth as their permanent drummer in 2016, replacing Chris Adler. He appears on Megadeth's *Dystopia* (2016) and has remained with the band since. His technically demanding, high-tempo style has made him sought-after across melodic death and thrash metal.
