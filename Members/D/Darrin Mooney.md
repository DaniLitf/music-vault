**Drummer. Scottish.**

Darrin Mooney is the drummer for Primal Scream, the influential Scottish rock band that blends alternative rock, electronic, and psychedelic influences. As part of Primal Scream's current lineup alongside Bobby Gillespie and Andrew Innes, Mooney has been instrumental in maintaining the band's powerful dynamic and driving their continued evolution as musicians and performers.
