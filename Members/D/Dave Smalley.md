# Dave Smalley

**Vocals. American.**

Dave Smalley is an American punk vocalist from the Washington, D.C. area who first made his name fronting the Boston hardcore bands DYS and Dag Nasty before becoming the first lead singer of the Descendents spin-off [[Artists/A/All]] in 1987. He sang on All's debut album *Allroy Sez* (1988) and the *Allroy for Prez* EP (1988) before leaving in 1989 to eventually front the melodic punk band Down by Law, and later the band Don't Sleep.
