**Guitarist, keyboardist, vocalist. American. Born January 15, 1951.**

Douglas "Duke" Erikson is an American musician, producer, and filmmaker best known as a co-founder of Garbage, one of the most successful alternative rock bands of the 1990s and beyond. A versatile instrumentalist and vocalist, Erikson has also founded and led earlier bands including Spooner and Fire Town. Beyond music, he has worked as a screenwriter, film producer, and documentary composer.

## Biography

Duke Erikson was born in Lyons, Nebraska, and discovered his musical passion in early adolescence. He studied drawing and painting at Wayne State College and began his professional music career in the mid-1970s as a founding member of Spooner, a post-punk/new wave band that released two albums in the 1980s. Following Spooner's initial disbandment, he formed Fire Town in 1986, an indie rock project that earned critical recognition before dissolving in 1989. In 1993, Erikson co-founded Garbage with producer Butch Vig and engineer Steve Marker. Combining his songwriting, guitar, keyboard, and vocal talents, Erikson became integral to Garbage's sound, contributing to every album from their 1995 debut through the present day. Beyond Garbage, Erikson co-founded Smart Studios, an influential recording facility in Madison, Wisconsin, and has worked as a composer and producer for film and television. He composed the James Bond theme "The World Is Not Enough" (1999) and co-founded the documentary project American Epic (2017), which explores the history of recorded music in America.
