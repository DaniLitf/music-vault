# Danny Keough

**Bassist. American. Born 1964.**

Danny Keough is an American bassist and musician, best known as Lisa Marie Presley's first husband and as a member of [[Artists/T/Ten Inch Men]] from 1987 to 1993. He played bass on the album *Pretty Vultures* (1993).
