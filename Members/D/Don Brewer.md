# Don Brewer

**Drummer, vocalist. Born September 3, 1948.**

Don Brewer is an American drummer and vocalist best known as a founding member and drummer of Grand Funk Railroad, where he also sang lead on hits including "We're an American Band" and "The Loco-Motion." He has remained the band's leader through every incarnation, from the original trio to the current touring lineup. Brewer also played with Bob Seger & the Silver Bullet Band and Terry Knight and the Pack.
