# Dale Alexander

**Drummer. Born ?. NATIONALITY.**

Dale Alexander is a Minneapolis-based drummer and a childhood friend of Prince. He played in an early trio with Prince and André Cymone, and later toured as the drummer for the live lineup of Madhouse during Prince's 1987 Sign o' the Times European tour.
