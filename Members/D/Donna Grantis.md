# Donna Grantis

**Guitarist. Born 11 July 1982 in Toronto, Canada.**

Donna Grantis is a Canadian guitarist who joined Prince's New Power Generation in 2010. She played on *20Ten* and was part of Prince's later touring band before becoming a founding member of 3rdeyegirl. She released her solo debut *Diamonds & Dynamite* in 2018.
