# Dee Snider

**Dee Snider is an American singer and the lead vocalist of Twisted Sister, best remembered for the anthems "We're Not Gonna Take It" and "I Wanna Rock," and for his memorable Congressional testimony against the PMRC's proposed music labeling in 1985.**

Snider has also pursued acting and solo projects throughout his career.
