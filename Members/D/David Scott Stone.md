# David Scott Stone

**Bassist, experimental musician. American.**

David Scott Stone is an American musician who played bass with [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] in the 2000s, including fill-in live work after Kevin Rutmanis's departure. He also supported film-related performances and experimental work connected to the band.
