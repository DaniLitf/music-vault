**Drummer. Swedish.**

Daniel Mullback is a drummer for Sabaton, the Swedish heavy metal band known for their historical and military-themed lyrics. As a member of Sabaton, Mullback has contributed to the band's powerful live shows and recorded work, helping to propel the band to international recognition in the heavy metal community.
