# David Husvik

**Drummer. Norwegian.**

David Husvik is a Norwegian drummer who co-founded Christian extreme metal band Extol at age 16 with his cousin, guitarist Christer Espevoll. He also drummed for the Scandinavian heavy metal supergroup Doctor Midnight & The Mercy Cult from 2009 to 2011.
