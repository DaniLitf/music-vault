# David Husvik

**Drummer. Norwegian.**

David Husvik is a Norwegian drummer best known as a former member of Christian metal band Extol. He drummed for the Scandinavian heavy metal supergroup Doctor Midnight & The Mercy Cult from 2009 to 2011.
