# David Lindqvist

**Bass. Born DATE.**

David Lindqvist is a Swedish musician, best known as the bassist for the Swedish rock band [[Artists/C/Caesars]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/C/Caesars]] – bass (1995–present)

## Biography

Lindqvist co-founded Twelve Caesars (later Caesars) in 1995 alongside César Vidal and Joakim Åhlund. He has remained the band's bassist throughout all incarnations — Twelve Caesars, Caesars Palace, and Caesars.
