# Dean Ween

**Dean Ween (born Michael Melchiondo) is an American guitarist born September 17, 1970, co-founder of [[Artists/W/Ween]].**

He performed on Pigface recordings and tours as a guest musician.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/P/Pigface]] – guitar (guest)
- [[Artists/W/Ween]] – guitar, vocals (1984–present)
