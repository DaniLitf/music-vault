**DJ, producer. Latvian. Born December 18, 1972.**

DJ Lethal is a Latvian-born DJ and producer best known as the turntablist and production partner for the hardcore hip-hop trio House of Pain. After the group disbanded, he became the DJ for the nu metal band Limp Bizkit, helping to define the band's signature sound during their commercial peak. He also reunited with Everlast and Danny Boy as a member of the rap supergroup La Coka Nostra.

## Biography

Leor Dimant emigrated from Latvia and became a skilled turntablist and DJ. He was brought into House of Pain by Everlast as his former DJ and collaborated on the group's three studio albums throughout the 1990s. Following House of Pain's initial disbandment in 1996, Dimant joined Limp Bizkit in 1998 and became a core member, contributing to the band's multi-platinum success and helping pioneer the fusion of nu metal with hip-hop elements. He has maintained involvement with both Limp Bizkit and La Coka Nostra across multiple reunion tours and studio projects.
