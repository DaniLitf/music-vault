# David Byrne

**David Byrne is a Scottish-born American musician born May 14, 1952, co-founder and frontman of [[Artists/T/Talking Heads]].**

Byrne co-founded Talking Heads in 1975 and led the band through their critically acclaimed career. He has also pursued a successful solo career, collaborated with [[Artists/B/Brian Eno]], composed for film and theater, and written books on music.
