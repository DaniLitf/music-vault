# David "Dicksplash" Hewitt

**Guitarist. British.**

David "Dicksplash" Hewitt is an English guitarist who played for deathgrind band Gorerotted from 1999 to 2001, appearing on the album *Mutilated in Minutes* (2000). After leaving the band, he ran the solo death metal/thrash project Full of Hate, which released one album, *Created to Suffer*.
