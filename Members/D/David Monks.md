# David Monks

**Vocalist, bassist, songwriter. Canadian.**

David Monks is a Canadian musician best known as the lead vocalist and bassist of the indie rock band [[Artists/T/Tokyo Police Club]], which he co-founded in Newmarket, Ontario in 2005 and fronted until the band's farewell shows in 2024. He has also released solo material under the name Dave Monks. In 2025 he launched [[Artists/N/Netherwilds]], a medieval "peasant metal" project that grew out of his viral TikTok character Worm Chewer, whose fantasy peasant adventures spawned a full musical world signed to King Volume Records.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/Tokyo Police Club]] – lead vocals, bass (2005–2024)
