**Guitarist and bassist. Born 27 November 1952.**

Daryl Stuermer is an American guitarist and bassist best known for his 40-year career as Genesis's touring guitarist and bassist from 1978 onwards. A Milwaukee native who began playing guitar at age eleven, Stuermer initially formed the jazz rock band Sweetbottom in the early 1970s before joining Genesis as a touring musician in 1978. His partnership with Genesis frontman Phil Collins has been particularly enduring, as Stuermer also became Collins's lead guitarist for most of his solo tours and albums, earning writing credits on several Collins recordings. Beyond Genesis and Collins work, Stuermer has released nine solo albums and continues to tour with his own band while maintaining his Genesis commitments.
