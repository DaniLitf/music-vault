# Denis D'Amour

**Guitar. Born September 24, 1959. Died August 26, 2005.**

Denis "Piggy" D'Amour was a Canadian guitarist who co-founded [[Artists/V/Voivod]] in 1982 and remained its guitarist and principal riff-writer for the rest of his life, developing an angular, dissonant style that was highly influential on progressive and technical metal. He died of colon cancer in 2005 at age 45, shortly after which the band paused before eventually recruiting Daniel Mongrain as his replacement.
