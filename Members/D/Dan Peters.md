# Dan Peters

**American drummer. Born 18 August 1967.**

Dan Peters is an American drummer from Seattle who played in Bundle of Hiss before co-founding Mudhoney in 1988. He was also briefly Nirvana's drummer in mid-1990, appearing on the single "Sliver," and drummed for Screaming Trees from 1990 to 1991.
