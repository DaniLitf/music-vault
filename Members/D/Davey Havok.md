# Davey Havok

**American musician. Born 20 November 1975.**

Davey Havok (born David Paden Marchand) is an American musician, best known as the lead vocalist and frontman of the rock band AFI. He is also the lead vocalist of the electronic band Blaqk Audio and the hardcore punk band XTRMST, and has been involved in various other musical and creative projects.
