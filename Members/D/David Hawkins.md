# David Hawkins

**Guitarist. British.**

David Hawkins is a British guitarist who joined [[Artists/B/British Lion]], the hard rock project of Iron Maiden bassist Steve Harris, co-writing most of the band's songs alongside Harris and vocalist Richard Taylor.
