# Dirk Ivens

**Belgian electronic musician, founder of Dive, Klinik, Absolute Body Control, and Sonar. Born in Belgium.**

Dirk Ivens is a pioneering figure in the Belgian EBM and electro-industrial scene. He co-founded Absolute Body Control in 1979 and Klinik in 1981 before launching Dive as a solo project in 1990. Ivens is known for his minimalist, distortion-heavy sound built on abused drum machines and processed vocals. He performs live as a solo act with minimal equipment and visual presentation. Dive has been his primary creative outlet since 1990, though he has also participated in Blok 57 and co-founded Sonar. He also runs the Daft record label.
