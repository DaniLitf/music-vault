# Dean DeLeo

**Guitarist, producer. American. Born 1961-08-23.**

Dean DeLeo is the lead guitarist of Stone Temple Pilots, one of the defining bands of the grunge era, with whom he has recorded numerous platinum albums and toured extensively since 1989. His distinctive guitar work and songwriting have been central to STP's sound through multiple eras, lineup changes, and hiatuses. Beyond Stone Temple Pilots, DeLeo has participated in side projects including Army of Anyone (2005–2007) with his brother Robert and has worked as a producer on various albums.
