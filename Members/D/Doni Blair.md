# Doni Blair

**Bassist. American.**

Doni Blair is a bassist who co-founded [[Artists/H/Hagfish|Hagfish]] with his brother Zach in 1991, serving as the band's bassist from their formation through their original run from 1991 to 2001 and subsequent reunions. After Hagfish disbanded, Blair continued collaborative work with his brother including Armstrong (2002) and Only Crime (2003+) before joining longtime Dallas scene companions [[Artists/T/Toadies|Toadies]] following their 2008 reformation. Blair made his recorded debut with Toadies on the 2012 live album *Play.Rock.Music* and has toured behind studio releases *No Deliverance* (2008) and *Feeler* (2010).
