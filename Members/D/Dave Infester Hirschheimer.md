# Dave "Infester" Hirschheimer

**Drummer. British.**

Dave "Infester" Hirschheimer was a drummer who briefly played for deathgrind band Gorerotted in 1998, recording the *Her Gash I Did Slash* demo before leaving to focus on his band Infestation. He later drummed briefly for Cradle of Filth and now fronts Daken as singer/guitarist.
