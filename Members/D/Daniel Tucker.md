# Daniel Tucker

**Bassist. American.**

Daniel Tucker was the original bassist of Obituary from 1987 to 1993, replacing Jerome Grable. He played on the band's first three albums *Slowly We Rot* (1989), *Cause of Death* (1990), and *The End Complete* (1992). He left the band after their 1992 European tour and was replaced by Frank Watkins.
