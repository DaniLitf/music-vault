# Dean Pleasants

**Lead guitar (1989–present).**

Dean Pleasants is an American guitarist best known as the longtime guitarist for both [[Artists/S/Suicidal Tendencies]] and [[Artists/I/Infectious Grooves]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Infectious Grooves]] – lead guitar (1989–present)
- [[Artists/S/Suicidal Tendencies]] – guitar (1992–present)

## Biography

Pleasants has been a dual member of Suicidal Tendencies and Infectious Grooves since the late 1980s, contributing to both bands' distinctive sounds. His funky yet aggressive playing style bridges the gap between the two projects.
