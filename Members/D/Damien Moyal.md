# Damien Moyal

**Vocalist.**

Damien Moyal is an American hardcore vocalist from South Florida, best known as the founder and frontman of [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]], which he has led since 1996 (apart from a brief 2002 departure). A fixture of the 1990s Florida hardcore scene, he also fronted [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]], [[Artists/M/Morning Again]] and [[Artists/B/Bird of Ill Omen]], and has directed several of As Friends Rust's music videos.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] – lead vocals (1996–2002, 2008–present)
- [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]] – lead vocals (1994–1995, 1996–1998; reunions 2012–2016)
- [[Artists/M/Morning Again]] – lead vocals (1990s)
- [[Artists/B/Bird of Ill Omen]] – lead vocals (1996–1997)
- [[Artists/S/Shai Hulud]] – lead vocals (mid-1990s)
