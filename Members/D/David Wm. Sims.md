# David Wm. Sims

**Bassist. American. Born 1963-09-17.**

David Wm. Sims is a bassist and experimental musician known as the founding member of The Jesus Lizard, one of the most influential experimental rock and noise rock bands of the 1980s and 1990s. He was instrumental in shaping the band's dense, dissonant sound across their classic albums before the band's initial breakup in 1999. Sims reunited with The Jesus Lizard for periodic tours and recordings starting in 2008 and continues to perform with the band. Beyond The Jesus Lizard, he founded and played with Scratch Acid and has collaborated with numerous experimental and avant-garde artists.
