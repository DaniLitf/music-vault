# Dave Hunt

**Vocalist. British.**

Dave Hunt is an English extreme metal vocalist best known as the frontman of Anaal Nathrakh (under the pseudonym V.I.T.R.I.O.L.) and as the former vocalist of Benediction, where he performed from 1998 to 2019 on the albums *Organised Chaos* and *Killing Music*. He has also fronted Mistress and holds a PhD in philosophy, having written his thesis on moral error theory.
