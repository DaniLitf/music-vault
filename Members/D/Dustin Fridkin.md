# Dustin Fridkin

**Bassist. Born July 20, 1980.**

Dustin Fridkin is an American bassist who collaborated with Laura Jane Grace in the early Against Me! project, joining around 2000 and appearing on the band's early EPs and the acoustic self-titled EP (2001). He left in summer 2002 to return to college and was replaced by Andrew Seward, though he reunited with the band for a one-off performance at FEST 16 in October 2017.
