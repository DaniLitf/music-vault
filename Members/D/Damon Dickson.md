# Damon Dickson

**Dancer. Born in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

Damon Dickson was a dancer and backing vocalist in Prince's New Power Generation from its formation in 1990. He toured with Prince on the Nude Tour and the Diamonds and Pearls Tour, and contributed to the *Diamonds and Pearls* and *Love Symbol* albums. He left the band after the Diamonds and Pearls Tour.
