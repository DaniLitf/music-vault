# Dave Kushner

**Dave Kushner is an American guitarist, best known as the rhythm guitarist of Velvet Revolver and Infectious Grooves, and a member of the Kings of Chaos supergroup.**

Kushner played on the first Infectious Grooves demo in 1991 before pursuing other projects. He gained international fame as the rhythm guitarist of the supergroup Velvet Revolver alongside Slash, Duff McKagan, and Scott Weiland. He rejoined Infectious Grooves as a full member in 2024 and is a permanent member of Kings of Chaos.
