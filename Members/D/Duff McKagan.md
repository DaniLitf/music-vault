# Duff McKagan

**Duff McKagan** (born Michael Andrew McKagan, February 5, 1964) is an American bassist and singer best known as a member of [[Artists/G/Guns N' Roses]], [[Artists/V/Velvet Revolver]], [[Loaded (band)|Loaded]], and [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]].

- [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]] – bass (2010)
- [[Artists/G/Guns N' Roses]] – bass, backing vocals (1985–1997, 2016–present)
- [[Artists/V/Velvet Revolver]] – bass, backing vocals (2002–2008, 2012, 2019)
- [[Loaded (band)|Loaded]] – bass, vocals (1999–present)
