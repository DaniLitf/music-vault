# Dave Holland

**Drummer. Born April 5, 1948. Died January 16, 2018.**

Dave Holland was a British drummer who played with Judas Priest from 1979 to 1989, performing on six studio albums including *British Steel* and *Defenders of the Faith*. Before joining Judas Priest, he had played with the band Trapeze, and his steady drumming style helped define the band's sound during their most commercially successful period.
