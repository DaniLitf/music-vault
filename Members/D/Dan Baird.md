**Lead vocalist and guitarist. American. Born December 12, 1953.**

Daniel John Baird is best known as the lead singer and rhythm guitarist of The Georgia Satellites, a pioneering cowpunk and alt-country band that emerged in the 1980s. He formed the band in 1980 and has remained a central figure in its evolution, leaving in 1990 to pursue a solo career before later touring with various projects including Homemade Sin. A prolific solo artist and producer, Baird is credited as one of the pioneers of cowpunk, blending rock, country, and punk elements with witty, intelligent lyrics and energetic performances.
