**Turntablist and DJ.**

DJ Stepzak was an early turntablist and DJ for Lostprophets, appearing on the band's debut album The Fake Sound of Progress (2000). He was only in the band for approximately one year after the album's completion, deciding not to commit to touring with the group. He was replaced by Jamie Oliver, who became the band's permanent keyboardist and turntablist.
