# David Nolte

**Vocals, guitar, bass. American.**

David Nolte is an American musician who co-founded [[Artists/D/Descendents]] with Frank Navetta in 1977, writing early songs on acoustic guitar and later switching to bass. He left the band twice — in 1977 and finally in early 1979 — to join [[Artists/T/The Last]], the Los Angeles power pop/punk band he formed with his brothers Joe and Mike Nolte. He produced and mixed the Descendents' debut single "Ride the Wild" / "It's a Hectic World".
