# Dan Clements

**Vocals. American. Founding member and constant of [[Artists/E/Excel]].**

Dan Clements co-founded the band in 1983 (originally as Chaotic Noise) with guitarist [[Members/A/Adam Siegel]], renaming it Excel in 1985. He has remained the band's vocalist and only constant member through all lineup changes. In 2006 he also joined the one-off project [[Artists/A/Against]] with former [[Artists/S/Suicidal Tendencies]] members.
