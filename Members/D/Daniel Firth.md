**Guitarist. British.**

Daniel Firth is a guitarist and longtime member of the gothic/symphonic metal band Cradle of Filth. He has been an integral part of the band's sound since joining in the late 1990s, contributing to their studio albums and touring extensively across the globe. His work on Cradle of Filth's landmark releases has made him a significant figure in the symphonic metal genre.
