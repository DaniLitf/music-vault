**Guitarist, songwriter, and vocalist. Armenian-American. Born July 18, 1975.**

Daron Malakian is the guitarist, songwriter, and second vocalist of System of a Down, one of the most influential metal bands of the 21st century. As primary composer of much of System's material, Malakian has established himself as a distinctive and versatile guitarist known for incorporating Arabic musical scales and complex songwriting. Beyond System, he founded Scars on Broadway, a separate project that showcases his leadership and musical vision, and has collaborated with numerous artists including Linkin Park and Cypress Hill.
