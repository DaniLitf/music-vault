# David Pajo

**Guitarist and multi-instrumentalist. Born 1968-06-25.**

David Pajo is an American musician from Louisville, Kentucky, best known as co-founding guitarist of the hugely influential post-rock band Slint, whose *Spiderland* (1991) reshaped experimental rock. A restless collaborator, he has played guitar, bass, banjo, and drums across dozens of projects — from Tortoise's landmark *Millions Now Living Will Never Die* (1996) to Billy Corgan's Zwan and touring stints with Yeah Yeah Yeahs, Interpol, and Gang of Four — while releasing solo music as Aerial M, Papa M, and under his own name. He began in Louisville hardcore bands Obscene Routine, Maurice, and Solution Unknown, and later played in Bush League and formed the metal band Dead Child in 2005.

## Biography

Born in Texas and raised in Louisville, Kentucky, Pajo came up through the city's 1980s hardcore scene before co-founding Slint in 1986, playing on both *Tweez* (1989) and *Spiderland* (1991). After Slint's breakup he became one of underground rock's most in-demand players, joining Tortoise for *Millions Now Living Will Never Die* (1996) and *TNT* (1998), and contributing to Palace/Will Oldham records, Stereolab, Royal Trux, and King Kong. His solo work evolved from the instrumental Aerial M into the song-based Papa M, and from 2005 he also released albums under his own name. He is also a producer/engineer of his own home-recorded albums.
