# Dennis Lyxzén

**Vocalist. Born 1972-04-21 in Umeå, Sweden.**

Dennis Lyxzén is the vocalist and co-founder of Refused, one of the most politically engaged and influential voices in Scandinavian hardcore punk. He has been the frontman through both the band's original run (1991–1998) and their reunion. Outside Refused he has fronted The (International) Noise Conspiracy and INVSN, and contributed to Fake Names.
