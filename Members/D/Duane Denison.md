# Duane Denison

**Duane Denison is an American guitarist, best known as the guitarist of [[Artists/T/The Jesus Lizard]].**

He also plays in Tomahawk and contributed guitar to Pigface recordings and tours as a guest.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/P/Pigface]] – guitar (guest)
- [[Artists/T/The Jesus Lizard]] – guitar (1987–1999)
