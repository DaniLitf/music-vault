# David Coverdale

**Vocalist. British. Born 1951-09-22.**

David Coverdale is an English rock singer who rose to fame as the lead vocalist of Deep Purple from 1973 to 1976, delivering powerful performances on the band's comeback album *Perfect Strangers* (1984) and subsequent releases. In 1978 he founded Whitesnake, steering the blues-rock band to worldwide success in the 1980s with multi-platinum albums and iconic music videos. In 1991–1993 he collaborated with legendary guitarist Jimmy Page on the platinum-selling album *Coverdale Page*. He has also produced or co-produced much of his own work throughout his career.
