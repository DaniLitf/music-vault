**Guitarist, producer, multi-instrumentalist. American. Born April 12, 1966.**

Douglas "Doug" Grean is an American guitarist, record producer, and audio engineer best known for his role as lead guitarist and musical director of Scott Weiland and the Wildabouts. A versatile multi-instrumentalist skilled in guitar, bass, keyboards, mandolin, lap steel, and banjo, Grean has worked extensively in studio production and live performance across rock, metal, and alternative music. He co-founded Softdrive Records with Scott Weiland and maintains a professional studio in New York.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/S/Scott Weiland & The Wildabouts]] – Lead guitarist, musical director (touring and studio)

## Biography

Born in Portchester, New York, Doug Grean discovered guitar and mandolin at age 13 and formally studied music at Tulane University in New Orleans during the mid-1980s. He performed extensively in New Orleans jazz, blues, and funk venues before relocating to Los Angeles in the early 1990s, where he worked as an engineer and producer at Clubhouse Studios and Artisan Studios. In 1992, he co-founded Softdrive Records with Scott Weiland, serving as both producer and engineer on numerous projects over more than a decade. Grean has contributed as a session and collaborating musician to artists including Sheryl Crow, Slash, Velvet Revolver, Stone Temple Pilots, The Crystal Method, and Cyndi Lauper. As the lead guitarist for Scott Weiland and the Wildabouts, he has been instrumental in shaping the band's live sound and studio production. He currently operates Haven Sound, a recording studio in New York, where he continues production and engineering work.
