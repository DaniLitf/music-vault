**Vocalist. British. Born March 7, 1960.**

Douglas "Doogie" White is a Scottish vocalist best known for his tenure as the lead singer of Ritchie Blackmore's Rainbow (1994–1997). A prolific and versatile rock vocalist, he has fronted and appeared on recordings across multiple genres including hard rock, heavy metal, power metal, and neoclassical metal. White remains active as a solo artist and continues to perform with various projects, most notably his original band La Paz.

## Biography

Doogie White was born in Motherwell, Lanarkshire, Scotland, and began his music career as the founder and vocalist of La Paz in 1984. The band released material throughout the 1980s and 1990s, earning a dedicated following in Europe. White's most prominent role came with his tenure as the lead vocalist of Ritchie Blackmore's Rainbow from 1994–1997, where he contributed to some of the band's most acclaimed work. Following his time with Rainbow, he has been remarkably prolific, fronting numerous bands and collaborations across the metal and hard rock spectrum, including Yngwie Malmsteen's Rising Force, Tank, and the Alcatrazz. White has also recorded solo material and appeared on various tribute albums and collaborative projects. He remains active in the rock music scene and continues to perform with La Paz.
