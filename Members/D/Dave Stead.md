# Dave Stead

**Drummer. Born 1966.**

Dave Stead is an English drummer, best known as the drummer of [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]]

## Biography
Stead was the drummer for The Beautiful South from their formation in 1988 until their dissolution in 2007. He played on all ten of the band's studio albums, providing the steady rhythmic foundation for their trademark pop rock sound. Before The Beautiful South, Stead had been a touring drummer for various bands in the Hull area.
