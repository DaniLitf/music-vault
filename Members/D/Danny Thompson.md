# Danny Thompson

**Drummer. American.**

Danny Thompson is the drummer of the Southern California punk rock band [[Artists/F/Face to Face]], joining in 2008 after the band's reunion. He replaced Pete Parada, who had departed to join [[Artists/T/The Offspring]]. Thompson came to the band from Uprising and has been the band's drummer for all reunion-era recordings and tours.
