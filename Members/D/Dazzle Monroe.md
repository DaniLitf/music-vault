# Dazzle Monroe

**Drummer. British.**

Dazzle Monroe is a drummer best known in this vault as the long-running drummer for [[Artists/T/The Featherz]]. The Featherz article identifies Monroe as formerly connected with [[Artists/E/Extreme Noise Terror]] and as Cox's main remaining bandmate after the group became largely dormant.

- [[Artists/E/Extreme Noise Terror]] – drums
