# Derrick Bostrom

**American drummer. Born 23 June 1960.**

Derrick Bostrom is an American drummer and founding member of the Meat Puppets, formed in Phoenix in 1980 with the Kirkwood brothers. He remained until the band's 1996 hiatus, maintained its official website throughout, and rejoined for the 2017 reunion.
