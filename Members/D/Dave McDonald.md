# Dave McDonald

**Audio engineer, record producer. British.**

Dave McDonald is a British audio engineer and record producer who has been described as the "fourth member" of [[Artists/P/Portishead]] for his integral role in shaping their studio sound. He co-produced their second album *Portishead* (1997) and engineered their landmark debut *Dummy* (1994). Beyond his work with Portishead, McDonald has had a distinguished career as a front-of-house engineer for artists including Adele, Frank Ocean, and Air.
