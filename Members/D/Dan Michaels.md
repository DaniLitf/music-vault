# Dan Michaels

**Saxophonist and A&R executive. American.**

Dan Michaels is an American saxophonist best known as a member of the alternative rock band The Choir since 1986, and as a frequent session contributor to Adam Again. His saxophone and Lyricon playing became a signature element of both bands' sounds. Outside of performing, Michaels worked in A&R for the Christian music industry and managed the Choir's independent releases.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/A/Adam Again]] – saxophone (1986–2000)
- [[Artists/T/The Choir]] – saxophone, Lyricon (1986–present)

## Biography

Dan Michaels joined Adam Again as a saxophonist on their earliest recordings, beginning with their debut album *In a New World of Time* (1986). He also joined The Choir (originally Youth Choir) in 1986, appearing on their EP *Shades of Gray*, and has remained a core member ever since. In the music industry, Michaels worked in A&R and artist management, managing the Choir's independent releases through his Galaxy21 Music label. He also performed on tour with Crystal Lewis and Bryan Duncan.
