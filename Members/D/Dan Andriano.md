# Dan Andriano

**Bassist, co-lead vocalist. American.**

Dan Andriano is a musician from Elgin, Illinois, who joined [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio]] as bassist and co-lead vocalist in 1997, replacing original bassist Rob Doran, and has appeared on every studio album since. Before joining, he played bass in the punk-ska band [[Artists/S/Slapstick]] and fronted the band Tuesday; in 2004 he also formed [[Artists/T/The Falcon]] with former Slapstick bandmate Brendan Kelly. He records solo material as Dan Andriano in the Emergency Room.
