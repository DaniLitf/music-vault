# Dave Coutts

**Vocalist, guitarist. American.**

Dave Coutts is an American rock musician best known as the frontman of [[Artists/T/Ten Inch Men]] and later [[Artists/T/Talk Show]], the supergroup he formed with Dean DeLeo, Robert DeLeo, and Eric Kretz of Stone Temple Pilots. He also contributed songs to film soundtracks including *Hellraiser III: Hell on Earth* and *Dr. Giggles*.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/Talk Show]] – vocals (1994–1998)
