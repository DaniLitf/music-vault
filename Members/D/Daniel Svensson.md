# Daniel Svensson

**Drums. Born 1977-11-20.**

Daniel Svensson is a Swedish drummer best known for his long tenure as the drummer of [[Artists/I/In Flames]] and his work in [[Artists/T/The Halo Effect]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/In Flames]] – drums (1998–2015)
- [[Artists/T/The Halo Effect]] – drums, founding member
- [[Artists/S/Sacrilege GBG]] – drums, vocals (founding member, 1993, rejoined 2006)
- [[Artists/D/Diabolique]] – former drummer

## Biography

Svensson co-founded the melodic death metal band Sacrilege at age 16 in 1993, then also played in Diabolique before joining In Flames in 1998 to fill the drum seat vacated by Björn Gelotte's move to guitar. He appeared on every In Flames album from *Colony* (1999) through *Siren Charms* (2014), left the band in 2015 to spend more time with his family, and later co-founded The Halo Effect, reuniting with fellow former In Flames members Niclas Engelin and Peter Iwers.
