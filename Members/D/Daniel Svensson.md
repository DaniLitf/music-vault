# Daniel Svensson

**Drums (1998–2015).**

Daniel Svensson is a Swedish drummer best known for his long tenure as the drummer of [[Artists/I/In Flames]] and his work in [[Artists/T/The Halo Effect]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/In Flames]] – drums (1998–2015)
- [[Artists/T/The Halo Effect]] – drums, founding member
- [[Sacrilige]] – former drummer
- [[Diabolique]] – former drummer

## Biography

Svensson joined In Flames in 1998 and appeared on every album from *Colony* (1999) through *Siren Charms* (2014). He left the band in 2015 to focus on family life. He later co-founded The Halo Effect, reuniting with fellow former In Flames members Niclas Engelin and Peter Iwers.
