# Dave Sitek

**Dave Sitek** (David Andrew Sitek) is an American musician, songwriter, and record producer best known as a member of [[TV on the Radio]] and former bassist of [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]].

- [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]] – bass (2011; studio only)
- [[TV on the Radio]] – guitar, keyboards, production (2001–present)

Sitek played bass on and co-wrote Jane's Addiction's fourth album *The Great Escape Artist* (2011), though he did not tour with the band.
