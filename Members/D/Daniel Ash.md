# Daniel Ash

**Daniel Ash** (born July 31, 1957) is an English musician, singer, and songwriter best known as a member of [[Bauhaus (band)|Bauhaus]], [[Love and Rockets (band)|Love and Rockets]], and for filling in with [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]].

- [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]] – additional guitar (2022; fill-in)
- [[Bauhaus (band)|Bauhaus]] – guitar, saxophone (1978–1983, 1998, 2005–2008, 2019–2022)
- [[Love and Rockets (band)|Love and Rockets]] – guitar, vocals (1985–1999, 2007–2009)
