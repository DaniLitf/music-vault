# Dicky Moore

**Bassist. British.**

Dicky Moore (also known as Richard Moore) is a Welsh bassist who has been the bassist for Extreme Noise Terror since 2019. He also plays bass in Desecration alongside Ollie Jones and Michael Hourihan.
