# David Wallin

**Drummer, pyrotechnician. Swedish.**

David Wallin is a Swedish freelance drummer and pyrotechnician known for his work as a touring and session member with Pain, the Swedish industrial metal band, and as the drummer for HammerFall, a Swedish heavy metal band. He has been involved in numerous touring sessions and is recognized for his technical drumming abilities. In 2008, he suffered a serious assault while on tour with Pain supporting Nightwish in Leipzig, Germany.
