**Drummer. American. Born July 30, 1956.**

Jimmy Michael "Don Bolles" Giorsetti is an American drummer and musician best known for his work with the influential punk rock band The Germs during their classic lineup (1978–1980). A Phoenix native who played in several local bands before joining The Germs, Bolles later became a key collaborator with experimental and art rock acts. He has co-authored a definitive history of The Germs and remains active in Los Angeles's music and cultural scene as both a musician and DJ.

## Biography

Born in Phoenix, Arizona, Don Bolles played in several local punk and garage rock bands in the late 1970s before joining The Germs' classic lineup in 1978 as their drummer. His tenure with The Germs (1978–1980) coincided with their most productive and influential period, including the recording of their landmark album "GI" (1979). After The Germs initially disbanded, Bolles played with other West Coast punk acts including Nervous Gender and 45 Grave. In the 2000s, he reunited with The Germs for live performances and has become an important part of contemporary experimental music, recording and touring with Ariel Pink and other avant-garde artists. Bolles co-authored "Lexicon Devil: The Fast Times and Short Life of Darby Crash and the Germs" (2002), a definitive oral history of the band.
