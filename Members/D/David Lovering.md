# David Lovering

**Drummer. American. Born 1961-12-06.**

David Lovering is the drummer of The Pixies, having been instrumental in the band's original formation in 1986 and playing on all their classic albums before the group's breakup in 1992. After an initial hiatus, he rejoined The Pixies for their 2004 reunion and has remained with the band ever since. Beyond drumming, Lovering is a skilled magician and performer, known as "The Scientific Phenomenalist," who combines physics demonstrations with stage magic and has performed at The Magic Castle in Los Angeles. He is also an accomplished musician on multiple instruments and has worked with numerous other bands including Nitzer Ebb and The Martinis.
