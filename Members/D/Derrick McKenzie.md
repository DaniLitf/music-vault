# Derrick McKenzie

**Drummer. British.**

Derrick McKenzie is the drummer for Jamiroquai, the British funk and acid jazz band. He joined the group in 1993 and has been a core member throughout the band's most successful periods, performing on numerous platinum albums including *Travelling Without Moving* (1996) and *Synkronized* (1999). McKenzie's energetic and precise drumming has been integral to Jamiroquai's distinctive sound and live performances.
