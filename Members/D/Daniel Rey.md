# Daniel Rey

**Guitarist, producer, songwriter. American.**

Daniel Rey (born Daniel Rabinowitz) is an American guitarist, record producer, and songwriter from New York City. As a teenager he played guitar in the punk band Shrapnel, which also featured Dave Wyndorf of Monster Magnet. He later became the guitarist for Manitoba's Wild Kingdom with Handsome Dick Manitoba and Andy Shernoff of The Dictators. Rey is best known for his extensive production and songwriting work with the Ramones, co-writing songs like "Pet Sematary" and "Daytime Dilemma (Dangers of Love)."
