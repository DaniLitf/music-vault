# Dave Hemingway

**Dave Hemingway is an English singer born 1960, best known as a member of [[Artists/T/The Housemartins]] and co-founder of [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]].**

Hemingway joined The Housemartins as a drummer and occasional vocalist. When the band split, he co-founded The Beautiful South with Paul Heaton, becoming co-lead vocalist.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Housemartins]] – drums, backing vocals (1983–1988)
- [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]] – lead vocals, co-founder (1988–2007)
