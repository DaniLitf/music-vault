# Glen Buxton

**Lead guitarist and founding member of the Alice Cooper band. Born 1947-11-10, died 1997-10-19.**

Glen Buxton was an American guitarist best known as the lead guitarist and co-founder of the Alice Cooper band. He was a key songwriter on the band's early albums, co-writing classics such as "I'm Eighteen" and "School's Out." His aggressive, noisy guitar style was a defining element of the band's hard rock sound.

Buxton struggled with substance abuse throughout his career and was largely absent from the band's final album *Muscle of Love* (1973). He died of pneumonia in 1997 at age 49. The original Alice Cooper band was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2011, with Buxton honored posthumously. His guitar parts were also used posthumously on the 2025 reunion album *The Revenge of Alice Cooper*.
