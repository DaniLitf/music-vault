# Gil Sharone

**Drums. American. Former member of [[Artists/T/The Dillinger Escape Plan]].**

Gil Sharone, previously of Stolen Babies, replaced Chris Pennie as The Dillinger Escape Plan's drummer for *Ire Works* (2007), recommended to the band by Chris Hornbrook over more established candidates. He left in January 2009, replaced by Billy Rymer. In 2006–2007 he filled in on drums for [[Artists/0-9/+44]] while Travis Barker recovered from a broken arm.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Dillinger Escape Plan]] – drums (2007–2009)
- [[Artists/0-9/+44]] – drums, percussion (2006–2007, touring fill-in)
