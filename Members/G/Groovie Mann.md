# Groovie Mann

**Vocalist and co-founder of My Life With the Thrill Kill Kult. American.**

Frankie Nardiello, known professionally as Groovie Mann, is an American vocalist and the co-founder of the industrial band My Life With the Thrill Kill Kult. In 1987, he met producer Buzz McCoy while touring with Ministry, and together they formed TKK, which became one of the pioneering acts in industrial music throughout the late 1980s and 1990s. Mann's distinctive vocal style and provocative stage presence helped define the band's theatrical approach to electronic and dance music.
