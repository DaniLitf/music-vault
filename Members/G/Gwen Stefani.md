# Gwen Stefani

**American singer and songwriter. Born 3 October 1969.**

Gwen Stefani is an American singer, songwriter, and fashion designer best known as the lead vocalist of No Doubt. She launched a successful solo career in 2004 and is known for hits like "Hollaback Girl" and "Rich Girl."
