# Greg Saenz

**Drums. American. Former member of [[Artists/E/Excel]]; later co-founded [[Artists/M/My Head]].**

Greg Saenz drummed for Excel during the late-1980s/early-1990s era, and went on to co-found the grunge/alternative rock trio My Head with [[Members/A/Adam Siegel]] after both left Excel in 1993.
