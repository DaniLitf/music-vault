# Greg Isabelle

**Drummer, vocalist. Born in USA.**

Greg Isabelle is an American drummer and a childhood friend of the Borland brothers. He was a member of the comedic metal project [[Artists/B/Big Dumb Face]] during its original run, playing drums and contributing vocals.

## Associated Artists
