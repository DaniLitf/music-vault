**Guitarist and founding member of Screaming Trees. Born 22 August 1962.**

Gary Lee Conner is an American rock guitarist best known as a founding member and lead guitarist of Screaming Trees, the pioneering grunge band from Ellensburg, Washington. He formed the band with his brother Van Conner, Mark Pickerel, and vocalist Mark Lanegan in 1985, playing on seven studio albums until the band's split in 2000. Screaming Trees blended grunge with hard rock and psychedelic elements, becoming influential figures in the Seattle sound. Since 2010, Conner has issued regular solo albums under his own name.
