# Glenn Tipton

**Guitarist. Born October 25, 1947.**

Glenn Tipton is a British guitarist who has been a member of Judas Priest since 1974, co-writing many of the band's classic songs alongside K.K. Downing. He was diagnosed with Parkinson's disease in 2008, which has limited his ability to tour since 2018, though he continues to contribute to songwriting and studio recordings.
