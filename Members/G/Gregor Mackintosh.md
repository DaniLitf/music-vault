# Gregor Mackintosh

**Guitarist. Born 1970-06-20 in Halifax, England.**

Gregor Mackintosh is a founding guitarist of Paradise Lost, responsible for much of the band's signature melodic lead guitar work and a major architect of the gothic doom metal sound. He has been with the band since 1988 and has co-written the bulk of their material. He is also known as the sole member of Vallenfyre, his death/doom metal project, and has collaborated with Moonspell and other European metal acts.
