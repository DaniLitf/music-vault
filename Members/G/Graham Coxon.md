**Guitarist, singer, and songwriter. Born 12 March 1969.**

Graham Coxon is an English guitarist, singer and songwriter best known as a founding member and lead guitarist of the British rock band Blur. Born in West Germany where his father was stationed with the British Army, Coxon grew up in Colchester, Essex and met fellow Blur co-founder Damon Albarn at school at age 11. As Blur's lead guitarist and secondary vocalist, Coxon was essential to the band's distinctive guitar-driven sound during their rise in the 1990s and beyond. Noel Gallagher of Oasis has praised him as "one of the most talented guitarists of his generation," and he was voted the 15th greatest guitarist of the last 30 years in a 2010 BBC poll. Since 1998, Coxon has simultaneously pursued a prolific solo career where he writes, produces and plays all instruments.
