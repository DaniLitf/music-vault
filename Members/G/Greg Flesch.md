# Greg Flesch

**Guitarist. American.**

Greg Flesch is an American guitarist and multi-instrumentalist known for his work with the Lost Dogs and The Swirling Eddies, two Christian rock supergroups. He has contributed guitar, steel guitar, and other stringed instruments to numerous recordings in the Christian rock scene since the early 1990s.
