# Geir Bratland

**Norwegian keyboardist, known for Apoptygma Berzerk and Dimmu Borgir. Born 29 July 1970.**

Geir Bratland was born in Narvik, Norway, and began his career as a keyboardist in the black metal scene, appearing on Satyricon's *Nemesis Divina* (1996). He joined Apoptygma Berzerk in 1995, contributing to albums from *7* through *Rocket Science*. In 2009 he left APB to join Dimmu Borgir as their full-time keyboardist, and has also performed with The Kovenant, God Seed, and Emperor.
