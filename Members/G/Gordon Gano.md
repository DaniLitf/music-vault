# Gordon Gano

**Vocals, guitar. American. Born 1963-06-21.**

Gordon Gano is an American musician, best known as the singer, guitarist, and songwriter of folk-punk band [[Artists/V/Violent Femmes]]. He wrote lyrics for and sang lead vocals on "Only the Lonely" on [[Artists/T/The Heads]]' *No Talking, Just Head* (1996).

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/V/Violent Femmes]] – vocals, guitar, co-founder
- [[Artists/T/The Heads]] – guest vocals (1996)
