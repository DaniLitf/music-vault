# Greg Graffin

**Vocalist. Born 1966-11-06.**

Greg Graffin is the lead vocalist and primary lyricist of Bad Religion, a founding member who has remained with the band continuously since 1980. He holds a PhD in evolutionary biology and is known for his intellectually sophisticated punk rock lyrics. Graffin was also the lead vocalist of Bad Religion's various side projects and solo work.
