# Gene Trautmann

**Drummer. American.**

Gene Trautmann is an American drummer born and raised in Portland, Oregon, best known for his work with [[Artists/Q/Queens of the Stone Age]]. He joined Queens of the Stone Age in June 1999 and contributed drums to four tracks on their 2000 album *Rated R* and five B-side tracks before departing in September 2001. He also featured on two tracks on the band's 2002 album *Songs for the Deaf*. Beyond Queens of the Stone Age, Trautmann has worked with rock bands Dig and the Miracle Workers and as a touring member of [[Artists/E/Eagles of Death Metal]].
