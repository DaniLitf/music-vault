# Gabriel Coulon

**Producer, composer.**

Gabriel Coulon is a French electronic musician who records and performs as Ruby My Dear. Working in breakcore and related electronic styles, he has been active since the late 2000s and is known for his intricate, chaotic rhythmic constructions.
