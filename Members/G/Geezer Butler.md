**Bassist and primary lyricist of Black Sabbath. Born 17 July 1949.**

Terence "Geezer" Butler is an English bassist and lyricist best known as a founding member of Black Sabbath, one of the pioneering heavy metal bands. Largely self-taught on bass, Butler became the primary lyricist of Sabbath and is regarded as one of the most influential bassists in heavy metal history. His dark, socially conscious lyrics and heavy, syncopated bass lines were crucial to defining the band's sound across albums like Paranoid, Master of Reality, and Sabotage. Despite previously ruling out further reunions, Butler reunited with the original Black Sabbath lineup in July 2025 for a final show at Back to the Beginning.
