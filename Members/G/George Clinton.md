# George Clinton

**Bandleader, vocals, songwriter, producer. American. Founder of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

George Clinton formed the doo-wop group the Parliaments in New Jersey in the late 1950s, and by the late 1960s had built it into the twin bands Parliament and Funkadelic, touring and recording together as the Parliament-Funkadelic collective. As bandleader, chief songwriter, and mythmaker, he developed the P-Funk sound and its Afrofuturist "Mothership" mythology, and continued leading rotating lineups of the collective (including as George Clinton and the P-Funk All Stars) into the 2000s. He also produced albums for other artists, including Red Hot Chili Peppers' *Freaky Styley* (1985).
