# Graham Massey

**Graham Massey is a British musician and producer from Manchester, a co-founder of 808 State and previously a member of avant-garde post-punk group Biting Tongues.**

He has been a central figure in Manchester's electronic scene since the late 1970s and is the only member to appear on every 808 State release. He has also collaborated with Björk (co-writing tracks on *Post*) and released material under the solo alias Massonix.
