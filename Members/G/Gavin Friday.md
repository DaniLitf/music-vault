# Gavin Friday

**Vocals. Irish. Born 1959-10-08.**

Gavin Friday is an Irish singer, songwriter, and actor, best known as a founding member of post-punk band [[Artists/V/Virgin Prunes]]. He provided lead vocals for "Blue Blue Moon" on [[Artists/T/The Heads]]' *No Talking, Just Head* (1996).

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/V/Virgin Prunes]] – lead vocals, co-founder
- [[Artists/T/The Heads]] – guest vocals (1996)
