# Gunnar Ljungstedt

**Drums. Swedish. Founding member of [[Artists/E/Ebba Grön]] and [[Artists/I/Imperiet]].**

Gunnar Ljungstedt (nicknamed Gurra) co-founded Ebba Grön in 1977 and continued as its drummer through the band's 1983 breakup, then followed Thåström into Rymdimperiet/Imperiet, leaving in 1985.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/E/Ebba Grön]] – drums (1977–1983, founder)
