# Garry Maloney

**Drummer. British.**

Garry Maloney was the drummer for Discharge from 1981 to 1984, playing on the classic *Never Again* EP, *Hear Nothing See Nothing Say Nothing* album, and the "State Violence State Control" single. He left in 1984 along with guitarist Pooch.
