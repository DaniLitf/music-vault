# Gregg Bissonette

**Drummer. Born June 9, 1959.**

Gregg Bissonette is an American drummer who has played and recorded with a wide range of artists including David Lee Roth (1985–1988), Joe Satriani, Ringo Starr, Steve Vai, and Spinal Tap. He was a core member of David Lee Roth's solo band, appearing on the albums *Eat 'Em and Smile* (1986) and *Skyscraper* (1988).
