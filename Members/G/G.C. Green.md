**Bassist and vocalist. Founding member of Godflesh.**

G.C. Green is the bassist and founding member of the British industrial metal band Godflesh, co-founding the project with Justin Broadrick. Throughout most of Godflesh's career, the band operated as a duo with preprogrammed percussion, though they later collaborated with session drummers including Bryan Mantia and Ted Parsons. Green has been instrumental in shaping Godflesh's industrial metal sound alongside Broadrick's guitar and production work.
