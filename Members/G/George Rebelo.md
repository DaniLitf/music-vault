**Drummer. American.**

George Rebelo is the drummer of Hot Water Music and one of the band's four core founding members. He initially met Jason Black while playing together in jazz bands in school in Sarasota, Florida. The two attended the University of Florida together, and Rebelo was part of the group that eventually moved to Gainesville to form Hot Water Music in 1994. Beyond Hot Water Music, Rebelo has played with Against Me! (2009–2010) and has been a member of The Bouncing Souls since 2013, appearing on their 2016 album Simplicity.
