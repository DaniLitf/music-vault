# Geoff Downes

**British keyboardist, songwriter, and producer. Born 25 August 1952.**

Geoffrey Downes is a British keyboardist, songwriter, and producer best known as the keyboardist of progressive rock band Asia and former member of The Buggles and Yes. He co-wrote many of Asia's biggest hits including "Heat of the Moment" and "Only Time Will Tell."
