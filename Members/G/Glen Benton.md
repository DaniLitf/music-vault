# Glen Benton

**American bassist and vocalist. Born 10 June 1967.**

Glen Benton is an American bassist and vocalist best known as the frontman and bassist of the death metal band Deicide. He is known for his controversial anti-religious lyrics and aggressive vocal style, and has been the band's sole constant member since its formation in 1987.
