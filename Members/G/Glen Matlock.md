**Bassist and songwriter. Co-founder of The Sex Pistols. Born 27 August 1956.**

Glen Matlock is an English bassist and co-founder of the seminal punk rock band The Sex Pistols. Introduced to guitarist Steve Jones and drummer Paul Cook through Malcolm McLaren's Sex boutique in London, Matlock was the original bassist and a key songwriter of the band. He is credited as co-writer on 10 of the 12 songs on the landmark Never Mind the Bollocks, Here's the Sex Pistols. Despite his strong melodic sense, skilled musicianship, and songwriting contributions, Matlock was controversially fired in February 1977 and replaced by the musically inexperienced Sid Vicious. He has rejoined the original Sex Pistols members for reunion tours and continued his solo and collaborative work.
