# Gary Owens

**Voice actor, announcer. Born 1934-05-10; died 2015-02-12.**

Gary Owens was an American radio announcer and voice actor best known for his deadpan announcing style and work on *Rowan & Martin's Laugh-In*. He appeared as a guest on [[Artists/T/The Dwarves]]' 2004 album *The Dwarves Must Die*.
