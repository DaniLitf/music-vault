# Greg Perry

**Guitarist. American.**

Greg Perry is an American guitarist and a founding member of the Cleveland thrash/power metal band Purgatory, playing lead guitar on the band's early releases. He returned for the 2003 and 2018 reunions, and has also played in Attaxe, Zyklóne, Chemikill, and Victor Fist.
