# Gabba

**Guitarist. British.**

Gabba is a British punk guitarist best known as the guitarist of Chaos UK from 1983 to 1989, playing on the classic albums *Short Sharp Shock*, *Earslaughter*, and *The Chipping Sodbury Bonfire Tapes*. Previously of The Seats of Piss from Nottingham, he later formed the band FUK. He died in March 2026.
