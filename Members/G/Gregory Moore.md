# Gregory Moore

**Drums.**

Gregory Moore is an American drummer from Georgia, sometimes credited as Gregory Amoore or simply "Drummer," who has recorded and toured extensively with Greg Ginn's solo material and side projects Gone, El Bad, and Get Me High. He drummed for [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] at its 2003 reunion show, on its 2013 tour, and on the album *What The...* (2013).
