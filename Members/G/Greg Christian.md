**Bassist and founding member of Testament. Born 29 April 1966.**

Greg Christian is an American bassist best known as a founding member of the thrash metal band Testament. He is recognized for his distinctive finger-playing style and jazz-influenced bass parts, which helped define Testament's sound on eight studio albums including The Legacy (1987), The New Order (1988), Practice What You Preach (1989), and others. Christian left Testament in 1996 but rejoined in 2004, departing again in early 2014 due to financial disagreements with the band's controlling members. Beyond Testament, he has also played in HavocHate, Trinity Fallen, and Trauma. His playing style and technical contributions were integral to Testament's development as one of thrash metal's most respected bands.
