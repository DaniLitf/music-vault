# Greg Kurstin

**Multi-instrumentalist, songwriter, record producer. Born 1969-05-14.**

Greg Kurstin is an American multi-instrumentalist, songwriter, and record producer from Los Angeles. He began his career as a jazz pianist and co-founded the band Geggy Tah, which released three albums on David Byrne's Luaka Bop label. In 2004 he formed the indie pop duo The Bird and the Bee with singer Inara George. As a session musician he has played with Beck, Ben Harper, the Red Hot Chili Peppers, and many others, while his production work has earned him nine Grammy Awards.
