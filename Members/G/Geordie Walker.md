# Geordie Walker

**Geordie Walker was a British guitarist born December 18, 1958, died November 26, 2023, and a founding member of [[Artists/K/Killing Joke]].**

Known for his distinctive, chorus-heavy guitar sound, he performed on Pigface recordings as a guest.

- [[Artists/P/Pigface]] – guitar (guest)
