# Gene Hoglan

**Gene Hoglan is an American drummer known as "The Atomic Clock" for his precision, who has played for Death, Dark Angel, Testament, Strapping Young Lad, and Fear Factory, making him one of the most recorded drummers in extreme metal history.**

He played on Death's *Individual Thought Patterns* (1993) and *Symbolic* (1995), and has been associated with Testament at various points in their career.
