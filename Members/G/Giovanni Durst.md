# Giovanni Durst

**Drummer. Italian.**

Giovanni "Gio" Durst is the drummer of Benediction, joining the band in 2019. He has played on the albums *Scriptures* (2020) and *Ravage of Empires* (2025). Durst has also performed with White Wizzard and Crystal Sun.
