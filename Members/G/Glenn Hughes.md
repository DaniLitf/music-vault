**Bassist, vocalist, and guitarist. Born 21 August 1951.**

Glenn Hughes is an English musician renowned for his funk-inspired bass playing and soulful, wide-ranging countertenor vocal range. He is best known as the bassist and co-vocalist for hard rock legends Deep Purple (1973–1976), appearing on the classic albums Burn (1974), Stormbringer (1974), and Come Taste The Band (1975). Hughes shared lead vocal duties with David Coverdale during this period. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame as a member of Deep Purple in 2016. Beyond Deep Purple, he has maintained a prolific solo career, briefly fronted Black Sabbath in the mid-1980s, led the supergroup Black Country Communion (2010–present), and joined The Dead Daisies as bassist and vocalist in 2019.
