# Greg Garbowsky

**Bassist. Born September 10, 1986 in South Plainfield, New Jersey.**

Greg Garbowsky is an American musician best known as the longtime bass guitarist for the Jonas Brothers' touring band from 2005 to 2013. He was recommended for the role after playing at their Evangel Church and joined the band during their early Columbia Records days. He has also performed with Stevie Wonder, Taylor Swift, Brad Paisley, and Miley Cyrus, and co-wrote songs with Nick Jonas for the Nick Jonas and the Administration project.
