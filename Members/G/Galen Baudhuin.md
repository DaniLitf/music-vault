# Galen Baudhuin

**Bassist. American.**

Galen Baudhuin, also of the band Infera Bruo, became Trap Them's bassist in 2013, recording the band's final two albums *Blissfucker* (2014) and *Crown Feral* (2016) before the band's 2017 breakup.
