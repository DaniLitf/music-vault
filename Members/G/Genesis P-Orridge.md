# Genesis P-Orridge

**Singer, bassist, and performance artist. Born 22 February 1950 in Manchester, England. Died 14 March 2020 in New York City.**

Genesis P-Orridge was a foundational figure in industrial and experimental music, co-founding Throbbing Gristle in 1975 and later founding Psychic TV in 1981. They contributed vocals to Pigface as a guest collaborator, and were a central influence on the development of industrial music.
