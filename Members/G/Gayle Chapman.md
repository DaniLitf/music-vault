# Gayle Chapman

**Keyboardist and vocalist. Born in the United States.**

Gayle Chapman is an American musician who performed keyboards and backing vocals for Prince's band from 1979 to 1982. She was a member during the formative years of the band that would become The Revolution, appearing on the *Dirty Mind* and *Controversy* tours before leaving the music industry.
