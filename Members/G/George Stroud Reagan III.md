# George Stroud Reagan III

**Vocalist. American.**

George Stroud Reagan III is the lead vocalist of the Dallas punk band [[Artists/H/Hagfish|Hagfish]], joining in the early 1990s and singing on nearly all of their recorded work from 1993 onward. After Hagfish disbanded in 2001, he relocated focus to his family and briefly pursued hip hop with a homegrown group called The Brothers. In recent years, Reagan has been active in art-punk band La Glissière and maintains the blog "Hagfish is Dead," covering music recommendations and videos.
