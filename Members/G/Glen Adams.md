# Glen Adams

**Keyboardist, producer, and arranger. Born 1945-11-27, died 2010-12-17.**

Glen Adams was a Jamaican musician, composer, arranger, and producer who took up keyboards almost by accident at a 1968 session and went on to become a defining organ sound of the Upsetters under Lee "Scratch" Perry. He later relocated to Brooklyn, New York, where he ran his own Capo label and formed the band Blue Grass Experience while continuing to work with Perry as an arranger and keyboard player.
