# Gareth Hansome

**DJ, producer. British.**

Gareth Hansome, also known as GMoney, was Norman Cook's former flatmate and the other credited member of [[Artists/T/The Mighty Dub Katz]]. He is documented on the project's best-known single, "Magic Carpet Ride," alongside Cook.
