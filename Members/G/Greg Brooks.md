# Greg Brooks

**Dancer and backing vocalist.**

Greg Brooks is an American dancer and vocalist who performed with Prince's backing band The Revolution as a dancer and backing vocalist from 1980 to 1986. He was part of the on-stage dance troupe that accompanied The Revolution during Prince's live performances.
