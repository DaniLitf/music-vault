**Session musician and percussionist. American.**

Garrett Krinsky was a session musician for Hazen Street, providing tambourine and backing vocals during the band's early formation and recording period. He contributed to the distinctive sound of the band's self-titled debut album in 2004, bringing additional percussion textures to their rap rock and hardcore fusion sound.
