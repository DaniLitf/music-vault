# Ginger Wildheart

**Guitarist and vocalist. Born December 17, 1964.**

Ginger Wildheart is an English rock guitarist, singer, and songwriter, best known as the founder and frontman of The Wildhearts. He was briefly a member of Brides of Destruction in 2005 and has released numerous solo albums.
