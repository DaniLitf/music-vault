# Garry Shider

**Vocals, guitar. American. Longtime member of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Garry Shider was recruited into Funkadelic in 1972 by George Clinton, having grown up around Clinton's barbershop rehearsal space. He became a frequent lead vocalist across Parliament and Funkadelic albums, known for his gospel-inflected vocal and guitar style and for performing in a trademark towel "diaper," earning him the nickname "Diaper Man." He died in 2010.
