**Music producer, composer, and multi-instrumentalist. Born 9 December 1971.**

Geoff Barrow is an English music producer, composer, and DJ best known as a founding member of the trip hop band Portishead. Alongside singer Beth Gibbons and guitarist Adrian Utley, Barrow was instrumental in developing Portishead's distinctive sound on their landmark 1994 debut album Dummy, playing multiple instruments and handling much of the production work. His contributions to Portishead's downtempo, atmospheric sound made him a key figure in the trip hop genre's emergence in the 1990s. Beyond Portishead, Barrow has pursued various production and compositional work, including film scores and experimental electronic projects.
