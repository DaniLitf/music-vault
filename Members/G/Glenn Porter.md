# Glenn Porter

**Drummer, percussionist. American.**

Glenn Porter is a drummer from Chicago who co-founded [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio]] in 1996 with fellow bike messengers Matt Skiba and Rob Doran, drumming on the band's first two studio albums, *Goddamnit* (1998) and *Maybe I'll Catch Fire* (2000), before leaving in 2000. Prior to Alkaline Trio he drummed for [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]], appearing on their album *Behind Bars* (1995).
