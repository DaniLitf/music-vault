**Electronic musician and composer. Born 16 May 1967.**

Garry Cobain is an English electronic musician best known as one half of The Future Sound of London (FSOL), a boundary-pushing electronic duo formed with Brian Dougans at Manchester University in 1985. The duo achieved commercial success with singles like "Papua New Guinea" (1991) and "Cascade" (1993), and albums including Lifeforms (1994) and Dead Cities (1996). FSOL has explored techno, ambient, house, trip hop, psychedelia, and dub across their prolific career.
