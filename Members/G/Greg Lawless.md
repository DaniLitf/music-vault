# Greg Lawless

**Guitarist. American.**

Greg Lawless is an American guitarist best known as the lead guitarist for the alternative rock band Adam Again. He was a founding member of the band, having previously played with Gene Eugene and Paul Valadez in Martus, the precursor to Adam Again. His guitar work, ranging from funky rhythms to aggressive distorted leads, was a key component of Adam Again's sound across all five studio albums.
