# Gary Holt

**Guitarist. Born 1964-08-03 in Richmond, California, USA.**

Gary Holt is the co-founder and primary guitarist of Exodus, one of the original San Francisco Bay Area thrash metal bands. He began touring with Slayer in 2011 as a fill-in during Jeff Hanneman's illness, and after Hanneman's death in 2013 became Slayer's full guitarist through the band's final album *Repentless* (2015) and their farewell tour (2019), while continuing to perform with Exodus.
