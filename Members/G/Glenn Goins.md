# Glenn Goins

**Vocals, guitar. American. Former member of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Glenn Goins was recruited into Parliament-Funkadelic in 1975, known alongside Garry Shider for his gospel-style singing and guitar playing. In 1978 he departed acrimoniously with drummer Jerome Brailey to form the band Quazar with his younger brother Kevin Goins, but died of Hodgkin's lymphoma shortly after, at age 24.
