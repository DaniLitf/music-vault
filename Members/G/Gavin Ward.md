**Guitarist and lyricist. Founding member of Bolt Thrower.**

Gavin Ward is one of the two founding members of the British death metal band Bolt Thrower, formed in 1986. He initially played bass on the band's first demo before switching to guitar for the second demo and all subsequent releases. Ward has remained with the band throughout their career and contributes as both a guitarist and one of the primary lyricists, helping to develop Bolt Thrower's distinctive sound and war-themed conceptual approach to death metal.
