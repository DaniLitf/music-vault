# Gabi Delgado-López

**Vocalist, lyricist. Born May 18, 1958 in Germany — died March 22, 2020.**

Gabriel "Gabi" Delgado-López was a German vocalist best known as the co-founder and frontman of DAF. His distinctive shouted/spoken vocal delivery in German, combined with aggressive, sexually charged lyrics, became a hallmark of the band's sound. He also released solo work as DAF/DOS and collaborated with various electronic artists.
