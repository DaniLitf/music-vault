# Gautier Serre

**Multi-instrumentalist, producer, composer. Born June 5, 1984.**

Gautier Serre is a French musician and producer, best known as the creator and sole constant member of the experimental project [[Artists/I/Igorrr]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Igorrr]] – guitar, programming, vocals (2005–present)
- [[Artists/W/Whourkr]] – all instruments, vocals (2006–2014)
- [[Artists/C/Corpo-Mente]] – all instruments, duet with Laure Le Prunenec (2015–present)

## Biography

Serre started the Igorrr project in 2005, self-releasing demo albums *Poisson Soluble* (2006) and *Moisissure* (2008). After signing to Ad Noiseam, his debut album *Nostril* (2010) brought him international attention. He has cited Meshuggah, Chopin, Cannibal Corpse, Bach, Domenico Scarlatti, Taraf de Haïdouks, and Aphex Twin as influences.

Alongside Igorrr, Serre co-founded the grindcore/breakcore project Whourkr with vocalist Laurent Lunoir, and the baroque/experimental duo Corpo-Mente with vocalist Laure Le Prunenec.
