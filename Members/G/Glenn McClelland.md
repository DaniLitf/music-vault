**Keyboardist. Ween keyboardist since 1996.**

Glenn McClelland is an American keyboardist best known as the keyboardist for the experimental rock/comedy band Ween since 1996. He began playing professionally at age 16 in a piano bar and subsequently played with notable blues and jazz musicians including Sonny Rhodes, Johnny Copeland, and Richie Cole. McClelland spent eight years (1987–1995) as a keyboardist for Blood Sweat & Tears before joining Ween. According to Dean Ween, they met at a bar in 1996 where Dean invited him to perform, leading to McClelland joining the band. He has been a core member since, contributing keyboard arrangements to the band's prolific and eclectic catalog.
