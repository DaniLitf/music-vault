# Greg Puciato

**Vocals. American. Former lead vocalist of [[Artists/T/The Dillinger Escape Plan]].**

Greg Puciato joined The Dillinger Escape Plan in late 2001, chosen from over a hundred singers who submitted audition recordings, and fronted the band from *Miss Machine* (2004) through its 2017 breakup. After DEP dissolved he continued with his electronic band The Black Queen and the supergroup Killer Be Killed.
