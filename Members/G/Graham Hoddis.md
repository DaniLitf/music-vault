# Graham "Nutty Strangeways" Hoddis

**Vocalist. British.**

Graham "Nutty Strangeways" Hoddis was a vocalist for deathgrind band Gorerotted from 1998 to 1999, joining from local Walthamstow/Leytonstone death metal band Apostasy and departing because he was unhappy performing gore-themed lyrics. He later reunited with fellow original Gorerotted vocalist Jason "Mr Gore" Merle in the project Gorebound.
