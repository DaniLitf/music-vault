# Geoff Pinckney

**British keyboardist, former live member of Mesh and member of Tenek.**

Geoff Pinckney joined Mesh as a live keyboardist in 2006 following Neil Taylor's departure, initially replacing him at live shows. He performed with the band until 2011, when he left to focus on his own musical project, Tenek.
