# Guillermo Amador

**Bassist.**

Guillermo Amador is an American bass guitarist who joined the rebuilt [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] line-up in late 2000 and appears on the single *Morningleaver / This Is Me Hating You* (2001). He was replaced by Thomas Rankine in April 2001, before the recording of *Won*.
