# Greg Lake

**British singer, guitarist, and bassist. Born 10 November 1946, died 7 December 2016.**

Gregory Stuart Lake was a British musician best known as the bassist and vocalist of progressive rock bands King Crimson (1968–1970) and Emerson, Lake & Palmer (1970–2010). He was known for his rich baritone voice and melodic bass playing.
