# Greg Falzone

**Drummer. American.**

Greg Falzone was the drummer for the Las Vegas grindcore band Righteous Pigs from 1988 to 1989. He played on the band's debut album *Live and Learn* (1989). He also played in the bands Lethal Injection and Eliminate.
