# Gladstone "Gladdy" Anderson

**Pianist and bandleader. Born 1934-06-18, died 2015-12-03.**

Gladstone Anderson was a Jamaican pianist and session musician who played a key role in defining the ska and rocksteady sound, working at Duke Reid's Treasure Isle studio and as a member of Lynn Taitt's group The Jets. He led Gladdy's All-Stars, the studio band that later became The Upsetters when working with producer Lee "Scratch" Perry, and had solo success as a singer, notably as one half of Stranger and Gladdy with Stranger Cole.
