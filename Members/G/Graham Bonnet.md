**Rock and metal vocalist. Born 23 December 1947.**

Graham Bonnet is an English rock singer best known for his work as lead vocalist of Rainbow, the hard rock supergroup led by guitarist Ritchie Blackmore. Bonnet was chosen to replace Ronnie James Dio and sang on the landmark 1979 album Down to Earth, which spawned two hit singles: "Since You Been Gone" (reaching #5 on the UK chart) and "All Night Long". Though his tenure with Rainbow was brief, it marked a significant musical departure for Bonnet, shifting his focus from R&B to hard rock. Beyond Rainbow, he has had a successful solo career and has worked with numerous metal and hard rock projects including Michael Schenker Group, Alcatrazz, and Impellitteri.
