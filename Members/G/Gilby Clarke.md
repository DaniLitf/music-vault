**Guitarist and producer. Former Guns N' Roses rhythm guitarist.**

Gilby Clarke is an American guitarist, singer, songwriter and record producer best known for his three-year tenure as rhythm guitarist of Guns N' Roses (1991–1994), appearing on the Use Your Illusion Tour and the album "The Spaghetti Incident?" (1993). Clarke was also a founding member of Slash's Snakepit, Slash's post-Guns N' Roses project, alongside drummer Matt Sorum, Alice in Chains bassist Mike Inez, and vocalist Eric Dover. Their debut album It's Five O'Clock Somewhere was released in 1995. Beyond these high-profile projects, Clarke has maintained a solo career as a guitarist, singer and producer.
