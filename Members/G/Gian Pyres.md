**Guitarist. Former member of Cradle of Filth.**

Gian Pyres is an Italian guitarist best known for his work as second guitarist with the British symphonic black metal band Cradle of Filth. He joined during the recording of Dusk and Her Embrace and recorded guitars for four subsequent albums: Cruelty and the Beast (1998), Midian (2000), Bitter Suites to Succubi (2001), and Lovecraft & Witch Hearts (2002). Pyres contributed to some of Cradle of Filth's most celebrated works before leaving the band in August 2002.
