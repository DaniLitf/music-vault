# Gian Pyres

**Guitarist. Born 20 October 1973.**

Gian Pyres (born Gianpiero Giuseppe Piras) is an English guitarist best known for his work as second guitarist with the British symphonic black metal band Cradle of Filth. He joined during the recording of *Dusk... and Her Embrace* and recorded guitars for several subsequent albums before leaving the band in the early 2000s. He has also played lead guitar for Extreme Noise Terror and, as Gianpiero Piras, guitar for deathgrind band The Rotted (2007–2009), contributing to the album *Get Dead or Die Trying* (2008); he has additionally guested on releases by Bal-Sagoth, Christian Death, and Theatres des Vampires.
