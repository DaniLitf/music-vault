# Glenn Ljungström

**Guitars (1990–1997).**

Glenn Ljungström is a Swedish musician best known as the original guitarist and a founding member of [[Artists/I/In Flames]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/In Flames]] – guitars (1990–1997)
- [[Artists/D/Dimension Zero]] – former guitarist

## Biography

Ljungström co-founded In Flames in 1990 with Jesper Strömblad. He played on the band's first four releases, including *Lunar Strain* (1994), *Subterranean* (1995 EP), *The Jester Race* (1996), and *Whoracle* (1997), before leaving in 1997.
