# GosT

**Darksynth producer and multi-instrumentalist. Born March 16, 1980, died April 1, 2026.**

James Cody Lollar, known by the stage name GosT, was an American darksynth and synthwave producer from Longview, Texas, active from 2013 until his death in 2026. He was known for horror-influenced synthesizer compositions fused with heavy metal aesthetics across albums like *Behemoth* (2015) and *Possessor* (2018), and later contributed synth production to Frozen Soul's *Glacial Domination* (2023).
