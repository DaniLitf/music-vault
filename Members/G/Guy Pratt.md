# Guy Pratt

**Bass. Born 3 January 1962.**

Guy Pratt is an English session bassist, best known as [[Artists/P/Pink Floyd]]'s touring and session bassist from 1987 onward. His extensive session career includes work with Madonna, Michael Jackson and Icehouse, and he played bass on the December 1993 [[Artists/C/Coverdale-Page]] Japan tour.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/P/Pink Floyd]] – bass, touring and sessions (1987–present)
- [[Artists/C/Coverdale-Page]] – bass, Japan tour (1993)
