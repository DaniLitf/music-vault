# Grace Slick

**Grace Slick (born Grace Barnett Wing, October 30, 1939, in Highland Park, Illinois) is an American rock vocalist and songwriter, best known as the lead singer of Jefferson Airplane and Jefferson Starship.**

She joined Jefferson Airplane in 1966, replacing original vocalist Signe Toly Anderson, and became the iconic voice of the San Francisco psychedelic scene with her powerful mezzo-soprano. She sang lead on "Somebody to Love" and "White Rabbit" from *Surrealistic Pillow* (1967). She continued with the band through its evolution into Jefferson Starship and later Starship, retiring from music in 1989.
