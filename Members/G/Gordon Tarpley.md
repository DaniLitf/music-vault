# Gordon Tarpley

**Guitarist.**

Gordon Tarpley is an American guitarist who played in [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] from the band's June 1997 Gainesville reformation until mid-1998, appearing on *The Fists of Time* (1998). He briefly returned in 2002 to help the band fulfil touring obligations after Damien Moyal's departure.

## Associated Artists
