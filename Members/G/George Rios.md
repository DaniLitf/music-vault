# George Rios

**Drummer. Miami, Florida.**

George Rios is an American drummer from Miami, Florida, who played in the doom-sludge band [[Artists/F/Floor]] and the metalcore band Brethren before co-founding [[Artists/B/Bird of Ill Omen]] in 1996. He drummed on the band's debut *Self, Dare You Still Breathe?* and ran Askone Productions, the tape-dubbing operation that issued the band's early tour releases, before leaving in mid-1997.
