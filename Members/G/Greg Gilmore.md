# Greg Gilmore

**American drummer. Born 3 January 1962.**

Greg Gilmore is a French-born, Seattle-raised drummer who played in 10 Minute Warning and Skin Yard before joining Mother Love Bone in 1988. The band dissolved in 1990 after Andrew Wood's death, and Gilmore later co-founded the First World Music record label.
