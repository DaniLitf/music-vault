# Gerold Phelps

**Bassist. Born ?.**

Gerold Phelps, also known as Stiv, is an American bassist best known as a member of the Los Angeles punk band Wasted Youth. He played bass on the band's debut album *Reagan's In* (1981) and contributed to the early 1980s LA hardcore punk scene.
