# Gaahl

**Vocalist. Norwegian.**

Gaahl (Kristian Eivind Espedal) is a Norwegian vocalist best known as the longtime singer of black metal band Gorgoroth from 1998 to 2007. Following a legal dispute over the Gorgoroth name, he and bandmate King ov Hell formed God Seed in 2009, departing later that year before rejoining in 2012 to release *I Begin*.
