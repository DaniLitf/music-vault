# Gerald Simpson

**Keyboards, production. Born February 16, 1967.**

Gerald Simpson is an English electronic musician and producer, best known as a founding member of [[Artists/0-9/808 State]] and for his solo project [[Artists/A/A Guy Called Gerald]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/0-9/808 State]] – keyboards (1987–1989)
- [[Artists/A/A Guy Called Gerald]] – solo project (1988–present)

## Biography

Simpson co-founded 808 State in 1987 with Graham Massey and Martin Price. He left the group in 1989 to focus on his solo project A Guy Called Gerald, which produced the influential acid house track "Voodoo Ray". He is recognised as a pioneer of the UK acid house and jungle scenes.
