# Grady Thomas

**Vocals. American. Original member of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Grady Thomas started as bass vocalist for the Parliaments before moving to baritone, and (with Bernie Worrell) was responsible for recruiting drummer Jerome Brailey to the collective. After leaving P-Funk in 1977 with Fuzzy Haskins and Calvin Simon, he formed the Shady Bunch, later returning briefly to the P-Funk All Stars in the 1990s and co-founding and leading Original P with his former Parliaments bandmates.
