# Gizz Butt

**Touring guitarist. Born 1966-08-03 in Manchester, England.**

Gizz Butt is a British guitarist best known as The Prodigy's live guitarist through their most successful years in the late 1990s, joining after a recommendation to the band by a Kerrang! journalist. He is also the lead vocalist and guitarist of the punk band Janus Stark, and has played with English Dogs, Sabbat, and the German alternative metal band Pyogenesis.
