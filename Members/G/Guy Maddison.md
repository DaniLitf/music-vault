# Guy Maddison

**Australian bassist. Born 31 March 1965 in Perth.**

Guy Maddison is an Australian bassist who played in the noise rock band Lubricated Goat (1986–1989) and the blues-punk band Bloodloss with Mark Arm before joining Mudhoney in 2001, replacing Matt Lukin. He also works as a nurse and returned to Australia with his family in 2022.
