# Grant Estes

**Guitar. American. Original [[Artists/S/Suicidal Tendencies]] guitarist.**

Grant Estes played guitar in Suicidal Tendencies' earliest lineup and its offshoot Los Cycos. In 2006 he reunited with fellow Suicidal Tendencies/Los Cycos alumni to record as [[Artists/A/Against]] for the compilation *Welcome 2 Venice*.
