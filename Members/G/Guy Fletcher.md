# Guy Fletcher

**Keyboardist, guitarist, singer, songwriter. Born April 24, 1960 in London, England.**

Guy Fletcher is a British musician and record producer best known as a member of Dire Straits from 1984 to 1995, contributing keyboards, guitar, and backing vocals on the albums *Brothers in Arms* (1985) and *On Every Street* (1991). He has worked extensively as a session musician and producer for artists including Emmylou Harris, Randy Newman, and Mark Knopfler, and has released solo albums including *Inamorata* (2008).
