# Gina Mainwal

**American drummer and filmmaker.**

Gina Mainwal is an American musician and filmmaker from Las Vegas, best known as the drummer for Sweet 75, the band formed by Nirvana bassist Krist Novoselic. In December 1999 she drummed for the one-off protest supergroup No WTO Combo alongside Jello Biafra, Novoselic, and Kim Thayil, and has since made award-winning short documentary films.
