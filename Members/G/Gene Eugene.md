# Gene Eugene

**Keyboardist, producer, and songwriter. Born April 6, 1961.**

Gene Eugene, born Eugene Andrusco, was an American musician and producer who led the band Adam Again and was a member of the Lost Dogs and The Swirling Eddies (under the alias Prickly Disco). He produced several Starflyer 59 albums, from *Gold* (1995) through *Everybody Makes Mistakes* (1999), at his Green Room studio, and frequently contributed keyboards to their recordings. Earlier in life, he worked as a child actor appearing on the TV series *Bewitched*.
