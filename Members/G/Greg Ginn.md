# Greg Ginn

**Guitar. Born 1954-06-08.**

Greg Ginn is an American guitarist and record executive, the founder, primary songwriter, and sole constant member of [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] from its 1976 formation through its 1986 breakup and subsequent reunions in 2003, 2013–2014, and 2019–2025. He founded SST Records, growing it from his amateur radio mail-order business into one of the most influential American independent labels of the 1980s, home to Minutemen, Hüsker Dü, Meat Puppets, and the Descendents. He has also recorded prolifically under his own name and with side projects such as Gone.
