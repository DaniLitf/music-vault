# Grant Marshall

**British musician. Born 18 December 1959.**

Grant Marshall, known as Daddy G, is a British DJ born in Bristol to Barbadian parents who came up through the Wild Bunch sound system before co-founding Massive Attack in 1988 alongside Robert Del Naja and Andrew Vowles. He was largely absent from the band between 2001 and 2005 before rejoining for later albums including *Heligoland* (2010).
