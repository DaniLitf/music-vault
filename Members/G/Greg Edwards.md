**Guitarist, bassist, and multi-instrumentalist. Born 14 December 1970.**

Greg Edwards is an American musician and songwriter best known as guitarist and bassist for the rock band Failure, which he co-founded in 1990. A multi-instrumentalist, Edwards has been active in the progressive rock and experimental music scenes. Beyond Failure, he plays guitar and sings in the experimental band Autolux, and has been a touring member of Puscifer, where he played bass and keyboards on the album Existential Reckoning. Edwards also joined A Perfect Circle as a touring guitarist and keyboardist in 2018, continuing through subsequent tours including the 2024 Sessanta tour alongside Puscifer and Primus.
