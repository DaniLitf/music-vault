**Drummer. Born in Turin, Italy.**

Gee Anzalone is an Italian drummer best known as the current drummer of DragonForce, the British power metal band. Born and raised in Turin, Italy, Anzalone began playing drums at age 15 and graduated from the NAM Academy in Milan in 2005 before becoming a drum teacher specializing in rock and metal. He joined DragonForce in September 2014, making his live debut on the "Maximum Overload" UK Tour, replacing longtime drummer Dave Mackintosh. Beyond his work with DragonForce, Anzalone also teaches drumming and music production at his own academy, Extreme Music Performance Academy.
