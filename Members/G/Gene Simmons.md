# Gene Simmons

**Bassist, co-lead vocalist. Born August 25, 1949.**

Gene Simmons is an American musician, singer, songwriter, and actor, best known as the bassist, co-lead vocalist, and co-founder of KISS. As "The Demon", he is famous for his fire-breathing and blood-spitting stage antics. He is also a prolific businessman and television personality.
