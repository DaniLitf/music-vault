# Gary Cherone

**Vocals. American. Founding member of [[Artists/E/Extreme]].**

Gary Cherone co-founded Extreme in Boston in 1985 with drummer Paul Geary, later joined by guitarist Nuno Bettencourt and bassist Pat Badger. After Extreme's 1996 breakup he fronted [[Artists/V/Van Halen]] from 1996 to 1999, recording *Van Halen III* (1998), before returning to lead Extreme through its 2007 reunion and beyond.

## Associated Artists
