# George Harrison

**George Harrison was a British musician and songwriter who served as the lead guitarist of The Beatles, whose contributions — including "Something," "Here Comes the Sun," and "While My Guitar Gently Weeps" — grew substantially as the band evolved.**

After The Beatles, Harrison launched a major solo career and co-founded the Traveling Wilburys supergroup with Bob Dylan, Tom Petty, Roy Orbison, and Jeff Lynne. He died of lung cancer on November 29, 2001.
