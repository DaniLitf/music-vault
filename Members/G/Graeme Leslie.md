# Graeme Leslie

**Guitarist. British.**

Graeme Leslie is a British guitarist who first sent Iron Maiden bassist Steve Harris a demo tape in the early 1990s, beginning a mentorship that eventually led to the formation of [[Artists/B/British Lion]] two decades later.
