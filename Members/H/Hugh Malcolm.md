# Hugh Malcolm

**Drummer. Jamaican.**

Hugh Malcolm was one of the original drummers of The Upsetters and has been credited as one of the originators of the reggae drumming style. Beyond The Upsetters, he recorded solo singles and played drums for artists including Bob Marley and the Wailers, Dave and Ansel Collins, The Heptones, and Rupie Edwards.
