# Happy Tsugawa-Banta

**Vibraphonist and keyboardist. American.**

Happy Tsugawa-Banta is an American musician known for her work on vibraphone and keyboards with The Lassie Foundation, joining the band around 2000 and appearing on *The El Dorado LP* and subsequent releases. She is also a member of the Los Angeles-based guitar pop band Exploding Flowers and has performed with Ray Barbee.
