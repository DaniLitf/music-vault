# Howard Walmsley

**Howard Walmsley is a British guitarist who was a co-founder and member of Biting Tongues from 1979 to 1993.**

He contributed the angular, abrasive guitar work that defined the band's post-punk and industrial sound across their recorded output on the Manchester experimental scene.
