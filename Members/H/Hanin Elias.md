# Hanin Elias

**Hanin Elias is a German vocalist and musician, best known for her work with [[Artists/A/Atari Teenage Riot]].**

She contributed vocals to Pigface as a guest, bringing her digital hardcore energy to the collective.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/P/Pigface]] – vocals (guest)
- [[Artists/A/Atari Teenage Riot]] – vocals (1992–present)
