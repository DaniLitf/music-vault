# Howlin' Pelle Almqvist

**Lead vocals. Swedish. Born 1978-05-29.**

Per "Pelle" Almqvist, known onstage as Howlin' Pelle Almqvist, is a Swedish singer and co-founder of garage rock band [[Artists/T/The Hives]] with his brother Niklas. He has fronted the band since its 1993 formation, becoming known for his energetic showmanship and stage presence.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Hives]] – lead vocals, co-founder (1993–present)
