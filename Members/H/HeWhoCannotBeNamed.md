# HeWhoCannotBeNamed

**Guitarist. American.**

HeWhoCannotBeNamed is the masked guitarist and one of the core long-running members of [[Artists/T/The Dwarves]]. Known for his anonymous stage persona and provocative live presentation, he has been repeatedly identified with the band's central Blag Dahlia-led lineup since its early years.
