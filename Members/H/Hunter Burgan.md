# Hunter Burgan

**American musician. Born 27 May 1976.**

Hunter Burgan is an American musician, best known as the bassist of the rock band AFI. He joined AFI in 1997, replacing Jeff Harrell, and has been a consistent member since. He is also known for his work with his solo project Hunter.
