# Henry Rollins

**Henry Rollins (born Henry Garfield) is an American vocalist, author, and spoken word artist born February 13, 1961, best known as the frontman of [[Artists/R/Rollins Band]] and [[Artists/B/Black Flag]].**

Rollins joined Black Flag in 1981, replacing Ron Reyes, and fronted the band through their most intense period. After Black Flag dissolved in 1986, he formed the Rollins Band, which achieved mainstream recognition with *The End of Silence* (1992) and *Weight* (1994). He is also known for his spoken word performances, acting, and activism.
