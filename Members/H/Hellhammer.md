# Hellhammer

**Drummer. Born 2 August 1969.**

Jan Axel Blomberg, known as Hellhammer (a name taken from the Swiss extreme metal band of the same name), is a Norwegian drummer who joined Mayhem in 1988 following founding drummer Kjetil Manheim's departure, and is the only member to appear on all of the band's studio albums. He also formed the avant-garde metal band Arcturus in 1987 and has served as a session and live drummer for Dimmu Borgir.
