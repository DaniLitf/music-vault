# Herbert Munkhammar

**Swedish rapper. Born 15 March 1985.**

Herbert Munkhammar, also known as Afasi, is a Swedish hip hop artist from Uppsala who rose to prominence in the duo Afasi & Filthy (2002–2009) with producer Magnus Lidehäll before co-founding the electro/hip hop group Maskinen with Frej Larsson in 2007.
