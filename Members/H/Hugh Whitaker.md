# Hugh Whitaker

**Drummer, vocalist. Born 1961.**

Hugh Whitaker is an English drummer best known as a member of [[Artists/T/The Housemartins]] during their first album and breakthrough singles. He left in 1987 and suggested his school friend [[Members/D/Dave Hemingway]] as his replacement.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Housemartins]] – drums, vocals (1985–1987)
