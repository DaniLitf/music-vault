# Hunt Sales

**Drummer. American.**

Hunt Sales is an American drummer, son of entertainer Soupy Sales, who backed David Bowie and Iggy Pop on the 1977 tour for *The Idiot* alongside his brother Tony Sales before the pair co-founded [[Artists/T/Tin Machine]] with Bowie and Reeves Gabrels in 1988.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/Tin Machine]] – drums, vocals (1988–1992)
- [[Artists/I/Iggy Pop]] – touring drums (1977)
