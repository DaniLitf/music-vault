**Bassist for Madball. American.**

Jorge "Hoya Roc" Guerra is the bassist for Madball, the legendary New York hardcore band, joining the group in 1993. As a core member of Madball for over three decades, he has been instrumental in the band's sound and longevity within the NYHC scene. Guerra has participated in numerous reunions, tours, and festival appearances, including the Rebellion Tour 2023 with H2O and Hazen Street across Europe.
