# Henry Olmino

**Guitarist.**

Henry Olmino is an American guitarist and a founding member of [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]], playing in the original Davie, Florida line-up from September 1996 to February 1997. His recordings from the band's 1996 demo were later released on *The Fists of Time* (1998) and subsequent compilations.
