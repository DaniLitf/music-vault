# Hank von Helvete

**Vocalist. Norwegian.**

Hank von Helvete is a Norwegian singer best known as the longtime frontman of deathpunk band Turbonegro. He fronted the Scandinavian heavy metal supergroup Doctor Midnight & The Mercy Cult from 2009 to 2011, alongside former Marilyn Manson bassist Tim Sköld and members of Cadaver, Apoptygma Berzerk, and Extol.
