# Holly Nelson

**Vocalist. American.**

Holly Nelson is an American vocalist who contributed to Cush in the early 2000s, appearing on the self-titled album as well as the *Spiritual EP/1* and *Spiritual EP/2*. She has also released solo music through Northern Records, including the album *Ages, Vol. 1* (2021).

## Associated Artists

- Holly Nelson (solo artist)
