# Hisashi Imai

**Hisashi Imai is a Japanese guitarist and co-founder of Buck-Tick, where he has been the primary songwriter and creative driving force since 1983.**

Known for his experimental approach to guitar, Imai has pushed Buck-Tick's sound through numerous stylistic evolutions, from post-punk to industrial to electronic-inflected gothic rock.
