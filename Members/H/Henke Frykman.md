# Henke Frykman

**Bassist. Died March 25, 2011.**

Henke Frykman was a Swedish bassist and a founding member of the crust punk band [[Artists/D/Disfear]], which he started in 1989 as Anti-Bofors alongside guitarist Björn Peterson. He performed on all Disfear releases from the early demos through *Misanthropic Generation* and was the band's bassist for 22 years until his death from cancer at age 40.
