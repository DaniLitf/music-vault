# Hiili Hiilesmaa

**Drummer, keyboardist, vocalist. Finnish.**

Hiili Hiilesmaa (born Kai Hiilesmaa) is a Finnish musician and record producer active across multiple roles in the rock and metal scene. He is the drummer of the doom metal band KYPCK, former keyboardist of the rock band Daniel Lioneye, and vocalist of The Skreppers. As a producer, he is best known for his long association with HIM and his work with Apocalyptica, Amorphis, Moonspell, and Sentenced.
