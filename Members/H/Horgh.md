# Horgh

**Drummer. Norwegian.**

Reidar Horghagen, known as Horgh, is a Norwegian drummer who joined black metal band Immortal in 1996, providing the band with its first stable lineup outside founders Abbath and Demonaz. He remained with the band through most of its history, later becoming embroiled in a legal dispute with Demonaz over rights to the Immortal name.
