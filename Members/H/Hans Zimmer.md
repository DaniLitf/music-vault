# Hans Zimmer

**Hans Zimmer is a German composer and music producer based in Los Angeles, one of the most celebrated film score composers of all time, with over 150 film scores including *The Lion King* (1994 Academy Award), *Gladiator*, *Inception*, and *Dune*.**
