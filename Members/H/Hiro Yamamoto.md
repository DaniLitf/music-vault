# Hiro Yamamoto

**Bassist. Born Los Angeles, California.**

Hiro Yamamoto is an American rock bassist who was a founding member and bassist of Soundgarden from 1984 to 1989. He played on the band's early albums including the Sub Pop debut *Ultramega OK* and *Louder Than Love*, helping establish their distinctive heavy sound. Yamamoto left the band in 1989 and was succeeded by Jason Everman, later replaced by Ben Shepherd.
