# Warren Defever

**Multi-instrumentalist, producer. American.**

Warren Defever is an American musician and producer best known as the founder, primary songwriter, and only constant member of the experimental rock project His Name Is Alive. He began recording in his basement in 1985 while still in high school, and over the course of decades has released over 15 studio albums under the His Name Is Alive name, spanning dream pop, soul, noise, folk, and experimental music. He also runs the Time Stereo studio and label.
