# Winston Wright

**Organist. Born 1943-09-05, died 1993-03-18.**

Winston Wright was a Jamaican Hammond organist widely regarded as one of the instrument's foremost players in Jamaican music, active as a session musician throughout the rocksteady and early reggae era. He played in Tommy McCook's Supersonics and Clancy Eccles' Dynamites, and is the uncredited lead organist on the Harry J All Stars' 1969 instrumental hit "The Liquidator."
