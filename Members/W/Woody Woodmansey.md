# Woody Woodmansey

**Drummer. British.**

Woody Woodmansey is a British drummer who played in David Bowie's backing band the Spiders from Mars during the Ziggy Stardust era, and later toured and recorded as a founding member of Holy Holy, a project performing Bowie's early material.
