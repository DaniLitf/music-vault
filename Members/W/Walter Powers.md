# Walter Powers

**Bass. Active 1970–1972.**

Walter Powers is an American bassist, best known for his work with [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]] in the post-Lou Reed era.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]]

## Biography
Walter Powers played bass with The Velvet Underground during their post-Lou Reed touring period from 1970 to 1972. He joined when Doug Yule took over as frontman, helping the band fulfill touring commitments after Reed's departure. Little is known about Powers' musical activities after leaving the Velvet Underground.
