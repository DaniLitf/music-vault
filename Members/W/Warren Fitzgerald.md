# Warren Fitzgerald

**Guitarist. American.**

Warren Fitzgerald is a guitarist who joined The Vandals in 1989 as a second guitarist, adding another layer to the band's sound during their peak comedic punk era. He has remained with the band continuously since joining, contributing to their albums throughout the 1990s, 2000s, and beyond, and helping solidify The Vandals' signature two-guitar arrangement.
