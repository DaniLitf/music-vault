# Waltteri Väyrynen

**Drummer. Born 1990-03-28 in Finland.**

Waltteri Väyrynen is a Finnish drummer who has performed with several prominent European metal bands. He served as the drummer for Paradise Lost from 2015 to 2022 before joining Opeth, where he became the full-time drummer from 2022 onward. His precise and dynamic playing style suits both the gothic heaviness of Paradise Lost and the progressive intricacy of Opeth.
