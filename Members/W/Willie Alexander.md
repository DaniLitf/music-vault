# Willie Alexander

**Keyboards. Born January 13, 1943.**

Willie Alexander is an American keyboardist and singer, best known for his work with [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]] in the early 1970s.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]]

## Biography
Alexander joined The Velvet Underground in 1971, playing keyboards after Lou Reed's departure. He was part of Doug Yule's version of the band that toured and recorded during this period.
