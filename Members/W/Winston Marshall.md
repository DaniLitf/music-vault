# Winston Marshall

**British musician. Born 20 December 1987.**

Winston Marshall, sometimes credited as Country Winston, played in the bluegrass-influenced Captain Kick and the Cowboy Ramblers before co-founding Mumford & Sons in 2007, playing banjo, guitar, bass, and dobro and winning two Grammys. He departed in 2021 amid controversy over his political commentary and has since become a right-wing political commentator with his own podcast.
