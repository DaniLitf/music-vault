# Wayne Everett

**Drummer, guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter. American.**

Wayne Everett is an American musician who played drums for The Prayer Chain before co-founding and fronting The Lassie Foundation as vocalist and guitarist. He was the studio drummer for Starflyer 59 on several albums and has also played in Cush. He released a solo album titled *KingsQueens* in 2003 on Northern Records, followed by *Two Ghosts* in 2020.
