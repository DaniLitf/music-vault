# Waylon Reavis

**Vocalist (harsh and rap vocals). American.**

Waylon Reavis, known professionally as Wizlo, is an American vocalist best known for his time with the industrial metal band Mushroomhead, where he provided harsh and rap vocals from 2004–2015. He replaced Jason Popson after the latter's departure in mid-2004 and contributed vocals to the *Savior Sorrow*, *Beautiful Stories for Ugly Children*, and *The Righteous & the Butterfly* albums before departing in October 2015.
