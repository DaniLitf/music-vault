# Warren Oakes

**Drummer.**

Warren Oakes is an American drummer who replaced Kevin Mahon in [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]] in 2001 and played on the band's first three studio albums during its longest-serving classic lineup. He left the band amicably in 2009 to focus on the restaurant business, later co-owning Boca Fiesta, a Mexican restaurant and music venue in Gainesville, Florida.
