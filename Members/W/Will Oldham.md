# Will Oldham

**Will Oldham is an American singer-songwriter, guitarist, and actor born on December 24, 1970 in Louisville, Kentucky.**

Oldham began recording in the early 1990s under the Palace Brothers name, releasing lo-fi folk and country records on Drag City. He adopted the Bonnie "Prince" Billy alias in 1999 and released his most celebrated work under that name, including *I See a Darkness* (1999) and *Master and Everyone* (2003). He has also appeared in numerous films as an actor.
