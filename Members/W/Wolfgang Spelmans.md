# Wolfgang Spelmans

**Guitarist. Born 1957 in Germany.**

Wolfgang Spelmans is a German guitarist who was an original member of DAF from 1978 to 1981, playing guitar on the band's early albums. After leaving DAF he played in the band Mau Mau alongside former DAF bandmate Michael Kemner.
