**Drummer and backing vocalist. Born June 21, 1963, died June 8, 2010.**

Walt Woodward III was an American drummer best known for his work with Shark Island and The Scream. He replaced Scott Travis in The Scream and played on their only studio album *Let It Scream* (1991). Woodward died of alcohol poisoning in 2010 at the age of 46.
