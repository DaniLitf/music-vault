# Wes Borland

**Guitarist, vocalist, artist. Born 7 February 1975 in Richmond, Virginia, USA.**

Wes Borland is an American musician and visual artist best known as the guitarist for [[Artists/L/Limp Bizkit]] (1995–2001, 2004–present). He has also fronted [[Artists/B/Black Light Burns]], been a member of [[Artists/B/Big Dumb Face]], and collaborated with [[Members/R/Richard Patrick]] and [[Members/J/Josh Freese]] in [[Artists/T/The Damning Well]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/L/Limp Bizkit]] – lead guitar (1995–2001, 2004–present)
- [[Artists/B/Big Dumb Face]] – vocals, guitar, bass, drums, programming (1998–2004, 2017–present)
- [[Artists/B/Black Light Burns]] – lead vocals, guitars, bass, keyboards, programming (2005–2016)
- [[Artists/T/The Damning Well]] – guitar (2002–2003)

## Biography

Borland joined Limp Bizkit in 1995 and became known for his distinctive appearance and experimental guitar work on albums like *Three Dollar Bill, Y'all$* (1997) and *Significant Other* (1999). He left the band in 2001 due to creative differences and formed Black Light Burns, releasing *Cruel Melody* (2007). He rejoined Limp Bizkit in 2004 and continues to record and tour with them. He also works as a visual artist, creating album art and merchandise designs.
