# Wayne Southworth

**Vocalist. British. Died 2005.**

Wayne Southworth was a Welsh vocalist who fronted Doom from 1995 until his death from an epileptic seizure on 18 March 2005. He appeared on the albums *Monarchy Zoo*, *Rush Hour of the Gods*, and *World of Shit*.
