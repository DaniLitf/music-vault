# Wally Safford

**Dancer and backing vocalist.**

Wally Safford is an American dancer and vocalist who performed with Prince's backing band The Revolution as a dancer and backing vocalist from 1980 to 1986. He appeared in the film *Purple Rain* and was a regular presence in Prince's live shows during the Revolution era.
