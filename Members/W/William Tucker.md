# William Tucker

**Guitarist. Born in the United States.**

William Tucker is an American guitarist who has been a core member of Pigface since its formation. He has also played with Ministry, Revolting Cocks, and My Life With the Thrill Kill Kult. His aggressive guitar work has been a consistent element of Pigface's sound across their discography.
