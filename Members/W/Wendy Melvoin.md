# Wendy Melvoin

**Guitarist and vocalist. Born 26 January 1964 in Los Angeles, California.**

Wendy Melvoin is an American guitarist and vocalist best known as a member of Prince's backing band The Revolution, which she joined in 1983 after replacing Dez Dickerson. She and keyboardist Lisa Coleman formed the duo Wendy & Lisa after leaving Prince, releasing acclaimed albums and composing scores for television and film.
