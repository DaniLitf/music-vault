# Warren DeMartini

**Guitarist. Born April 10, 1963.**

Warren DeMartini is an American guitarist best known as the lead guitarist of the glam metal band Ratt. He joined Ratt in 1981 at age 18 and became known for his fast, melodic guitar solos and twin-lead harmonies with Robbin Crosby. His playing is featured on all of Ratt's studio albums and he is widely regarded as one of the defining guitarists of the 1980s glam metal scene.
