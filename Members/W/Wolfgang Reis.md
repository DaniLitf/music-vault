# Wolfgang Reis

**Dancer. Dutch.**

Wolfgang Reis, known by the stage name Jacks, is a Dutch dancer who performed as part of the Eurodance group Twenty 4 Seven. He joined the group alongside Giovanni Falco (Hanks) and Tony Dawson-Harrison (Captain Hollywood) in 1990, contributing choreographed dance routines to the group's live performances and music videos. He returned to the group in 2021 alongside Nance and Hanks.
