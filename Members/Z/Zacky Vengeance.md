# Zacky Vengeance

**Rhythm guitarist. Born 1981-12-11.**

Zacky Vengeance (Zachary James Baker) is an American guitarist, born in Olympia, Washington, and a founding member of Avenged Sevenfold. He has played rhythm guitar and contributed backing vocals for the band since its 1999 formation in Huntington Beach, California.
