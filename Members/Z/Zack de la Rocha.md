# Zack de la Rocha

**Vocalist, lyricist. Born 1970-01-12.**

Zack de la Rocha is an American vocalist and political activist best known as the co-founder and vocalist of Rage Against the Machine, one of the most commercially successful and politically outspoken bands of the 1990s. His confrontational rap-rock style and left-wing political lyrics made him an influential figure in alternative metal and hip-hop. After Rage Against the Machine's initial dissolution, he formed One Day as a Lion with The Mars Volta drummer Jon Theodore in 2008.
