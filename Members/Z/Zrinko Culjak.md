# Zrinko Culjak

**Bass. Swedish.**

Zrinko Culjak was a founding bassist of [[Artists/C/Cemetary]], playing on the band's early death-doom material through their 1992 debut *An Evil Shade of Grey* before departing in 1993.

- [[Artists/C/Cemetary]] – bass (1989–1993, founding member)
