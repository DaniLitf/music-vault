# Zola Jesus

**Vocalist, songwriter. Born 11 April 1989.**

Zola Jesus is the stage name of Nika Roza Danilova, an American singer-songwriter from Wisconsin known for her operatic voice and dark, electronic-tinged art pop. She has released a string of acclaimed albums under the Zola Jesus name and guested on [[Artists/M/M83]]'s *Hurry Up, We're Dreaming* (2011), singing on the opening track "Intro".
