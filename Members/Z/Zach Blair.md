# Zach Blair

**Guitarist. American.**

Zach Blair is a guitarist best known for co-founding [[Artists/H/Hagfish|Hagfish]] with his brother Doni in 1991, serving as the band's guitarist and backing vocalist from their formation through their 1993 debut album and beyond. Prior to and during Hagfish's run, Blair performed as Flattus Maximus with shock rock band [[Artists/G/Gwar|Gwar]] from 1999 to 2002, appearing on their live album *You're All Worthless and Weak* and studio album *Violence Has Arrived*. After Hagfish disbanded, Blair continued collaborating with his brother in projects including Armstrong and Only Crime before becoming lead guitarist for [[Artists/R/Rise Against|Rise Against]] in 2007, making his recorded debut on *Appeal to Reason* (2008) and contributing to their most successful album, *Endgame* (2011).
