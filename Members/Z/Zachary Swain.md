# Zachary Swain

**Drummer.**

Zachary Swain is an American drummer who joined [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] in March 2001 and played on the debut album *Won* (2001), the EP *A Young Trophy Band in the Parlance of Our Times* (2002) and the live video *Camden Underworld, London – 16 November 2001*. He remained through the band's 2002 transition to the name Salem.
