# Maggi Ronson

**Guest vocalist. British.**

Maggi Ronson is the sister of guitarist Mick Ronson and appeared with [[Artists/T/The Featherz]] at a David Bowie memorial concert. She guested on vocals for the band's performance of "Ziggy Stardust."
