# Moe Tucker

**Drums, percussion, vocals. Born August 26, 1944.**

Moe Tucker is an American musician, best known as the drummer of [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]]

## Biography
Tucker replaced Angus MacLise in November 1965 and became known for her minimalist drumming style — often using mallets instead of sticks and playing a simplified kit without a hi-hat or ride cymbal. Her distinctive, primal rhythms were a key element of the Velvet Underground's sound. After the band disbanded, she released several solo albums.
