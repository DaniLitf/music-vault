# Mark Lanegan

**Vocalist. Born 1964-11-25 in Ellensburg, Washington, USA. Died 2022-02-22.**

Mark Lanegan was an American singer with one of the most distinctive voices in alternative rock — deep, gravelly, and deeply expressive. He was the lead vocalist of Screaming Trees, one of the key bands of the Seattle scene, and a prolific solo artist throughout his career. He became a recurring collaborator with Queens of the Stone Age, appearing extensively on *Rated R* (2000) and *Songs for the Deaf* (2002), and released collaborative albums with Isobel Campbell, Greg Dulli, and others.
