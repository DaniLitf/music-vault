**Bassist. Born August 20, 1985.**

Michael Shuman is an American bassist best known for his work with Queens of the Stone Age, where he joined in 2007. He has been a key member of QOTSA during their most successful period, appearing on *Era Vulgaris* (2007), *...Like Clockwork* (2013), *Villains* (2017), and *In Times New Roman...* (2023). Shuman also performs with Mini Mansions and has toured with various rock acts.
