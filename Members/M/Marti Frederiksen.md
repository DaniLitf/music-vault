# Marti Frederiksen

**Songwriter, record producer, and multi-instrumentalist. Born 1962-07-01.**

Marti Frederiksen is an American songwriter, record producer, and musician known for co-writing Aerosmith's "Jaded," Carrie Underwood's "Undo It," and Buckcherry's "Sorry." He has performed keyboards, percussion, and backing vocals on multiple The Dead Daisies albums and also sang lead vocals for the fictional band Stillwater in the film *Almost Famous*.
