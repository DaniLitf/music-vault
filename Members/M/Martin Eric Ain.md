# Martin Eric Ain

**Bassist. Died 2017-10-21.**

Martin Eric Ain co-founded Hellhammer in 1982 alongside Tom Gabriel Fischer, and the two rebranded the band as Celtic Frost in 1984, with Ain playing bass across both of the band's active periods until its final breakup in 2008. He died in 2017, shortly after Celtic Frost's 2017 reunion show.
