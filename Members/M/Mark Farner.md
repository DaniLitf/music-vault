# Mark Farner

**Guitarist, vocalist, songwriter. Born September 29, 1948.**

Mark Farner is an American guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter best known as the lead singer and guitarist of Grand Funk Railroad, for which he wrote the majority of the band's material including "I'm Your Captain (Closer to Home)," "Some Kind of Wonderful," and "Bad Time." After leaving Grand Funk in 1998, he pursued a solo career and became a contemporary Christian music artist.
