# Mark Watrous

**Multi-instrumentalist. American.**

Mark Watrous is an American singer, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist from Richland, Washington, who toured with [[Artists/T/The Raconteurs]] from 2006 to 2008 on keyboards, fiddle, percussion, and backing vocals. He has also toured with [[Artists/T/The Greenhornes]], Shudder to Think, Brendan Benson, and Karen Elson.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Raconteurs]] – touring keyboards, fiddle, percussion, backing vocals (2006–2008)
- [[Artists/T/The Greenhornes]] – touring keyboards, guitar, vocals (2010)
