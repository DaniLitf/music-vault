# Mike Dillard

**Drummer. American.**

Mike Dillard was the original drummer of [[Artists/T/The Melvins]], forming the band with Buzz Osborne and Matt Lukin in 1983. He later reunited with Osborne and Dale Crover in the Melvins 1983 configuration for albums such as *Tres Cabrones*, *Working with God*, and *Thunderball*.
