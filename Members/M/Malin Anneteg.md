# Malin Anneteg

**Vocals. Swedish.**

Malin Anneteg is a Swedish vocalist whose only documented recording credit is providing lyrics and lead vocals for "No More Lonely Nights" on [[Artists/T/The Heads]]' album *No Talking, Just Head* (1996). No further biographical details are reliably documented.
