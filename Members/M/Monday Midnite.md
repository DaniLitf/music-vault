**Vocalist.**

Monday Midnite (born Monday Osaigbovo Agbonze) is a Nigerian-Belgian vocalist who fronted [[Artists/T/Technotronic|Technotronic]] during their late-1990s revival, appearing on the 1999 singles "Like This" and "The G-Train". He brought a new vocal direction to the project as it attempted to modernise its sound for a changing dance music landscape.
