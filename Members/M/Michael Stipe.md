# Michael Stipe

**Vocalist, lyricist. Born 1960-01-04 in Decatur, Georgia, USA.**

Michael Stipe is the lead vocalist and primary lyricist of R.E.M., one of the most distinctive and celebrated voices in alternative rock. His oblique, poetic lyrics and expressive vocal delivery shaped R.E.M.'s identity across three decades. He was also an active visual artist and film producer, and collaborated with numerous artists including Patti Smith and Thom Yorke. He has pursued solo and collaborative projects since R.E.M.'s dissolution in 2011.
