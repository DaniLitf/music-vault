# Mats Rydström

**Bassist. Swedish.**

Mats Rydström is a Swedish bassist who became [[Artists/A/Avatarium]]'s permanent studio bassist starting with *Hurricanes and Halos* (2017), following founder Leif Edling's stepping back from touring duties.
