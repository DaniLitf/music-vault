# Madonna Wayne Gacy

**American keyboardist. Born 6 March 1964.**

Stephen Bier, known by his stage name Madonna Wayne Gacy or "Pogo," is an American keyboardist who joined Marilyn Manson in 1989 and contributed calliope, Hammond organ, theremin, and other instrumentation up through *Eat Me, Drink Me* before departing in 2007 amid a lawsuit over unpaid partnership proceeds.
