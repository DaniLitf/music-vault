# Mick Jagger

**Mick Jagger is a British singer, songwriter, and actor who co-founded The Rolling Stones in 1962 and has served as the band's lead vocalist ever since, becoming one of the defining rock frontmen in history.**

Jagger's partnership with Keith Richards as the "Glimmer Twins" songwriting duo has produced some of the most enduring songs in rock history. He has also pursued intermittent solo projects.
