# Mick Mars

**Guitarist. Born May 4, 1951.**

Mick Mars is an American guitarist and founding member of Mötley Crüe, known for his distinctive riff-driven guitar work that defined the sound of glam metal in the 1980s. Despite chronic health issues, he remained a cornerstone of the band throughout its career and iconic era.
