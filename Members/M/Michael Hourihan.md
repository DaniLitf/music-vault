# Michael Hourihan

**Drummer. British.**

Michael "Mic" Hourihan is a Welsh drummer who has been the drummer for Extreme Noise Terror since 2008 and for Desecration since 1998. He has also performed with Tigertailz, Onslaught, and several other Welsh metal bands.
