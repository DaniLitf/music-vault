# Morris Hayes

**Keyboardist, musical director. Born 3 June 1965.**

Morris Hayes is an American keyboardist who joined Prince's New Power Generation in 1992 after Rosie Gaines left. He served as the band's musical director for over two decades, becoming the longest-tenured member outside of Prince himself. He also led Prince tribute tours after Prince's death.
