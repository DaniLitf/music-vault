# Marr Starr

**Keyboardist. American.**

Marr Starr, born Marvin Gunn, was a keyboardist of Mazarati, an American R&B and funk band from Minneapolis active from 1986 to 1989. After the band's disbandment, he formed the musical duo The Wild Pair with fellow member Tony Christian (Bruce DeShazer), and together they voiced the animated MC Skat Kat on Paula Abdul's chart-topping 1989 single "Opposites Attract." In 2011, Starr reformed the band under the name Mazarati Revisited alongside lead guitarist Craig "Screamer" Powell, focusing on the group's back catalogue and performing live, and later reconvened with other original members in 2013 to collaborate on potential new recordings.
