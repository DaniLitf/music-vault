# Mark Sogomian

**Bassist and guitarist. American.**

Mark Sogomian is an American musician who plays bass and guitar in Exploding Flowers. He was previously a member of The Moon Upstairs, playing electric guitar on their 2007 album *Guarding the Golden Apple*.
