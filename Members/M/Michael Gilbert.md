# Michael Gilbert

**Guitar. American.**

Michael Gilbert is an American guitarist who has played multiple stints in [[Artists/F/Flotsam and Jetsam]] since 1985, including the band's most commercially successful period around *No Place for Disgrace* (1988), and has been a permanent member again since 2010.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/F/Flotsam and Jetsam]] – guitar (1985–1997, 1999–2000, 2010–present)
