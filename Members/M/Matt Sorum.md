# Matt Sorum

**Matt Sorum (born November 19, 1960, in Long Beach, California) is an American drummer, best known as the drummer of Guns N' Roses (1990–1997) and Velvet Revolver (2002–2008), and co-founder of the Kings of Chaos supergroup.**

Sorum joined Guns N' Roses in 1990, replacing Steven Adler, and played on *Use Your Illusion I & II*. He co-founded Velvet Revolver with Slash, Duff McKagan, and Dave Kushner in 2002. He later co-founded the rotating rock supergroup Kings of Chaos in 2012.
