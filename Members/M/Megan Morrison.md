# Megan Morrison

**Keyboardist and vocalist. American.**

Megan Morrison is an American musician who played keyboards and sang backup vocals for Duraluxe. She joined the band in Athens, Georgia when Duraluxe decided to expand their sound, and appeared on their split CD with The Lassie Foundation (2001) and their second album *The Suitcase* (2002).
