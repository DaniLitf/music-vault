# Mikey "Boo" Richards

**Drummer and percussionist. Died November 2021.**

Mikey "Boo" Richards was a Jamaican session and touring drummer who first came to prominence with The In Crowd in the early 1970s before playing in bands including Now Generation, 809, and Jimmy Cliff's Oneness. His drumming appears on landmark recordings such as Lee "Scratch" Perry's *Super Ape*, The Congos' *Heart of the Congos*, and Bob Marley and the Wailers' *Survival*, and he was an early mentor to Sly Dunbar.
