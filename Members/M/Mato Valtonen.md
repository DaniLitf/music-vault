# Mato Valtonen

**Finnish musician and actor. Born 1957.**

Mato Valtonen is a Finnish musician and actor who, alongside Sakke Järvenpää, was a member of comedy rock band Sleepy Sleepers before the pair co-conceived Leningrad Cowboys with director Aki Kaurismäki in 1986. He played guitar and vocals for the band from 1986 to 1997, appearing in Kaurismäki's *Leningrad Cowboys Go America* (1989).
