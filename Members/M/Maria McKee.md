# Maria McKee

**Vocals, guitar. American. Born 1964-08-17.**

Maria McKee is an American singer-songwriter, best known as the frontwoman of country-rock band [[Artists/L/Lone Justice]] before pursuing a solo career. She wrote lyrics for and provided vocals, guitar, and synthesizer on "No Big Bang" on [[Artists/T/The Heads]]' *No Talking, Just Head* (1996).

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/L/Lone Justice]] – lead vocals, guitar
- [[Artists/T/The Heads]] – guest vocals (1996)
