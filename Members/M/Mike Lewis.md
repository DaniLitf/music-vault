**Rhythm guitarist and bassist. Born 17 August 1977.**

Mike Lewis was a founding member of Lostprophets, serving as rhythm guitarist and bassist throughout the band's existence from 1997 to 2013. He provided essential support to the band's musical structure and was part of all five studio album releases, contributing to the evolution of the band's sound across multiple genres.
