# Mika Pääkkö

**Guitarist. Finnish.**

Mika Pääkkö, also known as "Pätkä," was a founding member of Impaled Nazarene, playing guitar from 1990 to 1992. His guitar work in the band's early years contributed to the development of their extreme black metal sound.
