# Marthe Alexandre

**Mezzo-soprano vocals (2022–present).**

Marthe Alexandre is a French mezzo-soprano vocalist best known as the operatic vocalist for [[Artists/I/Igorrr]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Igorrr]] – mezzo-soprano vocals (2022–present)

## Biography

Alexandre joined Igorrr in 2022, replacing vocalist Aphrodite Patoulidou. Her operatic mezzo-soprano vocals bring a dramatic, classical dimension to the band's live performances, continuing the tradition of operatic vocals in Igorrr established by Laure Le Prunenec.
