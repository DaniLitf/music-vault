# Mikael Stanne

**Mikael Stanne is a Swedish vocalist who has been the frontman of Dark Tranquillity since 1989, one of the founding voices of the Gothenburg melodic death metal scene, and more recently the vocalist of supergroup The Halo Effect.**

Stanne is the only remaining original member of Dark Tranquillity and has sung on all of the band's studio albums. In 2021 he joined The Halo Effect, a band formed by former In Flames members, connecting the two pillars of the Gothenburg scene.
