# Mike Scott

**Guitarist. Born in Los Angeles, California.**

Mike Scott is an American guitarist who joined Prince's New Power Generation in 1996 alongside Kat Dyson. He contributed to Prince's *Newpower Soul* and *Rave Un2 the Joy Fantastic* and toured as part of Prince's band for several years. He has also worked with Lalah Hathaway and Sheila E.
