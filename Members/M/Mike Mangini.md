# Mike Mangini

**Drums. American. Former member of [[Artists/E/Extreme]].**

Mike Mangini replaced Paul Geary as Extreme's drummer in 1994, playing on parts of *Waiting for the Punchline* (1995) before the band's 1996 breakup. He went on to become the drummer for Dream Theater in 2010.

## Associated Artists
