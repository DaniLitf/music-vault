# Mike Kunka

**Bassist, collaborator. American.**

Mike Kunka is an American musician known for godheadSilo and for collaborating with [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] on *Three Men and a Baby*. The project was started in 1999 and completed for release in 2016.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] – collaborator on *Three Men and a Baby* (2016)
