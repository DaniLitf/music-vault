# M. Shadows

**Lead vocalist. Born 1981-07-31.**

M. Shadows (Matthew Charles Sanders) is an American singer and a founding member of heavy metal band Avenged Sevenfold, formed in Huntington Beach, California in 1999. He is the band's primary lyricist and has guided its evolution from metalcore roots into a broader hard rock and heavy metal sound across eight studio albums.
