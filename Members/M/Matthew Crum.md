# Matthew Crum

**Drummer.**

Matthew Crum is an American drummer and a founding member of [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]], playing in the original Davie, Florida line-up from September 1996 to February 1997. His drumming on the band's 1996 demo appears on *The Fists of Time* (1998), *Eleven Songs* (1999) and later archival releases.
