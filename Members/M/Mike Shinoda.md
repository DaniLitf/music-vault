# Mike Shinoda

**Vocalist, guitarist, keyboardist, songwriter. Born February 11, 1977.**

Mike Shinoda is an American musician, singer, and rapper best known as the co-lead vocalist, rhythm guitarist, keyboardist, and primary songwriter of Linkin Park. He co-founded the band in 1996 with Brad Delson and Rob Bourdon, and has been the creative force behind the group's fusion of rock, hip-hop, and electronic music. Shinoda also performs as a solo artist and has produced albums for other artists.
