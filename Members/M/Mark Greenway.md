# Mark Greenway

**Vocalist. British.**

Mark "Barney" Greenway is the vocalist of Napalm Death since 1989 and one of the primary songwriters and lyricists. Known for his intense vocal delivery and politically conscious songwriting, he has been instrumental in shaping Napalm Death's sound and message throughout the band's most prolific period.
