# Mark Greenway

**Vocalist and lyricist. British. Born 13 July 1969.**

Mark Andrew "Barney" Greenway is the lead singer and primary lyricist of Napalm Death since 1989, where he has shaped the band's musical direction and socially conscious songwriting. Known for his distinctive gruff vocal style and politically charged lyrics, Greenway is recognized as having one of extreme metal's most distinctive voices. Beyond Napalm Death, he has recorded with Benediction, Extreme Noise Terror, and numerous guest appearances with bands ranging from Dream Theater to Bloodbath, establishing himself as a respected figure in extreme metal.
