# Mick Taylor

**Mick Taylor is a British guitarist who served as the lead guitarist of The Rolling Stones from 1969 to 1974, having joined after Brian Jones' departure and dismissal.**

Taylor's fluid, blues-influenced lead playing contributed greatly to the Stones' classic run of albums from *Let It Bleed* through *It's Only Rock 'n Roll*, including *Exile on Main St.* He left the band in 1974 and was later replaced by Ronnie Wood.
