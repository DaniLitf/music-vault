# Martin Brelin

**Martin Brelin is a Swedish musician, producer, and synthesizer player who co-founded the Stockholm electropop duo Alice in Videoland in 2001.**

Brelin handles production and synthesizers for Alice in Videoland, shaping the band's danceable, layered synth-pop sound. He is the brother of Lasse Brelin, who was an early member of the band.
