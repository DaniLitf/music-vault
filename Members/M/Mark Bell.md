# Mark Bell

**British electronic musician and producer. Born 1971, died 2014.**

Mark Bell was a British electronic musician and producer best known as one half of the bleep techno duo LFO. He was a pioneer of the Leeds electronic music scene and later worked as a producer and remixer for artists including Björk and Depeche Mode.
