# Martyn LeNoble

**Martyn LeNoble** is a Dutch bassist best known as a member of [[Artists/P/Porno for Pyros]] and former bassist of [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]].

- [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]] – bass (2001–2002)
- [[Artists/P/Porno for Pyros]] – bass (1992–1998)
- [[Artists/T/The Cult]] – bass (touring)

LeNoble was a founding member of Porno for Pyros and later briefly joined Jane's Addiction in 2001 for the Jubilee reunion tour, recording most of *Strays* before being replaced by [[Members/C/Chris Chaney]].
