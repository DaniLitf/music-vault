# Mika Luttinen

**Vocalist. Finnish.**

Mika Luttinen has been the vocalist and frontman of Impaled Nazarene since the band's formation in 1990, providing the distinctive harsh vocal style that defines their sound. He also leads the side project Nuclear Holocaust and has been the primary creative force behind the band's controversial and extreme musical output.
