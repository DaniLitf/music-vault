# Michael Münzing

**Record producer, musician. German.**

Michael Münzing is a German record producer and musician, best known as one half of the production duo behind the Eurodance group Snap!, alongside Luca Anzilotti. He co-wrote and co-produced Snap!'s international hits "The Power" (1990) and "Rhythm Is a Dancer" (1992). Prior to Snap!, Münzing collaborated with Anzilotti and Sven Väth in the group Off (Organisation for Fun) and the side project 16 BIT.
