# Michael Jackson

**Michael Jackson (born Michael Joseph Jackson, August 29, 1958 – June 25, 2009) was an American singer, songwriter, and dancer from Gary, Indiana, known as the "King of Pop" and one of the most significant cultural figures of the 20th century.**

Jackson rose to fame as a child performer in the Jackson 5 before launching a historic solo career. *Thriller* (1982), with seven top-ten singles, is the best-selling album of all time. He transformed popular music and dance, pioneering the music video as an art form and creating signature moves like the moonwalk. He died from acute propofol and benzodiazepine intoxication.
