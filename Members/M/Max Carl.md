# Max Carl

**Vocalist, guitarist. Born July 5, 1950.**

Max Carl is an American singer and guitarist best known as the lead vocalist for Grand Funk Railroad from 2000 to 2026, when he retired due to medical reasons. He previously fronted Jack Mack & the Heart Attack and was a member of 38 Special, with whom he sang on hits including "Caught Up in You" and "If I'd Been the One."
