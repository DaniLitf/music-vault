# Martin Glover

**British bassist and producer. Born 27 October 1960.**

Martin Glover, known by his stage name Youth, is a British bassist and record producer best known as the bassist of post-punk band Killing Joke and as a highly influential producer who has worked with The Verve, Paul McCartney, and many others. He is considered one of the most important figures in the development of trip hop and electronic music.
