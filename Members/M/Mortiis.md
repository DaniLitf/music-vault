# Mortiis

**Håvard "Mortiis" Ellefsen (born February 25, 1975, in Notodden, Norway) is a Norwegian musician, the original bassist of Emperor before departing to pursue his Mortiis solo project.**

Ellefsen was a founding member of Emperor but left in 1993 to focus on Mortiis, a dark ambient and dungeon synth project he performed in elaborate goblin-esque makeup and costume. Over the years the project evolved from primitive dark ambient to industrial rock before partially returning to its roots in later years.
