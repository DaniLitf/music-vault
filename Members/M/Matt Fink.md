# Matt Fink

**Keyboardist. Born 8 February 1958 in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

Matt Fink, known professionally as Dr. Fink, is an American keyboardist best known as a member of Prince's backing band The Revolution. He joined Prince's live band in 1978 and remained until 1990, performing on landmark albums including *1999*, *Purple Rain*, and *Sign o' the Times*. He also played on Prince's side project Madhouse and was a member of the New Power Generation.
