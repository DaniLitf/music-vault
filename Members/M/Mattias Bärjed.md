# Mattias Bärjed

**Guitarist and film composer. Born 1973-05-21.**

Mattias Bärjed is a Swedish guitarist and film composer, best known as a member of The Soundtrack of Our Lives. He joined the band in March 1997, replacing founding guitarist Björn Olsson, and remained until their 2012 breakup and 2023 reunion. He also founded the band Free Fall in 2009, has played guitar in Refused since 2015, and has worked as a session/touring guitarist for acts including The Hellacopters and Håkan Hellström.
