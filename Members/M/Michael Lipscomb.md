# Michael Lipscomb

**Bassist.**

Michael Lipscomb is an American bass guitarist who performed with [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] at Furnace Fest in September 2022, during the period when the band operated without a permanent bassist. He appears on no studio releases by the band.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] – bass guitar (2022, live fill-in)
