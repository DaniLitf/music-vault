**Backing vocalist. Born 23 November 1949.**

Marcia Griffiths is a Jamaican singer best known as a member of the I Threes, the female backing vocal trio for Bob Marley and the Wailers from 1974 to 1981. She had already established a successful career as a recording artist before joining, notably as part of the duo Bob and Marcia with [[Members/B/Bob Marley]].
