# Matt Walst

**Lead vocals. Canadian. Member of [[Artists/T/Three Days Grace]]; former lead singer of My Darkest Days.**

Matt Walst, younger brother of bassist Brad Walst and previously lead singer of My Darkest Days, joined Three Days Grace as lead vocalist in 2013 after having been a longtime songwriter for the band. He remained as co-lead vocalist alongside the returning Adam Gontier from 2024.
