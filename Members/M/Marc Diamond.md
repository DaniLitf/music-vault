# Marc Diamond

**Guitarist. American.**

Marc Diamond is an American guitarist best known for playing with [[Artists/T/The Dwarves]] under the name The Fresh Prince of Darkness. He has also worked with Motochrist and [[Artists/M/Mondo Generator]], linking the Dwarves to the broader Los Angeles punk and desert-rock scenes.
