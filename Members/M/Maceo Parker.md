# Maceo Parker

**Saxophone. American. Horn player for [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Maceo Parker joined James Brown's band in 1964, later leading his own group Maceo & All the King's Men, then rejoined Brown before leaving with trombonist Fred Wesley and other Brown band members in 1975 to join George Clinton's Parliament-Funkadelic as part of the Horny Horns.
