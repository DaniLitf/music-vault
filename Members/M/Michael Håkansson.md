# Michael Håkansson

**Bassist. Swedish.**

Michael Håkansson is a Swedish bassist who supported [[Artists/A/All Ends]] as one of several touring/session bass players in the mid-2000s, prior to Anders Janfalk becoming the band's permanent bassist in 2008.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/A/All Ends]] – bass (c. 2005–2008)
