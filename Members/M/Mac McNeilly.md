# Mac McNeilly

**Drummer. American.**

Mac McNeilly is an American drummer best known as the classic-era drummer for [[Artists/T/The Jesus Lizard]]. His precise, powerful playing shaped the band's albums from *Head* through *Down*, and he returned for the band's reunion periods.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Jesus Lizard]] – drums (1989–1996, 2008–2010, 2017–present)
