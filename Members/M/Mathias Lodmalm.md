# Mathias Lodmalm

**Vocals, guitars, keyboards. Swedish.**

Mathias Lodmalm founded [[Artists/C/Cemetary]] in Borås, Sweden in 1989 and has remained its only constant member through the band's shift from death metal to gothic metal and across all of its hiatuses and reunions. During a mid-2000s break from Cemetary he recorded similarly styled material under the aliases Sundown and Cemetary 1213.
