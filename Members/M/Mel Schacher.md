# Mel Schacher

**Bassist. Born April 8, 1951.**

Mel Schacher is an American bassist best known as the bassist for Grand Funk Railroad, joining Mark Farner and Don Brewer as the third member of the original trio in 1969. Before Grand Funk, he played with ? and the Mysterians, performing on their classic hit "96 Tears." Schacher has been a constant presence in Grand Funk Railroad through every era and remains the bassist in the current touring lineup.
