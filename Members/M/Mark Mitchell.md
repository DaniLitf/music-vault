# Mark Mitchell

**Vocalist.**

Mark Mitchell is an American hardcore vocalist and a founding member of Florida vegan straight edge metalcore band [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]], which he formed in December 1993 with guitarist Richard Thurston and drummer Joshua Williams. He was replaced by Damien Moyal in September 1994 but rejoined after Moyal's 1995 departure, fronting the band during the *Deforestation* era until Moyal returned in late 1996.
