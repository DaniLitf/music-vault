# Masami Akita

**Masami Akita (born December 7, 1956, in Tokyo, Japan) is a Japanese noise musician who records under the name Merzbow, one of the most prolific and influential figures in noise music with hundreds of releases since 1979.**

Akita named his project after Merzbau, Kurt Schwitters' Dadaist construction project. He has worked across audio cassette, vinyl, CD, and digital formats, exploring extreme noise as a compositional medium. He is a committed animal rights activist and his later work often carries political themes related to animal liberation.
