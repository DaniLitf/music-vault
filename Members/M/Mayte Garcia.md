# Mayte Garcia

**Dancer, vocalist. Born 12 November 1973 in St. Paul, Minnesota.**

Mayte Garcia is an American dancer and singer who joined Prince's New Power Generation in 1994. She married Prince in 1996 and appeared on the *Exodus* album and *The Gold Experience*. After their divorce in 2000 she pursued acting and authored a memoir.
