# Marc Hudson

**Bass, organ, production. American.**

Marc Hudson is an American musician, recording engineer, and producer based at his own studio, Rancho Recordo, near Fenton, Michigan. A longtime collaborator and touring member of [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]], he produced the band's *Shape Shift with Me* (2016) before joining frontperson [[Members/L/Laura Jane Grace|Laura Jane Grace]] and drummer [[Members/A/Atom Willard|Atom Willard]] as bassist and organist in [[Artists/D/Devouring Mothers|Devouring Mothers]]. See [[Producers/M/Marc Hudson]] for his production credits.
