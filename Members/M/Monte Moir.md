# Monte Moir

**Keyboardist and vocalist.**

Monte Moir is an American keyboardist who was a founding member of The Time, playing keyboards from 1981 to 1983 before departing alongside Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis. He later worked with Jam and Lewis on various production projects and returned to The Time for their 1990 reunion. He toured with the band periodically through the 2000s and 2010s, leaving permanently in 2019.
