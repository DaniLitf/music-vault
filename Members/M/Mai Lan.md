# Mai Lan

**Vocalist, songwriter.**

Mai Lan is the stage name of Mai Lan Chapiron, a French-Vietnamese singer, songwriter, and visual artist from Paris. She featured prominently on [[Artists/M/M83]]'s album *Junk* (2016), singing on several tracks including the singles "Go!" and "Bibi the Dog", and has released her own albums including *Autopilote* (2018).

## Associated Artists
