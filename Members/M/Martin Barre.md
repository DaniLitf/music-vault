# Martin Barre

**British guitarist. Born 17 November 1946, died 5 January 2024.**

Martin Barre was a British guitarist best known as the lead guitarist of progressive rock band Jethro Tull, a position he held from 1969 to 2012. He replaced Mick Abrahams and became one of the longest-serving members of the band.
