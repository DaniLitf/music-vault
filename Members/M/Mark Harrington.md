# Mark Harrington

**Bassist. British.**

Mark Harrington, formerly of the London-based band Demagogue, was hired as To-Mera's bassist in early 2008 after founding member Lee Barrett departed following the release of *Delusions*. He remained the band's bassist through their final album *Exile* (2012) and the band's subsequent hiatus.
