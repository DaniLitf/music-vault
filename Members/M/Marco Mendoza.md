# Marco Mendoza

**Bassist. Mexican-American.**

Marco Mendoza is a Mexican-American bassist renowned for his work with Thin Lizzy and Whitesnake. He played bass for The Dead Daisies in 2013 and again from 2014 to 2019, anchoring their rhythm section across multiple albums and tours.
