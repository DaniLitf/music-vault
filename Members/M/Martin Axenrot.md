# Martin Axenrot

**Drummer. Born 1979-08-25 in Sweden.**

Martin Axenrot is a Swedish drummer who served as Opeth's drummer from 2006 to 2021, appearing on *Watershed* (2008) through *In Cauda Venenum* (2019). He is also the longtime drummer of Bloodbath, the Swedish death metal supergroup, and has played with Witchery and other extreme metal acts. His powerful and technically precise style suited both Opeth's progressive evolution and Bloodbath's brutal death metal.
