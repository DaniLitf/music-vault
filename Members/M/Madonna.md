# Madonna

**Madonna (born Madonna Louise Ciccone, August 16, 1958, in Bay City, Michigan) is an American singer, songwriter, actress, and entrepreneur, known as the "Queen of Pop" and one of the best-selling music artists of all time.**

Madonna moved to New York City in 1977 and released her self-titled debut album in 1983. She became a global icon through constant reinvention of her music and image across four decades, from "Material Girl" to "Vogue" to "Ray of Light." She is celebrated for using her platform to advocate for LGBTQ+ rights and for pushing boundaries of sexuality and religion in art.
