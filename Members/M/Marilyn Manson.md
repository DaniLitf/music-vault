# Marilyn Manson

**American singer and songwriter. Born 5 January 1969.**

Brian Hugh Warner, known professionally as Marilyn Manson, is an American singer, songwriter, and musician best known as the founder, lead vocalist, and only constant member of the industrial metal band that bears his stage name. He is known for his controversial image and lyrics, and for his theatrical live performances.
