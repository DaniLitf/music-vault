# Mattias Hellberg

**Guitar. Swedish. Born 1973-06-13.**

Mattias Hellberg is a Swedish musician from Karlstad. He joined [[Artists/T/The Hellacopters]] as a touring guitarist in 1998, filling in on their U.S./Canada tour after Dregen's departure, and went on to work as a session and touring musician with numerous other Swedish rock acts.
