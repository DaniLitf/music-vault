# Mark Knopfler

**Guitarist, singer, songwriter, producer. Born August 12, 1949 in Glasgow, Scotland.**

Mark Knopfler is a British guitarist, singer, and songwriter best known as the frontman and lead guitarist of Dire Straits, one of the world's best-selling bands. Known for his distinctive fingerstyle guitar technique and literate songwriting, he wrote hits including "Sultans of Swing," "Money for Nothing," and "Brothers in Arms." Beyond Dire Straits, he has released numerous solo albums, composed film scores (*The Princess Bride*, *Local Hero*), and produced albums for artists including Emmylou Harris, Bob Dylan, and Randy Newman.
