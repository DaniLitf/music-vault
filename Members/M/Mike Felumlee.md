# Mike Felumlee

**Drums, percussion. American.**

Mike Felumlee is an American drummer from Crystal Lake, Illinois, best known as the drummer of [[Artists/S/Smoking Popes|Smoking Popes]]. After that band's 1999 breakup he joined [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio|Alkaline Trio]], recording *From Here to Infirmary* (2001) before leaving the same year due to personal friction with [[Members/M/Matt Skiba|Matt Skiba]]. He went on to co-found the short-lived band Duvall with Smoking Popes singer Josh Caterer, and later rejoined Smoking Popes.
