# Mark Hockings

**British vocalist, lyricist, and guitarist for Mesh. Born in England.**

Mark Hockings co-founded Mesh in Bristol in 1991 with Richard Silverthorn after meeting at a concert. He writes all of Mesh's lyrics and handles lead vocals and guitar. Under the solo project name Blackcarburning, he released the debut album *Watching Sleepers* in 2023.
