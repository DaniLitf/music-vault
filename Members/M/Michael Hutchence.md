# Michael Hutchence

**Vocals. Australian. 1960–1997.**

Michael Hutchence was the lead singer of Australian rock band [[Artists/I/INXS]], known for his charismatic stage presence throughout the 1980s and 1990s. He sang lead vocals on the track "The King Is Gone" for [[Artists/T/The Heads]]' *No Talking, Just Head* (1996), one of his final recordings before his death in 1997.
