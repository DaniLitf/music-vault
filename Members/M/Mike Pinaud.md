# Mike Pinaud

**Drummer.**

Mike Pinaud is an American drummer best known for his work with the metalcore band [[Artists/C/Curl Up and Die]], as well as [[Artists/A/Airiel]], [[Artists/T/Tomorrows Gone]], and [[Artists/Z/Zero in Trust]]. He was the credited drummer of [[Artists/E/Explode and Make Up]] around the time of the band's 2009 recording sessions, though session musician [[Members/T/Tim Den]] ultimately played drums on the group's self-titled EP.
