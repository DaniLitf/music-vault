# Mick "Irrumator" Kenney

**Multi-instrumentalist. British.**

Mick Kenney is an English multi-instrumentalist from Birmingham who co-founded extreme metal band Anaal Nathrakh in 1999 with vocalist Dave Hunt, handling guitars, bass, drums, and programming across the band's entire discography. He also leads the grindcore/electronic project Manes and has produced numerous other extreme metal releases.
