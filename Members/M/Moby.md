# Moby

**Moby (born Richard Melville Hall, September 11, 1965, in New York City) is an American electronic musician, DJ, and singer-songwriter, best known for *Play* (1999), one of the best-selling electronic albums of all time, where every track was licensed for use in films, TV, and commercials.**

Hall adopted the name Moby because he is a distant relative of Herman Melville, author of *Moby-Dick*. He began as a hardcore punk musician before pivoting to electronic music in the late 1980s. He is a long-standing vegan and animal rights activist.
