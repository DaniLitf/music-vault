**Bassist, guitarist, and founding member. Born 2 October 1950.**

Mike Rutherford is the co-founder, bassist, and guitarist of Genesis, serving as one of only two continuous members throughout the band's history alongside Tony Banks. He initially performed as the band's bass guitarist and backing vocalist, frequently playing rhythm guitar on twelve-string guitar alongside successive lead guitarists Anthony Phillips and Steve Hackett. Following Hackett's departure in 1977, Rutherford expanded his role to include lead guitar on studio albums. As one of Genesis's principal songwriters, he penned many of their biggest international hits, including "Follow You Follow Me," "Turn It On Again," "Land of Confusion," and "Throwing It All Away." Beyond Genesis, Rutherford formed Mike + The Mechanics during the band's break in 1985, establishing the side project as a successful chart-topping act.
