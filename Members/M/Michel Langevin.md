# Michel Langevin

**Drums. Born May 30, 1963.**

Michel "Away" Langevin is a Canadian drummer and visual artist from Jonquière, Quebec, who co-founded [[Artists/V/Voivod]] in 1982 and is the band's only constant member across every lineup and album. He is also known for designing most of Voivod's album artwork and its recurring mascot/alter-ego, Korgull the Exterminator.
