# Martín Méndez

**Bassist. Born 1973-06-12 in Montevideo, Uruguay.**

Martín Méndez is a Uruguayan-born bassist who has been the bass player for Opeth since 1997, making him one of the band's longest-serving members. Born in Uruguay, he moved to Sweden as a child and became part of the Swedish rock scene before joining Opeth. His melodic and precise bass work has been a defining element of the band's sound across their transition from death metal to progressive rock.
