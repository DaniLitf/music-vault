# Mike Brown

**Guitarist. American.**

Mike Brown is a guitarist who performed with Dallas punk band [[Artists/H/Hagfish|Hagfish]] in 1992, brought into the band by drummer John Frum to expand the band's sound. Brown and Frum's tenure with Hagfish was brief, ending after an attempted recording project due to internal conflict. The Blair brothers subsequently continued Hagfish as a core duo, recruiting new vocalist George Stroud Reagan III and drummer Tony Barsotti for the band's stable lineup.
