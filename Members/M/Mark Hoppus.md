# Mark Hoppus

**Bass, co-lead vocals. Born 1972-03-15.**

Mark Hoppus is an American musician, co-founder, bassist, and co-lead vocalist of [[Artists/B/Blink-182]], and the only member to have remained in the band throughout its entire history since 1992. Known for his well-defined midrange bass tone and self-deprecating pop-punk lyricism, he formed [[Artists/0-9/+44]] with Travis Barker during Blink-182's 2005–2009 hiatus and the synth-driven duo [[Artists/S/Simple Creatures]] with All Time Low's Alex Gaskarth in 2018. He was diagnosed with lymphoma in 2021 and declared cancer-free later that year, an event that helped precipitate Tom DeLonge's return to the band.
