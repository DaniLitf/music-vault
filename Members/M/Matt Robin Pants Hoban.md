# Matt "Robin Pants" Hoban

**Guitarist. British.**

Matt "Robin Pants" Hoban is an English guitarist who played for deathgrind band Gorerotted from 2001 to 2007, appearing on the albums *Only Tools and Corpses* (2003) and *A New Dawn for the Dead* (2005). He parted ways with the band in summer 2007 after six years, and went on to join the band The Agonyst.
