# Matt Rubano

**Bass. Born 1977-03-10.**

Matt Rubano is an American bassist who began his career at 21 playing bass on Lauryn Hill's Grammy-winning *The Miseducation of Lauryn Hill* (1998). He joined [[Artists/T/Taking Back Sunday|Taking Back Sunday]] in 2003, appearing on albums including *Where You Want to Be* and *Louder Now* before departing in 2010, and became the bassist of [[Artists/A/Angels & Airwaves|Angels & Airwaves]] in 2019, replacing Matt Wachter.
