# Mikael Åkerfeldt

**Vocalist, guitarist, composer. Born 1974-04-17 in Stockholm, Sweden.**

Mikael Åkerfeldt is a Swedish musician and the sole constant member and primary creative force of Opeth, which he has led since joining in 1989 and has steered through a remarkable stylistic evolution from death metal to progressive rock. He is widely regarded as one of the most accomplished vocalists in metal, capable of shifting between a deep death growl and a clear melodic tone within the same song. He is also a member of the Swedish death metal supergroup Bloodbath, for which he served as vocalist on their early recordings.
