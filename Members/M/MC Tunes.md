# MC Tunes

**MC Tunes (born Nicholas Lockett, c. 1970, in Manchester, England) is a British rapper best known for his 1990 collaboration with 808 State on *The North at Its Heights*, a landmark fusion of rave music and hip-hop.**

Lockett worked with 808 State on the singles "The Only Rhyme That Bites" and the album *Tunes Splits the Atom*. After the collaboration, he largely stepped away from music. He remains associated with the early Manchester rave scene.
