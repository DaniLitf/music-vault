# Mark Chatfield

**Guitarist. American.**

Mark Chatfield is an American guitarist who joined Grand Funk Railroad in 2024, replacing Bruce Kulick. He previously played with The Godz, Rosie, and Bob Seger and the Silver Bullet Band.
