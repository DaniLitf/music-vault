# Mark Jonathan Davis

**Vocalist and comedian. Born 29 June 1963 in Los Angeles, California, USA.**

Mark Jonathan Davis is an American singer and entertainer who created and performs as the lounge singer character Richard Cheese, known for covering hip-hop, heavy metal, and pop songs in a swing and big band style.
