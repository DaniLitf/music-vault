# Merit Hemmingson

**Merit Hemmingson (born October 13, 1950, in Stockholm, Sweden) is a Swedish musician, accordion player, and vocalist, a pioneering figure in Swedish folk music who fused traditional folk with jazz and psychedelic elements in the early 1970s.**

Hemmingson was part of the Swedish folk revival and progressive rock scene in the early 1970s and collaborated with various Swedish artists. She is associated with the progg movement in Swedish music.
