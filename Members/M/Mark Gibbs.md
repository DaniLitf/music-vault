# Mark Gibbs

**Guitarist. American.**

Mark Gibbs is an American guitarist who joined the Cleveland thrash/power metal band Purgatory in 2024, becoming the band's guitarist during their most recent reformation. He has also played in Boulder, Procreation, Sweet Roxx, and Sloth.
