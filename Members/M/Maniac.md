# Maniac

**Norwegian vocalist. Born 4 February 1969.**

Sven Erik Kristiansen, known as Maniac, is a Norwegian vocalist best known as the vocalist of Mayhem (1986–1987, 1995–2004). He also fronted the band Skitliv.
