# Mick Brockett

**Mick Brockett is a British lighting and visual effects designer who served as the fifth member of Nektar from 1969 to 1980, creating the elaborate light shows that were integral to the band's live performances.**

Brockett's light shows were considered part of Nektar's artistic identity rather than mere staging, earning him unofficial membership status within the band.
