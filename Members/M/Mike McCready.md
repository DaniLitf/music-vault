# Mike McCready

**Lead guitarist. Born 5 April 1966 in Pensacola, Florida, USA.**

Mike McCready is the lead guitarist and a founding member of Pearl Jam. Alongside his work with Pearl Jam he co-founded the Seattle supergroup Mad Season in 1994 with Layne Staley of Alice in Chains, and briefly played guitar with Mind Funk.
