# Mark Deutrom

**Bassist, producer. American.**

Mark Deutrom is an American musician and producer who produced early [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] material and later joined the band on bass. He played with Melvins during the Atlantic Records era.

- [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] – bass, production (1993–1998)
