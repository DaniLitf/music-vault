# Mick Ronson

**Guitarist, arranger. British.**

Mick Ronson was a British guitarist and arranger best known as the lead guitarist of David Bowie's backing band the Spiders from Mars during the Ziggy Stardust era, and for his string arrangements on Bowie's early-1970s albums. After leaving Bowie's band in 1974 he briefly joined Mott the Hoople, and later played guitar in Bob Dylan's Rolling Thunder Revue tour (1975–76). From 1975 until his death he was former Mott the Hoople frontman Ian Hunter's most frequent collaborator, playing guitar and producing across many of Hunter's solo albums. He died of liver cancer in 1993.
