# Miko Weaver

**Rhythm guitarist.**

Michael "Miko" Weaver is an American guitarist who performed rhythm guitar for Prince's backing band from 1980 to 1986. He was part of the touring and recording lineup that appeared on *Controversy*, *1999*, *Purple Rain*, *Around the World in a Day*, and *Parade*.
