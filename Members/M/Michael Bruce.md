# Michael Bruce

**Rhythm guitarist, keyboardist, and founding member of the Alice Cooper band. Born 1948-03-16.**

Michael Bruce is an American musician best known as the rhythm guitarist and keyboardist for the original Alice Cooper band. He joined the band in 1966 after replacing John Tatum in their earlier incarnation, the Spiders. Bruce was a prolific songwriter for the band, co-writing many of their classic hits including "I'm Eighteen," "No More Mr. Nice Guy," and "Billion Dollar Babies."

After the Alice Cooper band dissolved in 1975, Bruce formed the short-lived spin-off band Billion Dollar Babies, releasing the album *Battle Axe* (1977). He later reunited with Alice Cooper for several reunion shows and contributed to later albums including *Welcome 2 My Nightmare* (2011), *Detroit Stories* (2021), and the 2025 reunion album *The Revenge of Alice Cooper*.
