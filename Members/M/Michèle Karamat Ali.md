**Vocalist. Dutch.**

Michèle Karamat Ali is a Dutch singer who performed as a touring vocalist for [[Artists/0-9/2 Unlimited]] during the 2000s, appearing alongside [[Members/M/Marjon van Iwaarden]] in later live incarnations of the group. Little else is publicly documented about her career outside this period.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/0-9/2 Unlimited]] – touring vocals (2000s)
