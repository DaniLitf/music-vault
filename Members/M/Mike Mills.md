# Mike Mills

**Bassist, keyboardist, vocalist. Born 1958-12-17 in Orange County, California, USA.**

Mike Mills is the bassist, keyboardist, and backing vocalist of R.E.M., whose melodic contributions gave the band a warmth often contrasted with Michael Stipe's more cryptic lyricism. He co-wrote many R.E.M. songs and has remained active in various collaborations since the band's dissolution in 2011.
