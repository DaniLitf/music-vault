# Mariqueen Maandig

**Vocalist, keyboardist. Born 1985.**

Mariqueen Maandig is an American vocalist best known as the frontwoman of the electronic rock band West Indian Girl, with whom she recorded two albums in the mid-2000s. She married Trent Reznor in 2009 and co-founded How to Destroy Angels the same year, serving as co-lead vocalist on all of the band's releases including the debut studio album *Welcome Oblivion* (2013).
