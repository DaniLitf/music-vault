# Mike Inez

**Bass. Born May 14, 1966.**

Mike Inez is an American bassist best known as the bassist of [[Artists/A/Alice in Chains]] since 1993 and for his earlier work with [[Artists/O/Ozzy Osbourne]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/O/Ozzy Osbourne]] – bass (1989–1993)
- [[Artists/A/Alice in Chains]] – bass (1993–present)

## Biography

Inez replaced Mike Starr as Alice in Chains' bassist in 1993, just as the band was reaching the height of its fame with the *Dirt* album cycle. Before joining, he had served as bassist for Ozzy Osbourne from 1989 to 1993, touring extensively in support of Osbourne's solo albums. Inez has remained Alice in Chains' bassist through the band's hiatus and reformation.
