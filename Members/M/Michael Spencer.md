# Michael Spencer

**Bass. American.**

Michael Spencer is an American bassist who took over for Jason Newsted in [[Artists/F/Flotsam and Jetsam]] in late 1986, playing on the bulk of the band's commercial-era albums, and later returned for a second stint in the 2010s.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/F/Flotsam and Jetsam]] – bass (1986–1991, 2013–2020)
