# Mark Templin

**Guitarist. American.**

Mark Templin is an American guitarist who was a founding member of [[Artists/T/Ten Inch Men]], playing on all of their releases from the 1986 EP *Hours in Pain* through the 1993 album *Pretty Vultures*.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/Ten Inch Men]] – guitar (1983–1994)
