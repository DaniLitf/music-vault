# Martyn Clément

**Guitar (2019–present).**

Martyn Clément is a French guitarist best known as the lead guitarist for the experimental project [[Artists/I/Igorrr]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Igorrr]] – guitar (2019–present)

## Biography

Clément joined Igorrr in 2019, initially as a touring guitarist, and became an official member during the lineup expansion of 2021 after the departure of Laurent Lunoir and Laure Le Prunenec. He has performed on all subsequent Igorrr live shows and recordings.
