# Maynard James Keenan

**Vocals. Born April 17, 1964.**

Maynard James Keenan is an American vocalist best known as the frontman of [[Artists/T/Tool]], [[Artists/A/A Perfect Circle]], and [[Artists/P/Puscifer]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/Tool]] – vocals, co-founder (1990–present)
- [[Artists/A/A Perfect Circle]] – vocals, co-founder (1999–present)
- [[Artists/P/Puscifer]] – vocals, founder (2003–present)

## Biography

Keenan co-founded Tool in Los Angeles in 1990, and the band became one of the most critically acclaimed acts in progressive and alternative metal. Alongside Tool, he co-founded A Perfect Circle in 1999 with guitarist Billy Howerdel, producing a more melancholic and introspective sound. In 2003 he launched Puscifer as a creative outlet for experimental and eclectic material, describing it as "a creative expulsion." He is also a winemaker, operating Caduceus Cellars in Arizona.
