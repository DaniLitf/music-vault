# Mike Mushok

**Guitar. American. Founding member of [[Artists/S/Staind]].**

Mike Mushok co-founded Staind in 1995, bringing in drummer Jon Wysocki, and has remained its lead guitarist through all of the band's active periods. During Staind's early-2010s hiatus he joined Jason Newsted's band [[Artists/N/Newsted]] (2013–2014), and in 2014 co-founded [[Artists/S/Saint Asonia]] with former Three Days Grace singer [[Members/A/Adam Gontier]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/N/Newsted]] – guitar (2013–2014)
- [[Artists/S/Saint Asonia]] – lead guitar (2014–2024, 2025–present, co-founder)
