**Bassist. Died 20 November 2025.**

Gary Mounfield, known professionally as Mani, was an English bassist best known for his work with The Stone Roses and Primal Scream. He joined Primal Scream as full-time bassist in 1996, appearing on five consecutive albums from Vanishing Point (1997) through Beautiful Future (2008) before leaving in 2011 to rejoin The Stone Roses during their reunion. Mani was a key figure in both bands' distinctive sounds. He died on 20 November 2025, aged 63, due to respiratory issues caused by emphysema.
