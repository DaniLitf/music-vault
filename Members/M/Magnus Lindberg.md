# Magnus Lindberg

**Electronics, guitar. Swedish.**

Magnus Lindberg co-founded [[Artists/C/Cult of Luna]] in 1998, originally on drums before moving to electronics, percussion, and guitar to handle the band's ambient and textural elements.
