# Matt Lynch

**Drummer. American.**

Matt Lynch is an American drummer known for his work with Cynic and Trioscapes, who joined Dan Briggs and Richard Henshall's progressive jazz-fusion project Nova Collective as it expanded from a duo into a full band.
