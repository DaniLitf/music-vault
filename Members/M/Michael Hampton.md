# Michael Hampton

**Guitar. American. Lead guitarist of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Michael Hampton was recruited into Parliament-Funkadelic in 1973 at age 17 to replace Eddie Hazel, after an impromptu performance of Hazel's signature song "Maggot Brain." Known as "Kidd Funkadelic," he became the collective's lead guitarist for decades, credited with pushing the band's sound toward heavier rock and metal-influenced playing, before leaving in 2015.
