# Matt Chaikin

**Matt Chaikin** is an American drummer and original drummer of [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]].

- [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]] – drums (1985–1986)
- [[Carnival Art]] – drums

Chaikin was the original drummer of Jane's Addiction, joining the band alongside [[Members/P/Perry Farrell]] and [[Members/E/Eric Avery]]. He failed to show for rehearsals and was replaced by [[Members/S/Stephen Perkins]] in 1986.
