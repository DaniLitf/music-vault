# Mikkey Dee

**Swedish drummer. Born 31 October 1963.**

Micael "Mikkey Dee" Delaoglou is a Swedish drummer who played with King Diamond in the late 1980s before Lemmy Kilmister recruited him for Motörhead in 1992, becoming the band's longest-serving drummer over 23 years. Since 2016 he has drummed for Scorpions.
