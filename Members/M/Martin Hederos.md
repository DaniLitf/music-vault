# Martin Hederos

**Keyboardist. Born 1972-06-30.**

Martin Hederos is a Swedish keyboardist, born in Karlstad, who co-founded Nymphet Noodlers and went on to become a founding member of Gothenburg rock band The Soundtrack of Our Lives in 1995. He has since worked extensively across the Swedish music scene, including with the jazz group Tonbruket and as a film and television composer.
