# Matthew Schultz

**Keyboardist, programmer, sampler. Born in the United States.**

Matthew Schultz is an American keyboardist and programmer known for his long tenure with Pigface, contributing sampling, keyboards, and programming since the band's early years. He has also worked with Lard and his own project Lab Report.
