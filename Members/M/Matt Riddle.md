# Matt Riddle

**Bassist, vocalist. American.**

Matt Riddle is the founding bassist and co-songwriter of the Southern California punk rock band [[Artists/F/Face to Face]], formed in 1991 with Trever Keith and Rob Kurth. Riddle was the band's primary bass player until 1995, after which he left to pursue other projects including stints with [[Artists/P/Pulley]], [[Artists/N/No Use for a Name]], and other bands. He was instrumental in the band's early sound and songwriting during their debut album era.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/F/Face to Face]] – bass guitar, vocals (1991–1995)
- [[Artists/P/Pulley]] – bass guitar
- [[Artists/N/No Use for a Name]] – bass guitar (post-Face to Face)
