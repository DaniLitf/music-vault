# Molly Spiers-MacLeod

**Bassist, backing vocalist. British.**

Molly Spiers-MacLeod is a bassist and backing vocalist who co-founded [[Artists/T/The Featherz]] after leaving Poussez Posse. She is the daughter of Spizz and was part of the band's original London lineup with Danie Cox and Rachael Smith.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Featherz]] – bass, backing vocals (2011–2014)
- [[Artists/P/Poussez Posse]] – bass, backing vocals (2011)
