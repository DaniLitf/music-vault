# Morgan Kibby

**Vocalist, keyboardist, songwriter, producer. Born 14 February 1984.**

Morgan Kibby is an American singer, keyboardist, and composer who was the most prominent collaborator in [[Artists/M/M83]] between 2007 and 2014, co-writing and singing on *Saturdays = Youth* and *Hurry Up, We're Dreaming*. She previously sang in the Los Angeles band [[Artists/T/The Romanovs]] and records her own material as [[Artists/W/White Sea]]. She has since built a career as a songwriter, producer, and film/TV composer.

## Associated Artists
