# Moeh

**Bassist. German.**

Moeh played bass for [[Artists/T/The Inchtabokatables]] after Oliver Riedel left the group. Sources note that Moeh had previously played in the industrial band T.A.S.S., fitting the band's later shift toward industrial textures.
