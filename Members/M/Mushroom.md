# Mushroom

**British musician. Born 10 November 1967.**

Andrew Vowles, known as Mushroom, is a British producer born in Knowle West, Bristol, who came up through the Wild Bunch sound system before co-founding Massive Attack in 1988. He was a key producer on the band's first three albums, leaving shortly after *Mezzanine* (1998).
