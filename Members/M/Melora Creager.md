# Melora Creager

**Vocalist, cellist. Born c. 1966 in USA.**

Melora Creager is the founder, sole consistent member, and creative force behind Rasputina. A classically trained cellist, she developed the band's unusual aesthetic of rock-influenced cello music with Gothic Victorian imagery starting in 1991 in New York City. Rasputina has operated largely as her solo project with a rotating cast of collaborators. She toured support with Nine Inch Nails and Marilyn Manson in the mid-1990s, bringing the band wider visibility.
