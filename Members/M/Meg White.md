# Meg White

**Meg White** (born Megan Martha White, December 10, 1974) is an American drummer and vocalist best known as the drummer of [[Artists/T/The White Stripes]].

- [[Artists/T/The White Stripes]] – drums, percussion, vocals (1997–2011)

White formed The White Stripes with then-husband [[Artists/J/Jack White]] in 1997. Her minimalist drumming style was a defining element of the band's sound. She largely retired from public life after the band's breakup in 2011.
