# Matt Lukin

**American bassist. Born 16 August 1964 in Aberdeen, Washington.**

Matt Lukin co-founded the Melvins in 1983 with Buzz Osborne and Mike Dillard before leaving to co-found Mudhoney in 1988 with former Green River members Mark Arm and Steve Turner, plus drummer Dan Peters. He left Mudhoney in 1999 but briefly rejoined for a tour from late 2000 into January 2001; Pearl Jam's 1996 song "Lukin" is named after him.
