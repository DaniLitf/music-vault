# Maja Francis

**Maja Francis is a Swedish pop singer-songwriter from Gothenburg, known for her soulful voice and emotionally direct songwriting.**

Francis debuted with *Out Loud* in 2013 and gained wider attention after competing in Melodifestivalen, Sweden's Eurovision selection, in 2014. Her music blends contemporary pop production with influences from soul and R&B.
