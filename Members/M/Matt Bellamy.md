# Matt Bellamy

**Lead vocalist, guitarist, and pianist. Born 1978.**

Matt Bellamy is the co-founder and lead vocalist of Muse, serving as the band's primary songwriter and multi-instrumentalist since the band's formation in 1994. Known for his expansive vocal range and virtuosic guitar and keyboard work, Bellamy has driven Muse's evolution from alternative rock foundations into progressive, electronic-influenced soundscapes across landmark albums like *Origin of Symmetry* (2001), *Absolution* (2003), and *The Resistance* (2009). His songwriting and performance style have established Muse as one of the defining rock acts of the 21st century.
