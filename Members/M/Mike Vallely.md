# Mike Vallely

**Lead vocals; professional skateboarder. Born 1970-06-29.**

Mike Vallely is an American professional skateboarder and musician who sang a guest appearance with [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] at its 2003 reunion show and was later brought in to replace Ron Reyes mid-performance in November 2013. He remained the band's lead vocalist until January 2025, making him its longest-tenured singer, and is also known for a decades-long professional skateboarding career and membership in several other punk bands.

## Associated Artists
