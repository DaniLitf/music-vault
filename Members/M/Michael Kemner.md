# Michael Kemner

**Bassist. Born 1958 — died 2026.**

Michael Kemner was a German bassist who was an original member of DAF from 1978 to 1980, playing on their debut album. He later became a member of the influential German post-punk band Fehlfarben and the band Mau Mau.
