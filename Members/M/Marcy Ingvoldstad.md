# Marcy Ingvoldstad

**Singer. Born in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

Marcy Ingvoldstad is an American vocalist who was a core member of the Minneapolis funk band 94 East. She sang background vocals and contributed to songwriting alongside Pepé Willie and Kristie Lazenberry, and was one of the three core members of 94 East alongside Pepé Willie and Kristie Lazenberry.
