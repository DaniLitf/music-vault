# Martin Price

**Keyboards, production. Born March 26, 1955.**

Martin Price is an English musician and record label owner, best known as a founding member of [[Artists/0-9/808 State]] and the owner of Manchester's influential Eastern Bloc Records.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/0-9/808 State]] – keyboards (1987–1991)

## Biography

Price owned Eastern Bloc Records and founded the Creed label in Manchester, which became a hub for the city's emerging acid house scene. He co-founded 808 State in 1987 with Graham Massey and Gerald Simpson. After leaving the band in 1991, he managed the group Kaliphz and released solo material under the name Switzerland.
