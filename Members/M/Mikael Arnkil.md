# Mikael Arnkil

**Bassist. Finnish.**

Mikael Arnkil, known by his nickname "Arkki," has been the bassist for Impaled Nazarene since 2000, providing the low-end foundation for the band's extreme metal compositions. His bass work has contributed to the band's aggressive sound across multiple albums and live performances.
