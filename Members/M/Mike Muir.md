# Mike Muir

**Vocals (1989–present).**

Mike Muir is an American vocalist best known as the frontman of the hardcore punk band [[Artists/S/Suicidal Tendencies]] and the funk metal side project [[Artists/I/Infectious Grooves]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/S/Suicidal Tendencies]] – vocals (1980–present)
- [[Artists/I/Infectious Grooves]] – vocals (1989–present)

## Biography

Muir formed Suicidal Tendencies in 1980 and has been its only constant member. In 1989, he founded Infectious Grooves as a funk-oriented outlet, releasing four albums. Known for his energetic stage presence and distinctive vocal style, Muir has remained active with both bands for over four decades.
