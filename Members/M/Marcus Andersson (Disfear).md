# Marcus Andersson

**Drums. Swedish.**

Marcus Andersson is a Swedish drummer who has been the percussionist for crust punk band [[Artists/D/Disfear]] since 1998, joining alongside vocalist Tomas Lindberg during the band's reformation. He has appeared on all Disfear releases from *Misanthropic Generation* onward.
