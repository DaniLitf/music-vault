# Mike Patton

**Mike Patton is an American vocalist and multi-instrumentalist from Eureka, California, widely regarded as one of the most versatile singers in rock, known for his five-octave range and membership in Faith No More, Mr. Bungle, Tomahawk, Fantômas, and many other projects.**
