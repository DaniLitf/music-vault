# Milo Aukerman

**Vocals. Born 1963-01-01.**

Milo Aukerman is an American punk singer and biochemist, the iconic frontman of [[Artists/D/Descendents]] since 1980. His caricature — the bespectacled "Milo" nerd drawn by classmate Roger Deuerlein and Jeff Atkinson — has served as the band's mascot since *Milo Goes to College* (1982), an album titled after his departure to study biology. He repeatedly left the band for a career in biochemistry (earning a PhD and working as a research scientist at DuPont) before committing to music full-time in 2016.
