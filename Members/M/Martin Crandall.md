# Martin Crandall

**Bassist and keyboardist. American.**

Martin Crandall is an American musician best known as the bassist and keyboardist for The Shins from the band's formation in 1996 until 2009. He contributed to the first three albums, including the critically acclaimed *Oh, Inverted World* and *Chutes Too Narrow*.
