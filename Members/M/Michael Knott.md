# Michael Knott

**Singer-songwriter. American.**

Michael Knott is an American Christian alternative rock singer-songwriter who fronted LSU (L.S.U.), Aunt Bettys, and Cush. Known for his distinctive vocal style and prolific solo career, he was also in Bombay Babies and Lifesavors, and was closely associated with the Tooth & Nail / Northern Records scene.
