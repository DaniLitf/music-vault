# Mark 'Oh

**German dance producer and DJ. Born in Germany.**

Mark 'Oh (born Marko Albrecht) is a German dance music producer best known for his eurodance and trance productions in the 1990s. He collaborated with Mesh on the track "Waves" (2000), which reached position 83 on the German singles chart.
