# Mike Scaccia

**American guitarist. Born 14 July 1965, died 22 December 2012.**

Mike Scaccia was an American guitarist best known as the lead guitarist of Ministry (1990–2012) and also of the bands Rigor Mortis and DevilElectric. He died of a heart attack on stage in 2012.
