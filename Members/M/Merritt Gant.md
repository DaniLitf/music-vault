# Merritt Gant

**Guitarist.**

Merritt Gant served as a guitarist in Overkill during the mid-1990s, playing on *W.F.O.* (1994) and *The Killing Kind* (1996) before his departure. He was one of several guitarists who cycled through the band in the post-Bobby Gustafson era.
