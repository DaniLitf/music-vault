# Marty Friedman

**American guitarist. Born 8 December 1962.**

Marty Friedman is an American guitarist best known as the lead guitarist of Megadeth (1990–2000). His neoclassical and Japanese-influenced guitar style became a signature element of Megadeth's sound during their commercial peak.
