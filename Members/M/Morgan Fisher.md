# Morgan Fisher

**Keyboardist. British.**

Morgan Fisher joined [[Artists/M/Mott the Hoople]] on keyboards in 1973, replacing Verden Allen, and remained until the band's 1974 split, later pursuing an experimental solo and film-score career.
