# Martin Molin

**Multi-instrumentalist, inventor. Born November 6, 1980.**

Martin Molin is a Swedish musician and inventor, co-founder of [[Artists/D/Detektivbyrån]] and founder of [[Artists/W/Wintergatan]], internationally famous for building the Marble Machine — a mechanical musical instrument powered by 2,000 marbles — whose 2016 YouTube video has been viewed over 275 million times.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/D/Detektivbyrån]] – glockenspiel, traktofon, toy piano, theremin (2005–2010, founding member)
- [[Artists/W/Wintergatan]] – vibraphone, electronics, Marble Machine (2012–present, founder)
