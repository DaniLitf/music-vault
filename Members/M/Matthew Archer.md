# Matthew Archer

**Drummer.**

Matthew Archer was the drummer of Paradise Lost from 1990 to 1994, appearing on *Gothic* (1991), *Shades of God* (1992), and *Icon* (1993). His drumming was a key part of the band's classic early sound. He departed in 1994 and was replaced by Lee Morris.
