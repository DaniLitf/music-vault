# Matt Skiba

**Guitarist, lead vocalist. American.**

Matt Skiba is a musician from Chicago best known as co-founder, guitarist, and co-lead vocalist of [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio]] since forming the band in 1996. From 2015 to 2022 he served as co-lead vocalist and guitarist of [[Artists/B/Blink-182]], recording the albums *California* (2016) and *Nine* (2019) after Tom DeLonge's departure. He has also released solo material and fronted the side project [[Artists/H/Heavens]].
