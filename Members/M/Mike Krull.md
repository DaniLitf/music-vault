# Mike Krull

**Drums. Fill-in musician.**

Mike Krull is an American drummer from the San Diego area who briefly filled in for Scott Raynor in [[Artists/B/Blink-182]] (then called Blink) in 1994, when Raynor's family relocated to Reno, Nevada. Little else is documented about his career; the band eventually saved money to fly Raynor out to shows until he moved back in 1995.
