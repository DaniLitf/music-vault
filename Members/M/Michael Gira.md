# Michael Gira

**Singer, guitarist, songwriter. Born 19 February 1954 in Los Angeles, California, USA.**

Michael Gira is the founder, frontman, and only constant member of Swans. He contributed vocals to Pigface and is known for his intense, experimental approach to music.
