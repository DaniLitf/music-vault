**Vocalist.**

Melissa Beckford was a vocalist who briefly performed with [[Artists/T/Technotronic|Technotronic]] in 1990, appearing on the single "Turn It Up" alongside Colin Case. She joined the project for a short period after Ya Kid K's initial departure and was presented as a new frontwoman.

Her tenure with Technotronic was brief, and she left the group alongside Colin Case after the "Turn It Up" single failed to replicate the success of earlier hits.
