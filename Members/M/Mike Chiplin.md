**Drummer. Welsh musician.**

Mike Chiplin was the founding drummer of Lostprophets, helping establish the band's rhythm section from the group's formation in 1997 until his departure in 2005. He performed on the band's first two studio albums and was instrumental in defining their early sound. After leaving Lostprophets, Chiplin founded Unit 40 Studios in Cilfynydd, Wales, and pursued other musical opportunities.
