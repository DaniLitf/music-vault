# Mower

**Vocalist. British.**

Kieran Moylett, known by the stage name Mower, is a British punk vocalist best known as the long-time frontman of Chaos UK. He joined the band in 1983 and performed on the classic lineup albums *Short Sharp Shock*, *Earslaughter*, and *The Chipping Sodbury Bonfire Tapes*. He left briefly in 1989 but returned in the 1990s and continues to perform with the band.
