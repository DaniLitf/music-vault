# Mark Jackson

**British drummer, former live member of VNV Nation.**

Mark Jackson was the live drummer for VNV Nation from the mid-1990s, appearing in concert lineups through the band's rise in the futurepop scene. He left VNV Nation in November 2017 to pursue other creative paths.
