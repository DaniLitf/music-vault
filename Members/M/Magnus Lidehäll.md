# Magnus Lidehäll

**Producer, composer, and songwriter. Born 10 June 1985.**

Magnus Lidehäll, known as Filthy, is a Swedish producer from Uppsala who formed the hip hop duo Afasi & Filthy with rapper Herbert Munkhammar, his schoolmate, releasing music together until 2009. He went on to a prolific production and songwriting career, writing for artists including Kylie Minogue, Britney Spears, and Sky Ferreira, and won Composer of the Year at the Swedish Grammy Awards in 2014.
