# Matt Blakout

**Drummer. Welsh.**

Matt Blakout, formerly of the band City Kidds, joined glam metal band Tigertailz in 2005 as part of their reunion lineup. He was replaced by Robin Guy in 2010 before rejoining the band in 2013.
