# Maria Andersson

**Vocalist and guitarist. Born 4 December 1981 in Umeå.**

Maria Andersson is a Swedish musician who grew up in Robertsfors and co-founded Sahara Hotnights with childhood friends in 1991, serving as the band's lead vocalist and guitarist. The band's international breakthrough came with *Jennie Bomb* (2001).
