**Drummer and percussionist for Cro-Mags. Born March 1969.**

Mackie Jayson is the drummer for Cro-Mags, one of the most influential bands in the New York hardcore scene. As a pivotal figure in NYHC since the late 1980s, Jayson's drumming has defined the sound of hardcore punk and metal fusion. He is recognized as one of the most influential figures in New York hardcore history, bringing technical skill and raw power to the genre.
