# Mike Post

**Composer, record producer, arranger, and musician. Born September 29, 1944.**

Mike Post (born Leland Michael Postil) is an American composer, record producer, arranger, and multi-instrumentalist from Berkeley, California. He is one of the most prolific television theme composers in history, creating iconic themes for *The Rockford Files*, *Hill Street Blues*, *Magnum, P.I.*, *The A-Team*, *L.A. Law*, *Quantum Leap*, and the *Law & Order* franchise. He began his career as a record producer, winning his first Grammy for arranging Mason Williams' "Classical Gas," and later produced albums for Dolly Parton and Van Halen. Post has won five Grammy Awards and an Emmy, and was inducted into the Television Hall of Fame in 2025.
