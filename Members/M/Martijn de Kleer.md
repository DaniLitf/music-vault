# Martijn de Kleer

**Guitarist. Dutch.**

Martijn de Kleer is a Dutch guitarist associated with [[Artists/T/The Legendary Pink Dots]], replacing Bob Pistoor and playing with the group through the 1990s and early 2000s. His solo work is also noted by the band's official listening guide.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Legendary Pink Dots]] – guitar (1990s–2004)
