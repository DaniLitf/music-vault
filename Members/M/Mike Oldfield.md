# Mike Oldfield

**Mike Oldfield (born Michael Gordon Oldfield, May 15, 1953, in Reading, England) is an English multi-instrumentalist, composer, and producer, best known for *Tubular Bells* (1973), the debut release on Virgin Records.**

Oldfield began his career as a session musician and made *Tubular Bells* at age 19, playing nearly every instrument himself. The album became a global phenomenon and launched Richard Branson's Virgin Records. He has continued to release albums across multiple genres including progressive rock, new age, and world music, and performed a notable section of *Tubular Bells* at the opening ceremony of the 2012 London Olympics.
