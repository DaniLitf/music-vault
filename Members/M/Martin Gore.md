# Martin Gore

**Martin Gore is a British guitarist, keyboardist, vocalist, and principal songwriter of Depeche Mode, who wrote virtually all of the band's classic catalogue.**

His dark, sensual lyrics and melodies shaped Depeche Mode's aesthetic, and his guitar playing became increasingly prominent in the band's later, harder rock sound.
