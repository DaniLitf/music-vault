# Martin Renck

**Drums. Active 1993–1996.**

Martin Renck is a Swedish drummer, formerly of [[Artists/T/Teddybears]] during their early hardcore punk period.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/Teddybears]] – drums (1993–1996)

## Biography
Martin Renck played guitar in the early incarnation of Teddybears STHLM during the 1990s, before Klas Åhlund joined the band. He was part of the band's transitional period from hardcore punk towards a more experimental sound. After leaving Teddybears, he left the Stockholm music scene.
