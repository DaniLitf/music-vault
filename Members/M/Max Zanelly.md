# Max Zanelly

**Lead vocals.**

Max Zanelly joined [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] as lead vocalist in April 2025, becoming the only woman to front the band and, after bassist Kira Roessler in 1983, only the second woman ever to join its ranks.

## Associated Artists
