# Marcus Sjöberg

**Drums. Swedish.**

Marcus Sjöberg is a Swedish drummer who joined [[Artists/D/Detektivbyrån]] in 2009 as a replacement for Jon Nils Emanuel Ekström, and co-founded [[Artists/W/Wintergatan]] with Martin Molin after Detektivbyrån's 2010 breakup. He also works as a mixing and mastering engineer.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/D/Detektivbyrån]] – drums (2009–2010)
- [[Artists/W/Wintergatan]] – drums (2012–present, founding member)
