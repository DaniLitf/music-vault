# MC Wildski

**MC Wildski is a British rapper who contributed MC duties to Beats International's early recordings and co-wrote the hit "Dub Be Good to Me."**

He had a brief period of profile in the UK dance and hip-hop scene in the late 1980s and early 1990s before fading from the mainstream.
