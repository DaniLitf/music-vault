# Matt Sweeney

**Guitar, backing vocals. Born 1971.**

Matt Sweeney is an American guitarist and vocalist, known for his work with Chavez, [[Artists/Z/Zwan]], and numerous collaborations.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/Z/Zwan]]

## Biography
Sweeney was the frontman of the indie rock band Chavez in the 1990s. He joined Billy Corgan and Jimmy Chamberlin in Zwan as guitarist and backing vocalist. He has also collaborated extensively with Bonnie "Prince" Billy, Iggy Pop, and others.
