# Mark Alexander

**Bassist. American.**

Mark Alexander is an American bassist best known as the longest-tenured bassist for the Cleveland thrash/power metal band Purgatory, which he co-founded in 1984 with Jeff Hatrix and Kenny Easterly. He played on the band's debut EP *Purgatory* (1985) and full-length *Tied to the Trax* (1986), and participated in every subsequent Purgatory reunion through 2025. He also played in Victor Fist and Chemikill.
