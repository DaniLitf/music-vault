# Martin Atkins

**Drummer and producer. Born 3 August 1959 in Coventry, England.**

Martin Atkins is a British drummer and record producer best known as a founding member of Pigface and as a drummer for Public Image Ltd (1979–1981). He has also played with Killing Joke and Ministry, and founded Invisible Records in 1987. As the driving force behind Pigface's revolving-door collective, he has recorded and toured with hundreds of musicians over three decades.
