# Morris Day

**Lead vocalist, drummer, and frontman. Born 1957-12-13.**

Morris Day is an American singer, songwriter, and actor best known as the frontman of The Time. A childhood friend of Prince, he was chosen to lead the band Prince assembled in 1981. Day's charismatic persona — flashy, comedic, and confident — made him the focal point of the band's live shows and film appearances, most notably in *Purple Rain*. After the Time's initial breakup in 1985, he pursued a solo career and has continued touring with various lineups of the band into the 2020s.
