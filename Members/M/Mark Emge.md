# Mark Emge

**Drummer. American.**

Mark Emge is an American drummer who joined Duraluxe after the band's recording sessions for *Dolorosa* in Chicago. He was brought from Chicago back to Nashville by Chris Colbert and Troy Daugherty, and became the band's drummer following Fred McKay's departure. Emge performed on Duraluxe's split CD with The Lassie Foundation and their second album *The Suitcase* (2002).
