# Marc Verhaeghen

**Belgian electronic musician and producer. Born 1965.**

Marc Verhaeghen is a Belgian electronic musician, vocalist, and producer best known as the founder and sole constant member of the EBM/industrial project Klinik. His work is characterized by dark, minimal electronic sounds and aggressive vocal styles.
