# Markus Nordberg

**Drums. Swedish.**

Markus Nordberg drummed for [[Artists/C/Cemetary]] from 1993 to 1997, spanning the band's transition into gothic metal across *Godless Beauty*, *Black Vanity*, *Sundown*, and *Last Confessions*. He is also credited as an early drummer of [[Artists/C/Ceremonial Oath]].
