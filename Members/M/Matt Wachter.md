# Matt Wachter

**Bass. American.**

Matt Wachter is a retired American bassist who spent six years in [[Artists/0-9/30 Seconds to Mars|30 Seconds to Mars]] (2001–2007) before joining [[Artists/A/Angels & Airwaves|Angels & Airwaves]] in 2007 to replace Ryan Sinn. He remained the band's bassist through *I-Empire*, *Love*, *Love: Part Two*, and *The Dream Walker* until stepping back in 2014 to focus on his family.
