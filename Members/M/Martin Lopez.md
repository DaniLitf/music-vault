# Martin Lopez

**Drummer. Born 1979-03-16 in Sweden.**

Martin Lopez was the drummer of Opeth from 1997 to 2006, appearing on the band's most acclaimed albums including *Blackwater Park* (2001), *Deliverance* (2002), and *Ghost Reveries* (2005). His jazz-influenced, technically demanding playing was considered a key element of Opeth's sound during their most celebrated period. He departed due to health issues and later performed with other Swedish acts.
