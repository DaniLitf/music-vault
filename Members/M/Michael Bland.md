# Michael Bland

**Drummer. Born in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

Michael Bland is an American drummer best known as the original drummer for Prince's New Power Generation. He played on *Diamonds and Pearls*, *Love Symbol Album*, and the NPG's own albums before being dismissed in 1996. He later played with Nick Jonas and the Administration and Soul Asylum.
