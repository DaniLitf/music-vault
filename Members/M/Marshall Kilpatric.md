# Marshall Kilpatric

**Drummer.**

Marshall Kilpatric is an American drummer who held the drum chair in [[Artists/B/Black Light Burns]]' touring band from 2007 to 2011. Before joining, he played in the Kansas post-hardcore band [[Artists/T/The Esoteric]].
