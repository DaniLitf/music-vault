# Marty Balin

**Marty Balin (born Martyn Jerel Buchwald, January 30, 1942 – September 27, 2018) was an American singer-songwriter and co-founding member of Jefferson Airplane.**

Balin co-founded Jefferson Airplane in San Francisco in 1965 and served as the band's original lead vocalist, co-writing songs like "Today" and "Volunteers." He had a reduced role during parts of the Jefferson Starship era but returned for the 1981 hit "Hearts." He pursued a solo career alongside his band work and died in 2018.
