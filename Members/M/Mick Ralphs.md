# Mick Ralphs

**Guitarist. British.**

Mick Ralphs was a founding guitarist of [[Artists/M/Mott the Hoople]] from 1969 to 1973, before leaving to co-found the hard rock band Bad Company with Paul Rodgers, for whom he wrote hits including "Can't Get Enough."
