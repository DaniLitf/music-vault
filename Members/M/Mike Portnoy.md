# Mike Portnoy

**Mike Portnoy is an American drummer and co-founder of Dream Theater, widely regarded as one of the greatest and most technically accomplished drummers in progressive and heavy metal, who temporarily filled in for Avenged Sevenfold after The Rev's death in 2009.**

After departing Dream Theater in 2010 he formed several other bands including Winery Dogs and Flying Colors, and rejoined Dream Theater in 2023.
