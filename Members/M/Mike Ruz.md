**Vocalist. American.**

Mike Ruz is an American vocalist best known as the frontman of the Cleveland sludge metal band Kriadiaz since its formation in 2010. He has also performed as vocalist for the Cleveland power/thrash metal band Ritual and its earlier incarnation Torment. Under the name Mike Lobo, he also fronted the band Loborec.
