# Elijah Shaw

**Bassist. American.**

Elijah "Lij" Shaw is an American musician who served as the original bass guitarist for the Jonas Brothers' early touring band from 2005 to 2007. He played bass during the band's initial rise on the Disney Channel and their first major tour dates.
