**Guitarist. Dutch experimental rock musician.**

Erik Drost is the guitarist of Dutch avant-garde rock band The Legendary Pink Dots, joining the group in 2004 as a replacement for founding member Martijn de Kleer. He has contributed to the band's innovative and experimental releases since the mid-2000s, maintaining the group's unconventional approach to rock music composition and performance.
