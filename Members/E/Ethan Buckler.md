**Multi-instrumentalist. Born 1967.**

Ethan Buckler is an American musician best known as the founding bass player of post-rock pioneers Slint (1986-1987) and the founder/leader of King Kong. Despite his brief tenure with Slint, Buckler's departure from the band proved formative in its later development, as he went on to establish King Kong as a psychedelic/blues-rock project that has released multiple albums since 1989.
