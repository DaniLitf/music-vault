# Eddie Hazel

**Guitar, vocals, songwriter. American. Original lead guitarist of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Eddie Hazel was Funkadelic's original lead guitarist, brought into the fold by his friend Billy Bass Nelson, and was a major creative force on the band's earliest albums. His Hendrix-inspired playing is best captured on the ten-minute guitar solo "Maggot Brain" (1971). He contributed sporadically to the collective after the early 1970s and released solo albums with the group's help, before dying in 1992.
