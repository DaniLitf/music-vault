# Eek-A-Mouse

**Eek-A-Mouse (Ripton Joseph Hylton) is a Jamaican reggae and dancehall artist from Kingston, known for pioneering the "singjay" vocal style that blends singing and toasting, and for his catchphrase syllables "wa-do-dem."**
