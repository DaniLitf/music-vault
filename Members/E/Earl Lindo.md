**Keyboardist. Born 7 August 1953.**

Earl "Wya" Lindo was a Jamaican keyboardist who played with the Wailers during two stints — in 1973 and again from 1978 to 1981 — contributing to albums including *Burnin'*, *Survival*, *Uprising*, and *Confrontation*. He also played with [[Members/T/Tyrone Downie]] in the later line-up.
