**Rapper, producer, vocalist. American. Born August 18, 1969.**

Everlast is a rapper and singer best known as the lead member of the hardcore hip-hop trio House of Pain, where his distinctive voice and Irish-American identity became iconic in 1990s hip-hop. After House of Pain disbanded in 1996, he pursued a successful solo career, transitioning to acoustic rap and singing on his multi-platinum 1998 album Whitey Ford Sings the Blues. He later co-founded the rap supergroup La Coka Nostra with former House of Pain DJ DJ Lethal and others.

## Biography

Erik Schrody grew up in Woodland Hills, California, and attended William Howard Taft High School where he met Danny Boy. Before House of Pain, he released the unsuccessful solo album Forever Everlasting (1990). After House of Pain's initial run ended in 1996, he developed a unique style blending rap with acoustic guitar and singing, achieving mainstream success with his 1998 album Whitey Ford Sings the Blues. Between reunions with House of Pain and La Coka Nostra, he has maintained an active solo recording and touring career.
