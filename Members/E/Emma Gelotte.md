# Emma Gelotte

**Vocalist. Swedish.**

Emma Gelotte is a Swedish singer and the sister of [[Members/B/Björn Gelotte]]. She co-founded [[Artists/A/All Ends]] in 2003 as one of its two vocalists, singing on the band's EP and self-titled debut album before quitting in March 2009 for personal reasons; she was replaced by Jonna Sailon.

## Associated Artists
