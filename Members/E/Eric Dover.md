**Guitarist and vocalist. Born 19 January 1967.**

Eric Dover is an American guitarist and vocalist best known for his work as the lead vocalist on Slash's Snakepit's album "It's Five O'Clock Somewhere" (1995) and for his tenure with Imperial Drag. Dover has built a career spanning multiple rock and metal bands, bringing powerful vocals and skilled guitarwork to various projects. He is known for his versatility across rock and metal genres.

## Biography

Dover first gained significant exposure as a touring guitarist for Jellyfish in 1993–1994, before joining Slash's Snakepit as lead vocalist for their 1995 album release. He subsequently formed Imperial Drag with Roger Manning Jr., the keyboardist from Jellyfish, pursuing a power-pop rock sound. Throughout the late 1990s and 2000s, Dover has maintained a steady career with various projects, including a period as guitarist and vocalist with the Alice Cooper band (2001–2003). He founded the rock band Sextus, which has released multiple albums, and has collaborated with other musicians including Tim Smith. Dover's diverse project work demonstrates his adaptability as both a vocalist and guitarist across rock, metal, and pop-rock styles.
