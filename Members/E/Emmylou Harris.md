# Emmylou Harris

**Emmylou Harris is an American singer-songwriter born on April 2, 1947 in Birmingham, Alabama, widely regarded as one of the most important figures in country and Americana music.**

Harris rose to prominence in the mid-1970s after performing with Gram Parsons on his final two albums. Following Parsons' death in 1973, she launched a solo career that yielded a string of classic country albums. Her 1995 album *Wrecking Ball*, produced by Daniel Lanois, expanded her audience to alternative rock listeners and is considered among the finest albums in Americana. She has won fourteen Grammy Awards and collaborated widely with artists across country, folk, and rock.
