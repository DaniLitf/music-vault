# Eric Avery

**Eric Avery** (born April 25, 1965) is an American bassist and guitarist best known as a founding member and bassist of [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]].

- [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]] – bass, acoustic guitar (1985–1991, 2008–2010, 2022–2024)
- [[Deconstruction (band)|Deconstruction]] – bass (1993–1994)
- [[Polar Bear (band)|Polar Bear]] – bass (2009)

Avery co-founded Jane's Addiction with [[Members/P/Perry Farrell]] and wrote much of the music on the band's early albums. His melodic bass lines were a defining element of the band's sound.
