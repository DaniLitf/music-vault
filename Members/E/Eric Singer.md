# Eric Singer

**Drummer and vocalist. Born May 12, 1958.**

Eric Singer is an American hard rock drummer best known as the drummer for KISS across three stints (1991–1996, 2001–2002, 2004–2023), appearing on albums such as *Revenge* (1992), *Carnival of Souls* (1997), *Sonic Boom* (2009) and *Monster* (2012). He has also performed with Black Sabbath, Alice Cooper, Lita Ford, Badlands, Brian May, Gary Moore, and founded his own side project ESP (Eric Singer Project). Singer has appeared on over 75 albums and 11 EPs throughout his career.
