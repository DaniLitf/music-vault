# Ed O'Brien

**Guitarist. Born 1968-04-15 in Oxford, England.**

Ed O'Brien is a guitarist and founding member of Radiohead. His role in the band has evolved from rhythm guitar to encompassing loops, effects, and atmospheric textures. He is often the member most focused on production and sonic experimentation within the group. He released his debut solo album *Earth* in 2020.
