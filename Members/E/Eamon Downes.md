# Eamon Downes

**Producer/musician. British.**

Eamon Downes is a British electronic musician best known as one half of Liquid, the drum and bass / jungle project behind the 1992 hardcore rave anthem "Sweet Harmony." He was responsible for production on the group's material during the early UK rave scene.
