# Euronymous

**Norwegian guitarist. Born 22 March 1968, died 10 August 1993.**

Øystein Aarseth, known as Euronymous, was a Norwegian guitarist and founding member of Mayhem. He was a central figure in the Norwegian black metal scene, running the record label Deathlike Silence Productions and the record shop Helvete. He was murdered by Varg Vikernes in 1993.
