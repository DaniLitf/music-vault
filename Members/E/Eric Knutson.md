# Eric Knutson

**Vocals. Born March 7, 1965.**

Eric "A.K." Knutson is an American vocalist who joined [[Artists/F/Flotsam and Jetsam]] in 1982 and, despite the band's frequent lineup turnover, has remained its only constant member across more than four decades and fifteen studio albums.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/F/Flotsam and Jetsam]] – vocals (1982–present, founding member)
