**Keyboardist and electronic musician. Born 1970.**

Ed Simons is an English keyboardist and electronic musician best known as the co-founder and keyboard player of The Chemical Brothers, one of the most successful electronic acts of the 1990s and beyond. Alongside Tom Rowlands, Simons helped pioneer the big beat movement and electronic dance music's integration into mainstream popular culture. His work is characterized by dense arrangements, sampling, and the blending of diverse musical influences.

## Biography

Simons met Tom Rowlands while studying at university, and together they formed The Dust Brothers (later renamed The Chemical Brothers). As a founding member, Simons provided the technological backbone and keyboard arrangements that defined their breakthrough albums of the 1990s and 2000s. The Chemical Brothers achieved unprecedented commercial success with albums like "Exit Planet Dust" (1995), "Dig Your Own Hole" (1997), and "Block Rockin' Beats" (1997), which reached number 2 on the UK singles chart. Simons has remained committed to the project while also exploring production techniques and electronic music innovation throughout his career.
