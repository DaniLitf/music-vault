# Erik Melin

**Vocalist and lyricist. Swedish.**

Erik Melin is the vocalist and primary lyricist of Stockholm punk band Twin Pigs, formed in 2015. His lyrics draw on writers like John Darnielle of The Mountain Goats and Sean Bonnette of AJJ, and he has described his songwriting approach as taking a specific frustration and examining how an individual relates to it.
