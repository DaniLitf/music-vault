# Embee

**Producer, DJ. Born 1977-02-24.**

Magnus Bergkvist, known as Embee, is a Swedish hip-hop producer and DJ who co-founded Looptroop with Promoe in 1991, having met while attending school in Västerås, and has produced and written the group's music ever since through his studio The Wax Cabinet. He runs the record label David vs Goliath with his bandmates, and his 2005 solo album *Tellings from Solitaria* won a Swedish Grammy for best hip-hop/soul release.
