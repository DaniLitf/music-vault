# Elisa Fiorillo

**Vocalist. Born in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.**

Elisa Fiorillo is an American singer who performed with Prince's New Power Generation during the 2000s. She contributed vocals to several Prince albums and toured extensively with the band. She later formed the duo Dease & Reese Project with guitarist Tyler Reese.
