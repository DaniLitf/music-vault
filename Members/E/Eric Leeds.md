# Eric Leeds

**Saxophonist and flautist. Born 19 January 1952 in Milwaukee, Wisconsin.**

Eric Leeds is an American saxophone and flute player best known for his long collaboration with Prince. He joined Prince's circle in 1984 after his brother Alan Leeds, Prince's tour manager, introduced him. He became the featured horn player on Prince's records and tours from 1985 to 1989, playing on *Parade*, *Sign o' the Times*, and *Lovesexy*, and was the co-lead of the instrumental side project Madhouse. He has also released solo albums including *Times Squared* and *Things Left Unsaid*.
