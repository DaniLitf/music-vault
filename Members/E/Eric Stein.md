# Eric Stein

**Guitar, vocals. American. Former member of [[Artists/T/The Greenhornes]].**

Eric Stein played guitar and vocals in The Greenhornes from 2000 to 2002, joining after the departures of Brian Olive and Jared McKinney and appearing on *Dual Mono* (2002). He is also the guitarist and vocalist of The Griefs.
