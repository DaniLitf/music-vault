# Erlend Caspersen

**Bass (2017–present).**

Erlend Caspersen is a Norwegian bassist best known as the bassist for the experimental project [[Artists/I/Igorrr]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Igorrr]] – bass (2017–present)
- [[Decrepit Birth]] – former member (death metal)
- [[Blood Red Throne]] – former member (death metal)
- [[Spawn of Possession]] – former member (technical death metal)

## Biography

Caspersen joined Igorrr in 2017, bringing a background in technical death metal from his work with Decrepit Birth, Blood Red Throne, and Spawn of Possession. He has appeared on Igorrr's *Spirituality and Distortion* (2020) and subsequent releases.
