# Ed Kowalczyk

**Vocals, guitar. American. Born 1971-02-16.**

Ed Kowalczyk is an American singer, best known as the frontman of post-grunge band [[Artists/L/Live]]. He provided lyrics and lead vocals on "Indie Hair" on [[Artists/T/The Heads]]' *No Talking, Just Head* (1996).

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/L/Live]] – lead vocals, guitar, co-founder
- [[Artists/T/The Heads]] – guest vocals (1996)
