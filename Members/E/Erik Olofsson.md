# Erik Olofsson

**Guitar. Swedish.**

Erik Olofsson was a founding guitarist of [[Artists/C/Cult of Luna]], remaining a full-time member until stepping back after 2014's Beyond the Redshift festival; the band has described him as still "part of the Cult of Luna universe."
