# Eddie Vedder

**Vocalist and guitarist. Born 23 December 1964 in Evanston, Illinois, USA.**

Eddie Vedder is the lead vocalist and a founding member of Pearl Jam. He rose to fame with the band's debut album *Ten* (1991) and became one of the defining voices of the grunge era. He contributed vocals to Temple of the Dog (1991) and has also released several solo albums.
