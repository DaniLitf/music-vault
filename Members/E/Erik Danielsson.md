**Vocalist. Born 1976.**

Erik Danielsson is the founding vocalist and primary lyricist of Swedish black metal band Watain, formed in 1998. As the creative force behind the band's controversial theistic Satanist imagery and uncompromising chaotic live performances, Danielsson has established Watain as one of Scandinavia's most significant black metal acts, known for their raw production and provocative presentation.
