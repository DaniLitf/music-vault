# Eric Carr

**Drummer, vocalist. Born July 12, 1950.**

Eric Carr was an American musician, best known as the drummer for KISS from 1980 until his death in 1991. Known as "The Fox", he replaced original drummer Peter Criss and performed on eight studio albums.
