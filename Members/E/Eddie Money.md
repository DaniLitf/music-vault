# Eddie Money

**Singer, songwriter, multi-instrumentalist. Born March 21, 1949. Died September 13, 2019.**

Eddie Money was an American rock singer and songwriter whose string of hits in the late 1970s and 1980s included "Baby Hold On," "Two Tickets to Paradise," "Take Me Home Tonight" (featuring Ronnie Spector), and "Walk on Water." After a stint as a New York police trainee, he moved to Berkeley and was discovered by promoter Bill Graham, signing with Columbia Records. His self-titled debut album sold over two million copies, and his 1986 comeback *Can't Hold Back* earned a Grammy nomination for Best Male Rock Vocal Performance. He continued touring and recording into the 2010s, also appearing in television and film.
