# Earl Harvin

**Drummer.**

Earl Harvin is an American drummer based in Dallas, Texas, leader of the Earl Harvin Trio, who toured with [[Artists/A/Air]] during the *Talkie Walkie* and *Pocket Symphony* eras. He has also worked as a session drummer for artists including Seal and Sam Prekop.
