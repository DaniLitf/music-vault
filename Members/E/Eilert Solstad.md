# Eilert Solstad

**Bassist. Norwegian.**

Eilert Solstad replaced René Jansen as bassist of death metal band Cadaver shortly after the release of their debut album *Hallucinating Anxiety* (1990), remaining through *...In Pains* (1992) until the band's 1993 breakup. He rejoined for live shows during the band's 2020s era.
