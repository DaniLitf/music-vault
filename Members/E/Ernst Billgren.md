# Ernst Billgren

**Artist, musician. Born 1957.**

Ernst Billgren is a Swedish visual artist, born in Stockholm, best known for his paintings, drawings, and sculptures and as a key figure in the breakthrough of postmodernism in Sweden during the mid-1980s. He was elected to the Royal Academy of Fine Arts, Sweden in 1997, and lent his multidisciplinary talents to the multimedia/ambient music collective Lucky People Center.
