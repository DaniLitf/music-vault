# Eric Campuzano

**Bassist, guitarist, and songwriter. Born November 26, 1969.**

Eric Campuzano is an American musician best known as the bassist of The Prayer Chain and co-founder of The Lassie Foundation. After The Prayer Chain disbanded in 1995, he formed The Lassie Foundation with Wayne Everett, switching from bass to guitar. He also played bass on Starflyer 59's album *Americana* and co-founded the band Cush with Andy Prickett. Under the moniker Charity Empressa, he has released drone-based solo projects.
