**Drummer. American.**

Emery Ceo is an American drummer who has been a core member of the Cleveland metal scene since the 1980s. He is the drummer for Kriadiaz, has played in the progressive metal band Rotterdam and its later incarnations Torment and Ritual, and was also a member of Integrity, Arena, Dark Arena, and the Blindside Blues Band. His long career spans multiple genres from sludge to thrash to progressive metal.
