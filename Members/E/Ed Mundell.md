# Ed Mundell

**American guitarist.**

Edward Mundell is a self-taught American guitarist, from New Jersey, who was Monster Magnet's lead guitarist from 1992 to 2010, appearing on eight albums including the gold-certified *Powertrip* (1998). While in Monster Magnet he founded the side project the Atomic Bitchwax, and after leaving in 2010 formed the Ultra Electric Mega Galactic; he remains active as a studio musician.
