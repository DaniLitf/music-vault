**Drummer. Born 1965.**

Eric Kretz is the longtime drummer of Stone Temple Pilots, joining the band at its formation in 1989 and remaining through all of its iterations, breakups, and reunions. He is a foundational element of STP's heavy, groove-oriented sound and appears on all of the band's studio albums from the 1992 debut through the 2020 reunion record.
