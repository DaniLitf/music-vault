**Guitarist. Born May 14, 1964.**

Eric Peterson is the sole surviving original member of Testament, having co-founded the band (then called Legacy) in 1983. As the group's lead rhythm guitarist and primary songwriter, Peterson has shaped Testament's thrash metal sound across all albums since 1987's The New Order, making him an essential figure in the San Francisco Bay Area metal scene.
