# Eddie Van Halen

**Eddie Van Halen was a Dutch-American guitarist and co-founder of Van Halen, widely regarded as one of the greatest rock guitarists of all time, whose two-handed tapping technique revolutionized guitar playing when Van Halen's debut album appeared in 1978.**

He died of throat cancer on October 6, 2020.
