# Evelina Hägglund

**Keyboards. Swedish.**

Evelina Hägglund is a Swedish musician who has been a member of [[Artists/W/Wintergatan]] since its formation, specializing in keyboard instruments, synthesizers, and glockenspiel/typewriter percussion.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/W/Wintergatan]] – keyboards, synthesizers, glockenspiel (2012–present, founding member)
