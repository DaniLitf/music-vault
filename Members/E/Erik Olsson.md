# Erik Olsson

**Drums. Active 1990s.**

Erik Olsson is a Swedish drummer, formerly of [[Artists/T/Teddybears]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/Teddybears]]

## Biography
Erik Olsson was the drummer for Teddybears during their early hardcore punk period in the 1990s. He joined the band after the name change from Skull to Teddybears STHLM and played on their early releases. He left the band before their shift towards electronica and pop in the late 1990s.
