# Fast Eddie Clarke

**English guitarist. Born 5 October 1950, died 10 January 2018.**

Edward Clarke, known as Fast Eddie Clarke, was an English guitarist best known as the lead guitarist of Motörhead (1976–1982). He was the last surviving member of Motörhead's classic lineup. He died of pneumonia in 2018.
