# Eddie Breckenridge

**Bass. Born 1979-11-15.**

Eddie Breckenridge is an American bassist and founding member of post-hardcore band [[Artists/T/Thrice|Thrice]], which he joined as a teenager alongside childhood friends Dustin Kensrue and Teppei Teranishi. In mid-2014 he served as touring bassist for [[Artists/A/Angels & Airwaves|Angels & Airwaves]] during the gap between Matt Wachter's departure and Matt Rubano's arrival, while remaining Thrice's full-time bassist throughout.
