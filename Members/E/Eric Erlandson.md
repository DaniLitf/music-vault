**Guitarist and songwriter. Born 9 January 1963.**

Eric Erlandson is an American guitarist and songwriter best known as the co-founder and lead guitarist of Hole, the influential rock band fronted by Courtney Love. Erlandson's guitar work was instrumental in defining Hole's powerful, distortion-heavy sound during their most creatively fertile period in the 1990s. Beyond Hole, he has pursued various musical projects and has authored works on music and culture.

## Biography

Erlandson co-founded Hole in 1989 with Courtney Love, establishing himself as the primary guitarist and significant songwriting contributor to the band. His distortion-heavy, dynamic guitar work was crucial to Hole's sound on landmark albums including "Live Through This" (1994) and "Celebrity Skin" (1998). During the 1990s, Hole became one of the most influential rock bands of the decade, and Erlandson's compositions and arrangements were essential to their commercial and critical success. After the band's initial dissolution in 2002, he pursued various projects, including collaborations with Thurston Moore of Sonic Youth and experimental work with Vincent Gallo. Erlandson reunited with Hole in 2012, resuming his role as lead guitarist and continuing to contribute to the band's output. Beyond music, he is also a published author, having written about the music industry and contemporary culture.
