# Eddie Meduza

**Singer, songwriter, multi-instrumentalist. Swedish. Born June 17, 1948. Died January 17, 2002.**

Errol Leonard Norstedt, known by his stage name Eddie Meduza, was a Swedish musician and entertainer who released over 20 studio albums and numerous self-released cassettes between 1973 and 2001. Working mainly in the rockabilly genre with punk rock influences, he played most instruments on his recordings himself. Despite harsh critical reception and being banned from major newspapers for obscene material, he amassed a devoted cult following in Sweden. He also recorded under the alias E. Hitler for more sexually explicit material. He died of a heart attack in 2002 resulting from chronic alcohol abuse.
