**Vocalist and songwriter. Born 26 February 1966.**

Ebbot Lundberg is a Swedish vocalist and musician best known as the lead singer and co-founder of The Soundtrack of Our Lives, one of Sweden's most influential rock acts. He began his music career in the post-punk era with Union Carbide Productions in the 1980s before co-founding The Soundtrack of Our Lives in 1995. Throughout his career, he has collaborated with artists including Jane Birkin, Nina Persson, and The Cardigans, establishing himself as a significant figure in Swedish rock and alternative music.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Soundtrack of Our Lives]] – lead singer, co-founder (1995–2012)
- [[Artists/U/Union Carbide Productions]] – vocalist, founding member (1986–1993)

## Biography

Lundberg emerged during the post-punk revival of the 1980s with Union Carbide Productions, developing a distinctive aesthetic blending industrial and punk influences. As founder and primary vocalist of The Soundtrack of Our Lives, he led the band through a prolific period marked by conceptual albums and elaborate live performances. After the band's dissolution in 2012, he has pursued solo work and continued collaborative projects. He is also known for his work as a record producer and session musician, contributing to various Scandinavian artists' recordings.
