**Singer and co-lyricist. Born 15 October 1984.**

Elize Ryd is a Swedish soprano and vocalist best known as one of three vocalists and co-founder of Amaranthe, a symphonic metal band that emerged in the late 2000s. With her distinctive operatic soprano voice, Ryd has become known for her performances in the symphonic metal genre and her collaborative work with diverse metal and rock acts. She is also recognized for co-writing lyrics on numerous Amaranthe songs and for her contributions to various musical collaborations.

## Biography

Elize Ryd co-founded Amaranthe in 2009, which features a unique lineup with three vocalists performing different styles within a symphonic metal framework. Her powerful soprano vocals have become a signature element of Amaranthe's sound on albums including "Amaranthe" (2011), "The Nexus" (2014), "Maximized" (2020), and subsequent releases. Beyond Amaranthe, she has pursued guest vocal work with other metal acts, contributing to Kamelot's "Silverthorn" album and participating in the Raskasta Joulua metal Christmas project. Her versatility as a vocalist spans symphonic metal, operatic performance, and collaborative arrangements with artists from various subgenres within the metal and rock communities.
