# Eric Martin (Technotronic)

**Male vocalist. Belgian.**

Eric Martin is a Belgian vocalist known as the male voice on Technotronic's hits, including parts of "Pump Up the Jam." He should not be confused with the American rock vocalist of the same name from Mr. Big.
