# En Esch

**En Esch (born Sven Doering) is a German vocalist, guitarist, and drummer, best known as a founding member of [[Artists/K/KMFDM]] and [[Artists/S/Slick Idiot]], and a core member of [[Artists/P/Pigface]].**

He has been part of Pigface since the early 1990s, contributing vocals, guitar, and drums across multiple albums and tours.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/P/Pigface]] – vocals, guitar, drums (1990–present)
- [[Artists/K/KMFDM]] – co-founder, drums, vocals (1984–1999)
- [[Artists/S/Slick Idiot]] – co-founder
