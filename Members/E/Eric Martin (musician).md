**Vocalist and rapper. Welsh.**

Eric Martin, known professionally as MC Eric, is a Welsh-born singer and rapper who contributed vocals to [[Artists/T/Technotronic|Technotronic]]'s debut album, notably on the single "This Beat Is Technotronic". He was one of the actual vocalists on the debut album while fashion model Felly served as the public face.

Before joining Technotronic, he was a rapper active in the Belgian music scene and was brought in by producer Jo Bogaert to add hip-hop vocal elements to the project's fusion sound.
