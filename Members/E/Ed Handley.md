**Keyboardist and electronic musician.**

Ed Handley is an English keyboardist and synthesizer player best known as one half of Plaid, an influential electronic music duo established in 1991. He is also a founding member of The Black Dog, a pioneering group in the IDM (Intelligent Dance Music) scene. Handley's work is characterized by detailed sound design, complex rhythmic patterns, and innovative use of synthesis, making him a significant figure in the electronic music landscape.

## Biography

Handley's career in electronic music began in the early 1990s as part of the black dog collective before establishing Plaid with Andy Turner. Plaid's distinctive sound draws from techno, post-rock, and ambient influences, with Handley handling most keyboard work and synthesis. The duo has released numerous critically acclaimed albums and continues to perform and record. Beyond Plaid, Handley has worked on various electronic projects and pseudonyms, contributing to the broader IDM movement's development and evolution.
