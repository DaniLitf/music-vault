# Elizabeth Fraser

**Elizabeth Fraser is a Scottish vocalist and co-founder of Cocteau Twins, whose unique voice — using invented phonemes and syllables to create emotional impact beyond verbal meaning — became one of the most distinctive sounds in alternative music.**

She also sang on Massive Attack's "Teardrop" (1998), one of the most celebrated guest collaborations in electronic music history.
