# Emmi Silvennoinen

**Keyboardist. Finnish.**

Emmi Silvennoinen is a Finnish keyboardist who was a member of Ensiferum from 2007 to 2016, performing on *From Afar*, *Unsung Heroes*, and *One Man Army*.
