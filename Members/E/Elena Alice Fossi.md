# Elena Alice Fossi

**Italian musician and producer. Born 1975.**

Elena Alice Fossi is an Italian musician, producer, and the creative force behind the electro-industrial project Kirlian Camera. She took over the project's leadership and has been its primary member since the 2000s, working with a dark electronic and synthpop style.
