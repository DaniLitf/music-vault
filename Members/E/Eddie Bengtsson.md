# Eddie Bengtsson

**Musician, songwriter, and producer.**

Eddie Bengtsson is a Swedish synthpop pioneer, sometimes called "the Swedish Vince Clarke," who co-founded the influential electropop act Page in 1980 with Marina Schiptjenko, credited as the first band to bring synthpop to Sweden. In 1988 he wrote songs for Finn Albertsson's birthday party with vocalist Alexander Hofman, which grew into the band S.P.O.C.K.
