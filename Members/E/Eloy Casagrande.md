**Drummer and percussionist. Born 29 January 1991.**

Eloy Casagrande is a Brazilian drummer best known for his work as the current drummer of Slipknot, one of the most successful heavy metal bands of the 21st century. Previously, he was the longstanding drummer for Sepultura, the pioneering Brazilian thrash metal band, for over a decade. Casagrande's powerful and precise drumming style has made him a significant figure in contemporary heavy metal music.

## Biography

Casagrande began his professional drumming career in Brazilian metal bands before joining Sepultura in 2011, replacing departing drummer Igor Cavalera. During his thirteen-year tenure with Sepultura, Casagrande became integral to the band's sound, appearing on multiple studio albums and touring extensively worldwide. His technical proficiency and powerful style helped Sepultura maintain their presence as leaders in the global metal scene. In 2024, Casagrande joined Slipknot as the replacement for longtime drummer Jay Weinberg. He has also been involved in various side projects and collaborations within the Brazilian metal community, demonstrating his versatility across multiple metal subgenres including thrash metal, death metal, and Christian metal.
