# Emil Johnson

**Drums.**

Emil Johnson was a 15-year-old drummer briefly hired by [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] for the first half of 1982, appearing on the title track of the *TV Party* EP. He quit mid-tour that year after a dispute with the band's roadie.
