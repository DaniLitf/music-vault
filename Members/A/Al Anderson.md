# Al Anderson

**Al Anderson is an American guitarist who was lead guitarist for Bob Marley and the Wailers from 1974 to 1979, contributing a rock-influenced lead guitar style to the band's classic Island Records period.**

He appeared on albums including *Natty Dread*, *Rastaman Vibration*, and *Exodus* before being replaced by Junior Marvin.
