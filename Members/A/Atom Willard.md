# Atom Willard

**Drums. Born 1973-08-15.**

Atom Willard is an American drummer who joined San Diego punk band [[Artists/R/Rocket From The Crypt|Rocket From The Crypt]] as a teenager in 1990 and stayed a decade, later touring with [[Artists/T/The Offspring|The Offspring]] and co-founding [[Artists/A/Angels & Airwaves|Angels & Airwaves]] in 2005. After leaving Angels & Airwaves in 2011 he briefly joined [[Artists/S/Social Distortion|Social Distortion]] and later spent 2013–2020 as the drummer of [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]], also joining bandmate Laura Jane Grace's side project [[Artists/D/Devouring Mothers|Devouring Mothers]] in 2016. He drummed for [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio|Alkaline Trio]] from 2023 until departing in February 2026, when he was replaced by [[Members/T/Tosh Peterson|Tosh Peterson]].
