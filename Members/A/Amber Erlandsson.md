# Amber "Morrigan Hel" Erlandsson

**Vocalist. British.**

Amber Erlandsson, known as Morrigan Hel, is a model and vocalist who co-founded the gothic/industrial band Nemhain in 2006 with her husband, drummer Adrian Erlandsson. The band's music explores the darker side of sex, drugs, addiction, passion, and revenge.
