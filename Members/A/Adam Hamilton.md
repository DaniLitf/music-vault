# Adam Hamilton

**Multi-instrumentalist and producer. Born November 11, 1969.**

Adam Hamilton is an American multi-instrumentalist, record producer, and songwriter based in Los Angeles. He was the bassist for L.A. Guns from 2001 to 2007 and played keyboards in Brides of Destruction. He has also produced albums for William Shatner and David Hasselhoff.
