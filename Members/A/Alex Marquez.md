# Alex Marquez

**Drummer. American.**

Alex Marquez is an American drummer who performed with Righteous Pigs during their reunion show at Maryland Deathfest in 2013. He has been a member of numerous death metal bands including Malevolent Creation, Demolition Hammer, and Solstice.
