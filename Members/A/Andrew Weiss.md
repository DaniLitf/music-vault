# Andrew Weiss

**Andrew Weiss is an American bassist and producer known for his work with the Rollins Band and as a producer for Ween.**

Weiss was the bassist for the Rollins Band from their early days and also contributed as a guest bassist to Pigface. He later became a prominent record producer.
