# Atticus Ross

**Keyboardist, programmer, producer, composer. Born 16 January 1968 in London, England.**

Atticus Ross is a British musician, record producer, and composer best known as an official member of Nine Inch Nails (since 2016) and for his longstanding collaboration with Trent Reznor. Together they have won an Academy Award, a Golden Globe, and multiple Grammys for film scores including *The Social Network*, *The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo*, and *Soul*. Ross founded the band 12 Rounds in 1995 before joining Nine Inch Nails as a touring musician in 2005.

## Biography

Ross began his music career with 12 Rounds, the band he formed with Claudia Sarne. After two albums, he shifted focus to production and composition. His partnership with Trent Reznor began in 2005, leading to his full membership in Nine Inch Nails and a prolific film-scoring career. He has also produced albums for artists including Grace Jones and releases on his own label.
