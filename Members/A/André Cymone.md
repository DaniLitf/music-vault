# André Cymone

**Bassist and vocalist. Born 27 June 1958 in Minneapolis, Minnesota.**

André Cymone is an American bassist and childhood friend of Prince, performing as the original bassist in Prince's live band from 1979 to 1982. He played on *Dirty Mind*, *Controversy*, and *1999* before leaving for a solo career, later releasing albums on Columbia Records.
