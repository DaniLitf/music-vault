# Adam Gontier

**Vocals, guitar. Canadian. Founding member of [[Artists/T/Three Days Grace]] and co-founder of [[Artists/S/Saint Asonia]].**

Adam Gontier co-founded Three Days Grace (as Groundswell) in Norwood, Ontario in 1992 and fronted the band until his 2013 departure, after which he formed Saint Asonia with Staind guitarist Mike Mushok in 2014. He rejoined Three Days Grace as co-lead vocalist alongside Matt Walst in October 2024, prompting Saint Asonia's hiatus.
