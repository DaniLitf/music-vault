# Alex Van Halen

**Drummer. American.**

Alex Van Halen is an American drummer and co-founder of the legendary rock band [[Artists/V/Van Halen]], formed with his brother Eddie. Born in 1955, he was the band's drummer throughout their entire career until his death in 2022, and is widely recognized as one of the greatest rock drummers of all time.
