# Attila Csihar

**Hungarian vocalist. Born 29 March 1971.**

Attila Csihar is a Hungarian vocalist best known as the lead vocalist of Mayhem (1992–1993, 2004–present). He is known for his versatile vocal style ranging from deep growls to operatic singing, and has worked with numerous black metal and doom metal bands.
