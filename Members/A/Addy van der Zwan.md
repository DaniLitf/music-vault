# Addy van der Zwan

**Electronic music producer and DJ. Dutch.**

Addy van der Zwan is a pioneering Dutch electronic music producer who co-founded Klubbheads with Koen Groeneveld in the mid-1990s, establishing himself as a key figure in the happy hardcore and Eurodance scenes. The duo became known for their prolific output, recording under over 40 aliases including Hi_Tack, DJ Disco, and Drunkenmunky. Van der Zwan launched his independent label Nasty Tunes in 2009 to release house-oriented material under his own name.
