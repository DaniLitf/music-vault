# Alan Dubin

**Vocalist. Active 1986–present.**

Alan Dubin is an American vocalist best known as the frontman of the New York avant-garde industrial metal band OLD, which he co-founded with James Plotkin in 1986. After OLD disbanded in 1994, Dubin and Plotkin later reunited in Khanate, a New York drone/doom metal act active in the early 2000s. His harsh, tortured vocal style has been influential in experimental heavy metal circles.
