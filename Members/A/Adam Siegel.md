# Adam Siegel

**Adam Siegel is an American guitarist best known as a co-founder of [[Artists/I/Infectious Grooves]] and co-founder of the crossover thrash band [[Artists/E/Excel]].**

Siegel co-founded Excel (originally Chaotic Noise) with vocalist Dan Clements in 1983, playing guitar until the early 1990s. He separately co-founded Infectious Grooves with Mike Muir and Robert Trujillo, playing on all four studio albums, and left that band in 2024 after 35 years. After leaving Excel he co-founded the side project My Head with fellow Excel alum Greg Saenz.
