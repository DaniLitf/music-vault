# Andrew Sega

**American electronic musician. Born 1975.**

Andrew Sega (also known as Necros) is an American electronic musician best known as the vocalist, guitarist, and producer of the synthpop/EBM band Iris. He was previously known for his work in the demoscene and as a chiptune artist before transitioning to synthpop.
