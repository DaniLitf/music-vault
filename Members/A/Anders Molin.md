# Anders Molin

**Accordion, music box. Swedish.**

Anders "Flanders" Molin is a Swedish musician who co-founded [[Artists/D/Detektivbyrån]] with his brother Martin Molin and Jon Nils Emanuel Ekström in Gothenburg in 2005, playing accordion and music box for the duration of the band's run.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/D/Detektivbyrån]] – accordion, music box (2005–2010, founding member)
