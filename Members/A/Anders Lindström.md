# Anders Lindström

**Keyboards, percussion. Swedish.**

Anders Lindström, nicknamed "Boba Fett," is a Swedish keyboardist and percussionist, also a guitarist for Swedish rock band The Diamond Dogs. He joined [[Artists/T/The Hellacopters]] as a part-time keyboardist in 1997 before Kiss support dates, becoming a full-time member in 1999.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Hellacopters]] – keyboards, percussion (1997–present)
- [[Artists/T/The Diamond Dogs]] – guitar
