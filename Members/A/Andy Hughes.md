# Andy Hughes

**Producer, engineer. Born 1965-11-11; died 2009-06-12.**

Andy Hughes was a British producer and engineer who worked extensively with [[Artists/T/The Orb]] during the 1990s and early 2000s. He contributed to the group's live and studio work during the Island Records era.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Orb]] – engineering, production (1993–2001)
