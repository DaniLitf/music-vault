**Guitarist, vocalist. Born March 22, 1979.**

Aaron North is an American musician known for his unconventional chaotic guitar approach and dynamic stage presence. He co-founded the punk band The Icarus Line and toured as lead guitarist for Nine Inch Nails during their 2005–2007 era. North also founded the influential music website and record label Buddyhead Records.
