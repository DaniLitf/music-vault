**Drummer. Original member of Wasted Youth.**

Allen Stiritz is an American drummer best known as a founding member of LA's Wasted Youth, the early 1980s hardcore punk band from Los Angeles. Stiritz started drumming around age 8 and was influenced by drummers like John Bonham, Stewart Copeland, and Peter Criss. He performed with Wasted Youth during their formative years in the LA punk underground before leaving in 1986 to pursue modeling in Europe and the United States. He subsequently enlisted in the US Army and served in Germany from 1986 to 1993, later working for UPS Europe. Stiritz has occasionally reunited with Wasted Youth for performances.
