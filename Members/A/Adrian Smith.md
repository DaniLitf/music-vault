# Adrian Smith

**Adrian Smith is an English guitarist and songwriter born February 27, 1957, best known as a member of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].**

Smith joined Iron Maiden in 1980, replacing Dennis Stratton, and co-wrote classics including "Run to the Hills", "Wasted Years", and "The Evil That Men Do". He left briefly in 1990 to pursue his own project ASAP, returned in 1999, and has been a key songwriter in the band's second golden era.
