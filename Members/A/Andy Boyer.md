# Andy Boyer

**Vocalist. American.**

Andy Boyer is an American vocalist who sang for the Cleveland thrash/power metal band Purgatory from 1989 to 1990 and during the 1991 reunion, replacing founding vocalist Jeff Hatrix. He performed on the band's final pre-split EP. Outside of Purgatory, he has played in Battery, Violet Wind, and Hollow.
