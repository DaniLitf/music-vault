# Andrew Wood

**Vocalist. 1966–1990.**

Andrew Wood was an American vocalist who, at age 14, formed Malfunkshun in 1980 with his older brother Kevin Wood and drummer Regan Hagar, drawing on 1970s glam influences like KISS, Elton John, and Bowie. After Malfunkshun disbanded in 1988, he began jamming with Green River's Stone Gossard and Jeff Ament, forming Mother Love Bone when Green River split later that year. Days before the release of Mother Love Bone's debut album *Apple*, Wood died in March 1990 after a heroin overdose; his death was a catalyst for the formation of Temple of the Dog and, subsequently, Pearl Jam.
