**Drummer for Goon.**

Andy Polito is an American drummer currently performing with the Los Angeles-based indie rock band Goon. He has been a key part of the band's evolution from Kenny Becker's solo project into a full ensemble, contributing to their acclaimed album Dream 3 (2025). Polito's drumming provides the rhythmic foundation for Goon's introspective alternative rock sound.
