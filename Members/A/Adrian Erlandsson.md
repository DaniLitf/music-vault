# Adrian "Swede" Erlandsson

**Adrian Erlandsson is a Swedish drummer best known for his tenures with At the Gates and Paradise Lost, and who also played with Dissection in the early 1990s.**

He appeared on At the Gates' landmark *Slaughter of the Soul* (1995) and served as Paradise Lost's drummer from 2008 to 2015. He also co-founded The Haunted with Anders and Jonas Björler, played drums for Cradle of Filth from 1999 to around 2015, and co-founded the gothic/industrial band Nemhain with his wife Amber in 2006.
