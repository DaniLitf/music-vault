# Adrian Erlandsson

**Adrian Erlandsson is a Swedish drummer best known for his tenures with At the Gates and Paradise Lost, and who also played with Dissection in the early 1990s.**

He appeared on At the Gates' landmark *Slaughter of the Soul* (1995) and served as Paradise Lost's drummer from 2008 to 2015.
