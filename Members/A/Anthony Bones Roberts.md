# Anthony "Bones" Roberts

**Guitarist. British.**

Anthony "Bones" Roberts is a British punk guitarist best known as a founding member and lead guitarist of Discharge. He played on the band's classic early EPs and debut album, defining the D-beat guitar sound. He left in 1982 due to creative differences and formed Broken Bones with his brother Tezz, but rejoined Discharge in 2001 and continues to perform with them.
