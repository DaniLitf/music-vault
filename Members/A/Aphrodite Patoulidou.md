# Aphrodite Patoulidou

**Clean vocals (2021–2022).**

Aphrodite Patoulidou is a Greek soprano vocalist briefly known as the clean vocalist for the experimental project [[Artists/I/Igorrr]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Igorrr]] – clean vocals (2021–2022)

## Biography

Patoulidou joined Igorrr in 2021 alongside JB Le Bail and Martyn Clément, replacing vocalists Laurent Lunoir and Laure Le Prunenec. She performed with the band for approximately one year before being replaced by Marthe Alexandre in 2022.
