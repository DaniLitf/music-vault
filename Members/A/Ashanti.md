# Ashanti

**Singer, songwriter, rapper. American. Born 1980.**

Ashanti Douglas is a singer and songwriter who became the second major star of Murder Inc. Records after being signed in 2001. Her self-titled debut album *Ashanti* (2002) was the fastest-selling female R&B debut at the time, featuring collaborations with Ja Rule on "Always on Time" and spawning multiple chart hits. She released four studio albums under Murder Inc./The Inc. Records before departing in 2009, establishing herself as a solo artist with her own brand.
