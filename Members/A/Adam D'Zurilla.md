# Adam D'Zurilla

**Vocalist.**

Adam D'Zurilla is an American vocalist who fronted [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] from late March 2002, after Damien Moyal quit the band at the height of its popularity. He toured the United States, Canada, Europe and the United Kingdom with the band but appears on no studio releases, and stayed on as it became Salem in September 2002.
