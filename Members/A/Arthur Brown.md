# Arthur Brown

**Arthur Wilton Brown, known as Arthur Brown, is an English singer-songwriter born on June 24, 1942 in Whitby, Yorkshire.**

Brown rose to fame in 1968 with the wildly theatrical single "Fire!" performed under the name The Crazy World of Arthur Brown. His flamboyant stage persona — including a flaming helmet — made him an early progenitor of shock rock and glam rock, influencing artists like Alice Cooper and KISS. He has continued recording and performing into his eighties.
