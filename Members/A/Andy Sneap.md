# Andy Sneap

**Guitarist and record producer. Born July 18, 1969.**

Andy Sneap is a British guitarist and renowned metal producer who has been filling in for Glenn Tipton in Judas Priest's touring lineup since 2018. He first rose to prominence as the guitarist for thrash metal band Sabbat and has since established himself as one of the most sought-after producers in metal, working with acts like Machine Head, Megadeth, and Testament.
