# Adam Andersson

**Guitarist. Swedish.**

Adam Andersson is a Swedish guitarist who played for Driller Killer from 1999 until the band's breakup in 2009. He performed on the albums *And the Winner Is…* (2000), *Cold, Cheap & Disconnected* (2002), and *The 4Q Mangrenade* (2005), and also contributed backing vocals on recordings.
