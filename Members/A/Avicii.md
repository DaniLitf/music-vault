# Avicii

**Tim Bergling, known as Avicii, was a Swedish DJ, remixer, and record producer born on September 8, 1989 in Stockholm; he died on April 20, 2018 in Muscat, Oman.**

Bergling began producing music in his teens and rose to global fame with the progressive house sound, reaching the top of charts worldwide with tracks like "Levels" (2011) and "Wake Me Up" (2013). His debut album *True* blended electronic music with folk and country influences to massive commercial success. He retired from touring in 2016 due to health issues related to acute pancreatitis, and died by suicide in 2018 at age 28. His posthumous album *TIM* was released in 2019.
