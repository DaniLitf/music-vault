# Alexander Krull

**German musician and producer. Born 26 September 1972.**

Alexander Krull is a German musician and producer, best known as the vocalist and founder of the death metal/industrial metal band Atrocity. He is also the founder of the gothic metal band Leaves' Eyes and runs the Mastersound Entertainment studio.
