# Anders Sjöholm

**Keyboards, vocals, guitar. Swedish. Member of [[Artists/E/Ebba Grön]] and [[Artists/I/Imperiet]].**

Anders Sjöholm (nicknamed Stry) joined Ebba Grön on keyboards in 1981, bringing new melodic influences that shaped the band's final album. He continued into Rymdimperiet/Imperiet as a vocalist and guitarist before departing in 1984.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/E/Ebba Grön]] – keyboards (1981–1983)
- [[Artists/I/Imperiet]] – vocals, guitar (1983–1984, founder)
