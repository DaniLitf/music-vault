# Aaron Aedy

**Guitarist. Born 1969-09-29 in Bridlington, England.**

Aaron Aedy is a founding rhythm guitarist of Paradise Lost, present on every album since their debut *Lost Paradise* (1990). He contributes rhythm guitar and backing parts that support Gregor Mackintosh's lead work. Aedy is one of the band's most consistent members and has remained with them through all their stylistic phases.
