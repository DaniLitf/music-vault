**Guitarist and singer-songwriter.**

Aaron Lee Tasjan is an American guitarist and singer-songwriter best known as the original guitarist of [[Artists/S/Semi Precious Weapons]], co-founding the band in 2004 and departing in November 2008. After leaving the band, Tasjan pursued a solo career as a glam-rock folk singer, earning a Grammy nomination in 2021 for songwriting on Yola's *Stand for Myself*.
