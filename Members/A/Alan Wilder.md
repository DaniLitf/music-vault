# Alan Wilder

**Multi-instrumentalist, producer. Born 1959-06-01 in London, England.**

Alan Wilder was the fourth member of Depeche Mode, joining in 1982 as a touring keyboardist and quickly becoming an integral part of the band's production and arranging, widely credited with shaping their mature sound on albums like *Violator* (1990) and *Songs of Faith and Devotion* (1993). He departed in 1995 citing personal and professional tensions. Recoil, his solo project, predated his departure and has continued since as his primary outlet for cinematic, atmospheric electronic music.
