# Andy Rydell

**Guitar. Swedish (died 2017).**

Andy Rydell was a Swedish guitarist and a core member of the Malmö hardcore punk band Black Uniforms from 1981 until the band's dissolution in 1990. He played on all of the band's recordings. He also played in Virgin Sin and briefly in Driller Killer. He died in 2017.
