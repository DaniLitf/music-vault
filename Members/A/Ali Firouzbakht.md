# Ali Firouzbakht

**Guitarist. British.**

Ali Firouzbakht was the lead guitarist for Extreme Noise Terror from 1995 to 2005, appearing on the albums *Retro-bution*, *Damage 381*, and *Being and Nothing*. He also guested on Raw Noise's "Sound of Destruction" single alongside Dean Jones.
