# Andrew Naifman

**Guitarist. Ukrainian.**

Andrew Naifman (also known as Andriy Rieznikov) is a Ukrainian guitarist best known as a former member of [[Artists/0-9/1914]] (2014–2015), contributing to their debut album *Eschatology of War*.
