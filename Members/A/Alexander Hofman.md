# Alexander Hofman

**Vocalist.**

Alexander Hofman, known by the persona "Android," is the lead vocalist and sole constant member of the Swedish synthpop band S.P.O.C.K since its formation in 1988, when he sang songs written by Eddie Bengtsson for Finn Albertsson's birthday party.
