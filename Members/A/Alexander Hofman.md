# Alexander Hofman

**Keyboardist. Swedish.**

Alexander Hofman is a Swedish keyboardist who was a member of [[Artists/S/S.P.O.C.K]], the Swedish space rock/science fiction themed band. He contributed keyboards to the band's distinctive sound.
