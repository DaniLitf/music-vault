# Aryanne Maudit

**Guitarist. British.**

Aryanne Maudit is a guitarist who joined [[Artists/T/The Featherz]] around 2017. Her known vault-relevant role is as part of the band's later touring/recording lineup after Alice Atkinson and Chris Shepherd left.
