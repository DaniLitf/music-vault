# Aaron Melnick

**Guitar, vocals. American.**

Aaron Melnick is an American musician who played guitar and provided vocals for the metalcore band Integrity during its formative years from 1988 to 1998 and returned for a brief period in 2013–2014. He was a key contributor to the band's early sound and releases.
