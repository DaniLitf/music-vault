# Anton Hedberg

**Guitar. Swedish.**

Anton Hedberg joined [[Artists/C/Cemetary]] on guitar in 1992, shortly before the release of the band's debut album *An Evil Shade of Grey* (1992), and remained through the follow-up *Godless Beauty* (1993).
