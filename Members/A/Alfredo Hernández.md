# Alfredo Hernández

**Drummer. Born c. 1966 in California, USA.**

Alfredo Hernández was the drummer of Queens of the Stone Age during their early period (1998–2000), appearing on the self-titled debut. He was previously the drummer for Kyuss during their later years.
