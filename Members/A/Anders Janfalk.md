# Anders Janfalk

**Bassist. Swedish.**

Anders Janfalk is a Swedish bassist who became the permanent bass player of [[Artists/A/All Ends]] in 2008, after a series of touring bassists including Michael Håkansson, and remained with the band on its second album *A Road to Depression* and through its hiatus.

## Associated Artists
