# Andy Farrier

**Guitarist. British.**

Andy Farrier is a British punk guitarist who was the original guitarist of Chaos UK. He played on the band's first three releases — the *Burning Britain* EP, *Loud Political & Uncompromising* EP, and the self-titled debut LP — before leaving the band in 1983 due to frustration with their chaotic live performances.
