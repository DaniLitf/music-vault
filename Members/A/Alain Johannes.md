# Alain Johannes

**Alain Johannes is a Chilean-American guitarist, vocalist, and multi-instrumentalist, co-founder of Eleven alongside Natasha Shneider, and a prolific collaborator and producer who has worked with Chris Cornell, Queens of the Stone Age, and Them Crooked Vultures.**
