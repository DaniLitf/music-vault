# Andy Allo

**Vocalist, guitarist. Born 13 January 1989 in Bamenda, Cameroon.**

Andy Allo is a Cameroonian-American singer, guitarist, and songwriter who joined Prince's New Power Generation in 2011. She performed on *20Ten* and toured with Prince as a guitarist and backing vocalist. She also co-wrote songs with Prince and released her own solo albums.
