# Andy LaPlegua

**Norwegian electronic musician, founder of Combichrist and Icon of Coil. Born 24 October 1977.**

Andy LaPlegua began his musical career in the Fredrikstad EBM/industrial scene, initially playing keyboards for local projects Sector9 and Melt. In 1997 he founded Icon of Coil, which became a defining act in the futurepop and aggrotech genres. He later founded Combichrist in 2003, shifting toward a heavier industrial metal sound. LaPlegua is the sole consistent member of both projects.
