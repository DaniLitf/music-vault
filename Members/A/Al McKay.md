# Al McKay

**Guitarist. American.**

Alvin "Al" McKay is an American guitarist and a founding member of the funk and R&B group [[Artists/E/Earth, Wind & Fire]]. Born in 1948, McKay has been a core part of the band's sound since its inception, contributing guitar work to their numerous hit albums and remaining with the group throughout their career. He is recognized as one of the key guitarists in funk and R&B music history.
