# Anthony Gonzalez

**Vocalist, multi-instrumentalist, songwriter, producer. Born 13 March 1980.**

Anthony Gonzalez is a French musician from Antibes, founder and sole constant member of [[Artists/M/M83]]. He grew up alongside his brother, film director Yann Gonzalez, and turned to music at 14 after an injury ended his football ambitions; with Nicolas Fromageau he first played in the post-rock group My Violent Wish before forming M83 in 1999. Based in Los Angeles since 2010, he has also scored films (*Oblivion*, *You and the Night*, *Knife+Heart*, *Resurrection*), composed for Cirque du Soleil's *Volta*, and guested on [[Artists/J/Jean-Michel Jarre]]'s *Electronica 1: The Time Machine*.
