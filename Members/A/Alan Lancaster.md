# Alan Lancaster

**Bassist. British.**

Alan Lancaster is a British bassist and founding member of the rock band [[Artists/S/Status Quo]], formed in 1962. He has been with the band for decades, contributing to their signature boogie rock sound and remaining with them through numerous line-up changes, recording and touring extensively throughout his career.
