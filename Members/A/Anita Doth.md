**Lead vocalist. Born 28 December 1971.**

Anita Doth is a Dutch singer best known as the female lead vocalist of the Eurodance project [[Artists/0-9/2 Unlimited]], alongside rapper [[Members/R/Ray Slijngaard]]. Her powerful, melodic vocals defined the group's sound on international hits such as "No Limit", "Twilight Zone", and "Tribal Dance" from 1991 to 1996. After leaving 2 Unlimited, she briefly continued with Slijngaard as the duo Ray & Anita before embarking on a solo career and later participating in reunion performances.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/0-9/2 Unlimited]] – lead vocals (1991–1996, 2001–present)
- [[Artists/R/Ray & Anita]] – member (1996–1999)
