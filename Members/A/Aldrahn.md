# Aldrahn

**Vocalist and guitarist. Norwegian.**

Bjørn "Aldrahn" Gjerde co-founded avant-garde/black metal band Dødheimsgard in 1994 as guitarist and vocalist, appearing on their debut *Kronet til Konge* (1995). He departed in the mid-2000s but returned for the band's *A Umbra Omega* era (2013–2016) before leaving again.
