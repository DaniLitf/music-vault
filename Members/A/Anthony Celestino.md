# Anthony Celestino

**Bass. Born unknown.**

Anthony Celestino is an American bassist, a friend of [[Members/T/Travis Barker]], who joined [[Artists/B/Box Car Racer]] as touring bassist for the band's live debut on April 1, 2002. Tom DeLonge had played bass on the studio album himself, so Celestino handled the role exclusively for live performances until the band's final show in Detroit in December 2002.
