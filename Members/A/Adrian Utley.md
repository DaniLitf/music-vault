# Adrian Utley

**Guitarist, producer. British.**

Adrian Francis Utley is an English musician, record producer, and core member of the trip-hop band [[Artists/P/Portishead]]. Born in Northampton on April 27, 1957, Utley moved to Bristol in the mid-1980s, where he worked as a jazz guitarist, touring with musicians including Big John Patton and Art Blakey's Jazz Messengers. He joined Portishead in 1990 as co-producer, musician, and arranger, contributing to their landmark albums *Dummy* (1994), *Portishead* (1997), and *Third* (2005). Beyond Portishead, Utley is an accomplished producer and collaborator, with production credits on albums by Beth Gibbons and Rustin' Man's *Out of Season*, Torres, Marianne Faithfull, and Perfume Genius, becoming known for his work on dark, uncompromising rock projects.
