# Art Alexakis

**Vocalist and songwriter. American.**

Art Alexakis is an American musician and songwriter born in 1953, best known as the lead vocalist and primary songwriter of [[Artists/E/Everclear]], the alternative rock band from the 1990s.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/E/Everclear]] – vocals, guitar (1991–present)
