# Alison Wheeler

**Vocals. Born 1972.**

Alison Wheeler is an English singer, best known as a vocalist for [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]] from 2000 to 2007.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]]

## Biography
Wheeler joined The Beautiful South in 2000, replacing Jacqui Abbott as female vocalist. She remained with the band until their dissolution in 2007.
