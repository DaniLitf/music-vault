# Akos Pardanyi

**Keyboardist, programmer. Hungarian.**

Akos Pardanyi is a Hungarian keyboardist and programmer who served as a member of [[Artists/C/Combichrist]], the industrial/electronic rock band. He contributed keyboards and programming to the band's sound during his tenure with the group.
