# Aaron Lewis

**Lead vocals, rhythm guitar. American. Founding member of [[Artists/S/Staind]].**

Aaron Lewis co-founded Staind in Springfield, Massachusetts in 1995, and has remained the band's vocalist and primary lyricist through its multiple hiatuses and reunions. His vocals drew frequent comparisons to Pearl Jam's Eddie Vedder. He has also pursued a solo country music career since the early 2010s.
