# Adrian Belew

**Adrian Belew is an American guitarist and singer born on December 19, 1949, known for his distinctive experimental guitar style and his work with King Crimson, Talking Heads, David Bowie, and Frank Zappa.**

Belew's unique guitar playing — characterized by animal-like sounds, extensive use of delay, and unconventional techniques — made him a sought-after session and touring musician. He joined Talking Heads for the *Remain in Light* tour in 1980, was a longtime member of King Crimson from 1981 to 2009, and has maintained a prolific solo career.
