# Atlanta Bliss

**Trumpet player.**

Atlanta Bliss is an American trumpet player who performed with Prince's backing band The Revolution from 1985 to 1986. He appeared on the albums *Around the World in a Day* and *Parade* and toured as part of the Revolution horn section alongside saxophonist Eric Leeds.
