# Astor Piazzolla

**Astor Pantaleón Piazzolla was an Argentine tango composer and bandoneón player born on March 11, 1921 in Mar del Plata, Argentina; he died on July 4, 1992 in Buenos Aires.**

Piazzolla grew up partly in New York, where he was exposed to jazz, before returning to Argentina and studying classical composition under Alberto Ginastera. He revolutionized traditional tango with nuevo tango, blending it with jazz improvisation and classical counterpoint, facing fierce opposition from tango traditionalists. His 1982 album *Tango: Zero Hour* is widely regarded as his masterpiece. He is considered one of the most important composers of the 20th century.
