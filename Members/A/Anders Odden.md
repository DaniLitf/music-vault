# Anders Odden

**Norwegian guitarist and founder of Cadaver, also known for Apoptygma Berzerk and Satyricon. Born 20 December 1972.**

Anders Odden was born in Stavanger, Norway, and grew up in Fredrikstad. He founded the death metal band Cadaver in 1988 and co-founded the industrial rock duo Magenta (with his wife Vilde Lockert). He played guitar for Apoptygma Berzerk across two periods (1992–1999 and 2003–2006), was a touring guitarist for Satyricon and Celtic Frost, and founded the black metal band ORDER. He also wrote an autobiography, *Piratliv* (2011).
