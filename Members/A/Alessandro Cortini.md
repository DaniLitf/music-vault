# Alessandro Cortini

**Keyboardist, bassist, electronic musician. Born 26 August 1977 in Bologna, Italy.**

Alessandro Cortini is an Italian electronic musician and multi-instrumentalist best known as a longtime touring member of [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]], playing keyboards, bass, and guitar across multiple tours since 2004. Outside of NIN, he has released solo experimental electronic music under his own name and as part of the duo SØSLEEPER with [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] bandmate Ilan Rubin. He is also a co-founder of the synthesizer company Make Noise Music.

## Biography

Cortini began his music career in Italy before moving to the United States. He joined Nine Inch Nails in 2004 as a keyboardist and bassist for the *With Teeth* tour and continued through the *Year Zero* and *Lights in the Sky* tours. After a hiatus from NIN, he rejoined in 2013 and has been a permanent touring member since. In parallel, he has built a reputation as a respected figure in modular synthesizers, founding Make Noise Music and releasing ambient/experimental albums on labels like Important Records.
