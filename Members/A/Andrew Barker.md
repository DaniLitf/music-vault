# Andrew Barker

**Keyboards, bass. Born March 9, 1968 — died November 6, 2021.**

Andrew Barker was an English musician, best known as a member of the electronic group [[Artists/0-9/808 State]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/0-9/808 State]] – keyboards, bass (1989–2021)

## Biography

Barker joined 808 State in 1989 alongside Darren Partington as the Spinmasters, after the departure of Gerald Simpson. He played on all of the band's albums from *90* onwards and remained a member until his death in November 2021 at age 53.
