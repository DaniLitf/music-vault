# Alexi Laiho

**Alexi Laiho was a Finnish guitarist and vocalist, the co-founder and creative force behind Children of Bodom, widely regarded as one of the most technically gifted guitarists in metal history.**

He combined neoclassical shredding with aggressive melodic death metal in a style that made Children of Bodom one of Finland's most internationally successful metal bands. He died in January 2020 at age 41.
