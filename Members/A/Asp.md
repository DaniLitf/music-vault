# Asp

**Drummer. Swedish.**

Asp is a Swedish drummer who played for Driller Killer from 2001 to 2006, performing on the albums *Cold, Cheap & Disconnected* (2002) and *The 4Q Mangrenade* (2005). He was previously a member of the Swedish band Mangler.
