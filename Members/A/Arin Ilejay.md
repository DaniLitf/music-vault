# Arin Ilejay

**Drummer. Born 1988-02-17.**

Arin Ilejay is an American drummer who played with Avenged Sevenfold from 2011 to 2015, appearing on the 2013 album *Hail to the King* before being replaced by Brooks Wackerman. He drummed for the bands Confide and Tear the Sails prior to joining Avenged Sevenfold, and later replaced Eric Fraizer as drummer of Islander.
