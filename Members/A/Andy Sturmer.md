**Drummer, vocalist, and songwriter. Born 1965.**

Andy Sturmer is an American musician, singer, songwriter, and composer who co-founded the rock band Jellyfish with Roger Manning in 1989. He served as the band's lead vocalist, drummer, and primary songwriter, crafting much of the band's sophisticated pop-rock material. After Jellyfish disbanded in 1994, Sturmer has worked as a composer and producer for film and television.
