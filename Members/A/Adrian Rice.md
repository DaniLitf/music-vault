# Adrian Rice

**Bassist, vocalist. British.**

Adrian Rice, known by the stage names Chaos (or Kaos) and Lice, is a British punk musician best known as the founding member, bassist, and vocalist of Chaos UK. He is the only continuous member of the band since its formation in 1979, anchoring the group through numerous lineup changes across four decades.
