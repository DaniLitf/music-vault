# Anthony "Rat" Martin

**Guitarist. British.**

Anthony "Rat" Martin is a British punk guitarist who played for Discharge from 1993 to 1999 and again from 2001 to 2014. He also played in The Varukers. He is the longest-serving guitarist in Discharge after Bones.
