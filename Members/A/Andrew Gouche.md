# Andrew Gouche

**Bassist. Born in the United States.**

Andrew Gouche is an American bassist who played with Prince's New Power Generation during the 2000s. He replaced Rhonda Smith and performed on Prince's *The Chocolate Invasion* and *The Slaughterhouse* albums. He also played with Chaka Khan and the Clark Sisters.
