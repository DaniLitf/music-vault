# Alan Strong

**Drummer. American.**

Alan Strong was the drummer for the Las Vegas grindcore band Righteous Pigs from 1989 until the band's breakup in 1990. He played on the band's second album *Stress Related* (1990) and also participated in the 1993 one-off reunion show. He was also a member of Atomic Gods.
