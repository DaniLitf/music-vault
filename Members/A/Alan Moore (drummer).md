# Alan Moore

**Drummer. British.**

Alan Moore is a British drummer who had two separate stints with Judas Priest, playing with the band from 1971 to 1972 and again from 1975 to 1977. He performed on the albums *Sad Wings of Destiny* and *Sin After Sin* before departing for the final time.
