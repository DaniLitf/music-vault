# Alex James

**Bassist. British.**

Alex James is a British bassist born in 1968, best known as a member of [[Artists/B/Blur]], the British rock/Britpop band. He was a key part of the band's classic lineup during the 1990s and beyond, contributing to their distinctive sound.
