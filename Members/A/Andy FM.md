# Andy FM

**Vocalist, keyboardist. American.**

Andy FM is an American vocalist and keyboardist who became the lead vocalist for His Name Is Alive on the albums *Detrola* and *XMMER*. She was previously known as the keyboardist for the Detroit bands the Tranzistors and Sonapanic.
