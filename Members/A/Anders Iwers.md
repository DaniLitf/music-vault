# Anders Iwers

**Bass, guitar. Born October 6, 1972.**

Anders Iwers is a Swedish musician from Gothenburg, the older brother of former In Flames bassist Peter Iwers, best known as the long-serving bassist of gothic metal band [[Artists/T/Tiamat]] and, more recently, [[Artists/A/Avatarium]]. He co-founded the death metal band [[Artists/C/Ceremonial Oath]] (originally Desecrator) in 1989, played guitar in [[Artists/C/Cemetary]] during their gothic-metal-era albums, was a session bassist for [[Artists/I/In Flames]] on *Whoracle* (1997), and later spent six years as [[Artists/D/Dark Tranquillity]]'s bassist.
