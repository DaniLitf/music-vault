# Amon Tobin

**Amon Tobin is a Brazilian-born, UK-based electronic musician and producer born on February 7, 1972.**

Tobin grew up in Rio de Janeiro before moving to the UK, signing to the Ninja Tune label in the mid-1990s. He is known for intricate sample-based music drawing on jazz, drum and bass, and experimental electronics. His 2011 album *ISAM* was accompanied by an acclaimed large-scale audiovisual live performance. He has also composed film and game soundtracks.
