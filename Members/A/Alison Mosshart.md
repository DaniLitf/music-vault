# Alison Mosshart

**Vocals, guitar. American. Co-founder of [[Artists/T/The Kills]] and member of [[Artists/T/The Dead Weather]].**

Alison Mosshart fronted the punk band Discount before moving to London in 2000, where she met guitarist Jamie Hince and co-founded The Kills with him the following year. In 2009 she also joined the supergroup The Dead Weather alongside Jack White, Dean Fertita, and Jack Lawrence.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Kills]] – vocals, guitar (2001–present, co-founder)
- [[Artists/T/The Dead Weather]] – lead vocals, rhythm guitar, keyboards (2009–present)
