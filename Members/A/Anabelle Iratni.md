# Anabelle Iratni

**Vocalist, guitarist. British.**

Anabelle Iratni is a British vocalist and guitarist who was a member of [[Artists/C/Cradle of Filth]], the symphonic black metal band. She contributed vocals and guitar work to the band's theatrical and symphonic sound during her tenure.
