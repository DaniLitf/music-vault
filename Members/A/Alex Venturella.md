# Alex Venturella

**Guitarist. American.**

Alex Venturella is an American guitarist who is a member of [[Artists/S/Slipknot]], the Iowa-based heavy metal/alternative metal band. He joined the band as a replacement and has been contributing guitar work to their sound since his addition to the lineup.
