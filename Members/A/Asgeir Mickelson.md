# Asgeir Mickelson

**Asgeir Mickelson is a Norwegian drummer who was a member of Borknagar from 1996 to 2009, playing on the majority of their classic albums.**

He is also known for his work with progressive metal bands Spiral Architect and Ihsahn, demonstrating technical versatility across multiple extreme metal projects.
