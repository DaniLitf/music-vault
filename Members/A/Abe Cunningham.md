# Abe Cunningham

**Bassist. American. Born 1973-07-27.**

Abe Cunningham is the bassist for the alternative metal band Deftones, joining in 1988 when the band formed in Sacramento, California. He has remained a core member throughout the band's entire history, contributing to their distinctive heavy yet atmospheric sound across all studio albums and major tours.
