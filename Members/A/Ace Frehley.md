# Ace Frehley

**Lead guitarist, co-vocalist. Born April 27, 1951.**

Ace Frehley is an American musician and songwriter, best known as the lead guitarist and co-vocalist of KISS. As "The Spaceman" (originally "Space Ace"), his solo album sold well and he wrote "New York Groove". He also leads Frehley's Comet.
