# Alex Skolnick

**Guitarist. American.**

Alex Skolnick is an American guitarist born in 1968, best known as a member of [[Artists/T/Testament]], the thrash metal band. He has been a core part of the band's lineup and is recognized as a skilled guitarist in the metal community.
