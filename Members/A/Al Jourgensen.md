# Al Jourgensen

**Vocalist, guitarist, producer. Born 1958-10-09 in Havana, Cuba.**

Al Jourgensen is a Cuban-American musician and producer best known as the founder of Ministry, one of the most influential industrial metal bands. He transformed Ministry from a synth-pop act into a visceral, guitar-driven industrial force across the late 1980s and 1990s. He has run numerous parallel projects including Revolting Cocks, Lard (with Jello Biafra), and participated in the formation of Pigface.
