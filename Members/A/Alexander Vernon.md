# Alexander Vernon

**Drummer.**

Alexander Vernon is an American drummer who joined the rebuilt [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] line-up in late 2000 and appears on the single *Morningleaver / This Is Me Hating You* (2001). He was replaced by Zachary Swain in March 2001.
