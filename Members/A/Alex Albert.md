# Alex Albert

**Drummer. American.**

Alex Albert is an American drummer best known as the founding drummer of Project 86, serving with the Christian rock band from 1996 to 2002. He played on their self-titled debut, *Drawing Black Lines*, and *Truthless Heroes*.
