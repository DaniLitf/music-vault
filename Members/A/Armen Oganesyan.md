# Armen Oganesyan

**Bassist. Born in Ukraine.**

Armen Oganesyan, known on stage as Armin von Heinessen (sometimes spelled Hennesen), is the bassist and a founding member of 1914. His deep, booming bass lines are a key part of the band's slow-to-mid-tempo death-doom sound.
