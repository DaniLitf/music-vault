# Abbath

**Vocalist, bassist, guitarist. Norwegian.**

Olve Eikemo, known as Abbath Doom Occulta, is a Norwegian musician who co-founded black metal band Immortal in 1991 with Demonaz, originally playing bass and vocals before switching to guitar and vocals from 1998. He departed Immortal in March 2015 following a dispute with bandmates and went on to form the band Abbath.
