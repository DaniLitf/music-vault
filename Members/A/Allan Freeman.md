# Allan Freeman

**Allan "Taff" Freeman is a British keyboardist and co-founder of the progressive rock band Nektar, with the band since their formation in Hamburg in 1969.**

Freeman's keyboard work, drawing on classical and jazz influences, gives Nektar much of its prog-rock character. He has been a core member through the band's original run and multiple reunion phases.
