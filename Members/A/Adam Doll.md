# Adam Doll

**Bass. American. Founding member of [[Artists/T/The Dillinger Escape Plan]].**

Adam Doll co-founded The Dillinger Escape Plan in 1997. Shortly before the recording of *Calculating Infinity* (1999) he was paralyzed from the chest down in a car accident; Ben Weinman played bass on the album in his place, though liner notes credited Doll's contributions. Doll returned briefly to play keyboards at the band's final show in 2017.
