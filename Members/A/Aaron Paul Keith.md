# Aaron Paul Keith

**Keyboardist. American.**

Aaron Paul Keith was a keyboardist of Mazarati, an American R&B and funk band from Minneapolis active from 1986 to 1989. He played keyboards on the band's self-titled debut album on Paisley Park Records, released in 1986, and contributed to the synth-driven Minneapolis sound that defined the group's musical style. Mazarati was produced by former The Revolution bassist Brown Mark, and the band's association with Prince led to their recordings being connected to the broader Paisley Park roster.
