# Arne Franck

**Bassist. Swedish. Born 1948-01-20.**

Arne Franck is a Swedish bass player known for his work with the psychedelic rock band Hoola Bandoola Band, which became influential in Swedish progressive rock circles. Beyond Hoola Bandoola Band, he has participated in numerous musical projects including Grus I Dojjan, Johnny's Boogie Band, and Kabaréorkestern, maintaining an active presence in Swedish music.
