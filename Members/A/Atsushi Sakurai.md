# Atsushi Sakurai

**Atsushi Sakurai was a Japanese singer and the iconic vocalist of Buck-Tick from the band's formation in 1983 until his sudden death during a live performance in October 2023.**

Renowned for his deep baritone, dramatic stage presence, and fashion sensibility, Sakurai was one of the most charismatic figures in Japanese rock. His death on stage brought a 40-year career to an abrupt and tragic close.
