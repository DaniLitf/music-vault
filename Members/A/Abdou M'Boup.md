# Abdou M'Boup

**Percussion. Senegalese.**

Abdou M'Boup is a Senegalese percussionist who helped pioneer the mbalax style in the 1970s as a member of the Dakar group Xalam, later collaborating with artists including Nina Simone, Jon Hassell, and Harry Belafonte. He guested on percussion for two tracks on [[Artists/T/The Heads]]' album *No Talking, Just Head* (1996).
