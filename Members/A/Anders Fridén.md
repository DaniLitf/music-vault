# Anders Fridén

**Anders Fridén is a Swedish vocalist best known as the longtime lead singer of the melodic death metal band [[Artists/I/In Flames]].**

Fridén joined In Flames in 1995, replacing original vocalist Mikael Stanne (of Dark Tranquillity), and made his recorded debut on *The Jester Race* (1996). He has since appeared on every In Flames album and is known for his versatile vocal style ranging from harsh growls to clean singing. Prior to In Flames, he was a member of Ceremonial Oath alongside Jesper Strömblad and briefly served as a vocalist for Dark Tranquillity.
