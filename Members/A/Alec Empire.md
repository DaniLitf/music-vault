# Alec Empire

**Alec Empire is a German musician and producer born May 2, 1972, co-founder of the digital hardcore band [[Artists/A/Atari Teenage Riot]] and founder of Digital Hardcore Recordings.**

Empire co-founded Atari Teenage Riot in Berlin in 1992 with Hanin Elias and Carl Crack, developing the digital hardcore genre — a fusion of hardcore techno, industrial, and punk. He runs Digital Hardcore Recordings, the label that released most of ATR's output as well as music from related acts. He has also released solo material and collaborated extensively with Nic Endo.
