# Anders Björler

**Guitarist, vocalist. Swedish.**

Anders Björler is a Swedish guitarist and vocalist born in 1978, best known as a member of [[Artists/A/At the Gates]], the melodic death metal band. He has been instrumental in shaping the band's guitar work throughout his tenure.
