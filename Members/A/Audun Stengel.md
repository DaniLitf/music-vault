# Audun Stengel

**Norwegian guitarist for Apoptygma Berzerk.**

Audun Stengel, also known as Angel, has played guitar for Apoptygma Berzerk across two periods (2002–2009 and 2013–present). He has been a consistent presence in the band's live shows and recordings throughout their electronic rock era.
