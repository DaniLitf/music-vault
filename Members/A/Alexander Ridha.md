# Alexander Ridha

**Electronic producer and DJ. German-Iraqi.**

Alexander Ridha is a German-Iraqi electronic music producer and DJ who performs and records as Boys Noize. He built a dance remix of the *Challengers* film score into the album *Challengers [MIXED]* (2024), which led to an ongoing collaboration with Nine Inch Nails — including their *Tron: Ares* soundtrack work and the collaborative act [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Noize]].
