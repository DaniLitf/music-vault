# Alva "Reggie" Lewis

**Guitarist. Died 2013-02-04, aged 63.**

Alva "Reggie" Lewis was a Jamaican guitarist born in Manchester Parish who moved to Kingston as a teenager and became part of the Greenwich Farm music scene. He was a founding member of The Hippy Boys, who backed producer Lee "Scratch" Perry as The Upsetters on a 1969 UK tour, and played guitar on several of Bob Marley and the Wailers' recordings for Perry, including "Mr. Brown."
