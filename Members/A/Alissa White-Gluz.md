# Alissa White-Gluz

**Vocalist. Canadian.**

Alissa White-Gluz is a Canadian vocalist best known as the lead singer of the melodic death metal band [[Artists/A/Arch Enemy]]. Born in 1985, she joined the band in 2014 and has been the frontwoman since, bringing a powerful vocal presence to their music and becoming one of the prominent female voices in metal.
