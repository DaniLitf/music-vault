# Allen West

**Guitarist. Born October 16, 1967.**

Allen West is an American death metal guitarist known as a founding member and former lead guitarist of Obituary and Six Feet Under. He played on classic Obituary albums *Slowly We Rot* (1989), *Cause of Death* (1990), *The End Complete* (1992), and *World Demise* (1994) across several tenures. He and Obituary vocalist John Tardy have been the only constant targets of the band's royalties lawsuit (West v. Obituary, 2010).
