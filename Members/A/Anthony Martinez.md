# Anthony Martinez

**Drums.**

Anthony Martinez replaced Bill Stevenson as drummer of [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] in 1985, becoming the band's last drummer before its 1986 breakup. He toured North America with the group and appeared on the live album *Who's Got the 10½?* (1986) and the live EP *Annihilate This Week* (1986).
