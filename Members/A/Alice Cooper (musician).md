# Alice Cooper (musician)

**Vocalist, songwriter. Born February 4, 1948.**

Alice Cooper (born Vincent Damon Furnier) is an American rock singer, songwriter, and actor whose career spans over five decades. He first gained fame as the frontman of the Alice Cooper band, which pioneered a theatrical, horror-themed hard rock style on landmark albums like *Love It to Death* (1971), *Killer* (1971), and *School's Out* (1972). After the band dissolved in 1975, he launched a hugely successful solo career with hits like "Poison" and "School's Out," becoming one of the most influential and recognizable figures in rock history. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2011.
