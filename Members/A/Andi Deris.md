# Andi Deris

**Vocalist. German.**

Andi Deris is a German vocalist born in 1964, best known as the lead singer of [[Artists/H/Helloween]], the power metal band. He joined the band in 1990 and has remained the frontman since, bringing his distinctive vocal style to their legendary power metal sound.
