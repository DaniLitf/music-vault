# Abraham Laboriel Jr.

**Drummer. American.**

Abraham Laboriel Jr. is an American drummer and the son of renowned bassist Abraham Laboriel Sr. He has worked as a session and touring drummer for a wide range of artists including Paul McCartney, Eric Clapton, Sting, and Kylie Minogue. He played drums on the 1995 album *Co-Burn* by DC-10.
