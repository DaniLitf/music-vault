# Alice Atkinson

**Bassist. British.**

Alice Atkinson is a bassist who played in [[Artists/T/The Featherz]] during the mid-2010s and contributed compositions including "Forget All You Know." Her period in the band included the 2015 Rebellion Festival appearance and recording sessions later represented on *Five-Year-Itch*.

## Associated Artists
