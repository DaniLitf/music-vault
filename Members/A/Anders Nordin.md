# Anders Nordin

**Drummer.**

Anders Nordin was an original member of Opeth, serving as the band's drummer from their formation in 1989 through 1997. He appeared on the first four studio albums — *Orchid*, *Morningrise*, *My Arms, Your Hearse*, and *Still Life* — and his drumming helped establish the band's early death metal sound before he was replaced by Martin Lopez.
