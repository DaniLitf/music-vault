# Alexander Noyes

**Drummer. American.**

Alexander Noyes is an American musician who served as the original drummer for the Jonas Brothers' touring band from 2005 to 2006. He was replaced by Jack Lawless in early 2007. He later joined the band Honor Society, which was signed to Jonas Records, a label founded by the Jonas Brothers and Hollywood Records.
