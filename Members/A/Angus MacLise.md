# Angus MacLise

**Percussion. 1938–1979.**

Angus MacLise was an American percussionist, poet, and occultist, best known as the original drummer of [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]]

## Biography
MacLise was the original percussionist for The Velvet Underground in 1964–1965, playing in their earliest days at Andy Warhol's Factory. He left the band when they accepted a paying gig (he preferred unpaid artistic performances). He later worked as a poet and musician in the New York avant-garde scene. His body was discovered in Kathmandu, Nepal in 1979.
