# Anton Fig

**Drummer. Born August 8, 1952.**

Anton Fig is an American drummer, best known as the session drummer on KISS's *Dynasty* and *Unmasked* albums while Peter Criss was recovering from a car accident. He also played with Joe Perry, Frehley's Comet, and was the house drummer for Late Show with David Letterman for 25 years.
