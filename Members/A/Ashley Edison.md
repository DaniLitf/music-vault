# Ashley Edison

**Vocalist. Welsh.**

Ashley Edison is a Welsh singer who joined glam metal band Tigertailz as lead vocalist in December 2022. He has fronted British power metal band Dendera since 2010, appearing on their albums *The Killing Floor* (2013) and *Pillars of Creation* (2015), and joined power metal band Power Quest as vocalist in 2016.
