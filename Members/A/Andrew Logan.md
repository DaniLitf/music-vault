# Andrew Logan

**Guitarist, drummer. Miami, Florida.**

Andrew Logan is an American guitarist from Miami, Florida, who played in Crestfallen, the 1996 precursor to [[Artists/B/Bird of Ill Omen]], and started the grindcore outfit Hondo. He rejoined Bird of Ill Omen in January 1997, staying until the band's dissolution and returning for the 2012 reunion, and later played drums in the side-project Dead Mens' Theory and guitar in the reformed Springfields.
