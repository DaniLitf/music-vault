# Andreas Kleerup

**Andreas Kleerup (born August 12, 1977, in Stockholm, Sweden) is a Swedish musician, producer, and songwriter, best known for his solo electronic project Kleerup and his Grammy Award-winning work with Robyn on "With Every Heartbeat" (2007).**

Kleerup was a member of the Teddybears before launching his solo project. His debut album *Kleerup* (2008) featured international collaborations including the UK #1 hit "With Every Heartbeat" with Robyn. He has produced and written for numerous Swedish pop artists.
