# Andrew Seward

**Bassist.**

Andrew Seward is an American bassist, formerly of the Tennessee band Kill Devil Hills, who joined [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]] in 2002 and remained through the band's 2007–2010 commercial peak before departing in 2013. He rejoined in 2018, later also becoming a member of [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust|As Friends Rust]] and releasing music independently as one half of the duo Nighttime Flyers.

## Associated Artists
