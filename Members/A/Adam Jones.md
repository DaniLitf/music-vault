# Adam Jones

**Adam Jones is an American guitarist and co-founder of Tool, known for his heavily down-tuned, riff-based guitar work and his collaboration with visual artists including Alex Grey on the band's elaborate visual presentation.**

Jones studied sculpture at the California Institute of the Arts and worked as a special effects artist in Hollywood before forming Tool.
