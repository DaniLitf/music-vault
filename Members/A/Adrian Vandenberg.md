# Adrian Vandenberg

**Guitarist. Dutch.**

Adrian Vandenberg is a Dutch rock guitarist best known for his work with [[Artists/W/Whitesnake]], joining the band in 1990 for the self-titled album and remaining through 1994, recording three albums with the group. A technically proficient and virtuoso guitarist, Vandenberg has also had a solo career and has worked with various other rock and metal projects throughout his career.
