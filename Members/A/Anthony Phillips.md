**Guitarist and founding member. Born 23 December 1951.**

Anthony Phillips is an English guitarist and founding member of Genesis, serving as the band's original lead guitarist from 1967 until his departure in 1970 due to stage fright and health issues. Though his tenure with Genesis was relatively brief, Phillips contributed to important early albums including From Genesis to Revelation (1969) and Trespass (1970). Following his departure from Genesis, Phillips pursued a solo career and has remained active in music, exploring progressive rock, acoustic, and world music influences.
