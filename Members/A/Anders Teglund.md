# Anders Teglund

**Keyboards. Swedish.**

Anders Teglund played keyboards for [[Artists/C/Cult of Luna]] until unexpectedly leaving the band during the recording of *Vertikal* (2013), after which he was replaced by Kristian Karlsson.
