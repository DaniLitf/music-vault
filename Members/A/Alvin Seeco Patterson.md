# Alvin Seeco Patterson

**Percussionist. Born ?.**

Alvin "Seeco" Patterson is a Jamaican percussionist best known as a longtime member of Bob Marley and the Wailers. He joined the group in the late 1960s and contributed percussion and backing vocals on numerous classic reggae recordings, including the albums *Catch a Fire* (1973) and *Exodus* (1977).
