**Percussionist. Born 10 December 1930.**

Alvin "Seeco" Patterson was a Jamaican percussionist who was a member of Bob Marley and the Wailers from 1975 until Marley's death in 1981, providing percussion on every album from *Rastaman Vibration* onwards. He had previously been a member of the Jiving Juniors and worked as a session musician in the 1950s and 1960s.
