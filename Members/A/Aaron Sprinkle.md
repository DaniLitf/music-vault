# Aaron Sprinkle

**Guitarist, producer, and songwriter. American.**

Aaron Sprinkle is an American musician, producer, and songwriter best known as the lead vocalist and guitarist for the band Poor Old Lu. He has also produced albums for numerous Christian rock and indie acts, including Project 86 and Early Novelist, and was a member of Neon Horse alongside Jason Martin and Steven Dail.
