# A.J. Pero

**Drummer. Born October 14, 1959. Died March 20, 2015.**

A.J. Pero was an American heavy metal drummer best known as the longtime drummer of Twisted Sister, whom he joined in April 1982 and with whom he recorded the triple-platinum album *Stay Hungry*. He departed in 1986, played with New York bands Cities and Four by Fate, and rejoined Twisted Sister for its 1997 reunion, remaining until his death. From December 2013 he was the drummer of Adrenaline Mob, appearing on the band's 2017 album *We the People*. Pero died of a heart attack on March 20, 2015, while on tour with Adrenaline Mob, at age 55.
