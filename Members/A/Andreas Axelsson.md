# Andreas Axelsson

**Multi-instrumentalist. Born November 20, 1971.**

Andreas Axelsson is a Swedish musician best known as a guitarist for [[Artists/E/Edge of Sanity]], which he co-founded with Dan Swanö in 1989. He also provided vocals for black metal band [[Artists/M/Marduk]] on their debut *Dark Endless* and has been the bassist for [[Artists/D/Disfear]] since 2014. In 2016 he co-founded the death metal supergroup The Lurking Fear with Tomas Lindberg.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/E/Edge of Sanity]] – guitar (1989–1999), bass (1993), vocals (1993, 1997)
- [[Artists/M/Marduk]] – vocals (1990–1993)
