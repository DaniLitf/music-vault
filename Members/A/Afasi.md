# Afasi

**Rapper, vocalist. Swedish.**

Afasi is a Swedish rapper and vocalist best known as one half of the Swedish hip-hop duo [[Artists/A/Afasi & Filthy]]. The duo emerged from the Swedish hip-hop scene and gained popularity with their collaborative work, creating rap music that resonated with audiences throughout Scandinavia.
