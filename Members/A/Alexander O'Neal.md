# Alexander O'Neal

**Singer. Born 1953-11-15.**

Alexander O'Neal is an American R&B singer who was the lead vocalist of Flyte Tyme from 1979 to 1981 before being replaced by Morris Day when Prince transformed the band into The Time. He went on to a successful solo career in the 1980s and 1990s with hits including "Fake", "Criticize", and "The Lovers", working extensively with Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis as producers.
