# Ace Finchum

**Drummer. Died October 2025, aged 62.**

Ace Finchum (born Stephen Wayne Finchum) was a Welsh drummer who joined glam metal band Tigertailz soon after their 1984 formation, replacing original drummer Ian Welch. He played on the band's first three studio albums, including their Top 40 breakthrough *Bezerk* (1990), before rejoining briefly from 2011–2013.
