# Andrew Stockdale

**Vocalist, guitarist, songwriter. Australian.**

Andrew James Stockdale is an Australian singer, musician, songwriter, and record producer best known as the lead vocalist, lead guitarist, and only continuous member of the hard rock band [[Artists/W/Wolfmother]], which he founded in 2004. Born in Brisbane and raised in Ashgrove, he holds dual Australian and British citizenship. Stockdale's music is influenced by hard rock and heavy metal artists from the late 1960s and 1970s, and his vocal style has been compared to a "cross between Robert Plant and Ozzy Osbourne." Beyond Wolfmother, he released two solo albums—*Keep Moving* (2013) and *Slipstream* (2018)—and was featured on Slash's 2010 single "By the Sword."
