# Andy Partridge

**Vocals, guitar. British. Born 1953-06-11.**

Andy Partridge is an English musician and songwriter, best known as the co-founder, singer, and guitarist of new wave band [[Artists/X/XTC]]. He wrote lyrics for and sang and whistled on "Papersnow" on [[Artists/T/The Heads]]' *No Talking, Just Head* (1996), a track praised as a highlight of the album.
