# Andreas Hedlund

**Andreas Hedlund, known as Vintersorg, is a Swedish musician who has been a vocalist of Borknagar since 2001 and is also the sole member of his own folk/progressive metal project Vintersorg.**

He is known for his versatile voice, capable of both clean and extreme vocals, and has made Borknagar's sound more melodic and progressive over the years.
