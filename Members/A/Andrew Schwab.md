# Andrew Schwab

**Vocalist and songwriter. American.**

Andrew Schwab is an American musician best known as the founder, lead vocalist, and primary songwriter of Project 86, a Christian rock and alternative metal band formed in 1996 in Orange County, California. He has led the band through nine studio albums and remains its sole constant member.
