# Al Atkins

**Vocalist. Born 1949.**

Al Atkins is a British vocalist who was the original singer of Judas Priest from the band's formation in 1969 until 1973. He is credited with naming the band after the Bob Dylan song "The Ballad of Frankie Lee and Judas Priest" and co-wrote "Victim of Changes," which became one of the band's most celebrated songs.
