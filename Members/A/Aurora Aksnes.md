# Aurora Aksnes

**Singer-songwriter, producer. Born 1996-06-15.**

Aurora Aksnes is a Norwegian singer, songwriter, and producer known mononymously as [[Artists/A/AURORA]]. Her music blends art pop, electropop, folk-pop, and cinematic electronic textures; she has also contributed to film, television, and game soundtracks and formed [[Artists/T/Tomora]] with Tom Rowlands.

## Associated Artists
