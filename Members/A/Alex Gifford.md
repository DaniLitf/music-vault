# Alex Gifford

**Keyboardist, programmer. British.**

Alex Gifford is a British keyboardist and programmer who was a member of [[Artists/P/Propellerheads]], the electronic/dance music group. He contributed keyboards and programming to their electronic sound during his tenure.
