# Alex Vincent

**Guitarist. Born December 14, 1965.**

Alex Vincent is an American guitarist best known as a founding member of the Seattle proto-grunge band Green River, which he formed in 1984. He played a crucial role in the early Seattle music scene, with Green River being one of the first bands to blend punk rock with heavy metal influences. After Green River disbanded in 1988, Vincent has continued to participate in reunion performances and remains connected to the influential legacy of the band.
