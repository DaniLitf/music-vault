# Aston Barrett

**Aston "Family Man" Barrett is a Jamaican bassist and co-leader of the Wailers, widely considered one of the greatest bassists in reggae history.**

Along with his brother Carlton, he formed the rhythmic backbone of Bob Marley and the Wailers from 1969 onwards, playing on all the landmark Island Records albums. After Marley's death in 1981, he continued leading various Wailers lineups.
