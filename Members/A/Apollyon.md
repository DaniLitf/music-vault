# Apollyon

**Vocalist and bassist. Norwegian.**

Apollyon (Ole Jørgen Moe) is a Norwegian musician best known for his work in Immortal, Aura Noir, and Dødheimsgard, who also handled vocals and bass for Cadaver (billed as Cadaver Inc.) from 2002 to 2004, appearing on the album *Discipline* (2001).

- [[Artists/A/Aura Noir]] – vocals, guitars, bass, drums (1993–present)
- [[Artists/D/Dødheimsgard]] – guitars, vocals (1996), drums (1996–1998), bass (1999–2004)
