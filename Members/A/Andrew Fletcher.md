# Andrew Fletcher

**Andrew Fletcher (known as Fletch) was a British keyboardist and founding member of Depeche Mode, who died in May 2022.**

Though acknowledged as the least technically involved member musically, his business acumen and personality were vital to the band's management and cohesion across four decades.
