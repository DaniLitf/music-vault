# Adam Magnusson

**Musician, producer. Swedish.**

Adam Magnusson is a Swedish musician and producer who contributed choir vocals to Alarma Man's 2010 album *Love Forever* and co-produced the record alongside the band. See [[Producers/A/Adam Magnusson]] for his production credits.
