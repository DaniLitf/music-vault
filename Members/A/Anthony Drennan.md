# Anthony Drennan

**Touring guitarist and bassist. Irish.**

Anthony Drennan is an Irish musician who has served as touring guitarist and bassist for Genesis since 1997. He has also toured with Mike + The Mechanics and Paul Brady, and is a seasoned session and live performer.
