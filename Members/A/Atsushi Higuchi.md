# Atsushi Higuchi

**Atsushi Higuchi, better known by his stage name Yagami Toll, is a Japanese drummer and co-founder of Buck-Tick, the older brother of bassist Yutaka Higuchi.**

He has been the band's drummer since their formation in 1983 and is known for his powerful, precise playing across Buck-Tick's genre-spanning catalogue.
