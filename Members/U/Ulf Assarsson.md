# Ulf Assarsson

**Drums. Swedish.**

Ulf Assarsson was the original drummer of [[Artists/C/Ceremonial Oath]] (then known as Desecrator) from its 1989 formation until 1991, when he was replaced by Markus Nordberg.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/C/Ceremonial Oath]] – drums (1989–1991, founding member)
