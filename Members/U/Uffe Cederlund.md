# Uffe Cederlund

**Guitarist. Born October 3, 1971.**

Uffe Cederlund is a Swedish guitarist best known as a founding member of [[Artists/E/Entombed]], one of the pioneering bands of Swedish death metal. He played on all classic Entombed albums from *Left Hand Path* through *Serpent Saints* and also joined crust punk band [[Artists/D/Disfear]] as a full-time guitarist in 2004. He was also a member of Morbid (1987–1988) and Nihilist, the precursor to Entombed.
