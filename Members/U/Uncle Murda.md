# Uncle Murda

**Rapper. Born in Brooklyn, New York.**

Uncle Murda is an American rapper from the East New York section of Brooklyn, known for his gritty street records and his long-running annual "Rap Up" year-in-review songs. He signed to 50 Cent's G-Unit Records and joined G-Unit in 2016, during the group's final era before its 2022 dissolution.
