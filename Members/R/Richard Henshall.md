# Richard Henshall

**Guitarist, keyboardist, and multi-instrumentalist. Born 1984-08-09.**

Richard "Hen" Henshall is an English progressive metal multi-instrumentalist, classically trained on piano from age seven before teaching himself guitar. He is a founding member of Haken, joined To-Mera as keyboardist in 2007 (replacing Hugo Sheppard) recording the *Earthbound* EP and *Exile* album, and later co-founded Nova Collective with Between the Buried and Me's Dan Briggs in 2014.
