# Rob Doran

**Bassist, backing vocalist. American.**

Rob Doran is a musician who co-founded [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio]] in 1996 alongside Matt Skiba and Glenn Porter, playing bass on the band's earliest demo and its debut single "Sundials" (1997) before leaving to focus on visual art. He was replaced by Dan Andriano. Doran later played bass in the band Pit er Pat and continues to work in visual art and design.
