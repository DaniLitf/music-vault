# Ray Davis

**Vocals (bass). American. Original member of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Ray Davis was the bass singer of the Parliaments, his distinctive low voice heard on "Give Up the Funk (Tear the Roof off the Sucker)" and George Clinton's "Atomic Dog." Aside from Clinton, he was the only original Parliaments member not to leave in 1977. He later performed with former bandmates Fuzzy Haskins, Calvin Simon, and Grady Thomas as Original P. He died in 2005.
