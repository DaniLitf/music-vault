# Rob Sheridan

**Visual artist, art director, synthesizer, programmer.**

Rob Sheridan is an American visual artist and art director who served as Nine Inch Nails' creative director for over a decade, overseeing the band's visual identity, album artwork, music videos, and live show design. He contributed synthesizer and programming to How to Destroy Angels from 2013 onward and provided backing vocals on their *Welcome Oblivion* tour.
