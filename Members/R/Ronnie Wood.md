# Ronnie Wood

**Ronnie Wood is a British musician who has served as a guitarist in The Rolling Stones since 1975, and was previously a member of the Faces and the Jeff Beck Group.**

Wood's versatile style — equally adept at slide and rhythm guitar — made him a natural fit to replace Mick Taylor, and he has remained a core member of the Stones for five decades.
