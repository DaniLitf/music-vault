# Raymond Pettibon

**Visual artist; occasional bass. Born 1957-06-16.**

Raymond Pettibon (born Raymond Ginn) is an American visual artist and the younger brother of [[Members/G/Greg Ginn]]. He briefly played bass with [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] during its earliest 1976 rehearsals, suggested the band's name and designed its iconic bars logo, and created the stark, provocative single-panel illustrations that defined the band's sleeve art, posters, and flyers from 1979 to 1985. He went on to become one of the most acclaimed American illustrators of his generation, later contributing cover art to Sonic Youth's *Goo* (1990).

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/S/Sonic Youth]] – cover artist, *Goo* (1990)
