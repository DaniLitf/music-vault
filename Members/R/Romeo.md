# Romeo

**Bassist. American.**

Romeo, born Jerome Cox, was the bassist of Mazarati, an American R&B and funk band from Minneapolis. He met fellow student Brown Mark at a Minneapolis high school; Brown Mark later joined Prince's band The Revolution and went on to produce Mazarati's recordings. As one of the founding members alongside lead vocalist Sir Casey Terry, Romeo helped shape the band's funk-driven sound on their 1986 self-titled debut on Paisley Park Records and contributed to the backing vocals on Prince's hit "Kiss."
