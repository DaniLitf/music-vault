# Ryan Mahon

**Guitarist, bassist.**

Ryan Mahon is an American guitarist and backing vocalist who filled in for Joseph Simmons on [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]]'s European and UK tour in September–October 2023. He returned for the band's 2024 European tour, this time covering bass guitar and backing vocals.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] – guitar, backing vocals (2023, touring); bass guitar, backing vocals (2024, touring)
