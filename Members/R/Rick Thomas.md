# Rick Thomas

**Electronics, samples, keyboards, DJ. American.**

Rick Thomas, known professionally as St1tch, is an American musician and electronic artist best known for his extensive work with the industrial metal band Mushroomhead from 2001–2022. He provided samples, electronic programming, keyboards, water drums, and turntable work, contributing to every album from *XX* (2001) onwards and serving as a key sonic architect of the band's increasingly experimental industrial sound.
