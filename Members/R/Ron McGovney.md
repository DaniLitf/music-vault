# Ron McGovney

**Bass. Born November 2, 1963.**

Ron McGovney was [[Artists/M/Metallica]]'s original bassist, having previously played with James Hetfield in the garage band Leather Charm, and appeared on the band's earliest 1982 demos including *No Life 'til Leather*. Beyond playing bass, he bankrolled the band's early gigs and gear and let them rehearse in his parents' garage before quitting in December 1982 amid tension with Ulrich and Mustaine; he was replaced by Cliff Burton and later played in the thrash band Phantasm.
