# Ricky Phillips

**Bass. American.**

Ricky Phillips is an American bassist known for his tenures in the Babys, Bad English and, from 2003, Styx. He took part in the early Coverdale–Page writing rehearsals in Barbados and played bass on two tracks of the [[Artists/C/Coverdale-Page]] album (1993).

## Associated Artists
