# Randy Jackson

**Randy Jackson** (born June 23, 1956) is an American bassist, singer, and record producer best known as a judge on *American Idol* and for his session work, including with [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]].

- [[Artists/J/Jefferson Airplane]] – bass (1989; session)
- [[Journey (band)|Journey]] – bass (1985–1987)

Note: This is the bassist Randy Jackson, not the similarly-named member of the Jackson family.
