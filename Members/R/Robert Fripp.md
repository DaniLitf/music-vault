# Robert Fripp

**British guitarist and producer. Born 16 May 1946.**

Robert Fripp is a British guitarist and producer best known as the founder and sole constant member of progressive rock band King Crimson. He is considered one of the most influential guitarists in rock history, known for his distinctive style incorporating elements of classical music, jazz, and ambient music. He has also worked with Brian Eno, David Bowie, Peter Gabriel, and Talking Heads.
