# Robby Krieger

**Robby Krieger is an American guitarist and co-founder of The Doors, notable for writing several of the band's biggest hits including "Light My Fire," "Love Her Madly," and "Touch Me."**

Krieger's distinctive fingerpicking style and flamenco influences gave The Doors a unique sound. He has performed with various ensembles since the band's end and in reunions of surviving Doors members.
