# Rhonda Smith

**Bassist. Born in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada.**

Rhonda Smith is a Canadian-American bassist who played with Prince's New Power Generation from 1996 to 2002. She replaced Sonny T. and appeared on *Newpower Soul* and the *Rave Un2 the Joy Fantastic* tours. She has also played with Jeff Beck and Beyoncé.
