# Richard Potts

**Drummer. British.**

Richard Potts (also known as Potts or Spot) is a British punk drummer who was the original drummer of Chaos UK. Formerly of Damage Department, he played on the band's early EPs and debut LP before leaving in 1983 along with the original lineup changes.
