# Roger Waters

**Bassist, vocalist, lyricist. Born 1943-09-06 in Great Bookham, England.**

Roger Waters is a co-founding member of Pink Floyd and the primary creative force behind the band's most celebrated conceptual works, including *The Dark Side of the Moon* (1973), *Wish You Were Here* (1975), *Animals* (1977), and *The Wall* (1979). As the band's principal lyricist and bassist, his thematic preoccupations — alienation, war, madness, loss — gave Pink Floyd its philosophical depth. He departed in 1985 following creative conflicts and has maintained a long solo career, notably performing *The Wall* live in massive stadium productions. He was involved in a brief reunion with the band for Live 8 in 2005.
