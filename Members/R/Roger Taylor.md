# Roger Taylor

**Drummer, vocalist. Born 1949-07-26 in King's Lynn, Norfolk, England.**

Roger Taylor is the drummer and co-founder of Queen, known for his powerful playing and ability to sing high harmonies (and even lead vocals on songs like "Radio Ga Ga"). He has remained one of rock's most active drummers, continuing to tour with Queen + Adam Lambert into his seventies. He also led his own band The Cross in the late 1980s and early 1990s, and has released solo albums.
