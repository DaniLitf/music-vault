# Ron Reyes

**Vocals. Born 1960-07-24.**

Ron Reyes is an American singer of Puerto Rican descent who replaced Keith Morris as vocalist of [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] in December 1979, bringing a chaotic, anarchic stage presence, before quitting mid-performance at a May 1980 show in Redondo Beach. He was credited as "Chavo Pederast" on the *Jealous Again* EP (1980) and returned in 2013 to front the band's reunion lineup until being fired on stage later that year. He later fronted the band Piggy.
