# Ryan Sinn

**Bass, guitar. Born 1979-04-23.**

Ryan Sinn is an American bassist best known for [[Artists/T/The Distillers|The Distillers]], which he joined after their debut album, appearing on *Sing Sing Death House* (2002) and *Coral Fang* (2003) before leaving in 2005 (and later rejoining for the band's 2018 reunion). He was recruited by Tom DeLonge as a founding bassist of [[Artists/A/Angels & Airwaves|Angels & Airwaves]] in 2005, playing on the band's debut *We Don't Need to Whisper* (2006) before departing in 2007.
