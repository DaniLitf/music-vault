# Robin Guthrie

**Robin Guthrie is a Scottish guitarist and producer, co-founder of Cocteau Twins, known for his lush, effects-saturated guitar sound that defined the dream pop genre.**

After Cocteau Twins dissolved in 1997, he has maintained a solo ambient and electronic music career.
