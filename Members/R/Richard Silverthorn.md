# Richard Silverthorn

**British keyboardist, programmer, and composer for Mesh. Born in England.**

Richard Silverthorn co-founded Mesh in Bristol in 1991 with Mark Hockings after they met at a concert where Silverthorn's band was playing. He composes the majority of Mesh's music and handles keyboards, programming, and production. Together with Mark Hockings, he has been the sole constant member of the band since Neil Taylor's departure in 2006.
