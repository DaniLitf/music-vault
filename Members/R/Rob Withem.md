# Rob Withem

**Multi-instrumentalist. American.**

Rob Withem is an American multi-instrumentalist best known as a member of Project 86. He joined Starflyer 59 in the late 2010s, contributing keyboards, guitars, and other instrumentation on albums around 2019 and beyond.
