# Ricardo Overman

**Rapper. Dutch.**

Ricardo Overman, known as MC Fixxit, is a Dutch rapper who performed the rap verses on the debut Twenty 4 Seven single "I Can't Stand It" in 1989. He was the original rapper in the project before being replaced by Tony Dawson-Harrison (Captain Hollywood) in 1990.
