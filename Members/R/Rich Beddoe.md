# Rich Beddoe

**Drums. Canadian. Former drummer of [[Artists/F/Finger Eleven]] and founding member of [[Artists/S/Saint Asonia]].**

Rich Beddoe was the longtime drummer of Finger Eleven (1998–2013) before joining Adam Gontier and Mike Mushok as a founding member of Saint Asonia in 2014. He left Saint Asonia on good terms in 2017.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/F/Finger Eleven]] – drums (1998–2013)
- [[Artists/S/Saint Asonia]] – drums (2014–2017, founder)
