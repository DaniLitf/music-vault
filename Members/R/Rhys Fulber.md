# Rhys Fulber

**Electronic musician, record producer. Born October 10, 1970 in Vancouver, Canada.**

Rhys Fulber is a Canadian electronic musician and record producer best known as a member of Front Line Assembly and Delerium alongside Bill Leeb, and as the solo project Conjure One. He has been a prolific producer and remixer for industrial and metal acts including Fear Factory (producing *Demanufacture* and *Obsolete*), and has worked with numerous other artists. He won two Juno Awards with Delerium for Best Dance Recording and was nominated for the Jack Richardson Producer of the Year award in 2008.
