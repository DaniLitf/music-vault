# Rob Gommerman

**Drums. Canadian. Founding member of [[Artists/F/Finger Eleven]].**

Rob Gommerman was Finger Eleven's original drummer from its 1990 formation as Rainbow Butt Monkeys until his 1998 departure, after which he was replaced by Rich Beddoe.
