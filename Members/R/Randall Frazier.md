# Randall Frazier

**Synthesizer/electronics musician. American.**

Randall Frazier joined [[Artists/T/The Legendary Pink Dots]] in 2022 on synths, samples, and electronics after Phil Knight retired from touring. Sources also associate him with Orbit Service and A Star Too Far.

## Associated Artists
