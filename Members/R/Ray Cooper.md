# Ray Cooper

**Vocals, guitar. American.**

Ray Cooper is an American punk musician who fronted [[Artists/D/Descendents]] as vocalist during Milo Aukerman's college absences (1982–1983), then switched to guitar and replaced Frank Navetta for *I Don't Want to Grow Up* (1985) and *Enjoy!* (1986). He drew the cover artwork for *Enjoy!* under the pseudonym "Scoob Droolins" and performed as the Ascendents with Tony Lombardo during the band's mid-80s hiatus. AllMusic also lists him as an alum of the SST band [[Artists/S/SWA]]. Not to be confused with the British percussionist of the same name.
