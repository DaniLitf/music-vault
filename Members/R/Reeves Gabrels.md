# Reeves Gabrels

**Guitarist, producer. American.**

Reeves Gabrels is an American guitarist best known as a founding member of [[Artists/T/Tin Machine]] alongside David Bowie, with whom he continued to co-write and co-produce as a solo collaborator on *Outside* (1995), *Earthling* (1997), and *Hours...* (1999) after the band dissolved. He later joined [[Artists/T/The Cure]] as a touring and studio guitarist.
