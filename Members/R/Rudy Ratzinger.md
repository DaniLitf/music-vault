# Rudy Ratzinger

**German electronic musician. Born 1970.**

Rudy Ratzinger is a German electronic musician best known as the founder, vocalist, and sole consistent member of the dark electro/industrial project Wumpscut. He is known for his aggressive, sample-heavy production style.
