# Roger Glover

**Bass, keyboards, backing vocals. Born November 30, 1945.**

Roger Glover is the bassist of [[Artists/D/Deep Purple]], joining in 1969 as part of the Mark II lineup. He also played in [[Artists/R/Rainbow]] and has worked extensively as a record producer.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/D/Deep Purple]] – bass (1969–1973, 1984–present)
- [[Artists/R/Rainbow]] – bass (1975–1980)
