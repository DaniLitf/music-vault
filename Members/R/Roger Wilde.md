# Roger Wilde

**Early live musician. British.**

Roger "Dodger" Wilde is documented as an early on-loan musician used by [[Artists/T/The Housemartins]] before Hugh Whitaker settled into the lineup. His known vault-relevant connection is through the band's first live four-piece period in Hull.
