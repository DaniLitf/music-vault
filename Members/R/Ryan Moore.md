# Ryan Moore

**Drummer, producer, dub musician. Canadian.**

Ryan Moore played drums on studio and live performances for [[Artists/T/The Legendary Pink Dots]]. After leaving the band he focused on [[Artists/T/Twilight Circus Dub Sound System]] and produced work for reggae artists including Michael Rose of [[Artists/B/Black Uhuru]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Legendary Pink Dots]] – drums, studio/live performances
- [[Artists/T/Twilight Circus Dub Sound System]] – founder, producer
- [[Artists/B/Black Uhuru]] – producer connection via Michael Rose
