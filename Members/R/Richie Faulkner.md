# Richie Faulkner

**Guitarist. Born 1980.**

Richie Faulkner is a British guitarist who joined Judas Priest in 2011 as the replacement for founding member K.K. Downing. Prior to joining Judas Priest, he played in the Lauren Harris band and has since become an integral part of the band's modern sound and touring lineup.
