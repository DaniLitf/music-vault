# Royston Wainwright

**Bassist, guitarist. British.**

Royston "Rainy" Wainwright is a British punk musician and a founding member of Discharge. He played guitar initially before switching to bass, and has been the band's longest-serving constant member, playing on virtually every release. He was present for all of Discharge's active periods from 1977 onward.
