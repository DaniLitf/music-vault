**Vocalist.**

Réjane Magloire, known professionally as Reggie, is a vocalist who became the lead singer of [[Artists/T/Technotronic|Technotronic]] for their second album *Body to Body* (1991). She provided vocals on six of the album's tracks, including the singles "Move That Body", "Work", and "Money Makes the World Go Round".

She took over frontwoman duties after the brief tenures of Melissa Beckford and Colin Case, anchoring the group through their post-*Pump Up the Jam* era before the project eventually reunited with Ya Kid K.
