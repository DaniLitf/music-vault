# Ron Thal

**American guitarist and songwriter. Born 25 September 1969.**

Ron "Bumblefoot" Thal (born Ronald Jay Blumenthal) is an American guitarist, songwriter, and producer best known as one of two lead guitarists in Guns N' Roses from 2006 to 2014, and as a founding member of the supergroup Art of Anarchy. He has released numerous solo albums and is also a member of Sons of Apollo, Whom Gods Destroy, and Asia (2019–2022). See [[Producers/R/Ron Thal]] for his production credits.
