# Ron Thal

**American guitarist and producer. Born 25 September 1969.**

Ron Thal (born Ron David Thal) is an American guitarist best known by his stage name Bumblefoot. He was the lead guitarist of Guns N' Roses (2006–2014) and a member of the progressive metal supergroup Sons of Apollo. He is known for his virtuoso guitar technique and humorous songwriting style.
