# Robert Jörgensen

**Bassist. Swedish.**

Robert "Lefty" Jörgensen was a Swedish bassist who played for Driller Killer from 1996 to 1997. He also played bass in Anti Cimex (1991–1993) and was a member of Perukers and Black Uniforms. He is deceased.
