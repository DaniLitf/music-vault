# René Jansen

**Bassist. Norwegian.**

René Jansen joined death metal band Cadaver as bassist in 1989, appearing on the "Abnormal Deformity" demo, before being replaced by Eilert Solstad shortly after the release of the band's debut album *Hallucinating Anxiety* (1990).
