# Richard Taylor

**Vocalist. British.**

Richard Taylor is a British singer whose career was mentored for years by Iron Maiden bassist Steve Harris before joining Harris's hard rock side project [[Artists/B/British Lion]] as lead vocalist, co-writing most of the band's material with Harris and guitarist David Hawkins.
