# Riki Michele

**Vocalist and songwriter. American.**

Riki Michele (born Michele Bunch, now Michele Palmer) is an American singer best known as the female vocalist for the alternative rock band Adam Again. She was married to bandleader Gene Eugene from the early 1980s until their divorce in 1994, but continued recording with him until his death in 2000. Her vocal style—described as a "funky diva"—was a defining element of Adam Again's sound. She has also released several solo albums.

## Biography

Riki Michele began her musical career as a Christian alternative rock artist. She joined Adam Again shortly after its formation in 1982, providing lead and backing vocals that complemented Gene Eugene's singing. After Adam Again disbanded following Eugene's death in 2000, Michele continued making music, releasing solo albums including *Surround Me* (2002) and *Push* (2015). She also appeared on tribute albums such as *Making God Smile: An Artists' Tribute to the Songs of Beach Boy Brian Wilson*.
