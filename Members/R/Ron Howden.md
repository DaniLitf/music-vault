# Ron Howden

**Ron Howden is a British drummer and co-founder of the progressive rock band Nektar, with the band since their formation in Hamburg in 1969.**

Howden's drumming underpins Nektar's expansive, psychedelic compositions, providing the rhythmic foundation for the band's extended instrumental passages and conceptual works.
