# Robert Dahlqvist

**Lead guitar, vocals. Swedish. 1976–2017.**

Robert Dahlqvist, nicknamed "Strängen" ("Strings") for the strings he broke in rehearsals, was the lead guitarist of [[Artists/T/The Hellacopters]] from 1999 until the band's 2008 breakup, appearing on *High Visibility* (2000), *By the Grace of God* (2002), and *Rock & Roll Is Dead* (2005). He also led the band Dundertåget (originally Thunder Express) and was working on a solo album under the name Strängen when he died in 2017 at age 40.
