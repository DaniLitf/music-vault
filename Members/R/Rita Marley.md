**Backing vocalist. Born 25 July 1946.**

Rita Marley is a Jamaican singer and the widow of [[Members/B/Bob Marley]]. She was a member of the I Threes, the female backing vocal trio for Bob Marley and the Wailers from 1974 until Marley's death in 1981, and continued to perform and manage the Marley legacy thereafter. She also had a solo career, notably with the hit "One Draw".
