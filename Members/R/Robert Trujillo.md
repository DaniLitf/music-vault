# Robert Trujillo

**Bass (1989–1997, 2008–present). Born October 23, 1964.**

Robert Trujillo is an American bassist best known as the bassist of [[Artists/M/Metallica]] and a former member of [[Artists/S/Suicidal Tendencies]] and [[Artists/I/Infectious Grooves]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Infectious Grooves]] – bass (1989–1997, 2008–present)
- [[Artists/S/Suicidal Tendencies]] – bass (1989–1995)
- [[Artists/M/Metallica]] – bass (2003–present)
- [[Artists/B/Black Label Society]] – session bassist
- [[Artists/O/Ozzy Osbourne]] – touring bassist (1996–2003)

## Biography

Trujillo first gained fame in Suicidal Tendencies before co-founding Infectious Grooves in 1989. He toured with Ozzy Osbourne for seven years before joining Metallica in 2003, appearing on all subsequent albums including *Death Magnetic* (2008) and *72 Seasons* (2023).
