# Rex Brown

**Bassist. Born 1964-07-27 in Graham, Texas, USA.**

Rex Brown was the bassist of Pantera from 1987 through the band's dissolution in 2003, playing on all their major albums from *Power Metal* (1988) through *Reinventing the Steel* (2000). His heavy, melodic bass lines provided the low-end foundation to Pantera's groove metal sound. After Pantera he joined Down alongside fellow Pantera alumnus Phil Anselmo, and later fronted his own band Kill Devil Hill before releasing solo material.
