# Ronnie James Dio

**Ronnie James Dio was an American vocalist widely regarded as one of the greatest heavy metal singers of all time, who fronted Rainbow, Black Sabbath (1979–1982, 1991–1993), and his own band Dio.**

He popularised the "devil horns" hand gesture in rock. He died of stomach cancer in May 2010.
