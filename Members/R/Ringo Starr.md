# Ringo Starr

**Ringo Starr is a British musician who served as the drummer of The Beatles from 1962, replacing Pete Best and playing on every studio album from *With the Beatles* through *Let It Be*.**

Widely praised for his distinctive pocket drumming style, Starr has also maintained a solo career and tours regularly with his All-Starr Band, a rotating supergroup of classic rock musicians.
