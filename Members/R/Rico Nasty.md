# Rico Nasty

**Rapper. American.**

Rico Nasty (Maria-Cecilia Kelly) is an American rapper known for her aggressive, genre-blending style. She appears as a guest vocalist on "Vessel" from the 2026 collaborative album *Nine Inch Noize* by Nine Inch Nails and Boys Noize.
