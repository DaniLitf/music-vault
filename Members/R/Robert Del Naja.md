# Robert Del Naja

**British musician and artist. Born 21 January 1965.**

Robert Del Naja, known as 3D, is a British musician, artist, and founding member of Massive Attack. He is known for his politically charged lyrics and his distinctive vocal and production style. He is also a well-known graffiti artist and visual artist.
