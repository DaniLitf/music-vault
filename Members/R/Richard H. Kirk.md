# Richard H. Kirk

**Richard H. Kirk was a British musician and co-founder of Cabaret Voltaire in Sheffield in 1973 alongside Stephen Mallinder, and later a co-founder of Wrangler in 2013.**

Kirk was one of the most prolific and exploratory figures in industrial and electronic music, also releasing a vast catalogue of solo material and experimental work. He died in September 2021.
