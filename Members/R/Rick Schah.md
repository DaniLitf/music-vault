# Rick Schah

**Programmer. German — died 2022.**

Rick Schah was a German musician who provided programming and samples for And One from 1994 to 2001 and again from 2011 until his death in 2022. He contributed to the band's Virgin-label era albums including *Nordhausen*, *9.9.99 9 Uhr*, and *Virgin Superstar*.
