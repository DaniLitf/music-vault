# Richard Swift

**Keyboardist, singer-songwriter, and producer. Born March 16, 1977.**

Richard Swift was an American singer-songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, and producer known for his solo work blending pop, folk, and psychedelia. He played keyboards on Starflyer 59's album *Old* (2003) and was a touring member of The Shins from 2011 until his death. He also performed with The Arcs and worked as a session musician for The Black Keys, Damien Jurado, and Nathaniel Rateliff. He founded National Freedom studio in Cotage Grove, Oregon.
