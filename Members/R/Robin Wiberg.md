# Robin Wiberg

**Drums. Swedish.**

Robin Wiberg is a Swedish drummer who performed with [[Artists/D/Disfear]] from 1995 to 1998, replacing Jallo Lehto. He appeared on the album *Everyday Slaughter* before leaving the band prior to Tomas Lindberg joining in 1998.
