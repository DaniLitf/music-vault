# Regan Hagar

**Drummer. American.**

Regan Hagar is an American drummer who met future Malfunkshun bandmate Andrew Wood at Commodore Junior High School on Bainbridge Island, Washington, and played drums for the band from 1980 to 1988. He later co-founded the band Brad in 1992 with Pearl Jam's Stone Gossard, vocalist Shawn Smith, and bassist Jeremy Toback.
