# Russ Russell

**Record producer / engineer / musician. British.**

Russ Russell is a British record producer and audio engineer best known for his long-running collaboration with Napalm Death, producing many of their albums from the 1990s onward. He has also worked with Dimmu Borgir, The Exploited, and Amorphis, among others. Russell was listed as a member of Meathook Seed for the album *Basic Instructions Before Leaving Earth (B.I.B.L.E.)* (1999), contributing production to the project.
