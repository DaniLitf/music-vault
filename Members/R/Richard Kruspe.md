# Richard Kruspe

**Guitarist, vocalist. Born 1967-06-24 in Wittenberge, East Germany.**

Richard Kruspe (born Sven Kruspe) is a German musician best known as a co-founder and lead guitarist of Rammstein, the Neue Deutsche Härte band he helped form in 1994 after fleeing East Germany. Before Rammstein, he founded the industrial/experimental project Orgasm Death Gimmick in 1991, which released three demo tapes before disbanding in 1993. He also fronts his own solo project, Emigrate, through which he has released four studio albums since 2007.
