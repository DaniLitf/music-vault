# Rat Skates

**Drummer. Born 1962-04-01 in New Jersey, USA.**

Rat Skates (Rob Cangelosi) was a co-founding drummer of Overkill, playing on their first two studio albums *Feel the Fire* (1985) and *Taking Over* (1987). He was one of the original architects of the band's thrash metal sound before departing in 1987. He later directed a documentary about the New Jersey metal scene.
