**Keyboardist, vocalist, and songwriter. Born 1966.**

Roger Manning Jr. is an American keyboardist, singer, and songwriter who co-founded the band Jellyfish in 1989 with Andy Sturmer. He was a primary composer for the band's sophisticated pop-rock arrangements and played a key role in crafting their intricate harmonies and baroque pop sound. Beyond Jellyfish, Manning has worked extensively as a producer and composer, most notably with Beck, and maintains an active solo career.
