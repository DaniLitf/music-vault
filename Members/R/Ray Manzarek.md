# Ray Manzarek

**Ray Manzarek was an American musician and co-founder of The Doors who played keyboards and covered the bass parts using his left hand on a keyboard bass, giving the band its unique sound without a conventional bassist.**

A classically trained pianist, Manzarek was also the band's intellectual and visual anchor. He died of bile duct cancer on May 20, 2013.
