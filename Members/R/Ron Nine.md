# Ron Nine

**Vocalist. American.**

Ron Nine (Ronald Rudzitis) is an American vocalist, formerly of the experimental rock outfit Room Nine, who formed psychedelic grunge band Love Battery in Seattle in 1989. As the band's primary songwriter and lyricist, alongside guitarist Kevin Whitworth he has been one of its only two consistent members throughout its history. He also plays in Down With People and the band Vaporland alongside Whitworth, Tad's Kurt Danielson, and drummer Garrett Shavlik.
