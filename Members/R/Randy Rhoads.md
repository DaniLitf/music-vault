# Randy Rhoads

**Guitarist. American.**

Randy Rhoads was an American guitarist celebrated for his virtuoso classical-influenced playing style. He first gained recognition with Quiet Riot before becoming Ozzy Osbourne's guitarist, crafting iconic albums such as Blizzard of Ozz and Diary of a Madman. Rhoads tragically died in a plane crash on March 19, 1982, at the age of 25.
