# Randy Rhoads

**Guitar. Born 6 December 1956 in El Dorado, Arkansas; died 19 March 1982 in Leesburg, Florida.**

Randy Rhoads was an American guitarist best known for his virtuosic guitar work with [[Artists/O/Ozzy Osbourne]] and founding member of the band Quiet Riot. Rhoads' technical skill and innovative neo-classical approach to rock guitar made him one of the most influential guitarists of the 1980s despite his brief career, which was cut short by his death in a plane crash at age 25.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/O/Ozzy Osbourne]] – lead guitar (1979–1982)
- [[Artists/Q/Quiet Riot]] – lead guitar (1978–1979, 1982)

## Biography

Rhoads began his professional career as a member of the Los Angeles punk rock band Quiet Riot before joining Ozzy Osbourne's solo band in 1979. His influential work on Osbourne's first two solo albums, *Blizzard of Ozz* (1980) and *Diary of a Madman* (1981), established him as a major force in heavy metal music. His technical proficiency, speed, and innovative harmonic approach set new standards for rock and metal guitar playing.
