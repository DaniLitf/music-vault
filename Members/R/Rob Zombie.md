# Rob Zombie

**Vocalist, filmmaker. Born 1965-01-12 in Haverhill, Massachusetts, USA.**

Rob Zombie is the co-founder and frontman of White Zombie and a solo artist who has maintained a parallel career as a horror filmmaker. White Zombie dissolved in 1998 and he launched his solo career the same year with *Hellbilly Deluxe*, which went platinum. His aesthetic — heavy metal fused with vintage horror cinema — has remained consistent across both his music and his films (*House of 1000 Corpses*, *The Devil's Rejects*, *Halloween*).
