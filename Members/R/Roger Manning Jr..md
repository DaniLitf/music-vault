# Roger Manning Jr.

**Keyboardist, songwriter. Born 27 May 1966.**

Roger Joseph Manning Jr. is an American keyboardist and songwriter who co-founded the power pop band Jellyfish and the electronic duo Moog Cookbook, and has spent years as a member of [[Artists/B/Beck]]'s touring band. He also toured and recorded with [[Artists/A/Air]] on their *Moon Safari* and *10 000 Hz Legend* touring bands, contributing vintage synthesizer parts to the group's live sound.
