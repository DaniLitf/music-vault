# Robert Smith

**Robert Smith is a British musician and the founding vocalist, guitarist, and sole constant member of The Cure since 1978, one of the most iconic figures in post-punk and gothic rock.**

Smith has been The Cure's creative anchor for over four decades, writing virtually all of the band's material. During the early 1980s he also served as a touring and recording guitarist for Siouxsie and the Banshees, appearing on their albums *A Kiss in the Dreamhouse* (1982) and *Hyæna* (1984), and briefly contributed to The Creatures, Siouxsie Sioux's side project.
