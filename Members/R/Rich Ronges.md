# Rich Ronges

**Bassist. American.**

Rich Ronges was the bassist for the Cleveland heavy metal band Centurion, active from the band's formation in 1984 until its breakup in 1989. He played on the album *Cross and Black* (1987) and was also a member of Severe Warning and Rogue. He passed away in 2016.
