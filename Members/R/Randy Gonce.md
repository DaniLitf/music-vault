# Randy Gonce

**Guitarist. Born March 3, 1960.**

Randy Gonce is an American guitarist who played in the Cleveland thrash/power metal band Purgatory from 1985 to 1986, performing on the band's full-length debut *Tied to the Trax* (1986). He has also played in Blessed Realm, Infernal Opera, World Lost, and several other Cleveland-area metal bands.
