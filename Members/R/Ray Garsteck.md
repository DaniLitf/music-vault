**Vocalist. American.**

Ray Garsteck is an American vocalist who was the original frontman of the Cleveland heavy metal band Sacred Few, singing on their 1982 debut single "Sacred Few" b/w "Low Rider." His high-pitched vocal style was notably different from his replacement Sandy Rago's lower register. He left the band in 1983 before their album *Beyond the Iron Walls*.
