# Ron Matthews

**Drums (1975–1977).**

Ron "Rebel" Matthews is an English drummer best known as the original drummer of [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] – drums (1975–1977)

## Biography

Matthews was Iron Maiden's first drummer, playing on the band's earliest demos and live performances in the mid-1970s. He left in 1977 and was replaced by various drummers before the band stabilised.
