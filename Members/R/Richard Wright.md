# Richard Wright

**Keyboardist, vocalist. Born 1943-07-28 in London, England. Died 2008-09-15.**

Richard Wright was a co-founding member of Pink Floyd and the primary keyboard player throughout the band's classic period. His atmospheric organ and synthesizer work is integral to the sound of *The Dark Side of the Moon* (1973), *Wish You Were Here* (1975), and *The Wall* (1979). He was controversially fired by Roger Waters during *The Wall* sessions but continued as a paid session musician. He returned as a full member for *A Momentary Lapse of Reason* (1987) and *The Division Bell* (1994), and died of cancer in September 2008.
