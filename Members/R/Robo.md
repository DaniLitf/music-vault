# Robo

**Drums. Born 1955.**

Robo (Julio Roberto Valverde Valencia) is a Colombian-American drummer who joined [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] in 1978, developing a distinctive stiff, robotic playing style, and appeared on the *Jealous Again* and *Six Pack* EPs plus the *Damaged* album before visa problems prevented his return to the US after a 1981 UK tour. He relocated to New Jersey to join the Misfits full-time, recording *Earth A.D./Wolfs Blood* (1983), and later rejoined Black Flag for its 2003 reunion shows.
