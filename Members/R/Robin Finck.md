# Robin Finck

**Guitarist, multi-instrumentalist. Born 7 November 1971 in Marlborough, Massachusetts, USA.**

Robin Finck is an American guitarist best known for his work with [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] and [[Artists/G/Guns N' Roses]]. He first joined Nine Inch Nails as a touring guitarist for *The Downward Spiral* tour in 1994 and has remained a recurring member of the live band across multiple eras. He was a full member of Guns N' Roses from 2000 to 2008, performing on the *Chinese Democracy* album and tour, and rejoined NIN in 2013.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] – lead guitar (1994–2000, 2008–2009, 2013–present)
- [[Artists/G/Guns N' Roses]] – lead guitar (2000–2008)

## Biography

Finck began his professional career as a dancer before switching to guitar. He joined Nine Inch Nails in 1994 after sending a demo tape and was hired as the band's lead guitarist for the *Self-Destruct* tour. Known for his theatrical stage presence, he became a fan favorite during NIN's late-1990s tours. In 2000, he was recruited by Axl Rose to join Guns N' Roses, performing on the long-delayed *Chinese Democracy* (2008) and touring extensively.
