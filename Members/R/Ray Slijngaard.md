**Rapper and MC. Born 8 October 1971.**

Ray Slijngaard is a Dutch rapper and MC best known as the male vocalist and rapper of the Eurodance project [[Artists/0-9/2 Unlimited]], alongside vocalist [[Members/A/Anita Doth]]. He performed with the group from its formation in 1991 until his departure in 1996, providing the iconic rap verses on hits such as "No Limit" and "Get Ready for This". After leaving 2 Unlimited, he briefly formed the duo Ray & Anita with Doth before pursuing solo work and later returning to 2 Unlimited for reunion performances.
