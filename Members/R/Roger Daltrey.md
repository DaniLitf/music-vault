# Roger Daltrey

**Roger Daltrey is a British musician and co-founder of The Who, the band's original leader whose powerful, operatic voice became the instrument for Pete Townshend's ambitious songwriting.**

Daltrey co-founded the band in 1964 and remains a core member alongside Townshend. He has also pursued an acting career and solo recordings.
