# Ryan Farrell

**Multi-instrumentalist (bass, keyboards, composer). Born 1982.**

Ryan "Dr. F." Farrell is an American composer, multi-instrumentalist, and visual artist from Cleveland, Ohio who began playing music at age five. He formally studied music composition and piano at Cleveland State University (2002–2019, returning 2019–2022 for graduate work), where he ultimately earned a Master of Music in Composition in 2022. He officially joined Mushroomhead as bassist in 2012 (replacing Pig Benis), and has co-authored and recorded two Billboard top 20 albums with the band while pursuing his classical and compositional interests.
