# Ryan Farrell

**Bassist, keyboardist, and composer from Cleveland, Ohio. Born 1982.**

Ryan "Dr. F." Farrell is an American composer, multi-instrumentalist, and visual artist from Cleveland, Ohio who began playing guitar at age five and piano at fourteen. He started his own rock band at age eleven and performed throughout Northeast Ohio by age thirteen. He formally studied music composition and piano at Cleveland State University (2002–2007, returning 2019–2022 for graduate work), where he earned a Master of Music in Composition in 2022. In 2007, he joined Mushroomhead as a bassist and keyboardist, officially becoming a member in 2012, and has co-authored and recorded two Billboard top 20 albums with the band. He also founded and leads Antietam Creek, a hard rock/heavy metal project, while continuing to pursue classical composition and visual arts.
