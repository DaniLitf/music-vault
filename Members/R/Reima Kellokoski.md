# Reima Kellokoski

**Drummer. Finnish.**

Reima Kellokoski joined Impaled Nazarene as their drummer in 1995 and has remained a consistent rhythmic force in the band's lineup. His powerful drumming style has been integral to the band's extreme metal sound throughout their later career.
