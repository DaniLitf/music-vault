# Renato Neto

**Keyboardist. Born in Brazil.**

Renato Neto is a Brazilian keyboardist who played with Prince's New Power Generation during the 2000s. He performed on Prince's *3121* and *Planet Earth* albums and toured extensively with the band. He also played with Sheila E. and Larry Graham.
