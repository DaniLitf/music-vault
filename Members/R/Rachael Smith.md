# Rachael Smith

**Drummer. British.**

Rachael Smith is a drummer who co-founded [[Artists/T/The Featherz]] after leaving Poussez Posse with Danie Cox and Molly Spiers-MacLeod. She played in the early Feathers/Featherz lineup before Alex Gold replaced her.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/P/Poussez Posse]] – drums (2011)
