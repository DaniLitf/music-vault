# Rick Kiehl

**Drummer. American.**

Rick Kiehl is an American drummer who was the original drummer for the Cleveland band Abduction, and later joined Firedance (the precursor to Rotterdam) in 1985. He was part of the original lineup alongside Jim Hamar and Bob Allerton when the band was still called Firedance.
