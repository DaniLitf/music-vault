# Rémi Serafino

**Drums (2024–present).**

Rémi Serafino is a French drummer best known as the drummer for the experimental project [[Artists/I/Igorrr]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Igorrr]] – drums (2024–present)

## Biography

Serafino joined Igorrr in 2024, replacing longtime drummer Sylvain Bouvier. He had previously performed as a touring drummer for the band in 2023 before becoming a full member.
