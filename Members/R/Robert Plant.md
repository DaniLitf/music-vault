# Robert Plant

**British singer and songwriter. Born 20 August 1948.**

Robert Anthony Plant is a British singer and songwriter best known as the lead vocalist of Led Zeppelin (1968–1980). He is considered one of the greatest rock vocalists of all time, known for his powerful, high-pitched voice and charismatic stage presence. After Led Zeppelin's dissolution following John Bonham's death, Plant launched a successful solo career.
