# Rodrigo Pencheff

**Rapper. Swedish.**

Rodrigo Pencheff, known as Rodde, was an early member of [[Artists/T/The Latin Kings]]. He left after the group's first album to focus more on [[Artists/I/Infinite Mass]], but returned for the Spanish-language *Bienvenido a mi barrio* project.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Latin Kings]] – vocals/rap (early lineup; guest/return on *Bienvenido a mi barrio*)
- [[Artists/I/Infinite Mass]] – member
