# Roger Lewis

**Guitarist, vocalist. Born 1951-06-29.**

Roger Lewis is a Jamaican guitarist who co-founded reggae band Inner Circle in Kingston in 1968 with his brother Ian, remaining one of its two constant members throughout its history, including the band's 1993 Grammy-winning album *Bad Boys*.
