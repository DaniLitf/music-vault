# Robert Calvert

**Vocalist, poet. British.**

Robert Calvert was a South African-born British poet and vocalist who fronted [[Artists/H/Hawkwind]] intermittently through the 1970s, writing much of the spoken-word and lyrical content for *Space Ritual* (1973) and *Quark, Strangeness and Charm* (1977). He died in 1988.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/H/Hawkwind]] – vocals, lyrics (1972–1973, 1975–1978)
