# Ronan Harris

**Irish electronic musician, founder and sole permanent member of VNV Nation. Born 14 June 1967.**

Ronan Harris was born in Dublin, Ireland, and moved to London in 1988. He founded VNV Nation in 1990, writing, producing, and performing all studio recordings. Alongside Stephan Groth of Apoptygma Berzerk, he is credited with coining the term "futurepop". Harris also founded Modcom (instrumental electronic project), co-founded Dependent Records, and provided vocals for the Bruderschaft supergroup project. He produced the Angels and Agony album *Eternity* and contributed to AFI's *Decemberunderground*.
