**Lead vocalist. Born 8 September 1968.**

Ray Wilson is a Scottish singer and guitarist who achieved mainstream success in 1994 as the lead vocalist of the post-grunge band Stiltskin, whose debut single "Inside" topped charts across Europe following its use in a Levi's commercial. He joined Genesis as lead vocalist in 1996 following Phil Collins's departure, being officially announced in June 1997. Wilson performed on Genesis's final studio album "Calling All Stations" (1997), which became a top 10 worldwide hit. Following his tenure with Genesis, Wilson pursued a solo career, releasing multiple solo albums and continuing to perform live, frequently incorporating Genesis songs into his setlists.
