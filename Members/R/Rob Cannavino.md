# Rob Cannavino

**Guitarist.**

Rob Cannavino served as a guitarist in Overkill from 1990 to 1993, appearing on *Horrorscope* (1991) and *I Hear Black* (1993). He was part of the transitional lineup that followed founding guitarist Bobby Gustafson's departure.
