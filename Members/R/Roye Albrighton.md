# Roye Albrighton

**Roye Albrighton was a British guitarist and vocalist, co-founder and lead guitarist of the progressive rock band Nektar, born on August 23, 1947 and died on June 8, 2016.**

Albrighton was the primary songwriter and guitarist of Nektar, providing the melodic, blues-influenced guitar work that defined the band's sound from their 1969 formation through the group's various reunion phases. He was diagnosed with multiple sclerosis in the 1990s but continued to perform. He died of a heart attack in 2016.
