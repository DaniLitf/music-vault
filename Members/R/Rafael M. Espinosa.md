# Rafael M. Espinosa

**Spanish electronic musician, producer, and founder of Geistform.**

Rafael M. Espinosa has collaborated with Dirk Ivens on Dive albums, producing and co-writing tracks on *Behind the Sun* (2004) and *Underneath* (2017). He is also known for his own project Geistform.
