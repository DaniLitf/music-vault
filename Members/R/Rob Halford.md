# Rob Halford

**Vocalist. Born August 25, 1951.**

Rob Halford is a British vocalist best known as the lead singer of Judas Priest, a role he held from 1973 to 1992 and again from 2003 to the present. Known as "The Metal God," he has also fronted several other projects including Fight, Halford, and 2wo, and is widely regarded as one of the most influential vocalists in heavy metal history.
