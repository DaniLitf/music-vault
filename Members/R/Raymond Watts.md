# Raymond Watts

**Vocalist and producer. Born 1963 in England.**

Raymond Watts is a British musician and producer who founded the industrial project Pig in 1988. He has been a longtime collaborator with KMFDM, contributing vocals and songwriting to numerous albums throughout the 1980s and 1990s.
