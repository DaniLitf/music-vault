# Rick Richards

**Lead guitar, slide guitar, backing vocals.**

Rick Richards is an American guitarist best known as the lead guitarist of [[Artists/I/Izzy Stradlin and the Ju Ju Hounds]] and a former member of the Georgia Satellites.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/G/Georgia Satellites]] – lead guitar (1980–1990)
- [[Artists/I/Izzy Stradlin and the Ju Ju Hounds]] – lead guitar (1992–1994)

## Biography

Richards was a founding member of the Georgia Satellites, known for their hit "Keep Your Hands to Yourself". After the Satellites disbanded, he joined Izzy Stradlin's Ju Ju Hounds for their self-titled album and subsequent tour.
