# Ryan McKenney

**Vocalist. American.**

Ryan McKenney is an American vocalist who co-founded Trap Them in 2001 with guitarist Brian Izzi as a side-project while both were still active in Backstabbers Incorporated. He remained Trap Them's vocalist for the band's entire existence, through their final album *Crown Feral* (2016) and breakup in 2017.
