# Rob Bourdon

**Drummer. Born January 20, 1979.**

Rob Bourdon is an American drummer best known as the drummer and co-founder of Linkin Park. He co-founded the band in 1996 with Mike Shinoda and Brad Delson while they were classmates at Agoura High School. Bourdon's drumming has appeared on all of Linkin Park's studio albums.
