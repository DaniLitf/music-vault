# Robbin Crosby

**Guitarist. Born August 4, 1959. Died June 6, 2002.**

Robbin Crosby was an American guitarist best known as a co-founder and lead guitarist of the glam metal band Ratt. Alongside Warren DeMartini, he formed one of the most notable twin-guitar duos of the 1980s hair metal scene. He co-wrote many of Ratt's biggest hits including "Round and Round" and "Wanted Man." Crosby struggled with substance abuse and left Ratt in 1991 before dying of a heroin overdose in 2002.
