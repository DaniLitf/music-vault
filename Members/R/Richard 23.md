# Richard 23

**Vocalist. Born 1963-01-23 in Belgium.**

Richard 23 is the vocalist of Front 242, the Belgian electronic body music (EBM) group, and was also a vocalist and early member of Revolting Cocks during their formation period. His abrasive, percussive vocal style was central to Front 242's sound and contributed to the raw energy of early RevCo.
