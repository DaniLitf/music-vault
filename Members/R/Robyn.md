# Robyn

**Vocalist, songwriter. Born 1979-06-12 in Stockholm, Sweden.**

Robyn is a Swedish pop artist who broke through as a teenager in the mid-1990s before leaving her major label to found her own Konichiwa Records and release music on her own terms. Her self-titled 2005 album (international version 2008) and the *Body Talk* trilogy (2010) are widely regarded as defining pop albums of their era, blending dance-floor energy with emotional honesty.
