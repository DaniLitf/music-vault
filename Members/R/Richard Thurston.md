# Richard Thurston

**Guitarist and drummer.**

Richard Thurston is an American musician from the South Florida hardcore scene. A former guitarist of Timescape Zero and Ego Trip, he founded the vegan straight edge metalcore band [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]] in December 1993 with drummer Joshua Williams and vocalist Mark Mitchell, playing guitar until his departure in 1997. He later filled in on drums for the injured Timothy Kirkpatrick at [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]]'s Furnace Fest appearance in September 2022, appearing on no studio releases by that band.
