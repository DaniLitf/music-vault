# Rune Eriksen

**Guitarist. Norwegian.**

Rune Eriksen, known as Blasphemer, is a Norwegian guitarist best known for his tenure in Mayhem, which he joined in the mid-1990s. He joined black/thrash band Aura Noir as guitarist in 1996, contributing to their debut album *Black Thrash Attack* the same year, and remains a member.
