# Rob Kurth

**Drummer, vocalist. American.**

Rob Kurth is the founding drummer of the Southern California punk rock band [[Artists/F/Face to Face]], formed in 1991. Kurth was stationed at George Air Force Base outside Victorville when he joined Trever Keith and Matt Riddle to form the band. He drummed for Face to Face until 1998, when he departed and was replaced by Pete Parada.
