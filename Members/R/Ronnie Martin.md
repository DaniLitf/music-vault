# Ronnie Martin

**Synthesizer player, vocalist, and songwriter. American.**

Ronnie Martin is an American musician best known as the creative force behind Joy Electric, a synthpop project he founded in 1994. He began his musical career with his brother Jason Martin in Dance House Children before launching Joy Electric, releasing over a dozen albums on Tooth & Nail Records. Martin is known for his purist approach to analog synthesizers and his distinct fairy-tale lyrical style. He disbanded Joy Electric in 2012 to enter seminary, and has since released music under his own name.
