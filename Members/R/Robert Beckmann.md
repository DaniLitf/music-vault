# Robert Beckmann

**Vocalist, violinist. German.**

Robert Beckmann performed as B. Breuler in [[Artists/T/The Inchtabokatables]], singing and playing violin in the band's no-guitar string-rock lineup. After the band went on hiatus, he worked with [[Artists/I/In Extremo]] and later played in Grüßaugust with Titus Jany.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Inchtabokatables]] – vocals, violin as B. Breuler (1991–2002)
- [[Artists/I/In Extremo]] – collaborator
