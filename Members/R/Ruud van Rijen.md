# Ruud van Rijen

**Record producer and musician. Dutch.**

Ruud van Rijen is a Dutch record producer and the founder of the Eurodance project Twenty 4 Seven, formed in 1989 in Valkenswaard, Netherlands. He has produced all of the group's studio albums and singles, and continues to lead the project through multiple lineup changes.
