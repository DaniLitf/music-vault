# Rosie Gaines

**Keyboardist, vocalist. Born 26 June 1960 in San Francisco, California.**

Rosie Gaines is an American singer and keyboardist who was a key member of the original New Power Generation. She provided lead and backing vocals on *Diamonds and Pearls* and co-wrote "Money Don't Matter 2 Night." She left the band after the Diamonds and Pearls Tour to pursue a solo career.
