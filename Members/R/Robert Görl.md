# Robert Görl

**Drummer, electronic musician. Born September 8, 1954 in Düsseldorf, West Germany.**

Robert Görl is a German drummer and electronic musician best known as the co-founder and instrumental half of DAF alongside Gabi Delgado-López. Classically and jazz-trained, his minimalist drumming and sequencer-driven synthesizer work defined DAF's sound. After DAF's initial split he released solo albums including *Night Full of Tension* (1984) and became a respected techno artist.
