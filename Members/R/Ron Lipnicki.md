# Ron Lipnicki

**Drummer.**

Ron Lipnicki served as Overkill's drummer from 2005 to 2018, appearing on *Immortalis* (2007), *Ironbound* (2010), *The Electric Age* (2012), *White Devil Armory* (2014), and *The Grinding Wheel* (2017). He was replaced by Jason Bittner in 2018.
