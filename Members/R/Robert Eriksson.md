# Robert Eriksson

**Drums. Swedish. Born 1972-03-07.**

Robert "Robban" Eriksson is a Swedish drummer, a founding member of [[Artists/T/The Hellacopters]] who has played drums for the band since its formation in 1994 through its original run and 2016 reunion. He began playing drums in 1986 and has also led his own band, Tramp, and reunited with Swedish punk band Strindbergs.
