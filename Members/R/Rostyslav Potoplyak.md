# Rostyslav Potoplyak

**Drummer. Born in Ukraine.**

Rostyslav Potoplyak, known on stage as Rusty Potoplakht, was the long-time drummer for 1914 from 2016 to 2024. He performed on the band's second and third albums, *The Blind Leading the Blind* (2018) and *Where Fear and Weapons Meet* (2021), before being replaced by Igor Kovalenko.
