# Ralph Santolla

**Lead guitarist. Born December 1, 1964 — died June 7, 2018.**

Ralph Santolla was an American death metal guitarist known for his technical leads with Obituary, Deicide, and Death. He played lead guitar for Obituary from 1995 to 1997 and again from 2007 to 2010, appearing on *Back from the Dead* (1997), *Xecutioner's Return* (2007), and *Darkest Day* (2009). He toured with Death in 1993 and played on Deicide's *The Stench of Redemption* (2006) and *Till Death Do Us Part* (2008).
