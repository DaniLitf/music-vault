# Richard Hell

**Vocals, bass. American. Born 1949-10-02.**

Richard Hell is an American punk musician and writer, known as a co-founder of [[Artists/T/Television]] and frontman of Richard Hell and the Voidoids. He provided lyrics and lead vocals for "Never Mind" on [[Artists/T/The Heads]]' *No Talking, Just Head* (1996).

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/Television]] – bass, vocals, co-founder
- [[Artists/T/The Heads]] – guest vocals (1996)
