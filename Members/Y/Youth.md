# Youth

**Bassist, producer. Born 27 December 1960 in Africa.**

Youth (born Martin Glover) is the bassist and a founding member of Killing Joke. He has also worked extensively as a producer (The Verve's *Urban Hymns*) and as a member of The Fireman with Paul McCartney. He contributed to Pigface as a guest.
