# Young Buck

**Rapper. Born 1981-03-15.**

Young Buck is an American rapper from Nashville, Tennessee, who joined G-Unit in 2003 as a replacement during Tony Yayo's incarceration and became an official member. His solo debut *Straight Outta Cashville* (2004) went platinum, but he was ousted from the group in April 2008 over financial disputes and "inconsistent behavior," rejoining for the 2014 reunion before departing again in 2018.
