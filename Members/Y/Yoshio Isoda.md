# Yoshio Isoda

**Drummer. Japanese.**

Yoshio Isoda is a Japanese drummer from Sapporo, Hokkaido, best known for his long tenure with Saber Tiger (1993–1996, 1997–2004). He has also played in Captured (2006–2007), Sixride, Double Dealer, and Hard Gear.
