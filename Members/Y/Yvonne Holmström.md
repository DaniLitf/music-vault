# Yvonne Holmström

**Backing vocalist. Finnish (Swedish-speaking).**

Yvonne Holmström is a Swedish-speaking Finnish singer who provided backing vocals for Swedish pop band Trance Dance alongside her sister Susanne, a member from the band's 1985 formation until their 1990 breakup.
