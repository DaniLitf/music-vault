# Yosef Wolde-Mariam

**Producer, programmer. Belgian.**

Yosef Wolde-Mariam, also known as Yosev, was a founding member of the Belgian hip house project Hi Tek 3. He co-produced the group's album *The Easiest Way* and worked with Technotronic affiliate Jo Bogaert.
