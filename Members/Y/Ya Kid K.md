**Vocalist and rapper. Born 27 January 1972.**

Ya Kid K (born Manuela Kamosi in Kinshasa, Zaire, now Democratic Republic of the Congo) is a Congolese-Belgian vocalist and rapper best known as the lead singer of the Eurodance project [[Artists/T/Technotronic|Technotronic]]. Her powerful vocal delivery on "Pump Up the Jam" (1989) launched the group to international fame, though she was initially uncredited in favour of fashion model Felly who lip-synced in the video.

She moved to Belgium as a child and was a member of the Belgian rap group Fresh Beat Productions before joining Technotronic. After her initial departure from the group in 1990, she returned for the "Move This" single in 1992 and continued collaborating with Jo Bogaert on subsequent albums.
