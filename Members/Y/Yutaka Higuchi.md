# Yutaka Higuchi

**Yutaka Higuchi is a Japanese bassist and co-founder of Buck-Tick, having played bass in the band since their formation in 1983.**

He is the younger brother of drummer Atsushi Higuchi (Yagami Toll) and the two siblings have formed Buck-Tick's rhythm section throughout the band's entire history.
