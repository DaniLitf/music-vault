# Izzy Stradlin

**Lead vocals, rhythm guitar, harmonica.**

Izzy Stradlin is an American musician and songwriter best known as the rhythm guitarist and co-founder of [[Artists/G/Guns N' Roses]] and frontman of [[Artists/I/Izzy Stradlin and the Ju Ju Hounds]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Izzy Stradlin and the Ju Ju Hounds]] – vocals, guitar (1992–1994)
- [[Artists/G/Guns N' Roses]] – rhythm guitar, backing vocals (1985–1991)
- [[Artists/V/Velvet Revolver]] – songwriter (early formation, declined to join)
- Solo career – 11 studio albums (1998–present)

## Biography

Stradlin co-founded Guns N' Roses in 1985 and co-wrote many of the band's biggest hits including "Sweet Child o' Mine", "Paradise City", and "Patience". He left the band in 1991 and formed Izzy Stradlin and the Ju Ju Hounds the following year. He continues to release solo albums independently.
