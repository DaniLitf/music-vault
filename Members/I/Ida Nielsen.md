# Ida Nielsen

**Bassist, vocalist. Born 12 May 1975 in Aarhus, Denmark.**

Ida Nielsen is a Danish bassist who joined Prince's New Power Generation in 2010. She played on *20Ten* and remained with Prince until his death in 2016, later forming the band 3rdeyegirl with Hannah Ford Welton and Donna Grantis. She has released solo albums and tours as a tribute to Prince.
