# Inge Johansson

**Bassist. Born 1977-05-10.**

Inge Johansson is a Swedish bassist, a founding member of [[Artists/T/The (International) Noise Conspiracy|The (International) Noise Conspiracy]] and formerly one of the bass players in [[Artists/R/Refused|Refused]]. He joined [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]] in August 2013, replacing Andrew Seward, and remained the band's bassist until his final show with the group in May 2018.
