# Ian Hill

**Bassist. Born January 20, 1951.**

Ian Hill is a British bassist and founding member of Judas Priest, having been with the band since its inception in 1969. He is the only member to appear on every Judas Priest album and is the brother-in-law of lead vocalist Rob Halford, having married his sister Sue.
