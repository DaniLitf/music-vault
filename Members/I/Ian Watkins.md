**Lead vocalist. Born 30 July 1977.**

Ian Watkins was the founder and lead singer of Lostprophets, fronting the Welsh rock band from 1997 until 2012. He was instrumental in developing the band's sound from early nu-metal influences toward post-hardcore and alternative rock. Watkins was sentenced to 29 years in prison in December 2013 for child sexual offences and was killed in prison on 11 October 2025.
