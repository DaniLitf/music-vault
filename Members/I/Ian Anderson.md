# Ian Anderson

**Ian Anderson (born August 10, 1947, in Dunfermline, Scotland) is a British vocalist, flautist, guitarist, and songwriter, the founder and only constant member of Jethro Tull.**

Anderson is best known for his virtuosic flute playing, which became Jethro Tull's signature sound, as well as his theatrical stage presence. He wrote the majority of Jethro Tull's material, including the landmark albums *Aqualung* (1971) and *Thick as a Brick* (1972). He has also released solo albums and continues to tour and record with Jethro Tull.
