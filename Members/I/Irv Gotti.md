# Irv Gotti

**Record producer, music executive, label founder. American. (1970–2025)**

Irving "Irv Gotti" Lorenzo Jr. was a legendary hip-hop producer and record executive who founded Murder Inc. Records in 1999, becoming one of the most influential figures in East Coast hip-hop during the early 2000s. Working initially at Def Jam, he helped develop Ja Rule, DMX, and Jay-Z before launching his own imprint, which signed Ashanti, Charli Baltimore, Lloyd, and Vanessa Carlton. Murder Inc. Records sold over 30 million units worldwide and grossed over $500 million under his leadership.
