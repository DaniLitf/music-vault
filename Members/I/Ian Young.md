# Ian Young

**Saxophonist.**

Ian Young is an American saxophonist who performed as part of [[Artists/M/M83]]'s live band in the 2010s, most visibly on the *Hurry Up, We're Dreaming* world tour where his saxophone solos on "Midnight City" became a live highlight. He is listed among the band's former live members.
