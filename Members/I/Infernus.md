# Infernus

**Infernus (born Roger Tiegs) is a Norwegian musician, best known as the founder and guitarist of black metal band Gorgoroth, who also briefly played bass for Borknagar in 1995–1996.**

He is one of the most prominent figures in Norwegian black metal and has led Gorgoroth through various lineup controversies.
