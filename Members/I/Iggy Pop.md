# Iggy Pop

**Iggy Pop (James Newell Osterberg Jr.) is an American rock singer from Muskegon, Michigan, known as the "Godfather of Punk" — the frontman of The Stooges and a pioneering solo artist whose stage performances, including self-mutilation and crowd surfing, became legendary.**
