# Ian Hunter

**Vocalist, pianist, guitarist. British.**

Ian Hunter was the frontman, pianist, and primary songwriter of [[Artists/M/Mott the Hoople]] from 1969 until the band's split in 1974, and has maintained a long solo career since, including the album *Ian Hunter* (1975) and continued work with his backing band the Rant Band.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/M/Mott the Hoople]] – vocals, piano, guitar (1969–1974)
- [[Artists/I/Ian Hunter]] – solo artist (1975–present)
