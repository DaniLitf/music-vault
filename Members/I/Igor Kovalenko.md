# Igor Kovalenko

**Drummer. Born in Ukraine.**

Igor Kovalenko, known on stage as Reich, is the drummer for 1914, joining the band in 2024. He replaced longtime drummer Rostyslav Potoplyak and appears on the fourth studio album *Viribus Unitis* (2025).
