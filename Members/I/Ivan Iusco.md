# Ivan Iusco

**Italian composer, producer, and label founder. Born in Italy.**

Ivan Iusco is an Italian electronic musician and founder of Minus Habens Records. He has been a frequent studio collaborator with Dirk Ivens on Dive albums, co-writing and producing tracks on *Concrete Jungle*, *Snakedressed*, *True Lies*, and *Underneath*. His work spans ambient, industrial, and experimental electronic music.
