# Ilan Rubin

**Drums, keyboards, multi-instrumentalist. Born 1988-07-07.**

Ilan Rubin is an American multi-instrumentalist and producer who has drummed for the Welsh band [[Artists/L/Lostprophets|Lostprophets]] (2005–2008), [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails|Nine Inch Nails]] (2008–2025), and [[Artists/A/Angels & Airwaves|Angels & Airwaves]] (since 2011), joining [[Artists/F/Foo Fighters|Foo Fighters]] in 2025 after leaving Nine Inch Nails. He also records and performs largely solo as [[Artists/T/The New Regime|The New Regime]], playing nearly every instrument himself in the studio. His younger brother is producer and engineer [[Producers/A/Aaron Rubin|Aaron Rubin]], who has produced several of Angels & Airwaves' later records.

## Biography

Rubin began playing drums as a small child and was performing professionally by his early teens, joining Lostprophets as a teenager and appearing on their album *Liberation Transmission* (2006). He joined Nine Inch Nails as touring and studio drummer in late 2008 following Josh Freese's departure, remaining with the band until 2025. He replaced Atom Willard behind the kit in Angels & Airwaves in 2011 and has appeared on every studio album since *The Dream Walker* (2014).
