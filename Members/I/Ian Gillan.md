# Ian Gillan

**Ian Gillan is an English vocalist born August 19, 1945, best known as the lead vocalist of [[Artists/D/Deep Purple]].**

He joined Deep Purple in 1969 as part of the classic Mark II lineup and sang for [[Artists/B/Black Sabbath]] in 1983. He also fronted his own bands Gillan and the Ian Gillan Band. Known for his powerful wide-ranging vocals.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/D/Deep Purple]] – lead vocals (1969–1973, 1984–1989, 1992–present)
- [[Artists/B/Black Sabbath]] – lead vocals (1983)
