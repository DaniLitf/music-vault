# Ian Lewis

**Bassist, vocalist. Born 1953-11-01.**

Ian Lewis is a Jamaican bassist who co-founded reggae band Inner Circle in Kingston in 1968 with his brother Roger, remaining one of its two constant members throughout its history. He has run the band's Circle House studio in Miami since relocating there in the early 1980s.
