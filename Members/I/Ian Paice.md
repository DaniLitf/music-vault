# Ian Paice

**Ian Paice is an English drummer born June 29, 1948, and the last remaining original member of [[Artists/D/Deep Purple]], having been with the band since its formation in 1968.**

He is the only member to appear on every Deep Purple studio album. He has also played with [[Artists/W/Whitesnake]], Gary Moore, and Paul McCartney.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/D/Deep Purple]] – drums (1968–1976, 1984–present)
- [[Artists/W/Whitesnake]] – drums (1979–1982)
