# Ian Treacy

**Drummer. British.**

Ian Treacy was the original drummer of Benediction, playing on the band's debut album *Subconscious Terror* (1990) and *The Grand Leveller* (1991). He left the band in 1993 after the recording of *Transcend the Rubicon*.
