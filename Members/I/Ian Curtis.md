# Ian Curtis

**Ian Curtis (July 15, 1956 – May 18, 1980) was a British vocalist and lyricist, the founding singer and primary lyricist of Joy Division, known for his anguished baritone and hypnotic stage presence.**

Curtis was a founding member of Joy Division in Manchester in 1976 and became the band's magnetic frontman. His lyrics, drawing on existentialist themes, personal despair, and imagery from Dostoevsky and Kafka, defined Joy Division's bleak yet compelling aesthetic. He was diagnosed with epilepsy in 1978 and took his own life the night before Joy Division's first North American tour. The surviving members went on to form New Order.
