# Luca Morino

**Italian musician. Born 13 December 1962.**

Luca Morino is an Italian singer, composer, and record producer from Turin who founded the world music band Mau Mau in 1991 with Fabio Barovero and Tatè Nsongan, blending Piedmontese dialect lyrics with Mediterranean, African, and Latin influences.
