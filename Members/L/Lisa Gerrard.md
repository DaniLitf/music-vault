# Lisa Gerrard

**Lisa Gerrard (born April 12, 1961, in Melbourne, Australia) is an Australian singer, composer, and musician, best known as co-founder of Dead Can Dance, renowned for her glossolalic vocal technique — singing in invented languages — and for film work including the *Gladiator* (2000) soundtrack.**

Gerrard co-founded Dead Can Dance in Melbourne in 1981 with Brendan Perry. She developed a distinctive vocal style singing in invented languages alongside world music influences. Her solo career and film work with composers like Hans Zimmer established her as one of the most distinctive voices in ambient and neoclassical music.
