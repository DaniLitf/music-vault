# Leandra Ophelia Dax

**Keyboardist, live member of Apoptygma Berzerk.**

Leandra Ophelia Dax has been a live keyboardist for Apoptygma Berzerk since 2010, contributing to the band's stage performances during the *Rocket Science* and *Exit Popularity Contest* eras.
