# Lloyd

**Singer, songwriter. American. Born 1986.**

Lloyd is an R&B singer and songwriter signed to Murder Inc. Records (later The Inc.) from 2004 to 2009. He released three studio albums on the label: *Southside* (2004), *Street Love* (2007), and *Lessons in Love* (2008), establishing himself as a solo artist before departing the label in 2009 citing the need for more creative control.
