# Laurent Lunoir

**Clean and harsh vocals.**

Laurent Lunoir is a French vocalist best known as the long-time vocalist for the experimental project [[Artists/I/Igorrr]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Igorrr]] – clean and harsh vocals (2008–2021)
- [[Artists/W/Whourkr]] – vocals (grindcore/breakcore project)
- [[Öxxö Xööx]] – vocals (operatic extreme metal)

## Biography

Lunoir joined Igorrr in 2008 and performed on every studio album from *Nostril* (2010) through *Spirituality and Distortion* (2020). He left the project in 2021 due to personal choices regarding COVID-19 vaccination, which prevented international touring. He has also performed in the extreme metal band Öxxö Xööx and was a member of Whourkr alongside Gautier Serre.
