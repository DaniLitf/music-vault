**Drummer. Born 11 March 1981.**

Luke Johnson was a Welsh drummer who joined Lostprophets in 2009, replacing Ilan Rubin and serving as the band's drummer through the remainder of the group's existence until their breakup in 2013. He performed on the albums The Betrayed (2010) and Weapons (2012), completing the band's final years.
