# Lemmy

**Bass, vocals. December 24, 1945 – December 28, 2015.**

Lemmy (Ian Fraser Kilmister) was a British bassist and vocalist, best known as the founder, frontman, and primary songwriter of [[Artists/M/Motörhead]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/H/Hawkwind]] – bass, vocals (1971–1975)
- [[Artists/M/Motörhead]] – bass, vocals, founder (1975–2015)

## Biography

Kilmister began his career as a roadie for Jimi Hendrix before joining space rock band Hawkwind as bassist in 1971. He was fired in 1975 after an arrest at the Canadian border and immediately formed Motörhead, which became one of the defining bands at the intersection of heavy metal and punk. Known for his gravelly voice, distorted bass tone, and uncompromising lifestyle, Lemmy fronted Motörhead until his death four days after his 70th birthday. He was posthumously inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame with Motörhead in 2020.
