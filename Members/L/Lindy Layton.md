# Lindy Layton

**Lindy Layton is a British singer and rapper who was the featured vocalist on Beats International's 1990 UK number one single "Dub Be Good to Me."**

She contributed vocals to the debut album *Let Them Eat Bingo* and had a brief solo career in the early 1990s before stepping back from the music industry.
