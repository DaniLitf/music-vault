# Linda McCartney

**Linda McCartney was an American photographer, musician, and animal rights activist who was a member of Wings from 1971 to 1981, playing keyboards and providing backing vocals despite being a non-musician when she joined.**

She was Paul McCartney's wife from 1969 until her death from breast cancer on April 17, 1998.
