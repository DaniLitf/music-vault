# Liam Wilson

**Bass (2024–present).**

Liam Wilson is an American bassist best known for his work with [[Artists/T/The Dillinger Escape Plan]] and [[Artists/I/In Flames]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Dillinger Escape Plan]] – bass (2005–2017)
- [[Artists/I/In Flames]] – bass (2024–present)

## Biography

Wilson joined [[Artists/T/The Dillinger Escape Plan]] as bassist in 2005, remaining until the band's 2017 dissolution. He joined In Flames in 2023 as a touring bassist following the departure of Bryce Paul, becoming a full member in 2024.
