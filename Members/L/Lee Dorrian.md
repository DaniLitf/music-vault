# Lee Dorrian

**Vocalist. British.**

Lee Dorrian was the vocalist of Napalm Death from 1987 to 1989, appearing on the landmark debut *Scum*. After leaving Napalm Death, he founded Cathedral, a seminal band in the doom metal genre.
