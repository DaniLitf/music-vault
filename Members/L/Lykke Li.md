# Lykke Li

**Li Lykke Timotej Svensson, known as Lykke Li, is a Swedish singer-songwriter born on March 18, 1986 in Ystad, Sweden.**

Raised between Sweden, Portugal, Nepal, and India due to her parents' nomadic lifestyle, Lykke Li moved to Stockholm as a teenager and signed to the indie label LL Recordings. Her 2008 debut *Youth Novels*, co-produced with Björn Yttling of Peter Bjorn and John, established her internationally as a distinctive voice in indie pop. Subsequent albums grew progressively darker and more orchestral, earning her widespread critical acclaim. She has also collaborated with producers including Lenny Kravitz, Kanye West, and Greg Kurstin.
