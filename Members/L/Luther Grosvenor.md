# Luther Grosvenor

**Guitarist. British.**

Luther Grosvenor was a British guitarist, formerly of Spooky Tooth, who joined [[Artists/M/Mott the Hoople]] in 1973 as replacement for Mick Ralphs, performing under the stage name Ariel Bender until the band's 1974 split.
