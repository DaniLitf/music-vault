# Lee Perry

**Lee "Scratch" Perry (born Rainford Hugh Perry, March 20, 1936 – August 29, 2021) was a Jamaican record producer, singer, and songwriter, one of the most innovative figures in reggae and dub music.**

Perry worked at Coxsone Dodd's Studio One in the 1960s before founding his own Upsetter label and building the Black Ark studio. His pioneering production techniques — using the recording console as an instrument — shaped the entire dub genre. He produced Bob Marley and the Wailers, the Congos, and Max Romeo, among others. His Black Ark recordings are regarded as some of the most creative works in reggae history.
