# Lorde

**Lorde (born Ella Marija Lani Yelich-O'Connor, November 7, 1996, in Auckland, New Zealand) is a New Zealand singer-songwriter, who broke through at age 16 with the single "Royals" and went on to become one of the most critically acclaimed pop artists of her generation.**

Lorde signed to Universal Music Group at age 13 and released the EP *The Love Club* in 2012. "Royals" reached number one in the US and the UK and won two Grammy Awards. Her critically acclaimed second album *Melodrama* (2017) cemented her reputation as an important voice in contemporary pop.
