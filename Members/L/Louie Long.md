# Louie Long

**Drummer and vocalist.**

Louie Long is an American musician from the South Florida hardcore scene who drummed in Organized Pain and Reach before joining [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]], fronting the band as vocalist on its summer 1995 tour in support of *Born of You*. In December 1995 he co-founded [[Artists/M/Morning Again]] with fellow ex-Culture guitarist John Wylie.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]] – vocals (1995, touring)
- [[Artists/M/Morning Again]] – drums (1995–)
