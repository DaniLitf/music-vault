# Lakis Kyriacou

**Guitarist. British.**

Lakis Kyriacou is a guitarist who filled in for two Gorerotted shows in 1997 after guitarist Tim Carley broke his wrist. He was previously the driving force behind late-1990s band Area 54 and its successor Love & Bullets, and joined the gothic/industrial band Nemhain in 2008.
