# Les Binks

**Drummer. Born 1948.**

Les Binks is a British drummer who played with Judas Priest from 1977 to 1979, performing on the albums *Stained Class* and *Killing Machine*. He co-wrote the classic song "Beyond the Realms of Death" with Glenn Tipton, which remains one of the band's most beloved tracks.
