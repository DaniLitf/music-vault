# Lenny Kravitz

**Lenny Kravitz (born Leonard Albert Kravitz, May 26, 1964, in Manhattan, New York) is an American singer-songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, and record producer, known for playing nearly all instruments on his recordings and for his retro-influenced blend of rock, soul, and funk.**

Kravitz performed for years under the pseudonym Romeo Blue before signing to Virgin Records and releasing his debut *Let Love Rule* (1989). He became one of the defining artists of 1990s rock with hits like "Are You Gonna Go My Way" (1993) and "Fly Away" (1998), and has won four consecutive Grammy Awards for Best Male Rock Vocal Performance.
