**Guitarist. Born 21 May 1975.**

Lee Gaze was the co-founder and lead guitarist of Lostprophets, helping establish the band's signature sound from 1997 onwards. He was a core creative force throughout the band's 16-year existence, contributing to all five studio albums. Following Lostprophets' breakup in 2013, Gaze continued working in music as a composer and musician.
