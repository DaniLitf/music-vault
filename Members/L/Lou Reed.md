# Lou Reed

**Lead vocals, guitar. March 2, 1942 – October 27, 2013.**

Lou Reed was an American musician, singer, and songwriter, best known as the frontman and guitarist of [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]] and for his acclaimed solo career.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/The Velvet Underground]]

## Biography
Reed co-founded The Velvet Underground in 1964 with John Cale. His deadpan vocal delivery, literate lyrics, and minimalist guitar style were foundational elements of the band's sound. After leaving the band in 1970, he launched a solo career that spanned five decades, including the iconic album *Transformer* (1972) produced by David Bowie. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame with the Velvet Underground in 1996.
