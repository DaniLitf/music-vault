# Lovetta Pippen

**Vocalist. American.**

Lovetta Pippen is an American soul and gospel vocalist who became the primary vocalist for His Name Is Alive in the early 2000s, appearing on *Someday My Blues Will Cover the Earth* and *Last Night*. She was discovered after appearing as part of a gospel choir on the album *Stars on E.S.P.*
