# Levi Seacer Jr.

**Bassist, guitarist, and vocalist. Born 30 April 1961 in Richmond, California.**

Levi Seacer Jr. is an American musician who was a founding member of Prince's New Power Generation. He joined Prince's touring band in 1987 as bassist after the dissolution of The Revolution, switching to guitar when the NPG formed in 1991. He contributed to Prince's albums *Sign o' the Times*, *Lovesexy*, *Graffiti Bridge*, and *Diamonds and Pearls*, and also played on the Madhouse album *16*.
