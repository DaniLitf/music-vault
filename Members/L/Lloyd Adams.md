# Lloyd "Tin Leg" Adams

**Drummer. Died July 2024.**

Lloyd "Tin Leg" Adams was a Jamaican session drummer known for a distinctive, furiously off-beat drum style, active in the original Upsetters lineup alongside Hugh Malcolm from 1968 to 1969. He went on to play for Derrick Harriott's Now Generation and Inner Circle, before relocating to the United States in the mid-1970s, where he joined the Fabulous Titans.
