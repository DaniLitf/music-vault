# Lucy Lawbreaker

**Bassist. British.**

Lucy Lawbreaker is a bassist who joined [[Artists/T/The Featherz]] around 2017. Her documented vault-relevant role is as part of the band's later lineup during the *Five-Year-Itch* and *Ordinary Girl?* period.
