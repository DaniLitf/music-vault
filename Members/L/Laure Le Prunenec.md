# Laure Le Prunenec

**Clean / operatic vocals.**

Laure Le Prunenec is a French vocalist best known for her operatic vocals in [[Artists/I/Igorrr]] and the baroque/experimental duo [[Artists/C/Corpo-Mente]].

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/I/Igorrr]] – clean vocals (2010–2021)
- [[Artists/C/Corpo-Mente]] – vocals, duet with Gautier Serre (2015–present)
- [[Öxxö Xööx]] – guest vocals

## Biography

Le Prunenec joined Igorrr in 2010 and performed on *Hallelujah* (2012), *Savage Sinusoid* (2017), and *Spirituality and Distortion* (2020). She left the project in 2021. Together with Igorrr creator Gautier Serre, she formed the baroque/experimental duo Corpo-Mente.
