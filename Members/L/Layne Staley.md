# Layne Staley

**American singer. Born 22 August 1967, died 5 April 2002.**

Layne Thomas Staley was an American singer best known as the lead vocalist of Alice in Chains (1987–2002). He was one of the most influential vocalists of the grunge era, known for his distinctive harmonizing style with Jerry Cantrell and his dark, introspective lyrics. He also co-founded Mad Season in 1994.
