# Layne Staley

**Layne Staley was an American vocalist and co-founder of Alice in Chains, whose powerful voice and devastatingly honest songwriting about addiction gave the band a unique emotional weight among their grunge contemporaries.**

Staley's well-publicized struggles with heroin addiction eventually led to his near-withdrawal from public life after 1996. He died alone in his Seattle apartment on April 5, 2002, from a speedball overdose.
