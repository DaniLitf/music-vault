# Liam Howlett

**Liam Howlett is a British musician, DJ, and producer who founded and is the sole musical creative force behind The Prodigy, responsible for all of the band's production and songwriting.**

Howlett began as a DJ in the rave scene before forming The Prodigy in 1990. He is married to Natalie Appleton of All Saints.
