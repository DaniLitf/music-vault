# Lita Ford

**Lita Ford (born Lita Rossana Ford, September 19, 1958, in London, England) is an English-American guitarist and singer, who rose to fame as the lead guitarist of the all-female rock band the Runaways and later had a successful solo hard rock career in the 1980s.**

Ford joined the Runaways at age 16 and played on the band's most acclaimed albums. After the Runaways dissolved in 1979, she launched a solo career that peaked in the late 1980s with *Lita* (1988), which contained the hits "Kiss Me Deadly" and a duet with Ozzy Osbourne, "Close My Eyes Forever."
