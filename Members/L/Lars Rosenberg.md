# Lars Rosenberg

**Bassist. Swedish.**

Lars Rosenberg is a Swedish bassist who was a member of Entombed from 1991 to 1999, performing on *Clandestine*, *Wolverine Blues*, *DCLXVI*, and *Same Difference*.
