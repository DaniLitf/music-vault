# Lennart Eriksson

**Bass, backing vocals. Swedish. Founding member of [[Artists/E/Ebba Grön]].**

Lennart Eriksson (nicknamed Fjodor) co-founded Ebba Grön in 1977 and played bass through the band's 1983 breakup. A jail sentence for refusing military service kept him out of the band during its final months, effectively ending Ebba Grön; he did not continue into the successor band Imperiet.
