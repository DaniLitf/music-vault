# Loc

**Singer. Born ?.**

Loc (born Damien Behanan) is a singer who fronted Snap! in the mid-2000s, performing on the singles "Beauty Queen" (2005) and "Jumping!" (2008). His work with Snap! came during the group's post-hiatus period as they attempted to revive their career with new material.
