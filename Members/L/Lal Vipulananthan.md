# Lal Vipulananthan

**Musician. British.**

Lal Vipulananthan is an English musician who was part of the original 2000 lineup of brutal death metal band Infected Disarray, formed as a side project of members from the UK death metal underground. He had previously played in the band DAM before departing Infected Disarray shortly after its first live shows.
