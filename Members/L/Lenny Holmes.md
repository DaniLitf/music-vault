# Lenny Holmes

**Guitarist. American.**

Lenny Holmes was a guitarist associated with Mazarati, an American R&B and funk band from Minneapolis active from 1986 to 1989. Listed among the band's past members on Wikipedia, Holmes contributed to the group's funk-rock sound during their tenure on Paisley Park Records. Mazarati was formed and produced by former The Revolution bassist Brown Mark, and the band is best known for their 1986 hit single "100 MPH," written and co-produced by Prince.
