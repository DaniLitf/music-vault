# Lars Sköld

**Drummer. Swedish.**

Lars Sköld is a Swedish drummer, the long-time drummer of gothic metal band [[Artists/T/Tiamat]], who also joined the doom rock band [[Artists/A/Avatarium]] as a founding member in 2013 alongside fellow Tiamat-connected musicians.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/T/Tiamat]] – drums, backing vocals
- [[Artists/A/Avatarium]] – drums (2013–present)
