# Louiche Mayorga

**Bass. American. Original [[Artists/S/Suicidal Tendencies]] bassist.**

Louiche Mayorga played bass on Suicidal Tendencies' earliest releases and later in its offshoot Los Cycos. In 2006 he assembled the one-off band [[Artists/A/Against]], recruiting fellow Venice, California scene alumni to record for the compilation *Welcome 2 Venice*, a 21st-anniversary follow-up to Suicidal Tendencies bassist Robert Trujillo-era compilation *Welcome to Venice* (1985).
