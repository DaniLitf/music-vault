# Lloyd Banks

**Rapper. Born 1982-04-30.**

Lloyd Banks is an American rapper from South Jamaica, Queens, and a founding member of G-Unit alongside childhood friends 50 Cent and Tony Yayo. Known as the group's standout lyricist and "punchline king," he launched a successful solo career with *The Hunger for More* (2004), which debuted at number one on the Billboard 200. He left G-Unit and G-Unit Records in 2018.
