# Leila K

**Leila K (born Leila El Khalifi, September 6, 1971, in Stockholm, Sweden) is a Swedish singer and rapper of Moroccan descent, who became prominent in European Eurodance and hip house in the early 1990s.**

She broke through as a featured artist on Rob'n'Raz's "Got to Get" and "Rok the Nation" before launching a solo career. Her debut album *Electric* (1990) went platinum in Sweden. She largely retired from the spotlight in the mid-1990s.
