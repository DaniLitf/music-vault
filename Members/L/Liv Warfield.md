# Liv Warfield

**Vocalist. Born 29 September 1980 in Peoria, Illinois.**

Liv Warfield is an American singer who performed with Prince's New Power Generation during the 2000s. She appeared on Prince's *Lotusflow3r* and *MPLSound* albums and portrayed a singer in the film *Purple Rain* sequel. She has released solo albums including *The Enemy*.
