# London LeGrand

**Vocalist. Born July 30, 1966.**

London LeGrand is an American musician and vocalist best known as the lead singer of the hard rock supergroup Brides of Destruction. He later formed Souls of We with guitarist George Lynch and has worked on various other projects, including Rockstars on Mars.
