# Li-Ann

**Singer. Dutch.**

Li-Ann (born Lianne van Groen) is a Dutch singer who joined Twenty 4 Seven in 2010, replacing Sharon Doorson shortly after the group's revival. She has performed as the lead vocalist of the group from 2010 onward, initially alongside Stacey Seedorf.
