# Lancelot Hall

**Drummer. Born 1960-02-07.**

Lancelot Hall is a Jamaican drummer who joined reggae band Inner Circle during its 1986 reformation, playing on the band's international hit singles "Bad Boys" and "Sweat (A La La La La Long)" and remaining with the band since.
