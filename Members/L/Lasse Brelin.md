# Lasse Brelin

**Lasse Brelin is a Swedish musician who was an early member of the Stockholm electropop duo Alice in Videoland, playing bass and synthesizers from the band's founding until around 2010.**

Brelin is the brother of Martin Brelin, Alice in Videoland's primary producer. He was part of the band's original lineup before departing, leaving Martin and vocalist Lisa Lindén to continue as a duo.
