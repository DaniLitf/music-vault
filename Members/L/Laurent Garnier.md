# Laurent Garnier

**Laurent Garnier (born February 1, 1966, in Boulogne-Billancourt, France) is a French DJ and electronic musician, one of the most important figures in European techno and house music.**

Garnier worked at the French embassy in Manchester in the late 1980s and became a resident DJ at the Haçienda club, where he was introduced to British acid house and techno. Returning to France, he became a pioneer of the French techno scene. He co-founded the FCOMMUNICATIONS label and is known for landmark releases including *Unreasonable Behaviour* (2000).
