# Lol Tolhurst

**Lol Tolhurst is a British musician and founding member of The Cure, who served as the band's drummer from 1978 to 1982 and then keyboardist until 1989 when he was asked to leave.**

Tolhurst co-founded the group with Robert Smith as schoolmates in Crawley, West Sussex. He has since written a memoir about his time with the band and has reconciled with Smith, appearing at Cure performances in recent years.
