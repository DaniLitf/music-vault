# Louise Post

**Louise Post is an American guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter, born on March 12, 1969, co-founder of the Chicago alternative rock band Veruca Salt.**

Post co-founded Veruca Salt in 1992 with Nina Gordon and has been the band's constant presence through lineup changes and the 1998–2013 hiatus. She led the band as a solo venture under the Veruca Salt name after Gordon's departure, releasing *Resolver* (2000). Post and Gordon reunited the classic lineup in 2013, releasing *Ghost Notes* in 2015.
