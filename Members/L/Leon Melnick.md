# Leon Melnick

**Bass. American.**

Leon Melnick is an American musician who played bass for the metalcore band Integrity from 1989 to 1998 and returned for a brief period in 2013–2014. He is also known by the stage name "Micha" and was a long-standing member during the band's most active early years.
