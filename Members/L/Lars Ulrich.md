# Lars Ulrich

**Drums. Born December 26, 1963.**

Lars Ulrich co-founded [[Artists/M/Metallica]] with James Hetfield in Los Angeles in 1981 after placing a classified ad in a local music paper, and has remained the band's drummer and chief business strategist ever since. Born in Gentofte, Denmark, to jazz saxophonist Torben Ulrich, he moved to Los Angeles as a teenager and drew early inspiration from the NWOBHM scene he discovered on trips back to Europe.
