# Lisa Coleman

**Keyboardist and vocalist. Born in Los Angeles, California.**

Lisa Coleman is an American musician best known as the keyboardist and backing vocalist for Prince's backing band The Revolution. After leaving Prince, she formed the duo Wendy & Lisa with guitarist Wendy Melvoin, releasing multiple albums and scoring television series including *Heroes* and *Nurse Jackie*.
