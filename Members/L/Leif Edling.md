# Leif Edling

**Leif Edling is a Swedish bassist and songwriter, the founder of Candlemass and the primary composer behind their epic doom metal sound.**

He founded the band in Stockholm in 1984 and has written virtually all of their material. His slow, crushing bass riffs and lyrical themes of doom and mortality defined the sound of an entire metal subgenre.
