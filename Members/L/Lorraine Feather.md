# Lorraine Feather

**Backing vocalist. American.**

Lorraine Feather is an American backing vocalist who contributed to Grand Funk Railroad's albums *All the Girls in the World Beware!!!* and *Born to Die* in the mid-1970s.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/G/Grand Funk Railroad]] – backing vocals (1974–1975)
