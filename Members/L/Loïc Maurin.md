# Loïc Maurin

**Drummer, multi-instrumentalist.**

Loïc Maurin is a French drummer and multi-instrumentalist who has been part of [[Artists/M/M83]]'s live and studio line-up since around 2005. He was credited as part of the band in the liner notes of *Saturdays = Youth* (2008) and remains a member of the current touring group, also contributing guitar and keyboards.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/M/M83]] – drums, guitar, keyboards (2005–present)
