# L.J. Balvaz

**Guitarist. Norwegian.**

L.J. Balvaz (Lasse Johansen) played guitar for death metal band Cadaver (billed as Cadaver Inc.) from 2002 to 2004, appearing on the album *Discipline* (2001) and the band's ill-fated 2002 American tour.
