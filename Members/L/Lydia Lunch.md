# Lydia Lunch

**Singer, poet, writer. Born 2 June 1959 in Rochester, New York, USA.**

Lydia Lunch is a singer, poet, and writer who was a central figure in the No Wave scene with Teenage Jesus and the Jerks. She contributed spoken word and vocals to Pigface.
