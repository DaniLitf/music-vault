# Lisa Lindén

**Lisa Lindén is a Swedish vocalist and the frontwoman of Alice in Videoland, the Stockholm electropop duo she co-founded in 2001.**

Lindén handles lead vocals for Alice in Videoland, bringing a cool, detached delivery to the duo's synth-driven pop sound. She and Martin Brelin have kept the project active for over two decades, releasing albums across multiple labels.
