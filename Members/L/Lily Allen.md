# Lily Allen

**Lily Allen (born May 2, 1985, in Hammersmith, London) is an English singer-songwriter, who rose to fame in 2006 with her debut album *Alright, Still*, known for witty, satirical lyrics and an eclectic pop style drawing on ska, reggae, and British pop.**

Allen gained early attention via MySpace before signing with Regal Recordings. Her debut went platinum in the UK and produced the hits "Smile" and "LDN." She has released four studio albums and is also known for her public commentary on social and political issues.
