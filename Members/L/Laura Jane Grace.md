# Laura Jane Grace

**Vocalist, guitarist, songwriter. American.**

Laura Jane Grace is a singer-songwriter and guitarist who founded [[Artists/A/Against Me!]] in 1997 and has remained its frontperson ever since, writing the bulk of the band's material. In May 2012 she publicly came out as a trans woman, an experience that shaped the band's acclaimed album *Transgender Dysphoria Blues* (2014), which she also recorded and produced herself. Since Against Me!'s 2020 hiatus she has pursued a solo career, releasing albums including *Stay Alive* (2020) and *Hole in My Head* (2024), and has also recorded with the side project [[Artists/D/Devouring Mothers]].
