# Leopold Ross

**Musician, composer. Born in the United Kingdom.**

Leopold Ross is a British musician and the younger brother of Atticus Ross. He joined 12 Rounds as a member for their second album *My Big Hero* (1998) and has subsequently collaborated with his brother on several film scores and projects, including the band Error and recordings with Nine Inch Nails.
