# Lux Interior

**Vocalist. American. Born 1946, died 2009.**

Lux Interior was the lead vocalist and co-founder of the psychobilly band [[Artists/T/The Cramps]], known for his wild stage persona and distinctive, howling vocal style. He formed the band alongside guitarist Poison Ivy in 1976 and remained its frontman until his death in 2009.
