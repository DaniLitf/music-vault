# Lars Nedland

**Lars Nedland (also known as Lazare) is a Norwegian keyboardist and vocalist who joined Borknagar in 2004 and has been an important contributor to the band's progressive direction.**

He also performs with his brother Erlend Nedland in various projects and contributes clean vocals alongside his keyboard work.
