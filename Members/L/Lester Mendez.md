# Lester Mendez

**Keyboards, production. American.**

Lester Mendez is an American keyboardist, songwriter and record producer who began as a Miami session musician and later produced hits for artists including Shakira, Santana and Nelly Furtado. As a session player he contributed keyboards to the [[Artists/C/Coverdale-Page]] album (1993).
