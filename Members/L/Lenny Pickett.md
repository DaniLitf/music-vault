# Lenny Pickett

**Saxophone. American. Born 1954-04-10.**

Lenny Pickett is an American saxophonist, known for his tenure in funk band [[Artists/T/Tower of Power]] (1973–1981) and as longtime musical director of the *Saturday Night Live* band. He guested on saxophone and treated bamboo flute for "The King Is Gone" on [[Artists/T/The Heads]]' *No Talking, Just Head* (1996).
