# Lee Morris

**Drummer.**

Lee Morris served as Paradise Lost's drummer from 1994 to 2008, appearing on *Draconian Times* (1995) through *In Requiem* (2007). He presided over the band's most commercially successful period as well as their shift toward electronic and goth rock sounds in the late 1990s.
