# Lillie Mae Rische

**Lillie Mae Rische** (known mononymously as Lillie Mae) is an American singer, fiddle player, and guitarist best known for her work with [[Artists/J/Jack White]].

- [[Artists/J/Jack White]] – fiddle, mandolin, backing vocals (2012–2015)
- [[Artists/T/The Raconteurs]] – fiddle, backing vocals (2019)
