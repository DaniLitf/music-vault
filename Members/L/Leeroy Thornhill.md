# Leeroy Thornhill

**Dancer, occasional keyboardist. Born 1968-10-08.**

Leeroy Thornhill was a founding dancer of The Prodigy, known for his towering height and distinctive "shuffling" dance style, and occasionally contributed live keyboards, though he made no musical contributions to the band's studio albums. He left the group in 2000 to pursue solo electronic music projects under the names Longman and Flightcrank, and later formed the group Smash Hi-Fi and the record label Electric Tastebuds.
