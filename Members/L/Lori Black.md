# Lori Black

**Bassist. Born 1954-04-09.**

Lori Black, also known as Lorax, is an American bassist who played with [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] after Matt Lukin left the band. She is also known as the daughter of Shirley Temple.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] – bass (1987–1991, 1992–1993)
