# Louise Hoffsten

**Louise Hoffsten (born June 25, 1965, in Linköping, Sweden) is a Swedish blues singer, songwriter, and harmonica player, one of Sweden's most prominent blues artists.**

Hoffsten began her career in the 1980s and became a significant voice in Swedish blues rock. She was diagnosed with multiple sclerosis in the early 2000s and has become an advocate for MS awareness. Despite her illness, she has continued to record and perform.
