# Oleksa Fisyuk

**Guitarist. Born in Ukraine.**

Oleksa Fisyuk, known on stage as Liam Fessen, is a guitarist and a founding member of 1914. He has contributed to the band's distinctive blackened death-doom sound across all four studio albums.
