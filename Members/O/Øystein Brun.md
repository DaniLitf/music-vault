# Øystein Brun

**Øystein Brun is a Norwegian guitarist and the founder of Borknagar, who has been the sole constant member and primary composer throughout the band's existence since 1995.**

He created Borknagar as a vehicle for progressive, atmospheric black metal and has navigated the band through multiple vocalist and lineup changes while maintaining a consistent artistic direction.
