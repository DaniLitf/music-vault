# Oli Poulsen

**Multi-instrumentalist, record producer, mixing engineer. Born ca. 1963.**

Oli Poulsen is a Faroese multi-instrumentalist, record producer, and mixing engineer based in Copenhagen since 1987. He plays bass, keyboards, guitar, drums, percussion, and programming, often performing all instruments on his productions. He has worked with artists such as Michael Learns to Rock, Sissel Kyrkjebø, Grace Jones, the Georgia Satellites, and Hanne Boel.
