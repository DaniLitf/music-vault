# Ola Englund

**Guitarist. Swedish.**

Ola Englund is a Swedish guitarist known for his work with [[Artists/T/The Haunted]] and a brief stint as touring/session guitarist with [[Artists/S/Six Feet Under]] (2012–2013). He is also widely known as a gear-focused YouTube personality and co-founder of the guitar brand Solar Guitars.

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/S/Six Feet Under]] – guitar (2012–2013)
- [[Artists/T/The Haunted]] – guitar
