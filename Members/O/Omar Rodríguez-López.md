# Omar Rodríguez-López

**Omar Rodríguez-López is an American guitarist, composer, and filmmaker who co-founded At the Drive-In and The Mars Volta, and is one of the most prolific and experimental artists in alternative rock.**

Rodríguez-López served as producer, primary composer, and sonic architect of The Mars Volta's dense, jazz- and prog-influenced sound. He has released dozens of solo albums and continues to work prolifically across multiple genres.
