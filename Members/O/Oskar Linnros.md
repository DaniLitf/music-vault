# Oskar Linnros

**Rapper, producer, and singer. Born 15 August 1983.**

Hans Oskar Linnros, known as Kihlen, is a Swedish musician from Sundbyberg who rose to fame in the alternative rap duo Snook alongside Daniel Adams-Ray, releasing two critically acclaimed albums. He was briefly a member of the electro/hip hop group Maskinen, leaving after the single "Segertåget," and went on to a successful solo career and production work for artists including Petter and Veronica Maggio.
