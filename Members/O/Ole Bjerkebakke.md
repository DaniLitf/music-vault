# Ole Bjerkebakke

**Vocalist and drummer. Norwegian.**

Ole Bjerkebakke co-founded death metal band Cadaver with Anders Odden in 1988, serving as the band's vocalist and drummer through their original run until the 1993 breakup, and briefly again in 1995.
