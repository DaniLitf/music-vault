# Ossi Bonde

**Ossi Bonde is a Swedish drummer and vocalist, best known as one half of the Stockholm rock duo Johnossi.**

Bonde co-founded Johnossi with guitarist John Engelbert and contributes drums and vocals to the band's energetic, minimalist rock sound.
