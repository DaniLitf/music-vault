# Oscar Dronjak

**Guitar, vocals. Born December 27, 1972.**

Oscar Dronjak is a Swedish guitarist and co-founder of [[Artists/H/Hammerfall]], the band's primary songwriter and a driving force in the Swedish power metal revival of the 1990s. Before Hammerfall, he founded and sang/played guitar for the death metal band [[Artists/C/Ceremonial Oath]].

## Associated Artists
- [[Artists/C/Ceremonial Oath]] – vocals, guitar (1989–1993, 2012–present, founder)
- [[Artists/H/Hammerfall]] – guitar (1993–present, founder)
