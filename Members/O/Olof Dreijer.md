# Olof Dreijer

**Electronic musician, producer, DJ. Born 1981-11-27.**

Olof Dreijer is a Swedish electronic musician and producer best known as one half of [[Artists/T/The Knife]] with his sibling [[Members/K/Karin Dreijer]]. Outside The Knife, he has released EPs as Oni Ayhun and performed as DJ Coolof.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Knife]] – production, keyboards, programming (1999–2014)
