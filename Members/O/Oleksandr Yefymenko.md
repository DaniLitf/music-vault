# Oleksandr Yefymenko

**Musician. Ukrainian.**

Oleksandr Yefymenko is a Ukrainian musician and founding member of [[Artists/S/Skinhate]], formed in Pokrov, Ukraine in 1994. He is no longer part of the band's current lineup.
