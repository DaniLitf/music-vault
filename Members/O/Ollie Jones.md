# Ollie Jones

**Multi-instrumentalist. British.**

Ollie Jones is a Welsh death metal musician best known as the primary member of Desecration and guitarist for Extreme Noise Terror since 2005. He has played bass, drums, vocals, and guitar across various projects and has been a core member of both Desecration and ENT for over two decades.
