# Oliver Riedel

**Bassist. Born 1971-04-11.**

Oliver Riedel is a German bassist best known as a member of [[Artists/R/Rammstein]]. Before Rammstein, he played bass in [[Artists/T/The Inchtabokatables]] as Orgien-Olli from 1992 to 1994.

## Associated Artists

- [[Artists/T/The Inchtabokatables]] – bass as Orgien-Olli (1992–1994)
- [[Artists/R/Rammstein]] – bass (1994–present)
