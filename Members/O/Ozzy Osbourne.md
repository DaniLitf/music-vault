# Ozzy Osbourne

**Singer. Born 3 December 1948 in Aston, Birmingham, England.**

Ozzy Osbourne is a British heavy metal singer who co-founded Black Sabbath in 1968 and served as its vocalist through their most influential years. After being dismissed from the band in 1979, he launched a successful solo career that has continued for over four decades, collaborating with guitarists including Randy Rhoads, Jake E. Lee, and Zakk Wylde.
