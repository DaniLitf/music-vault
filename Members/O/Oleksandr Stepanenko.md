# Oleksandr Stepanenko

**Musician. Ukrainian.**

Oleksandr Stepanenko is a Ukrainian musician and founding member of [[Artists/S/Skinhate]], formed in Pokrov, Ukraine in 1994. He remains with the band as of its current lineup.
