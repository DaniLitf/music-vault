# Christian Gabel

**Record producer. Swedish.**

Christian Gabel is a Swedish musician, composer, engineer, and producer from Karlstad who is also documented in the vault as [[Members/C/Christian Gabel]]. He runs Studio Cobra in Stockholm and has released instrumental solo music under his own name and as [[Artists/0-9/1900]], where he frames the alias as a retro-technological project built from tape-like electronics and atmospheric composition.
