# Chris Cornell

**Record producer. See [[Members/C/Chris Cornell]] for biography.**

Chris Cornell was the vocalist and guitarist of [[Artists/S/Soundgarden]] and contributed to production on several of the band's albums, shaping their distinctive heavy yet melodic sound.
