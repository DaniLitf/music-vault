**Record producer and record label executive. British.**

Chris Blackwell is a British record producer and the founder of Island Records, who signed Bob Marley and the Wailers in 1972 and co-produced their early Island albums *Catch a Fire*, *Burnin'*, and *Natty Dread*. His production approach — blending reggae with rock production values — helped break reggae music to a global audience.
