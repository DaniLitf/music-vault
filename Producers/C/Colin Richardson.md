# Colin Richardson

**Record producer. British.**

Colin Richardson is a British record producer, mixer, and recording engineer who has worked on over 100 albums, most frequently associated with heavy metal and its subgenres. He has produced landmark albums for Napalm Death, Carcass, Machine Head, Fear Factory, Bolt Thrower, and many others, and produced Extreme Noise Terror's *Damage 381* (1997).
