# Curt Cuomo

**Record producer. American.**

Curt Cuomo is a Grammy-nominated American songwriter, record producer, and musician. He is best known for his work with KISS, co-writing their hit single "Psycho Circus" and working on *Carnival of Souls*. He has also produced and written for artists including John Fogerty, Duran Duran, Edgar Winter, and Axl Rose. His production style blends melodic hard rock with polished arrangements, and he owns Woodland Ranch Studios in Los Angeles.
