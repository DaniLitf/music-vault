# Christian Edgren

**Record producer. Swedish.**

Christian Edgren (born 8 February 1965 in Täby) is a Swedish studio technician and music producer. He owns and operates Traxton Recording in Stockholm and frequently collaborates with Johan Johansson. He produced Dia Psalma's early studio albums, recording at Traxton and Silence Studio.
