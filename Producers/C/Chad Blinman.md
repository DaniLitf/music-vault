# Chad Blinman

**Record producer, engineer, mixer. American.**

Chad Blinman is an American record producer, recording engineer, and mixer known for his work in punk rock and alternative music. He produced Face to Face's *Standards and Practices* (1999), *Ignorance Is Bliss* (1999), and *Reactionary* (2000), and also worked with Saves the Day, Alkaline Trio, and Viva Death. Blinman served as an associate professor at the Berklee College of Music. For his biography and full list of musical projects, see [[Members/C/Chad Blinman]].
