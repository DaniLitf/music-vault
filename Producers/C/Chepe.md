# Chepe

**Record producer. Swedish.**

Chepe is a Swedish-Chilean DJ and producer, also documented as [[Members/C/Chepe]], best known for production work with [[Artists/T/The Latin Kings]]. Alongside Salla, he helped define the group's sound after their breakthrough debut.
