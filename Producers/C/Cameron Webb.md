# Cameron Webb

**Record producer. American.**

Cameron Webb is a Grammy-winning American producer, engineer, and mixer based in Southern California who began his career as a runner at Larrabee Studios before winning a Grammy in 2004 for engineering Kelly Clarkson's *Breakaway*. He has produced or mixed albums for [[Artists/M/Motörhead|Motörhead]], [[Artists/M/Megadeth|Megadeth]], [[Artists/S/Social Distortion|Social Distortion]], Danzig, and [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio|Alkaline Trio]], for whom he produced both *Is This Thing Cursed?* (2018) and *Blood, Hair, and Eyeballs* (2024).
