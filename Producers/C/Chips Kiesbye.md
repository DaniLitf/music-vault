# Chips Kiesbye

**Record producer. Swedish.**

Chips Kiesbye is a Swedish record producer and frontman of the band Sator, who has worked with Sahara Hotnights, Millencolin, Thåström, and The Nomads, among others. He became [[Artists/T/The Hellacopters]]' producer of choice starting with *High Visibility* (2000), producing every subsequent studio album through *Eyes of Oblivion* (2022).
