# Chuck Ainlay

**Record producer. American.**

Chuck Ainlay is an American record producer and audio engineer with four Grammy Awards, best known for his long-running collaboration with Mark Knopfler on solo projects and Dire Straits material. He also received the Grammy for Best Surround Sound Album for the surround mix of *Brothers in Arms*, and has worked with George Strait, Miranda Lambert, and Taylor Swift among many others.
