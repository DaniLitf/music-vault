# Chris Tsangarides

**Record producer, sound engineer, and mixer. British. Born 17 August 1956; died 7 January 2018 of heart failure and lung infection.**

Chris Tsangarides was a British record producer, sound engineer, and mixer of Greek Cypriot origin, renowned for his work with hard rock and heavy metal acts. He began his career in 1974 as a tape operator at Morgan Studios (later Battery Studios) in London, becoming in-house producer by 1980. Over his career he produced albums for Judas Priest, Thin Lizzy, Ozzy Osbourne, Anvil, Exodus, Def Leppard, and Depeche Mode, among many others. He also co-founded Dark Lord Records and operated his own studio, Rainmaker Music Media. Tsangarides played piano, trumpet, and guitar.
