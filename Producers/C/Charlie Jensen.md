# Charlie Jensen

**Record producer and engineer. Danish.**

Charlie Jensen is a Danish recording and mastering engineer who worked extensively at Puk Recording Studios in Denmark. He co-produced and engineered the Georgia Satellites' album *Shaken Not Stirred* (1996).
