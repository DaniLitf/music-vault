# Todd Rundgren

**Record producer. American.**

Todd Rundgren is an American record producer and musician who produced two of Grand Funk Railroad's most commercially successful albums, *We're an American Band* (1973) and *Shinin' On* (1974). Both albums yielded number-one hit singles and are regarded as the band's peak commercial output.

Todd Rundgren is also a musician. See [[Members/T/Todd Rundgren]] for biography.
