# Nick Raskulinecz

**Record producer. American.**

Nick Raskulinecz (born February 4, 1970) is an American record producer from Knoxville, Tennessee, best known for his work with Foo Fighters, Alice in Chains, Deftones, Rush, Mastodon, and Ghost. He began his career as an assistant engineer at Sound City Studios in Los Angeles before breaking through as a producer with Foo Fighters' *One by One* (2002). His production style blends heavy rock energy with polished, radio-friendly clarity, and he has earned multiple Grammy nominations and wins for his work.
