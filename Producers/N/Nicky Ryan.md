# Nicky Ryan

**Record producer. Irish.**

Nicky Ryan was an Irish music producer, recording engineer, and manager, best known as the longtime creative partner and producer for Enya. Born and raised in Dublin, he began his career as a sound engineer for Irish folk groups including Planxty and Clannad, eventually becoming Clannad's manager. In 1982, he left Clannad with vocalist Enya to form a unique artistic triumvirate with his wife, lyricist Roma Ryan. Together, they produced all of Enya's albums including *Watermark* (1988), *Shepherd Moons* (1991), *The Memory of Trees* (1995), and *A Day Without Rain* (2000), defining the ethereal, multi-layered Celtic new age sound that made Enya one of the best-selling artists of all time.
