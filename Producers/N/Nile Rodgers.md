# Nile Rodgers

**Record producer. American.**

Nile Rodgers is an American guitarist and record producer, co-founder of Chic, who produced David Bowie's *Let's Dance* (1983) and *Black Tie White Noise* (1993), giving both a sleek, funk-inflected pop sound. He has also produced Madonna, Diana Ross, and Duran Duran.
