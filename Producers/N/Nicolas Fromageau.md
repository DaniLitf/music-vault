# Nicolas Fromageau

**Record producer. French.**

Nicolas Fromageau is a French musician and producer who co-founded [[Artists/M/M83]] and co-produced the project's first two albums with Anthony Gonzalez, shaping their early blend of ambient textures and distorted synth-rock. After leaving M83 in 2004 he went on to front [[Artists/T/Team Ghost]]. See also [[Members/N/Nicolas Fromageau]].
