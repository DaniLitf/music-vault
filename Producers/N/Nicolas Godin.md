# Nicolas Godin

**Record producer. French.**

Nicolas Godin is a French musician and producer who, together with [[Members/J/Jean-Benoît Dunckel]], self-produced all of the studio albums by his duo [[Artists/A/Air]], co-producing two of them (*Talkie Walkie*, *Pocket Symphony*) alongside Nigel Godrich. His production style favours vintage analogue synthesizers, Rhodes electric piano, and orchestral arrangement layered with live instrumentation. See also [[Members/N/Nicolas Godin]] for his biography as a performing musician.
