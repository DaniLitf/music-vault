# Nico Elgstrand

**Record producer. Swedish.**

Nico Elgstrand is a Swedish musician and record producer, best known as a former member of Entombed, where he served as bassist from 2004 to 2010 before switching to guitar from 2010 to 2014. He co-produced the band's album *Uprising* (2000) alongside the group, and later engineered and mixed several Entombed releases.

Following the Entombed trademark dispute, Elgstrand joined L.G. Petrov, Olle Dahlstedt, and Victor Brandt in Entombed A.D., where he served as guitarist and contributed as producer and engineer on *Back to the Front*, *Dead Dawn*, and *Bowels of Earth*. He has also produced albums for Grand Magus and other acts.
