# Nigel Godrich

**Record producer. English.**

Nigel Godrich is an English record producer and engineer best known for producing every Radiohead album since *OK Computer* (1997). Renowned for his meticulous, texture-focused approach and close collaborative relationships with artists, he has also produced or mixed albums for Beck, Paul McCartney, U2, R.E.M., and [[Artists/A/Air]], co-producing the duo's *Talkie Walkie* (2004) and *Pocket Symphony* (2007).
