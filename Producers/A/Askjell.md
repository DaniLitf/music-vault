# Askjell

**Record producer. Norwegian.**

Askjell is a Norwegian producer and collaborator associated with Aurora's two-part album cycle and other Norwegian pop productions. In this vault he is linked to [[Artists/A/AURORA]] through the 2018–2019 albums.
