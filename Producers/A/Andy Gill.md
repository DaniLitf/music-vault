# Andy Gill

**Record producer. British.**

Andy Gill was a British guitarist and producer best known as a founding member of Gang of Four. He produced [[Artists/T/The Jesus Lizard]]'s *Blue* and was part of the production/engineering team for their 1998 self-titled EP.
