# Anthony Gonzalez

**Record producer. French.**

Anthony Gonzalez is the French musician behind [[Artists/M/M83]] and has produced or co-produced every M83 release, from bedroom 8-track recordings to widescreen, reverb-drenched synth-pop epics and film scores. His production style layers analog synthesizers, shoegaze guitars, and heavily processed vocals into cinematic walls of sound. See also [[Members/A/Anthony Gonzalez]].
