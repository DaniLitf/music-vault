# Andy LaPlegua

**Record producer. Norwegian.**

Andy LaPlegua is a Norwegian electronic musician and producer best known as the founder and frontman of several influential EBM and industrial projects. Born in Fredrikstad, Norway, he launched his career in the mid-1990s with Icon of Coil, which evolved from a solo project into a trio and became a cornerstone of the futurepop scene. As a producer, LaPlegua handles most of the songwriting, programming, and production work himself, crafting a distinctive sound that blends aggressive EBM rhythms with melodic synth-pop hooks. He also founded Combichrist, expanding into harsher industrial territory, and has produced material for multiple related projects.
