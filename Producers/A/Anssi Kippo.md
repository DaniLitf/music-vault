# Anssi Kippo

**Record producer. Finnish.**

Anssi Kippo is a Finnish record producer and sound engineer who founded Astia Studios in 1994 in Lappeenranta, Finland, where he continues to operate both Astia A and B recording facilities. He gained a reputation in the Finnish heavy metal scene for his technical expertise and attention to detail, becoming a sought-after producer for bands across the metal spectrum.

Kippo has produced albums for Children of Bodom, Ensiferum, Sonata Arctica, and many other Finnish metal acts. He is known for capturing powerful, dynamic performances and has worked with a wide variety of subgenres from melodic death metal to power metal.
