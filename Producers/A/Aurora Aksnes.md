# Aurora Aksnes

**Record producer. Norwegian.**

Aurora Aksnes is a Norwegian singer-songwriter and producer, also documented as [[Members/A/Aurora Aksnes]]. She is credited in sources as a record producer as well as performer, and her vault-relevant production work centers on her solo [[Artists/A/AURORA]] albums.
