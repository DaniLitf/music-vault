# Aaron Rubin

**Record producer, audio engineer. American.**

Aaron Rubin is a San Diego-based producer, engineer, and mixer who took over as [[Artists/A/Angels & Airwaves|Angels & Airwaves]]' primary studio collaborator after Jeff "Critter" Newell's death, co-producing *The Dream Walker* (2014) with Tom DeLonge and producing *Lifeforms* (2021) with the full band. He is the older brother of Angels & Airwaves drummer [[Members/I/Ilan Rubin|Ilan Rubin]] and has also worked on records for Blink-182 and Illenium.
