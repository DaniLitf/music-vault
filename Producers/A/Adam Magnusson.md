# Adam Magnusson

**Record producer. Swedish.**

Adam Magnusson is a Swedish producer who co-produced Alarma Man's 2010 album *Love Forever*, also contributing choir vocals to the record. See [[Members/A/Adam Magnusson]] for his performing credits.
