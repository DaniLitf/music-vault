# Atticus Ross

**Record producer and musician. British.**

Atticus Ross is a British-born producer, musician, and composer who became Trent Reznor's primary creative partner from *Year Zero* (2007) onward, and was officially inducted as a full member of Nine Inch Nails in 2016. He co-produces all NIN studio releases alongside Reznor, contributing to the more textured, layered sonic approach of their later work. Beyond NIN, Ross and Reznor have built a parallel career as film score composers, winning Academy Awards for *The Social Network* (2010) and *Soul* (2020).
