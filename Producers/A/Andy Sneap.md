# Andy Sneap

**Record producer, guitarist, and composer. British. Born 18 July 1969 in Belper, Derbyshire, England.**

Andy Sneap is an English record producer, guitarist, and composer with over 100 production credits spanning heavy metal, thrash, and hard rock. He first gained attention as the founder and guitarist of thrash metal band Sabbat, producing their albums himself before transitioning to a full-time production career. Operating from his Backstage Studios in Derbyshire, which he founded in 1994, Sneap has produced and mixed albums for Judas Priest, Megadeth, Killswitch Engage, Accept, Saxon, and Opeth, among many others. He received a Swedish Grammy for Opeth's *Deliverance* and a US Grammy nomination for Megadeth's *Dystopia*. See also [[Members/A/Andy Sneap]].
