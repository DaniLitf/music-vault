# Allen Reynolds

**Record producer. American.**

Allen Reynolds is an American record producer and songwriter inducted into the Nashville Songwriters Hall of Fame, best known for producing many of Garth Brooks's hit albums and for writing the 1965 pop hit "Five O'Clock World" for The Vogues. He also produced Crystal Gayle's signature soft rock records and purchased Jack Clement's Nashville studio, which Brooks later renamed Allentown.
