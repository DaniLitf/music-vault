# Russ Russell

**Record producer. British.**

Russ Russell is a British record producer and audio engineer who has worked extensively with Napalm Death, producing albums such as *Diatribes* (1996), *Inside the Torn Apart* (1997), and *Words from the Exit Wound* (1998). He has also produced or engineered for Dimmu Borgir, The Exploited, Amorphis, and many other metal acts. Russell was a member of Meathook Seed, producing their 1999 album *Basic Instructions Before Leaving Earth (B.I.B.L.E.)*. See [[Members/R/Russ Russell]] for his musician credits.
