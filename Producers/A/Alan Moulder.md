# Alan Moulder

**Record producer and mixing engineer. British.**

Alan Moulder is a British producer and mixing engineer who became one of the definitive sonic architects of 1990s and 2000s alternative and industrial rock. He built his reputation engineering and mixing for acts like My Bloody Valentine, The Jesus and Mary Chain, and Nine Inch Nails, where his ability to balance abrasive noise with melodic clarity proved essential. His long-running collaboration with Trent Reznor began in earnest on *The Fragile* (1999), which the two co-produced together over nearly two years, and continued through *Hesitation Marks* (2013). Outside of NIN, he has worked extensively with Smashing Pumpkins, Placebo, and Interpol.
