# Andy Johns

**Record producer. British.**

Andy Johns was a British sound engineer and record producer who worked on several iconic rock albums, including the Rolling Stones' *Exile on Main St.* (1972), Television's *Marquee Moon* (1977), and a series of albums by Led Zeppelin during the 1970s. He engineered or produced records that have sold in excess of 160 million copies worldwide.
