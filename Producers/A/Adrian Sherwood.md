# Adrian Sherwood

**Record producer. British.**

Adrian Sherwood is a British record producer and founder of On-U Sound Records (1979), best known for pioneering the application of dub mixing techniques to electronic and industrial music. Working closely with [[Producers/K/Keith LeBlanc]], Doug Wimbish, and Skip McDonald in the outfit Tackhead, Sherwood helped shape industrial hip-hop and noise throughout the 1980s and 1990s. His production contributions to Nine Inch Nails' *Pretty Hate Machine* (1989) brought his dub-influenced processing to one of industrial rock's landmark debuts.
