# Antoine Gaillet

**Record producer, mixer, engineer. French.**

Antoine Gaillet is a French producer, mixer, and engineer who has worked with a range of French rock and pop acts. He assisted Anthony Gonzalez in recording [[Artists/M/M83]]'s ambient collection *Digital Shades Vol. 1* (2007), recorded primarily at Gonzalez's home studio.
