# Tim Bran

**Record producer. British.**

Tim Bran is a British producer and musician cited among collaborators on [[Artists/A/AURORA]]'s 2018–2019 album sessions. He also worked with Aurora again around *The Dream*-style electronic and pop production contexts noted in sources.
