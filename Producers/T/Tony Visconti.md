# Tony Visconti

**Record producer. American.**

Tony Visconti is an American-born, London-based record producer best known as David Bowie's longest-serving collaborator, producing albums across nearly the whole of Bowie's career from *The Man Who Sold the World* (1970) to his final album *Blackstar* (2016), including the full Berlin Trilogy. He has also produced T. Rex, Thin Lizzy, and Morrissey, among many others.
