# Tony Pizarro

**Record producer. American.**

Tony Pizarro is an American producer and engineer closely associated with Tupac Shakur's mid-1990s work. He produced the classic single "Dear Mama" on *Me Against the World* (1995) and worked on material later released on Tupac's posthumous albums.
