# Tobias Forge

**Record producer. Swedish.**

Tobias Forge is a Swedish musician and the founder, frontman, and primary producer of Ghost. Under the pseudonym "Gene Walker" he produced or co-produced Ghost's *Opus Eponymous* (2010), *Seven Inches of Satanic Panic* (2019), and *Skeletá* (2025). For his biography and full list of musical projects, see [[Members/T/Tobias Forge]].
