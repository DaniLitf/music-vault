# Thorbjørn Akkerhaugen

**Record producer, engineer. Norwegian.**

Thorbjørn Akkerhaugen is a Norwegian record producer and engineer who operates Akkerhaugen Lydstudio in Norway. He produced Emperor's *IX Equilibrium* (1999) and *Live Inferno* (2009), and has worked with other Norwegian metal artists.
