# Tony Bongiovi

**Record producer, audio engineer. American.**

Tony Bongiovi is an American record producer and audio engineer best known for co-founding Power Station (now Avatar Studios) in New York City and for his early production work with [[Artists/T/Talking Heads]], [[Artists/R/Ramones]], and other artists. He produced Talking Heads' debut album *Talking Heads: 77* alongside Lance Quinn.
