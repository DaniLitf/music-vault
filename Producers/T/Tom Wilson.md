# Tom Wilson

**Record producer. American.**

Tom Wilson (born Thomas Blanchard Wilson Jr.; March 25, 1931 – September 6, 1978) was a legendary American record producer who played a pivotal role in shaping 1960s folk rock, experimental rock, and proto-punk. A Harvard graduate, Wilson produced Bob Dylan's early electric albums (*Bringing It All Back Home* and "Like a Rolling Stone"), Simon & Garfunkel's debut, the Mothers of Invention's *Freak Out!*, and the Velvet Underground's *White Light/White Heat*. He was one of the few African American producers working in rock music during the 1960s and is credited with helping launch the folk-rock genre.
