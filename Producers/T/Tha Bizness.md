# Tha Bizness

**Record production duo. American.**

Tha Bizness is an American production duo consisting of Justin "Henny" Henderson and Christopher "Dow Jones" Whitacre. They produced for a wide range of hip-hop and R&B artists in the late 2000s and contributed production to G-Unit's *T·O·S (Terminate on Sight)* (2008).
