# Trent Reznor

**Record producer and musician. American.**

Trent Reznor is the founder and sole official member of Nine Inch Nails, and the primary producer of every NIN studio album. Beginning with *Pretty Hate Machine* (1989), Reznor established a self-contained approach to production — writing, performing, and producing nearly all material himself, often building albums around a central sonic and narrative concept. He has also produced records for other artists, most notably Marilyn Manson's *Antichrist Superstar* (1996) and Saul Williams's *The Inevitable Rise and Liberation of NiggyTardust!* (2007). Since *Year Zero* (2007), he has co-produced all NIN releases alongside [[Producers/A/Atticus Ross]], with whom he also creates film scores.
