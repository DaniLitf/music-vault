# Tom Petta

**Record producer. American.**

Tom Petta is an American record producer and recording engineer, and the guitarist/vocalist of the New Jersey punk rock band Bigwig, of which he is the only constant member. Through his studio, The Bivouac Audio in Manahawkin, New Jersey, he has produced, mixed, and mastered numerous punk and hardcore releases.
