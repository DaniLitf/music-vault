# Terry Date

**Producer. American.**

Terry Date is a producer known for his work with Soundgarden, Deftones, and Pantera, among others. He co-produced Staind's major-label debut *Dysfunction* (1999) alongside Fred Durst.

## Notable Productions

- [[Artists/S/Staind]] – *Dysfunction* (1999, with Fred Durst)
