# Tomas Skogsberg

**Record producer. Swedish.**

Tomas Skogsberg is a Swedish record producer, engineer, and studio owner, widely credited as the architect of the Swedish death metal guitar tone. He runs Sunlight Studios, originally established in Stockholm in 1982 and later relocated to Gräddö, which became the birthplace of the genre-defining "buzzsaw" sound characterised by the Boss HM-2 heavy metal pedal with all knobs cranked to maximum.

Skogsberg's most influential work was with Entombed on *Left Hand Path* (1990), which codified the Swedish death metal sound. He went on to produce and engineer landmark albums for Dismember, Grave, Katatonia, Tiamat, and At the Gates, among many others, making Sunlight Studios one of the most important recording facilities in extreme metal history.
