# Toby Wright

**Record producer and mixing engineer. American.**

Toby Wright is an American record producer and mixing engineer who has worked with some of the most influential hard rock and metal acts of the 1990s and 2000s. He began his career in New York studios, eventually moving to Los Angeles where he worked as an engineer at One on One Studios, contributing to Metallica's *...And Justice for All* (1988). His career breakout came engineering Alice in Chains' *Jar of Flies* (1994), the first EP to debut at number one on the Billboard charts. Wright went on to produce Alice in Chains' self-titled album (1995) and *Unplugged* (1996), Korn's *Follow the Leader* (1998), Slayer's *Divine Intervention* (1994), and albums for Sevendust, Soulfly, Primus, and Third Eye Blind.
