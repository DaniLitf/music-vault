# Tom Dalgety

**Record producer, audio engineer. British.**

Tom Dalgety is an English record producer and audio engineer known for his work with Pixies, Ghost, Royal Blood, and The Cult. He grew up in Frome, Somerset, and began his career at Rockfield Studios in Wales before establishing himself as one of the most sought-after alternative rock producers. Dalgety earned the nickname "Fifth Pixie" for his extensive involvement in pre-production on Pixies' recent albums. He was nominated for Grammy Awards in 2019 for his production work on Ghost's album *Prequelle* (Best Rock Album) and the track "Rats" (Best Rock Song).
