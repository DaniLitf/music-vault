# Toshi Kasai

**Record producer, engineer. Japanese.**

Toshi Kasai is a producer and engineer closely associated with [[Artists/T/The Melvins]], beginning with *Hostile Ambient Takeover*. He also produced/recorded later projects including *Thunderball* and the Melvins/Napalm Death collaboration *Savage Imperial Death March*.

## Associated Projects

- [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] – *Hostile Ambient Takeover* (2002)
- [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] – *Thunderball* (2025)
- [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] – *Savage Imperial Death March* (2026)
