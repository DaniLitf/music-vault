# Thom Wilson

**Record producer and engineer. American.**

Thom Wilson was a legendary American punk rock producer and engineer who shaped the sound of some of the genre's most influential acts. Beginning his career in the mid-1970s, Wilson transitioned from soft rock engineering to pioneering work in punk and alternative rock. He is best known for producing [[Artists/T/The Offspring]]'s breakthrough album *Smash* (1994), which became the best-selling independent release of all time with over 6 million copies sold in the U.S. Beyond The Offspring, Wilson produced seminal albums for [[Artists/D/Dead Kennedys]], [[Artists/B/Bad Religion]], [[Artists/S/Social Distortion]], [[Artists/T/The Vandals]], and dozens of other acts across multiple decades. He continued working actively until 2003 and passed away in 2015.
