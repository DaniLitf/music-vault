# Tom DeLonge

**Record producer. American.**

Tom DeLonge is the guitarist and co-lead vocalist of [[Artists/B/Blink-182|Blink-182]] and founder of [[Artists/A/Angels & Airwaves|Angels & Airwaves]], for whom he has produced or co-produced nearly every album, typically working out of his own home studios alongside engineer [[Producers/J/Jeff 'Critter' Newell|Jeff "Critter" Newell]] and later [[Producers/A/Aaron Rubin|Aaron Rubin]]. See also [[Members/T/Tom DeLonge]] for his career as a musician.
