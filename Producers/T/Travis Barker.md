# Travis Barker

**Record producer. American.**

Travis Barker is an American drummer and record producer, best known as the drummer of [[Artists/B/Blink-182]] — see [[Members/T/Travis Barker]] for his biography as a musician. As a producer he became one of the key architects of the 2020s pop-punk revival, producing hit records for Machine Gun Kelly, Willow, and jxdn, and produced Blink-182's reunion album *One More Time...* (2023).
