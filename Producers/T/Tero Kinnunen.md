# Tero Kinnunen

**Record producer. Finnish.**

Tero Kinnunen is a Finnish record producer, sound engineer, and musician, best known as the long-time producer and live backliner for Nightwish, having worked with the band from their earliest recordings through to their most recent albums. Born in Kitee, Finland, he has been integral to shaping Nightwish's symphonic metal sound across multiple decades.

In addition to Nightwish, Kinnunen has produced and engineered albums for Ensiferum, Amorphis, Beast in Black, and other Finnish metal acts. His production work spans engineering, mixing, arrangement, and co-production roles.
