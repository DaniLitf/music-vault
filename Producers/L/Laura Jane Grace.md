# Laura Jane Grace

**Record producer. American.**

Laura Jane Grace is the singer-guitarist and founder of [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]], who acted as both recording engineer and producer for the band's sixth studio album, *Transgender Dysphoria Blues* (2014), an autobiographical record about her gender transition. See [[Members/L/Laura Jane Grace]] for her full biography as a musician.
