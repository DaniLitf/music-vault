# Lance Quinn

**Record producer, audio engineer. American.**

Lance Quinn is an American record producer and audio engineer. He co-produced Talking Heads' debut album *Talking Heads: 77* alongside Tony Bongiovi and worked as an engineer on several albums of the era.
