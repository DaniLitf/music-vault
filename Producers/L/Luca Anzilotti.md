# Luca Anzilotti

**Record producer. German.**

Luca Anzilotti is a German record producer who, alongside Michael Münzing, formed the production duo behind the Eurodance group Snap!. He co-produced and co-wrote all of Snap!'s albums, including *World Power* (1990), *The Madman's Return* (1992), and *Welcome to Tomorrow* (1994). He also performed with Münzing and Sven Väth in the group Off and the side project 16 BIT.
