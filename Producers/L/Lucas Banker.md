# Lucas Banker

**Record producer, engineer. American.**

Lucas Banker is an American record producer and audio engineer, best known as one half of the production team Dirty Icon Productions alongside [[Producers/L/Logan Mader]]. He co-produced [[Artists/D/Divine Heresy]]'s albums *Bleed the Fifth* (2007) and *Bringer of Plagues* (2009), handling recording, engineering, mixing, and mastering.
