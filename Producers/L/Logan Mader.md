# Logan Mader

**Record producer, guitarist. Canadian-American.**

Logan Conrad Mader is a respected record producer and guitarist best known for his foundational work with Machine Head, where he helped pioneer the groove metal sound on their 1994 debut *Burn My Eyes*. After his tenure with Machine Head and a brief stint with Soulfly, Mader transitioned into production and co-founded Dirty Icon Productions in Los Angeles, establishing himself as one of metal's most sought-after producers. He is known for his work with Gojira, Soulfly, and numerous other metal acts, and continues to record and perform with his band Once Human.
