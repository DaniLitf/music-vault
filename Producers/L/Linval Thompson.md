# Linval Thompson

**Record producer. Jamaican.**

Linval Thompson is a Jamaican conscious roots reggae vocalist, dub musician, and record producer who has been active since the 1970s. Born in Kingston, Jamaica, he began his career as a singer, working with Lee "Scratch" Perry at Black Ark studio and Bunny Lee, before transitioning into production. His productions helped shape the transition from roots reggae into early dancehall. He produced Eek-A-Mouse's early albums *Bubble Up Yu Hip* (1980) and *The Mouse and the Man* (1983), and has also produced for Dennis Brown, The Wailing Souls, Freddie McGregor, and Barrington Levy, with releases on Trojan and Greensleeves Records.
