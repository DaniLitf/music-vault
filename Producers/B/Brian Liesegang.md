# Brian Liesegang

**Record producer, songwriter, composer, and musician. American. Born 10 February 1970 in New York City.**

Brian Liesegang is an American songwriter, producer, composer, guitarist, and programmer, best known as a founding member of the industrial rock band Filter. Before forming Filter with Richard Patrick in 1993, Liesegang worked with Nine Inch Nails on the multi-platinum albums *Broken* and *The Downward Spiral*. With Filter, he co-wrote and produced the platinum-selling debut *Short Bus* (1995), featuring the hit "Hey Man Nice Shot." After leaving Filter in 1997, he produced Veruca Salt's *Resolver* and collaborated with Billy Corgan on *Future Embrace*. He later co-founded Ashtar Command and has contributed to film, television, and video game soundtracks. See also [[Members/B/Brian Liesegang]].
