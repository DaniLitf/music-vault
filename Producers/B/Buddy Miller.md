# Buddy Miller

**Record producer. American.**

Buddy Miller is an American singer, guitarist, songwriter, and producer who has won twelve Americana Music Honors & Awards and was named Artist of the Decade by *No Depression* magazine in 2008. He produced Patty Griffin's Grammy-winning *Downtown Church* and has toured as lead guitarist for Emmylou Harris, Robert Plant, and Steve Earle, as well as serving as executive music producer for the ABC series *Nashville*.
