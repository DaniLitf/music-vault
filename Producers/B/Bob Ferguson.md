# Bob Ferguson

**Record producer. American.**

Bob Ferguson was an American country music songwriter, record producer, and A&R executive at RCA Victor who helped shape the Nashville Sound of the 1960s–70s. Working under Chet Atkins, he produced hundreds of recordings for Porter Wagoner, Dolly Parton, Connie Smith, and over fifty other artists from 1963 to 1978. He also wrote two enduring country standards: "Wings of a Dove" (a 1960 hit for Ferlin Husky) and "The Carroll County Accident" (CMA Song of the Year 1969 for Porter Wagoner). Beyond music, Ferguson was a filmmaker, naturalist, and Choctaw Indian historian.
