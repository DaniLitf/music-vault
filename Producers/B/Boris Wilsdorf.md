# Boris Wilsdorf

**Record producer. German.**

Boris Wilsdorf is a German record producer, sound engineer, and studio owner, best known for his long-running collaboration with Einstürzende Neubauten. Born and based in Berlin, he began his career at Hansa Studios before becoming the band's go-to engineer and producer at his own studio, andereBaustelle. He produced Einstürzende Neubauten's *Lament* (2014) and has worked extensively on their later catalog including *Silence Is Sexy*, *Perpetuum Mobile*, *Alles wieder offen*, and *Rampen (apm: alien pop music)*. Wilsdorf has also produced for Swans, Mick Harvey, Throbbing Gristle, and Nadja.
