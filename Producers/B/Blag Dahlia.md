# Blag Dahlia

**Record producer. American.**

Blag Dahlia is an American punk vocalist, author, and producer best known as the frontman of [[Artists/T/The Dwarves]]; see also [[Members/B/Blag Dahlia]]. His production credits include Dwarves records and outside punk projects, with Wikipedia specifically documenting his production work across Dwarves releases and other bands.
