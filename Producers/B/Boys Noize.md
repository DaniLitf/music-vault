# Boys Noize

**Record producer. German-Iraqi.**

Boys Noize (Alexander Ridha) is a German-Iraqi electronic producer, DJ, and label head. He co-produced and mixed the 2026 album *Nine Inch Noize*, a collaboration with Nine Inch Nails that grew out of his earlier remix work on the *Challengers* film score and Nine Inch Nails' *Tron: Ares* soundtrack.
