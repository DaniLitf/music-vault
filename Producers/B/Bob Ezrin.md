# Bob Ezrin

**Record producer. Canadian.**

Bob Ezrin is a Canadian record producer from Toronto, one of the most influential figures in classic rock production, celebrated for his sweeping, theatrical arrangements and ability to shape ambitious concept records. He rose to prominence producing a run of Alice Cooper albums in the early 1970s, including *Billion Dollar Babies* (1973), and went on to produce landmark records such as Kiss's *Destroyer* (1976) and Pink Floyd's *The Wall* (1979), later reuniting with Pink Floyd for *A Momentary Lapse of Reason* (1987) and *The Division Bell* (1994). He co-produced [[Artists/0-9/30 Seconds to Mars|30 Seconds to Mars]]'s self-titled debut (2002) alongside [[Producers/B/Brian Virtue]].
