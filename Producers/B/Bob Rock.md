# Bob Rock

**Record producer. Canadian.**

Bob Rock is a Canadian record producer and engineer who rose to prominence at Vancouver's Little Mountain Sound Studios mixing and engineering hit albums for Loverboy, Bon Jovi, and Aerosmith before producing [[Artists/M/Metallica]]'s self-titled *Metallica* ("The Black Album," 1991), which pushed the band toward a more polished, commercial hard-rock sound. He continued as Metallica's primary producer through *Load*, *Reload*, and *St. Anger*, and was inducted into the Canadian Music Hall of Fame in 2007.
