# Bruno Kramm

**Record producer. German.**

Bruno Kramm is a German musician, record producer, and label founder, best known as co-founder of Das Ich and founder of the Danse Macabre record label. He has produced all of Das Ich's studio albums since 1991, as well as albums for Atrocity, Illuminate, and other Danse Macabre artists. For his biography and full list of musical projects, see [[Members/B/Bruno Kramm]].

## Associated Projects
