# Bryce Goggin

**Record producer. American.**

Bryce Goggin is an American record producer and sound engineer based in Brooklyn, New York, where he owns and operates Trout Recording. His career began in the early 1990s at Baby Monster Studios, and he first gained prominence mixing Pavement's *Crooked Rain, Crooked Rain* (1994). He has since worked with a wide range of artists including Phish, The Ramones, Spacehog, Swans, Sebadoh, The Lemonheads, and Antony and the Johnsons. Goggin produced Edie Brickell & New Bohemians' reunion album *Stranger Things* (2006), capturing the band's organic folk-rock sound with his characteristically warm and natural production style.
