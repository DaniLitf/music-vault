# Big D the Impossible

**Record producer. American.**

Big D the Impossible was a member of Digital Underground's Underground Railroad production team and one of the principal producers on Tupac Shakur's first two albums. His credits include much of *2Pacalypse Now* (1991) and *Strictly 4 My N.I.G.G.A.Z...* (1993).
