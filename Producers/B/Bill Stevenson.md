# Bill Stevenson

**Record producer. American.**

Bill Stevenson is an American record producer better known as the longtime drummer of [[Artists/D/Descendents|Descendents]] and a former member of [[Artists/B/Black Flag|Black Flag]]. See [[Members/B/Bill Stevenson]] for his career as a musician. With engineer [[Producers/J/Jason Livermore|Jason Livermore]] he co-founded The Blasting Room studio in Fort Collins, Colorado in 1994, where he has produced albums for Rise Against, NOFX, Rancid, and [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio|Alkaline Trio]].
