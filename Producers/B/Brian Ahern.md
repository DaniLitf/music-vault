# Brian Ahern

**Record producer. Canadian.**

Brian Ahern is a Canadian record producer and guitarist who was inducted into the Canadian Country Music Hall of Fame in 2006 and received the Americana Music Association's Lifetime Achievement Award for Producer/Engineer in 2010. His renowned mobile studio, the Enactron Truck, produced over 40 gold and platinum records, and his work with Emmylou Harris on albums such as *Elite Hotel* and *Luxury Liner* helped define the country-rock sound of the 1970s.
