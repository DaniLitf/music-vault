# Brian Virtue

**Record producer. American.**

Brian Virtue is a Grammy-nominated American record producer, engineer, and mixer based in Los Angeles. A Southern California native, he got his first major break co-producing [[Artists/0-9/30 Seconds to Mars|30 Seconds to Mars]]'s self-titled debut (2002) alongside [[Producers/B/Bob Ezrin]], and went on to produce, engineer, or mix for Jane's Addiction, Audioslave, Puddle of Mudd, and Chevelle.
