# Butch Vig

**Record producer and drummer. American.**

Butch Vig is an American record producer and drummer, best known for producing landmark alternative rock albums including Nirvana's *Nevermind* (1991) and for co-founding the band [[Artists/G/Garbage|Garbage]], in which he plays drums. He produced two [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]] albums, *New Wave* (2007) and *White Crosses* (2010), helping bring the punk band to a wider mainstream audience. See [[Members/B/Butch Vig]] for his biography as a musician.
