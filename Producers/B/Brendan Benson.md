# Brendan Benson

**Record producer. American.**

Brendan Benson is a Detroit power-pop musician and producer, known for his solo albums and as co-founder of The Raconteurs alongside Jack White. He produced [[Artists/T/The Greenhornes]]' *East Grand Blues* EP (2005) during the period the two bands' members grew close in Detroit. He is also a musician — see [[Members/B/Brendan Benson]] for biography.
