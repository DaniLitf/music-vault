# Brian Eno

**Record producer. British.**

Brian Eno is a British musician and producer who co-wrote and co-produced David Bowie's *Outside* (1995) after their earlier collaboration on the Berlin Trilogy, and is one of the most influential producers in rock, having also produced U2, Talking Heads, and Coldplay. See also [[Members/B/Brian Eno]] for his own artist biography.
