# Ben Lovett

**Record producer, composer, songwriter. American.**

Ben Lovett is an American record producer, film composer, and musician based in Georgia. Beyond his music production work, he has become a prolific film composer, scoring numerous independent and mainstream films including *The Ritual*, *The Night House*, and *Hellraiser*. His production discography includes work with indie and alternative rock projects, most notably producing [[Artists/H/Heavens]] and collaborating with Chris Wollard. He founded the independent record label Lovers Label.
