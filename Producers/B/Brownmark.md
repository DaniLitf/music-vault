# Brownmark

**Record producer and musician. American.**

Brownmark (Mark Brown) is an American bassist, songwriter, and record producer best known as a member of [[Artists/T/The Revolution|the Revolution]] (Prince's backing band). He founded and produced the funk band [[Artists/M/Mazarati|Mazarati]] and co-produced their debut album alongside Prince and David Z.

As a producer, he helped shape the Minneapolis sound of the 1980s. Brownmark also has a [[Members/B/Brownmark|member page]] documenting his performance career.
