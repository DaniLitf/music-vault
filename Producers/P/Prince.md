# Prince

**Record producer, songwriter, multi-instrumentalist. American.**

Prince Rogers Nelson was one of the most prolific record producers in popular music, producing the vast majority of his own recorded output and numerous albums for associated acts. He produced all of his own albums from *Dirty Mind* (1980) onward and produced for [[Artists/T/The Time]], [[Artists/T/The Revolution]], [[Artists/N/New Power Generation]], [[Artists/S/Sheila E.]], and many others. He controlled every aspect of his recordings, often playing all instruments himself.

## Associated Projects

- [[Artists/P/Prince]] – every album from *Dirty Mind* (1980) onward
- [[Artists/N/New Power Generation]] – *Diamonds and Pearls* (1991), *Love Symbol Album* (1992), *Gold Nigga* (1993), *Exodus* (1995), *Newpower Soul* (1998), *Planet Earth* (2007)
- [[Artists/T/The Time]] – *The Time* (1981), *What Time Is It?* (1982), *Ice Cream Castle* (1984) (credited as Jamie Starr / The Starr Company)
- [[Artists/T/The Revolution]] – *Purple Rain* (1984), *Around the World in a Day* (1985), *Parade* (1986)
- [[Artists/S/Sheila E.]] – *The Glamorous Life* (1984)
