# Paul Curcio

**Record producer. American.**

Paul Curcio was a Rochester, New York guitarist and studio owner, formerly of 1960s psych-folk act the Mojo Men, who founded Music America Studios in the late 1970s. He is best known for producing [[Artists/M/Metallica]]'s debut album *Kill 'Em All* (1983), a low-budget, rush job booked mainly for its cheap studio rate; the band was reportedly unhappy with his hands-off involvement, and he did not work with them again.
