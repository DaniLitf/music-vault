# Pepé Willie

**Record producer. American.**

Pepé Willie was an American musician and producer who founded the funk band 94 East and produced their recordings, including *Minneapolis Genius* (1985) and *Symbolic Beginning* (1995). He is widely recognized as the "Godfather of the Minneapolis Sound" and mentored a young Prince. See [[Members/P/Pepé Willie]] for full biography.
