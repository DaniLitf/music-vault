# Pat Moran

**Record producer. British.**

Pat Moran was a British record producer, sound engineer, and former vocalist/keyboardist for the 1970s progressive rock band Spring. Based at Rockfield Studios in Monmouth, Wales, he became one of the most sought-after engineers and producers of his era. He produced Edie Brickell & New Bohemians' debut *Shooting Rubberbands at the Stars* (1988), which featured the hit "What I Am." His extensive credits also include work with Robert Plant (*Pictures at Eleven*), Queen (*A Night at the Opera*), Rush, Iggy Pop, Big Country, and Lou Gramm.
