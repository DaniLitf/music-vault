# Pelle Gunnerfeldt

**Record producer. Swedish.**

Pelle Gunnerfeldt is a Swedish guitarist and record producer, best known as the guitarist of the post-hardcore/alternative rock band Fireside, which formed in Luleå in the early 1990s. In addition to his work with Fireside, Gunnerfeldt has produced albums for several Swedish and international acts, bringing a dynamic, live-feeling approach to his productions.

Gunnerfeldt produced Entombed's *Inferno* (2003), lending his production sensibility to the Swedish death metal pioneers' later career. He continues to record and perform with Fireside, who released *Bin Juice* in 2022 after a lengthy hiatus.
