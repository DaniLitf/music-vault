# Paul Johnston

**Record producer and engineer. British.**

Paul Johnston is a British record producer and engineer who owns and operates Rhythm Studios near Stratford Upon Avon, Warwickshire. He produced Benediction's *Transcend the Rubicon* (1993) and has recorded numerous death metal, doom metal, and heavy metal albums.
