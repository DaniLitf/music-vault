# Paul Finch

**Record producer. British.**

Paul Finch is credited in The Featherz sources as producer of Seacroft Studios material later included on [[Artists/T/The Featherz]]'s *Five-Year-Itch*. His vault-relevant production credit is tied to the band's 2016 Live and Unsigned session material.
