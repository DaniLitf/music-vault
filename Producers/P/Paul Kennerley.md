# Paul Kennerley

**Record producer. English.**

Paul Kennerley is an English songwriter and record producer based in Nashville, Tennessee, known for his acclaimed Civil War concept albums *White Mansions* (1978) and *The Legend of Jesse James* (1980). His songs have been recorded by artists including Emmylou Harris, The Judds, Johnny Cash, and Waylon Jennings, and several appeared in major films such as *A Star Is Born* and *Thelma & Louise*.
