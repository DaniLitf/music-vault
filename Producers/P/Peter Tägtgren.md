# Peter Tägtgren

**Record producer and multi-instrumentalist. Swedish.**

Peter Tägtgren is a prolific Swedish record producer and musician best known for founding the death metal band Hypocrisy and the industrial metal project Pain. He established The Abyss, a premier recording studio in Pärlby, Sweden, where he has produced numerous Scandinavian and international metal bands including Amon Amarth, Amorphis, Celtic Frost, Dark Funeral, Dimmu Borgir, Immortal, Marduk, and Sabaton. His production work is characterized by precise technical recording and a deep understanding of heavy music aesthetics.
