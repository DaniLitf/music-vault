# Pat Collier

**Record producer. British.**

Pat Collier is a British producer and musician credited in The Featherz sources as producer of early material later included on [[Artists/T/The Featherz]]'s *Five-Year-Itch*. The sessions were recorded at Perry Vale Studios in 2012.
