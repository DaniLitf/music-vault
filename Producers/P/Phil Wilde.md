**Record producer. Belgian.**

Phil Wilde is a Belgian record producer best known as the co-creator, producer, and songwriter of the Eurodance project [[Artists/0-9/2 Unlimited]] alongside [[Producers/J/Jean-Paul De Coster]]. He co-wrote and produced the group's landmark albums and singles that defined the Eurodance genre in the 1990s, including the multi-platinum hit "No Limit". He has also worked with other dance acts on the labels he co-founded.
