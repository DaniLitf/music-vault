# Pat McCarthy

**Record producer, engineer, mixer. Irish.**

Pat McCarthy is an Irish record producer and engineer from Dublin. He engineered R.E.M.'s *Monster* (1994) and *New Adventures in Hi-Fi* (1996) before replacing Scott Litt as the band's in-house producer for *Up* (1998), *Reveal* (2001), and *Around the Sun* (2004). He mixed Madonna's *Ray of Light* (1998) and worked with Counting Crows, The Waterboys, and U2.
