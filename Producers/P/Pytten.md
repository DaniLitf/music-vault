# Pytten

**Record producer, engineer. Norwegian.**

Eirik "Pytten" Hundvin is a Norwegian record producer and engineer, best known for his work at Grieghallen Studio in Bergen, Norway. He produced Emperor's landmark black metal albums *In the Nightside Eclipse* (1994) and *Anthems to the Welkin at Dusk* (1997), and has worked extensively with other Norwegian black metal acts including Immortal, Mayhem, and Gorgoroth.
