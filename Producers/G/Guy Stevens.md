# Guy Stevens

**Record producer. British.**

Guy Stevens was a British producer and A&R man who signed and named [[Artists/M/Mott the Hoople]], producing their first four albums, and later produced The Clash's *London Calling* (1979). He died in 1981.

## Associated Projects
- [[Artists/M/Mott the Hoople]] – *Mott the Hoople* (1969), *Mad Shadows* (1970), *Wildlife* (1971), *Brain Capers* (1971)
