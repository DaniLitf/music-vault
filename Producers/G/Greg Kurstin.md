# Greg Kurstin

**Record producer. American.**

Greg Kurstin (born May 14, 1969) is an American record producer, multi-instrumentalist, and songwriter. A nine-time Grammy Award winner including Producer of the Year, Non-Classical in 2017 and 2018, he has produced and co-written hits for Adele ("Hello"), Kelly Clarkson ("Stronger"), Sia ("Chandelier"), and Foo Fighters. A classically trained jazz pianist, he brings meticulous musicianship to his pop and rock productions, often playing all instruments on the records he produces.
