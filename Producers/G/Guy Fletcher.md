# Guy Fletcher

**Record producer. English.**

Guy Fletcher is an English musician and record producer best known as a keyboard player in Dire Straits from 1984 until the band's dissolution, and as a core member of Mark Knopfler's solo band. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame as a member of Dire Straits in 2018, and has co-produced numerous Knopfler albums including *Get Lucky* and *One Deep River*.
