# Glyn Johns

**Record producer. English.**

Glyn Johns is a legendary English recording engineer and producer inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2012, whose work spans landmark albums by the Rolling Stones, the Who, Led Zeppelin, the Eagles, and Eric Clapton. He developed the widely used "Glyn Johns method" for recording drums and authored the acclaimed autobiography *Sound Man* (2014).
