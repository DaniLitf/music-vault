# Greg Reely

**Record producer, recording engineer, and mastering engineer. Canadian.**

Greg Reely is a Vancouver-based record producer, engineer, and mixer who began his career in the early 1980s as a live sound engineer for artists including Images in Vogue, Skinny Puppy, and 54-40. He became closely associated with Vancouver's Nettwerk Productions, producing and recording artists such as Sarah McLachlan, Front Line Assembly, and Delerium. Reely is particularly known for his work with industrial and electronic acts; he co-produced Fear Factory's *Demanufacture* (1995) and *Obsolete* (1998) alongside Rhys Fulber. In later years he has focused primarily on mixing and mastering, working on albums by Overkill, 3 Inches of Blood, and many others.
