# Gary Klein

**Record producer and songwriter. American.**

Gary Klein (born September 28, 1942, in Brooklyn, New York) is an American songwriter and Grammy-nominated record producer. He graduated from Long Island University with a Bachelor of Arts in music and began his career co-writing the 1962 international hit "Bobby's Girl." Klein became known for working across genres, producing crossover pop and country hits for Dolly Parton, Glen Campbell, Barbra Streisand, and Johnny Cash in the 1970s. He later transitioned to music publishing as an executive at EMI Music Publishing, where he spent 20 years.
