# Geir Bratland

**Record producer. Norwegian.**

Geir Bratland is a Norwegian keyboardist and producer, known for his contributions to the futurepop and symphonic black metal scenes. He was a member of Apoptygma Berzerk from 1995 to 2009, co-producing several of the band's most acclaimed albums including *7*, *Welcome to Earth*, and *Harmonizer*. Bratland's keyboard work and production contributions helped shape APB's transition from EBM into a more expansive electronic rock sound. After leaving APB, he joined Dimmu Borgir as a touring and session keyboardist, bringing his electronic production sensibilities to extreme metal.
