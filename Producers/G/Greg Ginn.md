# Greg Ginn

**Record producer. American.**

Greg Ginn is the guitarist and sole constant member of [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] — see [[Members/G/Greg Ginn]] for his career as a musician — who also self-produced the band's 2013 reunion album *What The...* and much of his own extensive solo and side-project output on his SST Records label.
