# George Massenburg

**Record producer. American.**

George Massenburg is an American record producer, audio engineer, and inventor, widely recognized for his pioneering work in recording technology, most notably the invention of the parametric equalizer. Born in Baltimore, Maryland, he founded GML (George Massenburg Labs), which manufactures high-end audio equipment. Over his decades-long career, he has worked across virtually every genre, earning multiple Grammy Awards for his engineering and production work. He produced Dolly Parton, Linda Ronstadt, and Emmylou Harris's landmark collaborative album *Trio* (1987), and has also worked with artists such as Earth, Wind & Fire, Little Feat, and Mary Chapin Carpenter.
