# Gareth Jones

**Record producer. British.**

Gareth Jones is a British record producer and sound engineer, renowned for his work with influential electronic, post-punk, and industrial artists. Based in Berlin, Jones has been a key figure in shaping the sound of European electronic music since the 1980s, with a production style characterized by meticulous layering, atmospheric textures, and innovative use of studio technology. His credits include landmark albums by Einstürzende Neubauten, Depeche Mode, and Wire, among many others. He produced Mesh's *We Collide* album, bringing his distinctive approach to the British synth-pop band's sound.
