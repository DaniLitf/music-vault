# Gil Norton

**Record producer. British.**

Gil Norton is an English record producer born in Liverpool, known for his work with alternative rock bands including Pixies, Echo & the Bunnymen, Foo Fighters, and Counting Crows. Norton achieved international recognition in the late 1980s and early 1990s through his collaborations with Pixies on their breakthrough albums. He began his career in the late 1970s interning at Amazon Studios in Liverpool and quickly rose to prominence in post-punk and new wave scenes. His production work is characterized by a polished yet powerful alternative rock sound.
