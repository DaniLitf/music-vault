# Greg James

**Record producer. American.**

Greg James is an American record producer based in Cleveland, Ohio. He co-produced the 1987 album *Cross and Black* by the heavy metal band Centurion, recorded at Clockwerke Sound Studio in Cleveland in October 1986.
