# Greg Fidelman

**Record producer. American.**

Greg Fidelman is an American record producer, mixer, and engineer — a former guitarist in the rock band Rhino Bucket — who first worked with [[Artists/M/Metallica]] as engineer and mixer on *Death Magnetic* (2008) under Rick Rubin, and has since become the band's regular producer, credited on *Hardwired... to Self-Destruct* (2016) and *72 Seasons* (2023). He has also engineered or produced albums for Adele, Slipknot, Black Sabbath, and Red Hot Chili Peppers, winning a Grammy for Album of the Year for Adele's *21*.
