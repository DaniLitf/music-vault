# George Clinton

**Producer, bandleader. American. Leader of [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]].**

Beyond leading Parliament-Funkadelic, George Clinton produced albums for other artists, most notably Red Hot Chili Peppers' second album *Freaky Styley* (1985), bringing his P-Funk sensibility to the young band's sound.

## Notable Productions

- Red Hot Chili Peppers – *Freaky Styley* (1985)
