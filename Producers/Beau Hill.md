# Beau Hill

**Record producer. American.**

Beau Hill is an American record producer best known for his work with 1980s glam metal and hard rock bands. He produced Ratt's most commercially successful albums including *Out of the Cellar*, *Invasion of Your Privacy*, *Dancing Undercover*, and *Reach for the Sky*, as well as albums by Warrant, Alice Cooper, and Twisted Sister.
