# Frank Zappa

**Record producer. American.**

Frank Zappa was an American musician, composer, and record producer who produced Grand Funk Railroad's *Good Singin', Good Playin'* (1976). The album received some of the best critical reviews of the band's career but was a commercial disappointment.

Frank Zappa is also a musician. See [[Members/F/Frank Zappa]] for biography.
