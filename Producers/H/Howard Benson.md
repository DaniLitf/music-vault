**Record producer and engineer. American.**

Howard Benson is a highly accomplished record producer and engineer who has worked with some of rock and alternative music's biggest acts. Beginning as a keyboardist in rock bands from age 13, Benson studied engineering at Drexel University while also pursuing composition at the Philadelphia College for Performing Arts. His production credits span pop-punk, alternative rock, heavy metal, and more, with multiple Grammy Award nominations for Producer of the Year, Non-Classical (2007, 2008) and 16 total Grammy nominations.
