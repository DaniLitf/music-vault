# Hi-Tek

**Record producer. American.**

Hi-Tek is a Cincinnati producer who rose to prominence through his work with Talib Kweli as the duo Reflection Eternal. Known for soulful, sample-driven boom bap, he also produced hits for G-Unit and 50 Cent, including tracks on *Beg for Mercy* (2003).
