# Hurt-M-Badd

**Record producer. American.**

Hurt-M-Badd was an in-house producer at Death Row Records who rose from obscurity to co-produce most of Tupac Shakur's *The Don Killuminati: The 7 Day Theory* (1996), recorded in a seven-day burst at Can-Am Studios. His credits on the album include "Hail Mary".
