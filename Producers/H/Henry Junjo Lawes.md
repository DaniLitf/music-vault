# Henry "Junjo" Lawes

**Record producer. Jamaican.**

Henry "Junjo" Lawes was a highly influential Jamaican record producer and sound engineer, widely regarded as the defining producer of early dancehall reggae. Born in the Waterhouse district of Kingston, he founded the Volcano label and sound system, employing the Roots Radics as his house band to create a raw, street-level sound that revolutionized reggae in the early 1980s. He produced three albums for Eek-A-Mouse — *Wa-Do-Dem* (1981), *Skidip!* (1982), and *Mouseketeer* (1984) — and also discovered and produced Barrington Levy, Yellowman, Frankie Paul, and Cocoa Tea. Lawes was tragically killed in a drive-by shooting in London in 1999.
