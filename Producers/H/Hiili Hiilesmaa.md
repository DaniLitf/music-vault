# Hiili Hiilesmaa

**Record producer. Finnish.**

Hiili Hiilesmaa (born Kai Hiilesmaa) is a Finnish record producer, musician, and engineer known for his long association with HIM and his work with a wide range of Finnish and international rock and metal acts. He began producing professionally in 1995 and is known for heavy, overdriven guitar tones and experimental synthesiser programming. He has expressed a preference for analog recording, saying it demands more attitude and faster decision-making.

In addition to production, Hiilesmaa has been active as a musician: he is the drummer of the doom metal band KYPCK, former keyboardist of Daniel Lioneye, and vocalist of The Skreppers. His production credits include landmark albums for HIM, Apocalyptica, Amorphis, Moonspell, and Sentenced.
