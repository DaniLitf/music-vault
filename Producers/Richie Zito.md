# Richie Zito

**Record producer. American.**

Richie Zito is an American record producer, songwriter, and guitarist who has worked with a wide range of artists across pop, rock, and metal. He produced Ratt's sixth studio album *Ratt* (1999) and has also worked with artists such as Cheap Trick, Eddie Money, and Heart.
