# Keith LeBlanc

**Record producer and drummer. American.**

Keith LeBlanc (1954–2024) was an American drummer and record producer best known for bridging hip-hop, industrial, and electronic music. He got his start as the session drummer at Sugar Hill Records in the early 1980s, recording with Grandmaster Flash and the Furious Five and the Sugarhill Gang, before releasing "No Sell Out" (1983) — one of the first sample-based records. His production work crossed into industrial territory with Nine Inch Nails' *Pretty Hate Machine* (1989) and Ministry's *Twitch* (1986). He was a member of the industrial/hip-hop outfit Tackhead alongside [[Producers/A/Adrian Sherwood]].
