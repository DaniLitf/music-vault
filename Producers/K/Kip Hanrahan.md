# Kip Hanrahan

**Record producer, composer, percussionist. American.**

Kip Hanrahan is an American impresario, record producer, and percussionist, best known as founder of the American Clavé label and for his unconventional "film director" approach to assembling recording projects. He produced three of [[Artists/A/Astor Piazzolla]]'s most acclaimed late-career albums — *Tango: Zero Hour*, *The Rough Dancer and the Cyclical Night*, and *La Camorra* — recording Piazzolla's second Quintet in New York City during the mid-to-late 1980s. Beyond Piazzolla, Hanrahan has curated genre-blending sessions featuring avant-garde jazz, Latin, rock, and spoken-word artists including Jack Bruce, Sting, David Murray, Don Pullen, Taj Mahal, and poet Ishmael Reed (the Conjure series). He is also a musician; see [[Members/K/Kip Hanrahan]].
