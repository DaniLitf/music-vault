# Ken Scott

**Record producer. British.**

Ken Scott is a British record producer and engineer who co-produced David Bowie's pivotal early-1970s run — *Hunky Dory*, *The Rise and Fall of Ziggy Stardust and the Spiders from Mars*, *Aladdin Sane*, and *Pin Ups* — after starting his career as an engineer on several Beatles albums. He also produced Supertramp's most successful records.
