# Kent Wells

**Record producer. American.**

Kent Wells is an American musician and record producer, best known as a longtime guitarist and co-producer for Dolly Parton. He has been a member of Parton's touring band and has contributed to numerous of her albums over several decades. Wells co-produced Parton's 2023 rock album *Rockstar*, a star-studded collection of rock covers and originals featuring collaborations with numerous rock legends. His production work is characterized by a polished, versatile approach that bridges country, rock, and pop.
