# Kevin Armstrong

**Record producer. British.**

Kevin Armstrong is a British guitarist and producer who worked with [[Artists/T/The Featherz]] during 2014 sessions at Broadoak Studios. He is also documented elsewhere as a guitarist associated with artists including David Bowie, Iggy Pop, and Morrissey; see [[Members/K/Kevin Armstrong]] for his musician page.
