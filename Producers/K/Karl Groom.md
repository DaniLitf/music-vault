# Karl Groom

**Record producer. British.**

Karl Groom is a British guitarist and record producer, best known as the founding and only constant member of the progressive metal band Threshold, which he formed in 1988. He owns and operates Thin Ice Studios in Surrey, England, where he has produced and engineered albums for a wide range of progressive and power metal acts. Groom produced DragonForce's first two albums, *Valley of the Damned* (2003) and *Sonic Firestorm* (2004), which helped define the extreme power metal sound. He has also produced for Pendragon, Yes (post-Chris Squire material), Galahad, and Intense, among many others.
