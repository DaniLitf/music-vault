# Kristian Kohlmannslehner

**Record producer, engineer. German.**

Kristian "Kohle" Kohlmannslehner (born February 16, 1978) is a German record producer, mixing and mastering engineer, and owner of Kohlekeller Studio in Seeheim, Germany. He has produced, mixed, or mastered albums for Electric Callboy, Powerwolf, Aborted, Crematory, Kanonenfieber, and many other metal and hardcore bands over a career spanning more than two decades.
