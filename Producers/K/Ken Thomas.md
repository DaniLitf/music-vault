# Ken Thomas

**Record producer, engineer. British.**

Ken Thomas is a British producer and engineer whose career stretches from 1970s sessions at Trident Studios to defining work with atmospheric artists such as Sigur Rós, The Sugarcubes, [[Artists/C/Cocteau Twins]], and Suede. Known for capturing expansive, texture-rich soundscapes, he co-produced [[Artists/M/M83]]'s *Saturdays = Youth* (2008) with Ewan Pearson and Morgan Kibby.
