# Kyle Crusham

**Record producer. American.**

Kyle Crusham is an American record producer, engineer, composer, and musician based in Austin, Texas. He began his career at Tequila Mockingbird Studios, where he composed and engineered music for television and radio, before founding Crusham Music. He produced and co-wrote Edie Brickell & New Bohemians' albums *Rocket* (2018) and *Hunter and the Dog Star* (2021) at Arlyn Studios. Crusham has also worked with Charlie Sexton, Christopher Cross, Paul Simon, Ben Harper, and The Dixie Chicks, and has contributed music to films such as *Talladega Nights*, *Step Brothers*, and *Boyhood*.
