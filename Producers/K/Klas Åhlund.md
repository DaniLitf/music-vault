# Klas Åhlund

**Record producer. Swedish.**

Klas Åhlund (born April 11, 1972) is a Swedish songwriter, record producer, and guitarist. A member of the band Teddybears, he has produced and co-written for a wide range of international artists including Robyn, Ghost, Katy Perry, Kesha, the Weeknd, and Britney Spears. He produced Ghost's albums *Meliora* (2015) and *Impera* (2022), and has won multiple Grammy Awards for his production work.
