# Keith Cleversley

**Record producer, engineer, and mixer. American.**

Keith Cleversley is an American record producer and audio engineer best known for his work with The Flaming Lips, Mercury Rev, Hum, Spiritualized, and The Posies. He co-produced Duraluxe's debut album *Dolorosa* (1999) alongside Chris Colbert and Troy Daugherty at his Chicago studio The Playground. Cleversley's production style blends psychedelic textures with clear, powerful rock arrangements, and he was a key figure in the 1990s alternative rock production scene. In the 2010s he shifted focus to his Zen practice, options trading, and running WaveFront Mastering in California.

## Associated Projects

- Mercury Rev – *Yerself Is Steam* (1991)

- The Posies – *Amazing Disgrace* (1996)
