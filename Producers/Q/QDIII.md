# QDIII

**Record producer. Swedish-American.**

QDIII (Quincy Jones III), son of Quincy Jones, is a producer and film-maker who worked with Tupac Shakur from the mid-1990s onward. He produced on *The Don Killuminati: The 7 Day Theory* (1996) and on several posthumous Tupac albums, and later produced the documentary *Tupac: Resurrection*.
