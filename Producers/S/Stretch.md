# Stretch

**Record producer, rapper. American.**

Stretch (Randy Walker) was a rapper and producer from Queens, New York, and a member of the duo Live Squad. A close early collaborator of Tupac Shakur, he produced and guested on Tupac's first two albums before the pair fell out; he was murdered in a drive-by shooting in 1995. He is also a musician — one half of Live Squad.
