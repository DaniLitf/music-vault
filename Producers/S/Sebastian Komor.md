# Sebastian Komor

**Record producer. Norwegian.**

Sebastian Komor is a Norwegian electronic musician and producer, known for his work in the futurepop and EBM scenes. He joined Icon of Coil in 1998 as their second member, contributing keyboards, production, and electronic programming to the band's signature sound. Komor has been instrumental in shaping the melodic yet driving production style of multiple albums across the Norwegian electronic scene. He is also a member of the Bruderschaft collaboration project, connecting artists from across the futurepop and industrial genres.
