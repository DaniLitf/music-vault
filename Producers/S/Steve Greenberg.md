# Steve Greenberg

**Record producer, A&R executive. American.**

Steve Greenberg is an American record producer and A&R executive who discovered and signed the Jonas Brothers to Columbia Records. He produced their debut album *It's About Time* and has worked with artists including Joss Stone, Hanson, and the Baha Men. He previously served as president of Columbia Records and later founded S-Curve Records.
