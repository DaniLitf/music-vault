# Spot

**Record producer. American.**

Spot (Glen Lockett) was an American record producer and engineer, the de facto house producer for SST Records from 1979 to 1985. He produced or engineered defining hardcore and post-hardcore records by Black Flag, Minutemen, Hüsker Dü, Meat Puppets, and the Descendents, including the *Fat EP* (1981) and *Milo Goes to College* (1982). He died on March 4, 2023.
