# Steve Lillywhite

**Record producer. British.**

Steve Lillywhite is an English record producer credited on over 500 records since beginning his career in 1977. He is best known for producing U2's early run of albums — *Boy* (1980), *October* (1981), and *War* (1983) — and later returned to co-produce [[Artists/0-9/30 Seconds to Mars|30 Seconds to Mars]]'s *This Is War* (2009) alongside [[Producers/F/Flood]] and *Love, Lust, Faith and Dreams* (2013) with Jared Leto. His broad discography also includes contributions to the Rolling Stones' *Dirty Work* (1986), Dave Matthews Band, and The Killers.
