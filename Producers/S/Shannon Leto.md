# Shannon Leto

**Record producer. American.**

Shannon Leto is the drummer of [[Artists/0-9/30 Seconds to Mars|30 Seconds to Mars]] — see [[Members/S/Shannon Leto]] for full biography. He stepped into a co-producer role alongside his brother [[Producers/J/Jared Leto]] on the band's sixth studio album, *It's the End of the World but It's a Beautiful Day* (2023).
