# Steve Buckingham

**Record producer. American.**

Steve Buckingham is an American record producer, guitarist, and songwriter, best known for his extensive work in bluegrass and country music. A native of Kentucky, he began his career as a session guitarist before moving into production, where he became a key collaborator with Dolly Parton. He produced Parton's acclaimed bluegrass albums *The Grass Is Blue* (1999) and *Little Sparrow* (2001), both of which revitalized her career and earned widespread critical praise. Buckingham has also produced for artists such as The Whites and numerous other Nashville acts.
