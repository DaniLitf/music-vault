# Steve Albini

**Record producer (recording engineer). American.**

Steve Albini was an American recording engineer and musician famed for his raw, naturalistic recording approach and his refusal of the "producer" title and royalty points. He founded Electrical Audio studio in Chicago and engineered thousands of records, including landmarks like Nirvana's *In Utero* and Pixies' *Surfer Rosa*. He was also a musician in his own right — see [[Members/S/Steve Albini]] — fronting Big Black and Shellac.
