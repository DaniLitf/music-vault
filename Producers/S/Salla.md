# Salla

**Record producer. Swedish.**

Salla is a Swedish-Chilean DJ and producer, also documented as [[Members/S/Salla]], best known for his production work with [[Artists/T/The Latin Kings]]. Alongside Chepe, he produced much of the group's catalogue after the debut period.
