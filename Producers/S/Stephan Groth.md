# Stephan Groth

**Record producer. Norwegian.**

Stephan Groth is a Norwegian musician and producer, best known as the founder, vocalist, and primary songwriter of Apoptygma Berzerk. Born in Sarpsborg, Norway, he has been the driving creative force behind APB since founding the band in 1989, handling most production duties across their discography. Groth's production style is characterized by layered synthesizers, cinematic atmospheres, and driving EBM rhythms, helping to define the futurepop genre. He has also produced material for related projects and continues to lead Apoptygma Berzerk's evolving sound.
