# Scott Storch

**Record producer. American.**

Scott Storch is an American producer and keyboardist who began as a member of The Roots before becoming one of the most commercially successful hitmakers of the 2000s. His keyboard-driven productions powered hits for Dr. Dre, 50 Cent, Beyoncé, and Fat Joe, and he contributed production to G-Unit's *Beg for Mercy* (2003).
