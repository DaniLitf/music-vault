# Swizz Beatz

**Record producer. American.**

Swizz Beatz is a New York producer who rose to fame through Ruff Ryders and DMX in the late 1990s and became one of hip-hop's most in-demand hitmakers. He contributed production to Tupac Shakur's posthumous album *Pac's Life* (2006).
