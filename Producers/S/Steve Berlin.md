# Steve Berlin

**Record producer, saxophonist, and multi-instrumentalist. American. Born 14 September 1955 in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania.**

Steve Berlin is an American saxophonist, keyboardist, and record producer best known as a member of Los Lobos, having officially joined the band in 1984 after co-producing their EP *...And a Time to Dance*. Before Los Lobos, he played with the Blasters, Top Jimmy & the Rhythm Pigs, and the Flesh Eaters. As a producer, Berlin has worked with a diverse range of artists including Faith No More, the Replacements, Dave Alvin, Sheryl Crow, John Lee Hooker, and the Tragically Hip. He has won multiple Grammy Awards for his work with Los Lobos, Buckwheat Zydeco, Ozomatli, and John Lee Hooker. See also [[Members/S/Steve Berlin]].
