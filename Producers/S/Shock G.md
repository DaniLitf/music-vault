# Shock G

**Record producer. American.**

Shock G was the frontman of Digital Underground and a producer who mentored Tupac Shakur early in his career, co-producing his debut *2Pacalypse Now* (1991) as part of the Underground Railroad production team and producing the hit single "I Get Around". He is also a musician — Digital Underground's lead performer under multiple alter egos, most famously Humpty Hump.
