# Stephen Egerton

**Record producer. American.**

Stephen Egerton is an American record producer and engineer, better known as the guitarist of Descendents and All — see [[Members/S/Stephen Egerton]] for his career as a musician. He co-produced and engineered [[Artists/D/Descendents]] albums with Bill Stevenson, and operates Armstrong Recording studio in Tulsa, Oklahoma.
