# Jean-Benoît Dunckel

**Record producer. French.**

Jean-Benoît Dunckel is a French musician and producer who, together with [[Members/N/Nicolas Godin]], self-produced all of the studio albums by his duo [[Artists/A/Air]], co-producing two of them (*Talkie Walkie*, *Pocket Symphony*) alongside Nigel Godrich. See also [[Members/J/Jean-Benoît Dunckel]] for his biography as a performing musician.
