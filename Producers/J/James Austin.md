# James Austin

**Record producer. American.**

James Austin is an American music executive and compilation producer at Rhino Records, described by Emmylou Harris as "the world's best and most devoted music archeologist." He co-produced the compilation *The Complete Trio Collection* with Harris and unearthed the archived tapes that became *Ramble in Music City: The Lost Concert*, which he also co-produced.
