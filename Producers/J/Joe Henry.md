# Joe Henry

**Record producer. American.**

Joe Henry is an American singer-songwriter, guitarist, and record producer who has won three Grammy Awards for albums he produced, including Solomon Burke's *Don't Give Up on Me* and Carolina Chocolate Drops' *Genuine Negro Jig*. He has produced albums for Bonnie Raitt, Elvis Costello, Joan Baez, and Allen Toussaint, and was honored with a Lifetime Achievement Award at the 2025 Americana Music Honors & Awards.
