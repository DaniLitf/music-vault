# John Fryer

**Record producer and mixing engineer. British.**

John Fryer is a British record producer who emerged from London's post-punk and new wave scene in the early 1980s, working initially as an engineer at Blackwing Studios where he collaborated with Depeche Mode and Fad Gadget. He is best known for his production work on acts spanning goth, industrial, and alternative rock — including Nine Inch Nails' *Pretty Hate Machine* (1989), the Cocteau Twins, and Love and Rockets. He also performs as a member of the ambient/electronic project This Mortal Coil.
