# Jack Dangers

**Record producer, electronic musician. British.**

Jack Dangers is an English electronic musician, DJ, and record producer best known as the founding member and primary creative force behind Meat Beat Manifesto. As a producer and remixer, he has worked with or reworked material for David Bowie, Nine Inch Nails, David Byrne, [[Artists/D/DJ Spooky]], Merzbow, Depeche Mode, and many others. He is also a musician; see [[Members/J/Jack Dangers]].
