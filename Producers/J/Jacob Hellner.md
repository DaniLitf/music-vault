# Jacob Hellner

**Record producer and composer. Swedish.**

Jacob Hellner is a Swedish record producer, songwriter, and composer best known for his extensive work with Rammstein, which he produced from 1995 to 2017. He produced the band's first six studio albums, establishing their signature industrial metal sound and working as the essential orchestrator behind their arrangement and production aesthetic.
