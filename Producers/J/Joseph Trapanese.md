# Joseph Trapanese

**Composer, record producer. American.**

Joseph Trapanese is an American composer, arranger, and producer known for orchestral-electronic hybrid film and TV scores, including collaborations with Daft Punk (*Tron: Legacy* arrangements) and scores for *Straight Outta Compton* and *The Greatest Showman*. He co-wrote and co-produced the *Oblivion* soundtrack (2013) with [[Artists/M/M83]]'s Anthony Gonzalez.
