# Joe Hardy

**Record producer. American.**

Joe Hardy (July 27, 1952 – February 12, 2019) was an American record producer, engineer, and musician based in Memphis, Tennessee. He spent more than two decades at Ardent Studios, where he worked with ZZ Top, Alice Cooper, the Georgia Satellites, the Replacements, and Steve Earle, among many others. Known for his traditional, roots-oriented production style, he was also a multi-instrumentalist who frequently contributed to the records he produced.
