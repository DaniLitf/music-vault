# Jimmy Page

**Record producer. British.**

Jimmy Page is an English guitarist and producer who produced every Led Zeppelin album, defining much of the sound of 1970s hard rock through his recording and mic-placement techniques. He co-produced *Coverdale–Page* (1993) with David Coverdale and Mike Fraser. He is primarily a musician — see [[Members/J/Jimmy Page]] for biography.
