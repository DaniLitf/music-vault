# John Fields

**Record producer, multi-instrumentalist. American.**

John Fields is an American record producer and multi-instrumentalist known for his work with Jimmy Eat World, the Donnas, Miley Cyrus, and Selena Gomez. He produced The Dead Daisies' self-titled debut album and also performed on it as a session musician.
