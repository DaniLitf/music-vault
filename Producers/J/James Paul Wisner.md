# James Paul Wisner

**Record producer. American.**

James Paul Wisner is an American record producer, engineer and mixer who runs Wisner Productions, originally in Davie, Florida and later relocated to St. Cloud, Florida. He is a long-standing collaborator of [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]], producing the band from its 1996 demo through *Won* (2001) and mixing its 2020 comeback EP, and is known more broadly for shaping Florida punk, emo and post-hardcore records.
