# Johnny J

**Record producer. American.**

Johnny "J" was Tupac Shakur's most prolific producer, responsible for large parts of *All Eyez on Me* (1996) — including "How Do U Want It" and "All Bout U" — and for much of the vault material that formed Tupac's posthumous albums. His work with Tupac earned him multiple platinum certifications.
