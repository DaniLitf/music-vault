# Joe McGrath

**Record producer. American.**

Joe McGrath is an American recording engineer and producer who worked for years as one of [[Producers/J/Jerry Finn|Jerry Finn]]'s main engineers, handling sessions for Blink-182 and [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio|Alkaline Trio]] at Cello Studios in Hollywood. He later co-produced AFI's *Crash Love* (2009) alongside Jacknife Lee.
