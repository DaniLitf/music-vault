# Jeremy Staska

**Record producer and engineer. American.**

Jeremy Staska is an American producer and engineer who runs Studio 13 in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, and became a go-to producer for the 1990s South Florida hardcore and metalcore scene. His work is credited with capturing the signature "Florida hardcore sound", including Bird of Ill Omen's final EP, recorded at Studio 13 in November 1998.
