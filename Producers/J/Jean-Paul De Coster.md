**Record producer. Belgian.**

Jean-Paul De Coster is a Belgian record producer best known as the co-creator, producer, and songwriter of the Eurodance project [[Artists/0-9/2 Unlimited]] alongside [[Producers/P/Phil Wilde]]. He co-wrote and produced all of the group's major hits, including "No Limit", "Get Ready for This", and "Twilight Zone", which defined the Dutch Eurodance sound of the early 1990s. He also produced for other acts under his various record labels.
