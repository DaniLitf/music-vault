# Jack Endino

**Record producer and engineer. American.**

Jack Endino is a legendary Seattle-based engineer and producer best known for his work with Sub Pop Records and the grunge movement of the late 1980s and early 1990s. He recorded Nirvana's raw, influential debut album *Bleach* in just 30 hours on a limited budget of $606.17, helping define the sound of early grunge. Endino also produced albums for Soundgarden, Mudhoney, and other seminal Seattle bands, and served as guitarist for the post-hardcore band [[Artists/S/Skin Yard]].
