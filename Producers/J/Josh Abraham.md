# Josh Abraham

**Record producer. American.**

Josh Abraham is an American record producer, songwriter, and music executive whose career, beginning in the mid-1990s, spans rock, nu metal, and pop. He produced [[Artists/0-9/30 Seconds to Mars|30 Seconds to Mars]]'s breakthrough album *A Beautiful Lie* (2005), as well as Staind's *14 Shades of Grey* (2003), Velvet Revolver's *Contraband* (2004), and Slayer's *Christ Illusion* (2006). In 2004 he founded Pulse Recording in Los Angeles, on the site of the former Soundcastle Recording Studios.
