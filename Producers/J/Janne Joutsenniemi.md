# Janne Joutsenniemi

**Record producer. Finnish.**

Janne Joutsenniemi is a Finnish musician, songwriter, and record producer best known as the bassist of the alternative metal band Suburban Tribe and the groove metal band Stone. He has produced and engineered numerous albums for Finnish metal acts, including multiple Ensiferum records and several Stam1na studio albums that achieved gold certification in Finland.

Joutsenniemi began his career as a bassist in the early 1990s and transitioned into production and engineering, earning a reputation for technical expertise in the Finnish heavy metal scene. He has also contributed as a session musician and backing vocalist for several bands.
