# Jay Joyce

**Record producer. American.**

Jay Joyce is an American record producer, songwriter, and session musician based in Nashville, known for his work with Eric Church, Cage the Elephant, Little Big Town, and Miranda Lambert among many others. He has won multiple CMA and ACM Awards including Producer of the Year, and his production credits span rock, country, and pop.
