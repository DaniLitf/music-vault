# Jared Leto

**Record producer. American.**

Jared Leto is the frontman of [[Artists/0-9/30 Seconds to Mars|30 Seconds to Mars]] — see [[Members/J/Jared Leto]] for full biography. Starting with *Love, Lust, Faith and Dreams* (2013), he took over as the band's primary producer, a role he has retained through *America* (2018) and *It's the End of the World but It's a Beautiful Day* (2023, co-produced with [[Producers/S/Shannon Leto]]).
