# James Rushent

**Record producer. British.**

James Rushent is a British musician and producer, best known as the frontman of the dance-punk band Does It Offend You, Yeah? He co-produced The Prodigy's singles "Omen" and "Invaders Must Die" from the 2009 album *Invaders Must Die*, and has remixed tracks for Muse, The Raconteurs, and Bloc Party. He is the son of producer Martin Rushent.
