**Record producer. Belgian.**

Jo Bogaert is a Belgian record producer, songwriter, and musician best known as the creator and producer of the Eurodance project [[Artists/T/Technotronic|Technotronic]]. Working under the alias Thomas De Quincey, he produced all three Technotronic studio albums — *Pump Up the Jam* (1989), *Body to Body* (1991), and *Recall* (1995) — and crafted the signature hip house sound that produced worldwide hits including "Pump Up the Jam" and "Get Up! (Before the Night Is Over)".

Before Technotronic, Bogaert was active in the Belgian new beat scene with projects including Acts of Madmen and Nux Nemo. He is also a musician in his own right; see [[Members/J/Jo Bogaert]] for his biography.
