# J. Robbins

**Record producer. American.**

J. Robbins is an American musician and record producer, best known as the frontman of the post-hardcore bands Jawbox and Burning Airlines, and as the operator of The Magpie Cage recording studio in Baltimore, Maryland. He produced [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]]'s third album, *Searching for a Former Clarity* (2005), and has also worked with numerous other punk and post-hardcore acts.
