# John Hampton

**Record producer. American.**

John Hampton was an American record producer and engineer who worked for nearly four decades at Ardent Studios in Memphis, Tennessee, eventually becoming a part-owner. He engineered albums including the Replacements' *Pleased to Meet Me* (1987) and R.E.M.'s *Green* (1988), produced the Gin Blossoms' multi-platinum *New Miserable Experience* (1992), and won Grammy Awards for Jimmie Vaughan's *Do You Get the Blues?* (2001) and the White Stripes' *Get Behind Me Satan* (2005).
