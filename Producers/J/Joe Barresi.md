# Joe Barresi

**Record producer. American.**

Joe Barresi, nicknamed "Evil Joe," is an American record producer and engineer from New York City who studied music engineering at the University of Miami. He has worked extensively across rock and metal, engineering and mixing albums for Tool and producing or mixing releases by Queens of the Stone Age, Melvins, Bad Religion, Nine Inch Nails, and Slipknot, among others, and produced Avenged Sevenfold's *The Stage* (2016) and *Life Is But a Dream...* (2023).
