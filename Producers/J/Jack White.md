# Jack White

**Record producer. American.**

Jack White is an American musician and producer best known as the co-founder of The White Stripes, whose stripped-down blues-punk production style he has carried into his work producing other artists. He co-produced *Four Stars* (2010) for [[Artists/T/The Greenhornes]], a band that also backed his Loretta Lynn production *Van Lear Rose* and shares members with his own project [[Artists/T/The Raconteurs]]. He is also a musician — see [[Members/J/Jack White]] for biography.
