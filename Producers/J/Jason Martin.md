**Record producer and musician. American.**

Jason Martin is a record producer and musician who has self-produced virtually all of his band Starflyer 59's albums from *Old* (2003) onward, as well as the earliest albums *Silver* (1994) and *Gold* (1995). He also produces music for his side projects. See [[Members/J/Jason Martin]].
