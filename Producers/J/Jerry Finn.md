# Jerry Finn

**Record producer. American.**

Jerry Finn was an American record producer and mix engineer who became the defining sound of second-wave pop-punk in the late 1990s and early 2000s. He engineered and mixed Green Day's *Dookie* (1994) alongside Rob Cavallo before forging a long partnership with Blink-182, producing four of their albums beginning with *Enema of the State* (1999), and worked with AFI, Sum 41, Rancid, and [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio|Alkaline Trio]]. He died in 2008 following a brain hemorrhage.
