# Jason Livermore

**Record producer. American.**

Jason Livermore is an American record producer and engineer who moved to Fort Collins, Colorado in 1994 to work at the newly opened Blasting Room studio, of which he became co-owner in 2015 alongside [[Producers/B/Bill Stevenson|Bill Stevenson]]. Specializing in punk and melodic hardcore, he has engineered or produced the large majority of the studio's output, including a Latin Grammy-winning record in 2023, and co-produced [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio|Alkaline Trio]]'s *My Shame Is True* (2013).
