# Jon Caffery

**Record producer. British.**

Jon Caffery is a British record producer and sound engineer who has been a key figure in German post-punk and industrial music since relocating to Germany in 1983. He began his career at Gooseberry Studios in London in the late 1970s, engineering for punk and reggae acts, and worked with Martin Hannett on Joy Division's *Closer* (1980) at Britannia Row Studios. In Germany, he produced landmark albums for Einstürzende Neubauten (*Tabula Rasa* 1993, *Ende Neu* 1996), as well as for Die Toten Hosen, Abwärts, and Raymond Watts' project Pig (*Praise the Lard* 1991). His production style is characterized by raw, atmospheric textures suited to experimental and punk music.
