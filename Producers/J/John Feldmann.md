# John Feldmann

**Record producer. American.**

John Feldmann is an American record producer, songwriter, and the lead vocalist/guitarist of ska-punk band Goldfinger. As a producer he became a dominant force in 2000s–2010s pop-punk and post-hardcore, working with The Used, Good Charlotte, All Time Low, and 5 Seconds of Summer, and produced [[Artists/B/Blink-182]]'s comeback albums *California* (2016) — the band's first without Jerry Finn — and *Nine* (2019).
