# Justin Meldal-Johnsen

**Record producer, bassist. American.**

Justin Meldal-Johnsen is an American producer and bassist who moved from an in-demand session career (Beck, Air, Nine Inch Nails) into full-time production, known for punchy, detail-rich modern rock and synth-pop records for artists such as Paramore and Jimmy Eat World. He co-produced [[Artists/M/M83]]'s *Hurry Up, We're Dreaming*, *Junk*, and *Fantasy* with Anthony Gonzalez. See also [[Members/J/Justin Meldal-Johnsen]].
