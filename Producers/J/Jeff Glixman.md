# Jeff Glixman

**Record producer. American.**

Jeff Glixman is an American record producer who has worked with Georgia Satellites, Kansas, Black Sabbath, Gary Moore, and Yngwie Malmsteen, among many others. He is perhaps best known for producing Kansas's landmark albums *Leftoverture* (1976) and *Point of Know Return* (1977), as well as Georgia Satellites' self-titled 1986 breakthrough. He is Executive Vice President of StarCity Recording Company and has overseen numerous surround-sound remix projects.
