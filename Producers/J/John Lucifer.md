# John Lucifer

**Record producer. British.**

John Lucifer is credited in The Featherz sources as producer, compiler, funder, and recorder for material included on [[Artists/T/The Featherz]]'s album *Five-Year-Itch*. He worked with the band at Quadra Studios and also recorded live material used on the album.
