# Jeff "Critter" Newell

**Record producer, audio engineer. American.**

Jeff "Critter" Newell was an American audio engineer and producer who cut his teeth at Chicago Trax, working on industrial records for the likes of Ministry, Skinny Puppy, and Nine Inch Nails, before relocating to California. He became the longtime engineer and co-producer for [[Artists/B/Blink-182|Blink-182]] and [[Artists/A/Angels & Airwaves|Angels & Airwaves]], contributing to every Angels & Airwaves album up to his death in 2011 and earning the nickname the band's "fifth member."
