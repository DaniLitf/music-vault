# Ivan Iusco

**Record producer. Italian.**

Ivan Iusco is an Italian electronic musician, composer, and producer, known for his long-running collaboration with Belgian EBM artist Dirk Ivens. Based in Italy, Iusco runs the Minus Habens Records label and has been a key collaborator on several Dive albums since the mid-1990s. His production contributions add melodic and atmospheric dimensions to Dive's otherwise harsh and abrasive sound, creating a productive contrast that has defined the project's later work. Iusco has also released solo material and worked across various electronic music genres.
