# Emory Gordy Jr.

**Record producer. American.**

Emory Gordy Jr. is an American bassist, songwriter, and record producer who was a member of Emmylou Harris's Hot Band in the mid-1970s and later became a staff producer at MCA Records Nashville. He co-produced Steve Earle's seminal albums *Guitar Town* and *Exit 0*, George Jones's *Walls Can Fall*, and won the first Grammy for Best Bluegrass Album for Bill Monroe's *Southern Flavor*.
