# Ewan Pearson

**Record producer, remixer, DJ. British.**

Ewan Pearson is a British electronic producer, DJ, and remixer based for many years in Berlin, known for elegant, dance-literate production and remixes for acts such as Depeche Mode, Goldfrapp, and The Rapture. He has produced for Tracey Thorn and [[Artists/L/Ladytron]], and co-produced [[Artists/M/M83]]'s *Saturdays = Youth* (2008).
