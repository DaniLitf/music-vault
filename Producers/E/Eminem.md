**Record producer. American.**

Eminem is a multi-platinum rapper and record producer who discovered 50 Cent and executive produced his debut album *Get Rich or Die Tryin'* (2003) through Shady Records. He is also a musician — see [[Members/E/Eminem]] for biography.
