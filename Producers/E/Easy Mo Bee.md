# Easy Mo Bee

**Record producer. American.**

Easy Mo Bee is a Brooklyn hip-hop producer best known for his work with the Notorious B.I.G., Miles Davis, and Tupac Shakur. He produced several tracks on Tupac's *Me Against the World* (1995), including "Temptations" and "If I Die 2Nite".
