# Eric Thorngren

**Record producer, mixing engineer. American.**

Eric Thorngren is an American record producer and mixing engineer who worked with [[Artists/T/Talk Heads]], [[Artists/D/David Bowie]], and [[Artists/R/Robert Palmer]]. He co-produced Talking Heads' final album *Naked* (1988) alongside the band and Steve Lillywhite.
