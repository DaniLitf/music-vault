# E.T. Thorngren

**Record producer, engineer, mixer. American.**

Eric "E.T." Thorngren (1949 – May 6, 2024) was an American recording engineer, producer, and mixer whose career began at Sugar Hill Records, where he worked with Grandmaster Flash and the Sugarhill Gang. He is best known for his long collaboration with Talking Heads, engineering and mixing *Little Creatures* (1985), *True Stories* (1986), and the *Stop Making Sense* soundtrack. He also worked with Robert Palmer, Eurythmics, Violent Femmes, Cyndi Lauper, Grace Jones, and many others. He mixed the Bob Marley compilation *Legend* (1984) and later remixed the Talking Heads catalog for 5.1 and Dolby Atmos.
