# Jimmy Ienner

**Record producer. American.**

Jimmy Ienner is an American record producer who produced Grand Funk Railroad's *All the Girls in the World Beware!!!* (1974) and *Born to Die* (1976), which featured the hits "Some Kind of Wonderful" and "Bad Time." He also produced the soundtrack to the animated film *The Chipmunk Adventure* and worked with artists including The Raspberries.
