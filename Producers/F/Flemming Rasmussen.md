# Flemming Rasmussen

**Record producer. Danish.**

Flemming Rasmussen is a Danish sound engineer and producer who owned Sweet Silence Studios in Copenhagen, where he produced [[Artists/M/Metallica]]'s *Ride the Lightning* (1984), *Master of Puppets* (1986), and *...And Justice for All* (1988) — the trio of albums that established the band's sound and reputation as thrash pioneers. He won a Grammy for engineering Metallica's "One" in 1989 and has also worked with Rainbow and numerous Danish artists.
