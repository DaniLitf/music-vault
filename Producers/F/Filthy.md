# Filthy

**Record producer. Swedish.**

Filthy (real name Magnus Lidehäll) is a Swedish record producer and songwriter, best known as the production half of the Swedish hip-hop duo Afasi & Filthy. He has also achieved international success writing and producing for artists including Kylie Minogue, Britney Spears, Sky Ferreira, Lena Philipsson, and Madonna. He is also a musician — see [[Members/F/Filthy]] for his full biography and band involvement.
