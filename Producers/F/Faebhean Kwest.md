# Faebhean Kwest

**Record producer. British.**

Faebhean Kwest is credited in The Featherz sources as a producer/collaborator on later recordings connected to [[Artists/T/The Featherz]]'s *Five-Year-Itch* period. Public sources also associate the name with Cuddly Toys.
