# Flood

**Record producer and mixing engineer. British.**

Flood (born Mark Ellis) is one of the most influential record producers to emerge from the UK's post-punk and electronic scene. He began his career as a studio engineer in London in the early 1980s, working with acts like Nick Cave, Depeche Mode, and Nitzer Ebb through Mute Records, before becoming a sought-after full producer known for his ability to sculpt dense, layered sonic environments. His work spans industrial rock, synth-pop, alternative rock, and gothic post-punk, with landmark productions including Depeche Mode's *Violator* (1990), Nine Inch Nails' *The Downward Spiral* (1994), and three Smashing Pumpkins albums. He co-founded the Assault & Battery Studios complex in London with longtime collaborator [[Producers/A/Alan Moulder]].
