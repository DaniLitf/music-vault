# Oscar Nilsson

**Record producer. Swedish.**

Oscar Nilsson is the owner and in-house producer of Crehate Studios in Mölndal, Sweden. He co-produced both studio albums by [[Artists/T/The Halo Effect]], becoming a close collaborator of guitarist Niclas Engelin.
