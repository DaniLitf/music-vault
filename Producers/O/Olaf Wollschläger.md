# Olaf Wollschläger

**Record producer. German.**

Olaf Wollschläger is a German record producer and audio engineer, known for his work in the synth-pop and electronic rock scenes. Based in Germany, he has been the primary producer for Mesh since 2009, overseeing the band's studio albums *A Perfect Solution*, *Automation Baby*, *Looking Skyward*, and *The Truth Doesn't Matter*. Wollschläger's production style emphasizes polished electronic textures while maintaining the organic warmth of live instrumentation, helping Mesh refine their sound into a more sophisticated and atmospheric direction.
