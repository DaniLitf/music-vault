# Oumi Kapila

**Record producer, musician, and composer. British-born Australian. Born 16 September 1984 in Coventry, England.**

Oumi Kapila is a British-born Australian musician, songwriter, and record producer based in Los Angeles. Classically trained, he studied jazz performance at the Western Australian Academy of Performing Arts before relocating to the US. He is best known as the lead guitarist for Filter, having joined the band in 2015 and co-producing their seventh album *Crazy Eyes* (2016) with Richard Patrick. Kapila also produced Combichrist's *This Is Where Death Begins* (2016) and *One Fire* (2019), and has worked as a composer for film and television, including placements in *Rogue One: A Star Wars Story* and *The X-Files*.
