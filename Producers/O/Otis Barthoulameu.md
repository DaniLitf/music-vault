# Otis Barthoulameu

**Record producer, musician. American.**

Otis Barthoulameu, universally known as "O", is an American producer, engineer, and guitarist central to the 1990s San Diego punk scene, fronting the bands Olivelawn and Fluf. He helped [[Artists/B/Blink-182]] (then Blink) sign to Cargo Records and produced their debut album *Cheshire Cat* (1995), recorded in three days at Westbeach Recorders.
