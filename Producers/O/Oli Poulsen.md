# Oli Poulsen

**Record producer, musician, mixer. Faroese.**

Oli Poulsen (born 1963, Faroe Islands) is a Faroese record producer, multi-instrumentalist, and mixing engineer based in Copenhagen, Denmark since 1987. He has produced, engineered, and played on hundreds of albums with over 10 million units sold worldwide, working with artists such as Michael Learns to Rock, Sissel Kyrkjebø, Grace Jones, the Georgia Satellites, and Hanne Boel.
