# Michael Münzing

**Record producer. German.**

Michael Münzing is a German record producer who, alongside Luca Anzilotti, formed the production duo behind the Eurodance group Snap!. He co-produced and co-wrote all of Snap!'s albums, including *World Power* (1990), *The Madman's Return* (1992), and *Welcome to Tomorrow* (1994), which generated the international hits "The Power" and "Rhythm Is a Dancer". He also performed with Anzilotti and Sven Väth in the group Off and the side project 16 BIT.
