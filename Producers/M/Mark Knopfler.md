# Mark Knopfler

**Record producer. British.**

Mark Knopfler is a British singer-songwriter, guitarist, and record producer best known as the frontman of Dire Straits, and has had a prolific solo career producing and recording albums across rock, country, and folk. He has also produced soundtracks for films such as *Local Hero* and *The Princess Bride*, and won multiple Grammy Awards. See [[Members/M/Mark Knopfler]] for his biography as a musician.
