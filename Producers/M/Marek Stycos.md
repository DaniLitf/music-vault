# Marek Stycos

**Record producer and engineer. American.**

Marek Stycos is an American producer and engineer who worked out of Mad Hatter Studios in Florida in the late 1990s. Together with co-producer "Moe", he recorded Bird of Ill Omen's debut *Self, Dare You Still Breathe?* over a rushed, budget-strapped session in May 1997 — the first recording financed by Eulogy Recordings.
