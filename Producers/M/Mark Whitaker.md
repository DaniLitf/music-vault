# Mark Whitaker

**Record producer and music manager. American.**

Mark Whitaker is a San Francisco Bay Area-based music producer, engineer, and manager who was a key figure in the emerging Bay Area thrash metal scene of the 1980s. He managed Exodus and helped facilitate guitarist Kirk Hammett's departure to Metallica in 1983. As a producer, he is best known for helming Exodus's landmark debut *Bonded by Blood* (1985) and contributing to their second album *Pleasures of the Flesh* (1987), handling production, mixing, and engineering duties.
