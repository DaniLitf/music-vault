# Morgan Kibby

**Record producer, songwriter. American.**

Morgan Kibby is an American songwriter, producer, and composer who co-produced [[Artists/M/M83]]'s *Saturdays = Youth* (2008) alongside Ken Thomas and Ewan Pearson while serving as the band's vocalist and keyboardist. She has since produced and written for other artists and composed for film and television, and records her own music as White Sea. See also [[Members/M/Morgan Kibby]].
