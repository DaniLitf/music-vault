# Mike Elizondo

**Record producer. American.**

Mike Elizondo is an American record producer, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist from Pacoima, California, who rose to prominence as a protégé of Dr. Dre, co-writing and playing bass on hits including "In da Club" and "The Real Slim Shady." A three-time Grammy winner, he has worked across genres from hip hop (Eminem, 50 Cent) to rock and metal, producing Avenged Sevenfold's *Nightmare* (2010) and *Hail to the King* (2013).
