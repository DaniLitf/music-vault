# Mike Fraser

**Record producer. Canadian.**

Mike Fraser is a Canadian record producer, recording and mixing engineer who came up through Vancouver's Little Mountain Sound Studios. He is best known for his long association with AC/DC and for engineering and mixing work with Aerosmith, Metallica and many other hard rock acts. His extensive engineering and mixing work on the Coverdale–Page album earned him a co-production credit alongside the two artists.
