# Mark Trombino

**Record producer. American.**

Mark Trombino is an American record producer, engineer, and former drummer of San Diego post-hardcore band Drive Like Jehu. He became one of the defining producers of late-1990s and 2000s pop-punk and emo, producing [[Artists/B/Blink-182]]'s *Dude Ranch* (1997) as well as landmark records by Jimmy Eat World (*Clarity*, *Bleed American*), Midtown, Finch, and Something Corporate.
