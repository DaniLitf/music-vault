# Mike Post

**Record producer, composer, and musician. American.**

Mike Post (born Leland Michael Postil; September 29, 1944, in Berkeley, California) is an American composer, arranger, record producer, and musician best known for his iconic television theme music for *Law & Order*, *The A-Team*, *Hill Street Blues*, *The Rockford Files*, *Magnum, P.I.*, and many others. He began his career as a record producer, winning his first Grammy at age 23 for arranging Mason Williams' "Classical Gas," and went on to produce albums for Dolly Parton, Van Halen, and Kenny Rogers and The First Edition. Post holds five Grammy Awards, an Emmy, and was inducted into the Television Hall of Fame in 2025.

Also a musician, Post plays guitar, bass, keyboards, and piano, and recorded several albums as the Mike Post Coalition. See [[Members/M/Mike Post]] for biography.
