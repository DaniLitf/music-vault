# Malcolm Burn

**Record producer. Canadian.**

Malcolm Burn is a Canadian record producer, engineer, and musician who won the 2001 Grammy Award for Best Contemporary Folk Album for producing Emmylou Harris's *Red Dirt Girl*. He previously collaborated with Daniel Lanois as a musician and engineer on projects including Bob Dylan's *Oh Mercy* and The Neville Brothers' *Yellow Moon*, and has produced albums for Midnight Oil, Patti Smith, and Iggy Pop.
