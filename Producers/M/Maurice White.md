# Maurice White

**Record producer. American.**

Maurice White was an American singer, songwriter, musician, and record producer, best known as the founder, leader, and chief producer of Earth, Wind & Fire. Born in Memphis and raised in Chicago, he began his career as a session drummer at Chess Records before forming Earth, Wind & Fire in 1969. He produced the vast majority of the band's catalog, including landmark albums such as *That's the Way of the World* (1975), *Spirit* (1976), and *All 'n All* (1977), blending funk, soul, jazz, and R&B into a sound that defined an era. White won seven Grammy Awards and was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame and the Songwriters Hall of Fame. He also produced for Deniece Williams, The Emotions, Ramsey Lewis, and Barbra Streisand. See also [[Members/M/Maurice White]].
