# Mick Harris

**Drummer, producer, and electronic musician. Born 4 October 1967.**

Mick Harris is an English musician best known as the drummer for Napalm Death from 1985 to 1991, where he pioneered the blast beat and coined the term "grindcore". He produced Benediction's debut album *Subconscious Terror* (1990). After leaving Napalm Death, he joined Painkiller with John Zorn and Bill Laswell, and moved into electronic and ambient music with projects such as Scorn and Lull.
