# Marti Frederiksen

**Record producer, songwriter. American.**

Marti Frederiksen is an American songwriter and record producer known for his work with Aerosmith, Def Leppard, Buckcherry, Mötley Crüe, and many others. He has produced multiple albums for The Dead Daisies.
