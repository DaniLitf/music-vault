# Martin Atkins

**Record producer and drummer. British.**

Martin Atkins is a British drummer and record producer, best known as the founder and driving force of [[Artists/P/Pigface]]. He has produced every Pigface studio album, occasionally under the pseudonym "The Girl Brothers." He also founded Invisible Records in 1987. See [[Members/M/Martin Atkins]] for full biography.
