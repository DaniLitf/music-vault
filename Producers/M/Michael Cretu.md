# Michael Cretu

**Record producer. Romanian.**

Michael Cretu is a Romanian-German record producer, musician, and composer best known as the creator and founder of the Enigma project. He produced and self-recorded every Enigma album at his A.R.T. Studios in Ibiza, starting with the multi-platinum *MCMXC a.D.* (1990). See [[Members/M/Michael Cretu]] for his biography as a musician.

## Associated Projects

- [[Artists/E/Enigma]] – *MCMXC a.D.* (1990), *The Cross of Changes* (1993), *Le Roi Est Mort, Vive Le Roi!* (1996), *The Screen Behind the Mirror* (2000), *Voyageur* (2003), *A Posteriori* (2006), *Seven Lives Many Faces* (2008), *The Fall of a Rebel Angel* (2016)
