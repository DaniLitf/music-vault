# Matt Allison

**Record producer. American.**

Matt Allison is an American record producer and engineer from Chicago, Illinois, who founded Atlas Studios in 1996 to serve the city's punk rock scene. He has produced the bulk of [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio|Alkaline Trio]]'s catalog since their 1998 debut, as well as records by The Lawrence Arms, The Menzingers, and Rise Against.
