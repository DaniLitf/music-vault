# Mudrock

**Record producer. American.**

Mudrock (Andrew Murdock) is an American record producer from Providence, Rhode Island, best known for producing Godsmack's multi-platinum self-titled debut (1998) and *Awake* (2000). He went on to produce Avenged Sevenfold's second and third albums, *Waking the Fallen* (2003) and *City of Evil* (2005), and runs his own Los Angeles studio, The Hobby Shop, with Scott Gilman.
