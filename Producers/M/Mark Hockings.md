# Mark Hockings

**Record producer. British.**

Mark Hockings is a British musician and producer, best known as the vocalist, guitarist, and primary songwriter of the synth-pop band Mesh. Based in Bristol, England, he co-founded Mesh in 1991 with Richard Silverthorn and has been the driving creative force behind the band's melodic yet introspective sound. Hockings co-produced the band's first three studio albums, crafting a production style that blends synth-pop electronics with alternative rock sensibilities and emotional vocal delivery. His work helped establish Mesh as a leading act in the British electronic scene.
