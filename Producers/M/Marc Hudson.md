# Marc Hudson

**Record producer. American.**

Marc Hudson is an American record producer, mix engineer, and longtime collaborator of Laura Jane Grace. He produced [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]]'s seventh studio album, *Shape Shift with Me* (2016), and was also a founding member of Grace's side project [[Artists/D/Devouring Mothers|Devouring Mothers]].
