# Michael "Elvis" Baskette

**Record producer. American.**

Michael "Elvis" Baskette is an American record producer and mixer known for his work with hard rock and metal bands. He produced Ratt's seventh and final studio album *Infestation* (2010) and has also worked with Alter Bridge, Sevendust, and Chevelle.
