# Mark Hoppus

**Record producer. American.**

Mark Hoppus is an American musician and record producer, best known as bassist and co-lead vocalist of [[Artists/B/Blink-182]] — see [[Members/M/Mark Hoppus]] for his biography as a musician. As a producer he co-produced +44's *When Your Heart Stops Beating* (2006) with Travis Barker at their own Opera Music studio, and has produced records for other pop-punk acts including Motion City Soundtrack's *Commit This to Memory* (2005).
