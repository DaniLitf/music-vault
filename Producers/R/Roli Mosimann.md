# Roli Mosimann

**Record producer, drummer, and musician. Swiss. Born 7 November 1955 in Thurgau, Switzerland; died 15 September 2024 of lung cancer.**

Roli Mosimann was a Swiss-born record producer and drummer who first came to prominence as the drummer of the New York no wave band Swans from 1983 to 1984. He later collaborated with J.G. Thirlwell as the duo Wiseblood. As a record producer, Mosimann is best known for his work with The Young Gods, The The, New Order, Hair & Skin Trading Company, and Faith No More, producing their album *Album of the Year* (1997). His production style drew on his background in industrial and experimental music. See also [[Members/R/Roli Mosimann]].
