# Ron Nevison

**Record producer, engineer. American.**

Ron Nevison is an American record producer and audio engineer known for his work with Led Zeppelin, The Who, Ozzy Osbourne, Heart, Kiss, and many others. He produced Vince Neil's debut solo album *Exposed* (1993).
