# Rich Costey

**Record producer and mixer.**

Rich Costey is a Grammy-winning producer and mixer known for his meticulous, dynamic production sound. He has been a long-term collaborator with Muse across multiple albums, and has also worked extensively with numerous alternative rock and metal acts.
