# Ron Browz

**Record producer. American.**

Ron Browz is a Harlem producer and rapper best known for producing Nas's "Ether" (2001) and the Auto-Tune hit "Pop Champagne" (2008). He contributed production to both G-Unit studio albums, *Beg for Mercy* (2003) and *T·O·S (Terminate on Sight)* (2008).
