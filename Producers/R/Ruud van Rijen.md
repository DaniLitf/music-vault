# Ruud van Rijen

**Record producer. Dutch.**

Ruud van Rijen is a Dutch record producer and the founder of the Eurodance project Twenty 4 Seven, formed in 1989 in Valkenswaard, Netherlands. He has produced all four of the group's studio albums — *Street Moves* (1990), *Slave to the Music* (1993), *I Wanna Show You* (1994), and *Twenty 4 Hours A Day, Seven Days A Week* (1997) — as well as their many hit singles.
