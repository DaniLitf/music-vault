# Rhys Fulber

**Record producer, musician, and songwriter. Canadian. Born 10 October 1970 in Vancouver, British Columbia.**

Rhys Fulber is a Canadian electronic musician and producer best known as a core member of Front Line Assembly and Delerium, alongside Bill Leeb. He has also recorded under his own name and as Conjure One. Fulber's production career spans industrial, metal, and electronic genres; his collaboration with Fear Factory beginning in the mid-1990s helped define the band's signature sound, contributing keyboards, programming, and co-production to albums such as *Demanufacture* (1995) and *Obsolete* (1998). He has also produced for Paradise Lost, Megadeth, P.O.D., Josh Groban, and many others. Based in Los Angeles, he works out of his own studio, Surplus Sound in Van Nuys.
