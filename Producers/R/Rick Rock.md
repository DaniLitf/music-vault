# Rick Rock

**Record producer. American.**

Rick Rock is an Alabama-born, Bay Area-based producer known for his slapping West Coast sound and as leader of the Federation. He has produced for 2Pac, E-40, Jay-Z, and Busta Rhymes, and contributed production to G-Unit's *T·O·S (Terminate on Sight)* (2008).
