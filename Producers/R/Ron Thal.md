# Ron Thal

**Record producer. American.**

Ron "Bumblefoot" Thal is an American guitarist, songwriter, and producer. He produced and co-wrote the first two Art of Anarchy albums, blending hard rock with alternative metal. In addition to his production work, he is a celebrated guitarist — see [[Members/R/Ron Thal]] for his performance career and band history.
