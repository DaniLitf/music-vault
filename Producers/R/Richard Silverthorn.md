# Richard Silverthorn

**Record producer. British.**

Richard Silverthorn is a British keyboardist and producer, co-founder of the synth-pop band Mesh alongside Mark Hockings. Based in Bristol, Silverthorn has been responsible for the band's keyboard programming and electronic production since their formation in 1991. He co-produced Mesh's first three studio albums, contributing the layered synthesizer arrangements and electronic textures that define the band's sound. His production work emphasizes atmospheric depth and intricate programming, complementing Hockings' vocal and songwriting strengths.
