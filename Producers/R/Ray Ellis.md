# Ray Ellis

**Record producer. American.**

Ray Ellis was an American record producer, arranger, conductor, and saxophonist best known for orchestrating Billie Holiday's *Lady in Satin* (1958) and for composing themes for NBC's *The Today Show*. Born in Philadelphia, he worked across jazz, pop, and television music over a career spanning several decades, producing Emmylou Harris's debut album *Gliding Bird* (1970) among many other projects.
