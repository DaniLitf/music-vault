# Roberto Laghi

**Record producer. Swedish.**

Roberto Laghi is a Swedish record producer, engineer, and mixer based in Gothenburg, founder of Oral Majority Recordings. He is best known for his long-running collaboration with In Flames — including production and mixing work on *Siren Charms* — and produced All Ends' second album *A Road to Depression* (2010).
