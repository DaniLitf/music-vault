# Rick Rubin

**Record producer. American.**

Rick Rubin is an American record producer and co-founder of Def Jam Recordings who first made his name producing pioneering hip-hop acts (LL Cool J, Run-DMC, Public Enemy) before moving into rock and metal production for artists including Slayer, Red Hot Chili Peppers, and Johnny Cash. He produced [[Artists/M/Metallica]]'s *Death Magnetic* (2008), the band's first album since *...And Justice for All* not produced by Bob Rock, favoring a hands-off approach that pushed the band back toward a rawer thrash sound; he won the Grammy for Producer of the Year for the record.
