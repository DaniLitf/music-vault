# Richard Bennett

**Record producer. American.**

Richard Bennett is an American guitarist and record producer who toured with Neil Diamond for 17 years and has been a member of Mark Knopfler's band since 1994. As a session musician he played on records by Billy Joel, Barbra Streisand, and Rodney Crowell, and as a producer he has worked with Steve Earle, Emmylou Harris, and Marty Stuart.
