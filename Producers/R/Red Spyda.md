# Red Spyda

**Record producer. American.**

Red Spyda is an American hip-hop producer who crafted tracks for many G-Unit-affiliated releases in the early 2000s, including work with 50 Cent, Lloyd Banks, and D12. He contributed production to G-Unit's debut album *Beg for Mercy* (2003).
