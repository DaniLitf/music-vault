# Rob McGregor

**Record producer. American.**

Rob McGregor is an American recording engineer and producer based in Gainesville, Florida, where he runs Goldentone Studios. He produced [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]]'s first two studio albums, *Against Me! Is Reinventing Axl Rose* (2002) and *As the Eternal Cowboy* (2003), helping define the raw, live sound of the Gainesville punk scene of the era.
