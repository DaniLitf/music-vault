# Reeves Gabrels

**Record producer. American.**

Reeves Gabrels is an American guitarist and producer who co-produced David Bowie's *Earthling* (1997) and *Hours...* (1999), continuing their creative partnership after Tin Machine's split. See also [[Members/R/Reeves Gabrels]] for his full biography as a musician.
