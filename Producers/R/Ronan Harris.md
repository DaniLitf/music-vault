# Ronan Harris

**Record producer. British.**

Ronan Harris is an Irish-born British electronic musician and producer, best known as the founder and sole core member of VNV Nation. Based in London, Harris has single-handedly written, produced, and performed all of VNV Nation's studio albums since 1995, creating a distinctive sound that blends melodic synth-pop, EBM, and orchestral elements. His self-produced albums have charted across Europe and established VNV Nation as one of the most important acts in the futurepop genre. Harris has also provided production work and electronic sequences for other artists within the electronic and industrial scene.
