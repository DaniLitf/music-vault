# David Coverdale

**Record producer. British.**

David Coverdale is an English singer who has co-produced much of his own output, including Whitesnake albums and the Coverdale–Page project. On *Coverdale–Page* (1993) he shared production and mixing credits with Jimmy Page and Mike Fraser. He is primarily a vocalist — see [[Members/D/David Coverdale]] for biography.
