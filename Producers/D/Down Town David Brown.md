# "Down Town" David Brown

**Record producer. American.**

"Down Town" David Brown is an American record producer from the Cleveland, Ohio area, known for producing the 1994 Hatrix album *Collisioncoursewithnoplace* at Suma Recording Studio and Mars Studios.
