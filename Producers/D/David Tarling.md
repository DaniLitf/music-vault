# David Tarling

**Record producer. American.**

David Tarling is an American record producer and recording engineer active in the Los Angeles punk scene of the 1980s. He produced and engineered the Descendents' second album *I Don't Want to Grow Up* (1985) at Music Lab studios in Hollywood, released by New Alliance Records.
