# Don Cannon

**Record producer. American.**

Don Cannon is a Philadelphia-born, Atlanta-based producer and DJ who came up alongside DJ Drama in the Aphilliates and helped shape the Gangsta Grillz mixtape era. He produced for Young Jeezy, Ludacris, and 50 Cent, and contributed production to G-Unit's *T·O·S (Terminate on Sight)* (2008).
