# David Z

**Record producer and engineer. American.**

David Z (David Z Rivkin) is an American record producer and audio engineer best known for his work with [[Artists/P/Prince|Prince]] and related Minneapolis artists. He co-produced [[Artists/M/Mazarati|Mazarati]]'s debut album and engineered Prince's hit "Kiss", developing the distinctive gated-guitar sound on the recording.

He has also worked with artists such as [[Artists/B/Billy Idol|Billy Idol]], [[Artists/F/Fine Young Cannibals|Fine Young Cannibals]], and [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction|Jane's Addiction]].
