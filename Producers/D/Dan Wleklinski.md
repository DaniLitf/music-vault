# Dan Wleklinski

**Record producer and engineer. American.**

Dan "Mr. Precision" Wleklinski is a Chicago punk rock producer and engineer who owns and operates [[Studios/B/Bombshelter Recording Studio]]. Best known as guitarist of [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]] and an original member of Rise Against, he recorded, mixed, and produced 88 Fingers Louie's comeback album *Thank You for Being a Friend* and the live album *88 Fingers Louie LIVES*. He is also a musician — see [[Members/D/Dan Wleklinski]].
