# David Pajo

**Record producer. American.**

David Pajo is an American musician and self-producing recording artist who records his solo albums largely at home, famously tracking *Pajo* (2005) as laptop demos in GarageBand. His production style favours intimate, lo-fi minimalism. He is primarily a musician — see [[Members/D/David Pajo]] for his full biography.
