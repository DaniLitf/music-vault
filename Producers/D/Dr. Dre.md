**Record producer. American.**

Dr. Dre is a Grammy-winning record producer and rapper who co-executive produced 50 Cent's debut album *Get Rich or Die Tryin'* (2003) and contributed to several subsequent releases. He is also a musician — see [[Members/D/Dr. Dre]] for biography.
