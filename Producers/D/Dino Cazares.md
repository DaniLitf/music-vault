# Dino Cazares

**Record producer and guitarist. American. Born 5 September 1966.**

Dino Cazares is an American guitarist, songwriter, and record producer best known as the co-founder and guitarist of Fear Factory. While primarily recognized as a musician, Cazares took on production duties for Fear Factory's *Aggression Continuum* (2021), sharing credits with Damien Rainaud and Rhys Fulber. His production work draws on his decades of experience shaping Fear Factory's machine-like industrial metal sound through his distinctive down-tuned rhythm guitar style. He also founded the band Divine Heresy during his departure from Fear Factory. See also [[Members/D/Dino Cazares]].
