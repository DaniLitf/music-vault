# Donnell Cameron

**Record producer. American.**

Donnell Cameron is an American record producer and engineer who worked with Avenged Sevenfold on their 2001 debut album, recording and producing it alongside the band at Westbeach Recorders in Hollywood on a shoestring budget of around $2,000.
