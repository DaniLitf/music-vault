# Dirk Ivens

**Record producer. Belgian.**

Dirk Ivens is a Belgian electronic musician and producer, a pioneering figure in the EBM and industrial scenes. Born in Belgium, he co-founded the influential EBM band Absolute Body Control in the early 1980s before launching Klinik and eventually Dive as solo outlets for his increasingly minimalist and abrasive sound. As a producer, Ivens handles all aspects of Dive's recordings, crafting a raw, distorted aesthetic built on abused drum machines and processed vocals. His production work spans multiple projects and is characterized by its uncompromising harshness and minimal approach to electronic music.
