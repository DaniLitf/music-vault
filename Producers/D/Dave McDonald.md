# Dave McDonald

**Sound engineer, record producer. British.**

Dave McDonald is a British audio engineer and record producer best known for his longtime collaboration with the trip-hop band [[Artists/P/Portishead]]. Working with the group from their earliest recordings, McDonald co-produced their second studio album *Portishead* (1997) and engineered both *Dummy* (1994) and *Portishead* (1997), earning a reputation as an integral part of the band's studio sound — so much so that he is sometimes regarded as the fourth member. Beyond Portishead, McDonald has worked extensively as a front-of-house engineer for major international tours including Adele, Frank Ocean, Air, Broken Bells, Sigur Rós, Florence and the Machine, and Beth Gibbons' solo performances.
