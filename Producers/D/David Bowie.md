# David Bowie

**Record producer. British.**

David Bowie produced [[Artists/M/Mott the Hoople]]'s breakthrough album *All the Young Dudes* (1972), writing and giving them its title track after the band had nearly split up. See also [[Members/D/David Bowie]] and [[Artists/D/David Bowie]] for his own artist biography.
