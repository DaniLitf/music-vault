# Daniel Lanois

**Record producer. Canadian.**

Daniel Lanois is a Canadian record producer, musician, and songwriter known for his atmospheric production style and his work with U2 on *The Joshua Tree*, Bob Dylan on *Oh Mercy*, and Neil Young on *Harvest Moon*. He has won multiple Grammy Awards and is also recognized for his ambient solo albums and his influential studio work with artists across rock, country, and roots music.
