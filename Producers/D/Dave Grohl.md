# Dave Grohl

**Record producer. American.**

Dave Grohl is an American musician best known as the drummer of Nirvana and frontman of Foo Fighters, who has also produced records for other artists. He produced Ghost's *If You Have Ghost* EP (2013) at his own Studio 606. As a producer he brings a raw, energetic rock sensibility shaped by his extensive performing experience. For his biography and full list of musical projects, see [[Members/D/Dave Grohl]].
