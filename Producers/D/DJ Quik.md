# DJ Quik

**Record producer, rapper. American.**

DJ Quik is a Compton producer and rapper known for his funk-driven West Coast sound. He contributed production to Tupac Shakur's *All Eyez on Me* (1996), including work on "Heartz of Men". He is also a musician with a long-running solo career.
