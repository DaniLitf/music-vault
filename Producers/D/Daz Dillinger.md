# Daz Dillinger

**Record producer, rapper. American.**

Daz Dillinger is a Long Beach producer and rapper, one half of Tha Dogg Pound and a core in-house producer at Death Row Records. He produced several tracks on Tupac Shakur's *All Eyez on Me* (1996), including "Ambitionz Az a Ridah" and "2 of Amerikaz Most Wanted". He is also a musician — a rapper in Tha Dogg Pound.
