**Record producer, DJ. American. Born January 16, 1968.**

DJ Muggs is a legendary hip-hop producer best known for founding Cypress Hill and producing their platinum-selling debut album. He is renowned for his dark, heavy production style that incorporates Latin influences, samples, and deep basslines. Beyond his work with Cypress Hill, he has produced for numerous artists across hip-hop and has collaborated across genres, creating the iconic "Jump Around" beat for House of Pain.
