# Daniel Rey

**Record producer. American.**

Daniel Rey (born Daniel Rabinowitz) is an American musician, record producer, and songwriter from New York City, best known for his extensive work with the Ramones. As a teenager he played in the punk band Shrapnel alongside Dave Wyndorf of Monster Magnet, and later became the guitarist for Manitoba's Wild Kingdom with Handsome Dick Manitoba and Andy Shernoff of The Dictators.

Rey first collaborated with the Ramones on *Too Tough to Die* (1984), co-writing "Daytime Dilemma (Dangers of Love)" with Joey Ramone, and went on to produce three of the band's albums. He co-wrote the popular single "Pet Sematary" with Dee Dee Ramone and performed all guitar parts on Joey Ramone's solo album *Don't Worry About Me*. His production credits also include Misfits, White Zombie, Nashville Pussy, and Entombed's *Same Difference*.
