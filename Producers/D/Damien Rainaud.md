# Damien Rainaud

**Record producer, mixing engineer, and mastering engineer. French. Based in Los Angeles.**

Damien Rainaud is a French musician, producer, mixing and mastering engineer based in Los Angeles, primarily focused on metal and rock music. He has produced, engineered, mixed, and mastered albums for artists including DragonForce, Fear Factory, Babymetal, Almah, and Fairyland. His work with Fear Factory includes engineering on *The Industrialist* (2012), engineering and programming on *Genexus* (2015), and full production duties on *Aggression Continuum* (2021), the band's tenth studio album. Rainaud is known for his detailed approach to metal production, encompassing recording, editing, mixing, and mastering.
