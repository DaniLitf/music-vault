# Danny Lohner

**Record producer. American.**

Danny Lohner is an American producer and multi-instrumentalist, a longtime Nine Inch Nails associate who moved into production and film-score work in the 2000s. He co-produced [[Artists/B/Black Light Burns]]' debut *Cruel Melody*, shaping its industrial sound design alongside Wes Borland. He is also a musician — see [[Members/D/Danny Lohner]] for his full biography.
