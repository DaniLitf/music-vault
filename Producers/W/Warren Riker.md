# Warren Riker

**Record producer. American.**

Warren Riker is a three-time Grammy Award-winning American record producer, audio engineer, mixer, and multi-instrumentalist. Born in New Jersey and based in Nashville, he has worked across genres from hip hop to metal, with over 40 million records sold featuring his contributions. His Grammy wins came for his work on The Fugees' *The Score*, Lauryn Hill's *The Miseducation of Lauryn Hill*, and Santana's *Supernatural*. In the metal world, he produced and engineered Down's *Down II: A Bustle in Your Hedgerow* (2002) and has worked with artists such as Michael Jackson, Cathedral, and Anders Osborne.
