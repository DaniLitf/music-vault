# Wes Borland

**Record producer. American.**

Wes Borland is an American musician and producer, best known as the guitarist of [[Artists/L/Limp Bizkit]]. He self-produces the releases of his comedic metal project [[Artists/B/Big Dumb Face]] and runs his own label, Edison Sound, through which the project's later albums were released. He is also a musician — see [[Members/W/Wes Borland]] for his full biography.
