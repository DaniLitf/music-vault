# Murder Inc.

**Industrial rock supergroup from the United Kingdom. Active 1991–1992.**

Murder, Inc. were a British industrial rock supergroup formed in 1991 by [[Members/M/Martin Atkins]], uniting the then-current Jaz Coleman-less Killing Joke lineup with former Killing Joke drummer Paul Ferguson and vocalist Chris Connelly of Ministry/Revolting Cocks fame. Their sole, self-titled album was recorded by Steve Albini and released on Atkins' Invisible Records (Devotion in Europe) in 1992. The band played only five live dates before dissolving amid internal squabbles and the financially enticing prospect of a Killing Joke reformation; Connelly, Atkins, and Walker later reconvened in [[Artists/T/The Damage Manual]] (1998).
