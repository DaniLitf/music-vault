# Mott the Hoople

**Glam rock / rock from Hereford, England. Active 1969–1980 (reunions 2009, 2013, 2019).**

Mott the Hoople were an English rock band formed in Hereford in 1969, named by producer Guy Stevens after a novel by Willard Manus. Fronted by pianist and songwriter Ian Hunter, the band struggled commercially through four albums of hard-edged rock before nearly splitting up in 1972 — until David Bowie, a fan, gave them "All the Young Dudes" and produced the album of the same name, transforming them into glam rock stars. They split in 1974, though Hunter and various members have reunited for occasional tours since.
