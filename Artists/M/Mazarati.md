# Mazarati

**American funk/R&B band from Minneapolis, Minnesota. Active 1986–1989.**

Mazarati was a funk and R&B band formed by former Prince and the Revolution bassist [[Members/B/Brownmark|Brown Mark]], with strong behind-the-scenes support from [[Artists/P/Prince|Prince]]. The band signed to Prince's Paisley Park label and released their self-titled debut album in 1986, which produced the single "100 MPH" — written and co-produced by Prince. The album did not achieve major commercial success, but the band is notable for providing the demo of "Kiss" that led Prince to record his own version after reworking the arrangement.

The band released a second album, *Mazarati 2*, on Motown in 1989 (Canadian release only) before disbanding. Members have periodically reunited for tribute shows.
