# Meshuggah

**Meshuggah are a Swedish extreme metal band formed in Umeå in 1987, known for their extraordinarily complex polyrhythmic song structures, extended-range guitars tuned to low F, and founding influence on the djent movement, with landmark albums like *Destroy Erase Improve* (1995) and *obZen* (2008).**
