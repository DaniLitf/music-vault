# Murder Inc. Records

**Record label / hip-hop collective. United States. Active 1999–present.**

Murder Inc. Records is an American record label and hip-hop collective founded by brothers Chris and Irv "Irv Gotti" Gotti in 1999. Operating as an umbrella imprint under Universal Music Group, the label became synonymous with East Coast hip-hop and R&B in the early 2000s, generating over $500 million in revenue and 30 million units sold at its peak. The label's flagship artist Ja Rule established Murder Inc. as a major force through multi-platinum releases, while Ashanti's debut cemented the label's commercial dominance across genres.

## Legal & Organizational History

Murder Inc. was raided by federal agents on January 3, 2003, as part of an investigation into alleged money laundering connections to drug kingpin Kenneth "Supreme" McGriff. The Gotti brothers were charged in January 2005 but were acquitted of all charges in December 2005. The label was forced to leave Def Jam headquarters in March 2003. In December 2003, the label was rebranded as "The Inc. Records" to distance from controversy. Murder Inc. operated under Universal Motown from 2006–2009 before entering dormancy. The label was relaunched in 2013 under Irv Gotti's Visionary imprint and later partnered with 300 Entertainment in 2017. In July 2022, Irv Gotti sold his 50% stake in Murder Inc. master recordings to Iconoclast for $100 million.

Irv Gotti passed away on February 5, 2025, at age 54, following a stroke.
