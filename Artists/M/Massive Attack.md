# Massive Attack

**Massive Attack are an English electronic music collective formed in Bristol in 1988, widely credited as pioneers of trip-hop, known for genre-defining albums like *Blue Lines* (1991) and *Mezzanine* (1998), and for featuring a rotating cast of collaborating vocalists.**
