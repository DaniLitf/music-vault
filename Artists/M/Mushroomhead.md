# Mushroomhead

**Mushroomhead are an American heavy metal band formed in Cleveland, Ohio in 1993, known for their theatrical masks and costumes, experimental blend of industrial metal, nu metal, and alternative metal, and for their long-running rivalry with Slipknot over which band invented the masked metal aesthetic.**
