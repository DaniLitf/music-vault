# Mushroomhead

**Alternative metal / industrial metal from Cleveland, Ohio. Active 1993–present.**

Mushroomhead are an American heavy metal band formed in Cleveland, Ohio in 1993, known for their theatrical masks and costumes and experimental blend of industrial metal, nu metal, and alternative metal. The band is notable for their long-running rivalry with Slipknot over which group pioneered the masked metal aesthetic.
