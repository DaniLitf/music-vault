# Meat Beat Manifesto

**Electronic/industrial band from Sheffield, England. Active 1987–present.**

Meat Beat Manifesto (also known as MBM) are an English electronic and industrial band formed in Sheffield in 1987 by Jack Dangers (Jonny Stephens) and James Lovell. Widely recognized as pioneers of industrial hip-hop, breakbeat, and electronic music, they are credited with helping to develop the big beat genre alongside acts like The Prodigy and The Chemical Brothers. Their 1992 album *99* and 1996 album *Subliminal Sandwich* are considered genre-defining works.
