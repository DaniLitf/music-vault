# Motörhead

**Motörhead were an English rock band formed in London in 1975 by bassist and vocalist Lemmy Kilmister, known for their extraordinarily loud, fast, and raw sound bridging heavy metal and punk, and for hits like "Ace of Spades" and "Overkill."**
