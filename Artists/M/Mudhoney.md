# Mudhoney

**Mudhoney are an American grunge band formed in Seattle, Washington in 1988, one of the defining bands of the grunge movement, signed to Sub Pop Records, formed from the ashes of Green River by vocalist Mark Arm and guitarist Steve Turner.**
