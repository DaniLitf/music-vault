# Malfunkshun

**Malfunkshun were an American rock band formed in Seattle, Washington in 1980, one of the earliest bands in the Seattle proto-grunge scene, led by vocalist Andrew Wood, who later co-founded Mother Love Bone.**

| Year | Title | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| 1989 | *Return to Olympus* | recorded 1986; official release posthumous |
| 2001 | *Malfunkshun: The Andrew Wood Story* | documentary/compilation |

## Members

### Core / Official Members
- Andrew Wood — vocals, bass (1980–1990; died 1990; also in [[Artists/M/Mother Love Bone]])
- Kevin Wood — guitar (1980–1990)
- Regan Hagar — drums (1984–1990)

## Connections

- [[Artists/M/Mother Love Bone]] — Andrew Wood co-founded Mother Love Bone after Malfunkshun; his death in 1990 dissolved both bands.
