# Mau Mau

**Italian world music band formed in Turin in 1991. Active 1991–present.**

Mau Mau is an Italian band from Turin formed in 1991 by Luca Morino, Fabio Barovero, and Tatè Nsongan. Drawing from Mediterranean, African, Arabic, and Latin traditions, they primarily sing in Piedmontese. Their debut *Sauta Rabel* (1992), recorded at Peter Gabriel's Real World Studios, won the Club Tenco Award for best debut album.
