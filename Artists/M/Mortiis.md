# Mortiis

**Mortiis is a Norwegian musical project formed in 1993 by Håvard "Mortiis" Ellefsen, formerly the bassist of Emperor, beginning as dark ambient dungeon synth and evolving through various phases toward industrial rock.**

Mortiis is a solo artist. See [[Members/M/Mortiis]] for biography.
