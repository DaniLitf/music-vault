# Magenta

**Norwegian industrial/alternative rock band. Active 1995–present.**

Magenta are a Norwegian industrial rock and alternative rock band formed by vocalist Vilde Lockert and multi-instrumentalist Anders Odden (also of Cadaver and Satyricon) in the summer of 1995. Their 1997 EP produced the Norwegian chart hit "Secret Sky," and the band went on to chart in Germany and Greece with singles from *Little Girl Lost* (2002), later collaborating with acts including Apoptygma Berzerk and Mortiis.
