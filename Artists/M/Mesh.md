# Mesh

**Synth-pop / futurepop from Bristol, England. Active 1991–present.**

Mesh is a British electronic alternative band formed in Bristol in 1991 by vocalist Mark Hockings and keyboardist Richard Silverthorn, later joined by Neil Taylor. The band rose to prominence with their breakthrough album *The Point at Which It Falls Apart* (1999) and achieved commercial success in Germany and continental Europe. Their sound combines melodic synth-pop with introspective lyrics and driving electronic rhythms, drawing comparisons to Depeche Mode and VNV Nation.
