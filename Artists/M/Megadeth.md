# Megadeth

**Megadeth are an American thrash metal band formed in Los Angeles in 1983 by Dave Mustaine after his acrimonious dismissal from Metallica, one of the "Big Four" of thrash metal, known for technically demanding albums like *Peace Sells... but Who's Buying?* (1986) and *Countdown to Extinction* (1992).**
