# Madonna

**Madonna (born Madonna Louise Ciccone, August 16, 1958, in Bay City, Michigan) is an American singer, songwriter, actress, and entrepreneur, known as the "Queen of Pop," one of the best-selling music artists of all time, celebrated for constantly reinventing her music and image.**

Madonna is a solo artist. See [[Members/M/Madonna]] for biography.
