# Meathook Seed

**Industrial death metal / industrial metal project from United Kingdom / United States / France. Active 1992–1993, 1999.**

Meathook Seed was an industrial metal side project formed in 1992 by Napalm Death guitarist Mitch Harris. The debut album *Embedded* (1993) featured Obituary members Donald Tardy and Trevor Peres alongside Harris, blending death metal with industrial elements. A second album, *Basic Instructions Before Leaving Earth (B.I.B.L.E.)* (1999), saw a completely different lineup with Christophe Lamouret on vocals, Ian Treacy on drums, Shane Embury on bass, and producer Russ Russell.
