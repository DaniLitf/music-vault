# Mastodon

**Mastodon are an American heavy metal band formed in Atlanta, Georgia in 2000, known for their progressive and sludge metal sound, complex concept albums, and status as one of the most critically acclaimed metal bands of the 2000s, with landmark albums like *Leviathan* (2004) and *Crack the Skye* (2009).**
