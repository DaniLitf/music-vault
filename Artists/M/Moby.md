# Moby

**Moby (born Richard Melville Hall, September 11, 1965, in New York City) is an American electronic musician, DJ, and singer-songwriter, best known for his 1999 album *Play*, which became one of the best-selling electronic albums of all time through its widespread licensing of every track.**

Moby is a solo artist. See [[Members/M/Moby]] for biography.
