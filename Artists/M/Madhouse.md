# Madhouse

**Jazz fusion / Funk from Minneapolis, USA. Active 1987.**

Madhouse was an American jazz fusion band created by Prince as an instrumental side project. The group was presented as a fictional band with a fabricated backstory, but was in reality Prince (playing nearly all instruments) and saxophonist Eric Leeds. Two official albums, *8* and *16*, were released in 1987 on Paisley Park Records, featuring jazz-funk instrumentals with numbered titles. Several additional Madhouse albums were recorded but remain largely unreleased.
