# Mike Oldfield

**Mike Oldfield (born May 15, 1953, in Reading, England) is an English multi-instrumentalist, composer, and producer, whose debut album *Tubular Bells* (1973) — released as the first record on Richard Branson's Virgin Records — became a global phenomenon and established him as a leading figure in progressive and new age music.**

Mike Oldfield is a solo artist. See [[Members/M/Mike Oldfield]] for biography.
