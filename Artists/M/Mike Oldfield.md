# Mike Oldfield

**English multi-instrumentalist, composer, and producer. Born 15 May 1953.**

Mike Oldfield is an English multi-instrumentalist, composer, and producer best known for his debut album *Tubular Bells* (1973), which was the first release on Richard Branson's Virgin Records and became a global phenomenon. He is a leading figure in progressive and new age music.

Mike Oldfield is a solo artist. See [[Members/M/Mike Oldfield]] for biography.
