# Mad Season

**Mad Season were an American rock supergroup formed in Seattle in 1994, featuring Layne Staley of Alice in Chains on vocals, Mike McCready of Pearl Jam on guitar, Barrett Martin of Screaming Trees on drums, and John Baker Saunders on bass.**
