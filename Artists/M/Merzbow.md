# Merzbow

**Merzbow is a Japanese noise music project formed in 1979 by Masami Akita in Tokyo, one of the most prolific and influential acts in noise music, having released hundreds of albums of extremely dense, layered, abrasive electronic noise.**

Merzbow is a solo artist. See [[Members/M/Masami Akita]] for biography.
