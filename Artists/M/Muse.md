# Muse

**English rock band from Teignmouth, Devon. Active 1994–present.**

Muse are an English rock supergroup known for their ambitious, theatrical sound that blends hard rock with classical, electronic, and progressive influences. Formed in 1994 by three musicians from a small Devon town, the band achieved worldwide recognition through landmark albums like *Origin of Symmetry* (2001), *Absolution* (2003), and *Black Holes and Revelations* (2006). Their expansive approach to rock instrumentation—combining traditional rock with symphonic elements, electronic production, and complex song structures—has made them one of the defining rock acts of the 21st century.
