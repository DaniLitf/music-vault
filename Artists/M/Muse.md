# Muse

**Muse are an English rock band formed in Teignmouth, Devon in 1994, known for their grandiose, theatrical sound blending hard rock with classical, electronic, and progressive influences, and for landmark albums like *Origin of Symmetry* (2001) and *Absolution* (2003).**
