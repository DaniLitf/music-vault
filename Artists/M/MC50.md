# MC50

**MC50 were an American punk supergroup formed in 2018 by Wayne Kramer of MC5 to celebrate the 50th anniversary of the album *Kick Out the Jams*, featuring Kim Thayil of Soundgarden on guitar.**

| Year | Title | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| 2018 | *Kick Out the Jams 50th Anniversary Tour* | touring project; no studio album |

## Members

### Core / Official Members
- Wayne Kramer — guitar, vocals (MC5)
- [[Members/K/Kim Thayil]] — guitar (also in [[Artists/S/Soundgarden]])
- Dug Pinnick — bass, vocals (King's X)
- Brendan Canty — drums (Fugazi)
- Marcus Durant — vocals (Zen Guerrilla)

## Connections

- [[Artists/S/Soundgarden]] — Kim Thayil, guitarist of Soundgarden, performed with MC50 as part of the MC5 anniversary tour.
