# Mind Funk

**Mind Funk were an American rock band formed in New York City in 1989, drawing members from various Seattle-adjacent circles including Mike McCready of Pearl Jam and involving connections to the Mad Season supergroup.**

## Members

### Core / Official Members
- [[Members/M/Mike McCready]] — guitar (also in [[Artists/P/Pearl Jam]], [[Artists/M/Mad Season]])
- John Baker Saunders — bass (also in [[Artists/M/Mad Season]]; died 1999)
- Barrett Martin — drums (also in [[Artists/M/Mad Season]])
- Patrick Dubar — vocals

## Connections

- [[Artists/P/Pearl Jam]] — Mike McCready, lead guitarist of Pearl Jam, also played in Mind Funk.
- [[Artists/M/Mad Season]] — Mike McCready, John Baker Saunders, and Barrett Martin all went on to form Mad Season in 1994.
