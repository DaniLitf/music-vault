# Michael Jackson

**Michael Jackson (August 29, 1958 – June 25, 2009) was an American singer, songwriter, and dancer from Gary, Indiana, known as the "King of Pop," one of the best-selling music artists of all time, celebrated for *Thriller* (1982), the best-selling album in history, and for transforming popular music and dance.**
