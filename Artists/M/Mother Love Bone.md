# Mother Love Bone

**Mother Love Bone were an American rock band formed in Seattle, Washington in 1988, featuring Stone Gossard and Jeff Ament from Green River together with charismatic vocalist Andrew Wood, dissolved by Wood's heroin overdose in 1990, with Gossard and Ament going on to found Pearl Jam.**
