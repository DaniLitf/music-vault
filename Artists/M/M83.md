# M83

**Synth-pop / dream pop / shoegaze from Antibes, France. Active 1999–present.**

M83 is a French electronic project formed in Antibes in 1999, named after the spiral galaxy Messier 83. It began as a duo of multi-instrumentalists [[Members/A/Anthony Gonzalez]] and [[Members/N/Nicolas Fromageau]] (who had previously played together in the post-rock group My Violent Wish); after Fromageau's departure in 2004, Gonzalez has remained the sole constant member, writing and recording largely on his own with guest musicians and a rotating live band. The project broke through internationally in 2011 with the Grammy-nominated double album *Hurry Up, We're Dreaming* and its single "Midnight City".
