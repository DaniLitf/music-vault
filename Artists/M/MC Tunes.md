# MC Tunes

**MC Tunes (born Nicholas Lockett, 1970, in Manchester, England) is a British rapper best known for his 1990 collaboration with 808 State on the album *Tunes Splits the Atom*, a landmark fusion of hip-hop and rave music.**

MC Tunes is a solo artist. See [[Members/M/MC Tunes]] for biography.
