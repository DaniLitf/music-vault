# Merit Hemmingson

**Merit Hemmingson (born October 13, 1950, in Stockholm, Sweden) is a Swedish musician, accordion player, and singer, a pioneering figure in Swedish folk music who blended traditional Swedish folk with jazz and psychedelic elements.**

Merit Hemmingson is a solo artist. See [[Members/M/Merit Hemmingson]] for biography.
