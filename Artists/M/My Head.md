# My Head

**Grunge / alternative rock power trio from Los Angeles, California. Active 1993–1998.**

My Head formed in 1993 after guitarist/vocalist [[Members/A/Adam Siegel]] and drummer [[Members/G/Greg Saenz]] left crossover thrash band [[Artists/E/Excel]], joined by bassist [[Members/D/Dave Silva]] (ex-Bad Radio). Their sound blended their crossover thrash roots with the era's grunge movement. After a false start with Imago Records, the band signed to Capitol and released their only album, *Endless Bummer* (1996), before a label push to change their sound, name, and lineup led them to break the contract and disband in 1998. Even after breaking up, the band's recordings backed two later Mike Muir releases.
