# Metallica

**Thrash metal / heavy metal from Los Angeles, California (based in the San Francisco Bay Area since 1982), United States. Active 1981–present.**

Metallica are an American heavy metal band formed by James Hetfield and Lars Ulrich in Los Angeles in 1981, one of the "Big Four" of thrash metal and among the best-selling music acts of all time, known for albums like *Master of Puppets* (1986), *...And Justice for All* (1988), and *Metallica* (the "Black Album," 1991). Beyond their own catalogue, the band's revolving early lineup and Cliff Burton's 1986 death connect them to a wide network of thrash and metal acts across the vault.

## Connections

- [[Artists/M/Megadeth]] — Dave Mustaine was Metallica's original lead guitarist before being fired in 1983; he formed Megadeth directly in response.
- [[Artists/E/Exodus]] — Kirk Hammett was Exodus's original lead guitarist (1979–1983) before leaving to join Metallica.
- [[Artists/S/Suicidal Tendencies]] — Robert Trujillo played bass for Suicidal Tendencies (1990–1995) before joining Metallica in 2003.
- [[Artists/I/Infectious Grooves]] — Robert Trujillo co-founded Infectious Grooves with Mike Muir in 1989, continuing alongside his other commitments after joining Metallica.
- [[Artists/O/Ozzy Osbourne]] — Robert Trujillo was Ozzy's touring bassist (1996–2003) immediately before joining Metallica.
- [[Artists/V/Voivod]] — Jason Newsted, Metallica's bassist (1986–2001), recorded three Voivod albums (2003–2009) under the pseudonym "Jasonic."
- [[Artists/N/Newsted]] — Jason Newsted, Metallica's bassist (1986–2001), later formed his own band, Newsted, in 2012.
- [[Artists/F/Flotsam and Jetsam]] — Jason Newsted was Flotsam and Jetsam's founding bassist before leaving in 1986 to join Metallica.
