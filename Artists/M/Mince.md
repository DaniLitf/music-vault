# Mince

**Post-punk band from Leeds, UK. Active from 2024–present.**

Mince are a Leeds-based post-punk quintet known for their noisy, genre-blending sound drawing from artists ranging from Frankie Valli to The Wedding Present. They have released singles including "Sad Old Jumper" (2024) and "Roadworks on the B6369" (2025) via Kycker Music, and have been featured on BBC Radio 6 and Radio X.
