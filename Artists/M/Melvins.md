# Melvins

**Melvins are an American experimental heavy metal band from Montesano, Washington, active 1983–present, pioneers of sludge metal who evolved through numerous lineups and subgenres, from grunge and stoner rock to industrial and ambient experiments.**

Formed by Buzz Osborne and Dale Crover, the Melvins have been influential in shaping multiple heavy metal subgenres and remain one of the most prolific and experimental acts in heavy music.
