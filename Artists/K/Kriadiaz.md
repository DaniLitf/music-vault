**Sludge/Southern/Groove metal from Cleveland, Ohio. Active 2010–present.**

Kriadiaz is an American sludge/southern metal band formed in Cleveland in 2010 by guitarist Dave Felton, formerly of Mushroomhead. The band plays a groove-laden style of sludge metal with southern rock influences, combining heavy riffs with gritty vocals.
