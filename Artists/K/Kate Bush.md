# Kate Bush

**Kate Bush (born Catherine Bush, 1958) is an English singer-songwriter, musician, and producer from Bexleyheath, Kent, celebrated for her eclectic art-pop sensibility, unconventional lyrics, and innovative dance-driven performances.**

Bush was signed to EMI as a teenager after [[Members/D/David Gilmour]] of [[Artists/P/Pink Floyd]] heard her demo tape through a mutual friend and financed a more professional recording. In 1978, aged 19, she topped the UK Singles Chart with her self-written debut single "Wuthering Heights," becoming the first female artist to reach number one in the UK with a song she wrote entirely herself. She has produced all of her own albums since *The Dreaming* (1982) and duetted with Peter Gabriel on the 1986 hit "Don't Give Up." Bush took a twelve-year hiatus after *The Red Shoes* (1993) before returning with *Aerial* (2005), and was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2023.
