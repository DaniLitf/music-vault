# Kleerup

**Kleerup is the Swedish electronic pop project of producer Andreas Kleerup, formerly of the Teddybears, whose 2008 debut album featured vocal collaborations with artists including Robyn and established him as a central figure in Swedish pop production.**

Kleerup is a solo artist. See [[Members/A/Andreas Kleerup]] for biography.
