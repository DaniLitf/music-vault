# KISS

**Hard rock / heavy metal from New York City, United States. Active 1973–2023, 2025–present.**

KISS is an American rock band formed in New York City in 1973 by Paul Stanley, Gene Simmons, Ace Frehley, and Peter Criss. Known for their face paint, stage costumes, and theatrical live performances featuring fire-breathing, blood-spitting, and pyrotechnics, KISS became one of the most iconic rock bands of all time. They have earned 30 Gold albums, the most of any American band, and were inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2014.
