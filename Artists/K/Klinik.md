# Klinik

**Belgian EBM/industrial band from Brussels. Active 1982–present.**

Klinik are a Belgian EBM and industrial band formed in Brussels in 1982 by Marc Verhaeghen. A key act in the Belgian electronic body music scene, they are known for their harsh, minimalist sound combining pounding beats with dark, atmospheric electronics and commanding vocals. They have been influential in shaping the EBM genre alongside peers like Front 242.
