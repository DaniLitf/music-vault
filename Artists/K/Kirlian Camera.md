# Kirlian Camera

**Italian electronic/industrial band from Parma. Active 1980–present.**

Kirlian Camera are an Italian electronic and industrial band formed in Parma in 1980 by Elena Alice Fossi and Marco Milanesio. Known for their eclectic, genre-spanning approach blending darkwave, synthpop, industrial, and experimental electronic music, they are one of Italy's longest-running and most respected electronic acts.
