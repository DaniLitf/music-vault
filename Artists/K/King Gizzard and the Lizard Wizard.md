# King Gizzard and the Lizard Wizard

**King Gizzard and the Lizard Wizard are an Australian psychedelic rock band formed in Melbourne in 2010, celebrated for their extraordinary prolificacy — releasing dozens of albums — and restless genre-hopping across psychedelic rock, garage rock, microtonal music, and heavy metal.**
