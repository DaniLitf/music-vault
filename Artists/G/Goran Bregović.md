# Goran Bregović

**Goran Bregović is a Bosnian-Serbian musician and composer born on March 22, 1950, in Sarajevo, Yugoslavia, known for his Balkan brass and world music compositions, his long collaboration with director Emir Kusturica on films like *Underground* (1995) and *Arizona Dream*, and his band the Wedding and Funeral Band.**

Goran Bregović is a solo artist. See [[Members/G/Goran Bregović]] for biography.
