# Gamla Pengar

**Punk rock band from Gothenburg, Sweden.**

Gamla Pengar ("Old Money") are a Swedish punk band from Gothenburg, built around the guitar/vocal duo of Mikey Lennartsson and Johan Meiton alongside bassist/vocalist Fredrik Karlsson. The band has released a string of albums and splits, including *Gamla Pengar* (2011), *Bon Purée* (2018), and *En Attraktiv Profil* (2018), with a revolving cast of drummers and bassists.
