# Gorebound

**Death/grind band from England.**

Gorebound are an English death metal project reuniting Jason "Mr Gore" Merle and Graham "Nutty Strangeways" Hoddis, the two original vocalists of deathgrind band Gorerotted, following that band's 2008 split. The project released the EP *It's Choppin' Time* in the summer of 2010.
