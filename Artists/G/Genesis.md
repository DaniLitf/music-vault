**Progressive rock / art rock band. Formed Godalming, Surrey, England, 1967–2022.**

Genesis was an English rock band formed in January 1967 in Godalming, Surrey. Initially pioneering progressive rock in the 1970s with lead vocalist Peter Gabriel, the band achieved massive commercial success in the 1980s and 1990s with a more pop-oriented sound. The group went through several eras: the Gabriel-Hackett years (1971–1977), the Phil Collins years (1975–1996), and the Ray Wilson era (1996–2000). After disbanding in 2000, they reunited for two major tours (2006–2007 and 2020–2022) before finally ending due to Phil Collins' health issues.
