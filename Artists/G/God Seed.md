# God Seed

**Norwegian black metal project formed in Bergen. Active 2008–present.**

God Seed are a Norwegian black metal project formed in 2008 by Gaahl (Kristian Eivind Espedal) and King ov Hell (Tom Cato Visnes) following their legal battle over the Gorgoroth name. The project blends traditional Norwegian black metal with more progressive and experimental elements. They released one studio album, *Witnesses*, in 2009.
