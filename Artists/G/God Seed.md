# God Seed

**Norwegian black metal band from Bergen. Active 2008–2009, 2012–2015.**

God Seed were a Norwegian black metal band formed in March 2009 by former Gorgoroth members Gaahl and King ov Hell following the end of the Gorgoroth name dispute. Gaahl departed in July 2009 and the project went on hold — the music intended for their debut was instead released by King as Ov Hell with vocalist Shagrath — before Gaahl rejoined in 2012, releasing the live album *Live at Wacken* and the band's sole studio album, *I Begin* (2012), before disbanding again in 2015.
