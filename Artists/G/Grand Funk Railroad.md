# Grand Funk Railroad

**Rock / Hard rock / Arena rock from Flint, Michigan. Active 1969–1976, 1981–1983, 1996–1998, 2000–present.**

Grand Funk Railroad is an American rock band formed in Flint, Michigan, in 1969 by Mark Farner, Don Brewer, and Mel Schacher. Known for their crowd-pleasing arena rock style and massive commercial success, the band scored numerous hit singles including "We're an American Band," "I'm Your Captain (Closer to Home)," "The Loco-Motion," and "Some Kind of Wonderful." Despite frequent critical dismissals, Grand Funk released six platinum and seven gold-certified albums between 1969 and 1976, and remains one of the highest-grossing touring acts of the early 1970s.
