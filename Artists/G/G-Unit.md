# G-Unit

**Hardcore hip-hop / gangsta rap from Queens, New York City, USA. Active 1999–2014, 2014–2022.**

G-Unit (short for Guerilla Unit) was an American hip-hop group formed by childhood friends 50 Cent, Tony Yayo, and Lloyd Banks from South Jamaica, Queens. After a prolific run of self-released mixtapes in the early 2000s, the group signed to Interscope through 50 Cent's G-Unit Records imprint and released the multi-platinum debut *Beg for Mercy* (2003). The line-up shifted repeatedly — Young Buck and The Game joined and were later ousted, and the 2014 reunion added Kidd Kidd — before 50 Cent declared the group permanently disbanded in 2022.
