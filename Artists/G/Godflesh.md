# Godflesh

**Godflesh are an English industrial metal band formed in Birmingham in 1988 by Justin Broadrick and G.C. Green, widely credited as pioneers of the genre, known for fusing crushing metal guitar with drum machine rhythms on landmark albums like *Streetcleaner* (1989) and *Pure* (1992).**
