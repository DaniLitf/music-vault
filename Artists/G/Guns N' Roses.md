# Guns N' Roses

**Guns N' Roses are an American hard rock band formed in Los Angeles in 1985, achieving massive commercial success with *Appetite for Destruction* (1987) — still one of the best-selling albums of all time — known for raw, bluesy hard rock and the volatile chemistry of Axl Rose and Slash.**
