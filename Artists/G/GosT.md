# GosT

**GosT is an anonymous darksynth and electronic project releasing music since 2013, known for horror-influenced synthesizer compositions and heavy metal aesthetics, associated with the Perturbator and darksynth scene.**

GosT is a solo artist. See [[Members/G/GosT]] for biography.
