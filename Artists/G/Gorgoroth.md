# Gorgoroth

**Norwegian black metal band formed in Bergen in 1992 by Infernus.**

Gorgoroth are a Norwegian black metal band formed in Bergen in 1992 by guitarist Infernus (Roger Tiegs). The band is known for their controversial live performances and anti-religious imagery, as well as albums like *Antichrist* (1996) and *Twilight of the Idols* (2003). The band's name comes from a region in J.R.R. Tolkien's *The Lord of the Rings*. Vocalist Gaahl and bassist King ov Hell left in 2007 amid a dispute over the band name, later forming God Seed.
