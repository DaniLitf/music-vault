# Goldie

**Goldie (born Clifford Joseph Price on December 19, 1965, in Walsall, England) is a British DJ, producer, and actor, one of the most prominent figures in drum and bass music, known for the landmark album *Timeless* (1995).**

Goldie is a solo artist. See [[Members/G/Goldie]] for biography.
