# Gorillaz

**Gorillaz are a British virtual band created in 1998 by Damon Albarn of Blur and comic artist Jamie Hewlett, featuring animated characters as the nominal band members while Albarn and numerous guest collaborators supply the music, resulting in an eclectic mix of rock, hip-hop, and electronic pop.**
