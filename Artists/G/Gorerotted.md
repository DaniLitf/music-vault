# Gorerotted

**Grindcore / death metal from London, England. Active 1997–2014, 2026–present.**

Gorerotted are an English deathgrind band formed in London in 1997, known for their horror film-inspired lyrics, dual-vocal attack, and humorous song titles. The band disbanded in 2008 and reformed as The Rotted until 2014, before reuniting under their original name in 2026. They released three studio albums and one EP during their initial run, and have toured extensively across Europe and the US.
