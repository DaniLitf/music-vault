# Ghost

**Ghost are a Swedish rock band formed in Linköping in 2006, known for their elaborate theatrical performances, satanic/occult imagery, catchy hard rock songs, and rotating cast of anonymous "Nameless Ghouls" — with frontman Tobias Forge as the sole consistent member under various "Papa Emeritus" personas.**
