# New Power Generation

**Funk / R&B backing band from Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA. Active 1990–2013 (reunions 2015, 2017, 2019).**

The New Power Generation (also known as the NPG) was the backing band of Prince from 1990 to 2013, replacing The Revolution. They debuted on the Nude Tour in 1990 and first appeared on record on *Diamonds and Pearls* (1991). The band served as Prince's primary musical vehicle during his most commercially successful early-1990s period before becoming a side project that released its own albums. In February 2026, the band changed its name to the Minneapolis Sound All Star Band.
