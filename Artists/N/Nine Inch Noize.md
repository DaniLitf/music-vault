# Nine Inch Noize

**Industrial techno collaborative project between Nine Inch Nails and Boys Noize, from Los Angeles, USA / Berlin, Germany. Active 2025–present.**

Nine Inch Noize is a collaborative project between American industrial rock band [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] and German-Iraqi electronic producer Boys Noize (Alexander Ridha). It grew out of Ridha's dance remix of the *Challengers* film score, *Challengers [MIXED]* (2024), and the pair's subsequent work together on the *Tron: Ares* soundtrack (2025) and its remix companion *Tron Ares: Divergence* (2026), followed by Boys Noize supporting Nine Inch Nails on the Peel It Back Tour (2025–2026) and the act's debut live performance at Coachella 2026.
