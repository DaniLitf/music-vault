# Napalm Death

**Napalm Death are an English extreme metal band formed in Birmingham in 1981, widely credited as pioneers of grindcore, known for their ultra-fast tempos, blast-beat drumming, and politically charged lyrics, with early members who went on to notable roles in other vault bands.**
