# Nine Inch Nails

**Industrial rock / electronic project from Cleveland, Ohio, USA. Active 1988–present.**

Nine Inch Nails (abbreviated NIN) is an American industrial rock project founded in 1988 by [[Members/T/Trent Reznor]]. As the primary producer, singer, and multi-instrumentalist, Reznor is the sole official member of the band for recording purposes, though the live band has featured a rotating lineup of prominent musicians. NIN broke into the mainstream with *Pretty Hate Machine* (1989) and achieved critical and commercial peaks with *The Downward Spiral* (1994) and *The Fragile* (1999). After a hiatus following *The Slip* (2008), Reznor returned with *Hesitation Marks* (2013) and later albums, now working closely with [[Members/A/Atticus Ross]] — officially made a member in 2016. NIN has won multiple Grammy Awards and been named one of the most influential rock acts of the 1990s and 2000s.
