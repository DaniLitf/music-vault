# Nymphet Noodlers

**Rock band from Karlstad, Sweden. Active 1990s–1996, 1999, 2018–present (reunions).**

Nymphet Noodlers were a Swedish rock band from Karlstad, formed in the early 1990s from the remnants of local bands Ugly Jake and Christ Couldn't Come. Heavily influenced by Union Carbide Productions, whose singer Ebbot Lundberg produced their sole album, *Going Abroad* (1994), the band split in 1996 but has reunited periodically since, including a December 2017 show. Members Mattias Bärjed and Martin Hederos both went on to notable solo and band careers, the latter as a founding member of The Soundtrack of Our Lives.
