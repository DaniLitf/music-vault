# Neon Horse

**Alternative rock band from USA. Active 2005–2008.**

Neon Horse was an alternative rock side project featuring members from Starflyer 59 and Project 86. The band released a self-titled album in 2007 and worked on a follow-up, *The Ways We Go*, which ultimately went unreleased before the group disbanded.
