# Newsted

**Heavy metal from San Rafael, California, USA. Active 2012–2014, 2023–present.**

Newsted is an American heavy metal band formed in October 2012 by former [[Artists/M/Metallica]] bassist [[Members/J/Jason Newsted]] as his return to touring and recording after leaving Metallica in 2001. The band released the *Metal* EP and full-length *Heavy Metal Music* (both 2013) before going inactive in 2014; it was reactivated for live shows in 2023.
