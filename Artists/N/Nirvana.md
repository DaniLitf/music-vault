# Nirvana

**Nirvana were an American rock band formed in Aberdeen, Washington in 1987, who became one of the defining bands of the 1990s, bringing grunge and alternative rock to the mainstream with *Nevermind* (1991) and its lead single "Smells Like Teen Spirit," dissolving with Kurt Cobain's suicide in 1994.**

## Connections

- [[Artists/F/Fecal Matter]] — Kurt Cobain and Krist Novoselic co-founded Fecal Matter, Nirvana's direct predecessor.
- [[Artists/F/Foo Fighters]] — Dave Grohl founded Foo Fighters after Nirvana dissolved.
- [[Artists/G/Giants in the Trees]] — Krist Novoselic is a member of Giants in the Trees.
- [[Artists/E/Eyes Adrift]] — Krist Novoselic co-founded Eyes Adrift after Nirvana.
- [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] — Dale Crover, drummer of The Melvins, served as an early drummer for Nirvana.
- [[Artists/N/No WTO Combo]] — Krist Novoselic was a member of No WTO Combo.
