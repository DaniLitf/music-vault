# Nova Collective

**Instrumental progressive jazz-fusion supergroup from Raleigh, USA. Active 2014–present.**

Nova Collective are an instrumental progressive rock/metal and jazz fusion supergroup formed in Raleigh, North Carolina, in 2014, initiated by Between the Buried and Me/Trioscapes bassist Dan Briggs and Haken guitarist Richard Henshall after they began exchanging demos. The duo expanded into a full band with Cynic/Trioscapes drummer Matt Lynch and former Haken keyboardist Pete Jones, releasing their debut album *The Further Side* through Metal Blade Records in 2017.
