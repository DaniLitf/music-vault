# Nick Jonas and the Administration

**Pop rock / soul / blues rock band from Los Angeles, California, USA. Active 2009–2011, 2016.**

Nick Jonas and the Administration was an American band formed in late 2009 as a side project by Nick Jonas of the Jonas Brothers. The group allowed Jonas to explore a more mature, soulful sound influenced by R&B and blues, featuring musicians recruited from Prince's New Power Generation. They released one studio album, *Who I Am* (2010), before going on hiatus; the band reunited for a one-night show in Minneapolis in 2016.
