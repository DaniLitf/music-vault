# Nitzer Ebb

**Nitzer Ebb are an English electronic body music (EBM) band formed in Chelmsford, Essex in 1982, one of the pioneering acts in the EBM genre, known for their stripped-down, aggressive synthesizer-driven sound and punishing rhythms.**
