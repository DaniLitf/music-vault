# Netherwilds

**Medieval "peasant metal" from Newmarket, Ontario, Canada. Active 2025–present.**

Netherwilds is the medieval fantasy metal project of [[Members/D/David Monks]], vocalist and bassist of Tokyo Police Club, who performs in character as the dirt-covered peasant "Worm Chewer." Born from a viral TikTok series filmed in the Irish countryside, the project blends extreme metal, thrash intensity, industrial grit, punk energy, and medieval folk instrumentation with absurdist humor and storytelling about classism, subjugation, and feudal rebellion. The debut single "Return the Goat" was released via King Volume Records, with its official music video (directed by Noah Douglas and Jake Thompson) premiering in June 2026, and a debut album is due in fall 2026.
