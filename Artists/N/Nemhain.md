# Nemhain

**Gothic/industrial rock band from London, England. Active 2006–present.**

Nemhain are an English gothic/industrial rock band formed in London in 2006 by drummer Adrian Erlandsson (formerly of At the Gates, The Haunted, and Cradle of Filth) and his wife, model and vocalist Amber "Morrigan Hel" Erlandsson. Named after the Celtic goddess of battle fury, the band's music explores the darker side of sex, drugs, addiction, passion, and revenge, and released their debut album *From The Ashes* in January 2010.
