# Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds

**Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds are an Australian rock band formed in London in 1983, led by vocalist Nick Cave (also of Grinderman), and featuring Blixa Bargeld of Einstürzende Neubauten for twenty years, known for their dark, theatrical blend of post-punk, gothic rock, and blues on albums like *Murder Ballads* (1996) and *Push the Sky Away* (2013).**

## Connections

- [[Artists/E/Einstürzende Neubauten]] — Blixa Bargeld was a guitarist in Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds from 1983–2003 while remaining leader of Einstürzende Neubauten.
- [[Artists/G/Grinderman]] — Nick Cave, Warren Ellis, Mick Harvey, and Jim Sclavunos formed Grinderman as a side project from the same core Bad Seeds lineup.
