# Ulver

**Experimental electronic / avant-garde project from Oslo, Norway. Active 1993–present.**

Ulver ("wolves") was founded in Oslo in 1993 by then-16-year-old vocalist [[Members/K/Kristoffer Rygg]]. Their early "Black Metal Trilogie" — *Bergtatt* (1995), *Kveldssanger* (1996), and *Nattens Madrigal* (1997) — established them within the Norwegian black metal scene, blending folk-influenced atmosphere with raw extremity. After inviting composer [[Members/T/Tore Ylwizaker]] into the fold in 1997, the band abandoned metal entirely, moving through industrial and progressive textures on *Themes from William Blake's The Marriage of Heaven and Hell* (1998) and into moody electronica on *Perdition City* (2000). Author [[Members/J/Jørn H. Sværen]] joined in 2000 and British multi-instrumentalist [[Members/D/Daniel O'Sullivan]] in 2009, rounding out the band's long-running quartet. Ulver has continued to shift styles across chamber orchestral works, Krautrock, and gothic-tinged pop, earning a reputation as one of Norwegian music's most restless and unpredictable acts.
