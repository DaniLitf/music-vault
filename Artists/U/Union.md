# Union

**Hard rock / heavy metal from Los Angeles, California, USA. Active 1997–2002, 2005.**

Union is an American hard rock supergroup formed in 1997 by former KISS guitarist Bruce Kulick and former Mötley Crüe vocalist John Corabi. The band also featured bassist Jamie Hunting (David Lee Roth, Eddie Money) and drummer Brent Fitz, and released two studio albums through Mayhem Records. After taking a hiatus in 2002, the band briefly reunited in 2005 for tours in Japan and Europe.
