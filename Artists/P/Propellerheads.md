# Propellerheads

**Propellerheads are a British big beat duo formed in Bath in 1995, consisting of [[Members/A/Alex Gifford]] and [[Members/W/Will White]], known for energetic, sample-heavy productions that became synonymous with the late-1990s big beat movement.**
