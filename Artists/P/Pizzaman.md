# Pizzaman

**Pizzaman is a British dance/house music project by [[Members/N/Norman Cook]], the same artist who records as [[Artists/F/Fatboy Slim]] and who previously fronted [[Artists/B/Beats International]], active primarily in the early-to-mid 1990s.**

## Members

Pizzaman is a solo project. See [[Members/N/Norman Cook]] for biography.

## Connections

- [[Artists/F/Fatboy Slim]] — Pizzaman is one of several aliases used by Norman Cook, who records most prolifically as Fatboy Slim.
- [[Artists/B/Beats International]] — Norman Cook previously fronted Beats International before using the Pizzaman alias.
