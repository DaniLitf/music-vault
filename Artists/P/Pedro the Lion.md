# Pedro the Lion

**Indie rock band from Seattle, USA. Active 1995–2006, 2017–present.**

Pedro the Lion is an indie rock band formed in 1995 by David Bazan in Seattle, Washington. The project's introspective lyrics and lo-fi aesthetic made it a cornerstone of the Christian indie rock scene, with Bazan later reviving the band in 2017 after a decade-long hiatus.
