# Parliament-Funkadelic

**Funk / psychedelic funk collective from Plainfield, New Jersey / Detroit, Michigan. Active 1956–present.**

Parliament-Funkadelic (P-Funk) is a sprawling American musical collective headed by [[Members/G/George Clinton]], built around the twin funk bands Parliament and Funkadelic. Clinton formed the doo-wop group the Parliaments in New Jersey in the late 1950s; by the late 1960s, backed by a stable touring band, he split the concept into Funkadelic (a heavier, psychedelic-rock-tinged outfit) and Parliament (a smoother, sci-fi-themed funk vehicle), touring both together as "Parliament-Funkadelic." Landmark albums include *Maggot Brain* (1971), *Mothership Connection* (1975), and *One Nation Under a Groove* (1978), and their elaborate stage shows and Afrofuturist "P-Funk mythology" — complete with the Mothership stage prop — became hugely influential on funk, post-punk, hip-hop, and G-funk. Sixteen members were inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 1997, and the collective received a Grammy Lifetime Achievement Award in 2019.
