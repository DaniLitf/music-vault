# Power Quest

**English power metal band from Southampton. Active 2001–2023.**

Power Quest are an English power metal band formed in 2001 in Southampton by keyboardist Steve Williams, who previously played with DragonHeart/DragonForce. Known for their melodic keyboard-driven power metal style, they released six studio albums before disbanding in 2023.

## Members

### Core / Official Members
- Steve Williams — keyboards, backing vocals (2001–2013, 2016–2023)
- Alessio Garavello — vocals, rhythm guitar (2001–2009)
- Andrea Martongelli — guitar, backing vocals (2003–2009)
- Francesco Tresca — drums (2003–2009)
- Steve Scott — bass, backing vocals (2001–2009)
