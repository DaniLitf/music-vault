# Pentagram

**Doom metal / proto-metal from Alexandria, Virginia, USA. Active 1971–present (with hiatuses).**

Pentagram is an American doom metal band formed in Alexandria, Virginia in 1971, making them one of the earliest proto-doom acts alongside Black Sabbath. Led by vocalist Bobby Liebling, the band struggled for decades with lineup instability and Liebling's personal troubles before releasing their debut album *Relentless* in 1985 — fourteen years after formation. They are regarded as a foundational act of doom metal, influencing countless bands who discovered their existence before their official debut.
