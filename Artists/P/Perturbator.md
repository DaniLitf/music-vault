# Perturbator

**Synthwave / darksynth from Paris, France. Active 2010–present.**

Perturbator is the solo project of French electronic musician James Kent, born and based in Paris. One of the most prominent artists in the darker, more aggressive strain of synthwave sometimes called "darksynth" or "retrowave," Perturbator's sound blends 1980s synthesizer aesthetics with industrial and metal influences. *Dangerous Days* (2014) and *The Uncanny Valley* (2016) brought international recognition. With *Lustful Sacraments* (2021), Kent shifted toward a darker, more gothic rock and EBM-influenced direction.
