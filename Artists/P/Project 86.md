# Project 86

**Christian rock / alternative metal band from Orange County, USA. Active 1996–present.**

Project 86 is a Christian rock and alternative metal band formed in 1996 in Orange County, California. Fronted by Andrew Schwab, the band is known for their aggressive yet melodic sound and have remained a fixture in the Christian rock scene for over two decades.
