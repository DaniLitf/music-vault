# Purgatory

**Thrash/power/speed metal from Cleveland, Ohio, United States. Active 1984–1990, 1991, 2003–2004, 2018–2019, 2024–present.**

Purgatory is an American heavy metal band formed in Cleveland, Ohio in 1984 by vocalist Jeff Hatrix after his return from Chicago. The band developed a strong local following with their fast, aggressive blend of thrash, power, and speed metal, releasing the EP *Purgatory* (1985) and the full-length *Tied to the Trax* (1986) before going through multiple lineup changes. After Hatrix's departure in 1989, the band recorded a second album *Dark Alley Surgery* and an EP with new vocalist Andy Boyer before splitting in 1990; they have reformed several times since, most recently in 2024.
