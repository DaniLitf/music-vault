# Porno for Pyros

**Alternative rock / alternative metal from Los Angeles, California, USA. Active 1992–1998, 2019–present.**

Porno for Pyros is an American rock band formed by [[Members/P/Perry Farrell]], [[Members/S/Stephen Perkins]], and [[Members/P/Pete DiStefano]] after the breakup of [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]].
