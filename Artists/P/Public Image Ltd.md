# Public Image Ltd

**Public Image Ltd (PiL) are an English post-punk band formed in London in 1978 by [[Members/J/John Lydon]] after the dissolution of the [[Artists/S/Sex Pistols]], initially exploring abrasive, avant-garde territory before broadening their sound through the 1980s.**

## Connections

- [[Artists/S/Sex Pistols]] — John Lydon, known as Johnny Rotten in the Sex Pistols, founded Public Image Ltd immediately after the Sex Pistols' breakup in 1978.
- [[Artists/K/Killing Joke]] — Martin Atkins, PiL's drummer (1979–1981), also drummed for Killing Joke and was a founding member of Pigface.
- [[Artists/P/Pigface]] — Martin Atkins founded Pigface after his time with both PiL and Killing Joke.
