# Portishead

**Portishead are an English electronic band formed in Bristol in 1991, one of the founding acts of trip-hop alongside [[Artists/M/Massive Attack]] and [[Artists/T/Tricky]], known for their sample-based, cinematic soundscapes and Beth Gibbons' haunting vocals.**

Portishead emerged from Bristol's fertile post-rave scene in the early 1990s, blending hip-hop breakbeats with film-noir samples, torch-song vocals, and analogue production techniques. Their debut album *Dummy* (1994) won the Mercury Prize and became one of the defining records of the trip-hop movement. After a trilogy of studio albums — *Dummy*, *Portishead* (1997), and *Third* (2008) — the band has remained intermittently active, headlining festivals and working on individual projects while maintaining their legacy as one of the most distinctive and influential acts to come out of the UK.
