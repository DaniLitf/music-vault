# PAKT

**Improvisational supergroup from Brooklyn, New York. Active 2020–present.**

PAKT is an American improvisational supergroup formed in 2020, featuring bassist Percy Jones (Brand X), guitarist Alex Skolnick (Testament), drummer Kenny Grohowski (Imperial Triumphant), and guitarist Tim Motzer. They perform free-form progressive rock and jazz fusion, blending composition with spontaneous improvisation.

## Members

### Core / Official Members
- Percy Jones — bass
- Alex Skolnick — guitar
- Kenny Grohowski — drums
- Tim Motzer — guitar, electronics
