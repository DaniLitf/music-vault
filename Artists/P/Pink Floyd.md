# Pink Floyd

**Progressive rock / psychedelic rock from London, England. Active 1965–1995, 2005, 2014.**

Pink Floyd were an English rock band formed in London in 1965, initially led by Syd Barrett before Roger Waters took creative control following Barrett's departure in 1968. The band became one of the best-selling music acts of all time, known for their philosophical lyrics, elaborate live productions, and landmark concept albums including *The Dark Side of the Moon* (1973), *Wish You Were Here* (1975), and *The Wall* (1979). Internal conflicts led to Roger Waters' departure in 1985; David Gilmour and Nick Mason continued under the Pink Floyd name until officially disbanding in 1995, reuniting briefly in 2005 and 2014.

## Connections
- [[Artists/D/David Bowie]] – Bowie joined David Gilmour onstage at the Royal Albert Hall in 2006 for "Arnold Layne" and "Comfortably Numb," his last UK performance.
- [[Artists/R/Roy Harper]] – sang lead vocals as a guest on "Have a Cigar" (*Wish You Were Here*, 1975); David Gilmour returned the favor by guesting on Harper's *HQ* and co-writing "Short and Sweet" with him.
- [[Artists/K/Kate Bush]] – David Gilmour discovered and financed Bush's demo recordings, leading to her EMI signing (1975–1976)
