# Prince

**Prince was an American singer, songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, and record producer born Prince Rogers Nelson on June 7, 1958, who died April 21, 2016 — one of the most prolific and innovative artists in popular music, known for his virtuosity across multiple instruments and his eclectic fusion of funk, rock, R&B, and pop.**
