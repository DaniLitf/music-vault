# Prince

**Funk / Rock / Pop / R&B from Minneapolis, USA. Active 1978–2016.**

Prince Rogers Nelson was one of the most prolific and innovative artists in popular music, known for his virtuosity across multiple instruments and his eclectic fusion of funk, rock, R&B, and pop. He sold over 100 million records worldwide and won an Academy Award for the *Purple Rain* soundtrack.
