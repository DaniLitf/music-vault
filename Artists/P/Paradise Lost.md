# Paradise Lost

**Gothic metal / doom metal from Halifax, West Yorkshire, England. Active 1988–present.**

Paradise Lost is an English gothic metal and doom metal band formed in Halifax in 1988. Alongside My Dying Bride and Anathema, they co-created the death/doom metal genre in the early 1990s with albums like *Gothic* (1991), widely considered a founding document of gothic metal. The band has undergone significant stylistic shifts across their career — spanning death/doom, gothic metal, electronic, and alternative metal — while the classic lineup of Nick Holmes and Gregor Mackintosh has remained intact throughout.
