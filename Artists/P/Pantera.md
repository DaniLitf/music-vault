# Pantera

**Groove metal / heavy metal from Arlington, Texas, USA. Active 1981–2003 (reunited 2022).**

Pantera was an American heavy metal band formed in Arlington, Texas in 1981, initially as a glam metal act before reinventing themselves as a pioneering groove metal band with *Cowboys from Hell* (1990). Anchored by vocalist Phil Anselmo and guitarist "Dimebag" Darrell Abbott, they became one of the most commercially successful and critically recognized heavy metal bands of the 1990s. Dimebag Darrell was shot and killed on stage on 8 December 2004; the band reunited in 2022 with surviving members Anselmo and Rex Brown alongside Charlie Benante (Anthrax) and Zakk Wylde.
