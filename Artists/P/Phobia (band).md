# Phobia

**American grindcore band from Orange County, California. Active 1990–present.**

Phobia are an American grindcore band formed in 1990 in Orange County, California, known for their politically charged, punk-infused grindcore. They have toured with acts including Napalm Death, Eyehategod, and Municipal Waste, and released albums on Relapse Records and Willowtip Records.
