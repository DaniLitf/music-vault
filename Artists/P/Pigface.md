# Pigface

**Industrial rock supergroup / collective from Chicago, Illinois, USA. Active 1990–present.**

Pigface is an American industrial rock supergroup founded by drummers [[Members/M/Martin Atkins]] and [[Members/B/Bill Rieflin]] after Ministry's *The Mind Is a Terrible Thing to Taste* tour (1989–1990). Conceived as a revolving-door collective, the band has featured hundreds of guest musicians across its discography — from industrial pioneers to punk, metal, and experimental artists. Their debut *Gub* (1990) was recorded with Steve Albini and featured early contributions from Trent Reznor and David Yow. The band's name comes from a character in Sam Raimi's short film *The Evil Dead*.
