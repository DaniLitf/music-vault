# PJ Harvey

**PJ Harvey is a British singer, songwriter, and musician born Polly Jean Harvey on October 9, 1969, in Bridport, Dorset — one of the most critically acclaimed British artists of her generation and the only artist to win the Mercury Prize twice (*Stories from the City, Stories from the Sea* in 2001 and *Let England Shake* in 2012).**
