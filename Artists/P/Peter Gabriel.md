# Peter Gabriel

**Peter Gabriel (born 1950) is an English singer, songwriter, and human rights activist from Chobham, Surrey, celebrated as the original frontman of [[Artists/G/Genesis]] and, since 1977, as an innovative solo artist blending art rock with world music influences.**

Gabriel co-founded Genesis at Charterhouse School in 1967 and fronted the band through its progressive rock classics — *Nursery Cryme* (1971), *Foxtrot* (1972), *Selling England by the Pound* (1973), and *The Lamb Lies Down on Broadway* (1974) — known for his elaborate costumed stage theatrics. He left in 1975 to launch a solo career, releasing four albums simply titled *Peter Gabriel*. His fifth album, *So* (1986), became his commercial breakthrough; its single "Sledgehammer" won a record nine MTV Video Music Awards and became the most-played video in MTV history. A committed activist and world-music advocate, Gabriel released the anti-apartheid single "Biko" (1980), co-founded the WOMAD festival (1982) and Real World Records, and duetted with [[Artists/K/Kate Bush]] on the 1986 hit "Don't Give Up." He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame as a member of Genesis in 2010 and as a solo artist in 2014.
