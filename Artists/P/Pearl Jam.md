# Pearl Jam

**Pearl Jam are an American rock band formed in Seattle, Washington in 1990, rising from the ashes of [[Artists/G/Green River]] and [[Artists/M/Mother Love Bone]] to become one of the defining acts of the grunge era and one of the best-selling bands of all time.**

## Connections

- [[Artists/G/Green River]] — Stone Gossard and Jeff Ament were both members of Green River before forming Pearl Jam.
- [[Artists/M/Mother Love Bone]] — Stone Gossard and Jeff Ament co-founded Mother Love Bone, which directly preceded Pearl Jam after vocalist Andrew Wood's death.
- [[Artists/M/Mad Season]] — Mike McCready, Pearl Jam's lead guitarist, co-founded the Seattle supergroup Mad Season alongside Layne Staley of Alice in Chains.
- [[Artists/M/Mind Funk]] — Mike McCready briefly played guitar with Mind Funk during Pearl Jam's early years.
- [[Artists/S/Soundgarden]] — Matt Cameron, Pearl Jam's drummer since 1998, was also the longtime drummer of Soundgarden.
- [[Artists/T/Temple of the Dog]] — Eddie Vedder, Stone Gossard, Jeff Ament, and Mike McCready all participated in Temple of the Dog, the 1991 tribute to Andrew Wood.
