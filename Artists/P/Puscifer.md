# Puscifer

**Puscifer is an American rock band formed in 2007 by [[Members/M/Maynard James Keenan]], best known as the vocalist of [[Artists/T/Tool]] and [[Artists/A/A Perfect Circle]] — a creative outlet operating outside those bands' heavier aesthetic with a more eclectic, darkly humorous style.**

## Connections

- [[Artists/T/Tool]] — Maynard James Keenan, Puscifer's founder and vocalist, is also the vocalist of Tool.
- [[Artists/A/A Perfect Circle]] — Maynard James Keenan co-founded A Perfect Circle in 1999, another band he leads alongside Puscifer.
- [[Artists/P/Primus]] — Tim Alexander, who drummed for Puscifer (2009–2012), is the primary drummer of Primus.
