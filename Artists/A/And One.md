# And One

**Synthpop / EBM from Berlin, Germany. Active 1989–present.**

And One is a German synthpop and electronic body music band founded by Steve Naghavi and Chris Ruiz in Berlin in 1989. Their debut single "Metalhammer" (1990) became a significant club hit, and they won Germany's Best New Artist award in 1991. Known for energetic live shows and a distinctive blend of melodic synthpop with harder EBM beats, the band has released twelve studio albums and remains active in the European alternative electronic scene.
