# Asia

**English progressive rock supergroup formed in London. Active 1981–2024.**

Asia were an English progressive rock supergroup formed in London in 1981 by John Wetton (bass, vocals) and Geoff Downes (keyboards), joined by Steve Howe (guitar) and Carl Palmer (drums). Their self-titled debut album (1982) became a global smash hit, spending nine weeks at number one in the US and spawning the classic single "Heat of the Moment." The band was originally conceived as a commercial outlet for musicians from Yes, ELP, and King Crimson, and they continued to release albums and tour through the 2010s before John Wetton's death in 2017.
