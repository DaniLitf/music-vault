# Adam Again

**Alternative rock / funk rock from Huntington Beach, California. Active 1982–2000.**

Adam Again was an American alternative rock band formed in Huntington Beach, California, by vocalist and multi-instrumentalist Gene Eugene. The band blended alternative rock with funk, soul, and rock influences, creating a distinctive sound that made them a cornerstone of the Christian alternative rock scene. Despite critical acclaim and a dedicated following, the band never achieved commercial success and disbanded following Eugene's sudden death from a brain aneurysm in March 2000. AllMusic described them as "inventive and artfully canny."
