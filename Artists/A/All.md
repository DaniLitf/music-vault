# All

**Punk rock / skate punk from Los Angeles, California, USA (later Fort Collins, Colorado). Active 1987–2003, 2008–present.**

All (stylized ALL) formed in 1987 when Descendents singer Milo Aukerman left to pursue a graduate degree in biochemistry, leaving guitarist Stephen Egerton, bassist Karl Alvarez, and drummer Bill Stevenson to carry on under the title of the Descendents' final studio album, *ALL*. Fronted in turn by three different lead singers, the band built a prolific and influential skate-punk catalogue on Cruz Records before signing briefly to Interscope and then Epitaph, eventually running its own label and studio, The Blasting Room, alongside the parallel, reunited Descendents.
