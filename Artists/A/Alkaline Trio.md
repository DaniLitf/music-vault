# Alkaline Trio

**Pop-punk / punk rock band from Chicago, Illinois, USA. Active 1996–present.**

Alkaline Trio is an American punk rock band formed in Chicago in December 1996 by guitarist/vocalist [[Members/M/Matt Skiba|Matt Skiba]], bassist [[Members/R/Rob Doran|Rob Doran]], and drummer [[Members/G/Glenn Porter|Glenn Porter]]. Known for pairing melodic, hook-driven punk rock with a dark, gothic lyrical sensibility drawing on alcoholism, heartbreak, and death, the band built a devoted following through the early 2000s with albums like *Goddamnit* (1998) and *From Here to Infirmary* (2001) before breaking into the Billboard top tier with *Good Mourning* (2003) and *This Addiction* (2010). Across nearly three decades the band has weathered multiple lineup changes around the constant core of Skiba and bassist [[Members/D/Dan Andriano|Dan Andriano]], including Skiba's 2015–2022 tenure as co-lead vocalist of [[Artists/B/Blink-182|Blink-182]].
