# AFI

**American punk rock band from Ukiah, California. Active 1991–present.**

AFI (A Fire Inside) are an American punk rock band formed in Ukiah, California in 1991. Fronted by vocalist Davey Havok, the band evolved from hardcore punk through horror punk and gothic rock to a more polished alternative rock sound over their career. They are known for hits like "Miss Murder," "Girl's Not Grey," and "The Leaving Song Pt. II," and have maintained a devoted following across multiple genre shifts.
