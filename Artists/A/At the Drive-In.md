# At the Drive-In

**At the Drive-In were an American post-hardcore band formed in El Paso, Texas in 1993, whose frenetic, emotionally explosive performances and the album *Relationship of Command* (2000) made them one of the most influential post-hardcore acts before their sudden breakup in 2001.**
