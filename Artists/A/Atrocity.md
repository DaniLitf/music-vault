# Atrocity

**German death metal band from Weil am Rhein. Active 1985–present.**

Atrocity are a German death metal band formed in Weil am Rhein in 1985, evolving from brutal death metal into industrial and symphonic metal territories. Led by vocalist and guitarist Alexander Krull, the band became known for pushing genre boundaries and incorporating electronic and orchestral elements into their later work.
