# Avenged Sevenfold

**Heavy metal / hard rock from Huntington Beach, California, USA. Active 1999–present.**

Avenged Sevenfold are an American heavy metal band formed in Huntington Beach, California in 1999, who evolved from metalcore beginnings into one of the most commercially successful mainstream heavy metal acts of the 2000s and 2010s. The band's sound shifted decisively toward traditional heavy metal with 2005's *City of Evil*, and their 2007 self-titled album and 2010's *Nightmare* — completed following the death of founding drummer Jimmy "The Rev" Sullivan in December 2009 — cemented them as arena-level headliners.
