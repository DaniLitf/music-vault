# Alice in Videoland

**Alice in Videoland are a Swedish electropop band formed in Stockholm in 2001, known for their danceable synth-driven sound and bilingual Swedish/English lyrics.**

Note: sources (Wikipedia, Discogs) consistently identify the founding members as **Toril Lindqvist** (vocals) and **Carl "Calle" Lundgren**, later joined by **Anders Alexander** (drums/synth/production) — not "Lisa Lindén and Martin Brelin" as previously stated on this page.
