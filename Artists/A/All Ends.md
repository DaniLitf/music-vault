# All Ends

**Alternative metal / alternative rock duet act from Gothenburg, Sweden. Active 2003–2016.**

All Ends were a female-fronted duet alternative metal band from Gothenburg, formed in 2003 as a side project of In Flames guitarists [[Members/B/Björn Gelotte]] and [[Members/J/Jesper Strömblad]], alongside vocalists Tinna Karlsdotter and Emma Gelotte and drummer Joseph "Joey" Skansås. The band signed to GUN Records/Sony BMG, released two studio albums and an EP, and went on indefinite hiatus after 2010, which guitarist Björn Gelotte later called "essentially dead" in 2016.
