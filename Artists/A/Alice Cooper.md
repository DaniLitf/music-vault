# Alice Cooper

**American hard rock / shock rock from Phoenix, Arizona. Active 1968–1975, 1999–present.**

Alice Cooper is an American rock band formed in Phoenix, Arizona, in 1968, known for pioneering shock rock with theatrical, horror-infused stage shows. The original band consisted of lead vocalist Vincent Furnier (who adopted the stage name Alice Cooper), lead guitarist Glen Buxton, rhythm guitarist Michael Bruce, bassist Dennis Dunaway, and drummer Neal Smith. After the original band dissolved in 1975, Vincent Furnier legally changed his name to Alice Cooper and continued as a solo artist with a rotating cast of backing musicians.

The original Alice Cooper band rose to fame in the early 1970s with the hit single "I'm Eighteen" (1970) and the international No. 1 "School's Out" (1972). Their 1973 album *Billion Dollar Babies* topped charts worldwide. The band was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2011. As a solo artist, Cooper has released 30 studio albums and sold over 50 million records worldwide.
