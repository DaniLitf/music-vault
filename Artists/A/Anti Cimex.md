# Anti Cimex

**Swedish D-beat / crust punk from Gothenburg, Sweden. Active 1981–1993.**

Anti Cimex was a Swedish D-beat and crust punk band formed in 1981, one of the first bands to define Scandinavian hardcore punk. Their second 7-inch EP *Raped Ass* (1983) is considered a subgenre-defining D-beat record. The band went through numerous lineup changes over their 12-year existence, with only core member Tomas Jonsson and drummer Charlie Claeson remaining across most iterations. They disbanded in 1993, with members going on to form or join Driller Killer, Wolfpack, and other Swedish punk acts.
