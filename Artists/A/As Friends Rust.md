# As Friends Rust

**Melodic hardcore from Gainesville, Florida, USA. Active 1996–2002, 2008–present.**

As Friends Rust is an American melodic hardcore band formed in Davie, Florida in September 1996 around vocalist [[Members/D/Damien Moyal]], and re-established in Gainesville in 1997 with a line-up drawn largely from Moyal's other band Culture. Pairing hardcore urgency with wry, hook-heavy punk songwriting, the band released its debut album *Won* (2001), continued briefly as Salem after major line-up upheaval in 2002, and reunited with its classic 1998–2000 line-up in 2008. It remains active, releasing its second album *Any Joy* in 2023.

## Connections

- [[Artists/A/Against Me!]] – Andrew Seward (Against Me!'s long-time bassist has been As Friends Rust's bassist since 2022)
- [[Artists/H/Hot Water Music]] – Jason Black (Hot Water Music's bassist was nominally recruited for As Friends Rust's 1997 reformation)
- [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]] – Damien Moyal, Timothy Kirkpatrick, Joseph Simmons (the 1997–98 As Friends Rust line-up was drawn largely from Culture; the three toured Europe with Culture in 1998)
- [[Artists/M/Morning Again]] – Damien Moyal (Moyal fronted Florida hardcore band Morning Again in the same era)
- [[Artists/B/Bird of Ill Omen]] – Damien Moyal, Peter Bartsocas, Thomas Rankine (Moyal fronted the Bird 1996–1997 and named As Friends Rust after its lyric "Now Ruin Is"; Bartsocas and Rankine were the Bird's guitarist and bassist)
