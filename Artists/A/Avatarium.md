# Avatarium

**Doom metal / hard rock from Stockholm, Sweden. Active 2013–present.**

Avatarium are a Swedish doom rock band from Stockholm, founded in 2013 by Candlemass bassist and songwriter Leif Edling as a vehicle for material outside that band, before stepping back from touring for health reasons and becoming a behind-the-scenes songwriter. The band draws two of its members — drummer Lars Sköld and (for a touring stretch) bassist Anders Iwers — from the gothic metal band Tiamat.
