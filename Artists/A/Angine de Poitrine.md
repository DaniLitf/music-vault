# Angine de Poitrine

**Math rock / experimental rock from Saguenay, Quebec, Canada. Active 2019–present.**

Angine de Poitrine is a Canadian rock duo known for complex microtonal compositions, absurdist masked performances, and intricate rhythmic structures. The anonymous members perform as Khn de Poitrine and Klek de Poitrine.
