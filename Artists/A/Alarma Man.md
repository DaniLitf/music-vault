# Alarma Man

**Instrumental post-punk/math-rock band from Gothenburg, Sweden. Active 2000s.**

Alarma Man are a Swedish instrumental post-punk and math-rock band from Gothenburg, described by their label as "Göteborg, Sweden's most powerful instrumental post-punk math-rock spazz band." The band released their self-titled debut album in 2005, followed by *Love Forever* in 2010.
