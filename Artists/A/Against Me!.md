# Against Me!

**Punk rock / folk punk band from Gainesville, Florida, USA. Active 1997–2020 (hiatus).**

Against Me! is an American punk rock band formed in 1997 in Naples, Florida, by singer-guitarist [[Members/L/Laura Jane Grace|Laura Jane Grace]], who relocated the project to Gainesville, Florida the same year. Beginning as raw, acoustic-leaning folk punk, the band electrified over three independent albums before signing to Sire Records for 2007's *New Wave*, which became a critical and commercial breakthrough. In May 2012, Grace publicly came out as a trans woman, and the band's sixth album, *Transgender Dysphoria Blues* (2014), became an acclaimed, autobiographical account of her transition. The band entered an indefinite hiatus in 2020 amid the COVID-19 pandemic, with Grace pursuing a solo career.
