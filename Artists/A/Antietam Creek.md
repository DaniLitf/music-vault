# Antietam Creek

**Hard rock / heavy metal from Cleveland, Ohio, USA. Active 2025–present.**

Antietam Creek is a solo project created and fronted by Ryan Farrell, best known as the bassist and keyboardist Dr. F of the masked alternative metal band Mushroomhead. The project marks a departure from Farrell's work with Mushroomhead, blending hard rock and heavy metal with overtones of grunge, classic rock, classical, and jazz. Farrell wrote, performed, engineered, and produced the debut album entirely from his home studio.
