# Angels & Airwaves

**Alternative rock / space rock band from San Diego, California, USA. Active 2005–present.**

Angels & Airwaves is an American rock band founded in 2005 by [[Members/T/Tom DeLonge|Tom DeLonge]] after [[Artists/B/Blink-182|Blink-182]] went on indefinite hiatus. Built around a cinematic, U2- and Pink Floyd-influenced sound rather than the pop-punk of DeLonge's earlier band, the group has released six studio albums and become known for pairing its records with multimedia projects, including the documentary *Start the Machine* (2008) and the accompanying feature film *Love* (2011). DeLonge continued the band through Blink-182's 2009–2015 reunion and made it his primary focus after leaving Blink-182 again in 2015, though he later rejoined Blink-182 in 2022 while keeping Angels & Airwaves active.
