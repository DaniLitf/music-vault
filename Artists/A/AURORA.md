# AURORA

**Art pop / electropop singer-songwriter from Bergen, Norway. Active 2012–present.**

Aurora Aksnes, known mononymously as AURORA, is a Norwegian singer, songwriter, and producer whose music blends art pop, folk-pop, synth-pop, and electronic textures. She broke through internationally with *Running with the Wolves* and *All My Demons Greeting Me as a Friend*, later expanding into soundtrack work, collaborations, and the electronic duo [[Artists/T/Tomora]] with [[Members/T/Tom Rowlands]] of [[Artists/T/The Chemical Brothers]].
