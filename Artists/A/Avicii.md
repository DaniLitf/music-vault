# Avicii

**Avicii (born Tim Bergling, September 8, 1989 – April 20, 2018) was a Swedish DJ, remixer, and record producer whose melodic, folk-inflected take on progressive house helped bring EDM to Top 40 radio in the early 2010s, making him one of the best-selling electronic artists of all time.**

Avicii is a solo artist. See [[Members/A/Avicii]] for biography.
