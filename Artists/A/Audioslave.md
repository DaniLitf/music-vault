# Audioslave

**Audioslave were an American rock band formed in Los Angeles in 2001, combining the rhythm section and guitarists of Rage Against the Machine with Soundgarden vocalist Chris Cornell following RATM's breakup.**

## Connections

- [[Artists/S/Soundgarden]] – Chris Cornell was the lead vocalist of Soundgarden; Audioslave formed while Soundgarden was on indefinite hiatus
- [[Artists/P/Prophets of Rage]] – After Audioslave dissolved, Morello, Commerford, and Wilk returned to RATM and also formed Prophets of Rage
