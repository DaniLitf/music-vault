# Angels and Agony

**Dutch synthpop/EBM band from the Netherlands. Active 1990s–present.**

Angels and Agony are a Dutch synthpop and EBM band formed in the Netherlands in the 1990s. Known for their melodic synthesizer-driven sound blending catchy pop hooks with darker EBM and dark wave elements, they are part of the European dark electronic music scene.
