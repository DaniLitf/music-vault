# Apoptygma Berzerk

**Electronic rock / futurepop from Sarpsborg, Norway. Active 1989–present.**

Apoptygma Berzerk (often abbreviated as APB or APOP) is a Norwegian electronic band founded by Stephan Groth and Jon Erik Martinsen in 1989. The band evolved from EBM and futurepop roots into a broader electronic rock and synth-pop sound, achieving chart success in Germany and Scandinavia. They have toured extensively with VNV Nation, Icon of Coil, and Beborn Beton, and are considered pioneers of the futurepop genre.
