# Against

**Crossover thrash one-off project from Venice, California. Active 2006.**

Against was assembled in May 2006 by bassist [[Members/L/Louiche Mayorga]], reuniting original [[Artists/S/Suicidal Tendencies]]/Los Cycos members Mayorga, [[Members/G/Grant Estes]] (guitar), and [[Members/A/Amery Smith]] (drums), alongside [[Artists/E/Excel]] frontman [[Members/D/Dan Clements]] and former No Mercy vocalist [[Members/K/Kevin Guercio]]. The project recorded two tracks for the compilation *Welcome 2 Venice* (2006), a 21st-anniversary follow-up to the Venice, California scene compilation *Welcome to Venice* (1985).
