# Anaal Nathrakh

**Extreme metal duo from Birmingham, England. Active 1999–present.**

Anaal Nathrakh are an English extreme metal band formed in Birmingham in 1999 by multi-instrumentalist Mick Kenney and vocalist Dave Hunt, taking their name from a phrase in the film *Excalibur*. Drawing on Norwegian black metal bands such as Mayhem, Burzum, and Darkthrone, the band fuses grindcore, black metal, death metal, and industrial into an unrelentingly extreme sound, releasing eleven studio albums since their 2001 debut *The Codex Necro*.
