# Autechre

**Autechre are a British electronic music duo formed in Rochdale in 1987 by Rob Brown and Sean Booth, one of the most influential and consistently innovative acts in electronic music, whose work has pushed the boundaries of IDM (intelligent dance music) across three decades.**
