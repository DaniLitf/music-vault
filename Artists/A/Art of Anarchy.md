# Art of Anarchy

**Art of Anarchy are an American hard rock supergroup formed in 2011, notable for featuring Scott Weiland of Stone Temple Pilots and Velvet Revolver as their original vocalist before his death in 2015.**

| Year | Title | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| 2015 | *Art of Anarchy* | debut; Scott Weiland on vocals |
| 2017 | *The Madness* | Scott Stapp on vocals |
| 2019 | *Let There Be Anarchy* | |
