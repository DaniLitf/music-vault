# Angel City Outlaws

**Hard rock / glam metal from Los Angeles, California, USA. Active 2005–2006.**

Angel City Outlaws was a short-lived hard rock supergroup formed by RATT drummer Bobby Blotzer, bringing together several musicians with ties to the Los Angeles glam metal scene. The band featured vocalist John Corabi (Mötley Crüe, The Scream), bassist Robbie Crane (RATT), guitarist Keri Kelli (RATT, Alice Cooper), and drummer Bobby Blotzer (RATT). Former Warrant frontman Jani Lane also participated in early recordings. The project was active in the mid-2000s and produced several demo tracks including "Serial Killer," "Bruised," and "Exit," but never released a full album.
