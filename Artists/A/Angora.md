# Angora

**Hard rock / glam metal from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, USA. Active mid-1980s.**

Angora was a short-lived hard rock band from Philadelphia, formed in the mid-1980s by vocalist and guitarist John Corabi. The band gained the attention of KISS bassist Gene Simmons, who briefly managed them and suggested a name change to "8 Ball." They recorded a six-song demo tape and contributed the track "Shake Shake" (produced by Rob Cavallo) to KNAC radio's compilation *Son of Pure Rock* (1988). Despite building a local following and relocating to Los Angeles, internal tensions caused the band to disband before securing a recording deal.
