# A Perfect Circle

**A Perfect Circle are an American rock band formed in Los Angeles in 1999 by guitarist Billy Howerdel and Tool vocalist Maynard James Keenan, who recorded and toured during extended gaps in Tool's schedule.**

## Discography

### Studio Albums
| Year | Title | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| 2000 | *Mer de Noms* | Debut; #1 US; fastest-selling debut in history at the time |
| 2003 | *Thirteenth Step* | #2 US |
| 2004 | *eMOTIVe* | Covers album; political themes |
| 2018 | *Eat the Elephant* | 14 years after previous original album |
