# Aura Noir

**Black/thrash metal band from Oslo, Norway. Active 1993–present.**

Aura Noir are a Norwegian black/thrash metal band formed in Oslo in 1993 by Aggressor (Carl-Michael Eide) and Apollyon (Ole Jørgen Moe), heavily influenced by early thrash acts like Voivod, Slayer, Sodom, and Kreator. Guitarist Blasphemer (Rune Eriksen) of Mayhem joined in 1996 for their debut album, and the trio has remained the band's lineup ever since, continuing even after Aggressor was paralyzed from the ankles down in a 2005 accident.
