# Arch Enemy

**Arch Enemy are a Swedish melodic death metal band formed in Älmhult in 1995 by guitarist Michael Amott, who left Carcass to create his own melodic death metal project, later gaining international attention with vocalist Angela Gossow and then Alissa White-Gluz.**
