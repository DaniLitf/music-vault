# Air

**Electronic / downtempo duo from Versailles, France. Active 1995–present.**

Air (stylised AIR) is a French electronic music duo formed in Versailles in 1995 by [[Members/N/Nicolas Godin]] and [[Members/J/Jean-Benoît Dunckel]], who met as students and had previously played together in the band Orange. Their debut album *Moon Safari* (1998), anchored by the single "Sexy Boy", became an international breakthrough and helped define a strand of lush, vintage-synthesizer-driven downtempo pop associated with the "French touch" scene. The duo went on to score Sofia Coppola's *The Virgin Suicides* and released five further studio albums combining analogue synthesizers, live instrumentation, and orchestral arrangements.
