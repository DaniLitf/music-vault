# Alice in Chains

**Alice in Chains are an American rock band formed in Seattle in 1987, one of the defining acts of the grunge era, distinguished from their peers by the heavy metal roots of their guitar work and the unique dual-vocal harmonies of Layne Staley and Jerry Cantrell.**
