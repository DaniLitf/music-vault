# Astor Piazzolla

**Astor Piazzolla (1921-03-11 – 1992-07-04) was an Argentine tango composer and bandoneón player who revolutionized traditional tango into the nuevo tango genre, incorporating jazz and classical influences.**

Note: this is a curated list of canonical/major releases, not the complete Discogs catalog (Piazzolla has a very large number of reissues and alternate pressings across labels).
