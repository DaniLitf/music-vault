# A Guy Called Gerald

**A Guy Called Gerald is the stage name of Gerald Simpson, a British electronic musician from Manchester who co-founded [[Artists/0-9/808 State]] in 1987 before leaving to pioneer acid house, jungle, and drum and bass as a solo artist.**
