# Absolute Body Control

**Belgian EBM band from Brussels. Active 1981–present.**

Absolute Body Control are a Belgian EBM and synthpop band formed in Brussels in 1981, one of the earliest and most influential EBM acts, helping to define the genre alongside DAF and Front 242. They were pioneers of the European body music scene in the early 1980s.
