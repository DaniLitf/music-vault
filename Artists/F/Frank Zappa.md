# Frank Zappa

**Frank Zappa (December 21, 1940 – December 4, 1993) was an American musician, composer, satirist, and bandleader from Baltimore, one of the most prolific and musically eclectic figures in rock history, known for his virtuosic guitar playing, satirical lyrics, and a catalogue of over 60 albums spanning rock, jazz, classical, and avant-garde music.**

Frank Zappa is a solo artist. See [[Members/F/Frank Zappa]] for biography.
