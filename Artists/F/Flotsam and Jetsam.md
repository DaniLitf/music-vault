# Flotsam and Jetsam

**Thrash metal from Phoenix, Arizona, USA. Active 1981–present.**

Flotsam and Jetsam are an American thrash metal band formed in Phoenix, Arizona in 1981 (originally as Paradox, then Dredlox and The Dogz), best remembered for their debut *Doomsday for the Deceiver* (1986) and for having launched bassist Jason Newsted, who left in 1986 to join [[Artists/M/Metallica]]. Vocalist Eric "A.K." Knutson is the band's only member to have been present for its entire history.
