# Front 242

**Front 242 are a Belgian electronic body music band formed in 1981 in Brussels, widely regarded as the inventors of EBM (Electronic Body Music), a genre blending industrial electronics with danceable rhythms, influential on industrial, goth, and techno scenes worldwide.**
