# Fecal Matter

**Fecal Matter were a short-lived American punk band formed in Aberdeen, Washington in 1985, historically significant as the first band of Kurt Cobain and Krist Novoselic before they formed Nirvana.**

| Year | Title | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| 1985 | *Illiteracy Will Prevail* | demo tape; only release |

## Connections

- [[Artists/N/Nirvana]] — Kurt Cobain and Krist Novoselic went on to co-found Nirvana after Fecal Matter dissolved.
- [[Artists/T/The Melvins]] — Dale Crover drummed for Fecal Matter before returning to his primary band The Melvins.
