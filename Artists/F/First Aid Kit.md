# First Aid Kit

**First Aid Kit are a Swedish folk duo formed in Stockholm in 2007 by sisters Klara and Johanna Söderberg, known for their rich harmonies, Americana influences, and internationally acclaimed albums like *The Lion's Roar* (2012) and *Stay Gold* (2014).**
