# Fehlfarben

**German punk/new wave band from Moers. Active 1979–present.**

Fehlfarben are a German punk and new wave band formed in Moers in 1979. They are one of Germany's most important early punk and Neue Deutsche Welle (NDW) bands, best known for their 1980 hit single "Ein Jahr (Geht's vorbei)" and their influential debut album *Monarchie und Alltag* (1980), which blended punk energy with ska, reggae, and new wave influences.
