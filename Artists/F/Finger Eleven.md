# Finger Eleven

**Alternative rock / post-grunge from Burlington, Ontario, Canada. Active 1990–present.**

Finger Eleven formed in Burlington, Ontario in 1990 as Rainbow Butt Monkeys, featuring vocalist [[Members/S/Scott Anderson]], guitarists [[Members/J/James Black]] and [[Members/R/Rick Jackett]], bassist [[Members/S/Sean Anderson]], and drummer [[Members/R/Rob Gommerman]]. Renamed Finger Eleven in 1997, the band broke through in Canada with *The Greyest of Blue Skies* (2000) after [[Members/R/Rich Beddoe]] replaced Gommerman on drums. Their 2003 self-titled album went gold in the US and platinum in Canada on the strength of "One Thing," and *Them vs. You vs. Me* (2007) produced their biggest hit, "Paralyzer," which topped the Canadian Hot 100 and both US rock charts and won a Juno Award for Rock Album of the Year. Beddoe left at the end of 2013 and was replaced by [[Members/S/Steve Molella]] the following year, after session drummer Chris Powell filled in on *Five Crooked Lines* (2015). The band toured extensively with [[Artists/S/Seether]] and [[Artists/T/Three Days Grace]] in the early-2010s, and released their eighth studio album, *Last Night on Earth*, in 2025.
