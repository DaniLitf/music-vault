# Fold Zandura

**Alternative rock from California. Active 1995–present.**

Fold Zandura is an American alternative rock band from California, formed in December 1995 by Jyro Xhan and Jerome Fontamillas after the breakup of their previous band, Mortal. The band's sound blends post-grunge alt-rock with experimental electronic and industrial elements. Despite limited commercial success, they built a following in the Christian rock scene and released four albums between 1995 and 1999.
