# Free Fall

**Swedish rock band. Active 2009–present.**

Free Fall are a Swedish rock band formed in 2009 by guitarist Mattias Bärjed (The Soundtrack of Our Lives) with bassist Jan Martens (Nymphet Noodlers), drummer Ludwig Dahlberg (The [International] Noise Conspiracy), and vocalist Kim Fransson. Described by Bärjed as "freedom rock," their sound draws on early Van Halen, the Stooges, and the Who.
