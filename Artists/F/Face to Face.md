# Face to Face

**Punk rock / skate punk from Victorville, California, USA. Active 1991–2004, 2008–present.**

Face to Face is an American punk rock band formed in Victorville in 1991 by frontman Trever Keith, bassist Matt Riddle, and drummer Rob Kurth. The band rose to prominence with their 1995 album *Big Choice*, which featured the radio hit "Disconnected" that received heavy rotation on KROQ-FM and appeared in the films *Tank Girl* and *National Lampoon's Senior Trip*. After disbanding in 2004 to allow members to pursue other projects, the band reunited in 2008 and has since released five additional studio albums while continuing to tour and record.
