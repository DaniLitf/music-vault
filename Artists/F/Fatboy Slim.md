# Fatboy Slim

**Fatboy Slim is a British DJ and producer, the alias of Norman Cook (born July 31, 1963, in Brighton), one of the most successful and influential figures in big beat music, known for euphoric dancefloor anthems like "Praise You," "Right Here, Right Now," and "Weapon of Choice."**

Fatboy Slim is a solo artist. See [[Members/N/Norman Cook]] for biography.
