# Foetus

**Foetus (also known as You've Got Foetus on Your Breath, Foetus Inc., Scraping Foetus off the Wheel, and many other variations) is a project by Australian musician J.G. Thirlwell, a prolific and confrontational composer and performer active since 1981 who has been a major figure in the industrial and experimental music underground.**

J.G. Thirlwell is a solo artist. See [[Members/J/J.G. Thirlwell]] for biography.
