# Far & Son

**Swedish hip hop duo from Sweden. Active 2008–2018.**

Far & Son were a Swedish hip hop duo consisting of Frej Larsson and Simon Gärdenfors. Despite the name's suggestion of a father-son relationship, the pair are not related — the name is wordplay. The duo released a string of singles, EPs, and one album between 2008 and 2018.
