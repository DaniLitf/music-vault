# Funker Vogt

**German EBM/industrial band from Bremen. Active 1996–present.**

Funker Vogt are a German EBM and industrial band formed in Bremen in 1996 by Jens Kramer (vocals) and Gerrit Thomas (keyboards). Known for their aggressive, martial-influenced EBM sound with hard-hitting beats and commanding vocals, they are a prominent act in the European industrial and EBM scene.
