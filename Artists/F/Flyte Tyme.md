# Flyte Tyme

**Funk / R&B band from Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA. Active 1972–1981.**

Flyte Tyme was a Minneapolis funk band formed in the early 1970s that served as the precursor to The Time. The band originally featured Cynthia Johnson on lead vocals, later replaced by Alexander O'Neal, with members including Jimmy Jam, Terry Lewis, Monte Moir, and Jellybean Johnson. In 1981, Prince recruited several members of Flyte Tyme — along with Morris Day and Jesse Johnson — to form The Time.
