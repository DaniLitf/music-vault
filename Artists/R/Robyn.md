# Robyn

**Robyn (born Robin Miriam Carlsson on 1979-06-12 in Stockholm, Sweden) is a Swedish singer and songwriter who became a defining voice in electro-pop after breaking away from a major label to self-release her music, renowned for emotionally candid songs delivered with dancefloor intensity.**
