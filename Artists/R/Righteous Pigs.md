# Righteous Pigs

**Death metal / grindcore from Las Vegas, Nevada. Active 1986–1990, 2012–2013.**

Righteous Pigs were an American death metal and grindcore band formed in Las Vegas in 1986. They released two studio albums on Nuclear Blast Records — *Live and Learn* (1989) and *Stress Related* (1990) — before guitarist Mitch Harris left to join Napalm Death, leading to the band's breakup. The band briefly reunited for a Maryland Deathfest appearance in 2013.
