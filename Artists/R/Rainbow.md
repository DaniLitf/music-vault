# Rainbow

**Rainbow (also known as Ritchie Blackmore's Rainbow) is a British hard rock band formed in 1975 by guitarist [[Members/R/Ritchie Blackmore]] after his departure from [[Artists/D/Deep Purple]], featuring a string of vocalists including Ronnie James Dio and Graham Bonnet.**

## Members

### Core / Official Members
- [[Members/R/Ritchie Blackmore]] — guitar (1975–1997, 2016–present)
- [[Members/R/Ronnie James Dio]] — vocals (1975–1979; also [[Artists/D/Dio]]; died 2010)
- [[Members/D/Don Airey]] — keyboards (1979–1981; also [[Artists/D/Deep Purple]])
- [[Members/R/Roger Glover]] — bass (1979–1984; also [[Artists/D/Deep Purple]])

### Past Members
- [[Members/C/Cozy Powell]] — drums (1975–1980; died 1998)
- [[Members/G/Graham Bonnet]] — vocals (1979–1980)
- [[Members/J/Joe Lynn Turner]] — vocals (1980–1984)
- [[Members/D/Doogie White]] — vocals (1995–1997)

## Connections

- [[Artists/D/Deep Purple]] — Rainbow was founded by Ritchie Blackmore after leaving Deep Purple in 1975; Don Airey and Roger Glover later joined Rainbow having also served in Deep Purple.
- [[Artists/D/Dio]] — Ronnie James Dio, Rainbow's original vocalist (1975–1979), went on to found his own band Dio after leaving.
