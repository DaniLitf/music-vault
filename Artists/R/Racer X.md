# Racer X

**American heavy metal band from Los Angeles. Active 1984–1989, 1997–present.**

Racer X are an American heavy metal band formed in 1984 in Los Angeles, known for their technically proficient speed metal style. The band was co-founded by guitarist Paul Gilbert, who later co-founded Mr. Big, and bassist Juan Alderete.

## Members

### Core / Official Members
- Jeff Martin — vocals (1984–1989, 1997–present)
- Paul Gilbert — guitar (1984–1988, 1997–present)
- Juan Alderete — bass (1984–1989, 1997–present)
- Scott Travis — drums (1986–1989, 1997–present)

### Past Members
- Bruce Bouillet — guitar (1986–1989)
