# Rob Zombie

**Rob Zombie is an American musician and filmmaker born Robert Bartleh Cummings on 1965-01-12 in Haverhill, Massachusetts, who fronted White Zombie before launching a highly successful solo career fusing heavy metal with horror film aesthetics.**
