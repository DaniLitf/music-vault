# Ray & Anita

**Dutch electronic duo. Active 2009–2012.**

Ray & Anita is a Dutch electronic music duo comprising Ray Slijngaard and Anita Doth, who were previously the front people of the successful Eurodance project [[Artists/2/2 Unlimited]]. They reunited in 2009 to perform at the "I Love the 90's" concert in Hasselt, Belgium, and continued performing together until 2012.

## Members

### Core / Official Members
- Ray Slijngaard — vocals, rap
- Anita Doth — vocals

## Connections

- [[Artists/2/2 Unlimited]] — Ray Slijngaard and Anita Doth were the vocalists and public faces of 2 Unlimited from 1991 to 1996.
