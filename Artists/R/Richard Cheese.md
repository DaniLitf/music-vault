# Richard Cheese

**American lounge singer character created by vocalist [[Members/M/Mark Jonathan Davis]], known for his extravagant big-band lounge-style cover versions of heavy metal, hip-hop, and alternative rock songs.**

Richard Cheese is a solo artist project. See [[Members/M/Mark Jonathan Davis]] for biography.
