# Rotterdam

**Progressive metal from Cleveland, Ohio. Active 1986–1988 (as part of the Firedance → Rotterdam → Torment → Ritual lineage).**

Rotterdam was an American progressive metal band from Cleveland, Ohio, formed in 1986 out of the earlier band Firedance (1985–1986). The band was named after the Dutch city of Rotterdam and was a transitional name in an evolving musical project that would later become Torment (1988–1992) and eventually Ritual (1992–present). The band was founded by vocalist Jim Hamar after leaving Breaker and brought together members of Abduction. Rotterdam's sound blended progressive metal with power and thrash influences, characterized by intricate guitar work and soaring vocals.
