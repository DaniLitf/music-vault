# Regorge

**British brutal death metal band from Glasgow. Active 1997–2002.**

Regorge were a British brutal death metal band formed in 1997 in Glasgow, Scotland. The band released one studio album, *Kingdoms of Derision* (2002), on Dead Again Records before splitting up.

## Members

### Core / Official Members
- Richy Graham — vocals
- Phil Wilson — bass, vocals (1997–2002; also [[Artists/T/The Rotted]])
- Ged Leitch — guitar (1997–2002)
- John Lee — drums (1997–2002; also [[Artists/M/Man Must Die]])

## Connections

- [[Artists/T/The Rotted]] — Phil Wilson was a member of Regorge and also performed with The Rotted (formerly Gorerotted).
- [[Artists/M/Man Must Die]] — John Lee was the drummer of Regorge and also played with Man Must Die.
