# Roy Harper

**English folk rock singer-songwriter and guitarist from Manchester, England. Active 1964–present.**

Roy Harper is an English folk singer, songwriter, poet, and guitarist known for his distinctive fingerstyle playing and lengthy, complex compositions. Signed to Harvest Records in the early 1970s, he built a devoted following and an outsized influence on peers — Led Zeppelin dedicated "Hats Off to (Roy) Harper" to him, and he became a recurring collaborator with [[Members/J/Jimmy Page]] and with [[Artists/P/Pink Floyd]], singing lead vocals on their "Have a Cigar" (1975) after offering to fill in when Roger Waters strained his voice. He also duetted repeatedly with Kate Bush, and co-wrote "Short and Sweet" with David Gilmour.
