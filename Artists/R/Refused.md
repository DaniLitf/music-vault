# Refused

**Refused are a Swedish hardcore punk band formed in Umeå in 1991, who dissolved in 1998 at the height of their influence before reuniting in 2012, best known for *The Shape of Punk to Come* — a record widely credited with reshaping the sonic possibilities of hardcore.**
