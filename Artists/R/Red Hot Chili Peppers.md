# Red Hot Chili Peppers

**Red Hot Chili Peppers are an American rock band formed in Los Angeles in 1983, one of the best-selling bands of all time, known for their fusion of funk, punk, psychedelic rock, and hip-hop.**

## Connections

- [[Artists/P/Pearl Jam]] — Jack Irons, original RHCP drummer (1983–1988), later played drums for Pearl Jam (1994–1998).
- [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]] — Dave Navarro, RHCP guitarist (1993–1998), is a founding member of Jane's Addiction.
- [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]] — George Clinton produced *Freaky Styley* (1985).
- [[Artists/S/Satellite Party]] — Flea and John Frusciante guested on Perry Farrell's *Ultra Payloaded* (2007).
