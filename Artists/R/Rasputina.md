# Rasputina

**Rasputina are an American cello-based rock band founded in New York City in 1991 by Melora Creager, known for their unusual instrumentation, Victorian aesthetics, and dark, genre-bending compositions.**

## Members

### Current
- [[Members/M/Melora Creager]] – lead vocals, cello (1991–present)

### Past/Rotating
Rasputina has functioned largely as Melora Creager's solo project with a rotating cast of cellists. Notable past members include various uncredited session players; the lineup has rarely been stable outside of Creager herself.

## Connections

Rasputina toured as support for [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] and [[Artists/M/Marilyn Manson]] in the mid-1990s, though Melora Creager was never a formal member of either band. No direct membership overlaps with other vault artists.
