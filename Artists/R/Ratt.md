# Ratt

**Glam metal / heavy metal from Hollywood, California, USA. Active 1974–1992, 1996–2022.**

Ratt (stylized as RATT) was an American glam metal band formed in Hollywood, California, that achieved major commercial success in the 1980s. Known for hit singles "Round and Round" and "Lay It Down," the band sold millions of records worldwide and were a defining act of the Los Angeles hair metal scene. Their debut album *Out of the Cellar* (1984) is certified triple platinum.
