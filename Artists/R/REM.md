# R.E.M.

**R.E.M. were an American rock band formed in Athens, Georgia in 1980, pioneers of the college rock and indie scenes who became one of the most commercially successful and critically acclaimed bands of the 1980s and 1990s before dissolving in 2011.**

## Connections

- [[Artists/R/Revolting Cocks]] – Bill Rieflin, who joined R.E.M. as touring/studio drummer from 2004–2011, was a founding member of Revolting Cocks
- [[Artists/M/Ministry]] – Bill Rieflin was also a longtime member of Ministry, linking R.E.M. to the industrial scene
- [[Artists/K/King Crimson]] – Bill Rieflin later joined King Crimson, where he remained until his death in 2020
