# L.A. Guns

**Hard rock / glam metal from Los Angeles, California. Active 1983–present.**

L.A. Guns is an American hard rock band formed in Los Angeles in 1983 by guitarist Tracii Guns. The band was part of the Sunset Strip glam metal scene and released their self-titled debut in 1988. They have gone through numerous lineup changes over the decades with Tracii Guns as the constant member.
