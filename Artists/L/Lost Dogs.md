# Lost Dogs

**Christian alternative rock supergroup from USA. Active 1992–present.**

Lost Dogs is a Christian alternative rock supergroup formed in 1992, bringing together frontmen from several of the genre's most respected bands. The group blends folk, rock, and Americana influences, drawing members from Daniel Amos, The 77s, Adam Again, and Starflyer 59 circles.
