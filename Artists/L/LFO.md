# LFO

**LFO (Low Frequency Oscillation) were a British electronic music duo formed in Leeds in 1988, consisting of Mark Bell and Gez Varley, pioneers of the bleep techno style and Warp Records' early sound, best known for their 1990 single "LFO."**
