# Linkin Park

**Linkin Park are an American alternative metal band from Agoura Hills, California, active 1996–2017, 2024–present, one of the most commercially successful rock bands of the 2000s, blending heavy instrumentation with electronic elements and introspective lyrics.**

The band defined the nu-metal genre with albums like *Hybrid Theory* and *Meteora* before exploring more electronic and experimental directions. Frontman Chester Bennington died in 2017, but the band reunited in 2024 with a new vocalist.
