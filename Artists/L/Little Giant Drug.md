# Little Giant Drug

**Alternative rock side project of Napalm Death members Mitch Harris and Shane Embury from Birmingham, England. Active 1996–1998.**

Little Giant Drug was a side project formed in 1996 by Mitch Harris and Shane Embury of Napalm Death, driven by their shared love for 4AD-inspired indie rock. They enlisted vocalist/guitarist Simon Orme from nearby Walsall and recorded an album at Framework Studio in Birmingham in 1996. The resulting *Prismcast* was released in 2000 on Org Records and the band played a handful of shows before dissolving.
