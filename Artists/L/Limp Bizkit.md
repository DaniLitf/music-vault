# Limp Bizkit

**Nu metal / rap rock from Jacksonville, Florida, USA. Active 1994–present.**

Limp Bizkit is an American nu metal band formed in 1994 by frontman [[Members/F/Fred Durst]]. Known for their fusion of heavy metal, rap, and turntablism, the band achieved massive mainstream success in the late 1990s and early 2000s with albums like *Significant Other* (1999) and *Chocolate Starfish and the Hot Dog Flavored Water* (2000). Guitarist [[Members/W/Wes Borland]] has been a core member through most of the band's history, except for a hiatus (2001–2004).
