# Liaisons Dangereuses

**German industrial/EBM band formed in Düsseldorf in 1981. Active 1981–1983.**

Liaisons Dangereuses was a short-lived but highly influential German electronic music band formed in 1981 by Beate Bartel and Chrislo Haas (both formerly of CHBB and DAF), along with French-German vocalist Krishna Goineau. Their self-titled debut album, mixed at Conny Plank's studio, is considered a pioneering work in industrial dance music and EBM. The track "Los Niños del Parque" became a cult classic, frequently spun by Chicago house and Detroit techno DJs.
