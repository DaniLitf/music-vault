# Lisa Gerrard

**Lisa Gerrard (born April 12, 1961, in Melbourne, Australia) is an Australian composer and vocalist, best known as co-founder and vocalist of Dead Can Dance, and for her film scoring work including *Gladiator* (2000).**

Lisa Gerrard is a solo artist. See [[Members/L/Lisa Gerrard]] for biography.
