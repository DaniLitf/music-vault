# Leila K

**Leila K (born Leila El Khalifi, September 6, 1971, in Stockholm, Sweden) is a Swedish singer and rapper of Moroccan descent, who became a prominent figure in European Eurodance and hip house in the early 1990s, best known for "Got to Get" and "Rok the Nation" with Rob'n'Raz.**

Leila K is a solo artist. See [[Members/L/Leila K]] for biography.
