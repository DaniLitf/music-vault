# Lorde

**Lorde (born Ella Marija Lani Yelich-O'Connor, November 7, 1996, in Auckland, New Zealand) is a New Zealand singer-songwriter, who broke through at age 16 with "Royals" and the debut album *Pure Heroine* (2013), known for her minimalist production and introspective, anti-materialist lyrics.**

Lorde is a solo artist. See [[Members/L/Lorde]] for biography.
