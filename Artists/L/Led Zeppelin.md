# Led Zeppelin

**Led Zeppelin were an English rock band formed in London in 1968, consisting of Jimmy Page, Robert Plant, John Paul Jones, and John Bonham, widely regarded as one of the greatest and most influential rock bands of all time, known for albums like *Led Zeppelin IV* (1971) and a pioneering heavy sound that laid the groundwork for hard rock and heavy metal.**
