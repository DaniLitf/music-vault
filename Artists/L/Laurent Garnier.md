# Laurent Garnier

**Laurent Garnier (born February 1, 1966, in Boulogne-Billancourt, France) is a French DJ and electronic musician, a pioneer of techno and house in France, a resident DJ at the legendary Haçienda club in Manchester, and one of the most influential figures in European electronic music.**

Laurent Garnier is a solo artist. See [[Members/L/Laurent Garnier]] for biography.
