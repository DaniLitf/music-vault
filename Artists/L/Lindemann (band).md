# Lindemann

**German-Swedish industrial metal super-duo from Germany/Sweden. Active 2014–2020.**

Lindemann was a German-Swedish metal project fronted by [[Members/T/Till Lindemann]] of [[Artists/R/Rammstein]] with Swedish multi-instrumentalist and producer [[Members/P/Peter Tägtgren]] of [[Artists/H/Hypocrisy]] and [[Artists/P/Pain]]. The duo released two studio albums, combining Lindemann's theatrical baritone and German/English lyric style with Tägtgren's industrial metal production before ending their collaboration in 2020.
