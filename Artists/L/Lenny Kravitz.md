# Lenny Kravitz

**Lenny Kravitz (born May 26, 1964, in Manhattan, New York) is an American singer-songwriter, multi-instrumentalist, and record producer, known for his eclectic retro style blending rock, blues, soul, and funk, and for playing nearly every instrument on his records.**

Lenny Kravitz is a solo artist. See [[Members/L/Lenny Kravitz]] for biography.
