# Louise Hoffsten

**Louise Hoffsten (born June 25, 1965, in Linköping, Sweden) is a Swedish blues singer, songwriter, and harmonica player, one of the most prominent Swedish blues artists, known for her powerful voice and her advocacy work after being diagnosed with multiple sclerosis.**

Louise Hoffsten is a solo artist. See [[Members/L/Louise Hoffsten]] for biography.
