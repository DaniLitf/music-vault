# Laibach

**Laibach are a Slovenian avant-garde group formed in Trbovlje in 1980, part of the NSK (Neue Slowenische Kunst) collective, known for their confrontational political imagery, martial industrial sound, and totalitarian aesthetic that deliberately provokes and subverts nationalist and fascist iconography.**
