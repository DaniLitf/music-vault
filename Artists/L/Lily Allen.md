# Lily Allen

**Lily Allen (born May 2, 1985, in London, England) is an English singer-songwriter, best known for her witty, satirical lyrics and her 2006 debut *Alright, Still*, which established her as a voice of early 2000s British pop culture.**

Lily Allen is a solo artist. See [[Members/L/Lily Allen]] for biography.
