# Lita Ford

**Lita Ford (born September 19, 1958, in London, England) is an English-American guitarist and singer, who rose to fame as the lead guitarist of the Runaways and had a successful solo hard rock career in the 1980s, best known for "Kiss Me Deadly" and the duet "Close My Eyes Forever" with Ozzy Osbourne.**
