# Lo Tom

**Indie rock supergroup from USA. Active 2016–present.**

Lo Tom is an indie rock supergroup formed in 2016, featuring members drawn from several prominent indie and Christian rock acts. The band's self-titled debut album showcased a blend of driving guitars and melodic songwriting, drawing on the collective experience of its members from Starflyer 59, Pedro the Lion, and Velour 100.
