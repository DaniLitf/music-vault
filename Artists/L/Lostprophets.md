**Alternative rock / Post-hardcore from Pontypridd, Wales. Active 1997–2013.**

Lostprophets were a Welsh rock band formed in 1997 by Ian Watkins and Lee Gaze after their previous band Fleshbind disbanded. The group evolved from early nu-metal influences into a distinctive post-hardcore sound, gaining mainstream success in the 2000s. The band disbanded in October 2013 following serious criminal charges against frontman Ian Watkins.
