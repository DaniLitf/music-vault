# Lee Perry

**Jamaican record producer, singer, and songwriter. Active 1958–2021.**

Lee Perry is a solo artist. See [[Members/L/Lee Perry]] for biography.

## Connections
- [[Artists/B/Bob Marley]] – Perry produced the Wailers' 1970–1971 recordings and worked extensively with the Barrett brothers (Aston and Carlton Barrett) who played on many Upsetters recordings
- [[Artists/T/The Orb]] – Perry collaborated on *The Orbserver in the Star House* (2012), blending dub with ambient electronic music
