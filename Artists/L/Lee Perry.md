# Lee Perry

**Lee "Scratch" Perry (March 20, 1936 – August 29, 2021) was a Jamaican record producer, singer, and songwriter, a pioneering and visionary figure in reggae and dub music known for his revolutionary studio techniques, surrealist approach to recording, and production work with Bob Marley, the Wailers, and the Congos.**
