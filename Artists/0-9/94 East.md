# 94 East

**Funk / R&B from Minneapolis, USA. Active 1975–1979.**

94 East was an American funk band formed in December 1975 by Pepé Willie, the former husband of Prince's cousin. The group gave a teenage Prince his first studio recording experience, featuring him on guitar alongside childhood friend André Cymone on bass. Named after Interstate 94, the band was fronted by Colonel Abrams and recorded material between 1975 and 1979 before disbanding when Prince became a star. Their recordings were later released on compilation albums beginning with *Minneapolis Genius* in 1985.
