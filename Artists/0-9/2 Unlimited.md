**2 Unlimited is a Dutch Eurodance project formed in 1991 by producers Jean-Paul De Coster and Phil Wilde. Active 1991–present.**

2 Unlimited was one of the most commercially successful Eurodance acts of the 1990s, known for international hits such as "Get Ready for This", "Twilight Zone", "No Limit", and "Tribal Dance". The project was created by Belgian producers [[Producers/J/Jean-Paul De Coster]] and [[Producers/P/Phil Wilde]], who recruited rapper [[Members/R/Ray Slijngaard]] and vocalist [[Members/A/Anita Doth]] as the fronting performers. After Slijngaard and Doth departed in 1996, the project continued with new vocalists [[Members/K/Kim Vergouwen]] and [[Members/R/Romy van Ooijen]] for the 1998 album *II*, with subsequent touring incarnations featuring [[Members/M/Marjon van Iwaarden]] and [[Members/M/Michèle Karamat Ali]].
