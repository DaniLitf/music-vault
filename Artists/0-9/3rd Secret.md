# 3rd Secret

**Alternative rock / grunge from Seattle, Washington, USA. Active 2021–present.**

3rd Secret is an American alternative rock supergroup formed in late 2021, featuring members of Nirvana, Pearl Jam, Soundgarden, and Void. The band released their self-titled debut album in April 2022 and a follow-up, *The 2nd 3rd Secret*, in June 2023.
