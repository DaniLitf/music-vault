# 1914

**Blackened death metal / death-doom from Lviv, Ukraine. Active 2014–present.**

1914 is a Ukrainian extreme metal band whose lyrics and themes revolve around World War I. Their sound blends death metal's heaviness with doom's slow tempo and black metal's atmosphere, often incorporating samples of wartime speeches and battle sounds. The band has released four critically acclaimed studio albums — *Eschatology of War* (2015), *The Blind Leading the Blind* (2018), *Where Fear and Weapons Meet* (2021), and *Viribus Unitis* (2025) — on Napalm Records and Archaic Sound.
