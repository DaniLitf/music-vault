# 3rdeyegirl

**American funk rock band from Minneapolis. Active 2012–2016.**

3rdeyegirl (stylized as 3RDEYEGIRL) was an all-female power trio assembled by [[Members/P/Prince]] in late 2012 as his backing band. The band consisted of Danish bassist Ida Nielsen, Canadian guitarist Donna Grantis, and American drummer Hannah Welton. Together with Prince, they released the album *Plectrumelectrum* in 2014 and toured extensively until Prince's death in 2016.
