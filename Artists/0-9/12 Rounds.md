# 12 Rounds

**Trip hop / rock from London, England. Active 1995–2000.**

12 Rounds is a British rock band formed by [[Members/A/Atticus Ross]] and singer [[Members/C/Claudia Sarne]]. They released two albums — *Jitter Juice* (1996) on Polydor and *My Big Hero* (1998) on Trent Reznor's Nothing Records — blending trip-hop textures with alternative rock. The band toured with [[Artists/S/Sneaker Pimps]] and VAST, and opened [[Artists/M/Marilyn Manson]]'s *Mechanical Animals* promo tour in 1998. Their song "Something's Burning" appeared on the soundtrack to *All Over Me*.
