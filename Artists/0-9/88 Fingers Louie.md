# 88 Fingers Louie

**Melodic hardcore / punk rock from Chicago, Illinois, USA. Active 1993–1996, 1998–1999, 2009–2010, 2013–present.**

88 Fingers Louie is an American punk rock band formed in Chicago in 1993 by vocalist Denis Buckley, guitarist Dan "Mr. Precision" Wleklinski, bassist Joe Principe, and drummer Dom Vallone, taking their name from a piano-selling gangster in *The Flintstones*. Signed to Fat Wreck Chords and Hopeless Records, the band became one of Chicago's most revered 1990s punk acts before twice imploding from internal fights; after the 1999 breakup, Wleklinski and Principe went on to form [[Artists/R/Rise Against]]. The band reunited in 2009 and again in 2013, releasing their first album in 19 years, *Thank You for Being a Friend*, in 2017.
