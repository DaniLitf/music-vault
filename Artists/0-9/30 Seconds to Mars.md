# 30 Seconds to Mars

**Alternative rock / progressive metal from Los Angeles, California, USA. Active 1998–present.**

30 Seconds to Mars (formally Thirty Seconds to Mars) is an American rock band formed in Los Angeles in 1998 by brothers [[Members/J/Jared Leto|Jared Leto]] (vocals, guitar, bass, keyboards) and [[Members/S/Shannon Leto|Shannon Leto]] (drums), with the Leto brothers as the only constant members through numerous line-up changes. The band's sound has evolved dramatically across its career — from the progressive metal and space rock of its 2002 debut, through the platinum-selling alternative rock of *A Beautiful Lie* (2005) and *This Is War* (2009), to the more electronic, art-pop-leaning sound of *America* (2018). Jared Leto's parallel career as an Academy Award-winning actor has repeatedly fueled the band's insistence on being judged as musicians in their own right.
