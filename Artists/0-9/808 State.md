# 808 State

**Electronic / acid house / techno from Manchester, England. Active 1987–present.**

808 State are an English electronic music group formed in Manchester in 1987 by [[Members/G/Graham Massey]], [[Members/M/Martin Price]], and [[Members/G/Gerald Simpson]]. Named after the Roland TR-808 drum machine, they are regarded as one of the most influential acts in British dance music, pioneering the acid house and Madchester sounds. Their 1989 single "Pacific State" brought them international recognition.
