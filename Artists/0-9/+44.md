# +44

**Pop-punk / alternative rock supergroup from Los Angeles, California, USA. Active 2005–2009.**

+44 (read as "Plus Forty-four") was an American rock supergroup formed in Los Angeles in 2005 by [[Members/M/Mark Hoppus]] and [[Members/T/Travis Barker]] immediately after Blink-182's initial breakup, completed by lead guitarist [[Members/S/Shane Gallagher]] of the Nervous Return and rhythm guitarist [[Members/C/Craig Fairbaugh]] of Mercy Killers. The name refers to the UK's international dialing code, the country where Hoppus and Barker first discussed the project. Early recordings were largely electronic and featured vocals by [[Members/C/Carol Heller]], but the sound grew heavier once the duo purchased their own North Hollywood studio, Opera Music. The band released one album on Interscope and went inactive when Blink-182 reunited in 2009.
