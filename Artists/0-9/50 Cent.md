# 50 Cent

**East Coast hip-hop from New York City, USA. Active 1996–present.**

50 Cent is the stage name of Curtis James Jackson III, an American rapper, actor, and businessman who rose to fame with his 2003 debut album *Get Rich or Die Tryin'*. Discovered by Eminem and signed to Shady Records, he became one of the best-selling hip-hop artists of the 2000s, known for his gritty street narratives, distinctive delivery, and entrepreneurial ventures including G-Unit Records.

50 Cent is a solo artist. See [[Members/0-9/50 Cent]] for biography.
