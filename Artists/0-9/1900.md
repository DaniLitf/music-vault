# 1900

**1900 is the solo project of Swedish multi-instrumentalist [[Members/C/Christian Gabel]] (also of [[Artists/B/bob hund]] and [[Artists/S/Sagor & Swing]]), known for lo-fi, melancholic tape experiments with a nostalgic and filmic quality.**
