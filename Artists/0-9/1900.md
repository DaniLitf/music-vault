# 1900

**Lo-fi electronic / experimental pop from Stockholm, Sweden. Active 2000s–present.**

1900 is the solo recording project of Swedish musician, producer, and composer [[Members/C/Christian Gabel]], a drummer in [[Artists/B/bob hund]] and founder of Studio Cobra. Gabel describes the alias as "retro-technological," and the project's instrumental releases combine tape-saturated electronics, melancholic pop miniatures, field-recording atmospheres, and filmic nostalgia.
