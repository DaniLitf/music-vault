# Opeth

**Progressive death metal / progressive rock from Stockholm, Sweden. Active 1989–present.**

Opeth is a Swedish progressive metal band formed in Stockholm in 1989 by vocalist and guitarist Mikael Åkerfeldt, who has remained the sole constant member and creative force throughout their career. Originally rooted in death metal, the band gradually incorporated progressive rock, folk, and jazz influences, and from *Heritage* (2011) onward largely abandoned death metal entirely. Their album *Blackwater Park* (2001), produced by Steven Wilson of Porcupine Tree, is widely regarded as a landmark of progressive metal.
