# One Day as a Lion

**Rap rock / experimental rock from Los Angeles, USA. Active 2008–2009.**

One Day as a Lion is an American rock duo formed in 2008 by Zack de la Rocha, vocalist of Rage Against the Machine, and Jon Theodore, drummer of The Mars Volta. The band released a single self-titled EP on Anti- Records in 2008 before going on indefinite hiatus. The project marked a reunion of sorts between de la Rocha's politically charged rap-rock style and Theodore's intricate drumming in a stripped-down two-piece format.
