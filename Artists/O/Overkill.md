# Overkill

**Thrash metal from Newark, New Jersey, USA. Active 1980–present.**

Overkill is an American thrash metal band formed in Newark, New Jersey in 1980, anchored by vocalist Bobby "Blitz" Ellsworth and bassist D.D. Verni, who remain the only two members to have been present for the band's entire career. One of the most prolific acts in thrash metal, they have released over twenty studio albums across four decades without significant hiatus. Their early albums *Feel the Fire* (1985) and *Taking Over* (1987) helped establish the New York/New Jersey thrash scene alongside Anthrax.
