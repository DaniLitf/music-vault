# OLD

**Industrial metal / avant-garde metal from New York, USA. Active 1986–1994.**

OLD (Original Loudness Demo) was an American industrial metal and avant-garde band from New York City, founded by guitarist and programmer James Plotkin and vocalist Alan Dubin. Part of the early 1990s underground alongside acts like Godflesh, OLD blended noise rock, death metal, and industrial into a distinctly abrasive sound across three studio albums. Plotkin and Dubin later reunited in the drone/sludge act Khanate.
