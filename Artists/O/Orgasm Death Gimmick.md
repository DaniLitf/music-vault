# Orgasm Death Gimmick

**Industrial / experimental from Schwerin, Germany. Active 1991–1993.**

Orgasm Death Gimmick was a German industrial and experimental band formed in Schwerin in 1991 by guitarist Richard Kruspe, who would go on to co-found Rammstein. The band released three demo tapes before disbanding in 1993. That same year Kruspe formed Tempelprayers with future Rammstein bandmates Oliver Riedel and Christoph Schneider, which became Rammstein in 1994. Orgasm Death Gimmick is notable primarily as one of the earliest documented bands featuring Kruspe.
