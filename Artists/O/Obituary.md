# Obituary

**Death metal from Tampa, Florida, USA. Active 1984–1998, 2003–present.**

Obituary is an American death metal band formed in Tampa, Florida in 1984, originally called Executioner and later Xecutioner before settling on Obituary in 1988. Along with Death, Morbid Angel, and Deicide, they are considered one of the pioneering bands of the Florida death metal scene. Known for John Tardy's distinctive guttural vocals and a signature groove-laden riffing style, Obituary has released eleven studio albums and influenced countless death metal acts worldwide.
