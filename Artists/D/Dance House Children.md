# Dance House Children

**Techno / synthpop from California. Active 1991–1992.**

Dance House Children was the early 1990s music group of Ronnie Martin and Jason Martin (the brothers who later formed Joy Electric and Starflyer 59 respectively). The duo released two albums on Blonde Vinyl, *Songs & Stories* (1991) and *Jesus* (1992), before parting ways when Jason left to focus on Starflyer 59. Ronnie continued in an electronic direction, releasing the transitional album *Rainbow Rider: Beautiful Dazzling Music No. 1* before launching Joy Electric.
