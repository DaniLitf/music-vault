# Doo Rag

**Doo Rag were an American blues-punk duo from Austin, Texas, active from 1991 to 1996, consisting of guitarist/vocalist Bob Log III (Robert Patterson III) and drummer Thermos Malling, known for their raw, minimal, lo-fi approach.**
