# Doo Rag

**Doo Rag were an American blues-punk duo from Tucson, Arizona, active from 1990 to 1996, consisting of guitarist/vocalist Bob Log III (Robert Patterson III) and drummer/inventor Thermos Malling, known for their raw, minimal, lo-fi approach.**

Doo Rag formed in Tucson in 1990, building a cult following through relentless touring and a self-released debut cassette. Malling's homemade percussion setup — assembled from a Budweiser box, a tin bucket, and other found objects — paired with Log's slide-guitar playing gave the band a distinctive junkyard-blues sound that drew comparisons to Fred McDowell filtered through punk. The duo supported Beck, Sonic Youth, and the Jon Spencer Blues Explosion before dissolving in 1996, after which Log continued as a solo one-man band.
