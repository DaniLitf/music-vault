# Discharge

**Hardcore punk / D-beat band from Stoke-on-Trent, England. Active 1977–1987, 1991–1999, 2001–present.**

Discharge are an English hardcore punk band formed in Stoke-on-Trent in 1977, widely regarded as the inventors of the D-beat subgenre. Their minimalist, grinding sound and shouted anarcho-pacifist lyrics revolutionized UK punk and heavily influenced thrash metal, black metal, crust punk, and grindcore. Bands such as Metallica, Anthrax, Sepultura, and Arch Enemy have covered their songs. Their landmark album *Hear Nothing See Nothing Say Nothing* (1982) is considered a genre-defining classic.
