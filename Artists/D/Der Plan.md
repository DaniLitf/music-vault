# Der Plan

**German synthpop/EBM band from Düsseldorf. Active 1980–present.**

Der Plan are a German synthpop and EBM band formed in Düsseldorf in 1980. Part of the early German electronic music scene alongside DAF and others, they are known for their minimal, catchy synthpop songs with German-language lyrics and a playful, subversive aesthetic.
