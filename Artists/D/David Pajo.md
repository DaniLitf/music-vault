# David Pajo

**Slowcore / folk rock from Louisville, Kentucky, USA. Active 2005–present.**

David Pajo is the eponymous solo project of the Slint guitarist, distinct from his Papa M and Aerial M aliases. The releases under his own name (credited simply "Pajo") are intimate, laptop-recorded lo-fi folk, beginning with *Pajo* (2005) on Drag City, and later including a whispered acoustic Misfits covers album, *Scream with Me* (2009).
