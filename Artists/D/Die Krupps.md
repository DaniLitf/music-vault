# Die Krupps

**Die Krupps are a German industrial metal and EBM band from Düsseldorf, Germany, active 1980–present, founded by Jürgen Engler, who pioneered the fusion of industrial electronic music with heavy metal guitars.**

Their 1992 cover of Metallica's "One" and subsequent collaborations with Metallica members helped bridge the industrial and metal worlds and influenced the development of industrial metal as a genre.
