# DAF

**Electropunk / EBM from Düsseldorf, Germany. Active 1978–2021.**

Deutsch Amerikanische Freundschaft (DAF) was a pioneering German electropunk and electronic body music band formed in Düsseldorf in 1978. The band initially operated as a quintet before reducing to the core duo of Gabi Delgado-López (vocals) and Robert Görl (drums, electronics) for their most influential work. Their minimalist, aggressive sound—driven by sequencer basslines, analogue synthesizers, and Delgado's spoken/shouted German vocals—produced landmark albums including *Alles ist gut* (1981), *Gold und Liebe* (1981), and *Für immer* (1982). DAF is widely regarded as a foundational influence on techno, EBM, and the Neue Deutsche Welle movement.
