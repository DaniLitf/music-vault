# Dolly Parton

**Dolly Parton is an American country singer-songwriter, actress, and philanthropist, born on January 19, 1946, in Locust Ridge, Tennessee — one of the most celebrated and decorated country artists of all time, known for songs like "Jolene" and "I Will Always Love You."**

Dolly Parton is a solo artist. See [[Members/D/Dolly Parton]] for biography.
