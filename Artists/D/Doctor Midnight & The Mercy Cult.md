# Doctor Midnight & The Mercy Cult

**Scandinavian heavy metal supergroup. Active 2009–2011.**

Doctor Midnight & The Mercy Cult (DMTMC) were a Scandinavian heavy metal supergroup formed in 2009, bringing together former Turbonegro vocalist Hank von Helvete, former Marilyn Manson/KMFDM multi-instrumentalist Tim Sköld, guitarists Anders Odden (Cadaver, Satyricon) and Audun Stengel (Apoptygma Berzerk, The Kovenant), and former Extol drummer David Husvik. The band signed to Season of Mist in 2011, released their sole album *I Declare: Treason* the same year, and disbanded shortly after.
