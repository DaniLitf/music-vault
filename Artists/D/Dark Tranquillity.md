# Dark Tranquillity

**Dark Tranquillity are a Swedish melodic death metal band from Gothenburg, Sweden, active 1989–present, one of the three founding bands of the Gothenburg melodic death metal sound alongside In Flames and At the Gates.**

Unlike their contemporaries, Dark Tranquillity have maintained a consistent membership, with vocalist Mikael Stanne the only constant member throughout their career. He also fronts The Halo Effect.
