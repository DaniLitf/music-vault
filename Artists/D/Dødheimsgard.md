# Dødheimsgard

**Avant-garde extreme metal band from Norway. Active 1994–present.**

Dødheimsgard (also known as DHG) are a Norwegian extreme metal band formed in 1994 by Vicotnik, Fenriz, and Aldrahn, whose name translates roughly as "Realm of Death." They began as a black metal band but shifted into experimental, avant-garde, and industrial territory starting with *666 International* (1999), going through some of the most extensive lineup changes in the genre while Vicotnik remained the sole constant member.
