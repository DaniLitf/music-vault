# De/Vision

**German synthpop/EBM band from Bielefeld. Active 1988–present.**

De/Vision are a German synthpop and EBM band formed in Bielefeld in 1988 by Steffen Sojka and Marcus Meyn. They are one of the most enduring acts in the European dark electronic music scene, known for melodic synthpop songs with introspective German and English lyrics, spanning from classic synthpop to more rock-influenced electronic music.
