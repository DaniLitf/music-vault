# DJ Spooky

**DJ Spooky is an American DJ, producer, and multimedia artist, born Paul D. Miller in 1970 in Washington, D.C., known for conceptual electronic music blending hip-hop, classical, and avant-garde elements under the name "DJ Spooky That Subliminal Kid."**

DJ Spooky is a solo artist. See [[Members/D/DJ Spooky]] for biography.
