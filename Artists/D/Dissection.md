# Dissection

**Dissection were a Swedish melodic black/death metal band from Gothenburg, Sweden, active 1989–2006, whose debut *The Somberlain* (1993) and *Storm of the Light's Bane* (1995) are considered two of the greatest albums in extreme metal.**

Founder Jon Nödtveidt served a prison sentence for accessory to murder (1997–2004) and reformed the band for one final album before taking his own life in 2006.
