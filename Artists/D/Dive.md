# Dive

**EBM / electro-industrial from Belgium. Active 1990–present.**

Dive is a Belgian electronic body music project formed in 1990 by Dirk Ivens (formerly of Absolute Body Control and Klinik). Conceived as a solo outlet for complete creative control, Dive is known for its minimal, abrasive sound built on abused drum machines, heavy distortion, and processed vocals. Live performances feature Ivens alone with his recordings and minimal visual presentation. The project has also collaborated extensively with Ivan Iusco of Minus Habens Records.
