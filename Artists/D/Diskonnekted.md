# Diskonnekted

**Belgian EBM/synthpop band from Antwerp. Active 1990s–present.**

Diskonnekted are a Belgian EBM and synthpop band formed in Antwerp, part of the Belgian electronic music scene. Known for blending melodic synthpop with harder EBM beats, they have been active in the European alternative electronic scene.
