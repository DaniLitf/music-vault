# Death Grips

**Death Grips are an American experimental hip-hop group from Sacramento, California, active 2010–present, known for their abrasive, confrontational music that merges industrial noise, punk energy, and hip-hop into one of the most challenging sounds in contemporary music.**

The trio broke into public consciousness with *Exmilitary* (2011) and caused controversy by leaking their own album *No Love Deep Web* in defiance of a record label deal. Their enigmatic, disruptive persona is as much a part of their art as the music.
