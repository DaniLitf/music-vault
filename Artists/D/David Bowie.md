# David Bowie

**David Bowie (1947–2016) was a British rock musician and actor from London, England, one of the most critically acclaimed and influential artists of the 20th century, known for constant artistic reinvention across multiple distinct personas and musical phases.**

Bowie's catalogue spans glam rock (*Ziggy Stardust*), plastic soul (*Young Americans*), Berlin-era krautrock/ambient (*Low*, *"Heroes"*, *Lodger*), new wave pop (*Let's Dance*), and late-career electronic and jazz-inflected work, making him one of music's most chameleonic figures. Between 1988 and 1992 he stepped back from his solo persona to front the hard rock band [[Artists/T/Tin Machine]] as one of four equal members before returning to a solo career.

## Members

David Bowie was primarily a solo artist — see [[Members/D/David Bowie]] for biography. From 1988 to 1992 he was one of four equal members of the band [[Artists/T/Tin Machine]], rather than its solo leader.

### Past Touring & Session Members (Key)

- [[Members/T/Trevor Bolder]] – bass, "the Spiders from Mars" (1970–1973; died 2013)
- [[Members/W/Woody Woodmansey]] – drums, "the Spiders from Mars" (1970–1973)

## Connections

- [[Artists/T/Tin Machine]] – David Bowie (co-founding member of this separate band project, 1988–1992)
- [[Artists/I/Iggy Pop]] – David Bowie (co-wrote and produced *The Idiot* and *Lust for Life*, 1977; the Sales brothers of Tin Machine also backed Iggy Pop on the 1977 tour for *The Idiot*)
- [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] – David Bowie (collaborated on the "I'm Afraid of Americans" remix and co-headlined a joint 1995 tour with Trent Reznor)
- [[Artists/Q/Queen]] – David Bowie (co-wrote and co-performed "Under Pressure" with Freddie Mercury, 1981)
- [[Artists/B/Brian Eno]] – David Bowie (co-wrote and produced the Berlin Trilogy — *Low*, *"Heroes"*, *Lodger* — and later *Outside*, 1977–1995)
- [[Artists/T/The Beatles]] – David Bowie (John Lennon co-wrote and played guitar on "Fame", 1975)
- [[Artists/T/The Rolling Stones]] – David Bowie (duet with Mick Jagger on "Dancing in the Street", 1985)
- [[Artists/P/Pink Floyd]] – David Bowie (live duets with David Gilmour on "Arnold Layne" and "Comfortably Numb" at the Royal Albert Hall, 2006)
- [[Artists/M/Mott the Hoople]] – Mick Ronson (Bowie wrote and produced their hit "All the Young Dudes", 1972; Ronson briefly joined Mott the Hoople in 1974 after leaving Bowie's band)
- [[Artists/B/Bob Dylan]] – Mick Ronson (guitarist for both Bowie's Spiders from Mars and Dylan's Rolling Thunder Revue tour, 1975–1976)
