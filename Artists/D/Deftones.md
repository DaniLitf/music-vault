# Deftones

**Deftones are an American alternative metal band from Sacramento, California, active 1988–present, who evolved beyond the nu-metal wave with an increasingly atmospheric, shoegaze-influenced sound that has made them one of the most critically respected heavy rock bands of their era.**

The band's 2000 album *White Pony* is frequently cited as a landmark in alternative metal, blending aggression with dreamlike textures. Bassist Chi Cheng was critically injured in a car accident in 2008 and died in 2013.
