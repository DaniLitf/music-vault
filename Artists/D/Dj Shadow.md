# DJ Shadow

**DJ Shadow is an American DJ and producer, born Joshua Paul Davis in 1972 in Hayward, California, widely regarded as a pioneer of instrumental hip-hop — his debut album *Endtroducing.....* (1996) was constructed almost entirely from samples and is frequently cited as one of the greatest albums ever made.**

DJ Shadow is a solo artist. See [[Members/D/DJ Shadow]] for biography.
