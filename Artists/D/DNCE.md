# DNCE

**Dance-rock / pop from Los Angeles, California, USA. Active 2015–2019, 2022–2024.**

DNCE was an American dance-rock band formed in 2015 by Joe Jonas after the Jonas Brothers' breakup. The band name is a purposeful misspelling of "dance" without the "a." They signed with Republic Records and released their debut single "Cake by the Ocean" on September 18, 2015, which became a multi-platinum international hit reaching number nine on the Billboard Hot 100. Their self-titled debut studio album followed in 2016, peaking at number 17 on the Billboard 200. After a hiatus from 2019 to 2022 during which Jonas and drummer Jack Lawless returned to the Jonas Brothers, DNCE reunited as a trio (without Cole Whittle) before going on hiatus again in 2024.
