# Dimension Zero

**Dimension Zero were a Swedish melodic death metal band from Gothenburg, Sweden, active 1995–2010, featuring former In Flames co-founder Jesper Strömblad alongside Meshuggah vocalist Jens Kidman.**

The band were a notable supergroup within the Gothenburg scene, combining the melodic death sensibilities of Strömblad with the more aggressive thrash tendencies of the other members.
