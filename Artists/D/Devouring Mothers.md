# Devouring Mothers

**Punk rock / indie rock trio from Chicago, Illinois, USA. Active 2016–2018.**

Devouring Mothers is an American rock trio formed in 2016 as the touring band behind [[Members/L/Laura Jane Grace|Laura Jane Grace]]'s solo material, pairing the [[Artists/A/Against Me!|Against Me!]] frontperson with that band's drummer [[Members/A/Atom Willard|Atom Willard]] and longtime engineer/producer [[Members/M/Marc Hudson|Marc Hudson]] on bass and organ. The group's sole studio album, *Bought to Rot* (2018), gathered songs Grace had written but felt didn't belong on an Against Me! record, and drew on the influence of Rowland S. Howard and Tom Petty. Released on Bloodshot Records (and reissued by Red Scare Industries in 2022), the album was finished quickly so it could come out before Grace underwent facial feminization surgery as part of her gender transition.
