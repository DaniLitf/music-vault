# Dio

**Dio were an American heavy metal band from Los Angeles, California, active 1982–2010, founded by vocalist Ronnie James Dio (1942–2010) after his tenures with Rainbow and Black Sabbath, known for epic, fantasy-themed heavy metal and Dio's towering vocals.**

Ronnie James Dio introduced the "devil horns" hand gesture to rock and is considered one of the greatest vocalists in heavy metal history. He died of stomach cancer in May 2010.
