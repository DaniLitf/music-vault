# Das Ich

**Neue Deutsche Todeskunst / dark wave from Bavaria, Germany. Active 1989–present.**

Das Ich is a German dark wave / gothic-industrial band formed in 1989 in Bayreuth, Bavaria, by vocalist Stefan Ackermann and multi-instrumentalist Bruno Kramm. They are pioneers of the Neue Deutsche Todeskunst (New German Death Art) movement, known for classically inspired gothic-industrial music, theatrical live performances, and German-language lyrics. The band also co-founded the Danse Macabre record label.
