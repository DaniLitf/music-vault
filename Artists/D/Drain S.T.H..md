# Drain S.T.H.

**Drain S.T.H. were a Swedish all-female hard rock band formed in Stockholm in 1993, known for their heavy sound that blended grunge and alternative metal, active until 2000 when they disbanded.**

Formed in Stockholm as Drain, the band became known internationally as Drain S.T.H. (Stockholm) after a naming conflict with an American act. Guitarist Flavia Canel and drummer Martina Axén had played together since the punk band Livin' Sacrifice, eventually recruiting bassist Anna Kjellberg and vocalist Maria Sjöholm (later married to Black Sabbath's Tony Iommi, who co-wrote the song "Black" on *Freaks of Nature*). The band toured Ozzfest in 1997 and 1999 alongside acts like Black Sabbath, Machine Head, and Megadeth before disbanding in 2000.
