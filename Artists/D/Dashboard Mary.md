# Dashboard Mary

**Alternative rock from Houston, Texas, USA. Active 2000s.**

Dashboard Mary was an American alternative rock band from Houston, Texas. The band was formed by vocalist David Duet (also known as Dave Nobody) and bassist Lee Leal. They released one album, *Somebody's in Here* (2007), and a Christmas single in 2013. The band broke up when Duet moved back to Seattle.
