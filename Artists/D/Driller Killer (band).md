# Driller Killer

**Crust punk / death metal band from Malmö, Sweden. Active 1993–2009.**

Driller Killer was a Swedish crust punk band formed in Malmö in 1993, named after the Abel Ferrara horror film *The Driller Killer*. They were one of the first bands to fuse death metal heaviness into a crust punk framework, and their sound blended D-beat rhythms, hardcore aggression, and death metal growls. The band signed to Osmose Productions and released seven studio albums, with Osmose's Kron-H imprint created partly to showcase Driller Killer alongside Disfear, Loud Pipes, and Dellamorte. Vocalist and guitarist Cliff Lundberg was the only constant member throughout the band's 16-year existence.
