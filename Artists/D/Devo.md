# Devo

**Devo are an American new wave and art rock band from Akron, Ohio, active 1973–present, known for their absurdist concept of "de-evolution" — the regression of humanity — expressed through angular, synth-driven pop, matching uniforms, and satirical performance art.**

Their 1980 single "Whip It" remains their most recognisable work, though their earlier experimental work is arguably more influential in post-punk and art-rock circles.
