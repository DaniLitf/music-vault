# DAF.Partei

**German EBM/industrial project. Active 1990s.**

DAF.Partei is a German EBM and industrial project active in the 1990s, inspired by and paying homage to the pioneering German electronic duo DAF (Deutsch-Amerikanische Freundschaft).
