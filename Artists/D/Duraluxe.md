# Duraluxe

**Alternative / neo-psychedelic rock from Nashville, Tennessee / Athens, Georgia. Active 1997–2000s.**

Duraluxe was formed in 1997 by former Hoi Polloi guitarist Troy Daugherty and multi-instrumentalist Chris Colbert in Nashville, Tennessee. The pair built Studio Dolorosa and recorded several tapes before traveling to Chicago to work with producer Keith Cleversley, resulting in their debut album *Dolorosa* (1999). The band blended swirling psychedelic rock with noisy indie pop, drawing comparisons to The Flaming Lips and Sparklehorse. After relocating to Athens, Georgia and later California, Duraluxe released a split CD with The Lassie Foundation and a second album, *The Suitcase* (2002), before gradually dissolving.
