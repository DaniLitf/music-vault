# Detektivbyrån

**Electronica/folk trio from Karlstad, Sweden (based in Gothenburg from 2005). Active 2005–2010.**

Detektivbyrån were a Swedish electronica and folk trio who grew up together in Karlstad but didn't form the band until relocating to Gothenburg in 2005, known for whimsical instrumentals built from accordion, glockenspiel, toy piano, and theremin, partly inspired by the *Amélie* soundtrack. Their 2008 debut full-length *Wermland* earned two Grammis nominations. Martin Molin went on to found [[Artists/W/Wintergatan]] in 2012, bringing bandmate Marcus Sjöberg with him.
