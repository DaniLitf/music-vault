# Dog Blood

**Electro house duo project of Los Angeles, USA / Berlin, Germany. Active 2012–present.**

Dog Blood is a collaborative electronic project formed in 2012 by [[Members/S/Skrillex]] and [[Members/A/Alexander Ridha]] (Boys Noize), blending both producers' harder, club-oriented sensibilities. Their debut single "Next Order" / "Middle Finger" (2012) topped Beatport's techno chart, and the duo has released sporadic EPs and singles since, including *Turn Off the Lights* (2019), which featured the Grammy-nominated "Midnight Hour" with Ty Dolla $ign.
