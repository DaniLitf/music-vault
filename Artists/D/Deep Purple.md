# Deep Purple

**Hard rock / heavy metal from London, England. Active 1968–1976, 1984–present.**

Deep Purple are a British hard rock band formed in 1968, pioneers of heavy metal alongside Led Zeppelin and Black Sabbath. Known for their rotating lineups (Mark I through Mark IX), they have sold over 100 million albums worldwide.
