# Death

**Death were an American death metal band from Orlando, Florida, active 1983–2001, widely regarded as the founders of death metal, led by guitarist and vocalist Chuck Schuldiner (1967–2001), who is considered the "Father of Death Metal."**

The band's evolution from brutal speed/death metal to highly technical, progressive death metal across seven albums is one of the most significant artistic journeys in metal history. Schuldiner died of brain cancer in 2001.
