# Disfear

**Crust punk / death metal from Nyköping, Sweden. Active 1989–present.**

Disfear is a Swedish crust punk band formed in 1989 under the name Anti-Bofors by guitarist Björn Peterson and bassist Henke Frykman. The band built a following in the Scandinavian punk and metal underground with their aggressive D-beat sound, releasing *Soul Scars* (1995) and *Everyday Slaughter* (1997) before going on hiatus. They returned in 2003 with *Misanthropic Generation* featuring vocalist Tomas Lindberg (At the Gates) and later added Uffe Cederlund (Entombed) on second guitar. Their 2008 album *Live the Storm*, produced by Converge's Kurt Ballou, is considered a landmark of crust punk.
