# Dream Theater

**Dream Theater are an American progressive metal band from New York City, active 1985–present, known for complex, extended compositions, virtuoso musicianship, and concept albums that blend the technical ambition of progressive rock with heavy metal power.**

Their 1992 breakthrough *Images and Words* established them as the leading force in progressive metal. Drummer Mike Portnoy co-founded the band but departed in 2010; he returned in 2023.
