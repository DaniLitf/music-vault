# DragonForce

**DragonForce are a British power metal band formed in London in 1999, known for their ultra-fast guitar playing, fantasy-themed lyrics, and the viral hit "Through the Fire and Flames" (2006), which became famous through the video game *Guitar Hero III*.**

DragonForce formed in London in 1999 (initially as DragonHeart), releasing a self-produced demo before signing to Noise Records. Guitarists Herman Li and Sam Totman built the band's signature sound around extreme-tempo riffing and keyboard-driven melodies, drawing on Japanese video game and anime soundtracks as much as classic power metal. The band achieved mainstream crossover success when "Through the Fire and Flames" was featured in *Guitar Hero III: Legends of Rock* (2007), and they have continued releasing albums with several lineup changes, most notably the departure of original vocalist ZP Theart in 2011.
