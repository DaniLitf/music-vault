# Desecration

**Death metal band from Newport, Wales. Active 1992–present.**

Desecration are a Welsh death metal band formed in Newport, South Wales in 1992. They gained notoriety in 1995 when their debut album *Gore and Perversion* was famously seized and incinerated by local police due to its offensive content, and band members were arrested. The ensuing media furore established the band in the South Wales metal scene. The core of the band has long been Ollie Jones and Michael Hourihan, who also play together in Extreme Noise Terror.
