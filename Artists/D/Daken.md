# Daken

**Hard rock/metal band from London, England.**

Daken are an English hard rock and metal band fronted by singer/guitarist Dave "Infester" Hirschheimer, formerly of Gorerotted/The Rotted and Infestation. Hirschheimer, who began as a drummer before writing on guitar, serves as the band's primary songwriter.
