# Darkthrone

**Black/heavy metal duo from Kolbotn, Norway. Active 1986–present.**

Darkthrone are a Norwegian metal band formed in Kolbotn in 1986, initially as a death metal act named Black Death before shifting to black metal in the early 1990s. Their first three black metal albums, *A Blaze in the Northern Sky* (1992), *Under a Funeral Moon* (1993), and *Transilvanian Hunger* (1994) — known as the "Unholy Trinity" — are considered among the most influential recordings in the genre. Since guitarist Zephyrous departed in 1993, the band has been a duo of Fenriz and Nocturno Culto, later evolving through crust punk and traditional heavy metal styles across a prolific 22-album discography.
