# Doom (British band)

**Crust punk / hardcore punk band from Birmingham, England. Active 1987–1990, 1992–2005, 2010–present.**

Doom are an English crust punk band formed in Birmingham in 1987, considered pivotal in the development of crust punk. Originally formed as The Subverters, the band quickly renamed to Doom and recorded for Peaceville Records. Despite their original lineup lasting only until 1990, they are cited as an early precursor to grindcore and were a favourite of BBC Radio DJ John Peel. Their debut album *War Crimes (Inhuman Beings)* (1988) is a landmark of the crust punk genre.
