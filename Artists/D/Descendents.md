# Descendents

**Punk rock / melodic hardcore from Manhattan Beach, California, USA. Active 1977–present (hiatuses 1983–1985, 1987–1995, 1997–2002, 2004–2010).**

Descendents were formed in 1977 by guitarist Frank Navetta, bassist Tony Lombardo, and drummer Bill Stevenson as a power pop/surf punk band, and turned into a melodic hardcore pioneer after recruiting Stevenson's school friend Milo Aukerman as singer in 1980. Their 1982 debut *Milo Goes to College* fused hardcore speed with self-deprecating wit and pop melody, becoming one of the most influential punk albums ever — cited as formative by Blink-182, Green Day, NOFX, and The Offspring. Since 1986 the lineup has been Aukerman, Stevenson, guitarist Stephen Egerton, and bassist Karl Alvarez, who also operate in parallel as the band All.

## Connections

- [[Artists/B/Black Flag]] – Bill Stevenson, Dez Cadena (Stevenson drummed for Black Flag 1981–1985 during the Descendents' first hiatus; Black Flag guitarist Cadena sang backing vocals on *All*)

- [[Artists/F/FLAG]] – Bill Stevenson (drums in the Black Flag alumni band FLAG)
- [[Artists/T/The Last]] – David Nolte (co-founded the early Descendents, then left to join The Last with his brothers)
