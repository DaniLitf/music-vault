# Disturbed

**Disturbed are an American heavy metal band from Chicago, Illinois, active 1994–present, known for vocalist David Draiman's melodic-yet-aggressive style and the infectious "oh-wa-ah-ah-ah" opening of their debut single "Down with the Sickness" (2000).**

One of the most commercially successful post-nu-metal heavy rock bands, Disturbed have had five consecutive #1 albums on the US Billboard 200, a rare achievement in metal.
