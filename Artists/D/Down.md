# Down

**Down are an American heavy metal supergroup formed in New Orleans in 1991, featuring members of Pantera, Crowbar, and Corrosion of Conformity, known for their sludgy, Sabbath-influenced sound.**

Down's classic lineup brought together Phil Anselmo (Pantera) on vocals, Pepper Keenan (Corrosion of Conformity) and Kirk Windstein (Crowbar) on guitars, Jimmy Bower (Eyehategod, Crowbar) on drums, and originally Todd Strange (Crowbar) on bass. Their 1995 debut *NOLA* is regarded as a landmark of the sludge/southern metal sound, recorded during breaks between the members' primary bands. The group has released material intermittently across three-plus decades, going on extended hiatuses whenever members' other bands took priority.
