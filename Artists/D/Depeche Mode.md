# Depeche Mode

**Depeche Mode are a British synth-pop and electronic band from Basildon, Essex, England, active 1980–present, one of the most influential and successful electronic acts in history, who transformed from a pop synth group to a dark, industrial-influenced force with *Violator* (1990).**

With over 100 million records sold, Depeche Mode stand as one of the best-selling music acts of all time. The suicide of founding member Andrew Fletcher in 2022 left the band as a duo.
