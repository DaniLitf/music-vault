# Dimmu Borgir

**Dimmu Borgir are a Norwegian symphonic black metal band from Oslo, Norway, active 1993–present, who became the best-selling black metal band of all time by combining black metal aggression with elaborate orchestral arrangements and accessible production.**

Their progression from raw black metal on *Stormblåst* (1996) to the slick orchestral bombast of *Death Cult Armageddon* (2003) made them controversial in underground circles while achieving mainstream metal success.
