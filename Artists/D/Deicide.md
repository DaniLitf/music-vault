# Deicide

**American death metal band from Tampa, Florida. Active 1987–present.**

Deicide are an American death metal band formed in Tampa, Florida in 1987 by vocalist and bassist Glen Benton, guitarist Brian Hoffman, guitarist Eric Hoffman, and drummer Steve Asheim. They are one of the most controversial and influential bands in the death metal genre, known for their anti-Christian lyrical themes, brutal sound, and Benton's notorious public persona. The band has released twelve studio albums and remains a significant force in extreme metal.
