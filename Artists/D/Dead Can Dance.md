# Dead Can Dance

**Dead Can Dance are an Australian-British neoclassical and dark wave duo from Melbourne, Australia, active 1981–1998 and 2011–present, featuring Lisa Gerrard and Brendan Perry, whose transcendent, globally influenced music draws on ancient and medieval traditions from around the world.**

One of the most singular acts in alternative music, Dead Can Dance's layered, orchestral approach and Gerrard's unique glossolalic vocal technique have influenced gothic, neoclassical, and world music.
