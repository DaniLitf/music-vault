# Dendera

**Power metal band from Portsmouth, England. Active 2000s–present.**

Dendera are an English power metal band from Portsmouth, regarded as one of the heavier acts in the UK power metal scene, blending fast, thrashy riffs with classic melodic vocals. Ashley Edison took over as lead singer in 2010 after a six-month search, fronting the band's two studio albums.
