# Detrimentum

**Death/grind band from England.**

Detrimentum are an English death/grind band led by drummer John "Junky Jon" Rushforth, previously of Gorerotted/The Rotted and Infected Disarray, who also runs the extreme metal label Grindethic Records. The band released their debut album *Embracing This Deformity* in 2008, followed by *Inhuman Disgrace* in 2012.
