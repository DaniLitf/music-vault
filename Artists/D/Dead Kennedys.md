# Dead Kennedys

**Dead Kennedys were an American hardcore punk band from San Francisco, California, active 1978–1986 and 2001–present (without Jello Biafra), whose satirical political lyrics and caustic music made them one of the most culturally significant acts in American punk.**

Singer Jello Biafra became one of punk's most prominent political voices. The band's 1980 debut *Fresh Fruit for Rotting Vegetables* is a landmark of hardcore punk.
