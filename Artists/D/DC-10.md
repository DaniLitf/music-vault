# DC-10

**Funk rock / funk metal from the USA. Active 1995.**

DC-10 was a short-lived American funk rock project formed by members of Racer X and The Scream. The band featured vocalist Billy Fogarty, guitarists Bruce Bouillet and John Moore, bassist Juan Alderete, and drummers Abraham Laboriel Jr. and Walt Woodward III. They released one album, *Co-Burn* (1995), on Deep Cuttin' Records. The album was recorded at Tarasound studios and produced by Bruce Bouillet and Mario Caldato Jr.
