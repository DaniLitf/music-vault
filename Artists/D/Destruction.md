# Destruction

**Destruction are a German thrash metal band from Weil am Rhein, Baden-Württemberg, active 1982–present, one of the "Big Four" of German thrash alongside Sodom, Kreator, and Tankard, known for aggressive, technical thrash metal.**

Founding guitarist Mike Sifringer and founding vocalist/bassist Schmier (Marcel Schirmer) have been the core constants throughout the band's career.
