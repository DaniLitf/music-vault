# Queens of the Stone Age

**Queens of the Stone Age (QOTSA) are an American rock band formed in Palm Desert, California in 1996 by Josh Homme following the breakup of Kyuss, known for their hypnotic riffs, dark humor, and ever-shifting roster of collaborators.**

## Connections

- [[Artists/K/Kyuss]] – Josh Homme co-founded Kyuss and dissolved it to start QOTSA
- [[Artists/T/The Mars Volta]] – Jon Theodore was drummer for The Mars Volta before joining QOTSA
- [[Artists/O/One Day as a Lion]] – Jon Theodore formed One Day as a Lion with Zack de la Rocha between QOTSA commitments
- [[Artists/F/Foo Fighters]] – Dave Grohl joined QOTSA on drums for *Songs for the Deaf* and *...Like Clockwork*
- [[Artists/N/Nirvana]] – Dave Grohl, former Nirvana drummer, played on multiple QOTSA albums
- [[Artists/S/Screaming Trees]] – Mark Lanegan was the vocalist of Screaming Trees before becoming a recurring QOTSA collaborator
