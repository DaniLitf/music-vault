# Johnny Cash

**Johnny Cash (February 26, 1932 – September 12, 2003) was an American singer-songwriter from Kingsland, Arkansas, one of the best-selling music artists of all time, known for his deep bass-baritone voice, his Man in Black persona, and a career spanning rockabilly, country, folk, and rock, culminating in his late-career American Recordings series.**

Johnny Cash is a solo artist. See [[Members/J/Johnny Cash]] for biography.
