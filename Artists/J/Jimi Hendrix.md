# Jimi Hendrix

**Jimi Hendrix (November 27, 1942 – September 18, 1970) was an American guitarist, singer, and songwriter from Seattle, Washington, widely regarded as the greatest electric guitarist in history, known for his revolutionary technique and for albums like *Are You Experienced* (1967) and *Electric Ladyland* (1968).**
