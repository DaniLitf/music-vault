# Joy Electric

**Synthpop / electropop from California. Active 1994–present.**

Joy Electric is the brand label for a series of electropop and synthpop productions by Ronnie Martin. Martin began producing music under the Joy Electric name in 1994, after the demise of Dance House Children, a band he formed with his brother Jason Martin of Starflyer 59. The project is known for its dedication to analog synthesizers, whimsical lyrics, and medieval/fairy-tale themes, combining clock-like blips and swirling melodies into a signature sound that influenced the Christian electronic scene throughout the 1990s and 2000s. Joy Electric disbanded in 2012 when Martin entered seminary, though he has since released music under his own name.
