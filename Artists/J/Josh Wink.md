# Josh Wink

**Josh Wink (born Joshua Winkelman, April 20, 1970, in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania) is an American electronic music DJ, producer, and label owner, best known for his 1995 acid house hit "Higher State of Consciousness" and as a founder of the Ovum Recordings label.**

Josh Wink is a solo artist. See [[Members/J/Josh Wink]] for biography.
