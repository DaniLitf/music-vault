# Jack White

**Jack White (born John Anthony Gillis on July 9, 1975, in Detroit, Michigan) is an American musician, singer-songwriter, and producer, best known as the founder and guitarist of The White Stripes, and also a member of The Raconteurs and The Dead Weather, as well as a prolific solo artist.**
