# Jimmy Cliff

**Jimmy Cliff (born James Chambers, July 30, 1948, in Somerton, Saint James, Jamaica) is a Jamaican reggae singer, songwriter, musician, and actor, one of the first Jamaican artists to gain international attention with songs like "Many Rivers to Cross," "The Harder They Come," and "I Can See Clearly Now."**
