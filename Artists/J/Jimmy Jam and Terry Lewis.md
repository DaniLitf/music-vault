# Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis

**Songwriting and record production duo from Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA. Active 1983–present.**

Jimmy Jam and Terry Lewis are an American songwriting and record production duo who first gained prominence as members of The Time and Flyte Tyme. After being fired from The Time in 1983, they formed one of the most successful production partnerships in music history, producing multiplatinum albums for Janet Jackson, Human League, Boyz II Men, Usher, and many others. They have won multiple Grammy Awards and were inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2022.
