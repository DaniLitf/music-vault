# Jeff Mills

**Jeff Mills (born June 18, 1963, in Detroit, Michigan) is an American DJ, producer, and composer, a seminal figure in Detroit techno known for his superhuman DJ technique, and a founding member of Underground Resistance alongside Mad Mike Banks.**

Jeff Mills is a solo artist. See [[Members/J/Jeff Mills]] for biography.
