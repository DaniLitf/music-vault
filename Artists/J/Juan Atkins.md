# Juan Atkins

**Juan Atkins (born September 12, 1962, in Detroit, Michigan) is an American electronic musician and DJ widely regarded as the originator of techno music, a founding member of the Detroit techno collective the Belleville Three alongside Derrick May and Kevin Saunderson, and a founding member of Underground Resistance.**

Juan Atkins is a solo artist. See [[Members/J/Juan Atkins]] for biography.
