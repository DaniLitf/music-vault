**Indie rock. Hollywood, California. Active 2007–2008.**

Jubilee was a short-lived indie rock band formed in 2007 by multi-instrumentalist Aaron North. Their sound drew from classic rock and alternative influences, described as combining The Replacements, The Stone Roses, Neil Young, Blur, Jane's Addiction, and The Verve. The band released two singles in 2008 and home-recorded live sessions in 2009 before disbanding.
