# Janis Joplin

**Janis Joplin (January 19, 1943 – October 4, 1970) was an American rock and blues singer from Port Arthur, Texas, one of the most iconic vocalists of the 1960s counterculture, known for her powerful, raw delivery as the frontman of Big Brother and the Holding Company and as a solo artist.**

Janis Joplin is a solo artist. See [[Members/J/Janis Joplin]] for biography.
