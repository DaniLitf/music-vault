# Jonas Brothers

**Pop rock / pop-punk from Wyckoff, New Jersey, USA. Active 2005–2013, 2019–present.**

The Jonas Brothers are an American pop rock band formed in 2005, comprising brothers Kevin Jonas (lead guitar), Joe Jonas (lead vocals), and Nick Jonas (vocals, guitar, keyboards). Raised in Wyckoff, New Jersey, the trio signed with Columbia Records in 2005 and later moved to Hollywood Records, becoming breakout stars through the Disney Channel with films *Camp Rock* (2008) and its sequel *Camp Rock 2: The Final Jam* (2010), as well as their own television series *Jonas*. The band has sold over 17 million albums worldwide and reunited in 2019 after a six-year split, releasing the chart-topping comeback single "Sucker."
