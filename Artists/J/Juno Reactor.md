# Juno Reactor

**Juno Reactor are a British electronic music group founded in 1990 by Ben Watkins, known for fusing electronic music with world music and orchestral elements, and best known internationally for providing music to *The Matrix Reloaded* and *The Matrix Revolutions* soundtracks.**
