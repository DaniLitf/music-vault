# Judas Priest

**Heavy metal from Birmingham, England. Active 1969–present.**

Judas Priest are an English heavy metal band formed in Birmingham in 1969. One of the most influential metal bands of all time, they have sold over 50 million albums and are frequently cited as pioneers of the new wave of British heavy metal (NWOBHM), as well as a formative influence on speed metal, thrash metal, and power metal. The band struggled commercially until 1980's *British Steel* brought mainstream attention, and they went on to release a string of platinum albums including *Screaming for Vengeance* (1982) and *Defenders of the Faith* (1984). Inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2022, the band's classic sound was defined by the twin-guitar attack of K. K. Downing and Glenn Tipton, the operatic vocals of Rob Halford, and the anchoring bass of Ian Hill — the only member to appear on every album.
