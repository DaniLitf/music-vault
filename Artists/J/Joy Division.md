# Joy Division

**Joy Division were an English post-punk band formed in Salford in 1976, consisting of Ian Curtis, Bernard Sumner, Peter Hook, and Stephen Morris, who became one of the most influential bands in rock history before dissolving following vocalist Ian Curtis's suicide in 1980, after which the remaining members reformed as New Order.**
