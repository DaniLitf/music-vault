# Jean-Michel Jarre

**Jean-Michel Jarre (born August 24, 1948, in Lyon, France) is a French composer, performer, and record producer, a pioneer of electronic music who broke through with the landmark albums *Oxygène* (1976) and *Équinoxe* (1978), and who has staged some of the largest outdoor concerts in history.**
