**Power pop / pop rock from San Francisco, California. Active 1989–1994.**

Jellyfish was an American rock band led by songwriters Andy Sturmer (drums, vocals) and Roger Manning (keyboards, vocals), known for their sophisticated blend of 1960s classic rock, XTC-style power pop, and baroque pop arrangements. Despite their short career, they influenced numerous acts including Ben Folds Five and became cult favorites for their intricate harmonies and lush production.
