# Wintersun

**Wintersun are a Finnish melodic death metal band formed in 2003 by Jari Mäenpää following his departure from Ensiferum, known for their dense, symphonic approach to melodic death metal and the notoriously lengthy development periods between albums.**

## Discography

### Studio Albums
| Year | Title | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|

| 2012 | *Time I* | 8 years in development |
| 2017 | *The Forest Seasons* | Crowdfunded |
| 2024 | *Time II* | Completed the Time saga after 12 more years |
