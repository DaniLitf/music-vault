# Whom Gods Destroy

**American progressive metal supergroup. Active 2023–present.**

Whom Gods Destroy are an American progressive metal supergroup formed in 2023, featuring former Dream Theater keyboardist Derek Sherinian and vocalist Dino Jelusick (Whitesnake, Trans-Siberian Orchestra). They deliver high-energy progressive metal with technical complexity and melodic vocals.
