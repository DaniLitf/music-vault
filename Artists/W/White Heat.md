**Heavy metal from Chicago, Illinois. Active 1982–1984.**

White Heat was an American heavy metal band from Chicago, Illinois, formed in 1982 after evolving from the instrumental band Onyx. The band is best known as the first recording project of vocalist Jeff Hatrix (later of Purgatory, Hatrix, and Mushroomhead), who joined them after their original formation and sang on their only release, a 1982 demo often referred to as "The Junkyard Tape." After Hatrix moved back to his hometown of Cleveland in 1984, White Heat dissolved.
