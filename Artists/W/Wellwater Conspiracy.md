# Wellwater Conspiracy

**Wellwater Conspiracy were an American rock band formed in 1996 by Matt Cameron (Soundgarden) and Ben Shepherd (Soundgarden), who used the project to explore a more psychedelic and experimental side of rock during Soundgarden's hiatus.**
