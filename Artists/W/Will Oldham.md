# Will Oldham

**Will Oldham (born 1970-12-24), also known as Bonnie "Prince" Billy and Palace Brothers, is an American singer-songwriter and actor from Louisville, Kentucky, known for his lo-fi, intimate folk and country recordings and prolific output under multiple aliases.**
