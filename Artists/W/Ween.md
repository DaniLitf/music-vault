# Ween

**Ween are an American rock band formed in New Hope, Pennsylvania in 1984 by Dean Ween and Gene Ween, one of the most musically eclectic and unpredictable acts in rock, known for their genre-hopping albums spanning country, metal, reggae, electronic, and everything between.**
