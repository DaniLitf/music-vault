# Wintergatan

**Folktronica from Gothenburg, Sweden. Active 2012–present.**

Wintergatan are a Swedish folktronica band formed in Gothenburg in 2012 by Martin Molin and Marcus Sjöberg, both formerly of [[Artists/D/Detektivbyrån]], joined by Evelina Hägglund and David Zandén. They became a worldwide phenomenon through Molin's YouTube documentation of the Marble Machine — a hand-built music box powered by 2,000 steel marbles — whose 2016 debut video has been viewed over 275 million times, and its unfinished touring successor, the Marble Machine X.
