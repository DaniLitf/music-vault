# Wendy & Lisa

**Pop/synth-pop duo from Los Angeles, California. Active 1986–present.**

Wendy & Lisa are an American pop duo formed in Los Angeles in 1986, consisting of Wendy Melvoin (guitar, vocals) and Lisa Coleman (keyboards, vocals). Both were members of Prince's Revolution before launching their own duo career, bringing a blend of pop, new wave, and R&B influences to their collaborative work.
