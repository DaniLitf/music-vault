# Wumpscut

**German/US industrial/EBM project. Active 1991–present.**

Wumpscut (also stylized as wumpscut:) is an industrial and EBM project originally formed in Germany in 1991 by Rudy Ratzinger (born Rudiger Brautigam). Known for their harsh, aggressive sound combining pounding industrial beats with distorted vocals and dark atmospheres, the project has been a long-running fixture of the international industrial music scene, later relocating to the United States.
