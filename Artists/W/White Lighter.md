# White Lighter

**Indie rock band from USA. Active 2000s.**

White Lighter was an indie rock band formed in the 2000s, featuring members drawn from Starflyer 59, Velour 100, and Project 86. The project shared much of its lineup with Velour 100, essentially serving as a continuation of that band's sound.
