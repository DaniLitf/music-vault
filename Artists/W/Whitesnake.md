# Whitesnake

**Hard rock / blues rock from London, England. Active 1978–present.**

Whitesnake is an English hard rock band formed in 1978 by [[Members/D/David Coverdale]] after his departure from [[Artists/D/Deep Purple]]. The band achieved massive commercial success in the 1980s with albums like *Slide It In* (1984) and the self-titled *Whitesnake* (1987), which featured the hit singles "Here I Go Again" and "Is This Love". The lineup has included numerous notable musicians including [[Members/J/Jon Lord]], [[Members/I/Ian Paice]], [[Members/S/Steve Vai]], and [[Members/T/Tommy Aldridge]].
