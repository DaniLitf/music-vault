# White Zombie

**White Zombie were an American heavy metal band formed in New York City in 1985 by Rob Zombie and Sean Yseult, who evolved from a noise rock act into one of the defining groove metal bands of the early 1990s before dissolving in 1998 when Rob Zombie pursued a solo career.**
