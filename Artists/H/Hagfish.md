# Hagfish

**Punk rock and alternative rock from Dallas, Texas. Active 1991–2001, reunions 2003–2004, 2006, 2010, 2012, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019.**

Hagfish were an American rock band that originated in Sherman, Texas and rose to notoriety through performances in the Deep Ellum district of Dallas. Formed by brothers Zach and Doni Blair in 1991, the band emerged as part of the 1990s Dallas/Fort Worth alternative rock scene alongside contemporaries Tripping Daisy and Toadies, drawing heavy influence from the Descendents and All. The stable core lineup of vocalist George Stroud Reagan III, guitarist Zach Blair, bassist Doni Blair, and drummer Tony Barsotti recorded nearly all of the band's studio work from 1993 onward. After officially disbanding in 2001, the band reunited occasionally for live performances throughout the 2000s and 2010s.
