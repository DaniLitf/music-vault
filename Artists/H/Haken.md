# Haken

**Progressive metal band from London, England. Active 2007–present.**

Haken are an English progressive metal band formed in London in 2007, known for blending intricate, technically demanding instrumentation with strong pop-influenced melodies and ambitious concept-driven songwriting. They debuted with *Aquarius* (2010) and have since built a reputation as one of the leading acts in the modern progressive metal scene through albums including *Visions* (2011), *The Mountain* (2013), *Affinity* (2016), *Vector* (2018), *Virus* (2020), and *Fauna* (2023).
