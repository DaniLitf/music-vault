# HEALTH

**Industrial rock / experimental rock from Los Angeles, California. Active 2005–present.**

HEALTH (stylized HEALTH) formed in Los Angeles in 2005, emerging from the city's underground experimental scene around the DIY venue The Smell. Vocalist/guitarist Jake Duzsik and bassist John Famiglietti founded the band, enlisting guitarist Jupiter Keyes and drummer B.J. Miller. Early noise-rock releases gave way to a more melodic, electronic-leaning sound on *Death Magic* (2015) and later albums, and the band has become known for scoring video games including *Max Payne 3*, *Grand Theft Auto V*, and *Cyberpunk 2077*, as well as collaborating widely — including with [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]] on "Isn't Everyone" (2021) and with [[Artists/P/Perturbator]] on "Body/Prison" (2018).
