# Hollow

**Heavy metal from Cleveland, Ohio. Active 1990s.**

Hollow was a Cleveland-area heavy metal band featuring Kevin Skelly on bass. Limited information is available about the band's discography or full lineup. Skelly was the band's primary constant member, also playing in Hatrix and Integrity during the same period.
