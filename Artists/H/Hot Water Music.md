**Punk rock / post-hardcore / emo from Gainesville, Florida, USA. Active 1994–1998, 1998–2006, 2008–present.**

Hot Water Music is an American punk rock band formed in October 1994. The band's name comes from a collection of short stories by Charles Bukowski. Known for their energetic live performances and introspective songwriting, they have been influential in the post-hardcore and emo scenes, cited by bands like Against Me!, Senses Fail, and My Chemical Romance.
