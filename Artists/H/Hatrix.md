# Hatrix

**Thrash metal from Cleveland, Ohio, United States. Active 1989–1996, 2003–2004.**

Hatrix is an American thrash metal band formed in Cleveland, Ohio in 1989, fronted by future-Mushroomhead vocalist Jeff Hatrix after his departure from Purgatory. The band developed a strong local following with their blend of Pantera-influenced groove and traditional thrash metal, releasing one full-length album, *Collisioncoursewithnoplace* (1994), on Massacre Records. Their debut live show took place at Cleveland's Phantasy Theater in December 1989 as part of the Heavy Artillery compilation release party, and they continued playing sporadically through 2005 before going on hold.
