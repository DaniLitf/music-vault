**Rap rock / hardcore punk supergroup from New York City, USA. Active 2004–2006; 2009; 2011; 2012–present.**

Hazen Street is an American supergroup formed in early 2004, bringing together members from influential New York hardcore and punk bands. The group blends hard-hitting contemporary hardcore with metal, rap-rock, and pop overtones. The band's name derives from Hazen Street in Queens, a two-way street leading to the Rikers Island Bridge and prison facility. Their self-titled debut album was released on Joel and Benji Madden's DC Flag Records and achieved chart success, with tracks featured on major video game and sports soundtracks including NHL 2005, Madden NFL 2005, NBA 2K5, and WWE Survivor Series 2006.
