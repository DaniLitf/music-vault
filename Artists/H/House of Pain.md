**Hardcore hip-hop / Irish rap from Los Angeles, California. Active 1990–1996; 2010–2011; 2017.**

House of Pain was an American hardcore hip-hop trio known for pioneering Irish rap aesthetics in hip-hop. Formed in 1990 with members of Irish-American descent, they achieved major commercial success with their 1992 debut album and the iconic hit single "Jump Around," which reached number 3 on the Billboard Hot 100. The group disbanded in 1996 but reunited for tours in 2010–2011 and again in 2017.
