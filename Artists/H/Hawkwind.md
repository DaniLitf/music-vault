# Hawkwind

**Space rock from London, England. Active 1969–present.**

Hawkwind are an English rock band formed in London in 1969, widely credited as pioneers of space rock — a fusion of hard-driving rock, synthesizers, science-fiction themes, and elaborate light shows. Guitarist Dave Brock has been the band's sole constant member across a lineup that has changed dozens of times over five decades. Their most celebrated era came in the early-to-mid 1970s, anchored by the live album *Space Ritual* (1973) and bassist Lemmy Kilmister, who was fired from the band in 1975 and went on to found Motörhead.
