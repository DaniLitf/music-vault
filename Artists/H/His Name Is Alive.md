# His Name Is Alive

**Experimental rock / Dream pop from Livonia, Michigan. Active 1985–present.**

His Name Is Alive is an American experimental rock band/project from Livonia, Michigan, founded by Warren Defever. The band debuted on 4AD Records in 1990 and gained a cult following with their ethereal, genre-defying sound that blends dream pop, folk, noise, and soul. Defever has been the only constant member, with a rotating cast of vocalists and musicians contributing over the decades.
