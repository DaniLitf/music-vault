# Heavens

**Alternative/indie rock duo from Los Angeles, United States. Active 2004–2007.**

Heavens was a musical collaboration between [[Members/M/Matt Skiba]] of [[Artists/A/Alkaline Trio]] and [[Members/J/Josiah Steinbrick]] of [[Artists/F/F-Minus]]. Formed in Los Angeles in the early 2000s after Skiba relocated from Chicago, the duo drew from influences like The Sisters of Mercy, Wire, Brian Eno, and Television—a departure from their primary punk and hardcore projects. They recorded their sole album *Patent Pending* over a single week in August 2004, later fleshing out the tracks in spring 2005 with producer [[Producers/B/Ben Lovett]]. The album was released on Epitaph Records in September 2006. The band toured briefly with Darker My Love but disbanded after a short run.
