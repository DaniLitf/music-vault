**Hardcore punk / straight-edge band from New York City, USA. Active 1994–present.**

H2O is an iconic American hardcore punk band formed in 1994 by [[Members/T/Toby Morse]] and founding members. As one of the most influential straight-edge bands of all time, H2O pioneered the sound of New York hardcore and became a cornerstone of the international hardcore scene. The band has maintained its commitment to positive messaging, drug-free living, and youth activism throughout its 30-year history, with Morse's straightedge philosophy extending into his broader work with the "One Life, One Chance" nonprofit organization.
