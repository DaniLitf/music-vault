# Hi Tek 3

**Hip house from Belgium. Active 1990–1991.**

Hi Tek 3 was a Belgian hip house project formed by Yosef Wolde-Mariam, Koenraad Van-Lishout, and Stijn Lauwers. They are best known for their 1990 single "Spin That Wheel" (featuring Ya Kid K), which was featured on the soundtrack to the *Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles* film and became an international club hit. The group released one studio album, *The Easiest Way*, and several singles before disbanding in 1991.
