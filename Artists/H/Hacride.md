# Hacride

**French death metal band from Angers. Active 1998–present.**

Hacride are a French death metal band formed in Angers in 1998, known for blending technical and progressive elements with a heavy, aggressive sound. They gained international recognition with their album *Luzifer* (2007), which showcased a shift toward more atmospheric and experimental territory while maintaining their extreme metal core.
