# Hans Zimmer

**Hans Zimmer (born September 12, 1957, in Frankfurt, Germany) is a German composer and music producer, one of the most celebrated film score composers of all time, known for blending orchestral music with electronic elements on scores for *The Lion King* (1994), *Inception* (2010), *Interstellar* (2014), and many others.**

Hans Zimmer is a solo artist. See [[Members/H/Hans Zimmer]] for biography.
