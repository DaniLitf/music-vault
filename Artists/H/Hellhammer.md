# Hellhammer

**Extreme metal band from Switzerland. Active 1982–1984.**

Hellhammer were a Swiss extreme metal band formed in May 1982 in Fribourg, initially named Hammerhead, by guitarist/vocalist Tom Warrior (Thomas Gabriel Fischer). Though short-lived, their primitive, crushing sound on releases such as the *Apocalyptic Raids* EP proved hugely influential on the development of black, death, and thrash metal. The band disbanded in 1984, with Fischer and bassist Martin Eric Ain reuniting immediately afterward to form the far more celebrated Celtic Frost.
