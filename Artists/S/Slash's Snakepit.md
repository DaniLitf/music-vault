# Slash's Snakepit

**Slash's Snakepit was an American hard rock band formed by [[Members/S/Slash]] in 1994 while on a hiatus from [[Artists/G/Guns N' Roses]], featuring fellow GN'R members and exploring a rawer blues-rock direction.**

| Year | Title | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| 1995 | *It's Five O'Clock Somewhere* | debut |
| 2000 | *Ain't Life Grand* | |

## Members

### Core / Official Members
- [[Members/S/Slash]] — lead guitar (1994–2000; also [[Artists/G/Guns N' Roses]])
- [[Members/M/Matt Sorum]] — drums (1994–1997; also [[Artists/G/Guns N' Roses]], [[Artists/K/Kings of Chaos]])
- [[Members/M/Mike Inez]] — bass (1994–1997; also [[Artists/A/Alice in Chains]])

### Past Members
- [[Members/E/Eric Dover]] — vocals (1994–1997)
- [[Members/G/Gilby Clarke]] — rhythm guitar (1994–1997)

## Connections

- [[Artists/G/Guns N' Roses]] — Slash's Snakepit was founded by Slash, lead guitarist of Guns N' Roses, with Matt Sorum, GN'R's drummer, also participating.
- [[Artists/A/Alice in Chains]] — Mike Inez, bassist of Alice in Chains, also served as bassist for Slash's Snakepit (1994–1997).
