# Screamin' Daemon

**British blackened death metal band from Norwich, England. Active 2003–?**

Screamin' Daemon was a British blackened death metal band formed in 2003 in Norwich, Norfolk, featuring members who later formed or joined [[Artists/G/Gorerotted]] and [[Artists/T/The Rotted]].
