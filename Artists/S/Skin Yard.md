# Skin Yard

**Skin Yard were an American grunge band formed in Seattle, Washington in 1985, one of the earliest bands of the Seattle sound, notable for featuring [[Members/M/Matt Cameron]] on drums before he joined [[Artists/S/Soundgarden]].**

## Connections

- [[Artists/S/Soundgarden]] — Matt Cameron, Skin Yard's original drummer, left to join Soundgarden in 1986, where he became their permanent drummer and played on all their major albums.
