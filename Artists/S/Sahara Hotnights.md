# Sahara Hotnights

**Sahara Hotnights are a Swedish rock band formed in Robertsfors in 1991, known for their energetic garage rock sound and one of the most celebrated Swedish rock bands of the 2000s, breaking internationally with *Jennie Bomb* (2001).**
