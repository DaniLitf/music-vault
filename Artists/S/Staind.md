# Staind

**Alternative metal / post-grunge from Springfield, Massachusetts. Active 1995–2012, 2014, 2017, 2019–present.**

Staind formed in Springfield, Massachusetts in 1995 when vocalist [[Members/A/Aaron Lewis]] and guitarist [[Members/M/Mike Mushok]] brought in bassist [[Members/J/Johnny April]] and drummer [[Members/J/Jon Wysocki]]. After building a regional following, the band was discovered in 1997 by [[Members/F/Fred Durst]] of [[Artists/L/Limp Bizkit]], who signed them to Flip Records despite initially trying to have them pulled from the bill over their album art. Their major-label debut *Dysfunction* (1999) was co-produced by Durst and Terry Date, and the follow-up *Break the Cycle* (2001) — powered by the hit "It's Been Awhile" — became their best-selling album, going seven times platinum worldwide. The band became one of the defining acts of post-grunge before going on extended hiatus in 2012, with Lewis pursuing a solo country career and Mushok joining Jason Newsted's band [[Artists/N/Newsted]] and later co-founding Saint Asonia. Staind reunited for live shows from 2019 and released *Confessions of the Fallen* (2023).
