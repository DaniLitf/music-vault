# Stevie Wonder

**Stevie Wonder is an American singer, songwriter, musician, and record producer born Stevland Hardaway Morris on May 13, 1950, one of the most celebrated and influential figures in popular music history, known as a child prodigy and the creator of a string of landmark albums in the 1970s.**
