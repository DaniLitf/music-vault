# Starflyer 59

**Alternative rock / shoegaze band from Riverside, California. Active 1993–present.**

Starflyer 59 is an American alternative rock band founded in 1993 by Jason Martin, one of the first signees to Tooth & Nail Records. The band's early sound was rooted in the shoegaze movement with heavily distorted guitars and reverb-drenched production, as heard on their first two albums *Silver* (1994) and *Gold* (1995). Over the decades, Martin's songwriting evolved through dream pop, indie rock, and increasingly polished alternative rock, with a prolific output of 18 studio albums. While Jason Martin has been the sole constant member and primary songwriter, the band has featured numerous notable musicians including [[Members/J/Jeff Cloud]], [[Members/F/Frank Lenz]], [[Members/R/Richard Swift]], and [[Members/S/Steven Dail]].
