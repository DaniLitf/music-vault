# Soundgarden

**Soundgarden were an American rock band formed in Seattle, Washington in 1984, one of the four founding bands of the grunge movement alongside Nirvana, Alice in Chains, and Pearl Jam, known for Chris Cornell's extraordinary vocal range and the band's heavy, psychedelic approach to metal.**
