# Samson

**Samson were a British heavy metal band formed in London in 1977, one of the early New Wave of British Heavy Metal acts, best known as the band in which [[Members/B/Bruce Dickinson]] first came to prominence before joining [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].**

## Members

### Core / Official Members
- [[Members/P/Paul Samson]] — guitar (1977–2002; died 2002)
- [[Members/B/Bruce Dickinson]] — vocals (1979–1981; also [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]])
- [[Members/C/Clive Burr]] — drums (1979–1981; also [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]])

### Past Touring & Session Members
- [[Members/C/Chris Aylmer]] — bass (1978–1987)
- [[Members/T/Thunderstick]] — drums (1977–1979)

## Connections

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] — Bruce Dickinson, Samson's vocalist (1979–1981), left to join Iron Maiden and became their definitive singer. Clive Burr also served as Iron Maiden's drummer (1979–1982).
