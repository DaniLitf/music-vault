# Samson

**Samson were a British heavy metal band formed in London in 1977, one of the early New Wave of British Heavy Metal acts, best known as the band in which [[Members/B/Bruce Dickinson]] first came to prominence before joining [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]].**

## Members

### Core / Official Members
- [[Members/P/Paul Samson]] — guitar, vocals (1977–2002, founding member; died 2002)
- [[Members/C/Chris Aylmer]] — bass (1977–1987, founding member)
- [[Members/B/Bruce Dickinson]] — vocals (1979–1981; also [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]])

### Past Members
- [[Members/C/Clive Burr]] — drums (1977–1978; also [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]])
- [[Members/T/Thunderstick]] — drums (1978–1981)

## Connections

- [[Artists/I/Iron Maiden]] — Bruce Dickinson, Samson's vocalist (1979–1981), left to join Iron Maiden and became their definitive singer. Clive Burr, Samson's original drummer (1977–1978, before Dickinson's time in the band), also drummed for Iron Maiden (1979–1982), overlapping there with Dickinson from 1981.
