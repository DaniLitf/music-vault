# Sonic Youth

**Sonic Youth were an American rock band formed in New York City in 1981, pioneers of noise rock and alternative rock whose exploration of alternate guitar tunings, extended techniques, and dissonant song structures influenced virtually every subsequent generation of experimental rock.**
