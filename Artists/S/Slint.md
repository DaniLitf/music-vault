# Slint

**Post-rock / math rock from Louisville, Kentucky, USA. Active 1986–1990, 2007, 2014.**

Slint was an American rock band formed in 1986, considered pioneers of post-rock and math rock. Their 1991 album *Spiderland* is regarded as one of the most influential albums of the 1990s, despite the band having broken up before its release.
