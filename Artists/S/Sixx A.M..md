# Sixx A.M.

**Sixx:A.M. is an American hard rock band formed in 2006 by [[Members/N/Nikki Sixx]] of [[Artists/M/Mötley Crüe]], along with guitarist DJ Ashba and vocalist James Michael, initially to create a soundtrack for Sixx's memoir *The Heroin Diaries*.**
