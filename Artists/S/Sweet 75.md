# Sweet 75

**Sweet 75 were an American rock band formed in 1995 by [[Members/K/Krist Novoselic]] of [[Artists/N/Nirvana]] alongside Venezuelan vocalist Yva Las Vegass, exploring eclectic, genre-blending territory after Nirvana's dissolution.**

### Studio Albums
| Album | Year | Producer | Studio |
|-------|------|----------|--------|
| *Sweet 75* | 1997 | Yva Las Vegass, Bill Rieflin | - |

## Members

### Core / Official Members
- [[Members/K/Krist Novoselic]] — bass, guitar, accordion (1995–2000; also [[Artists/N/Nirvana]])
- [[Members/Y/Yva Las Vegass]] — vocals, guitar (1995–2000)
- [[Members/B/Bill Rieflin]] — drums (1995–2000; died 2020)

## Connections

- [[Artists/N/Nirvana]] — Krist Novoselic, Sweet 75's bassist, was the founding bassist of Nirvana alongside Kurt Cobain and Dave Grohl.
