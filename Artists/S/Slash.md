# Slash

**American rock guitarist from London, England. Active 1981–present.**

Slash (born Saul Hudson) is an American rock guitarist best known as the lead guitarist of [[Artists/G/Guns N' Roses]] (1985–1996, 2016–present), whose work on *Appetite for Destruction* made him one of the most iconic guitarists in rock history. After departing GN'R, Slash formed [[Artists/S/Slash's Snakepit]] and later [[Artists/S/Slash featuring Myles Kennedy and the Conspirators]]. He was inducted into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2012 as a member of Guns N' Roses.
