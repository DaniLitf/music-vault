# Sparks

**Sparks are an American pop/rock duo formed in Los Angeles in 1971 by brothers [[Members/R/Ron Mael]] and [[Members/R/Russell Mael]], one of the most enduring and eclectic acts in pop music, known for shifting effortlessly across glam rock, new wave, and electronic styles over a career spanning six decades.**
