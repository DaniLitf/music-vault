# Six Feet Under

**American death metal band from Tampa, Florida. Active 1993–present.**

Six Feet Under is an American death metal band formed in 1993 in Tampa, Florida, by vocalist Chris Barnes as a side project during his time with [[Artists/C/Cannibal Corpse]]. After Barnes was dismissed from Cannibal Corpse in 1995, Six Feet Under became his full-time focus. The band became known for their mid-tempo, groove-oriented death metal with guttural vocals and graphic horror lyrics. By 2003, Nielsen SoundScan data ranked them as the fourth best-selling death metal band in the U.S. with over 370,000 units sold.
