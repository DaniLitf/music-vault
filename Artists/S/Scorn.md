# Scorn

**English electronic music project from Birmingham. Active 1991–1997, 2000–2011, 2019–2022.**

Scorn is an English electronic music project formed in 1991 in Birmingham by drummer Mick Harris and vocalist/bassist Nicholas Bullen, both former members of grindcore pioneers [[Artists/N/Napalm Death]]. Initially an industrial metal band, Scorn quickly evolved into a bass-heavy, dub-influenced electronic project that helped pave the way for illbient, dubstep, and other styles of dark, bass-driven music. After Bullen's departure in 1995, the project continued as Harris's solo venture until 2011, was revived in 2019, and was permanently retired in late 2022.
