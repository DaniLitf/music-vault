# Sons of Apollo

**American progressive metal supergroup. Active 2017–present.**

Sons of Apollo are an American progressive metal supergroup formed in 2017, featuring former Dream Theater drummer Mike Portnoy, ex-Dream Theater keyboardist Derek Sherinian, guitarist Ron "Bumblefoot" Thal (ex-Guns N' Roses), bassist Billy Sheehan (ex-Mr. Big, David Lee Roth), and vocalist Jeff Scott Soto (ex-Journey, Yngwie Malmsteen). Their debut album *Psychotic Symphony* (2017) was acclaimed for its technical proficiency and melodic songwriting.
