# Semi Precious Weapons

**American glam rock band from New York City. Active 2004–2014.**

Semi Precious Weapons was an American glam rock band formed in New York City in 2004 by Berklee College of Music graduates Justin Tranter, Cole Whittle, Dan Crean, and Aaron Lee Tasjan. Known for their flamboyant visual style and high-energy live performances, the band drew from 1970s glitter rock and 1980s dance-punk. They gained widespread attention opening for Lady Gaga on The Monster Ball Tour (2009–2011) and became fixtures of the New York underground scene. After disbanding in 2014, Tranter became a prolific pop songwriter, Whittle joined DNCE, and Pyne and Crean joined Gwen Stefani's backing band.
