# Spiritualized

**Spiritualized are an English rock band formed in Rugby in 1990 by [[Members/J/Jason Pierce]] (J. Spaceman) after the breakup of Spacemen 3, known for their epic, orchestrated blend of drone rock, gospel, and psychedelia.**
