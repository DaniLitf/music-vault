# Satyricon

**Norwegian black metal band from Oslo. Active 1990–present.**

Satyricon are a Norwegian black metal band formed in Oslo in 1990 by Satyr (Sigurd Wongraven) and Frost (Kjetil Haraldstad). They are one of the most prominent and influential bands in the Norwegian black metal scene, known for evolving from raw, traditional black metal on their early releases to a more streamlined, rock-influenced approach from *Volcano* onward. Their 1996 album *Nemesis Divina* is considered a landmark of the genre.
