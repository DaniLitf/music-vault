# Slayer

**Slayer were an American thrash metal band formed in Huntington Park, California in 1981, one of the "Big Four" of thrash metal alongside Metallica, Megadeth, and Anthrax, known for their unrelenting speed, dark subject matter, and landmark albums *Reign in Blood* and *Seasons in the Abyss*. They disbanded in 2019.**
