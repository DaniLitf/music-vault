# Saint Asonia

**Post-grunge / hard rock supergroup from Toronto, Ontario, Canada. Active 2015–2024, 2025 (on hiatus).**

Saint Asonia formed in Toronto in 2014 when [[Artists/S/Staind]] guitarist [[Members/M/Mike Mushok]] and former [[Artists/T/Three Days Grace]] singer [[Members/A/Adam Gontier]] began writing songs together without initially intending to form a band; they enlisted former Finger Eleven drummer [[Members/R/Rich Beddoe]] and former Dark New Day bassist [[Members/C/Corey Lowery]] to complete the lineup. Their 2015 self-titled debut, led by the single "Better Place," topped the US Top Hard Rock Albums chart. Beddoe left in 2017 and was replaced by Mushok's Staind bandmate [[Members/S/Sal Giancarelli]]; Lowery departed in 2018 to join Seether full-time and was replaced by Gontier's cousin [[Members/C/Cale Gontier]]. Their second album, *Flawed Design* (2019), followed, and Art of Dying drummer [[Members/C/Cody Watkins]] took over on drums in 2020. The band went on hiatus in October 2024 after Gontier rejoined Three Days Grace full-time.
