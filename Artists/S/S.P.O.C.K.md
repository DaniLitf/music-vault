# S.P.O.C.K

**S.P.O.C.K (Space Pirates Of Cydonia's Kingdom) is a Swedish synthpop band formed in Stockholm in 1988, known for their science fiction-themed lyrics drawing heavily on Star Trek, Doctor Who, and other sci-fi properties.**
