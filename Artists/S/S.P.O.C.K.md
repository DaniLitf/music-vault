# S.P.O.C.K

**Swedish synthpop band from Stockholm. Active 1988–present.**

S.P.O.C.K (Space Pirates Of Cydonia's Kingdom, also known as Star Pilot On Channel K) is a Swedish synthpop band formed in 1988 in Stockholm, known for their science fiction-themed lyrics drawing heavily on Star Trek, Doctor Who, and other sci-fi properties. Together with bands like Page, Elegant Machinery, and Sista Mannen På Jorden, they helped define the sound of the Swedish synthpop movement.
