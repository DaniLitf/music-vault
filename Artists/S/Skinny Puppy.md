# Skinny Puppy

**Skinny Puppy are a Canadian industrial band formed in Vancouver in 1982, co-founded by Nivek Ogre and cEvin Key, pioneers of electro-industrial and dark ambient who helped define the industrial sound that influenced Ministry, Nine Inch Nails, and countless others.**
