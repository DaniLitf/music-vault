**Heavy metal from Cleveland, Ohio. Active 1977–1985, 2016–?.**

Sacred Few was an American heavy metal band from Cleveland, Ohio, formed in 1977. They released one studio album, *Beyond the Iron Walls* (1985), which has since become a cult classic of the early US heavy metal scene. Notable for being fronted by female vocalist Sandy Rago (R.I.P. 2022), the band's sound was heavily influenced by the New Wave of British Heavy Metal, with raw guitar riffs and a distinctly retro style that set them apart from the thrash metal emerging at the time.
