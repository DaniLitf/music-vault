# Sly and Robbie

**Jamaican reggae and dub rhythm section duo. Active 1970s–present.**

Sly and Robbie were the Jamaican rhythm section duo of drummer [[Members/S/Sly Dunbar]] and bassist [[Members/R/Robbie Shakespeare]] (died 2021), who together formed one of the most prolific and influential rhythm sections in reggae history. They played on thousands of recordings across reggae, dub, dancehall, and pop, working with artists such as [[Artists/B/Bob Marley and the Wailers]], [[Artists/G/Grace Jones]], [[Artists/N/No Doubt]], and many others.
