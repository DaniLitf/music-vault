# Schwein

**Schwein was a collaborative industrial project formed in 2003 by [[Members/S/Sascha Konietzko]] of [[Artists/K/KMFDM]] and members of Japanese visual kei rock band [[Artists/B/Buck-Tick]], combining German industrial with Japanese rock.**

| Year | Title | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| 2003 | *We Are Schwein* | sole album |

## Members

### Core / Official Members
- [[Members/S/Sascha Konietzko]] — production (also [[Artists/K/KMFDM]])
- [[Members/A/Atsushi Sakurai]] — vocals (also [[Artists/B/Buck-Tick]])
- [[Members/H/Hisashi Imai]] — guitar (also [[Artists/B/Buck-Tick]])
- [[Members/L/Lucia Cifarelli]] — vocals (also [[Artists/K/KMFDM]])

## Connections

- [[Artists/K/KMFDM]] — Sascha Konietzko, KMFDM's founder and primary force, co-created Schwein with Buck-Tick members; Lucia Cifarelli also co-participates as a KMFDM member.
- [[Artists/B/Buck-Tick]] — Atsushi Sakurai and Hisashi Imai, vocalist and guitarist of Buck-Tick, joined Schwein as collaborators.
