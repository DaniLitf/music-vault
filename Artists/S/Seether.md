# Seether

**Post-grunge / hard rock from Pretoria, Gauteng, South Africa. Active 1999–present.**

Seether formed in Pretoria in 1999 as Saron Gas, led by vocalist/guitarist [[Members/S/Shaun Morgan]] alongside guitarist [[Members/J/Johan Greyling]] and drummer [[Members/D/Dave Cohoe]]. After relocating to the United States in 2002, the band renamed itself Seether to avoid confusion with the nerve agent sarin, and broke through with the US Active Rock number-one single "Fine Again" from their major-label debut *Disclaimer* (2002). Bassist [[Members/D/Dale Stewart]] joined in 2000 and drummer [[Members/J/John Humphrey]] joined for the band's second album, both becoming long-term members alongside Morgan, the band's only original member to remain throughout. The lineup saw further turnover on guitar through the 2000s — [[Members/N/Nick Oshiro]] briefly drummed, [[Members/P/Pat Callahan]] played guitar from 2002 to 2006, and [[Members/T/Troy McLawhorn]] served as guitarist from 2008 to 2011 — before [[Members/C/Corey Lowery]] joined as second guitarist in 2018. Seether's biggest commercial success came with *Finding Beauty in Negative Spaces* (2007) and *Isolate and Medicate* (2014), the latter topping Billboard's Rock, Alternative, and Hard Rock album charts.
