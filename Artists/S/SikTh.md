# SikTh

**English progressive metal band from Watford, Hertfordshire. Active 1999–2008, 2013–present.**

SikTh (also typeset as SikTh) are an English progressive metal band formed in Watford in 1999, known for their technical, dissonant guitar work, dual vocalists, and unconventional song structures. They are widely regarded as innovators in the djent and mathcore scenes, influencing a generation of progressive metal bands. The band went on hiatus in 2008 before reuniting in 2013.
