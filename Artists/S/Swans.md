# Swans

**Swans are an American experimental rock band formed in New York City in 1982 by Michael Gira, one of the most uncompromising and extreme acts in rock history — shifting across decades from confrontational noise rock to epic, transcendent post-rock while releasing some of the loudest, most intense music ever recorded.**
