# Snap!

**Eurodance / hip house / house from Frankfurt, Germany. Active 1989–1996, 2000–present.**

Snap! is a German Eurodance group formed in 1989 by record producers Michael Münzing and Luca Anzilotti. The group became one of the most successful Eurodance acts of the 1990s with international hits including "The Power" (1990) and "Rhythm Is a Dancer" (1992), both of which reached No. 1 in multiple countries. The act has undergone several lineup changes over the years, featuring various American singers, songwriters, and rappers.
