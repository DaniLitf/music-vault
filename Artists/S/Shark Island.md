# Shark Island

**American glam metal band from Los Angeles, California. Active 1979–1992, 2017–present.**

Shark Island is an American glam metal band formed in 1986 in Los Angeles, growing out of the ashes of the cult Los Angeles glam rockers the Sharks. Originally the house band at Gazzari's on the Sunset Strip, the band is best known for their cult hit album *Law of the Order* (1989) and for contributing tracks to the *Bill & Ted's Excellent Adventure* soundtrack. After disbanding in the early 1990s, the band reformed in the 2000s and released new material in 2006 and 2019.
