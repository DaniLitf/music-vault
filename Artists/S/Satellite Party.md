# Satellite Party

**Alternative rock / electronica project from the United States. Active 2004–2008.**

Satellite Party was formed by [[Members/P/Perry Farrell]] following the 2004 breakup of [[Artists/J/Jane's Addiction]], initially as a collaboration with Extreme guitarist [[Members/N/Nuno Bettencourt]]. The band released a single studio album, *Ultra Payloaded* (2007), featuring guest contributions from [[Members/F/Flea]] and [[Members/J/John Frusciante]] of [[Artists/R/Red Hot Chili Peppers]], Fergie of the Black Eyed Peas, New Order/Joy Division bassist Peter Hook, and a "lost" vocal from the late Jim Morrison of the Doors on "Woman in the Window." Bettencourt and drummer Kevin Figueiredo departed in mid-2007 to rejoin a reunited Extreme; the band dissolved in 2008 when Farrell reunited Jane's Addiction full-time.
