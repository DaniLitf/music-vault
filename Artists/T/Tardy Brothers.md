# Tardy Brothers

**Death metal/groove metal project from Gibsonton, Florida. Active 2008–2010.**

Tardy Brothers was a death metal project formed in 2008 by [[Members/J/John Tardy]] (vocals) and [[Members/D/Donald Tardy]] (drums) of [[Artists/O/Obituary]]. The brothers recorded their debut album *Bloodline* in 2009, featuring guest guitarists Jerry Tidwell (Xecutioner), Ralph Santolla (Obituary, Deicide), John Li (Order of Ennead), and Scott Johnson.
