# Ten Inch Men

**Alternative/hard rock band from Long Beach, California. Active 1983–1994, with later reunions.**

Ten Inch Men was an American rock band formed in Long Beach, California in 1983 by record producer Dana Allen on his Obelisk Records label. Their music found rotation on college radio stations across the US, and their 1986 single "Flower Power" reached No. 1 in New York City. The band is notable as the launching point for singer Dave Coutts, who later fronted Talk Show alongside members of Stone Temple Pilots.
