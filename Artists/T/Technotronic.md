**Belgian electronic music project from Aalst, Belgium. Active 1987–2001.**

Technotronic was a Belgian electronic music project formed in 1987 by producer [[Members/J/Jo Bogaert|Jo Bogaert]] (under the alias Thomas De Quincey), best known for their 1989 single "Pump Up the Jam". The project featured a rotating cast of vocalists led by [[Members/Y/Ya Kid K|Ya Kid K]], whose vocals powered the group's biggest hits. Technotronic helped bring hip house and Eurodance into the pop mainstream, selling over 14 million albums worldwide.

Initially, Congolese fashion model [[Members/F/Felly|Felly Kilingi]] was presented as the group's frontwoman, lip-syncing in the music video and appearing on the album cover, while Ya Kid K provided the actual vocals — a deception similar to [[Artists/B/Black Box|Black Box]] and Milli Vanilli. The truth emerged after the debut album's success, and Ya Kid K became the public face of the group.
