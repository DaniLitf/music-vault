# The Greenhornes

**Garage rock / blues rock from Cincinnati, Ohio. Active 1996–2012.**

The Greenhornes formed in 1996 in Cincinnati, founded by vocalist/guitarist [[Members/C/Craig Fox]], bassist [[Members/J/Jack Lawrence]], and drummer [[Members/P/Patrick Keeler]]. By 2003 the band had settled into that trio lineup after early members Brian Olive and Jared McKinney departed. In the mid-2000s Lawrence and Keeler backed Loretta Lynn on *Van Lear Rose* (2004, produced by Jack White) and then spent much of their time in [[Artists/T/The Raconteurs]] with White and Brendan Benson, sharply curtailing Greenhornes activity. The band reunited in 2010 for the album *Four Stars*. Lawrence also plays bass in [[Artists/T/The Dead Weather]].
