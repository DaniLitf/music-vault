# The Prodigy

**Electronic / big beat / rave from Braintree, Essex, England. Active 1990–present.**

The Prodigy are an English electronic group formed in Braintree, Essex in 1990 by producer, keyboardist, and songwriter Liam Howlett, one of the defining acts of the big beat and rave era, responsible for bringing aggressive, high-energy electronic music to mainstream rock audiences. At their commercial and critical peak they combined Howlett's productions with MC Maxim's vocals and dancers Keith Flint and Leeroy Thornhill's stage presence, scoring seven consecutive UK number one albums and the UK number one singles "Firestarter" and "Breathe" from 1997's *The Fat of the Land*.
