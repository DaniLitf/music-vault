# Tin Machine

**Hard rock band from London, England / New York, USA. Active 1988–1992.**

Tin Machine was a British–American hard rock band formed in 1988 by [[Members/D/David Bowie]] as an equal-member ensemble rather than a solo vehicle, alongside guitarist [[Members/R/Reeves Gabrels]] and the rhythm section of brothers [[Members/T/Tony Sales]] (bass) and [[Members/H/Hunt Sales]] (drums) — the same Sales brothers who had backed Bowie and Iggy Pop on tour in 1977. The band released two studio albums and a live album before dissolving in 1992, after which Bowie returned to his solo career.
