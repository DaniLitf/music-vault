# Tigertailz

**Glam metal band from Cardiff, Wales. Active 1984–1996, 2005–present.**

Tigertailz are a Welsh glam metal band formed in Cardiff in 1984. They gained commercial success with their second album *Bezerk* (1990), which reached the UK Top 40 and spawned the hit singles "Love Bomb Baby" and "Heaven". Known for their flamboyant style and high-energy performances, the band disbanded in 1996 but reformed in 2005 and continue to tour and record.
