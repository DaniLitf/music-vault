# The KLF

**The KLF were a British electronic music duo formed in London in 1987 by Bill Drummond and Jimmy Cauty, known for chart-topping ambient house music, their controversial art pranks, and their 1992 deletion of their entire back catalogue.**

| Year | Title | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| 1988 | *1987 (What the Fuck Is Going On?)* | debut; as The JAMs |
| 1989 | *The White Room* | ambient house masterpiece |
| 1990 | *Chill Out* | ambient techno landmark |
| 1991 | *The White Room* | re-release with pop singles |
| 1992 | *The Black Room* | unreleased; catalogue deleted |
