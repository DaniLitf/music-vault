# Tupac Shakur

**West Coast hip-hop / gangsta rap from New York City / Los Angeles, USA. Active 1989–1996 (posthumous releases through 2006).**

Tupac Shakur, known by his stage names 2Pac and Makaveli, was an American rapper and actor, widely regarded as one of the most influential musical artists of the 20th century. Starting as a dancer and roadie for Digital Underground, he broke through with the politically charged *2Pacalypse Now* (1991) and became the defining figure of 1990s West Coast hip-hop, selling more than 75 million records worldwide. He was murdered in a drive-by shooting in Las Vegas in September 1996, at the height of the East Coast–West Coast hip-hop rivalry.

Tupac Shakur is a solo artist. See [[Members/T/Tupac Shakur]] for biography.
