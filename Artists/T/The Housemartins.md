# The Housemartins

**The Housemartins were an English indie pop band formed in Hull in 1983, known for their jangle pop sound and socialist lyrics, whose members went on to form Beats International and The Beautiful South after the band dissolved in 1988.**

| Year | Title | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| 1986 | *London 0 Hull 4* | debut |
| 1987 | *The People Who Grinned Themselves to Death* | final album |
