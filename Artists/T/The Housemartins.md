# The Housemartins

**Indie pop / jangle pop band from Hull, England. Active 1983–1988.**

The Housemartins were an English indie pop band formed in Hull by [[Members/P/Paul Heaton]] and [[Members/S/Stan Cullimore]], later joined by members including [[Members/N/Norman Cook]] and [[Members/D/Dave Hemingway]]. Their two studio albums reached the UK top ten, their a cappella cover of "Caravan of Love" reached number one, and their breakup led directly to [[Artists/T/The Beautiful South]], [[Artists/B/Beats International]], and Cook's later [[Artists/F/Fatboy Slim]] career.
