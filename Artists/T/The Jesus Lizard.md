# The Jesus Lizard

**Noise rock / post-hardcore band from Austin, Texas, USA. Active 1987–1999, 2008–2010, 2017–present.**

The Jesus Lizard are an American noise rock band formed by vocalist [[Members/D/David Yow]], guitarist [[Members/D/Duane Denison]], and bassist [[Members/D/David Wm. Sims]], later joined by drummer [[Members/M/Mac McNeilly]]. After relocating to Chicago, they made a string of influential records with [[Producers/S/Steve Albini]] for Touch and Go, becoming one of the defining American underground rock bands of the 1990s before reuniting and releasing *Rack* in 2024.
