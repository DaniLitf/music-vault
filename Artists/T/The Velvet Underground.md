# The Velvet Underground

**Proto-punk / art rock from New York City, USA. Active 1964–1973, 1990, 1992–1993, 1996.**

The Velvet Underground was one of the most influential bands in rock history. Formed in New York City, they integrated rock and roll with avant-garde art, addressing taboo subjects with a raw, minimalist sound. Though commercially unsuccessful during their initial run, their music became foundational for punk, new wave, and alternative rock.
