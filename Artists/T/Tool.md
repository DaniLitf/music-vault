# Tool

**Tool are an American progressive metal band formed in Los Angeles in 1990, known for their unconventional song structures, philosophical and provocative lyrical content, and elaborate visual presentation including the work of artist Alex Grey.**

## Discography

### Studio Albums
| Year | Title | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|

| 1996 | *Ænima* | #2 US; "Stinkfist"; "Forty Six & 2" |
| 2001 | *Lateralus* | Fibonacci sequence in song structure |
| 2006 | *10,000 Days* | #1 US |
| 2019 | *Fear Inoculum* | 13 years after previous release |
