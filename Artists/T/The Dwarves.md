# The Dwarves

**Punk rock / hardcore punk band from Chicago, Illinois, USA. Active 1985–present.**

The Dwarves are an American punk rock band formed in Chicago and later associated with San Francisco, led by vocalist [[Members/B/Blag Dahlia]] and the masked guitarist [[Members/H/HeWhoCannotBeNamed]]. Starting as the garage-psych outfit Suburban Nightmare, the band became known for short, confrontational hardcore-punk records, provocative imagery, and a rotating lineup that has included musicians from [[Artists/Q/Queens of the Stone Age]], [[Artists/K/KMFDM]], [[Artists/M/Mondo Generator]], and other punk/rock projects.
