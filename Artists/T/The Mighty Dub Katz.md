# The Mighty Dub Katz

**House / big beat project from the United Kingdom. Active mid-1990s.**

The Mighty Dub Katz (often credited as Mighty Dub Katz) was a house and big beat project by [[Members/N/Norman Cook]] and Gareth Hansome, also known as GMoney. The project is best known for the 1995 single "Magic Carpet Ride," a Latin-house-flavoured club hit built around samples and released during Cook's transition from earlier projects toward his later [[Artists/F/Fatboy Slim]] breakthrough.
