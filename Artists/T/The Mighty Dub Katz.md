# The Mighty Dub Katz

**The Mighty Dub Katz is an alias used by [[Members/N/Norman Cook]] for big beat and house productions, one of several monikers Cook employs alongside Fatboy Slim, Beats International, and Pizzaman.**

| Year | Title | Notes |

## Members

The Mighty Dub Katz is a solo project. See [[Members/N/Norman Cook]] for biography.

## Connections

- [[Artists/F/Fatboy Slim]] — The Mighty Dub Katz is one of several aliases used by Norman Cook, who records most widely as Fatboy Slim.
- [[Artists/B/Beats International]] — Norman Cook previously recorded as Beats International before adopting the Fatboy Slim and Mighty Dub Katz aliases.
