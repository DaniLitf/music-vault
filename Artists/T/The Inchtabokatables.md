# The Inchtabokatables

**Folk rock / punk rock / industrial band from Berlin, Germany. Active 1991–2002.**

The Inchtabokatables were a German band formed in Berlin in 1991 and known for a strict "no guitar" lineup built around violin, cello, bass, and drums. Their sound moved from folk-punk and medieval-influenced rock toward industrial elements on later releases, and former bassist [[Members/O/Oliver Riedel]] went on to join [[Artists/R/Rammstein]].
