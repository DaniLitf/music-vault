# Twin Pigs

**Punk rock band from Stockholm, Sweden. Active 2015–present.**

Twin Pigs are a Swedish punk band formed in Stockholm in 2015, known for pairing catchy, hook-driven songwriting with pointed, socially observant lyrics. The band has released albums through Spastic Fantastic Records and Luftslott/Nightmare Purple Records, touring Europe alongside acts like Division of Laura Lee.
