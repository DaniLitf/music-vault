# Tricky

**Tricky (born Adrian Thaws, 1968-01-27) is an English musician and producer from Bristol, a key figure in the trip-hop scene who rose to prominence as an associate member of Massive Attack before launching a solo career with the landmark album *Maxinquaye*.**
