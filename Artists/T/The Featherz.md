# The Featherz

**Glam punk / punk rock band from London, England. Active 2011–present; dormant since early 2019.**

The Featherz are a Welsh-English glam punk band led by vocalist and guitarist [[Members/D/Danie Cox]], who later used the name Centric. Originally formed as The Feathers by three former Poussez Posse members, the group developed a self-described "Flock Rock" style, toured with Boy George and The Damned, and released the album *Five-Year-Itch* in 2017 before Cox shifted focus to the Slade tribute project Slady.
