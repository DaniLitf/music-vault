# The Latin Kings

**Swedish hip hop group from Botkyrka / Stockholm, Sweden. Active 1991–2005; reunion activity from 2019.**

The Latin Kings were a pioneering Swedish hip hop group from Botkyrka, led by [[Members/D/Dogge Doggelito]] with the Salazar brothers [[Members/S/Salla]] and [[Members/C/Chepe]]. Their 1994 debut *Välkommen till förorten* helped establish Swedish-language rap rooted in suburban slang and immigrant experience, and the group later founded Redline Records.
