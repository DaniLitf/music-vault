# The Soundtrack of Our Lives

**The Soundtrack of Our Lives are a Swedish psychedelic rock band formed in Gothenburg in 1995, known for their sprawling, retro-influenced sound that drew comparisons to The Beatles and The Rolling Stones. Active 1995–2012, reunited 2023–present.**
