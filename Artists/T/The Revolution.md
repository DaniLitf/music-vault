# The Revolution

**R&B / funk rock / psychedelic pop band from Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA. Active 1979–1986, 2012, 2016–present.**

The Revolution was the backing band for [[Artists/P/Prince]] from 1979 to 1986, evolving from his earlier live band into a distinct musical entity. They were officially named the Revolution for the *Purple Rain* era onward, contributing to Prince's most commercially successful period with a string of landmark albums and tours. The band disbanded after the *Parade* tour in 1986 but has reunited for occasional performances following Prince's death.
