# The Violet Burning

**Christian alternative rock from Orange County, California / Boston, Massachusetts. Active 1989–present.**

The Violet Burning is an independent American Christian alternative rock band formed in 1989 in Orange County, California by Michael J. Pritzl. The band has released over a dozen albums and EPs, toured North America and Europe multiple times, and sold over 100,000 albums.
