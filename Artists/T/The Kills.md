# The Kills

**Indie rock / garage rock duo from London, England / United States. Active 2001–present.**

The Kills are an English-American rock duo formed in London in 2001 by American singer [[Members/A/Alison Mosshart]] (previously of the punk band Discount) and English guitarist [[Members/J/Jamie Hince]]. Originally performing as "VV" and "Hotel," the pair built a minimalist, drum-machine-driven sound, recording their debut *Keep on Your Mean Side* (2003) at Toe Rag Studios, where The White Stripes had recorded *Elephant*. Their first four albums all charted in the UK; *Ash & Ice* (2016) reached the UK top 20. Mosshart also fronts the rock supergroup [[Artists/T/The Dead Weather]] alongside Jack White.
