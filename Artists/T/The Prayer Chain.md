# The Prayer Chain

**Christian alternative rock band from California, USA. Active 1991–1995, 1998, 2003, 2017–2018.**

The Prayer Chain was a Christian alternative rock band formed in California in 1990, known for producing moody, angst-ridden music that pushed against the boundaries of the contemporary Christian music scene. The band released three studio albums — *The Neverland Sessions* (1992), *Shawl* (1993), and *Mercury* (1995) — all produced by [[Producers/S/Steve Hindalong]]. Despite creative differences with their label Reunion Records, the band developed a devoted following and influenced the Christian alternative rock scene of the 1990s. After disbanding in 1995, the members went on to form or join [[Artists/T/The Lassie Foundation]], [[Artists/C/Cush]], [[Artists/S/Starflyer 59]], and other projects.
