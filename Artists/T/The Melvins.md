# The Melvins

**Sludge metal / experimental rock band from Montesano, Washington, USA. Active 1983–present.**

Melvins are an American rock band formed in 1983 by [[Members/B/Buzz Osborne]], [[Members/M/Matt Lukin]], and [[Members/M/Mike Dillard]], with [[Members/D/Dale Crover]] joining in 1984 and becoming Osborne's long-running core partner. Their slow, heavy, experimental approach helped shape sludge metal, alternative metal, and grunge, and their orbit connects them to [[Artists/N/Nirvana]], [[Artists/M/Mudhoney]], [[Artists/F/Fantômas]], [[Artists/B/Big Business]], [[Artists/R/Redd Kross]], [[Artists/B/Butthole Surfers]], and [[Artists/N/Napalm Death]].

## Connections

- [[Artists/N/Nirvana]] – Dale Crover, Buzz Osborne, Kurt Cobain, Krist Novoselic (Crover drummed on Nirvana's early demo material; Osborne was a close associate and introduced Cobain/Novoselic to Dave Grohl; Cobain helped with *Houdini*; Novoselic guested on *Basses Loaded*.)
- [[Artists/M/Mudhoney]] – Matt Lukin (Lukin co-founded Mudhoney after leaving Melvins; Melvins later released a split with Mudhoney.)
- [[Artists/F/Fantômas]] – Buzz Osborne, Trevor Dunn (Osborne and Dunn are Fantômas members; Melvins released *Millennium Monsterwork 2000* with Fantômas.)
- [[Artists/B/Big Business]] – Jared Warren, Coady Willis (both Big Business members joined Melvins in the 2006 two-drummer lineup.)
- [[Artists/R/Redd Kross]] – Steven Shane McDonald (current Melvins bassist is also in Redd Kross.)
- [[Artists/B/Butthole Surfers]] – Jeff Pinkus, Paul Leary (Pinkus played bass with Melvins; Leary joined for *Hold It In*.)
- [[Artists/N/Napalm Death]] – Shane Embury, Barney Greenway, John Cooke (Napalm Death members collaborated with Melvins on *Savage Imperial Death March*.)
- [[Artists/D/Dead Kennedys]] – Jello Biafra (Biafra collaborated with Melvins on two albums.)
- [[Artists/F/Flipper]] – split release (Melvins released *Hot Fish* with Flipper.)
