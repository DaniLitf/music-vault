# The Kovenant

**Norwegian black metal/industrial metal band from Lillestrøm. Active 1994–present.**

The Kovenant (originally Covenant) are a Norwegian black metal and industrial metal band formed in Lillestrøm in 1994 by Nagash (Stian Arnesen) and Blackheart (Amund Svensson). They were forced to change their name from Covenant in 2001 due to a legal dispute with the Swedish EBM band of the same name. The band evolved from atmospheric black metal on their debut to a more industrial-influenced sound on later albums.
