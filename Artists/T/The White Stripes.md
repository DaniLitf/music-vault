# The White Stripes

**Rock / garage rock revival from Detroit, Michigan, USA. Active 1997–2011.**

The White Stripes were an American rock band formed by [[Members/J/Jack White]] (vocals, guitar, piano) and [[Members/M/Meg White]] (drums, vocals). Known for their minimalist approach and distinctive sound blending blues, punk, and folk.
