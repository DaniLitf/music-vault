# Type O Negative

**Type O Negative were an American gothic metal band formed in Brooklyn, New York in 1989 by Peter Steele, whose dark, slow-burning sound blending Black Sabbath heaviness with gothic atmosphere and sardonic humor made them one of the defining acts of gothic metal.**
