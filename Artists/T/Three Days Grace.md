# Three Days Grace

**Post-grunge / hard rock from Norwood, Ontario, Canada. Active 1992–1995 (as Groundswell), 1997–present.**

Three Days Grace formed in Norwood, Ontario in 1992 as Groundswell, featuring vocalist/guitarist [[Members/A/Adam Gontier]], drummer [[Members/N/Neil Sanderson]], and bassist [[Members/B/Brad Walst]]. The band disbanded in 1995 and regrouped under its current name in 1997, adding lead guitarist [[Members/B/Barry Stock]] in 2003. Their self-titled 2003 debut produced the hit "I Hate Everything About You," and *One-X* (2006) and *Life Starts Now* (2009) cemented them as one of the era's most successful post-grunge acts, with the latter reaching No. 3 on the Billboard 200. Gontier departed in 2013 and co-founded [[Artists/S/Saint Asonia]]; he was replaced by Brad Walst's younger brother [[Members/M/Matt Walst]], previously of My Darkest Days and a longtime Three Days Grace songwriter. In October 2024, Gontier officially rejoined as co-lead vocalist alongside Matt Walst, making the band a dual-vocalist quintet for the first time since its Groundswell days.
