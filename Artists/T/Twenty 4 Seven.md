# Twenty 4 Seven

**Eurodance group from Valkenswaard, Netherlands. Active 1989–1991, 1993–present.**

Twenty 4 Seven is a Dutch Eurodance group formed in 1989 by producer Ruud van Rijen. The project is best known for the hit singles "I Can't Stand It", "Are You Dreaming?", "Slave to the Music", "Is It Love", and "Take Me Away", which charted across Europe in the 1990s. The group has gone through numerous lineup changes over the decades, with original singer Nancy "Nance" Coolen returning in 2021 alongside dancers Jacks and Hanks.
