# The Dead Weather

**Rock / alternative rock from Nashville, Tennessee, USA. Active 2009–present.**

The Dead Weather is a rock supergroup formed by [[Members/J/Jack White]], [[Members/A/Alison Mosshart]] ([[Artists/T/The Kills]]), [[Members/D/Dean Fertita]] ([[Artists/Q/Queens of the Stone Age]]), and [[Members/J/Jack Lawrence]] ([[Artists/T/The Raconteurs]], [[Artists/T/The Greenhornes]]).
