# The Dead Daisies

**Hard rock band from Sydney, Australia. Active 2012–present.**

The Dead Daisies are an Australian-American hard rock supergroup formed in Sydney in 2012 by guitarist David Lowy and vocalist Jon Stevens. The band has functioned as a collective with a rotating lineup of musicians from notable rock bands including Whitesnake, Guns N' Roses, Black Sabbath, Mötley Crüe, Deep Purple, and Journey.
