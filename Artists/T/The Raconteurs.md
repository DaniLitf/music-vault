# The Raconteurs

**Rock / alternative rock from Detroit, Michigan, USA and Nashville, Tennessee, USA. Active 2005–2012, 2019–present.**

The Raconteurs (known as The Saboteurs in Australia) are a rock band formed by [[Members/J/Jack White]], [[Members/B/Brendan Benson]], [[Members/J/Jack Lawrence]], and [[Members/P/Patrick Keeler]].
