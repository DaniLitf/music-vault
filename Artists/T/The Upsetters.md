# The Upsetters

**Reggae and dub house band from Kingston, Jamaica. Active 1968–1978.**

The Upsetters were the studio and touring band for legendary Jamaican producer Lee "Scratch" Perry, formed in 1968. Originally drawn from Gladdy's All-Stars (led by pianist Gladstone Anderson), the band later incorporated the Hippy Boys, whose members included brothers Aston "Family Man" Barrett and Carlton Barrett — who went on to form the core of Bob Marley's Wailers band. The Upsetters backed countless classic reggae recordings and released numerous instrumental albums, including the UK hit "Return of Django" (1969).

## Connections

- [[Artists/L/Lee Scratch Perry]] – The Upsetters were Lee Perry's house band; he produced and directed all of their recordings.
- [[Artists/M/Max Romeo]] – The Upsetters backed Max Romeo on *War Ina Babylon* (1976).
- [[Artists/B/Bob Marley]] – Aston and Carlton Barrett left The Upsetters to join Bob Marley's Wailers, forming the core of the band; Hugh Malcolm, Mikey "Boo" Richards, and Vin Gordon also contributed session/touring work.
