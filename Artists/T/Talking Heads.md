# Talking Heads

**Talking Heads were an American new wave band formed in New York City in 1975, one of the most critically acclaimed bands of the post-punk era, known for blending punk, art rock, funk, and world music — and for their landmark collaboration with producer Brian Eno.**

## Connections

- [[Artists/T/Tom Tom Club]] — Chris Frantz and Tina Weymouth, Talking Heads' drummer and bassist, co-founded Tom Tom Club as a side project in 1981.
- [[Artists/B/Brian Eno]] — Brian Eno produced three of Talking Heads' most acclaimed albums (*More Songs About Buildings and Food*, *Fear of Music*, *Remain in Light*).
- [[Artists/P/Parliament-Funkadelic]] — Bernie Worrell, P-Funk's co-founding keyboardist, toured and recorded with Talking Heads from 1980.
