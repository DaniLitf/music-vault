# The Future Sound of London

**The Future Sound of London (FSOL) are a British electronic music duo formed in Manchester in 1991 by Brian Dougans and Garry Cobain, known for pioneering ambient techno and IDM with their landmark albums *Accelerator* and *ISDN*.**
