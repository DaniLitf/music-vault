# The Scream

**Hard rock / blues rock from Los Angeles, California, USA. Active 1989–1993.**

The Scream was an American hard rock band formed in Los Angeles in 1989 by former Racer X members Bruce Bouillet and Juan Alderete, alongside vocalist John Corabi. They released one studio album, *Let It Scream* (1991), on Hollywood Records before Corabi left to join Mötley Crüe. The band attempted to continue with new vocalist Billy Fogarty, recording an unreleased second album before disbanding in 1993.
