# Tenek

**UK synthpop/electronic band. Active 2000s–present.**

Tenek are a British synthpop and electronic band formed in the 2000s, known for their melodic, layered electronic sound blending classic synthpop songwriting with modern production techniques, part of the UK's vibrant synthpop revival scene.
