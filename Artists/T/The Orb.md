# The Orb

**Ambient house / electronic collective from London, England. Active 1988–present.**

The Orb are an English electronic music group founded by [[Members/A/Alex Paterson]] and [[Members/J/Jimmy Cauty]], and one of the defining acts of ambient house and chill-out music. Built around Paterson's long-running curatorial role and a rotating cast of producers, engineers, and collaborators, The Orb's discography runs from the landmark *The Orb's Adventures Beyond the Ultraworld* and *U.F.Orb* through collaborations with [[Members/D/David Gilmour]] and [[Producers/L/Lee Scratch Perry]].

## Connections

- [[Artists/T/The KLF]] – Jimmy Cauty, Alex Paterson (Cauty co-founded The Orb and The KLF; Paterson contributed uncredited/contested material to *Chill Out* sessions.)
- [[Artists/K/Killing Joke]] – Alex Paterson, Youth (Paterson worked as a Killing Joke roadie; Youth was Killing Joke's bassist and an Orb collaborator.)
- [[Artists/P/Pink Floyd]] – David Gilmour (The Orb collaborated with Gilmour on *Metallic Spheres*.)
- [[Artists/T/The Upsetters]] – Lee Scratch Perry (Perry collaborated with The Orb; Perry is linked in the vault via The Upsetters.)
- [[Artists/M/Meat Beat Manifesto]] – shared EP (The Orb released *Battersea Shield* with Meat Beat Manifesto.)
- [[Artists/S/System 7]] – Steve Hillage (Hillage contributed to early Orb recordings and is associated with System 7.)
- [[Artists/M/Morcheeba]] – Joe's Garage studio connection (The Orb sold their studio Joe's Garage to Morcheeba's Godfrey brothers.)
