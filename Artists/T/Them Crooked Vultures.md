# Them Crooked Vultures

**Them Crooked Vultures are an American rock supergroup formed in 2009, bringing together Dave Grohl (Foo Fighters/Nirvana), Josh Homme (Queens of the Stone Age/Kyuss), and John Paul Jones (Led Zeppelin) for one self-titled album and tour.**
