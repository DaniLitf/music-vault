# The Creatures

**The Creatures were a British post-punk duo formed in 1981 as a side project of [[Members/S/Siouxsie Sioux]] and drummer [[Members/B/Budgie]] from [[Artists/S/Siouxsie and the Banshees]], known for their percussive, exotic soundscapes.**

## Members

### Core / Official Members
- [[Members/S/Siouxsie Sioux]] — vocals (1981–2004; also [[Artists/S/Siouxsie and the Banshees]])
- [[Members/B/Budgie]] — drums, percussion (1981–2004; also [[Artists/S/Siouxsie and the Banshees]])

## Connections

- [[Artists/S/Siouxsie and the Banshees]] — The Creatures were formed as a side project by Siouxsie Sioux and Budgie, who were both core members of Siouxsie and the Banshees throughout that band's existence.
