# The Legendary Pink Dots

**Experimental rock / psychedelic electronic band from London, England; later based in Amsterdam, Netherlands. Active 1980–present.**

The Legendary Pink Dots are an Anglo-Dutch experimental rock band formed in London in 1980 by [[Members/E/Edward Ka-Spel]] and collaborators including [[Members/P/Phil Knight]], later relocating to Amsterdam. Their vast catalogue blends neo-psychedelia, electronic music, tape experiments, post-punk, goth rock, ambient textures, and surreal songwriting, and the group has sustained a dedicated international cult following for more than four decades.
