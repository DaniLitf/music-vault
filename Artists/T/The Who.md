# The Who

**The Who are an English rock band formed in London in 1964, one of the greatest and most influential rock bands in history, defined by Pete Townshend's windmilling guitar attack, Roger Daltrey's powerful voice, John Entwistle's melodic bass, and Keith Moon's chaotic, bombastic drumming.**
