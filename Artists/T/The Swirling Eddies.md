# The Swirling Eddies

**Christian rock band from USA. Active 1980s–present.**

The Swirling Eddies are a Christian rock band formed in the late 1980s, featuring members from Daniel Amos, Adam Again, and Lost Dogs. The group is known for their eclectic, playful approach to rock music, often incorporating humor and experimental textures alongside their more straightforward rock songs.
