# The Shins

**Indie rock band from Albuquerque, USA. Active 1996–present.**

The Shins are an indie rock band formed in 1996 in Albuquerque, New Mexico, by James Mercer. The band relocated to Portland, Oregon and gained widespread attention after their music was featured in the film *Garden State* (2004). Their sound blends jangly guitars with pop melodies, making them one of the most influential indie rock acts of the 2000s.
