# Teddybears

**Alternative rock / electronica from Stockholm, Sweden. Active 1991–present.**

Teddybears (formerly Teddybears STHLM) is a Swedish music group known for their eclectic blend of genres — starting as a hardcore punk band before incorporating hip hop, pop, rock, dancehall, and electronica. They are known for wearing large bear heads in performances.
