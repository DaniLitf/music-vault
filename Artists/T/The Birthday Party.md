# The Birthday Party

**The Birthday Party were an Australian post-punk band formed in Melbourne in 1977, fronted by [[Members/N/Nick Cave]], whose violently intense, dark sound proved hugely influential on gothic rock and post-punk — before disbanding in 1983 and giving way to Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds.**

## Members

### Core / Official Members

- [[Members/M/Mick Harvey]] — guitar, drums, backing vocals (1977–1983)
- [[Members/R/Rowland S. Howard]] — lead guitar (1977–1983; died 2009)
- [[Members/T/Tracy Pew]] — bass (1977–1983; died 1986)
- [[Members/P/Phil Calvert]] — drums (1977–1983)

## Connections

- [[Artists/N/Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds]] — Nick Cave dissolved The Birthday Party in 1983 and immediately formed Nick Cave and the Bad Seeds, carrying over key collaborators including Mick Harvey.
- [[Artists/E/Einstürzende Neubauten]] — Blixa Bargeld of Einstürzende Neubauten briefly joined The Birthday Party as guitarist in 1983, linking the two bands.
