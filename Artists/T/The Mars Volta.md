# The Mars Volta

**The Mars Volta were an American progressive rock band formed in El Paso, Texas in 2001 by Cedric Bixler-Zavala and Omar Rodríguez-López following the dissolution of At the Drive-In, producing dense, sprawling concept albums that pushed the boundaries of progressive and experimental rock.**
