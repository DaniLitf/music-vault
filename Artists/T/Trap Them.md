# Trap Them

**Grindcore/hardcore punk band from Salem, New Hampshire. Active 2001–2017.**

Trap Them were an American grindcore and hardcore punk band formed in Salem, New Hampshire in 2001 by Ryan McKenney and Brian Izzi, both former members of Backstabbers Incorporated. Named after the 1977 exploitation film *Trap Them and Kill Them*, the band became known for pairing d-beat and crust punk with the buzzsaw Boss HM-2 guitar tone popularized by Swedish death metal bands, releasing five studio albums before disbanding in 2017.
