# The Family

**R&B / pop band from Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA. Active 1985.**

The Family was a short-lived Minneapolis band formed in 1985 from the remaining members of The Time after its initial breakup — Jerome Benton, Jellybean Johnson, and Paul Peterson — along with new vocalists. The band was produced by Prince and released one self-titled album in 1985 on Paisley Park Records, featuring the song "Nothing Compares 2 U", later made famous by Sinéad O'Connor.
