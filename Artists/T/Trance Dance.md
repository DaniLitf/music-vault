# Trance Dance

**Pop/rock band from Sweden. Active 1985–1990.**

Trance Dance were a Swedish pop group formed in 1985 as a seven-piece, notable for a guitar-driven, rock-influenced sound unusual for a late-1980s pop act. Their singles "Don't Say Go" and "You're Gonna Get It" both reached number 2 on the Swedish singles chart, and their debut album *A Ho Ho* (1986) was also released in the United States; the band contracted to a trio for their final album, *Twang* (1990), before disbanding.
