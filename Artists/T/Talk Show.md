# Talk Show

**Talk Show were an American rock band formed in 1995 by three members of Stone Temple Pilots — Dean DeLeo, Robert DeLeo, and Eric Kretz — following a break from the main band, with vocalist Dave Coutts replacing Scott Weiland.**
