# Trëllebörg

**Thrash metal from Cleveland, Ohio. Active 1986–1989.**

Trëllebörg was a thrash metal band formed in 1986 on the east side of Cleveland, Ohio by teenage musicians who grew up alongside members of Andromeda, Raging Grace, and Famine. They released a single self-titled demo cassette in 1989, recorded at Mars Recording Compound. Several members went on to play in Hatrix and Mushroomhead.
