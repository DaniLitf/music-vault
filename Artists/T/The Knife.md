# The Knife

**Electronic / synth-pop duo from Gothenburg, Sweden. Active 1999–2014.**

The Knife were a Swedish electronic duo formed by siblings [[Members/K/Karin Dreijer]] and [[Members/O/Olof Dreijer]], who also ran Rabid Records. Their masked public image, anti-industry stance, and albums such as *Deep Cuts*, *Silent Shout*, and *Shaking the Habitual* made them one of the most distinctive electronic acts of the 2000s and early 2010s.
