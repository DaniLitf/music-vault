# The Knife

**The Knife were a Swedish electronic music duo formed in Gothenburg in 1999 by siblings Karin Dreijer and Olof Dreijer, known for their eerie, unconventional synth-pop and their landmark album *Silent Shout*.**

| Year | Title | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| 2001 | *The Knife* | debut |
| 2003 | *Deep Cuts* | breakthrough; "Heartbeats" |
| 2006 | *Silent Shout* | critical peak |
| 2013 | *Shaking the Habitual* | final album; 95-minute experimental work |
