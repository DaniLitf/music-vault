# Thåström

**Swedish singer-songwriter's solo career, from Stockholm, Sweden. Active 1989–present.**

[[Members/J/Joakim Thåström]] (known simply as Thåström) built his reputation as singer and guitarist of the pioneering Swedish punk band [[Artists/E/Ebba Grön]] (1977–1983), and then fronted its successor [[Artists/I/Imperiet]] through the mid-1980s, one of Sweden's biggest rock acts of that decade. He launched a solo career in 1989, moving through synth-heavy industrial rock (as heard on the Peace, Love & Pitbulls side project of the 1990s) before returning to guitar-driven rock with the acclaimed *Det är ni som e dom konstiga, det är jag som e normal* (1999). He has released ten solo studio albums to date, backed for much of his career by [[Members/C/Christian Gabel]] on drums, and briefly fronted the one-off band Sällskapet (2007).
