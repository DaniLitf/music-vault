# The Heads

**New wave / art rock from New York City, USA. Active 1994–1996.**

The Heads were an American band formed by [[Artists/T/Talking Heads]] instrumentalists [[Members/J/Jerry Harrison]], [[Members/T/Tina Weymouth]], and [[Members/C/Chris Frantz]] after years of failing to convince vocalist David Byrne to reunite the band. Backed by a rotating cast of guest vocalists, they recorded one studio album, *No Talking, Just Head* (1996); a planned full-time follow-up career and live release was scrapped after Byrne sued the trio, arguing the name and presentation infringed on the Talking Heads trademark, and the suit was settled out of court.
