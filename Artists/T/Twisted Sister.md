# Twisted Sister

**Twisted Sister were an American heavy metal band formed in New Jersey in 1972, best known for their anthemic glam metal hits "We're Not Gonna Take It" and "I Wanna Rock" and their flamboyant image that became a defining aesthetic of 1980s metal.**
