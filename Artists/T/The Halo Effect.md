# The Halo Effect

**The Halo Effect are a Swedish melodic death metal band formed in 2021, consisting entirely of former members of In Flames and featuring Mikael Stanne of Dark Tranquillity on vocals — a supergroup from the core of the Gothenburg metal scene.**
