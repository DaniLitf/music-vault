# The Halo Effect

**Melodic death metal supergroup from Gothenburg, Sweden. Active 2021–present.**

The Halo Effect formed in 2021, consisting entirely of former members of In Flames and featuring Mikael Stanne of Dark Tranquillity on vocals — a supergroup drawn from the core of the Gothenburg metal scene. Their debut *Days of the Lost* (2022) was widely received as a return to the classic Gothenburg melodic death metal sound.
