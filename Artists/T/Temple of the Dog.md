# Temple of the Dog

**Temple of the Dog were an American rock supergroup formed in Seattle in 1990 as a tribute to Andrew Wood, the frontman of Mother Love Bone who died of a heroin overdose that year, bringing together members of Soundgarden and the nascent Pearl Jam lineup.**
