# The Hives

**Garage rock / punk rock from Fagersta, Sweden. Active 1993–present.**

The Hives are a Swedish garage rock band formed in 1993 by brothers [[Members/H/Howlin' Pelle Almqvist]] (vocals) and [[Members/N/Nicholaus Arson]] (guitar), joined by childhood friends [[Members/V/Vigilante Carlstroem]] (guitar), [[Members/D/Dr. Matt Destruction]] (bass), and [[Members/C/Chris Dangerous]] (drums). Known for their matching black-and-white suits and energetic live shows, they rose to worldwide prominence in the early 2000s garage rock revival with *Veni Vidi Vicious* (2000) and its single "Hate to Say I Told You So." All songwriting is officially credited to "Randy Fitzsimmons," a fictional sixth member the band claims manages and writes for them — a pseudonym widely reported to belong to Nicholaus Arson.
