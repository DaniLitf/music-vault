# The Dillinger Escape Plan

**Mathcore / metalcore from Morris Plains, New Jersey. Active 1997–2017, 2023–present.**

The Dillinger Escape Plan formed in 1997 out of the earlier hardcore band Arcane, founded by guitarist [[Members/B/Ben Weinman]], bassist [[Members/A/Adam Doll]], vocalist [[Members/D/Dimitri Minakakis]], and drummer [[Members/C/Chris Pennie]]. Known for dissonance, odd time signatures, and unconventional structures, the band's debut *Calculating Infinity* (1999) became a landmark of the mathcore genre. [[Members/G/Greg Puciato]] took over as vocalist in 2001, fronting the band through its most successful era, while the lineup otherwise shifted continually — Weinman was the only member to remain for the band's entire run. They announced their breakup ahead of final album *Dissociation* (2016), playing farewell shows at Terminal 5 in December 2017, and reunited briefly in 2024 for anniversary shows with Minakakis. Weinman later joined [[Artists/S/Suicidal Tendencies]] as rhythm guitarist in 2018.
