# Tom Tom Club

**New wave / post-punk / funk from New York, USA. Active 1981–present.**

Tom Tom Club is an American new wave band formed by [[Artists/T/Talking Heads]] members [[Members/C/Chris Frantz]] and [[Members/T/Tina Weymouth]]. Known for their hit "Genius of Love" (1981), which became one of the most sampled songs in hip-hop history.
