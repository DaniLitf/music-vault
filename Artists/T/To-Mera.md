# To-Mera

**Progressive metal/jazz fusion band from London, England. Active 2005–2012.**

To-Mera are a British progressive metal and jazz fusion band formed in London in 2005. They are known for their complex, genre-blending approach that combines heavy progressive metal with jazz improvisation, world music influences, and avant-garde experimentation. The band released two critically acclaimed studio albums before disbanding in 2012.
