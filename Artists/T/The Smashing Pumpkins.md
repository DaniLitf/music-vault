# The Smashing Pumpkins

**The Smashing Pumpkins are an American alternative rock band formed in Chicago in 1988 by Billy Corgan, whose ambitious studio craft and willingness to blend heavy guitars with orchestral arrangements made them one of the defining bands of 1990s rock.**
