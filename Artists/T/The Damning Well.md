# The Damning Well

**Alternative/industrial rock supergroup. Active 2002–2003.**

The Damning Well was an American alternative/industrial rock supergroup formed in 2002 featuring [[Members/R/Richard Patrick]] ([[Artists/F/Filter]]), [[Members/J/Josh Freese]] ([[Artists/A/A Perfect Circle]], [[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]]), [[Members/W/Wes Borland]] ([[Artists/L/Limp Bizkit]]), and [[Members/D/Danny Lohner]] ([[Artists/N/Nine Inch Nails]]). The project released one self-titled EP in 2003 before disbanding.
