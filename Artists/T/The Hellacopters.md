# The Hellacopters

**Garage rock / hard rock from Stockholm, Sweden. Active 1994–2008, 2016–present.**

The Hellacopters are a Swedish garage rock band formed in 1994 by [[Members/N/Nicke Andersson]] (vocals, guitar), [[Members/D/Dregen]] (guitar), [[Members/K/Kenny Håkansson]] (bass), and [[Members/R/Robert Eriksson]] (drums). Together with [[Artists/T/The Hives]], they are considered one of the most influential bands of the Swedish garage rock revival. The band disbanded amicably in 2008 and reunited in 2016, releasing new material from 2022 onward.
