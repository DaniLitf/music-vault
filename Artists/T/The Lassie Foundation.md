# The Lassie Foundation

**Noise pop / shoegaze from Los Angeles, California. Active 1996–2006, reunions 2008–present.**

The Lassie Foundation was an American indie band from Southern California, known for their "pink-noise pop" sound blending shoegaze guitar textures with West Coast pop harmonies. Formed in 1996 by former The Prayer Chain members Wayne Everett and Eric Campuzano, the band released three studio albums and several EPs before disbanding in 2006, though they have reunited multiple times since. Their song "El Rey" was featured on *Buffy the Vampire Slayer* and other television shows.
