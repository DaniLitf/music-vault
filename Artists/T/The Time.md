# The Time

**Funk / soul / R&B band from Minneapolis, Minnesota, USA. Active 1981–1985, 1990–1991, 1995–present.**

The Time is an American funk rock band formed in Minneapolis in 1981, assembled by [[Artists/P/Prince]] under a clause in his contract with Warner Bros. Led by singer [[Members/M/Morris Day]], the band became a defining act of the Minneapolis sound, scoring hits such as "Jungle Love", "Cool", "777-9311", "The Bird", and "Jerk Out". The original lineup featured [[Members/J/Jimmy Jam]] and [[Members/T/Terry Lewis]], who went on to become one of the most successful production duos in music history.
