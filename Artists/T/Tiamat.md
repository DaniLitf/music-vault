# Tiamat

**Gothic metal / rock from Stockholm, Sweden. Active 1987–present.**

Tiamat are a Swedish band formed near Stockholm in 1987 (initially as Treblinka), led throughout their career by vocalist/guitarist Johan Edlund, the band's sole constant member. They began as a raw death/doom metal act before evolving through the 1990s into a pioneering force in gothic metal, and later into a more atmospheric, psychedelic rock-influenced sound.
