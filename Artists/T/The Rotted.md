# The Rotted

**Deathgrind band from London, England. Active 1997–2014, 2026–present (as Gorerotted 1997–2008, 2026–present; as The Rotted 2008–2014).**

The Rotted (originally Gorerotted) are an English deathgrind band formed in London in 1997. Known for horror-themed lyrics, humorous song titles, and a distinctive dual-vocal style, they released three albums as Gorerotted on Metal Blade Records before rebranding as The Rotted in 2008 with a shift toward a death metal/punk sound. The band disbanded in 2014 but announced a reunion under their original name Gorerotted in 2026 for Damnation Festival.
