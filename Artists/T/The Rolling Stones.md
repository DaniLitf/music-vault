# The Rolling Stones

**The Rolling Stones are an English rock band formed in London in 1962, one of the most enduring and influential rock acts in history, built on a blues foundation and defined by the chemistry of Mick Jagger and Keith Richards as songwriting partners and the rhythm guitar mastery of Richards.**
