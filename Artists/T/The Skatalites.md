# The Skatalites

**Jamaican ska band from Kingston, Jamaica. Active 1963–1965, 1983–present.**

The Skatalites are a Jamaican ska band formed in Kingston in 1963 that pioneered the ska genre, combining Caribbean mento and calypso with American jazz and R&B. The original group featured many of Jamaica's finest session musicians, including trombonist [[Members/D/Don Drummond]] and saxophonist [[Members/T/Tommy McCook]]. Though the original lineup only lasted from 1963 to 1965, the band was revived in 1983 and continues to tour and record.
