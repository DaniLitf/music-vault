# The Doors

**The Doors were an American rock band formed in Los Angeles in 1965, one of the most iconic bands of the 1960s and 1970s, defined by Jim Morrison's poetic, often transgressive lyrics and charismatic stage presence, and the unique organ-driven sound created without a bassist.**
