# Tomora

**Electronic supergroup duo from Europe. Active 2025–present.**

Tomora is an electronic duo formed by [[Members/T/Tom Rowlands]] of [[Artists/T/The Chemical Brothers]] and [[Members/A/Aurora Aksnes]] of [[Artists/A/AURORA]]. The project grew from studio sessions between the two artists and released its debut album *Come Closer* in 2026.
