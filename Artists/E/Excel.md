# Excel

**Crossover thrash from Venice, California. Active 1983–present.**

Excel formed in Venice, California in 1983 as Chaotic Noise, founded by vocalist [[Members/D/Dan Clements]] and guitarist [[Members/A/Adam Siegel]]; the band was renamed Excel in 1985. One of the West Coast's pioneering crossover thrash acts, Excel blended hardcore punk speed with thrash metal riffing alongside scene contemporaries like [[Artists/S/Suicidal Tendencies]]. The band has gone through numerous lineup changes over four decades, with Clements remaining the sole constant member. After leaving Excel, Siegel and drummer [[Members/G/Greg Saenz]] went on to form the side project [[Artists/M/My Head]].
