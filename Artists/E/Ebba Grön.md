# Ebba Grön

**Punk rock from Stockholm, Sweden. Active 1977–1983, 1995, 2003 (temporary reunions).**

Ebba Grön formed in Rågsved, a working-class Stockholm suburb, in December 1977 when [[Members/J/Joakim Thåström]] (vocals, guitar), [[Members/G/Gunnar Ljungstedt]] (drums), and [[Members/L/Lennart Eriksson]] (bass) started a band called the Haters, renamed Ebba Grön days later. Pioneers of Swedish punk, they became notorious for anarchist, anti-establishment lyrics — the song "Beväpna er" ("Arm Yourselves") was too controversial to print on the sleeve — and for chaotic gigs, including one where Eriksson fought off neo-Nazi attackers with his bass. Their cover of Blå Tåget's "Den ena handen vet vad den andra gör," renamed "Staten och kapitalet," became their best-known song. Keyboardist [[Members/A/Anders Sjöholm]] joined in 1981, steering the band's sound away from raw punk on its final album, *Ebba Grön* (1982). Eriksson's imprisonment for refusing military service effectively ended the band in early 1983; the remaining members continued as Rymdimperiet, soon renamed [[Artists/I/Imperiet]].
