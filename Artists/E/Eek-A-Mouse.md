# Eek-A-Mouse

**Eek-A-Mouse is a Jamaican reggae and dancehall artist, born Ripton Joseph Hylton in 1957 in Kingston, Jamaica, known for his unique "singjay" style and nonsense syllables, particularly the phrase "wa-do-dem."**

Eek-A-Mouse is a solo artist. See [[Members/E/Eek-A-Mouse]] for biography.
