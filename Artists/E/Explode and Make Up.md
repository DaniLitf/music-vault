# Explode and Make Up

**Melodic hardcore / punk rock band from Chicago, Illinois, USA. Active c. 2006–2010.**

Explode and Make Up were an American melodic hardcore band from Chicago that united veterans of the city's punk scene: vocalist [[Members/D/Denis Buckley]] of [[Artists/0-9/88 Fingers Louie]], guitarist [[Members/J/Jeff Dean]] of [[Artists/T/The Bomb]], and bassist [[Members/P/Pete Mittler]] of The Bomb and [[Artists/T/The Methadones]]. Playing old-school melodic hardcore in the vein of 88 Fingers Louie, The Bomb, and Dag Nasty, the band recorded a self-titled EP across three studios before it was released on Underground Communiqué Records in 2009.
