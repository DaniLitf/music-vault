# Earth, Wind & Fire

**Earth, Wind & Fire are an American funk and soul band founded in Chicago in 1969 by Maurice White, widely regarded as one of the greatest music groups of all time and known for hits like "September," "Shining Star," and "Boogie Wonderland."**
