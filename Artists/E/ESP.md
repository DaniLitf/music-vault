# ESP

**American rock supergroup. Active 1990s–present.**

ESP (Eric Singer Project) is an American rock covers band/supergroup founded in the 1990s by [[Members/E/Eric Singer]], drummer for KISS, Black Sabbath and Alice Cooper. The band also features [[Members/B/Bruce Kulick]] (KISS, Grand Funk Railroad) on guitar, [[Members/J/John Corabi]] (Mötley Crüe, Ratt) on guitar, bass and vocals, and [[Members/C/Chuck Garric]] (Alice Cooper) on bass. Their sole studio album, *Lost and Spaced* (1998), is a collection of classic rock covers from artists such as Sweet, Deep Purple, KISS and The Who.
