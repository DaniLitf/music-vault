# Extreme Noise Terror

**Grindcore / crust punk band from Ipswich, England. Active 1985–present.**

Extreme Noise Terror (often abbreviated to ENT) are a British extreme metal band formed in Ipswich in 1985, considered one of the earliest and most influential crust punk and grindcore bands. They are noted for their pioneering use of dual vocalists, highly political lyrics, ultra-fast tempos, and for recording multiple Peel Sessions for BBC Radio 1. The band famously collaborated with The KLF on a cover of "3 a.m. Eternal" and performed at the 1992 BRIT Awards.
