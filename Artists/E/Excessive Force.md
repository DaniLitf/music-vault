# Excessive Force

**Excessive Force were an industrial and EBM project on the Wax Trax! Records label, active in the early 1990s, formed as a collaboration between Sascha Konietzko (KMFDM) and Buzz McCoy (My Life with the Thrill Kill Kult).**
