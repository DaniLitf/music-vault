# Extol

**Christian extreme metal band from Bekkestua, Norway. Active 1993–2007, 2012–2017, 2023–present.**

Extol are a Norwegian Christian extreme metal band formed in Bekkestua in 1993 by 16-year-old cousins, drummer David Husvik and guitarist Christer Espevoll, blending death metal, progressive metal, black metal, and thrash metal with explicitly Christian lyrical themes. The band has gone through multiple hiatuses and reformations, most recently reuniting in 2023.
