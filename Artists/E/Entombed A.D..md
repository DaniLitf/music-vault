# Entombed A.D.

**Swedish death metal band from Stockholm. Active 2014–present.**

Entombed A.D. is a Swedish death metal band formed in Stockholm in 2014 by L.G. Petrov and other former members of Entombed, following a trademark dispute over the "Entombed" name. The band continues the death metal and death 'n' roll style of Entombed's later work.
