# Eddie Meduza

**Swedish rockabilly / punk rock singer-songwriter from Gothenburg, Sweden. Active 1973–2001.**

Eddie Meduza (born Errol Leonard Norstedt) was a Swedish singer, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist known for his provocative rockabilly and punk-influenced songs about alcohol, women, and cars, often with obscene lyrics. Despite poor critical reception, he developed a massive cult following in Sweden and Scandinavia, releasing dozens of albums and cassettes over a 28-year career. He also recorded under the alias E. Hitler for more explicit material. He is a solo artist. See [[Members/E/Eddie Meduza]] for biography.
