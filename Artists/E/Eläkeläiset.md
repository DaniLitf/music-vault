# Eläkeläiset

**Eläkeläiset ("The Retirees") are a Finnish humppa band formed in 1993 in Tampere, known for their comedic covers of international rock and pop songs rearranged in the style of humppa — a fast-paced Finnish dance music derived from foxtrot.**

Eläkeläiset is led by Erkki Hämäläinen and has maintained the same comedic concept across dozens of albums.
