# Extreme

**Hard rock / funk metal from Boston, Massachusetts. Active 1985–1996, 2004–2006 (reunions), 2007–present.**

Extreme formed in Boston in 1985 when guitarist [[Members/N/Nuno Bettencourt]] joined vocalist [[Members/G/Gary Cherone]] and drummer [[Members/P/Paul Geary]] (former bandmates in Adrenalin and The Dream), with bassist [[Members/P/Pat Badger]] completing the lineup in 1986. Their funk-metal-inflected second album *Extreme II: Pornograffitti* (1990) produced the acoustic ballad "More Than Words," a US number-one hit, and the band's memorable appearance at the 1992 Freddie Mercury Tribute Concert widened their audience considerably. The band split amicably in 1996 as Bettencourt pursued a solo career and Cherone joined [[Artists/V/Van Halen]]; Extreme reunited for touring in 2004–2006 and returned to recording with *Saudades de Rock* (2008), continuing to release new music into the 2020s.
