# Enya

**Enya is an Irish singer, songwriter, and musician, born Eithne Pádraigín Ní Bhraonáin on May 17, 1961, in Gweedore, County Donegal — one of the best-selling music artists of all time, known for her ethereal, multi-layered vocal style and Celtic new age sound.**

Enya is a solo artist. See [[Members/E/Enya]] for biography.
