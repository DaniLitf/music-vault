# Exploding Flowers

**Jangle pop / indie rock band from Los Angeles, California. Active 2010–present.**

Exploding Flowers is a Los Angeles-based quartet known for their guitar-driven pop sound with elements of jangle pop, power pop, and Paisley Underground. Formed around 2010 and led by frontman [[Members/S/Sharif Dumani]], the band has released three studio albums and features a rotating cast of guest musicians. Their music has been described as reverb-drenched power pop with chiming 12-string guitars, continuing a lineage of melody-driven indie pop.
