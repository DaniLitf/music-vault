# Error

**Electronic/industrial punk project from Los Angeles, USA. Active 2003.**

Error is an American electronic and punk project formed in 2003 by [[Members/A/Atticus Ross]] and his brother Leopold Ross, alongside Bad Religion guitarist Brett Gurewitz and vocalist Greg Puciato of The Dillinger Escape Plan. The band released one self-titled album before members returned to their respective primary projects.
