# YE

**YE (born Kanye West, legally changed name in 2021) is an American rapper, record producer, and fashion designer from Chicago, one of the most critically acclaimed and commercially successful hip hop artists of all time, whose ambitious production and genre-blending across 20+ years has been profoundly influential.**
