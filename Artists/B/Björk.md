# Björk

**Björk is an Icelandic musician, singer, and actress from Reykjavík, one of the most artistically adventurous and influential artists of the past 30 years, whose solo work spans art pop, electronic, orchestral, ambient, and experimentalforms in a constantly evolving body of work.**
