# Blink-182

**Pop-punk / punk rock band from Poway, California, USA. Active 1992–2005, 2009–present.**

Blink-182 is an American pop-punk band formed in Poway, a suburb of San Diego, in 1992 by [[Members/M/Mark Hoppus]], [[Members/T/Tom DeLonge]], and [[Members/S/Scott Raynor]]. Originally called Blink, the band added "-182" after a legal threat from an Irish band of the same name. With *Enema of the State* (1999) they became one of the biggest rock acts in the world, spearheading pop-punk's second mainstream wave; the band has sold over 50 million albums worldwide. Hoppus is the only member to have remained throughout the band's history — Raynor was replaced by [[Members/T/Travis Barker]] in 1998, and DeLonge left twice (2005, 2015) before returning in 2022, with [[Members/M/Matt Skiba]] handling guitar and co-lead vocals in between.
