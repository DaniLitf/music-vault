# Bon Voyage

**Synthpop / shoegaze duo from Aliso Viejo, California. Active 1995–present.**

Bon Voyage is an American musical duo consisting of Jason Martin (of Starflyer 59) and his wife Julie Martin. The project began in 1995 when Jason Martin had written songs beyond what he could release as Starflyer 59, and the pair released two 7-inch singles on Velvet Blue Music before signing to BEC Recordings for their 1998 self-titled debut album. Their sound blends dreamy synthpop with shoegaze textures, drawing comparisons to My Bloody Valentine and Blondie. After a hiatus following *Lies* (2008), the duo returned in 2025 with new singles.
