# Black Flag

**Hardcore punk pioneers from Hermosa Beach, California, USA. Active 1976–1986, with reunions in 2003 and 2013–2025.**

Black Flag were formed in 1976 by guitarist [[Members/G/Greg Ginn]] and vocalist [[Members/K/Keith Morris]], initially as Panic before adopting the Black Flag name in 1978. Widely considered one of the first hardcore punk bands and a pioneer of post-hardcore, the group built the American DIY touring network almost single-handedly through relentless, low-budget cross-country tours, while Ginn's label SST Records became one of the most important American independent labels of the 1980s. Aside from Ginn, the band's sole constant member, Black Flag burned through 16 lineups and 17 musicians in its first decade, with their sound evolving from the raw, minute-long blasts of *Nervous Breakdown* (1979) to the slow, sludgy, metal-influenced material of *My War* (1984) that directly influenced sludge and doom metal.
