# Black Uniforms

**Swedish D-beat / hardcore punk from Malmö, Sweden. Active 1981–1990 (hiatus 1983–1986).**

Black Uniforms was a Swedish hardcore punk band from Malmö known for blending D-beat with thrash/speed metal influences. Formed in 1981, they split up briefly in 1983 when members joined Moderat Likvidation and The Bristles, reforming in late 1986. Though somewhat overlooked compared to other Swedish punk acts, they released several cult records including *Faces of Death* (1989) and the compilation *Straight Edge, My Ass* (1990). After disbanding in 1990, members went on to play in Anti Cimex, Driller Killer, and other key Swedish punk bands.
