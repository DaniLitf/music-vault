# Bad Religion

**Bad Religion are an American punk rock band formed in Los Angeles in 1980, one of the most influential and longest-running punk acts, known for their melodic approach to hardcore punk, three-part harmonies, and intellectually sophisticated lyrics — often penned by vocalist Greg Graffin, who holds a PhD in evolutionary biology.**
