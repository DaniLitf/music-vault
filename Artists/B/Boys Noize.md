# Boys Noize

**Electronic producer/DJ solo project from Berlin, Germany (originally Hamburg). Active 2003–present.**

Boys Noize is the solo electronic project of German-Iraqi producer Alexander Ridha, known for gritty, distorted electro-house productions and for founding the independent label Boysnoize Records in 2005. Beyond his solo albums, he has produced and remixed for artists including Lady Gaga (co-writing the Grammy-winning "Rain on Me"), Depeche Mode, Snoop Dogg, and Nine Inch Nails, and records as one half of the duo [[Artists/D/Dog Blood]] with Skrillex.
