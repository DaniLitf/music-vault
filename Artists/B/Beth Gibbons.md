# Beth Gibbons

**Alternative folk / art-pop / trip-hop-adjacent singer-songwriter from Exeter, England. Active 2002–present (solo).**

Beth Gibbons is the solo and collaborative recording identity of the English singer best known as the voice of [[Artists/P/Portishead]]. Her work outside Portishead spans hushed folk-noir balladry, a classical vocal recording, and, most recently, a fully solo studio album exploring ageing, motherhood, and loss.
