# Black Light Burns

**Industrial rock / alternative rock from Los Angeles, California, USA. Active 2005–2016.**

Black Light Burns was an American industrial rock band founded and fronted by [[Members/W/Wes Borland]] after his 2005 departure from [[Artists/L/Limp Bizkit]]. The project grew out of Borland's collaboration with [[Members/D/Danny Lohner]], [[Members/J/Josh Freese]] and [[Members/J/Josh Eustis]] following the collapse of [[Artists/T/The Damning Well]], and evolved from a mellow solo record into a full band after Trent Reznor encouraged Borland toward a heavier sound. Borland disbanded the project in 2016.
