# Borknagar

**Borknagar are a Norwegian black and progressive metal band from Bergen, Norway, active 1995–present, founded by guitarist Øystein Brun, known for blending atmospheric black metal with progressive rock ambition and multiple vocalist changes.**

The band's shifting lineup has included prominent figures from the Scandinavian black metal scene, and their sound evolved significantly from raw second-wave black metal towards lush, keyboard-driven progressive metal.
