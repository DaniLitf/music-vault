# Bird of Ill Omen

**Dark, sludgy metalcore from Miami, Florida, USA. Active 1996–1998 (one-off reunion 2012).**

Bird of Ill Omen was a Florida metalcore band formed in Miami in the summer of 1996 out of the short-lived Crestfallen, briefly using the name Fork in the Road before vocalist Damien Moyal rechristened it. Heavily influenced by fellow Floridians Bloodlet, the band became the first signing to John Wylie's Eulogy Recordings and a fixture of the 1990s South Florida hardcore scene alongside Morning Again, Shai Hulud and Culture. Their December 1997 tour ended when drummer José Flores was denied re-entry into the United States, and after recording a final EP the band dissolved in 1998–1999, reuniting once for a show at Churchill's Pub in Miami in September 2012.

## Connections

- [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] – Damien Moyal, Peter Bartsocas, Thomas Rankine (Moyal founded As Friends Rust, naming it after a lyric from Bird of Ill Omen's "Now Ruin Is"; Bartsocas played guitar/bass there 1998–2015; Rankine played bass 2001–2002)
- [[Artists/M/Morning Again]] – Damien Moyal, Joseph Simmons, Peter Bartsocas (Moyal was Morning Again's original vocalist; Simmons left the Bird to join Morning Again in January 1997; Bartsocas quit Morning Again to join the Bird in October 1996; the bands toured together in 1997)
- [[Artists/C/Culture (American band)]] – Damien Moyal, Joseph Simmons (Moyal fronted Culture concurrently, leaving the Bird in March 1997 to focus on it; Simmons also played guitar in Culture)
- [[Artists/S/Shai Hulud]] – Damien Moyal (Moyal was an early Shai Hulud vocalist; the two bands shared many South Florida bills)
- [[Artists/P/Poison the Well]] – José Martinez (Martinez later played in To the Bitter End and Poison the Well)
- [[Artists/F/Floor]] – George Rios (Rios drummed in doom-sludge band Floor before co-founding the Bird)
