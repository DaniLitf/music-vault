# Box Car Racer

**Post-hardcore / pop-punk band from San Diego, California, USA. Active 2001–2003.**

Box Car Racer was a side project of [[Artists/B/Blink-182]] members [[Members/T/Tom DeLonge]] and [[Members/T/Travis Barker]], formed in 2001 so DeLonge could explore darker, Fugazi- and Refused-influenced material he felt unsuited to Blink-182. Guitarist [[Members/D/David Kennedy]] of Hazen Street completed the studio lineup, with [[Members/A/Anthony Celestino]] joining on bass for live shows. Their sole album reached number 12 on the Billboard 200; the project's exclusion of Mark Hoppus caused lasting tension within Blink-182, and DeLonge later described [[Artists/A/Angels & Airwaves]] as its continuation.
