# Bob Log III

**Bob Log III is an American one-man blues and garage rock act from Phoenix, Arizona, active 1996–present, who performs solo on guitar and drums simultaneously while wearing a flight suit and a helmet with a telephone mouthpiece for a microphone.**

His hyper-physical performances and raw, unhinged delta blues style have made him a cult favourite on the international garage rock and blues festival circuit.
