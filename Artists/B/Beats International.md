# Beats International

**Beats International were a British dance act from Brighton, England, active 1989–1992, led by Norman Cook, best known for the UK #1 single "Dub Be Good to Me" (1990).**

The project was one of several musical identities for Norman Cook, who blended house, hip-hop, and reggae influences before achieving global fame as Fatboy Slim. Lindy Layton and MC Wildski were key collaborators on the debut album.
