# Biting Tongues

**Biting Tongues were a British post-punk and industrial group from Manchester, England, active 1979–1993, known for jagged, percussive rhythms and tape-loop experiments on the Factory Records-adjacent Manchester scene.**

The band drew on funk, dub, and industrial textures and were a significant though underrecognised part of the Manchester avant-garde scene. Keyboardist Graham Massey later co-founded 808 State.
