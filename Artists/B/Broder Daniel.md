# Broder Daniel

**Broder Daniel were a Swedish indie rock and post-punk band from Gothenburg, Sweden, active 1988–2008, regarded as one of the most beloved and influential Swedish indie bands of their generation.**

Known for emotionally charged, English-language lyrics and an energetic live presence, Broder Daniel built an intensely loyal cult following before dissolving after the death of guitarist Henrik Berggren's struggles with addiction. The band reunited briefly in 2011.
