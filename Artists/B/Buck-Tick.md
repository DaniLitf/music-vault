# Buck-Tick

**Buck-Tick are a Japanese gothic rock and post-punk band from Fujioka, Gunma, Japan, active 1983–2023, one of the longest-running and most influential acts in Japanese rock, who helped shape the visual kei aesthetic and blended Western gothic and industrial sounds with Japanese sensibility.**

The band's history ended with the sudden death of vocalist Atsushi Sakurai on stage in October 2023. They released 23 studio albums across four decades.
