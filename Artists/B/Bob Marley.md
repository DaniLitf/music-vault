# Bob Marley

**Bob Marley (and the Wailers) were a Jamaican reggae band from Kingston, Jamaica, active 1963–1981, led by Robert Nesta Marley (1945–1981), who became the global ambassador of reggae music and Rastafarianism.**

Marley's blend of infectious rhythm and spiritual/political messaging made him one of the best-selling music artists of all time, with *Legend* (1984) the best-selling reggae album ever.
