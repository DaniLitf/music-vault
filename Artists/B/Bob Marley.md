# Bob Marley

**Jamaican reggae band from Kingston, Jamaica. Active 1963–1981.**

Bob Marley and the Wailers were a Jamaican ska, rocksteady, and reggae band formed in 1963, originally as the Teenagers. Led by [[Members/B/Bob Marley|Bob Marley]], the band became the global face of reggae music, blending infectious rhythms with spiritual and political messaging. The band's founding members were [[Members/P/Peter Tosh|Peter Tosh]] and [[Members/B/Bunny Wailer|Bunny Wailer]] alongside Marley, and after Tosh and Wailer departed in 1974, Marley continued with a new line-up including the I Threes. Their compilation *Legend* (1984) remains the best-selling reggae album of all time.
