# Benediction

**Death metal from Birmingham, England. Active 1989–present.**

Benediction are a British death metal band formed in Birmingham in 1989 by guitarists Darren Brookes and Peter Rew, vocalist Mark "Barney" Greenway, bassist Paul Adams, and drummer Ian Treacy. They quickly signed to Nuclear Blast Records and released their debut album *Subconscious Terror* in 1990. Over three decades, Benediction have become a staple of the UK death metal scene, releasing nine studio albums and maintaining a consistent old-school death metal sound rooted in groove and aggression.
