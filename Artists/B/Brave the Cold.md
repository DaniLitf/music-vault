# Brave the Cold

**American extreme metal band founded in 2020 by Mitch Harris. Active present.**

Brave the Cold is an extreme metal project founded by Mitch Harris (guitarist of Napalm Death) in 2020. The band released their debut album *Scarcity* in October 2020 on the label Mission Two Entertainment.
