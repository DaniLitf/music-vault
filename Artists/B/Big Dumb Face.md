# Big Dumb Face

**Comedy metal / grindcore from Jacksonville, Florida, USA. Active 1998–2004, 2017–present.**

Big Dumb Face is an American comedic metal project formed in 1998 by brothers [[Members/W/Wes Borland]] and [[Members/S/Scott Borland]], serving as a side project from Wes's main band [[Artists/L/Limp Bizkit]]. The band is noted for its absurdist lyrics and wild genre shifts, mixing grindcore and death metal with funk, country, disco, reggae, psychedelia and pop; Wes Borland cited Ween and Mr. Bungle as influences. After 13 years of inactivity the project returned in 2017 with a second album released on Borland's own label Edison Sound.
