# Bob Dylan

**Bob Dylan is an American singer-songwriter from Duluth, Minnesota, active 1961–present, widely regarded as one of the most influential musicians of the 20th century, who transformed folk music into a vehicle for social commentary and literary expression.**

Dylan's adoption of electric rock in 1965 was one of the most controversial moments in popular music history. He was awarded the Nobel Prize in Literature in 2016.
