# Brian Eno

**Brian Eno is a British musician and record producer from Woodbridge, Suffolk, England, active 1971–present, a founding member of Roxy Music and the pioneer of ambient music, who has also produced landmark albums for Talking Heads, U2, and David Bowie.**

Eno coined the term "ambient music" with his 1978 release *Ambient 1: Music for Airports* and has shaped the sound of popular music through both his own recordings and his influential production work.
