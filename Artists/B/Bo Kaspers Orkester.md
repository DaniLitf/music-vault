# Bo Kaspers Orkester

**Bo Kaspers Orkester are a Swedish jazz-pop ensemble from Stockholm, Sweden, active 1992–present, led by singer-songwriter Bo Kaspers (Martin Kaspar Eriksson), known for sophisticated arrangements and Swedish-language lyrics.**

The band combines big-band jazz textures with accessible pop songwriting and has been a consistent presence on the Swedish music scene, earning multiple Grammis nominations.
