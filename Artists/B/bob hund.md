# bob hund

**bob hund are a Swedish indie rock and post-punk band from Malmö, Sweden, active 1989–present, known for oblique Swedish-language lyrics, abrasive guitar work, and a devoted cult following.**

The band are one of the most unconventional and enduring acts in Swedish independent music, refusing mainstream accessibility while accumulating a body of work that spans over three decades.
