# Brides of Destruction

**Hard rock / glam metal supergroup from Los Angeles, California. Active 2002–2006.**

Brides of Destruction was an American hard rock supergroup formed in 2002 by Mötley Crüe bassist Nikki Sixx and L.A. Guns guitarist Tracii Guns after Mötley Crüe went on hiatus. The band released two studio albums — *Here Come the Brides* (2004) and *Runaway Brides* (2005) — before disbanding in 2006.
