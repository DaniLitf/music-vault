# Veronica Maggio

**Veronica Maggio (born 1985-09-14) is a Swedish pop singer-songwriter from Stockholm, one of the most successful Swedish-language pop artists of the 2000s and 2010s, known for her warm, conversational lyrics and melodic indie pop.**
