# Velvet Revolver

**Velvet Revolver were an American rock supergroup formed in 2002, uniting three members of Guns N' Roses (Slash, Duff McKagan, Matt Sorum) with Stone Temple Pilots vocalist Scott Weiland and guitarist Dave Kushner.**
