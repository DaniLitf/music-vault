# Velour 100

**Indie rock / shoegaze band from USA. Active 1990s.**

Velour 100 was an American indie rock and shoegaze band active during the 1990s, featuring members who would go on to play in Starflyer 59, Pedro the Lion, and Project 86. The band's layered, atmospheric sound drew comparisons to My Bloody Valentine and Slowdive.
