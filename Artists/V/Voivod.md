# Voivod

**Progressive thrash metal from Jonquière, Quebec, Canada. Active 1982–present.**

Voivod are a Canadian metal band formed in Jonquière, Quebec in 1982, credited as pioneers of "technical thrash metal" for fusing NWOBHM speed, hardcore punk aggression, and progressive-rock structure into a dissonant, science-fiction-themed sound. Drummer Michel "Away" Langevin, also the band's longtime visual artist, is the only member to have appeared on every album; former Metallica bassist Jason Newsted recorded three albums with the band in the 2000s under the pseudonym "Jasonic."
