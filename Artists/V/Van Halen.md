# Van Halen

**Van Halen were an American rock band formed in Pasadena, California in 1972 by brothers Eddie and Alex Van Halen, whose 1978 debut introduced Eddie's revolutionary two-handed tapping guitar technique and redefined what was possible in rock guitar.**
