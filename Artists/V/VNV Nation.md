# VNV Nation

**Futurepop / EBM from London, England. Active 1990–present.**

VNV Nation (Victory Not Vengeance) is a British-Irish electronic music project founded and led by Ronan Harris. Emerging from the London electronic scene, Harris built VNV Nation into one of the defining acts of the futurepop genre, with chart success across Europe and a devoted global following. The project's sound blends melodic synth-pop, EBM, and industrial dance with orchestral and trance elements.
