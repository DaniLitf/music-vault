# Zen Lunatic

**Hard rock / heavy metal from Los Angeles, California. Active 2002.**

Zen Lunatic was a short-lived hard rock supergroup formed in 2002, featuring former Mötley Crüe frontman John Corabi alongside Bulletboys drummer Jimmy D'Anda and producer/bassist Stevo Bruno. The trio wrote and recorded several tracks at Klown Records in Santa Monica, California, including "Angel," "Stay," and "Open Your Eyes," but never released a full album before disbanding.

## Discography

No studio album was released. Recorded tracks include "Angel," "Stay," and "Open Your Eyes."
