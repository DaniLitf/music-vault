# Clutch

**Clutch are an American hard rock and stoner rock band from Germantown, Maryland, active 1991–present, known for Neil Fallon's distinctive baritone vocals, groove-driven riffs, and an encyclopaedic range of lyrical subjects from sci-fi to Southern mythology.**

One of the most respected bands in heavy rock, they have maintained artistic independence while building a large and devoted following across three decades.
