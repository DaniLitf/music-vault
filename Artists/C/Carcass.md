# Carcass

**Carcass are a British extreme metal band from Liverpool, England, active 1985–1995 and 2007–present, pioneers of both goregrind and melodic death metal, whose evolution from gore-lyric grindcore to polished metal on *Heartwork* (1993) influenced an entire generation of extreme metal bands.**

The band's 1993 album *Heartwork* is widely credited as a template for melodic death metal. Guitarist Michael Amott, who joined in 1990, went on to co-found Arch Enemy.
