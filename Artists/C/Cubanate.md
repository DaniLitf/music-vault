# Cubanate

**Cubanate were a British industrial metal duo from London, England, active 1991–1999, consisting of Phil Barry and Marc Heal, who blended EBM electronics with heavy metal guitars in an abrasive, high-energy style.**

They were part of the early 1990s British industrial scene and released material on Body Music Records.
