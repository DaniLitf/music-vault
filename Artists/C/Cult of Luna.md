# Cult of Luna

**Post-metal / sludge metal from Umeå, Sweden. Active 1998–present.**

Cult of Luna are a Swedish post-metal band formed in Umeå in 1998 from the remnants of hardcore punk band Eclipse, known for long, crushing, dynamically-building songs in the vein of Neurosis and Isis. They broke through with *Salvation* (2004) and *Somewhere Along the Highway* (2006), and later explored concept-driven records including the industrial-themed *Vertikal* (2013) and the space-themed *Mariner* (2016), a collaboration with American vocalist Julie Christmas.
