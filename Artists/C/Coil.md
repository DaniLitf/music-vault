# Coil

**Coil were a British experimental and industrial group from London, England, active 1982–2004, founded by John Balance and Peter Christopherson, whose dense, hallucinatory music drew on occultism, queer identity, and radical sound experimentation.**

One of the most revered and unclassifiable groups in post-industrial music, Coil's output ranges from brutal noise to delicate ambience. John Balance died in an accident in 2004; Peter "Sleazy" Christopherson died in 2010.
