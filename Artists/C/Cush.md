# Cush

**Alternative rock supergroup / collective from California. Active 2000.**

Cush was an American alternative rock collective formed in 2000, led by vocalist [[Members/M/Michael Knott]] and featuring a rotating cast of musicians from the Tooth & Nail Records / Christian alternative rock scene. Described more as a musical concept than a traditional band, Cush released a single self-titled album in 2000 on Northern Records, blending atmospheric rock with gospel-influenced songwriting. The project included members of [[Artists/T/The Prayer Chain]], [[Artists/S/Starflyer 59]], [[Artists/T/The Lassie Foundation]], [[Artists/A/Adam Again]], and [[Artists/F/Fold Zandura]].
