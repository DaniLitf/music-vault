# Captured

**Japanese glam metal / hard rock from Sapporo, Hokkaido. Active 1986–1994, 2006–?, 2014–present.**

Captured is a Japanese glam metal and hard rock band formed in Sapporo, Hokkaido in 1986. The band was originally active until 1994, then reformed in 2006 when guitarist Shin recruited new members. They have undergone numerous lineup changes over their decades-long career, with Shin remaining the only consistent member throughout.
