# Celtic Frost

**Swiss extreme metal band from Zürich. Active 1982–1993, 2003–2008.**

Celtic Frost are a Swiss extreme metal band formed in Zürich in 1982 by Tom Gabriel Fischer (Tom G. Warrior) and Martin Eric Ain. Widely regarded as one of the most influential extreme metal bands of all time, they pioneered the fusion of thrash metal, black metal, and avant-garde experimentation, directly influencing countless bands across the metal spectrum. The band originally formed as Hellhammer before rechristening themselves Celtic Frost in 1984. They disbanded in 1993, reformed in 2003, and dissolved again in 2008.
