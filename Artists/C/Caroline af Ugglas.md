# Caroline af Ugglas

**Caroline af Ugglas is a Swedish pop and chanson singer from Stockholm, Sweden, active 1989–present, known for her elegant stage presence, French-influenced pop sensibility, and work in both Swedish and international repertoire.**

She is the daughter of actress Margareta af Ugglas and has maintained a consistent career across recordings and theatrical work, with multiple Swedish chart successes.
