# Corpo-Mente

**French operatic/electronic project. Active 2013–present.**

Corpo-Mente is a French operatic and electronic project formed in 2013 by Gautier Serre, who is also known for his avant-garde metal project Igorrr. The project blends operatic vocals, baroque instrumentation, and electronic music into a unique, genre-defying sound.
