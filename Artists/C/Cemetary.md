# Cemetary

**Gothic metal / death metal (early) from Borås, Sweden. Active 1989–1997, 2004–2005, 2022–present.**

Cemetary are a Swedish metal band founded by Mathias Lodmalm in Borås in 1989, starting out in death/doom metal before evolving into gothic metal across their mid-to-late-1990s albums. During a mid-2000s hiatus Lodmalm released similarly styled solo material under the aliases Sundown and Cemetary 1213; the band reunited in 2022 and signed a new record deal.
