# Coverdale-Page

**Hard rock / blues rock duo from England. Active 1991–1993.**

Coverdale–Page (stylised as Coverdale • Page) was a collaboration between [[Artists/W/Whitesnake]] frontman [[Members/D/David Coverdale]] and [[Artists/L/Led Zeppelin]] guitarist [[Members/J/Jimmy Page]], brought together by Geffen A&R executive John Kalodner after Whitesnake went on hiatus and a Led Zeppelin reunion fell through. Their sole self-titled album (1993) reached number four in the UK and number five on the US Billboard 200, going platinum in the US and Canada. After a short Japanese tour in December 1993, the pair went their separate ways.
