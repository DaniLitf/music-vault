# Coldplay

**Coldplay are a British rock band from London, England, active 1996–present, one of the best-selling music acts in the world, known for anthemic, piano-driven rock that has evolved from post-Britpop to increasingly expansive pop production.**

Formed at University College London, Coldplay's *A Rush of Blood to the Head* (2002) and *X&Y* (2005) established them as a global arena rock force. They have won multiple Grammy and Brit Awards.
