# Cabaret Voltaire

**Cabaret Voltaire were a British industrial and electronic music pioneer group from Sheffield, England, active 1973–1994, whose tape manipulation, synthesizers, and confrontational aesthetic made them one of the foundational acts of industrial music.**

The duo of Stephen Mallinder and Richard H. Kirk were instrumental in developing the Sheffield electronic scene and influenced countless artists in industrial, EBM, and electronic dance music. Kirk died in 2021. Mallinder continued performing and later co-founded Wrangler.
