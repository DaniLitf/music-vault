# Cari Lekebusch

**Cari Lekebusch is a Swedish techno producer and DJ from Stockholm, Sweden, active since the early 1990s, one of the most prolific and respected figures in the Swedish and European underground techno scene.**

Operating across labels including his own H-Productions, Lekebusch has released hundreds of tracks and records under his own name and numerous aliases, helping define a distinctly Nordic approach to hard, functional techno.
