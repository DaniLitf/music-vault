# Ceremonial Oath

**Death metal / melodic death metal from Mölndal, Sweden. Active 1989–1996, 2012–present.**

Ceremonial Oath are a Swedish death metal band formed in Mölndal in 1989 as Desecrator, renaming in 1991 and disbanding in 1996 after two studio albums. Though never commercially prominent themselves, they are one of the great hidden hubs of the Gothenburg metal scene: former members went on to found or join In Flames, HammerFall, Tiamat, Cemetary, and Dark Tranquillity. The band reunited for festival shows starting in 2013 with no plans to record new material.
