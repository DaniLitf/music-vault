# Chicago

**Chicago are an American rock band from Chicago, Illinois, active 1967–present, one of the longest-running and best-selling music groups in rock history, known for incorporating jazz, R&B, and blues elements into their horn-driven rock sound.**

The band achieved their greatest commercial success with soft rock ballads in the late 1970s and 1980s, and have sold over 100 million records worldwide. Guitarist Terry Kath, considered the band's most talented musician, died in a gun accident in 1978.
