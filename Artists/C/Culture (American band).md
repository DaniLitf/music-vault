# Culture (American band)

**Vegan straight edge metalcore from Fort Lauderdale (later Gainesville), Florida, USA. Active 1993–1998 (reunions 2012–2016).**

Culture was a Florida metallic hardcore band formed in December 1993 in the Fort Lauderdale/Pompano Beach area, widely considered South Florida's first vegan straight edge band. After vocalist [[Members/D/Damien Moyal]] joined in September 1994 the band embraced its militant vegan and straight edge identity and recorded the influential debut *Born of You*, becoming a fixture of the 1990s Florida metalcore scene alongside Morning Again, Shai Hulud and Bird of Ill Omen — and, by all accounts, far bigger in Europe than at home. The band relocated to Gainesville in 1996, broke up after a European tour in September 1998, and regrouped for reunion shows between 2012 and 2016.

## Connections

- [[Artists/A/As Friends Rust]] – Damien Moyal, Timothy Kirkpatrick, Joseph Simmons, Christopher Beckham, Jason Dooley (Moyal reformed As Friends Rust in Gainesville in 1997 largely with then-members of Culture; Kirkpatrick, Simmons and Beckham all played in both, and Dooley was briefly AFR's drummer in 1997)
- [[Artists/M/Morning Again]] – John Wylie, Louie Long, Damien Moyal, Christopher Beckham, Joseph Simmons (ex-Culture members Wylie and Long founded Morning Again in December 1995 with Moyal fronting; Beckham left Morning Again to join Culture on bass in 1997; Simmons played guitar in both)
- [[Artists/B/Bird of Ill Omen]] – Damien Moyal, Joseph Simmons (Moyal fronted Bird of Ill Omen in 1996–1997 while a member of Culture; Simmons was a founding Bird of Ill Omen guitarist)
- [[Artists/S/Shai Hulud]] – Damien Moyal (Moyal fronted Shai Hulud in 1995–1996 between his two stints in Culture)
- [[Artists/T/Timescape Zero]] – Richard Thurston (Thurston played guitar in Miami's Timescape Zero before founding Culture)
- [[Artists/O/On Bodies]] – Damien Moyal (Moyal reunited with ex-Culture members in the hardcore band On Bodies, 2009–2017)
