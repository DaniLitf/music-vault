# Crystal Fairy

**Crystal Fairy are an American-Mexican psychedelic rock and noise rock supergroup, active 2014–present, featuring Teri Gender Bender of Le Butcherettes on vocals with three members of Melvins.**

The collaboration was formed spontaneously when Teri Gender Bender joined Melvins on a Mexican tour, resulting in a self-titled album that captures a raw, improvised psychedelic energy.
