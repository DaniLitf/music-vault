# Cadaver

**Norwegian death metal band from Råde/Fredrikstad. Active 1988–1993, 1999–2005, 2019–present.**

Cadaver are a Norwegian death metal band formed in 1988 by guitarist Anders Odden and Ole Bjerkebakke, one of the earliest death metal bands in Norway, predating the black metal wave. Their early demo attracted the attention of Carcass members Bill Steer and Jeff Walker, leading to a deal with Earache's Necrosis imprint; the band folded in 1993, briefly returned as "Cadaver Inc." from 1999–2002, disbanded again in 2004/2005, and was revived by Odden in 2019 alongside Megadeth drummer Dirk Verbeuren.
