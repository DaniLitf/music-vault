# Cancer

**Death metal from Liverpool, England. Active 1987–1996, 2010–present.**

Cancer are an English death metal band formed in Liverpool in 1987, part of the UK's early death metal scene alongside acts like Bolt Thrower. They are best known for their 1991 debut album *To the Gory End* and for a mid-1990s lineup that briefly included American guitarist James Murphy (also of Death, Obituary, and Testament).
