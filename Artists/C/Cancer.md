# Cancer

**Death metal band from Liverpool, England. Active 1987–1996, 2013–present.**

Cancer are an English death metal band formed in Liverpool in 1987 by John Walker (guitar, vocals), Ian Waugh (bass), and Carl Stokes (drums). They were part of the UK's early death metal scene alongside Bolt Thrower and Benediction. They are best known for their 1991 debut *To the Gory End* and their 1993 album *The Sins of Mankind*. A mid-1990s lineup briefly included American guitarist James Murphy (Death, Obituary, Testament). The band reformed in 2013.
