# Cradle of Filth

**Cradle of Filth are a British extreme metal band from Ipswich, Suffolk, England, active 1991–present, fronted by Dani Filth, whose theatrical black/gothic metal aesthetic, operatic female vocals, and horror/literary lyrics made them one of the most commercially visible extreme metal bands in the world.**

The band's theatrical excess and controversially accessible approach to black metal has earned both devoted fans and critical scorn from genre purists.
