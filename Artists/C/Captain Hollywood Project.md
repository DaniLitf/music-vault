# Captain Hollywood Project

**Eurodance project from Nuremberg, Germany. Active 1992–1996, 2008–present.**

Captain Hollywood Project is a German Eurodance music project founded in 1990 by American rapper, dancer, and producer Tony Dawson-Harrison (aka Captain Hollywood). The project became one of the defining acts of the early 1990s Eurodance explosion, scoring major hits with "More and More" (1992), "Only with You" (1993), and "Flying High" (1995). The project's sound featured Harrison's rapped verses paired with female sung choruses, a formula that became a Eurodance standard. After a hiatus between 1996 and 2008, the project returned as a live duo act with vocalist Shirin von Gehlen and continues to tour globally.
