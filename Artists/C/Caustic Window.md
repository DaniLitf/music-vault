# Caustic Window

**Caustic Window is an alias used by Richard D. James (Aphex Twin) for a series of releases in the early 1990s, characterised by harder, more raw rave and acid techno sounds than his other projects.**

A long-rumoured unreleased album *Caustic Window LP* was crowdfunded and officially released in 2014 after a test pressing surfaced. The alias represents one of several parallel identities James maintained throughout the early rave era.
