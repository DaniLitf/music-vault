# Cannibal Corpse

**American death metal band from Buffalo, New York (later Tampa, Florida). Active 1988–present.**

Cannibal Corpse is an American death metal band formed in 1988 in Buffalo, New York, and later relocated to Tampa, Florida. They are one of the most successful and influential death metal bands of all time, known for their graphic lyrical content and consistently high sales within the extreme metal underground. The band has had notable lineup changes, including the tenure of vocalist [[Members/C/Chris Barnes]] (1988–1995) and guitarist [[Members/J/Jack Owen]] (1988–2004).
