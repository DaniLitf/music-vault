# Carola

**Pop / schlager / gospel from Stockholm, Sweden. Active 1983–present.**

Carola (Carola Häggkvist) is one of Sweden's most successful singers. Her debut album *Främling* (1983) remains the best-selling album in Swedish music history, and she won the Eurovision Song Contest in 1991 with "Fångad av en stormvind" after earlier entries in 1983 (third) and 2006 (fifth). She has combined mainstream pop and schlager success with a prominent gospel and Christian music career.
