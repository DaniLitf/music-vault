# Carola

**Carola (Carola Häggkvist) is a Swedish pop and schlager singer from Stockholm, Sweden, active 1981–present, one of Sweden's most successful artists, who won the Eurovision Song Contest in 1991 with "Fångad av en stormvind."**

She has combined mainstream pop success with a prominent gospel and Christian music career, and remains one of the best-selling artists in Swedish history.
