# Chaos UK

**Hardcore punk / anarcho-punk band from Portishead, England. Active 1979–present.**

Chaos UK is an English punk rock band formed in Portishead, Somerset in 1979, emerging as part of the anarcho-punk scene. They developed a blisteringly fast and aggressive hardcore punk style that, alongside fellow Bristolians Disorder and Stoke's Discharge, helped revolutionize the UK hardcore scene. Chaos UK's debut LP on Riot City Records was promoted as the "fastest, noisiest LP in the cosmos." The band had a particularly strong influence on Japanese hardcore punk and were the first English punk band to tour Japan.
