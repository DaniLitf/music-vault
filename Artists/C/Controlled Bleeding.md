# Controlled Bleeding

**American industrial/experimental band from New York. Active 1978–present.**

Controlled Bleeding are an American industrial and experimental band formed in New York in 1978 by Paul Lemos. Known for their extreme, boundary-pushing sound that spans industrial noise, dark ambient, experimental rock, and electronic music, they are one of the longest-running and most respected acts in the American industrial underground.
