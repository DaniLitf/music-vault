# Cocteau Twins

**Cocteau Twins were a Scottish dream pop and shoegaze band from Grangemouth, Scotland, active 1979–1997, known for Elizabeth Fraser's ethereal, phoneme-blurring vocals and Robin Guthrie's lush, effects-laden guitar textures.**

One of the most influential groups in alternative music, their sound defined a strand of ethereal, emotionally intense rock that influenced decades of dream pop, shoegaze, and indie music.
