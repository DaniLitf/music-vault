# Centurion

**Heavy/power metal from Cleveland, Ohio, USA. Active 1984–1989.**

Centurion was an American heavy metal band from Cleveland, Ohio, formed in 1984. They released one studio album, *Cross and Black* (1987), and appeared on the compilation *Northcoast Steel: Cleveland Heavy Metal* (1988) with the track "Peacefully". The band featured a lineup of Cleveland-area musicians who would go on to play in Hatrix, Mushroomhead, and other regional acts.
