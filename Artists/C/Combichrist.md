# Combichrist

**Combichrist are a Norwegian aggrotech and industrial metal project from Bergen, Norway, active 2003–present, founded by Andy LaPlegua, known for abrasive, high-BPM industrial dance music and subsequent heavier, metal-influenced direction.**

The project is one of the most commercially successful acts in modern industrial music and has toured extensively as support for major metal acts.
