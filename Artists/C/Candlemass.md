# Candlemass

**Candlemass are a Swedish doom metal band from Stockholm, Sweden, active 1984–present, founded by bassist Leif Edling, widely considered the definitive epic doom metal band whose debut *Epicus Doomicus Metallicus* (1986) defined the genre.**

The band's operatic vocal style and crushing slow tempos influenced virtually every doom metal act that followed. They have cycled through several vocalists over the decades, with Johan Längquist returning for *Psalms for the Dead* (2012).
