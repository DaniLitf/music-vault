# Children of Bodom

**Children of Bodom were a Finnish melodic death metal band from Espoo, Finland, active 1993–2019, led by guitarist and vocalist Alexi Laiho (1979–2020), who combined neoclassical guitar shredding with aggressive death metal energy.**

The band were one of the most commercially successful melodic death metal acts, with *Follow the Reaper* (2000) and *Hate Crew Deathroll* (2003) as career high points. Alexi Laiho dissolved the band in 2019 and died in January 2020.
