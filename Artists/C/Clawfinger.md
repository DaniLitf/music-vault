# Clawfinger

**Clawfinger are a Swedish-Norwegian rap metal band from Gothenburg, Sweden, active 1989–2015, who blended Rage Against the Machine-style riff-driven metal with hip-hop delivery and socially engaged, anti-racist lyrics.**

The band were a significant commercial force in Scandinavia in the 1990s, with the single "Nigger" (a provocative anti-racism statement) launching them to international attention in 1993.
