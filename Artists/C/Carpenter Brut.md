# Carpenter Brut

**Darksynth / synthwave from Poitiers, France. Active 2012–present.**

Carpenter Brut is the darksynth project of French musician Franck Hueso, known for a cinematic, metal-influenced take on 1980s synthesizer aesthetics evoking horror film soundtracks and action movie scores. The name is a play on filmmaker John Carpenter and the champagne Charpentier Brut. His compilation *Trilogy* (2015) became a definitive entry point for the genre, and his music has featured in *Hotline Miami 2*, *Furi*, and the film *Blood Machines*.
