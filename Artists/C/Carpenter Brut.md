# Carpenter Brut

**Carpenter Brut is a French synthwave artist from Poitiers, France, active 2012–present, known for a cinematic, metal-influenced take on 1980s synthesizer aesthetics, evoking horror film soundtracks and action movie scores.**

Carpenter Brut operates anonymously (real name undisclosed) and has become one of the most acclaimed artists in the synthwave revival, with the compilation *TRILOGY* (2015) serving as the definitive introduction to their style.
