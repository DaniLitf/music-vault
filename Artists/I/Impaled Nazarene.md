# Impaled Nazarene

**Black metal / Blackened thrash metal from Oulu, Finland. Active 1990–present.**

Impaled Nazarene is a Finnish extreme metal band formed in Oulu in 1990. Known for their aggressive, genre-blending sound that incorporates black metal, grindcore, thrash, and hardcore punk, the band has released 13 studio albums and built a reputation for their confrontational nationalist and anti-religious lyrics. Frontman Mika Luttinen has been the sole constant member since the band's founding.
