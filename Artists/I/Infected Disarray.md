# Infected Disarray

**Brutal death metal band from England. Active 2000–present.**

Infected Disarray are an English brutal death metal band formed in 2000 as a side project of members from the UK death metal underground, including bassist Steve "Mr. Smith" Smith and drummer John "Junky Jon" Rushforth, both formerly of Gorerotted. Their original lineup recorded a demo in 2001 before splitting, later reforming to release the album *Redisseminating Obscenity* through Unique Leader Records in 2009.
