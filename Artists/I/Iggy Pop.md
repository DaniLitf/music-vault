# Iggy Pop

**Iggy Pop (born James Newell Osterberg Jr., April 21, 1947, in Muskegon, Michigan) is an American singer, songwriter, and performer, known as the "Godfather of Punk" — frontman of the pioneering proto-punk band The Stooges and a hugely influential solo artist.**

Iggy Pop is a solo artist. See [[Members/I/Iggy Pop]] for biography.
