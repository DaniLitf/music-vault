# Igorrr

**Experimental breakcore / death metal / baroque from France. Active 2005–present.**

Igorrr is the solo project of French multi-instrumentalist and producer Gautier Serre, known for fusing breakcore, death metal, baroque music, and electronic production into a chaotic, genre-defying sound. Since 2017 it has evolved into a full live band. Serre's work has been released on Ad Noiseam and Metal Blade Records.
