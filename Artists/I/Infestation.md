# Infestation

**Death metal band from England. Active 1998–present.**

Infestation are an English death metal band that drew both its drummer, Dave "Infester" Hirschheimer, and guitarist, Jeremy "Milky" Gray, from deathgrind band Gorerotted in 1998 and 1999 respectively. The band has appeared on compilations for Terrorizer and Zero Tolerance magazines.
