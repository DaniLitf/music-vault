# Icon of Coil

**EBM / futurepop from Fredrikstad, Norway. Active 1997–present.**

Icon of Coil is a Norwegian electronic music band established as a solo project by Andy LaPlegua in 1997. He was soon joined by Sebastian Komor and Christian Lund, solidifying a trio that defined the futurepop and EBM scene in the early 2000s. Their debut album *Serenity Is the Devil* (2000) reached number one on the Deutsche Alternative Charts. The band has released three studio albums and continues to perform sporadically.
