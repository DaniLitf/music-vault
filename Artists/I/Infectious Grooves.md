# Infectious Grooves

**Funk metal from Venice, California. Active 1989–2000, 2008–present.**

Infectious Grooves is an American funk metal supergroup formed in 1989 by Suicidal Tendencies frontman Mike Muir and bassist Robert Trujillo. A lighter, comedic counterpart to Suicidal Tendencies' hardcore, the band blends funk grooves with heavy metal riffs.
