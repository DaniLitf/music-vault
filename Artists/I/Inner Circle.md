# Inner Circle

**Reggae band from Kingston, Jamaica. Active 1968–1980, 1981–1982, 1986–present.**

Inner Circle, also known as "The Bad Boys of Reggae," are a Jamaican reggae band formed in Kingston in 1968 by brothers Ian and Roger Lewis. Fronted through the 1970s by vocalist Jacob Miller, they became one of Jamaica's most popular live acts before Miller's death in a car crash in 1980. The band reformed in 1986 and achieved international crossover success with the singles "Bad Boys" (which later became the theme to the TV series *Cops*) and the 1993 Grammy-winning "Sweat (A La La La La Long)."
