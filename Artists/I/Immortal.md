# Immortal

**Black metal band from Bergen, Norway. Active 1991–2003, 2006–present.**

Immortal are a Norwegian black metal band from Bergen, founded in 1991 by Abbath Doom Occulta and Demonaz Doom Occulta out of the earlier bands Old Funeral and Amputation. Known for raw, high-speed black metal built around lyrical themes of the fictional realm Blashyrkh, the band achieved a stable lineup with drummer Horgh in 1996 and became one of the most acclaimed acts in the genre through albums like *At the Heart of Winter* (1999) and *Sons of Northern Darkness* (2002). The band split in 2003, reformed in 2006, and continued despite Abbath's 2015 departure and a subsequent legal dispute over the band's name.
