# Ian Hunter

**Solo rock career of the Mott the Hoople frontman. British. Active 1975–present.**

Ian Hunter launched a solo career in 1975 after Mott the Hoople's split, releasing his eponymous debut *Ian Hunter* (1975) featuring the hit "Once Bitten, Twice Shy." He became a frequent collaborator with guitarist/producer [[Members/M/Mick Ronson]] (former Bowie sideman) until Ronson's death in 1993, and has continued releasing albums and touring with his backing group, the Rant Band, into the 2020s. His best-known solo song, "Cleveland Rocks" (from *You're Never Alone with a Schizophrenic*, 1979), became a Cleveland sports anthem and a *Drew Carey Show* theme via a cover by the Presidents of the United States of America.
