# In Flames

**Melodic death metal / alternative metal from Gothenburg, Sweden. Active 1990–present.**

In Flames are a Swedish heavy metal band formed in Gothenburg in 1990 by Jesper Strömblad. Alongside At the Gates and Dark Tranquillity, they are considered pioneers of melodic death metal. Over their career they have evolved from a pure melodeath sound into a more accessible alternative metal style, while maintaining a devoted international following. They have released fourteen studio albums to date.
