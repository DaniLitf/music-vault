# In Slaughter Natives

**In Slaughter Natives is a Swedish industrial and dark ambient project founded by Jouni Havukainen in Motala in 1985, a pioneering force in the martial industrial and death industrial subgenres and a cornerstone of the Cold Meat Industry label.**
