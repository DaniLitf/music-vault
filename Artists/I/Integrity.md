# Integrity

**Metalcore / hardcore punk from Cleveland, Ohio. Active 1988–present.**

Integrity is an American hardcore punk band formed in Cleveland, Ohio in 1988 by vocalist Dwid Hellion. The band is considered a pioneer of the metalcore genre, blending hardcore punk with heavy metal, gothic rock, industrial, and noise influences. Integrity has been based in Belgium since 2003, with Hellion as the only constant member across dozens of lineups. The band's iconic run of 1990s albums — *Those Who Fear Tomorrow* (1991), *Systems Overload* (1995), and *Seasons in the Size of Days* (1997) — cemented their influence on Converge, Hatebreed, Killswitch Engage, and countless others.
