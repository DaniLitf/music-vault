# Imperiet

**Post-punk / rock from Stockholm, Sweden. Active 1981–1988 (as Rymdimperiet 1981–1983).**

Imperiet grew out of Rymdimperiet, a 1981 side project of three [[Artists/E/Ebba Grön]] members — [[Members/J/Joakim Thåström]] (vocals, guitar), [[Members/A/Anders Sjöholm]] (vocals, guitar), and [[Members/G/Gunnar Ljungstedt]] (drums) — joined by bassist [[Members/C/Christian Falk]] and saxophonist/keyboardist [[Members/P/Per Hägglund]]. When Ebba Grön broke up in early 1983, the side project took over as Thåström's main band and was renamed Imperiet that spring. Their debut album *Rasera* (1983) still carried traces of Ebba Grön's punk energy, but the band moved further into mainstream rock, breaking through commercially with *Blå himlen blues* (1985). Lineup turnover continued through the mid-1980s — Sjöholm left in 1984, Ljungstedt in 1985 (replaced by [[Members/F/Fred Asp]]) — and the band became one of the biggest acts in Swedish rock before splitting in December 1988, shortly after Hägglund's departure and an English-language final album.
